Faricimab is another treatment option that works in a similar way. Evidence from clinical trials shows that faricimab is as effective as aflibercept. An indirect comparison of faricimab with ranibizumab also suggests similar clinical effectiveness. A cost comparison suggests faricimab has similar costs and overall health benefits to aflibercept or ranibizumab. So, faricimab is recommended for treating diabetic macular oedema if it is used in the same population as aflibercept and ranibizumab.
