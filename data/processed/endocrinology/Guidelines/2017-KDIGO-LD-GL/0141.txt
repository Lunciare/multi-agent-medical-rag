RESEARCH RECOMMENDATIONS • Develop contemporary estimates of 5.10, the risk of peri- nephrectomy complications according to an individual- ized profile of predonation characteristics, accounting for changes in baseline comorbidity burdens. • Assess the predictive value of novel risk factors for perioper- ative complications and readmission after donor nephrec- tomy, including use of opioids and other pharmaceuticals.113 • Perform RCTs to formally assess the efficacy of evaluation and perioperative management techniques to minimize the risk of perioperative complications after living donor nephrectomy.
