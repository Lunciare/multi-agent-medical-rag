Donor candidates should have a detailed evaluation including a careful history and medical record review of any prior or current kidney stones. In the case of a prior history of stones, additional details that should be assessed include the time and location of prior episodes, and any prior investigations or treatments. The frequency of kidney stones varies by sex, race and climate.
