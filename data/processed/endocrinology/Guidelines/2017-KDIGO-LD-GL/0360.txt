A subse- quent systematic review of 34 prospective studies of postdonation HRQoL (1990 to 2014) found that, after mild reductions early after nephrectomy, HRQoL returned to baseline or was slightly reduced by 3 to 12 months, particu- larly for fatigue, but was still comparable with general popu- lation norms.420 In general, donors demonstrate good quality of life and have low rates of donation regret. That said, living donation is not without psychosocial risk, and some donors have expe- rienced psychosocial difficulties after donation (eg, depression, impacted their functioning and ability to return to work.
