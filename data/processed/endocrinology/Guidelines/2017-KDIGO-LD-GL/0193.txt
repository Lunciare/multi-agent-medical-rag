The estimated prevalence of microscopic hematuria varies widely from 0.18% to 16%.160 A recent population study of 1.2 million persons aged 16 to 25 years in Israel identified prevalent asymptomatic persistent microscopic hematuria in 0.3% of individuals.163 Persistent microscopic hematuria may be associated with urologic abnormalities (eg, stones, tu- mors) or glomerular disease.
