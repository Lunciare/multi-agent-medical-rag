This evaluation helps deter- mine if a candidate is psychologically fit for donation, for ex- ample, by assessing if the donor candidate’s motivations are appropriate and identifying psychosocial risk factors for poor outcomes. The evaluation also helps address any donor candidate concerns, and ensures potential psychosocial risks and benefits of kidney donation are disclosed and under- stood.
