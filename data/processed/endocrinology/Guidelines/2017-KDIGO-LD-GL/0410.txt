Documentation of a donor’s acknowledgement of their responsibility to partici- pate in follow-up through a signed care plan has been pro- posed as part of education,525 although efficacy in achieving follow-up participation has not been formally evaluated. Particular attention should be given to donors with risk factors for follow-up deficiencies and to those with baseline factors associated with increased risk of CKD over time. Donor education before and at the time of donation is crit- ical to support an informed donation decision, but there is a need to continue education on health promoting practices as the donor recovers and ages. A donor in the midst of making a decision to donate or dealing with the early outcomes of do- nation such as postoperative pain and financial pressures may not absorb all the information on long-term issues.
