Examination of a series of 510 implantation biopsies at a large center in Tokyo (including 446 living donors) found latent mesangial IgA deposition to be relatively common in healthy Japanese living donors, present in 16.1% of living donor allograft biopsies.177 Mesangial IgA deposition was associated with a mild degree of microhematuria, mesangial proliferation and glomerular macrophage infiltration in some of the af- fected individuals, especially with C3 deposition.
