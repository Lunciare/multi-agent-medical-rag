Dyslipidemias Dyslipidemia is a modifiable risk factor for CVD, and hypertriglyceridemia and low HDL-C are components of the metabolic syndrome. With regard to associations of lipid levels with outcomes after donation, one study identified by the ERTcompared kidney function and albuminuria at 5 years among donors with versus without predonation metabolic syndrome (Evidence Report Table 18, SDC, lww.com/TP/B434; Supplemental Appendix Table D15, SDC, Although small differ- ences in the outcomes were suggested, statistical comparisons were not performed. No studies were identified comparing postdonation outcomes based on lipid status alone. A recent meta-analysis based on data from nearly 5 million persons identified from 7 general population cohorts found no asso- ciations of total cholesterol or LDL-C with ESKD risk over median cohort follow-up of 4 to 16 years.7 Several prior guidelines recommend fasting lipid profiling as selection criteria based on lipid levels alone.
