Although data on the outcomes associated with smoking among living kidney donors are limited, an analysis of linked US transplant registry and national death records found that while smoking was not significantly associated with perinephrectomy mortality, donors who smoked had approximately 5-times the adjusted mortality over 12 years compared with nonsmoking donors.23 See chapter 4 (Preop- erative Evaluation) for a discussion of the smoking-related risks before general surgery.
