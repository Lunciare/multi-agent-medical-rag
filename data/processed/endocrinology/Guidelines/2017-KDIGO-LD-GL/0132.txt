CHAPTER 4: PREOPERATIVE EVALUATION AND MANAGEMENT The ERT search parameters did not identify evidence from eligible studies pertinent to the recommendations in chapter 4 and therefore the following recommendations are “Not Graded.” 4.1: Donor candidates should receive guideline-based evalua- tion and management used for other noncardiac surgeries to minimize risks of perioperative complications, includ- ing a detailed history and examination to assess risks for cardiac, pulmonary, bleeding, anesthesia-related and other perioperative complications. 4.2: Donor candidates who smoke should be advised to quit at least 4 weeks before donation to reduce their risk of perioperative complications, and commit to lifelong ab- stinence to prevent long-term complications.
