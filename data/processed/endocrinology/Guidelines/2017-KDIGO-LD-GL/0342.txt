A study from Taiwan also described a greater risk of ESKD in preg- nant women who developed a hypertensive disorder during pregnancy compared with a normal pregnancy (approximate 12-year cumulative incidence after pregnancy of 0.6% vs < 0.1%)398 In general, characteristics associated with a lower long-term risk of ESKD after a history of hypertension in preg- nancy include: i) a mild (vs severe) hypertensive event during pregnancy; ii) more than 10 years since last hypertension event in pregnancy; iii) no evidence of hypertension, albuminuria or low GFR in the current donor evaluation; and iv) no wish for future pregnancies. Gestational diabetes is a strong risk factor for subsequent diabetes mellitus. A history of gestational diabetes mellitus is also associated with a higher risk of ESKD.399 For recom- mendations related to gestational diabetes see Recommen- dations 11.5 and 11.7.
