For example, the 2014 American Urological Association Guideline provides comprehensive consensus and evidence- based recommendations for the evaluation of adult patients with kidney stones186: 1. A clinician should perform a screening evaluation consisting of a detailed medical and dietary history, serum chemistries and urinalysis on a patient newly diagnosed with kidney or ureteral stones.
