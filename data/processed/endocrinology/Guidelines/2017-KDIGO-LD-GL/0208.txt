Possible Consequences of Kidney Stones Kidney stones may be associated with a higher risk of CKD and ESKD, with possible mechanisms including obstructive uropathy or pyelonephritis, crystal plugs at tips of the renal papilla and parenchymal injury from treatments such as shockwave lithotripsy.183,189 One or more episodes of kidney stones were associated with a 2-fold higher risk of ESKD in one population-based study from Alberta, Canada.190 The association was stronger in patients with 2 or more episodes of kidney stones versus a single episode of kidney stones.
