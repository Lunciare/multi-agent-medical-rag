CHAPTER 1: GOALS OF EVALUATION, FRAMEWORK FOR DECISION-MAKING, AND ROLES AND RESPONSIBILITIES Goals and Principles of Evaluation 1.1: The donor candidate’s willingness to donate a kidney vol- untarily without undue pressure should be verified. 1.2: The benefits and risks of kidney donation should be assessed for each donor candidate. 1.3: The decision to accept or exclude a donor candidate should follow transplant program policies. 1.4: Donor candidate decision-making should be facilitated through education and counseling on individualized risks and benefits, methods to minimize risks, and the need for postdonation follow-up. 1.5: For an accepted donor candidate, a plan for donation care and follow-up should be formulated to minimize risks of donation. 1.6: For an excluded donor candidate, a plan for any needed care and support should be formulated.
