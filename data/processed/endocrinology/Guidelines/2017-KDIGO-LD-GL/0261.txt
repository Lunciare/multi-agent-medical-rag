RATIONALE Goals of Evaluation and Definitions The goals of infection screening in donor candidates are to identify illnesses that may affect the donor candidate or the intended recipient.273-276 Evaluation of donor candi- dates to reduce the risk of transmissible infections should include assessment of the individual’s history of past infec- tions and infectious disease risk factors (eg, risk of local en- demic infections or travel to endemic areas), awareness of current patterns of geographically endemic infections, and focused microbiological screening.
