KDIGO Clinical Practice Guideline on the Evaluation and Care of Living Kidney Donors
KEYWORDS: donor, donation, kidney, risk, candidate, donor candidate, candidates, transplant, donor candidates, living, evaluation, evidence, guideline, outcomes, recipient

KDIGO, Brussels, Belgium. Guideline Cochairs, K.L.L, A.X.G., contributed equally. This Clinical Practice Guideline is based upon systematic literature searches last conducted in September 2014 supplemented with additional evidence through January 2017. It is designed to assist decision making. It is not intended to define a standard of care, and should not be interpreted as prescribing an exclusive course of management. Variations in practice will inevitably and appropriately occur when considering the needs of individual patients, available resources, and limitations unique to an institution or type of practice. Healthcare professionals using these recommendations should decide how to apply them to their own clinical practece.
