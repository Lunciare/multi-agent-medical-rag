Of note, as for most surgical procedures, experience is likely to be an important determinant of outcomes, and trials and observational series were generally performed at experi- enced centers by limited numbers of surgeons. Operative Time Fonouni et al448 reported a trend towards longer average operative time for laparoscopic compared with open donor nephrectomy, and Yuan et al and Wilson et al confirmed lon- ger operative times for laparoscopic procedures.450,451 Meta- analysis by Yuan et al451 estimated that laparoscopic ne- phrectomy requires an average of 51 minutes longer surgical time (95% CI, 33-68 minutes). Surgery times averaged 2 to 3 hours for open nephrectomy and 3 to 4 hours for laparo- scopic nephrectomy. Blood Loss Five of the 8 publications included in the review by Fonouni et al448 reported less perioperative blood loss with laparoscopic nephrectomy, while the other 3 found no signif- icant differences in blood loss between the approaches.
