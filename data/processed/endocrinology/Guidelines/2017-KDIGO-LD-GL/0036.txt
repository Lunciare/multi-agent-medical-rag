METHODS FOR GUIDELINE DEVELOPMENT AIM The overall aim of this project was to develop an evidence- based clinical practice guideline on the evaluation and care of living kidney donors. The guideline consists of recom- mendation statements and supporting rationale, including summaries of systematically generated evidence on relevant predefined clinical topics.
