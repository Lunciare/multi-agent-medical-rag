Living donation affords sufficient time for microbiological testing, donor treatment and deferral of donation and transplantation until resolution of infection. Organ donation may be possible after treatment of the donor candidate before donation, informed consent of the recipient, as well as recipient monitor- ing and possible prophylaxis after transplant (Table 19). Strongyloidiasis typically occurs only in the setting of specific environmental exposures.
