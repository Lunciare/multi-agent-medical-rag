(2D) 17.4: Robotic, single-port, and natural orifice transluminal nephrectomy should generally not be used for donor nephrectomy. 17.5: Nontransfixing clips, (eg, Weck Hem-o-lok) should not be used to ligate the renal artery in donor nephrectomy; instead, renal artery transfixation by suture ligature or anchor staple within the vessel wall should be used. 17.6: In the absence of reasons to procure the right kidney (vascular, urological or other abnormalities), the left kidney should be procured in laparoscopic donor ne- phrectomy because of the relative technical ease associ- ated with a longer venous pedicle. 17.7: We suggest laparoscopic procurement of the right rather than the left living donor kidney may be performed if the surgeon has adequate training and experience.
