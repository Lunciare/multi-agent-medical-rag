Based on these data, we suggest that donor candidates should be informed that BP may rise with aging, and that donation may accelerate the rise in BP and the need for antihypertensive treatment over that expected with normal aging, especially if BP is high-normal before donation and among African American donor candidates. Furthermore, antihypertensive medication is more likely to be prescribed after donation.
