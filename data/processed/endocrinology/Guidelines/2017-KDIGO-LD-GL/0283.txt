The laboratory methods for CMV di- agnosis are serology, culture, antigenemia, and molecular methods such as CMV NAT, which is most commonly per- formed using real-time polymerase chain reaction.309 The main clinical utility of CMV serology is stratification of a transplant recipient’s risk of CMV disease based on donor and recipient status.279,280,310 The presence of anti-CMVantibodies in a donor candidate indicates prior infection, with the potential that the latent vi- rus will reactivate and cause infection, particularly in the CMV- recipient. The detection of anti-CMV antibodies does not preclude donation, and infection risk can be anticipated and managed. Primary CMV infection is generally more se- vere than reactivation and recipients at highest risk are those who are CMV seronegative and receive a kidney transplant from a CMV seropositive donor.
