Kidney transplant recipients from anti-HBcAb +/HBsAg-/HBV DNA- donors appear to have little risk of ac- quiring active HBV infection.275,292 HBV NAT testing should be performed in donor candidates with isolated HBcAb+ status to further define risk of transmission. If the donor is anti-HBc + and HBV DNA-, the risk of transmission is negligible,293 especially if the recipient is anti-HBsAb + or has been effectively immunized against HBV. Still, the recipient should be informed of the small potential risk of disease trans- mission, and posttransplant monitoring should be performed.
