Tobacco Use 11.13: The use of tobacco products should be assessed before donation. 11.14: Donor candidates who use tobacco products should be counseled on the risks of perioperative complications, cancer, cardio-pulmonary disease and kidney failure, should be advised to abstain from use of tobacco prod- ucts, and should be referred to a tobacco cessation sup- port program if possible. 11.15: The decision to approve donor candidates who are ac- tive tobacco users should be individualized based on demographic and health profile in relation to the trans- plant program’s acceptable risk threshold.
