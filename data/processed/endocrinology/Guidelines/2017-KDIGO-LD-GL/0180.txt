Consistent with the 2012 KDIGO CKD guideline, test- ing for specific urine proteins such as α1-microglobulin, β2 microglobulin, monoclonal heavy or light chains (also known as “Bence Jones” proteins) can be undertaken if sig- nificant nonalbumin proteinuria is suspected. Selection: Criteria for Acceptable Predonation Albuminuria AER in the General Population The normal level of AER in healthy young men and women is less than 10 mg/d. The coefficient of variation for repeated measurements is approximately 30%.157 Because of the high coefficient of variation, repeated measurements are preferred for assessment of albuminuria. AER rises with age, although the cause of rise is not known and the rate of rise appears widely variable. Most data are based on cross-sectional studies and mean AER is higher in older populations than in young populations. As discussed in chapter 5, there are often abnormalities in kid- ney function and structure in the elderly and there is debate about whether higher AER in older people represents nor- mal aging or disease.
