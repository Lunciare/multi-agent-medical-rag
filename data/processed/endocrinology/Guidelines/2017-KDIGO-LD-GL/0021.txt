CHAPTER 9: HYPERURICEMIA, GOUT, AND MINERAL AND BONE DISEASE Evaluation 9.1: Donor candidates should be asked about prior epi- sodes of gout. Counseling 9.2: Donor candidates may be informed that donation is as- sociated with an increase in serum uric acid concentra- tion, which may increase the risk for gout. 9.3: Donor candidates and donors with prior episodes of gout should be informed of recommended methods to reduce their risk of future episodes of gout.
