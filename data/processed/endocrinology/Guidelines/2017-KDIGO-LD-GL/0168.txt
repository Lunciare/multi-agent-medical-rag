The average length and volume of a single kidney in healthy adults are approximately 11 cm and 150 mL, respectively, but vary based on age, sex, and body size.149-151 On average the normal right kidney is approximately 5% smaller than the normal left kidney. Asymmetry in kidney size is generally considered as a difference in kidney size greater than 10% (for example, a difference in kidney length > 1.1 cm or kid- ney volume > 15 mL). An equivalent difference in kidney function would be greater than 10% (>55% vs <45% in split kidney function).
