With permission of the intended recipient, information about the intended recipient’s cause of kidney failure should be reviewed carefully by the donor evaluation team and shared with the donor candidate if it could affect the decision to donate. Some donor candidates have a family history of genetic kidney disease.350 Some of these diseases first manifest later in life and are not identified when donor evaluation occurs at a younger age. Donation could increase the lifetime risk of kidney failure in such a candidate. For this reason, it is im- portant that transplant programs have a strategy for evaluat- ing for inherited kidney disease in donor candidates when there is a family history of kidney failure and the recipient’s cause of kidney failure is uncertain.
