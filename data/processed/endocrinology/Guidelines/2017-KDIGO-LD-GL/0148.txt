GFR as an Index of Kidney Function The level of GFR is widely accepted as the best overall index of kidney function in health and disease. The foun- dations for this acceptance are the well-known relation- ships of alterations in kidney structure and GFR in kidney disease, and well known pathophysiologic relationships of kidney disease complications to decreased GFR.
