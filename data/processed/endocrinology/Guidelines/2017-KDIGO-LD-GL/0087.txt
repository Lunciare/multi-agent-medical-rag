The resulting risk models were incorporated into an online risk prediction tool for 15-year and lifetime ESKD risk ( Although the risk tool was developed specifically for the United States, the methods used may be adapted to other countries with the availability of local data sources. The WG endorses use of the online tool as the foundation for a new evalua- tion framework centered on simultaneous consideration of multiple demographic and health characteristics to predict long-term risk of an important outcome, while recognizing limitations in precision of risk estimates and uncertainty in donation-attributable risk. These models should be refined through further research to improve the precision and gener- alizability of predonation risk estimates and to incorporate estimates of indivdualized risk attributable to donation.
