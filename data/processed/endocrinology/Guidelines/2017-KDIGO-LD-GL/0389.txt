A 2011 ASTS survey reported that Hem-o-lok or other clips are still used by some surgeons as sole means of arterial control in laparo- scopic donor nephrectomy.469 Subsequently, in a 2013 survey of 645 members of the European Society for Organ Transplan- tation, 20% of respondents reported use of clips (locking and nonlocking) to close the arterial stump.471 Of the 121 re- ported hemorrhagic events, slippage and dislodgement of clips occurred in at least 58 (47.9%).471 All surgeons operat- ing on a living organ donor should select vascular control techniques that entail tissue transfixion and assure a safe op- erative recovery. The Hem-o-lok and other surgical clips should not be used to ligate the donor renal artery.
