Evidence Profiles The structured GRADE approach was used to grade the quality of the overall evidence (Table 3). Evidence profiles consisting of tables examining all relevant outcomes, including a summary of the results and judgments about the certainty and quality of the evidence, were used to facilitate this process. The GRADE approach is prescriptive in how evidence quality is assessed. The study design suggests the initial quality of evidence: high for RCTs and low for observational studies. Of note for the current guide- line, most studies of outcomes in living kidney donation are not ethically or practically amenable to randomized con- trolled designs, limiting the possible quality rating based on the nature of possible study designs.
