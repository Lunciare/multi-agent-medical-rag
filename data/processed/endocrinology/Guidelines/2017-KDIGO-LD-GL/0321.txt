Apolipoprotein L1 (APOL1) Risk Alleles 14.8: Apolipoprotein L1 (APOL1) genotyping may be offered to donor candidates with sub-Saharan African ancestors. Donor candidates should be informed that having 2 APOL1 risk alleles increases the lifetime risk of kidney fail- ure but that the precise kidney failure risk for an affected in- dividual after donation cannot currently be quantified. RATIONALE Goals of Evaluation Genetic kidney diseases include autosomal dominant poly- cystic kidney disease (ADPKD), APOL1-related kidney disease, atypical hemolytic uremic syndrome (aHUS), Alport syndrome, Fabrydisease,familialfocalsegmentalglomerulosclerosis(FSGS), and autosomal dominant tubulointerstitial kidney disease (ADTKD). A family history of a genetic kidney disease with an autosomal recessive mode of inheritance (such as cystinosis or some forms of familial FSGS) is usually not a contraindication for living kidney donation, although long-term follow-up data are not available in the literature.
