Kidney disease from PKD2 presents later in life than PKD1. Age-dependent imaging criteria for diagnosis and disease exclusion have been established for at-risk adults with unknown ADPKD gene type (PKD1 or PKD2).353 For example, for candidates 40 years or older with a first degree relative with ADPKD, the finding of normal kidneys or a sin- gle cyst seen in one kidney with no cysts seen in the other kid- ney reliably rules out the presence of ADPKD.354 However, the utility of ultrasound to rule out ADPKD in younger do- nor candidates is more limited, where an absent or limited number of cysts on CT or MRI can be considered to rule out ADPKD.352 DNA testing can also sometimes help diag- nose or exclude the condition.355 Linkage-based genetic diag- noses of ADPKD using polymorphic markers flanking the 2 disease genes is now rarely performed.
