In a series of 242 living kidney donors at one center in Japan, persistent predonation hematuria was identified in 8.3% (18.6% vs 6% in those with vs without family history of IgA nephropathy or Alport syndrome).178 95% of those with persistent predonation hematuria continued to have persistent hematuria after donation over median 27 month follow-up (compared with 28% of those with predonation occasional hematuria and 5% without predonation hematu- ria). Predonation hematuria was associated with increased likelihood of persistent proteinuria (dipstick ≥1+) after do- nation (without dysmorphic red blood cells: adjusted OR 3.8; with dysmorphic red blood cells: adjusted OR 12.3). Predonation hematuria was not associated with postdonation GFR, but persistent postdonation hematuria with dysmorphic red blood cells was associated with significant GFR decline over the study period.
