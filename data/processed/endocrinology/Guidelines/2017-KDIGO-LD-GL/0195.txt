Selection The sequence of the evaluation for microscopic hematuria, like other testing in this guideline, is designed to perform less invasive/expensive tests before more invasive/expensive tests, and only if the less invasive/expensive tests do not preclude donation (Figure 13).168 Reversible causes of microscopic hematuria, for example, treatable urinary tract infection, should generally not preclude donation.
