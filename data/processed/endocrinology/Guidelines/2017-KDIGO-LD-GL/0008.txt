Original data analyses were undertaken to produce a “proof-in-concept” risk-prediction model for kidney failure to support a framework for quantitative risk assessment in the donor candidate evaluation and defensible shared decision making. This framework is grounded in the si- multaneous consideration of each candidate's profile of demographic and health characteristics. The processes and frame- work for the donor candidate evaluation are presented, along with recommendations for optimal care before, during, and after donation. Limitations of the evidence are discussed, especially regarding the lack of definitive prospective studies diction and novel approaches to estimating donation-attributable risks, are also provided.
