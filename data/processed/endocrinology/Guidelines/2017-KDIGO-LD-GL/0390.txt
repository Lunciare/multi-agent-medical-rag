What Prior Guidelines Recommend The CARI guideline states that the use of a nontransfixing technique for securing the renal artery is not recommended, particularly with laparoscopic donor nephrectomy.453 In the current guideline, we recommend that nontransfixing clips should never be used to ligate the renal artery in donor nephrectomy.
