Although the risk for developing diabetes follows HbA1c levels as a continuum, i.e., higher values are associated with higher risk for future development of diabetes (320–322), an International Expert Committee (22) rec- ommended HbA1c levels from 6.0% to 6.4% and the ADA has recommended HbA1c levels from 5.7% to 6.4% (2) as those that deﬁne high risk to develop fu- ture diabetes (prediabetes). The concen- tration chosen to deﬁne high risk may depend on resources available to address prevention. Recommendation: Point-of-care HbA1c testing for diabetes screening and diagno- sis should be restricted to FDA-approved devices at CLIA-certiﬁed laboratories that perform testing of moderate complexity or higher.
