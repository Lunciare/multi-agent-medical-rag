Albuminuria testing is recommended 5 years after diagnosis of type 1 diabetes, although a baseline read- ing at the time of diagnosis may be appro- priate. Most longitudinal cohort studies report signiﬁcant increases in the preva- lence of moderately increased albumin- uria only after type 1 diabetes has been present for 5 years (456,457).
