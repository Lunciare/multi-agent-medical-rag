Emerging Considerations and Knowledge Gaps/Research Needs Capillary Kits for Measurement of HbA1c Capillary blood sample kits have been used in research studies and shown to perform well compared with venous whole-blood samples when assayed with a high-performance chromatography method (353,354). The capillary tubes are ﬁlled with a ﬁngerstick sample and can be mailed to a central laboratory. Although the capillary tubes are not currently ap- proved by the FDA, they may prove to be useful when in-person clinical visits are not possible. diabetesjournals.org/care Sacks and Associates e175 Use of Other Glycated Proteins Including Ad- vanced Glycation End Products for Routine Management of Diabetes Further studies are needed to determine whether other glycated proteins such as fructosamine or glycated albumin are clini- cally useful for routine monitoring of glyce- mic status. The limited period of glycemia that they reﬂect limits their clinical utility. Similarly,the limited data that support their relationship with risk of complications makes them less useful than HbA1c.
