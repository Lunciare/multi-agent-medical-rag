Use/Rationale Recommendation: Real-time CGM should be used in conjunction with insulin as a tool to lower HbA1c levels and/or reduce hypoglycemia in teens and adults with type 1 diabetes who are not meeting glycemic targets, have hypoglycemia unawareness, and/or episodes of hypo- glycemia. A (high) Recommendation: Consider using inter- mittently scanned CGM in conjunction with insulin as a tool to lower HbA1c lev- els and/or reduce hypoglycemia in adults with type 1 diabetes who are not meet- ing glycemic targets, have hypoglycemia unawareness and/or episodes of hypo- glycemia. B (moderate) Recommendation: Consider using real- time continuous glucose monitoring to improve HbA1c levels, time in range, and neonatal outcomes in pregnant women with type 1 diabetes.
