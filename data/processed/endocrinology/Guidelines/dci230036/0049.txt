Intensive glyce- mic control is well established as beneﬁcial in reducing the risk for microvascular com- plications. However, except for the poten- tial use of BGM in people with type 2 diabetes using insulin, BGM likely adds cost without beneﬁt (110). Four meta- analyses have reported the effects of BGM on HbA1c in people with type 2 diabetes who were not using insulin (111–114). The decreases of HbA1c in those using BGM were similar to the decreases in compara- bly treated people who did not use BGM. For example, the meta-analysis by Farmer et al. (112) found that the mean pooled reduction in HbA1c was 0.88% in BGM- assigned groups and 0.69% in the usual care groups.
