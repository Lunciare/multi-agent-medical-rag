Citrate-containing blood collection tubes that stabilize glucose concentrations (71) may provide another option for screening in remote areas when venipuncture is available. Monitoring/Prognosis Recommendation: Frequent blood glu- cose monitoring (BGM) is recommended for all people with diabetes who use in- tensive insulin regimens (with multiple daily injections or insulin pump therapy) and who are not using continuous glu- cose monitoring (CGM). A (high) Recommendation: Routine use of BGM is not recommended for people with type 2 diabetes treated with diet and/ or oral agents alone. A (high) Intensive glycemic control can decrease microvascular complications as shown by the DCCT for individuals with type 1 (50) diabetes and by the UKPDS for type 2 (52) diabetes. In the DCCT, partic- ipants with type 1 achieved glycemic con- trol by performing BGM at least four times per day to guide insulin therapy (50). Therapy in participants with type 2 diabetes in the UKPDS (52) was adjusted according to FPG concentrations—BGM was not utilized.
