ANALYTICAL CONSIDERATIONS These issues are covered comprehensively in the Glucose section above. A summary of aspects that particularly pertain to GDM is provided here. Preanalytical The diagnosis of GDM is totally depen- dent on accurate measurement of glu- cose. The diagnostic thresholds for GDM, especially for FPG, are substantially lower than those for diabetes i.e., 92 mg/dL (5.1 mmol/L) or 95 mg/dL (5.3 mmol/L) by IADPSG or Carpenter–Coustan criteria, respectively (Table 7). Furthermore, in view of the relatively short interval be- tween diagnosis of GDM and delivery, conﬁrmatory diagnostic testing is not rou- tinely recommended as it is in nonpreg- nant individuals. Therefore, preparation and timing of testing and analytical accu- racy of glucose measurements are impor- tant for correct classiﬁcation of GDM.
