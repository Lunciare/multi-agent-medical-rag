HEMOGLOBIN A1C Description/Introduction/ Terminology Glycation refers to the nonenzymatic at- tachment of glucose to available amino groups on proteins.The extent of glycation reﬂects the exposure of the protein to mean glycemia integrated over time as a function of the life span and turnover of the protein. Hemoglobin in the RBC has an average circulating life span of approxi- mately 120 days, and glycated hemoglobin therefore usually indicates the average glu- cose concentration over the preceding 60 to 90 days.The terms glycated hemoglobin, glycohemoglobin, glycosylated, and gluco- sylated hemoglobin, HbA1, HbA1c, and A1C have all been used; however, these terms are not interchangeable.
