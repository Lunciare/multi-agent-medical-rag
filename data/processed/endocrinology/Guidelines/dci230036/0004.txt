Diabetes is a common disease. World- wide prevalence in 2021 was estimated to be approximately 537 million and is fore- cast to reach 783 million by 2045 (7). Based on 2017–2020 National Health and Nutri- tion Examination Survey (NHANES) data and 2018–2019 National Health Interview Survey (NHIS) data,the U.S. Centers for Dis- ease Control and Prevention (CDC) esti- mated that there were 37.3 million people (11.3% of the U.S. population) with diabe- tes (8). The number of adults with diabe- tes has also increased in other parts of the world. For example, China and India were thought to have 140.9 million and 74.2 million adults with diabetes in 2021 and are expected to have 174.4 million and 124.9 million, respectively, by 2045 (7).
