Because of the increasing prevalence of type 2 diabetes in children, screening of children is now advocated (2,28). Starting at 10 years of age (or at the on- set of puberty if puberty occurs at a younger age), testing should be per- formed every 3 years in overweight youth (BMI >85th percentile) who have one or more risk factors, namely family history, race/ethnicity recognized to in- crease risk, signs of insulin resistance or conditions associated with insulin resis- tance, and maternal history of diabetes or GDM during the child’s gestation (2).
