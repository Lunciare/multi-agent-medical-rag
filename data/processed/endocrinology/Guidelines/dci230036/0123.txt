ANALYTICAL CONSIDERATIONS Preanalytical Patient Variables—Age and Race. HbA1c results are not signiﬁcantly affected by acute ﬂuctuations in blood glucose con- centrations, such as those that occur with illness or after meals. However, age and race are reported to inﬂuence HbA1c. Pop- ulation data show age-related increases in mean HbA1c in people without diabetes of approximately 0.1% per decade after age 30 years (334,335). Careful phenotyp- ing of subjects with OGTT supports an in- crease in HbA1c with age, even after removing those with otherwise undiag- nosed diabetes and people with impaired glucose tolerance from the study popula- tion (336).
