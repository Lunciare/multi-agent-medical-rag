The use of blood collection tubes con- taining citrate, sodium ﬂuoride, and EDTA offers a practical solution to the problem of glycolysis. A 2009 study showed that acidiﬁcation of blood using citrate buffer inhibits in vitro glycolysis far more effec- tively than ﬂuoride (70).The mean glucose concentration in samples at 37C de- creased by only 0.3% at 2 h and 1.2% at 24 h when blood was drawn into tubes containing citrate buffer (citric acid and e156 sodium citrate), sodium ﬂuoride, and so- dium EDTA.
