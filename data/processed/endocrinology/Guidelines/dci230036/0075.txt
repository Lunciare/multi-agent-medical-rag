CGM devices have not been approved for use in hospitalized patients, in part due to concerns about accuracy, concomitant medication use, or theoretical alterations in the usually high correlation between in- terstitial and blood glucose concentrations caused by serious illness. However, during the coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID-19) pandemic, the FDA allowed use of CGM devices with remote monitoring in hos- pitals in the U.S. to potentially reduce transmission of the virus (194). Although this guidance was only in effect during the declared public health emergency of the pandemic, use of CGM in hospitalized patients (and of closed-loop insulin deliv- ery systems based on CGM) has theoreti- cal beneﬁts and warrants future study.
