The ADA summarized uses of BGM as early as 1987 (see reference 103 and references therein), and by 1993 BGM was being performed at least once a day by 40% and 26% of individuals with type 1 and 2 diabetes, respectively, in the U.S. (104). The ADA currently recommends that most people with type 1 diabetes use intensive insulin regimens, aiming for gly- cemia as close to the nondiabetes range as safely possible (usually HbA1c <7% for many nonpregnant individuals), with mul- tiple daily injections or an insulin pump, e158 and with selection of doses guided by BGM, CGM, or both (105). The beneﬁt of BGM is less clear for people who are not using intensive insu- lin therapy, although the ﬁnancial costs are large. Glucose meters and their asso- ciated supplies are thought to represent a multibillion-dollar expense for diabetes care worldwide.
