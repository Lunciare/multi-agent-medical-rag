A 2014 study compared 17 commercially available urine albumin measurement procedures to an e184 isotope dilution mass spectrometry refer- ence measurement procedure (498). Mean biases were large and ranged from 35% to 34% at 15 mg/L. The authors concluded that calibration bias was the main source of error for differences among methods and precision was adequate for most as- says. Based on the performance of mea- surement procedures, the National KidneyDiseaseEducationProgram(NKDEP) Laboratory Working Group in 2017 recom- mended the following analytical perfor- mance goals for measurement of urine albumin: between-day precision #6%, bias #7% to 13%, and total allowable error of #24% to 30% (499).
