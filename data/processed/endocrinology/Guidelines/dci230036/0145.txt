GENETIC MARKERS Description/Introduction/ Terminology Type 1 diabetes results from a selective autoimmune destruction of the pancre- atic b-cell functional mass, eventually leading to an absolute lack of insulin and consequent hyperglycemia. The mode of inheritance is complex, and around 80% to 85% of newly diagnosed cases occur sporadically without familial aggregation. Among identical twins or HLA-identical siblings of type 1 diabetes, about 20% to 30% eventually manifest the disease. Type 1 diabetes is genetically linked to HLA of the major histocompatibility com- plex (MHC) on chromosome 6.
