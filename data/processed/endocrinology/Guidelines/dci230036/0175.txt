The old nomenclature of “nephrotic- range,” i.e., AER >2,200 mg/day; uACR >2,200 mg/g (>220 mg/mmol); PER >3,500 mg/day, and uPCR >3,500 mg/g (>350 mg/mmol), is no longer used for staging. Note that nephrotic syndrome would typically have hypoalbuminemia (with edema and hyperlipidemia in most cases) along with high urine albumin loss.
