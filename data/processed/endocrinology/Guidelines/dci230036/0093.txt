Individuals should have normal meals without carbohydrate restriction for at least 3 consecutive days before testing. An 8- to 10-h period of fasting must pre- cede an OGTT which must be conducted during the morning because of circadian inﬂuences on circulating glucose (276). Stringent sample handling procedures to minimize glycolysis after phlebotomy are essential. As discussed in the Glucose section above, the best method is to col- lect blood in a tube containing granulated citrate buffer. Sodium ﬂuoride alone is not adequate to prevent glycolysis. Sepa- rating plasma from cells by centrifugation within a few minutes of phlebotomy will attenuate glycolysis. Alternatively, blood drawn into sodium ﬂuoride–containing tubes can be placed in an ice-water slurry until centrifugation (provided cells are separated within 15 to 30 min), as was done in the HAPO study (269). Unfortu- nately, several studies have reported inac- curate GDM detection by failure to handle specimens properly to prevent glycolysis.
