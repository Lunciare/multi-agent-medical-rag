Knowledge of intraindividual (within-person) variability of FPG concentrations is essential for meaningful interpretation of patient val- ues. Careful evaluation over several con- secutive days in normoglycemic individuals revealed that biological variation of FPG (mean glucose of 4.9 mmol/L [88 mg/dL]) exhibited within- and between-subject CVs of 4.8% to 6.1% and 7.5% to 7.8%, re- spectively (90–92).
