An important limi- tation of all these data is that the dipstick methods were compared with local labora- tory methods, which, as indicated above, exhibit large biases (498). A cost-effectiveness analysis of 1,881 individuals with diabetes published in 2020 evaluated medical costs of CKD and concluded that the semiquantitative uACR dipstick method could be an ap- propriate screening tool for albuminuria in people with diabetes. Moreover, the authors point out that it can minimize the testing time and inconvenience and signiﬁcantly reduce national health costs (503).
