Oral antiplatelets and anticoagulants for adults 1.6.25 Offer antiplatelet medicines to adults with CKD for the secondary prevention of cardiovascular disease, but be aware of the increased risk of bleeding. 1.6.26 For guidance on oral anticoagulants for people with CKD, see NICE's guidelines on atrial fibrillation and venous thromboembolic diseases.
