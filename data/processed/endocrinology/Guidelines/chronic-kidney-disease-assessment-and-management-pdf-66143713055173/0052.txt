Take into account all of the following: Chronic kidney disease: assessment and management (NG203) Page 42 of 78 • preferences of the person with anaemia of CKD or, if appropriate, their family or carers • nursing and administration costs • cost of local medicine supply • provision of resuscitation facilities. High-dose and low-frequency iron is a maximum of 2 infusions, with a minimum of 500 mg of iron in each infusion for adults. Low dose and high frequency is more than 2 infusions with 100 mg to 200 mg of iron in each infusion for adults. In August 2021, this was an off-label use of intravenous iron products for some ages of children and young people.
