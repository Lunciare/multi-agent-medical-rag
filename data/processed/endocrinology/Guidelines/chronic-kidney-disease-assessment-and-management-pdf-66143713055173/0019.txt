education for people with CKD 1.4.1 Offer people with CKD (and their family members or carers, as appropriate) education and information tailored to the severity and cause of CKD, the associated complications and the risk of progression. For more guidance, see: • the information on enabling patients to actively participate in their care in NICE's guideline on patient experience in adult NHS services • NICE's guideline on shared decision making • the section on shared decision making in NICE's guideline on babies, children and young people's experience of healthcare. 1.4.2 When developing information or education programmes, involve adults with CKD in their development from the outset.
