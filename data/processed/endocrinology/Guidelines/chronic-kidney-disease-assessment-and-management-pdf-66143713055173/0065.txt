Chronic kidney disease: assessment and management (NG203) Page 52 of 78 Markers of kidney damage These include albuminuria (ACR more than 3 mg/mmol), urine sediment abnormalities, electrolyte and other abnormalities due to tubular disorders, abnormalities detected by histology, structural abnormalities detected by imaging, and a history of kidney transplantation. Pre-dialysis Usually regarded to be CKD stages 4 and 5, although there is no accepted definition. Pre- dialysis includes people with a failing transplant and people having conservative management. Renal replacement therapy (RRT) Life-supporting treatments for severe acute kidney injury or stage 5 CKD. This includes haemodialysis, haemofiltration, haemodiafiltration, peritoneal dialysis and kidney transplantation. Renin–angiotensin–aldosterone system antagonist A medicine that blocks or inhibits the renin–angiotensin–aldosterone system, including angiotensin-converting enzyme (ACE)
