Lifestyle advice 1.4.6 Encourage adults with CKD to take exercise, achieve a healthy weight and stop smoking. Low-protein diets 1.4.9 Do not offer low-protein diets (dietary protein intake less than 0.6 to 0.8 g/kg/ day) to adults with CKD.
