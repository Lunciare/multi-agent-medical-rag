Providing ESAs 1.9.6 Agree a treatment plan between the prescriber and the person with anaemia of CKD that ensures ESA therapy is clinically effective, consistent and safe. The plan should be person-centred and include: • continuity of medicine supply • flexibility of where the medicine is delivered and administered • the person's lifestyle and preferences • cost of medicine supply • desire for self-care if appropriate • regular review of the plan in light of changing needs.
