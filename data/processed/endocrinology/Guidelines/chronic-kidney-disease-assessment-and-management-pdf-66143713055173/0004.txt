Chronic kidney disease: assessment and management (NG203) Page 7 of Reporting and interpreting GFR values 1.1.5 Clinical laboratories should report eGFR either as a whole number if it is 90 ml/ min/1.73 m 2 or less, or as 'greater than 90 ml/min/1.73 m 2'. 1.1.6 If eGFR is greater than 90 ml/min/1.73 m 2, use an increase in serum creatinine concentration of more than 20% to infer significant reduction in kidney function. 1.1.7 Interpret eGFR values of 60 ml/min/1.73 m 2 or more with caution, bearing in mind that estimates of GFR become less accurate as the true GFR increases. 1.1.8 Confirm an eGFR result of less than 60 ml/min/1.73 m 2 in an adult not previously tested by repeating the test within 2 weeks.
