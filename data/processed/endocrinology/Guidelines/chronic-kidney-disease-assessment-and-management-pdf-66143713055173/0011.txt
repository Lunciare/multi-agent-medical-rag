Be aware that: • increased ACR is associated with increased risk of adverse outcomes • decreased GFR is associated with increased risk of adverse outcomes • increased ACR and decreased GFR in combination multiply the risk of adverse outcomes. 1.2.2 Do not determine management of CKD solely by age.
