[2008, amended 2014] Risk factors associated with CKD progression in adults 1.3.9 Work with adults who have any of the following risk factors for CKD progression to optimise their health: • cardiovascular disease • proteinuria • previous episode of acute kidney injury • hypertension Chronic kidney disease: assessment and management (NG203) Page 17 of • diabetes • smoking • African, African-Caribbean or Asian family origin • chronic use of NSAIDs • untreated urinary outflow tract obstruction. 1.3.10 In adults with CKD the chronic use of NSAIDs may be associated with progression and acute use is associated with a reversible decrease in GFR. Exercise caution when giving NSAIDs to people with CKD over prolonged periods of time.
