When highly accurate measures of GFR are needed 1.1.9 If a highly accurate measure of GFR is needed, for example, during monitoring of chemotherapy and in the evaluation of kidney function in potential living donors, consider a reference standard measure (inulin, 51Cr-EDTA, 125I-iothalamate or iohexol). Investigations for proteinuria 1.1.10 Do not use reagent strips to identify proteinuria in children and young people. 1.1.11 Do not use reagent strips to identify proteinuria in adults unless they are capable of specifically measuring albumin at low concentrations and expressing the result as an albumin:creatinine ratio (ACR). 1.1.12 For the initial detection of proteinuria in adults, children and young people: • use urine ACR rather than protein:creatinine ratio (PCR) because of the greater sensitivity for low levels of proteinuria Chronic kidney disease: assessment and management (NG203) Page 8 of • check an ACR between 3 mg/mmol and 70 mg/mmol in a subsequent early morning sample to confirm the result.
