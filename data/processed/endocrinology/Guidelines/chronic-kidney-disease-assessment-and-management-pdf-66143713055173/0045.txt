[2006, amended 2011] Correcting iron deficiency 1.9.17 Offer iron therapy to adults, children and young people with anaemia of CKD who are receiving ESAs to achieve: • percentage of hypochromic red blood cells less than 6% (unless ferritin is greater than 800 micrograms/litre) • reticulocyte Hb count or equivalent tests above 29 pg (unless serum ferritin is greater than 800 micrograms/litre). If these tests are not available or the person has thalassaemia or thalassaemia trait, iron therapy should maintain transferrin saturation greater than 20% and serum ferritin level greater than 100 micrograms/litre (unless serum ferritin is greater than 800 micrograms/litre).
