Clinical utility of ESA therapy in people with sufficient iron 1.8.3 Discuss the pros and cons of a trial of anaemia management with the person with anaemia of CKD, and their families and carers if agreed. 1.8.4 ESAs need not be administered if the presence of comorbidities, or the prognosis, is likely to negate the benefits of correcting the anaemia. 1.8.5 Start a trial of anaemia correction when there is uncertainty over whether the presence of comorbidities, or the prognosis, would negate benefit from correcting the anaemia with ESAs. 1.8.6 If a trial of ESA therapy is carried out, assess the effectiveness of the trial after an agreed interval. Agree with the person with anaemia of CKD (and their families and carers, if appropriate) whether or not to continue ESA therapy. 1.8.7 Review treatment in all people started on ESA therapy after an agreed interval to decide whether or not to continue using ESAs. Hypoxia-inducible factor prolyl-hydroxylase inhibitors 1.8.8 Roxadustat is recommended as an option in NICE technology appraisal guidance for treating symptomatic anaemia in adults with stage 3 to 5 CKD and no iron deficiency who are not on dialysis.
