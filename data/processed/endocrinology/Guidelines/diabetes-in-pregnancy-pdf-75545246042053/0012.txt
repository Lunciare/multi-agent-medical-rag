This can help to reduce the risk of adverse outcomes, such as a baby that is large for gestational age, trauma during birth, neonatal hypoglycaemia and perinatal death. The likelihood of caesarean section and neonatal intensive care unit admissions should also be lower. Women should be offered real-time CGM (rtCGM) but, if they are unable to use rtCGM or would prefer to use intermittently scanned CGM (isCGM, commonly referred to as 'flash'), this should be offered instead.
