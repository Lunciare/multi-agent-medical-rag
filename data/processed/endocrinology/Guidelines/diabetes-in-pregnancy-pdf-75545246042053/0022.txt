Page 18 of Commissioners (integrated care systems) ensure that they commission services in which women diagnosed with gestational diabetes have postnatal testing to exclude the possibility of pre-existing type 1 or type 2 diabetes. They also ensure the services refer women diagnosed with gestational diabetes whose blood glucose levels returned to normal after the birth to the National Diabetes Prevention Programme. Women diagnosed with gestational diabetes are offered a test of their blood glucose to check whether they have persisting hyperglycaemia (high glucose levels) after they have 13 weeks after their baby is born to check again whether they have type 1 or type 2 offered testing afterwards.
