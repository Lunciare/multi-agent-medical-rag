The National Pregnancy in Diabetes Audit collects data on congenital anomalies and serious adverse outcomes and their association with HbA1c levels in early pregnancy. It also collects data on the relationship between HbA1c levels in late pregnancy and the percentages of babies admitted to neonatal care, preterm live births before 37 weeks, perinatal deaths and babies who are large for gestational age. b) Time in range during pregnancy for women with diabetes.
