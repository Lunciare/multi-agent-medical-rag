Duration of Treatment and Follow-Up Duration of Treatment Tyrosine kinase inhibitors are cytostatic, and not cy- totoxic drugs; thus, they exert a break to the cell growth that can restart immediately when the break is removed . This is the rationale why an MKI therapy should be continued until the evidence of no further clinical benefit. The concept of clinical benefit is not well de- fined, but we can recapitulate it according to a series of clinical factors such as: (a) the evidence of partial remis- sion or stabilization of the tumour burden, (b) the im- provement of symptoms, (c) the slow progression of the disease or the progression of only one lesion while the others are stable.
