How to Advise the Patient and the Family Decisions regarding the management of advanced RAI-R TCs are complicated and challenging for the pa- tient and the medical team. As in all situations in medi- cine, a careful decision making process involving medi- cal, emotional, social, and spiritual factors is essential to find the optimal approach for a patient. In this process, the following elements can be identified: inventory, de- velopment of management plans, information, decision making. These elements are addressed separately, but will in most cases take place in parallel.
