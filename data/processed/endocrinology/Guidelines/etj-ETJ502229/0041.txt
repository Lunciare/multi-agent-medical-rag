Two to 3 months after the initiation of the MKI ther- apy, an appropriate imaging (usually by CT and/or MRI) should be performed to verify the response to the drug. An 18-FDG PET/CT could also be useful to verify the re- duction of the metabolic activity of the metastatic lesions, though whether this has a prognostic or management im- pact still needs to be demonstrated. During these visits, a measurement of serum Tg concentrations and Tg anti- bodies should be done and evaluated together with the results of the imaging. After the first 2 months, the follow-up visits may take place at monthly intervals until the 6th month and then every 3 months until the completion of the 1st year of MKI treatment. Afterwards, if patients are stable, follow- up intervals can be extended to 4 months with periodical imaging.
