Some pa- tients will have been living with their disease in an indo- lent phase for many years, and the psychological impact of accepting the change in the nature of their disease to an aggressive, symptomatic phase can be difficult to deal with. Specialists in palliative care medicine have expertise in managing multiple complex symptoms, psychosocial support, and assistance with decision making . The involvement of the palliative care team in a patient’s man- agement at an earlier time than the end of life phase is extremely beneficial in terms of both quality and quan- tity of life for the patient and their carers. A relation- ship can be developed between clinician and patient with an understanding of patients’ wishes and concerns. This should lead to a more controlled and hopefully more comfortable life with advanced disease. The typical long natural history of the disease means that many patients will have had multiple treatments over many years, and hence the management of ad- vanced TC requires a case-by-case approach with an in- dividualized management plan.
