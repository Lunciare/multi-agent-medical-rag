Cementoplasty of Bone Metastases It is a palliative treatment for osteolytic bone metasta- ses, to be performed alone or in combination with other local treatments, such as radiofrequency ablation or EBRT. Its aim is to obtain the reinforcement of bone de- fect through percutaneous injection of polymethylmeth- acrylate cement. This minimally invasive technique can be performed in any metastatic site and results in signifi- cant pain relief, offering stability of the affected bones . Fig. 2.
