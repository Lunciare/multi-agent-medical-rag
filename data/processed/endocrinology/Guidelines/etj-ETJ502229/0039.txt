Follow-Up The follow-up of patients treated with MKI is crucial to the success of this therapy. In particular, it is funda- mental that the patients have a facilitated access to reach either specialist doctors or nurses who must have been trained to follow them with their specific clinical and psy- chological problems. Patients treated with MKIs must be strictly followed especially during the first 6 months of therapy. This need is related to the multiple side effects that the patients can experience (see also the section Man- agement of Side Effects). Blood pressure should be well controlled before starting the MKI, especially when start- ing lenvatinib : if pathologically altered or even if bor- derline, a specific therapy must be started and then tight- ly controlled.
