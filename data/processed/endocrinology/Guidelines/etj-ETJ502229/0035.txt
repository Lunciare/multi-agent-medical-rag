Development of Management Plans Based on the above-mentioned information, integral management plans can be designed, not only focussing on medical management (including symptomatic and palliative treatment), but also addressing the social, emo- tional, and spiritual needs of the patient. The problem with developing medical plans is that there is limited evi- dence based on clinical studies that can be translated di- rectly to the situation of the individual patient. As a con- sequence, developing these plans is based mostly on ex- pert opinion and the experience of a MDT in which all healthcare specialists are represented, not restricted to medical specialists, but also general practitioners, social, psychological, and spiritual caregivers.
