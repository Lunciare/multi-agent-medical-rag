24 of Quality statement 6: Access to mental health professionals with an understanding of type 1 or type 2 diabetes Quality statement Children and young people with type 1 or type 2 diabetes are offered access to mental health professionals with an understanding of diabetes. Rationale Psychological issues (such as anxiety, depression, behavioural problems, eating disorders, conduct disorders and family conflict) and psychosocial issues have a significant and adverse impact on the management of type 1 and type 2 diabetes, and on the general wellbeing of children and young people and their family members or carers. Children and young people with diabetes are at high risk of anxiety and depression, and it is important that they have early access to mental health professionals when they need it. Mental health professionals who have an understanding of diabetes and the particular problems it causes are essential for delivering psychological interventions and engaging with children, young people and their families.
