Quality measures The following measures can be used to assess the quality of care or service provision specified in the statement. They are examples of how the statement can be measured, and can be adapted and used flexibly. Structure Evidence of local arrangements and written clinical protocols to ensure that children and young people presenting in primary care with suspected diabetes are referred to and seen by a multidisciplinary paediatric diabetes team on the same day. Data source: No routinely collected national data for this measure has been identified. Data can be collected from information recorded locally by provider organisations, for example from local referral pathways and service agreements.
