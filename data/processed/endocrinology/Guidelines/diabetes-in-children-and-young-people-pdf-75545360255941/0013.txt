Page 12 of Quality statement 3: Intensive insulin therapy and level 3 carbohydrate- counting education for type 1 diabetes Quality statement Children and young people with type 1 diabetes are offered intensive insulin therapy and level 3 carbohydrate-counting education at diagnosis. Rationale The aim of intensive insulin therapy is to reach near normal blood glucose levels, to reduce the risk of long-term complications and improve quality of life. Dietary management can also improve control of blood glucose and HbA1c levels. When using intensive insulin therapy, it is important to match the insulin dose to carbohydrate intake, in line with individualised insulin-to-carbohydrate ratios (level 3 carbohydrate-counting).
