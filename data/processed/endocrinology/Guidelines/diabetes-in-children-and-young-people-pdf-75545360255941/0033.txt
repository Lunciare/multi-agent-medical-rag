What the quality statement means for different audiences Service providers (secondary care providers) ensure that systems are in place to offer children and young people with type 1 or type 2 diabetes access to mental health professionals with an understanding of diabetes. Page 27 of Healthcare professionals (such as consultants) offer children and young people with type 1 or type 2 diabetes access to mental health professionals who have an understanding of diabetes and the particular problems it causes and can deliver psychological interventions and engage with children, young people and their families. Commissioners commission services that offer children and young people with type 1 or type 2 diabetes access to mental health professionals with an understanding of diabetes. Children and young people with type 1 or type 2 diabetes are able to see mental health professionals who understand the types of problems people with diabetes can have. The mental health professional should be one of the main members of the diabetes team. Source guidance Diabetes (type 1 and type 2) in children and young people: diagnosis and management.
