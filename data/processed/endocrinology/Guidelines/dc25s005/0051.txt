Behavior modiﬁcation goals should ad- dress physical activity, calorie restriction, healthy weight management strategies, and motivation. There is strong and con- sistent evidence that modest, sustained weight loss can delay the progression from prediabetes to type 2 diabetes (82,186,187) (see Section 3, “Prevention or Delay of Diabetes and Associated Comorbidities”) and is beneﬁcial for type 2 diabetes management (see Section 8, “Obesity and Weight Management for the Prevention and Treatment of Type 2 Diabetes”). In prediabetes, the weight loss goal is at least 3–7% from baseline body weight, and higher for reducing risk of progres- sion to type 2 diabetes. In conjunction with support for healthy lifestyle behav- iors, medication-assisted weight loss can be considered for people at risk for type 2 diabetes when needed to achieve and sustain 7–10% weight loss (188,189) (see Section 8, “Obesity and Weight Management for the Prevention and Treatment of Type 2 Diabetes”).
