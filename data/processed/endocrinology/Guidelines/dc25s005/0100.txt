Diabetes education programs offer po- tential to systematically reach and engage individuals with diabetes in smoking ces- sation efforts. A cluster randomized trial found statistically signiﬁcant increases in quit rates and long-term abstinence rates (>6 months) when smoking cessation in- terventions were offered through dia- betes education clinics, regardless of motivation to quit at baseline (340). The increased prevalence in availability and use of tobacco and cannabis prod- ucts and the effect on the health of peo- ple with diabetes highlights the need to ask about use of these products, educate individuals regarding the associated risks, and provide support for cessation.
