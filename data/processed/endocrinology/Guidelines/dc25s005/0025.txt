The diabetes plate method (96) is a com- monly used visual approach for providing basic meal planning guidance for individ- uals with type 1 and type 2 diabetes. This simple graphic (featuring a 9-in plate) shows how to portion foods (one-half of the plate for nonstarchy vegetables, one-quarter of the plate for protein, and one-quarter of the plate for carbohydrates). Carbohydrate counting is a more advanced skill that helps plan for and track how much carbohydrate is consumed at meals and snacks.
