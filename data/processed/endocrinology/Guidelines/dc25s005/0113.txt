Diabetes distress negatively affects medication-taking behav- iors and is linked to higher A1C, lower self- efﬁcacy, and less optimal eating and exer- cise behaviors (5,403,407). Diabetes dis- tress is also associated with symptoms of related quality of life (408). The experience of stigma related to living with diabetes may contribute to increased diabetes distress (409,410). Diabetes distress should be routinely monitored (411) using diabetes-speciﬁc validated measures (1), such as those available through the ADA’s website (professional.diabetes.org/sites/default/ ﬁles/media/ada_mental_health_toolkit_ questionnaires.pdf).
