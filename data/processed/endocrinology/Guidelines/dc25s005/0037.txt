Adjusting insulin doses to account for high-fat and/or high-protein meals requires determination of anticipated nutrient intake to calculate the mealtime dose. Food literacy, numer- acy, interest, and capability should be eval- uated. For individuals on a ﬁxed daily insulin schedule, meal planning should em- phasize a relatively ﬁxed carbohydrate con- sumption pattern with respect to both time and amount while considering insulin action. Attention to hunger and satiety cues will also help with nutrient modiﬁca- tions throughout the day (50). Most commercially available automated in- sulin delivery systems still require basic diabetes management skills, including carbohydrate counting and understand- ing of the effect of protein and fat on postprandial glucose response (126).
