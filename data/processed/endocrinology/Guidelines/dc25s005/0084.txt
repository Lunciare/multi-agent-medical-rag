Individuals of childbearing potential with preexisting diabetes, particularly type 2 diabetes, and those at risk for or presenting with gestational diabetes mel- litus should be advised to engage in reg- ular moderate-intensity physical activity prior to and during their pregnancies, as tolerated (267).
