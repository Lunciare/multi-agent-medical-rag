Supporting behaviour change This recommendation should be read in conjunction with NICE's guideline on behaviour change: general approaches. Changing people's health-related behaviour involves: 11 of • Helping them to understand the short, medium and longer-term consequences of health-related behaviour. • Helping them to feel positive about the benefits and value of health-enhancing behaviours and changing their behaviours. • Recognising how people's social contexts and relationships may affect their behaviour. • Helping people plan changes in terms of easy sustainable steps over time. • Identifying and planning for situations that might undermine the changes people are trying to make, and planning explicit 'if–then' coping strategies to maintain changes in behaviour.
