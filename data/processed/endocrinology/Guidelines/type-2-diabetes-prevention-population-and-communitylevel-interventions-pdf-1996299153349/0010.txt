Effective weight-loss programmes Effective weight-loss programmes should: • address the reasons why someone might find it difficult to lose weight • be tailored to individual needs and choices • be sensitive to the person's weight concerns • be based on a balanced, healthy diet • encourage regular physical activity • expect people to lose no more than 0.5 to 1 kg (1 to 2 lb) a week • identify and address barriers to change. Also see NICE's guideline on overweight and obesity management.
