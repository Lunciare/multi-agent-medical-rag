Provide details of the local support services available. • Address any misconceptions in the local community about the risk of diabetes and other non-communicable diseases that could act as a barrier to change. This includes the belief that illness is inevitable (fatalism) and any misconceptions about what constitutes a healthy weight. Also address any stigma surrounding these conditions. • Ensure messages and information are disseminated locally to groups at higher risk of type 2 diabetes than the general population, including black and minority ethnic and lower socioeconomic groups. Use local newspapers, online social media and local radio channels targeted at these groups.
