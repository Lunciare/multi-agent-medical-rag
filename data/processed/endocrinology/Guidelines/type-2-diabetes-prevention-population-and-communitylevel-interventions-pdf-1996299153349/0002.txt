The second piece of guidance will consider the effectiveness and cost effectiveness of interventions to prevent type 2 diabetes among individuals at high-risk. Factors which influence someone's risk of type 2 diabetes include: weight, waist circumference, age, physical activity and whether or not they have a previous history of gestational diabetes or a family history of type 2 diabetes. In addition to these individual risk factors, people from certain communities and population groups are particularly at risk.
