DIAGNOSIS OF CHRONIC KIDNEY DISEASE IN PEOPLE WITH DIABETES CKD in people with diabetes is usually a clinical diagnosis made based on the pres- ence of albuminuria and/or reduced eGFR in the absence of signs or symptoms of other primary causes of kidney damage.
