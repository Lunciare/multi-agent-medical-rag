The most recently published clinical trial was EMPA-KIDNEY (Study of Heart and Kidney Protection with Empagliﬂozin) (115). This study enrolled participants with kidney disease with an eGFR of at least 20 but less than 45 mL/min/1.73 m2 or who had an eGFR of at least 45 but less than 90 mL/min/1.73 m2 with a UACR of at least 200 mg/g creatinine. Ap- proximately one-half of the 6,609 partici- pants had diabetes. The empagliﬂozin- treated participants had lower risk of pro- gression of kidney disease and lower risk of death from cardiovascular causes (HR 0.72 [95% CI 0.64–0.82]; P < 0.001).
