Endoscopic sleeve gastroplasty for obesity (HTG711) Page 6 of 9 3 Committee considerations The evidence 3.1 NICE did a rapid review of the published literature on the efficacy and safety of this procedure. This comprised a comprehensive literature search and detailed review of the evidence from 8 sources, which was discussed by the committee. The evidence included 1 randomised controlled trial, 4 systematic reviews and meta-analyses, 2 cohort studies and 1 propensity score matched study. It is presented in the summary of key evidence section in the overview.
