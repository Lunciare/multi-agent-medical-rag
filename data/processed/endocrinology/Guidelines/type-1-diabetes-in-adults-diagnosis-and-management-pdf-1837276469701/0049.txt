Acute painful neuropathy from rapid improvement of blood glucose control 1.14.29 Reassure adults with type 1 diabetes that acute painful neuropathy resulting from rapid improvement of blood glucose control is a self-limiting condition and symptoms improve over time. 1.14.30 Explain to adults with type 1 diabetes that the specific treatments for acute painful neuropathy resulting from rapid improvement of blood glucose control: • aim to make symptoms tolerable until the condition resolves • may not relieve pain immediately and may need to be taken regularly for several weeks to be effective. 1.14.31 Use simple analgesics (paracetamol, aspirin) and local measures (bed cradles) as measures.
