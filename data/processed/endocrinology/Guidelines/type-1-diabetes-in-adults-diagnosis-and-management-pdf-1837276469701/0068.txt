Although most of the research was focused on type 2 diabetes, the committee thought that the evidence on the bidirectional link between increased HbA1c and periodontitis was also applicable to people with type 1 diabetes. In the committee's experience, people with diabetes are often unaware of the link between diabetes and periodontitis and may not be having regular oral health reviews. To address this, the committee recommended routinely discussing the risk of periodontitis at annual reviews, alongside eye disease and foot problems. The evidence also showed that periodontal treatment is cost effective for people with type 1 diabetes, assuming improvements in HbA1c are maintained. This was tested with health economic modelling in a range of different scenarios.
