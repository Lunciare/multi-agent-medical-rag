Self-monitoring of capillary blood glucose Frequency of self-monitoring of blood glucose 1.6.19 Advise adults with type 1 diabetes who are using capillary blood glucose monitoring to routinely self-monitor their blood glucose levels, and to measure at least 4 times a day (including before each meal and before bed).
