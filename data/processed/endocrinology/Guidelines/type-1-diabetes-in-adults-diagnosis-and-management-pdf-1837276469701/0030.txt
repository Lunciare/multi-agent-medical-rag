Adjuncts 1.7.26 Consider adding metformin to insulin therapy for adults with type 1 diabetes if: • they have a BMI of 25 kg/m 2 or above (23 kg/m 2 or above for people from South Asian and related family backgrounds) and • they want to improve their blood glucose control while minimising their effective insulin dose. In August 2015, this was an off-label use of metformin.
