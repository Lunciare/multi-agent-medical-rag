People with stage 3 or stage 4 CKD with albuminuria usually receive ACE inhibitors or ARBs, at the maximum tolerated licensed dose, as first-line therapy. Second-line SGLT2 inhibitors can be added, in line with NG28 and TA775. The company explained that it did not expect finerenone to replace existing therapies, because it has a different mode of action. Rather, it expands on current treatment options. The company also did not view SGLT2 inhibitors as established treatments in the NHS. So, the company submission focused on finerenone as a second-line treatment which would be added to first-line ACE inhibitors and ARBs. The clinical experts stated that although SGLT2 inhibitors were only recently recommended by NICE, their use is expected to increase, although this could take time.
