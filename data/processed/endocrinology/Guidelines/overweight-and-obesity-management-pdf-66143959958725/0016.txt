Give ongoing support (including appropriate written materials) in person or by phone, letter, email or online and social media resources. 1.4.2 Offer individually tailored, multicomponent interventions and ongoing support to improve diet (and reduce energy intake). For example, interventions that include dietary modification, targeted advice, family involvement and goal setting. 1.4.3 Ensure that interventions to prevent obesity, including promotional, awareness- raising activities, are part of a long-term, multicomponent intervention rather than one-off activities, and are accompanied by targeted follow up with different population groups. 1.4.4 At times when weight gain is more likely, such as after pregnancy, around menopause and when stopping smoking, ask permission to discuss weight, dietary intake and activity.
