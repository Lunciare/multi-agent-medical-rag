These classifications can be used for children and young people aged 5 years and over of both sexes and all ethnicities. The health risks associated with higher central adiposity levels include type 2 diabetes, hypertension and cardiovascular disease. [2022, amended 2026] 1.10.11 When talking to a child or young person aged 5 years or over, and their families and carers, explain that they should try and keep their waist to less than half their height (so a waist-to-height-ratio of under 0.5).
