Assessing and managing comorbidities in children and young people 1.10.12 After the initial assessment of overweight or obesity, identify any comorbidities and other factors that may affect or be affected by the person's weight. Take into account the timing of the assessment, the degree of overweight or obesity, and the results of previous assessments. 1.10.13 Consider assessing comorbidities for children with a BMI at or above the 98th centile. 1.10.14 Start managing comorbidities as soon as they are identified; do not wait until the child or young person has lost weight.
