How the recommendations might affect practice Providing weight maintenance support after a weight management intervention for children and young people may initially need additional resources, but it is expected to reduce relapses and downstream costs related to obesity management, which will increase the overall efficiency of the NHS. There might be some costs associated with the tailored multicomponent approach to Page 160 of embedding overweight and obesity management interventions for children and young people. A focus on addressing the drivers of overweight and obesity is likely to increase the effectiveness and cost effectiveness of the interventions.
