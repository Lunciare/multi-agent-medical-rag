When to refer adults for assessment for bariatric surgery 1.18.1 Offer adults a referral for a comprehensive assessment by specialist overweight and obesity management services providing multidisciplinary management of obesity, to see whether bariatric surgery is suitable for them if they: • have a BMI of 40 kg/m 2 or more, or between 35 kg/m 2 and 39.9 kg/m 2 with a significant health condition that could be improved if they lost weight (see box 2 for examples) and • agree to the necessary long-term follow up after surgery (for example, lifelong annual reviews).
