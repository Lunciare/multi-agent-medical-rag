Ongoing support from providers of overweight and obesity management interventions These recommendations are for providers of overweight and obesity management interventions. 1.14.32 Offer a range of options for follow-up sessions after the intervention active phase has been completed. This should include at different times and in easily accessible and suitable venues. 1.14.33 Discuss with the child or young person, their family and carers any local services and activities that can provide further long-term support to help them manage their weight, for example, local leisure services and walking, cycling or youth groups.
