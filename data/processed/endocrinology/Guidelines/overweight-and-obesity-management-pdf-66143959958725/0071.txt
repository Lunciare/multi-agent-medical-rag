Care for the wider family 1.14.21 Offer assessment of overweight, obesity or central adiposity to families and carers. 1.14.22 Emphasise that the behavioural overweight and obesity management intervention may benefit the whole family. 1.14.23 Offer information about local and national behavioural overweight and obesity management interventions to family members and carers who are living with overweight or obesity.
