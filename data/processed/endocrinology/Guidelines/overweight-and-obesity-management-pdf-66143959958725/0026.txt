Headteachers and chairs of governors of schools 1.6.9 In collaboration with parents and pupils, assess the whole school environment and ensure that the ethos of all school policies helps children and young people to maintain a healthier weight, eat a healthy diet and be physically active, in line with existing standards and guidance. This includes policies relating to: • building layout and recreational spaces • catering (including vending machines) and the food and drink pupils bring into school for themselves or others • rewards
