This could be done by lowering specific macronutrient content (for example, low-fat or low- carbohydrate diets) or using other methods to limit overall energy intake. 1.16.4 Ensure that dietary approaches for children and young people keep their total energy intake at or below the recommended daily calorie intake for their age and sex, depending on their level of overweight or obesity and any weight-related comorbidities. Focus on healthy eating rather than on overly restrictive diets. See Page 77 of the Scientific Advisory Committee on Nutrition guidance on feeding young children aged 1 to 5 years.
