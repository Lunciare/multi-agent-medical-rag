Ongoing support from healthcare and other professionals 1.14.34 Use information from the intervention provider (such as change in weight, BMI centile or waist-to-height ratio, reported improvements to fitness and mental wellbeing) and continue to measure when appropriate to help monitor progress and give ongoing tailored support. 1.14.35 Offer support and follow-up sessions, depending on the needs of the child or young person and their family and carers. 1.14.36 Give children and young people, and their family and carers, information about any other local sources of long-term support as part of a multidisciplinary team approach.
