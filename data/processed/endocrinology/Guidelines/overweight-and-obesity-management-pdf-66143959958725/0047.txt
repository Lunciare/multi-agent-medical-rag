Choosing interventions with adults 1.11.5 Ensure that healthcare professionals involved in identifying overweight, obesity and central adiposity are familiar with the local overweight and obesity management pathway, including: • local and national behavioural overweight and obesity management interventions and what these may involve • referral criteria and process for funded referrals • the capacity of services and are aware of services that are available locally and nationally. 1.11.6 Discuss and agree the type and level of intervention with adults who: • are living with overweight or obesity or • have increased health risk based on their waist-to-height ratio.
