Work with all referrers and providers to develop systems to share relevant information about people referred to overweight and obesity management interventions, in line with information governance and data protection requirements. 1.20 Planning and commissioning interventions for children and young people See also the section on raising awareness of overweight and obesity management options. 1.20.1 When commissioning interventions, take into account the needs of children and young people who are living with obesity or overweight and have special needs or disabilities. This could include offering specific interventions, if available, or making reasonable adaptations to mainstream interventions (including training staff), and evaluating both. 1.20.2 Ensure those with more complex needs, their families and carers, have a contact in specialist services who can help them manage their weight.
