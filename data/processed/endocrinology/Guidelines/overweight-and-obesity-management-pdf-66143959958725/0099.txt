Preoperative assessment and discussions 1.18.11 Carry out a comprehensive preoperative assessment of any psychological or clinical factors that may affect adherence to postoperative care requirements (such as changes to dietary intake, eating behaviours and taking nutritional supplements) before performing surgery.
