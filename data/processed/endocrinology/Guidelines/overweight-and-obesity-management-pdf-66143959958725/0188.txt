How the recommendation might affect practice There are a few training programmes specifically for managing overweight and obesity in children and young people, such as the training by the World Obesity Federation, European Health Education England. Some of these need to be updated to include measuring waist circumference and interpreting waist-to-height ratio, which might lead to extra training costs. Healthcare professionals may need extra time to teach older children and young people, and their families and carers, how to measure the waist accurately and calculate waist-to-height ratio. However, the committee agreed that extra costs of training and staff time are unlikely to result in a significant resource impact and are justified by the long-term health benefits associated with a reduction in obesity-related conditions.
