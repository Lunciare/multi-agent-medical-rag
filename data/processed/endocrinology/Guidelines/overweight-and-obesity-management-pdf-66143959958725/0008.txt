[2006, amended 2014] 1.2.2 Ensure that interventions for children and young people address behaviours within the family and in social settings that affect weight, including the wider determinants and context of overweight and obesity (see recommendation 1.1.1 in the section on discussion, communication and follow up). [2006, amended 2014] 1.2.3 Encourage families or carers to take the main responsibility for behavioural changes in children and young people, especially children under 12.
