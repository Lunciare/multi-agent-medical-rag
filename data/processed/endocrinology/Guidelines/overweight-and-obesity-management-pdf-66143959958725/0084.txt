Low-energy and very-low-energy diets for adults 1.16.8 Consider low-energy diets (800 to 1,200 kilocalories per day, also known as low- calorie diets) only as part of a multicomponent overweight and obesity management strategy with long-term support within a specialist overweight and obesity management service (or other services for the management of long-term conditions such as type 2 diabetes) for people: • living with obesity (with or without diabetes) or • living with overweight and with type 2 diabetes.
