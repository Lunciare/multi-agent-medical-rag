(orlistat SPC) Semaglutide and liraglutide are recommended for use within specialist weight management services, which are usually accessed for up to 2 years. For tirzepatide, semaglutide and liraglutide, use lower BMI thresholds (usually reduced by 2.5 kg/m 2) for people from South Asian, Chinese, other Asian, Middle Eastern, Black African or African-Caribbean ethnic backgrounds. Non-diabetic hyperglycaemia is defined as a haemoglobin A1c level of 42 mmol/mol to 47 mmol/mol (6.0% to 6.4%) or a fasting plasma glucose level of 5.5 mmol/litre to 6.9 mmol/litre.
