Developing a tailored plan to meet individual needs These recommendations are for providers of behavioural overweight and obesity management interventions. 1.14.7 Assess and record the child or young person's degree of overweight, obesity or wellbeing (including whether their weight is a consequence of circumstances that have affected their mental wellbeing), in particular for those who: • have self-referred to the intervention • have not been assessed by a healthcare professional.
