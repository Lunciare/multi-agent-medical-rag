Staff qualifications 1.15.1 Ensure staff leading supervised physical activity sessions are qualified and insured, for example, a physiotherapist or a practitioner member of the CIMSPA (Chartered Institute for the Management of Sport and Physical Activity). Ensure that people running children's sessions have a paediatric CPR qualification.
