Raising professional and public awareness of what is available and maintaining an up-to-date list of local interventions will enable efficient referral and self- referral. Based on their experience the committee discussed that healthcare professionals want to be able to share online and social media resources with adults. They agreed that many people prefer to access information about overweight, obesity and possible interventions online, so it is important for healthcare professionals to have reliable sources at hand. How the recommendations might affect practice The recommendations reflect general principles of care and are largely in line with current practice. Raising professional and public awareness could have a cost, but the benefits of better awareness are expected to offset any investment.
