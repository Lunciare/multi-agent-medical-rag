The assessment group explained that because this analysis used a partitioned survival model, after disease progression patients could only transition to the death state. This meant that treatment after disease progression continued until death. The committee noted that this resulted in an unrealistic overestimation of costs. The clinical experts stated that if imaging showed disease progression, clinicians would normally stop treatment. They explained that treatment may continue if imaging showed only 1 lesion growing and others to be stable, but emphasised that this was uncommon and treatment would only continue for another 1 or 2 months.
