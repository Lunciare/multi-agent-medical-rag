Vandetanib does not meet the short life expectancy criterion so the end-of-life criteria do not apply 3.20 For the short life expectancy criterion, the company's new analysis predicted a mean survival with best supportive care of less than 24 months in the restricted group. However, the committee had concluded that this analysis was not sufficiently robust for decision making (see section 3.6 and section 3.7), and that data on cabozantinib from EXAM were a more reliable source of survival estimates in the population that reflected clinical practice (see section 3.8). The committee was aware that the modelled mean and median overall survival estimates were 47 and 21 months respectively and that some patients with unresectable, locally advanced or metastatic medullary thyroid cancer live for a long time (see NICE's technology appraisal guidance on cabozantinib). The committee had not seen any new evidence that was robust enough to change its conclusion that vandetanib did not meet the short life expectancy criterion.
