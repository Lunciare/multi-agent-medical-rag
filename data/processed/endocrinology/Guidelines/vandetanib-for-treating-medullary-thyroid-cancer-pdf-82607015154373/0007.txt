For the primary outcome of centrally reviewed median progression-free survival, the results showed a statistically significant benefit for vandetanib compared with placebo, which was 28.0 months for vandetanib and 16.4 months for placebo (hazard ratio [HR] 0.47; 95% confidence interval [CI] 0.29 to 0.77), with a median trial follow-up of 24 months. The investigator-assessed median progression-free survival was 22.1 months for vandetanib and 8.3 months for placebo (HR 0.33; 95% CI 0.20 to 0.53). The committee considered that the substantial difference between the centrally reviewed and investigator-assessed results in the placebo arm (median of 16.4 months compared with 8.3 months) introduced further uncertainty into the evidence.
