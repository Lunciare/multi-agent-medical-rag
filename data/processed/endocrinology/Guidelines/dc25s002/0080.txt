An advantage of the A1C test is its convenience, as it can be added to the prenatal laboratories and does not re- quire an early-morning fasting appoint- ment. Disadvantages include inaccuracies in the presence of increased red blood cell turnover and hemoglobinopathies (usually reads lower) and higher values with anemia and reduced red blood cell turnover (181).
