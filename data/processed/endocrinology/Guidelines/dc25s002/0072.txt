Genetic screening (i.e., next-generation sequencing) is increasingly available and cost-effective (152). Consultation with a center specializing in diabetes genetics is recommended to understand the signiﬁ- cance of genetic mutations and how best to approach further evaluation, treatment, and genetic counseling. Genetic counsel- ing is recommended to ensure that af- fected individuals understand the patterns of inheritance and the importance of a correct diagnosis and to address compre- hensive cardiovascular risk. A diagnosis of one of the three most common forms of MODY, HNF1A-MODY, GCK-MODY, and HNF4A-MODY, allows for more cost-effective personalized therapy (i.e., no therapy for GCK-MODY and sulfo- nylureas as ﬁrst-line therapy for HNF1A- MODY and HNF4A-MODY).
