Correct diagnosis has critical im- plications, because 30–50% of people with KATP-related neonatal diabetes will exhibit improved blood glucose levels when treated with high-dose oral sulfonylureas instead of insulin. Insulin gene (INS) mu- tations are the second most common cause of permanent neonatal diabetes, with insulin therapy being the preferred treatment strategy.
