As with the glucose measures, several prospective studies that used A1C to pre- dict the progression to diabetes demon- strated a strong, continuous curvilinear association between A1C and subsequent diabetes. In a systematic review of 44,203 individuals from 16 cohort studies with a follow-up interval averaging 5.6 years (range 2.8–12 years), those with A1C be- tween 5.5% and 6.0% (between 37 and 42 mmol/mol) had a substantially in- creased risk of diabetes (5-year incidence from 9% to 25%).
