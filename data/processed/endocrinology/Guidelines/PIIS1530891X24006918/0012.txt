The task force focused on pharmacologic treatments for adults 18 years of age with elevated LDL-C and/or triglyceride (TG) that were approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA). Dyslipidemia was deﬁned as LDL-C levels >130 mg/dL (3.4 mmol/L) in the general population and >70 mg/dL (1.8 mmol/L) in individuals with CVD. Hypertriglyceridemia was deﬁned as TG levels >150 mg/dL (1.7 mmol/L). Severe hyper- triglyceridemia was deﬁned as TG levels 500 mg/dL (5.7 mmol/L).
