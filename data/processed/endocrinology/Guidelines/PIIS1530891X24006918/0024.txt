Overall, the cohort studies showed c-statistic results for risk cal- culators (or risk factors) alone with a range of 0.693 to 0.84, which indicates that risk calculators or traditional models are good at predicting future ASCVD events. The addition of a risk enhancer only slightly improved the ability of the different models to predict ASCVD risk.
