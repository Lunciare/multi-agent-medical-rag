Therapeutic options included PCSK9 inhibitors (alirocumab, evo- mibe, and ﬁbrates were considered as part of the comparison or usual care group, or as cointerventions. Recent trial data demon- strated a lack of evidence in support of the use of ﬁbrates, when added to statins, for the prevention of CVD, and the task force only evaluated this class of agents when investigating outcome of pancreatitis in patients with hypertriglyceridemia. The task force prioritized patient-oriented outcomes for each question, which included mortality, MI, stroke, coronary revascu- larization, PVD events, pancreatitis, treatment discontinuation due to adverse events, and changes in risk assessment. All outcomes were rated as critical for clinical decision-making except for the outcome of PVD events which was rated as important. To assess imprecision, the task force used a minimally contextualized approach to identify a single threshold (minimally important dif- ference [MID]) for each outcome a priori. The MID was set as the smallest clinically meaningful difference in the absolute risk for each outcome by consensus following a discussion of observed frequencies for each outcome in untreated populations and overall clinical judgment.
