Conclusions and Evidence Gaps Heart disease has remained a major cause of premature mor- tality (and morbidity) in heavily industrialized nations and has replaced cancer as the number one cause of premature mortality in almost all of these countries.125 This is now true for both men and women, having replaced breast cancer in the latter group as the major cause of premature mortality.126 Heart disease is also re- ported to now become a major factor in disease-related premature death in the rest of the world.
