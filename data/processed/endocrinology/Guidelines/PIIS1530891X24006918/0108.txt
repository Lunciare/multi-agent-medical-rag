This review used data from a combination of studies using either alirocumab or evolocumab; individual study data were not reported. The baseline ASCVD risk for individuals in each group was not reported, though most participants had documented ASCVD and were being treated for secondary pre- vention. Absolute risk differences compared with control groups were calculated and there was no substantial clinical beneﬁt in all-cause mortality, CV-related mortality, MI, or stroke observed in those achieving LDL-C <50 mg/dL.
