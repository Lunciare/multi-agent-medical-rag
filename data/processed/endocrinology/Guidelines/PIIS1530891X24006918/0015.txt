Systematic reviews and randomized controlled trials (RCTs) were prioritized in the searches; however, cohort studies were included for speciﬁc questions if RCTs were not available. A medical librarian aided in the development and execution of the literature searches. PubMed (Medline), Cochrane Database of Systematic Reviews, and clinicaltrials.gov were searched for En- glish-language studies published from database inception until November 7, 2023. The literature search for the risk assessment PICO question was limited to 2018 to present because of the availability of an evidence report from 2018 by the U.S. Preventive Services Task Force.22 Additional articles were found by hand-searching reference lists of included studies.
