Overall, there was low certainty of evidence, based on the lowest certainty of evidence across the critical outcomes, including all-cause mortality, CV-related mortality, MI, and stroke. The other outcomes of coronary revascularization and discontinuation of treatment were rated as moderate certainty of evidence. Based on the absolute risk estimates derived from the included RCTs, the task force determined that niacin may not result in a clinically meaningful decrease in all-cause mortality, CV-related mortality, stroke, or coronary revascularization. Niacin may result in a trivial decrease in MI, though the CI includes both potential beneﬁt and harm.
