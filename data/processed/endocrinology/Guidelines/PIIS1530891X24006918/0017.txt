Consensus was obtained from the workgroups on individual ratings of risk of bias, indirectness, inconsistency, and imprecision. Work- group members then worked with the evidence team to complete draft evidence-to-decision frameworks by providing key input into each judgment resulting in draft recommendation language and justiﬁcations.
