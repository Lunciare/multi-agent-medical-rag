The therapeutic approach to early weight loss may be different than that used to maintain weight loss over an extended period. Different or lower doses of obesity medications that are well-tolerated, accessible, and safe, particularly with respect to muscle and bone loss, may be advantageous. Further research and medication development is needed to inform optimal strategies for long-term effective and safe therapy.
