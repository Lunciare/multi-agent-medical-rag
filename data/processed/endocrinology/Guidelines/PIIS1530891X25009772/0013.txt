The measurement of waist-to-height ratio involves the measurement of waist circumference (WC), and BMI and WC should be interpreted considering age, sex, and ethnicity. Addi- tional modalities such as dual-energy x-ray absorptiometry (DXA) or impedance plethysmography may be used to assess body composition and/or distribution of adipose tissue when the anthropometric measurements and examination are discordant or do not match the clinical findings. BMI measurements should be interpreted clinically, taking into account muscle mass, edema, soft tissue masses, and any contributions to body weight other than adipose tissue.
