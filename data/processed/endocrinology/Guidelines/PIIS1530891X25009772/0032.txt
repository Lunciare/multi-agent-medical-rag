Several bariatric procedures are now usually performed via minimally invasive surgical approaches (laparoscopic or robotic-assisted). Other endoscopic therapies that are short term, such as intragastric balloons, can be less efficacious and lacking in evidence for long-term benefits at the present time. Nevertheless, endoscopic devices may be considered and could represent effective adjunctive therapy in combination with obesity medications. A BMI of ≥40.0 kg/m 2 , and a BMI of ≥35.0 kg/m 2 with ORCD, have long been the proposed thresholds for surgical indications.
