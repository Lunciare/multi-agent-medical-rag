It is important to individu- alize the physical activity prescription based on the preferences and capabilities of each patient. Two adages apply: first, begin at a low intensity level and increase slowly as tolerated; second, any activity is better than none. 1 It can be a challenge for clinicians to assist a patient with limited exercise experience or with disabil- ities to safely begin an appropriate activity regimen.
