Major Adverse Cardiovascular Event Prevention Semaglutide is the only obesity medication, thus far, that has been shown to reduce risks of major adverse cardiovascular events (MACE) in a cardiovascular outcome trial (CVOT) involving sec- ondary prevention in people with obesity and not diabetes. 129 Tirzepatide may also be efficacious for cardioprotection based on post hoc analyses of the SURMOUNT 1 extension trial, which showed reduced hazard ratios for MACE. 251 Liraglutide 1.8 mg per day demonstrated cardioprotection in people with obesity and T2D in the LEADER CVOT. 129,252 A morbidity and mortality outcome trial is ongoing for tirzepatide. Definitive data for car- dioprotection are lacking for orlistat, naltrexone/bupropion, and phentermine/topiramate.
