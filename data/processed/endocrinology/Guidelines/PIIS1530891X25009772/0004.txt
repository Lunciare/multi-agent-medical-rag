Anthropometric and clinical evaluations are then used to stage the severity of the disease which can guide goals and intensity of the complication-centric treatment plan. 6-8 In 2017, AACE published a position statement that proposed a diagnostic term to explicitly identify obesity as a chronic disease: adiposity-based chronic disease (ABCD). 9 The European Association for the Study of Obesity followed suit in 2019 with a position statement endorsing ABCD as a diagnostic term in line with their previous proposal to improve the International Classification of Diseases diagnostic criteria beyond BMI. 10 ABCD refers to what is being treated (neurohormonal-driven dysregulation of energy balance leading to abnormalities in the mass, distribution, and function of adipose) and why the disease is treated (a chronic disease with complications that impair quality of life and confer morbidity and mortality).
