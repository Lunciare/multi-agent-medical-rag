For example, most available evidence for sulfonylureas for HbA1c and weight gain was from studies in which it was given with metformin. This may not represent their effectiveness when used as monotherapy. • Several trials noted issues with the durability of the effect of sulfonylureas (that is, the initial response was followed by a relatively rapid deterioration). In 1 trial the AG noted that 34% of people taking sulfonylureas needed additional treatment within 5 years compared with 15% of those taking rosiglitazone.
