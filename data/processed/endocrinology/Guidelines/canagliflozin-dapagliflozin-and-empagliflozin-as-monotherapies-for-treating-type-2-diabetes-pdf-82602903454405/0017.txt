It used trials of 24 to 26 weeks in which placebo was the comparator. • All SGLT-2 inhibitors were more effective than placebo for HbA1c and weight change. • Compared with sitagliptin, SGLT-2 inhibitors were either more effective for HbA1c (canagliflozin 100 mg and 300 mg, and dapagliflozin) or there was no difference (empagliflozin 10 mg and 25 mg), and all SGLT-2 inhibitors were more effective for weight change. • Compared with sulfonylureas, there were no differences for HbA1c, and SGLT-2 inhibitors were more effective for weight change. • Compared with pioglitazone, dapagliflozin and empagliflozin 10 mg were less effective for HbA1c (no differences compared with other SGLT-2 inhibitors), and SGLT-2 inhibitors were more effective for weight change. 3.22 The AG considered the effectiveness of the SGLT-2 inhibitors compared with each other. It noted that both doses of canagliflozin lowered HbA1c more than dapagliflozin and both doses of empagliflozin. It stated that some of this reduction may be because studies suggested that canagliflozin, unlike other SGLT-2 inhibitors, may also have an effect on the SGLT-1 receptor (which reduces absorption of glucose in the gut).
