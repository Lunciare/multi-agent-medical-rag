Other factors Recommending dexamethasone intravitreal implant would address many of the important equalities issues identified 3.15 The company submission noted that no equality considerations relating to using dexamethasone intravitreal implant were identified or anticipated. At scoping it was raised that if a person is registered as blind or partially sighted, it is considered a disability, as stated in the Equality Act 2010. Therefore, the committee noted the patient population addressed in this appraisal is a protected group under this act. The patient expert emphasised that there is a high prevalence of diabetes among people with a learning disability.
