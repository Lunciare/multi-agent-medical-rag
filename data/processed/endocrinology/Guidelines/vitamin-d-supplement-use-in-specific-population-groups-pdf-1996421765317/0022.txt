D supplements Supplements containing vitamin D are available on prescription or for sale from pharmacies or shops. However, there is wide variation in the content and price and some supplements may not be suitable for particular at-risk groups. (For example many multivitamins contain vitamin A, which pregnant women should avoid during pregnancy.) In 2013, the Chief Medical Officer for England recommended a review of the cost effectiveness of extending the provision of free Healthy Start vitamins to every child. This was due to concerns that 'providing free vitamins to targeted groups has not led to high enough levels of uptake' (CMO's annual report 2012: our children deserve better Department of Health). Up to April 2013, people not eligible for Healthy Start were able to buy the supplements at a much lower cost than commercial preparations.
