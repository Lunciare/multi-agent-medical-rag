A safe intake is used where there is insufficient evidence to set a RNI. The safe intake is the amount judged to be enough for almost everyone, but below a level that could have undesirable effects. • a 'safe intake' of 8.5 to 10 micrograms per day for all infants from birth to 1 year of age • a 'safe intake' of 10 micrograms per day for children aged 1 to 4 years. All population groups are currently advised to take a supplement that meets 100% of the reference nutrient intake for their age group (as above). The reference nutrient intake for population groups is 10 micrograms/day (1 microgram=40 international units, so 10 micrograms=400 IU).
