Vitamin D: supplement use
KEYWORDS: vitamin, supplements, groups, health, population, people, population groups, start, healthy, healthy start, specific, vitamin supplements, local, specific population, children

this guideline, a supplement refers to supplements of vitamin D, either alone or contained in multi-vitamin products (including Healthy Start supplements). It includes licensed products available only on prescription or through pharmacies and (unlicensed) food supplements available from a range of pharmacies and retail outlets. SACN review The Scientific Advisory Committee on Nutrition (SACN) reviewed the dietary reference values for vitamin D intake in the UK population in 2016. They recommend that vitamin D supplements are made available for the entire population (4 years old or more) throughout the year. The recommendations should be read in conjunction with the SACN vitamin D 8 of and health report 2016. Clinical judgement Clinical judgement will be needed to determine whether NICE's recommendations in this guideline are suitable for people with conditions that increase the risk of vitamin D deficiency.
