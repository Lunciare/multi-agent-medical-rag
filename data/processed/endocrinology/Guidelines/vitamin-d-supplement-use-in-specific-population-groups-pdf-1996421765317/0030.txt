Members were concerned that users of the scheme may be unaware of the importance of taking the supplement. 1.12 The PHAC was aware that numerous aspects of the system for distributing Healthy Start vitamins may hinder implementation. For example a report commissioned by the Department of Health's Policy Research Programme Healthy start: understanding the use of vouchers and vitamins identified that senior public health practitioners may spend considerable time administering the scheme. In addition, the order and reimbursement processes are slow and complex, and there are ongoing concerns about shelf-life and storage.
