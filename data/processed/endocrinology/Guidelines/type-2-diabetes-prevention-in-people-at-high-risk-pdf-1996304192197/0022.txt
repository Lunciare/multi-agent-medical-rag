This could include the use of strategies such as impulse-control techniques (to improve management of food cravings). 1.9.3 Participants in intensive lifestyle-change programmes should be encouraged to involve a family member, friend or carer who can offer emotional, information, planning or other practical support to help them make the necessary changes. For example, they may be able to join the participant in physical activities, help them to plan changes, make or accept changes to the family's diet or free up the participant's time so they can take part in preventive activities. (It may sometimes be appropriate to encourage the participant to get support from the whole family.) 1.9.4 Participants should be encouraged to use self-regulation techniques.
