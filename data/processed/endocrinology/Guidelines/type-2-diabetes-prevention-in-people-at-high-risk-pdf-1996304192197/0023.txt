They should also review their progress towards achieving their goals, identify and find ways to solve problems and then revise their goals and action plans, where necessary. The aim is to encourage them to learn from experience. 1.10 Quality-assured, intensive lifestyle-change programmes: evaluation 1.10.1 Evaluate intensive lifestyle-change programmes by recording people's health outcomes at 12 months, or more frequently, if appropriate (for example, every 6 months). As a minimum, include the following measures: • number and demographics of adults registered • level of attendance • changes in the amount of moderate to vigorous physical activity undertaken 19 of each week • changes in dietary intake, with a focus on total intake of fat, saturated fat and fibre • changes in weight, waist circumference or BMI • changes in fasting plasma glucose or HbA1c levels. 1.10.2 Conduct an annual audit of how the programme was delivered.
