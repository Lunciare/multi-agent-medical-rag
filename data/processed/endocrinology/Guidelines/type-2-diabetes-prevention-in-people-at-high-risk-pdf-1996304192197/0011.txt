Explain that the risk can be reduced. Briefly discuss their particular risk factors, identify which ones can be modified and discuss how they can achieve this by changing their lifestyle. • Offer them a referral to a local, evidence-based, quality-assured intensive lifestyle-change programme(see recommendations 1.8.1 to 1.10.2).
