Clinical effectiveness 3.1 The company identified 11 studies that evaluated empagliflozin for treating type 2 diabetes. The company's submission included details of 8 of these 11 studies: 7 randomised controlled trials and 1 long-term extension study (1245.31). The 3 studies excluded were 1 that evaluated empagliflozin as monotherapy (1245.20), EMPA-REG OUTCOM (1245.25) and EMPA-REG-JAPAN (1245.52).
