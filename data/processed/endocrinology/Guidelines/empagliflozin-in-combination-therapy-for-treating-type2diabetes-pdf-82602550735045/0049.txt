The Committee concluded that empagliflozin 10 mg and 25 mg had been shown to be a cost-effective use of NHS resources compared with canagliflozin, dapagliflozin or sitagliptin as an add-on treatment to insulin because of its very small incremental costs and incremental QALYs. The Committee recommended empagliflozin as a treatment option for people with diabetes that is inadequately controlled by insulin with or without other oral antidiabetic drugs.
