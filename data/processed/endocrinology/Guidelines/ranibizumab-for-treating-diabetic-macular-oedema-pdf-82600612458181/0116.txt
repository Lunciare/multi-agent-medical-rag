Equalities considerations and social value judgements (section 4.28) NICE had received evidence that some people in full-time residential care had restricted access to treatment for diabetic macular oedema. However, consultees suggested that the national screening programme for diabetic retinopathy in England and Wales has reduced this inequality across the NHS. The Committee had been made aware that there is a higher prevalence of diabetes in people of South Asian, African and African–Caribbean family origin and that, among people with diabetes, sight-threatening eye disease is more common in people of African and African–Caribbean family origin than in white Europeans. However, the Committee agreed that this was an issue that could not be addressed in a technology appraisal.
