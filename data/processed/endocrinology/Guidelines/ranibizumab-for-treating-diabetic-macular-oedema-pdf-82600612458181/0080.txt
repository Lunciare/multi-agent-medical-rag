The Committee was aware that the studies were of relatively short duration, providing treatment for up to 3 years, and did not include long-term follow-up. The Committee concluded that the quality of the clinical-effectiveness evidence for ranibizumab was acceptable. 4.10 The Committee noted that, based on the results of the RESTORE study, ranibizumab (alone or in combination with laser photocoagulation) is associated with immediate and sustained gains in visual acuity in the treated eye, whereas improvement with laser photocoagulation alone is significantly less marked.
