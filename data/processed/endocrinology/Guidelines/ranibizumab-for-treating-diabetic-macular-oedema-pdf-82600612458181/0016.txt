In the second year of the model, based on data from DRCR.net, the manufacturer assumed 3 further ranibizumab injections in the ranibizumab monotherapy arm and 2 further ranibizumab injections in the combination therapy arm. For both the combination therapy arm and the laser photocoagulation alone arm, the model assumed 2 laser photocoagulation treatments in the first year and 1 additional laser treatment in the second year. The original model did not simulate any ranibizumab treatment or laser photocoagulation after the second year.
