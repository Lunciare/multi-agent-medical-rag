The Committee commented that it was unlikely that people who received 3 injections in year 3 would receive no further injections in year 4, especially if vision was assumed to remain stable during this period. The Committee was aware that some consultees had suggested that people with diabetic macular oedema would need more frequent treatment with ranibizumab than was assumed by the manufacturer. The Committee also noted that uncertainty remained about whether people would need ranibizumab beyond 4 years and, if they did, what the costs of ongoing treatment would be.
