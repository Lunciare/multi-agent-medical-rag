The Committee preferred the manufacturer's scenario analysis adjusting for factors that may influence the relationship between visual acuity and health- related quality of life. • The model underestimated the amount of ranibizumab that people with diabetic macular oedema were likely to need over time. Basing the number of injections for year 2 of the model's ranibizumab monotherapy arm on experience in DRCR.net overlooks the fact that the participants in the trial also received laser photocoagulation, which clinicians believe may have a ranibizumab-sparing effect. The declining number of ranibizumab injections assumed in years 3 and 4 is not evidence-based, and is unlikely to lead to stable vision during that period, as assumed. It may also have been unrealistic to assume that ranibizumab treatment will not continue beyond 4 years. • The model's assumption that the relative benefit achieved during the treatment phase lasts indefinitely was unrealistic.
