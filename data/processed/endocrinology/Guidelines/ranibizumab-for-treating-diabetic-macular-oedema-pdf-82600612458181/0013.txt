The manufacturer provided a meta-analysis of categorical visual acuity data from RESTORE and DRCR.net after 12 months of treatment. This suggested that the visual acuity of eyes randomised to ranibizumab plus laser photocoagulation was approximately twice as likely to improve by 10 letters than the visual acuity of eyes randomised to laser photocoagulation alone (relative risk=2.15, 95% CI 1.43 to 3.22, p<0.001; random-effects model; fixed-effects model produced similar results).
