Mortality rates were derived from life tables for England and Wales, modified by a relative risk representing the additional hazard of death for the modelled cohort when compared with the general population. In the original model, the manufacturer used a relative risk of 1.27, drawn from published literature comparing the risk of death for people with clinically significant diabetic macular oedema with the risk of death for people with diabetes, but without diabetic macular oedema. The manufacturer modelled adverse events on the basis of observed, treatment-specific rates of 4 events (cataract, endophthalmitis, retinal detachment and vitreous haemorrhage) in a pooled analysis of RESTORE and DRCR.net.
