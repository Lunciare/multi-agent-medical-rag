A clinical expert explained that primary care weight management services are being delivered. These include advice from GPs, diet and exercise support provided by nurses with specialist interest in obesity and access to dietitians for people with complex needs. The patient expert explained that their experience of care included regular touchpoints with GPs, referral to diet and exercise professionals and access to psychological support. The NHS commissioning expert noted that access to lifestyle weight management services and the level of support provided in primary care services varies across the country. Specialist weight management services provide longer and more comprehensive MDT assessments and interventions. NICE's guideline on overweight and obesity management defines these as specialist primary, community or secondary care-based services led by an MDT offering a combination of nutritional, psychological and surgical interventions, and medicines.
