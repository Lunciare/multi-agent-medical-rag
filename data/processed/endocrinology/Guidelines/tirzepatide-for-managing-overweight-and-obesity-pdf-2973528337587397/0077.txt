So, eligibility for tirzepatide will not be restricted by access to specialist weight management services. The committee was also aware that SURMOUNT-1 did not include people with a history of significant active or unstable major depressive disorder or other severe psychiatric disorders within the last 2 years. So, there was no evidence for the treatment effect of tirzepatide in this population, or any evidence for how much additional psychological support might be needed. But, despite the increased uncertainty this introduced into the model, the committee agreed that populations excluded from SURMOUNT-1 should not be excluded from the recommendations for tirzepatide.
