position statement by the Society for Endocrinology and Obesity Management Collaborative UK on phased introduction for new medical therapies for weight management (PDF only). In line with the NICE manual on health technology evaluations, NHS England is developing an interim commissioning policy that will apply to phase-in funding and that will manage access to tirzepatide during the extended funding variation period, describing how patient cohorts will be prioritised in line with these recommendations. To support this, NHS England will both referral prioritisation in specialist weight management services and priority cohorts in other settings (including primary care-based services).
