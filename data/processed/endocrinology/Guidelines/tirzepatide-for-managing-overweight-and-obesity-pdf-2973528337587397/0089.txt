The eligible population within NICE's guidance includes adults with a body mass index (BMI) of at least 35 kg/m 2 (or a lower threshold, usually reduced by 2.5 kg/m 2, for people from South Asian, Chinese, other Asian, Middle Eastern, Black African or African-Caribbean ethnic backgrounds) and at least 1 weight-related comorbidity.
