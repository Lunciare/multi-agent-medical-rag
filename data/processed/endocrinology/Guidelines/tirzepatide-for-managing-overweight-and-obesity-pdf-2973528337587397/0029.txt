The model used risk equations, which used surrogate outcomes including BMI to estimate the risk of an event in the model happening. The committee noted that using risk equations to estimate long-term outcomes, rather than trial data for those outcomes, contributed to the uncertainty around the treatment benefits in ongoing effects (such as temporary reversal of prediabetes, stroke or obstructive sleep apnoea) and one-off events such as knee replacement. The committee these had not been captured in the model. It concluded that although there were some uncertainties associated with the company's model, it was suitable for decision making.
