The EAG provided analysis that estimated the amount of overestimation of the risk of events for an average person in the model. It presented scenario analyses that included adjustments to the 10-year risk functions to account for these overestimations. It noted that these scenarios were illustrative and given the model structure it was not possible to quantify the exact impact of the potential compounding of the risk of events over time. The company also presented scenarios to show the impact on its base case if the risk of developing type 2 diabetes was reduced by 25% and 50% in all arms. The committee considered both the company's and the EAG's scenario analyses, noting that the EAG's adjustments especially had a limited impact on the ICER. It concluded that the compounding of the risk of events remained an uncertainty in the model.
