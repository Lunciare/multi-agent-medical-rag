This is because the model assumes that someone with a high baseline BMI that decreases during the model's time horizon has equal health risks as someone with a consistently lower BMI. The EAG suggested that this might not be appropriate. For example, someone with long-term insulin resistance caused by their obesity is unlikely to have similar long-term cardiovascular outcomes to someone who had never had insulin resistance.
