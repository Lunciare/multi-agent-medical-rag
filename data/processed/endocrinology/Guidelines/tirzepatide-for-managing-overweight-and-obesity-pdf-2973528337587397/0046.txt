They explained that for semaglutide, around two thirds of the weight lost while on treatment is regained within the first year after stopping. After the first committee meeting, the EAG updated its model to assume that weight would be regained in 2 years after stopping tirzepatide. The EAG noted that assuming either 2 or 3 years for time-to-weight-regain did not have a meaningful impact on the ICER. In response to draft guidance consultation, the company also updated its model to assume that weight would be regained in 2 years after stopping tirzepatide.
