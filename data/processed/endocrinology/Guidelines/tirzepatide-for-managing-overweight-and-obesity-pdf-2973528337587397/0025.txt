The patient expert explained that, based on experience with other similar treatments, it is important to titrate the dose up at the appropriate rate to avoid side effects. The committee concluded that it was likely that the highest tolerated dose of tirzepatide would be used, and for most people this would be 15 mg. The committee also concluded that dose escalation and de- escalation would need appropriate monitoring. How this was done would need to be considered in the implementation of the wraparound obesity management services for delivering tirzepatide.
