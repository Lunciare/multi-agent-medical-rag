Based on published values, HbA1c drift was assumed to be 0.14% for canagliflozin, dapagliflozin, DPP-4 inhibitors and GLP-1 analogues, 0.07% for thiazolidinediones, 0.24% for sulfonylureas and 0.15% for insulin. The drift values for systolic blood pressure, lipids, weight and eGFR were assumed to be the same for all treatments. The manufacturer's model allowed patients to take anti-dyslipidaemia and anti-hypertension medications, and applied rescue treatments when biomarker thresholds for dyslipidaemia and hypertension were exceeded.
