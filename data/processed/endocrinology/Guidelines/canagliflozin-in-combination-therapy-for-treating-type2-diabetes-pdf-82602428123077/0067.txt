The Committee therefore concluded that the clinical data incorporated into the manufacturer's model were acceptable for informing the cost-effectiveness estimates. 4.17 The Committee discussed specific areas of uncertainty relating to data used for the 2 SGLT-2 inhibitors, canagliflozin and dapagliflozin, which had been identified by commentators during consultation. It noted that the same values for changes in lipids had been used for the 2 drugs in the model, and was aware that the ERG considered the evidence to show that dapagliflozin had a more favourable profile than canagliflozin.
