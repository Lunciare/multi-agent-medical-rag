Manufacturer's sensitivity analyses 3.38 The manufacturer conducted deterministic and probabilistic sensitivity analyses for canagliflozin compared with a DPP-4 inhibitor (in dual therapy, triple therapy and as an add-on to insulin therapy) and with a sulfonylurea in dual therapy, because it perceived these to be the key comparators for canagliflozin.
