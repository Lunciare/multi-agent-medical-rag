Grade A; BEL 1 Recommendation 9.3 In persons with T2D and established HF (regardless of ejection fraction, background glucose-lowering or HF therapies, or A1C), use SGLT2is with proven HF beneﬁts to reduce the risk of hospitaliza- tion for HF or CV death, and to improve HFerelated symptoms.
