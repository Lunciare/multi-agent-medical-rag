Grade B; BEL 2 Q 19.3: Should men with diabetes mellitus and cardiometabolic disorders be assessed for hypogonadism? R 19.3 All men with CMD including prediabetes, metabolic syndrome, obesity, and T2D should be assessed for hypogonadism by history and physical examination; test for testosterone deﬁciency in persons with loss of libido and/or loss of muscle strength or mass, erectile dysfunction, osteopenia, or infertility. Grade B; BEL 1 Q 20.1: How should persons at risk for secondary diabetes be assessed? R 20.1 Persons with risk factors for developing secondary DM, such as postorgan transplantation, cystic ﬁbrosis, chronic pancreatitis/postpartial pancreatectomy, or on medication associated with hyperglycemia, should be monitored routinely for IFG, IGT, and/or overt DM.
