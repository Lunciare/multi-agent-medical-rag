Caffeine, exercise, and smoking should be avoided for at least 30 minutes prior to measurement. Measure- ment of BP in the standing position is also indicated in persons suspected to have postural hypotension. An appropriately sized cuff (ie, cuff bladder encircling at least 80% of the arm) should be used to ensure accuracy. At least 2, and preferably 3, serial measurements should be obtained, and the average BP recorded.
