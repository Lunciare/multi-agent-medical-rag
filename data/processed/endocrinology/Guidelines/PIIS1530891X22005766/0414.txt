Insulin therapy is efﬁcacious in all forms of secondary DM. There is limited evidence to support any particular non- insulin therapy in secondary DM. There are several studies on PTDM and oral antihyperglycemia agents, including repaglinide, DPP-4 inhibitors, and pioglitazone.1612 In particular, DPP-4 in- hibitors have been studied the most in several RCTs of persons with kidney transplant and have been shown to be safe and efﬁcacious.1612-1614 The choice to use immunosuppressive agents is also a consideration in both prevention and man- agement of PTDM. Use of antithymocyte globulin, belatacept, and minimization or elimination of tacrolimus in the immu- nosuppressive regimen has been shown to reduce hyperglyce- mia and new onset PTDM.1590,1615,1616 SGLT2is are emerging as a potential option for PTDM and have demonstrated safety and efﬁcacy.1612,1617-1619 Studies on intensive lifestyle intervention for secondary DM are limited. However, one prospective study demonstrated a beneﬁcial role of lifestyle intervention in persons with transplant.1620 Studies on CFRD are small and limited.
