Therapeutic lifestyle goals in persons with hypertension and T2D should include education about a healthy diet (such as the Mediterranean diet) with emphasis on weight management and reduced salt intake (such as the DASH diet), daily physical activity and regular exercise (several times a week) (also see the section on weight-loss therapy and lifestyle in Evidence Base 10).214-220 Individuals should be referred to a registered die- titian for diet education and as needed to a psychologist or CDCES to support long-term behavior change. Cognitive behavioral therapy can be used to support adherence to medications.221-225 Analysis of the UKPDS data suggests that BP lowering should be a priority in managing a person presenting with newly diagnosed hypertension and DM.
