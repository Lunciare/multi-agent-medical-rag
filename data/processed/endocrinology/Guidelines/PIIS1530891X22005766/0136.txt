We recommend the use of apo B measurements as this is more accurate than non-HDL-C and predicts ASCVD risk more accurately than LDL-C.319 In persons at high risk, the lipid targets should be LDL-C <100 mg/dL, apo B <90 mg/dL, and non-HDL-C <130 mg/dL.314,315,320-325 In persons at very high risk for ASCVD, the lipid targets should be LDL-C <70 mg/dL, apo B <80 mg/dL, and non-HDL- C <100 mg/dL.306 In persons with DM at extreme risk of ASCVD events, the lipid targets should be LDL-C <55 mg/dL, apo B <70 mg/ dL, and non-HDL-C <90 mg/dL.322-324,326 Note that the risk categories vary in name and deﬁnition in the references cited.
