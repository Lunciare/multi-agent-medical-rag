Question 21: What is the role of sleep medicine in the care of persons with DM? Recommendation 21.1 Health care professionals should assess persons with T2D for symptoms and signs of OSA, especially in the presence of obesity or suggestive clinical features of OSA. Grade B; BEL 2 Recommendation 21.2 Based on resources available locally, persons suspected to have OSA should be referred to an appropriate center for diagnosis and management of OSA. Grade B; BEL 4 and Expert Opinion of Task Force Recommendation 21.3 Weight loss is recommended as the predominant intervention to improve both OSA and insulin sensitivity. In addition, devices that provide positive airway pressure as prescribed by a sleep specialist are effective.
