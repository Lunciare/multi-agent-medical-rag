Tirzepatide is a dual glucose-dependent insulinotropic peptide and GLP-1 RA recently approved by the FDA for improvement of glycemic control in persons with T2D. Individual trials have assessed the clinical proﬁle of tirzepatide vs different comparators. A systematic analysis of seven completed trials with a total of 6609 participants1036 conﬁrmed a dose-dependent (5, 10, or 15 mg weekly subcutaneous administration) superiority on glycemic ef- ﬁcacy, and reduction in body weight was evident with tirzepatide vs placebo, GLP-1 RAs, and basal insulin. Tirzepatide was associated with increased incidence of GI adverse events but no increase in risk of hypoglycemia. Tirzepatide appears to be useful for those already on metformin therapy.
