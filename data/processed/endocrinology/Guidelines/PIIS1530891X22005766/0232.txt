Medications used for weight loss include several sympathomi- metic amines (phentermine, benzphetamine, and phendime- trazine) approved for short-term use (12 weeks), which makes these drugs ineffectual for treatment of ABCD as a chronic disease. There is a lack of rigorous long-term safety data available for the sympathomimetic amines because this criterion was not required at the time of their approval.
