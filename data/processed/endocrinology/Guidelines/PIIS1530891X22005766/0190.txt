Enhanced glucose control in people with T1D dramatically reduces the incidence of DPN (78% relative risk reduction),78,542,547,549 and glucose control remains the stron- gest risk factor for DPN in T1D even in contemporary cohorts.417,542- 544,547 Despite socioeconomic barriers being associated with DPN, one should aim to achieve near-normal glycemia in persons with T1D at risk of DPN.544 In contrast, enhanced glucose control in people with T2D reduces the risk of developing DPN more modestly.547,557 The ACCORD trial in individuals with T2D reported a modest but signiﬁcant DPN risk reduction with intensive glyce- mia intervention after 5 years of follow-up,69 but other trials re- ported inconclusive effects.547 Speciﬁc glucose-lowering strategies may contribute to the discrepancy.
