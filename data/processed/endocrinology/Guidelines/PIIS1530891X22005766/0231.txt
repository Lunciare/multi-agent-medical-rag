The addition of weight-loss medications has been shown to achieve signiﬁcantly more weight loss than lifestyle interventions alone and produce greater A1C lowering and improvements in cardiometabolic risk factors. The mechanism of action for all weight loss medications (except orlistat) is to blunt appetite at the level of the central nervous system hypothalamic satiety centers, which thereby helps individuals adhere to a reduced-calorie diet.
