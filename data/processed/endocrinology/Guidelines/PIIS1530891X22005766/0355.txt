If an indi- vidual has hypoglycemic unawareness and hypoglycemia-associated autonomic failure, several weeks of hypoglycemia avoidance may at least partially reverse hypoglycemia unawareness and may reduce the risk or prevent recurrence of severe hypoglycemia. Adjustment of an individual’s long-term antihyperglycemic regimen may be necessary to further avoid recurrence of hypoglycemia. Grade B; BEL 1 Recommendation 14.4 In persons with T2D who develop hypoglycemia and are being treated with alpha-glucosidase inhibitors or with pancreatic diabetes, oral glucose or lactose-containing foods (dairy products) must be given becausealpha-glucosidaseinhibitorsinhibit the breakdownand absorption of complex carbohydrates and disaccharides (eg, table sugars or starches).
