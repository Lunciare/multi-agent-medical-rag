The number in each box suggests the frequency of monitoring (number of times per year). Green indicates stable disease with annual follow-up measurements if CKD is present. Yellow indicates caution and calls for ≥1 measurement per year. Orange requires 2 measurements per year. Red calls for 3 measurements per year. Deep red may require close monitoring at a frequency of 4 times or more per year (at least every 1 to 3 months). These general parameters are based on expert opinion and must take into account underlying comorbid conditions and disease state, as well as the likelihood of a change in management for any individual.
