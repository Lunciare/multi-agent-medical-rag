The risk for both might be decreased by using lower doses of pioglitazone and avoiding the highest dose and/or perhaps use in combination with an SGLT2i and/or a GLP-1 RA.994 TZDs are not recommended in persons with symptomatic HF and are contraindicated in persons with New York Heart Association (NYHA) class III or class IV CHF.
