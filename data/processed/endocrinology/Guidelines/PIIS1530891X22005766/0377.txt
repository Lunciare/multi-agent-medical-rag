In insulin-naïve per- sonswith T2D, astarting insulinTDDbetween 0.3and0.5units/kg/day iseffective and safe in thosewho receive care for general medicine and surgery. Persons with T2D receiving insulin therapy before admission are at risk for severe hyperglycemia in the hospital if insulin therapy is discontinued.
