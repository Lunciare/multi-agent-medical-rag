Given the paramount importance of patient safety, reasonable glucose targets in the hos- pital setting should be set at modestly higher levels than targets for outpatients with DM. For noncritically ill persons, a premeal glucose target of <140 mg/dL and a random BG of <180 mg/dL are recom- mended,while avoidinghypoglycemia (BG <70 mg/dL). Additionally, glycemic targets should be modiﬁed according to clinical status. For persons who are able to achieve and maintain glycemic control without hypoglycemia, a lower target range may be reasonable. For persons with terminal illness, limited life expectancy, or high risk for hypoglycemia, higher target ranges may be reasonable.100,1259,1269- 1274 Refer to Section 1 on Screening, Diagnosis, Glycemic Targets, and Glycemic Monitoring for additional guidance.
