Grade A; BEL 1 Recommendation 5.7 Treatment targets for persons in a high ASCVD risk category are LDL-C <100 mg/dL, apolipoprotein B (apo B) <90 mg/dL, and non- HDL-C <130 mg/dL. Treatment targets for persons in a very high risk ASCVD category are LDL-C <70 mg/dL, apo B <80 mg/dL, and non-HDL-C <100 mg/dL. Treatment targets for persons with extreme risk of ASCVD include LDL-C <55 mg/dL, apo B <70 mg/dL, and non-HDL-C <90 mg/dL (Table 9 and Fig. 1).
