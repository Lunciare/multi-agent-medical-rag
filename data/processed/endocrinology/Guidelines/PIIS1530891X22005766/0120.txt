If unacceptable side effects develop, consideration should be given to reducing dosage and/or changing the class of medication while not alleviate symptoms, then consideration should be given to relaxing the target to a higher BP goal based upon individual char- acteristics, preferences, and priorities (Table 7). The UKPDS group performed a 10-year posttrial monitoring observational study that demonstrated a loss of beneﬁt within 2 years if tight BP control was not maintained.201 These data reinforce the imperative to initiate BP-lowering therapy at diagnosis, to intensify treatment as needed to reach and maintain BP goal, and to monitor treatment safety and tolerance for enhanced compliance. The introduction of ﬁxed-dose combination tablets combining 2 or 3 agents in 1 pill can facilitate compliance and adherence.
