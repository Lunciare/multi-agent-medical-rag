Beyond the well- known agents (glucocorticoids, nicotinic acid, thiazides, inter- feron gamma, high-dose statins), agents such as atypical antipsy- chotics, immune checkpoint inhibitors, PI3 kinase inhibitors, tacrolimus, and octreotide have been shown to induce DM. Screening and Diagnosis of GDM All pregnant women should be screened for GDM at 24 to 28 weeks’ gestation.
