Although severe hypoglycemia generally results in recognizable symptoms, mild-to-moderate hypoglycemia may remain asymptomatic and un- reported in persons with DM. Even severe hypoglycemia is often unrecognized in those with hypoglycemia unawareness.1198 Etiology of Hypoglycemia In persons with DM, iatrogenic hypoglycemia stems from an imbalance among insulin and/or insulinogenic (eg, SUs, glinides) therapy and food intake, physical activity, organ function (gluco- neogenesis), and counter-regulation with glucagon and/or epinephrine (hypoglycemia-associated autonomic failure). Hyper- insulinemia, increased alcohol intake, starvation, and organ failure may be aggravating factors.1108,1200,1201 Noniatrogenic hypoglyce- mia (ie, insulinoma) is beyond the scope of this guideline.
