Until recently, there were no large RCTs comparing the effect of one-step and two-step approaches on maternal and neonatal outcomes. An RCT performed at the Kaiser Permanente health systems in Cali- fornia and Hawaii examined the one-step (75 g) and the two-step (50 g) sequential approach on neonatal and gestational out- comes.65 The study randomized 23,792 women to either the one- step or two-step approach. The diagnosis of GDM was twice as high in the one-step group compared with the two-step group (16.5% vs 8.5%).
