Grade B; BEL 1 Recommendation 16.5 Insulin is the preferred therapeutic choice for pregnant women with GDM or T2D, but metformin has been given a category B for pregnancy with accumulating clinical evidence of metformin's safety during the ﬁrst trimester and beyond. Metformin has been shown to improve pregnancy and fetal outcomes except for increased rates of infants with SGA and later onset of obesity.
