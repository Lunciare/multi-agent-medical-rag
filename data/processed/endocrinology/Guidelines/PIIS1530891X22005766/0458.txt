Disclosures and Conﬂicts of Interest Policy The TaskForcewas empaneled in accordance with AACE’s Conﬂict of Interest (COI) Policyand approved by the AACE COI Subcommittee. All members of the expert Task Force completed AACE’s disclosure form regarding any multiplicities of interests related to commercial and direct ﬁnancial relationships within the preceding 12 months with companies that develop products connected with endocrine disorders. Categories for disclosure include employment, stock or other ownership, direct ﬁnancial relationships (eg, speaker or consultant), research funding, authorship or panel involvement on a guideline related to an overlapping topic, or other situations related to a perceived COI.
