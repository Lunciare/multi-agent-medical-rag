Grade A; BEL 1 Recommendation 12.2.15 When control of postprandial hyperglycemia is needed and a basal insulin and a GLP-1 RA are already being used, preference should be given to rapid-acting insulins (the analogs lispro, aspart, and glulisine or the rapid-acting inhaled human insulin powder) over regular human insulin (see Table 18). The former have a more consistent and a more rapid onset and offset of action with less risk of hypoglycemia.
