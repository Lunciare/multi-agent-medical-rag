Grade B; BEL 1 Evidence Base 8: How should neuropathy be diagnosed and managed in persons with DM? Diabetic neuropathy affects about half of all persons with DM, contributing to substantial morbidity and mortality and resulting in a huge economic burden for DM care due to the increased risk of associated complications such as pain, sleep disturbances, falls and fractures, reduced QoL, polypharmacy, and socioeconomic conse- quences.417,418,542-552 Persons with prediabetes and/or obesity may also develop DPN.547,548,553 Consensus in the ﬁeld is that diabetic neuropathies aredeﬁned by the presence of symptoms and/or signs of peripheral and autonomic nerve dysfunction in people with DM after the exclusion of other causes conﬁrmed through clinical examination and appropriate differential tests.
