Grade B; BEL 1 Q 9: How should antihyperglycemic agents be prioritized in persons with type 2 diabetes at high risk for or with established cardiovascular disease? R 9.1 In persons with T2D and established ASCVD or at high risk for ASCVD, use GLP-1 RAs with proven CV beneﬁts to reduce the risk of myocardial infarction, stroke, or CV death regardless of other glucose-lowering or CV therapies and independent of A1C.
