Grade B; BEL 1 Q 24: Is telehealth/virtual care an effective care-delivery model for the management of persons with diabetes mellitus? R 24 Grade A; BEL 1 Q 25: Which occupations have speciﬁc public safetyerelated diabetes management considerations? R 25 Persons with DM who are engaged in occupations with public safety implications, such as commercial drivers and pilots, have special management requirements for certiﬁcation. CGM to predict hypoglycemia in real time and pharmacotherapy that minimizes hypoglycemia are recommended as effective strategies for persons with DM who work in these occupations.
