Grade A; BEL 1 Recommendation 11.2 In persons with prediabetes and/or metabolic syndrome or identiﬁed to be at high risk of T2D based on validated risk-staging instruments, the prevention of T2D can be addressed by lifestyle modiﬁcations that include a healthy meal plan, regular physical with ABCD. The Mediterranean diet should be considered to reduce progression to T2D and risk of CVD. Low-fat, vegetarian, and DASH meal patterns can also be considered for prevention of T2D. Grade A, BEL 1 Recommendation 11.3 Clinicians should manage and monitor CVD risk factors in pre- diabetes and metabolic syndrome, including elevated BP, dyslipi- demia, and excessive weight, with the same targets as for a person with T2D. Grade B; BEL 2 Recommendation 11.4 Lifestyle intervention should include aerobic and resistance physical activity in all persons with prediabetes and/or metabolic syndrome.
