BEL 2 R 5.2 Therapeutic lifestyle interventions for dyslipidemia are recommended for all persons with prediabetes, T1D over the age of 40, or T2D, to include education with a registered dietitian about a healthy diet with emphasis on weight management, daily physical activity, and regular exercise (several times a week). Consultation with a psychologist or CDCES is recommended to support long-term behavior change.
