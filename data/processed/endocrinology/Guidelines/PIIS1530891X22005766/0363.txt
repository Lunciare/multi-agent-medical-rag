In T2D, hypoglycemia typically oc- curs in association with use of exogenous insulin, SUs (especially glyburide),1242 and glinides; symptoms may be mild, moderate, or severe. The risk of hypoglycemia may be further increased by the addition of other antihyperglycemic agents to SUs or insulin. Therefore, in adults with T2D, treatment strategies should emphasize the increased number of antihyperglycemic medication classes that are not associated with severe hypoglycemia (Table 16). Also, the role of hypoglycemia must be considered in determining ideal A1C goals for each patient. BGM and especially CGM are important tactics to help persons prevent, identify, and document hypoglycemia, although it is essential that the glucose meter and CGM meet accuracy standards and that users are provided with education and support.
