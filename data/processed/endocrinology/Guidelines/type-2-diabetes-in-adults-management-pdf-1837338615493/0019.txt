Take into account the: • effect of each medicine on HbA1c and weight • effectiveness of each medicine at preventing and managing cardiovascular and renal conditions • factors that might prevent the person from following a specific treatment option (for example, pioglitazone is contraindicated for people with heart 23 of failure) • cost: when more than 1 medicine from the same drug class are equally suitable for the person, use the least expensive.
