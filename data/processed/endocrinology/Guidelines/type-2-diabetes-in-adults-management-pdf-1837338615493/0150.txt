People are eligible for free dental care if they are: • pregnant • mothers with babies under 1 year old • on low income benefits, or under 20 and dependent on someone who is receiving low income benefits • having treatment in an NHS hospital by the hospital dentist. The recommendations may encourage more people with diabetes to have regular oral health reviews. Combined with proactive engagement and enhanced support for people with diabetes, this may broaden access to dental and oral healthcare and help to reduce oral health inequalities.
