Making decisions using NICE guidelines explains how we use words to show the strength (or certainty) of our recommendations, and has information about prescribing medicines (including off-label use), professional guidelines, standards and laws (including on consent and mental capacity), and safeguarding. Healthcare professionals should follow our general guidelines for people delivering care: • Patient experience in adult NHS services • Shared decision making • Medicines adherence • Medicines optimisation • Multimorbidity • Decision making and mental capacity.
