Return to recommendations Further treatment: people with early onset type 2 diabetes Recommendations 1.28.1 to 1.28.3 Why the committee made the recommendations GLP-1 receptor agonists and tirzepatide there was good evidence in the wider population of people with type 2 diabetes, which the committee agreed could be extrapolated to people with early onset type 2 diabetes. It showed that GLP-1 receptor agonists reduce the risk of cardiovascular events and lead to weight loss as a side effect.
