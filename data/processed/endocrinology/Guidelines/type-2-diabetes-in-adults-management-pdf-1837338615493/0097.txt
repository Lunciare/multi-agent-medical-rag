But the committee agreed that it was not 87 of sufficiently clinically effective to justify adding it to the recommendation. In addition, evidence was not available to the committee to evaluate tirzepatide for this population at this time. As a result, the committee agreed to specifically recommend subcutaneous semaglutide (Ozempic), up to 1 mg once a week, rather than any other GLP-1 receptor agonist or tirzepatide. The committee noted that there are potential serious side effects with GLP-1 receptor agonists and tirzepatide, and a potential for misuse. However, they did not make a recommendation on monitoring because it is covered by MHRA drug safety updates.
