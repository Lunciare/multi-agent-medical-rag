Return to recommendation Initial medicines: people with type 2 diabetes and no relevant comorbidities Recommendations 1.13.1 and 1.13.2 Why the committee made the recommendations Metformin and SGLT-2 inhibitors There is good evidence that managing type 2 diabetes should aim to improve health holistically (in particular, cardiovascular and renal protection), rather than just aim to meet HbA1c targets. The evidence on antidiabetic therapies covered a diverse patient group, including, in varying proportions, people: • with multiple cardiovascular risk factors • with atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease, heart failure or chronic kidney disease • who likely had a lower cardiovascular risk.
