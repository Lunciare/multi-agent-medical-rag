However, the committee agreed that people with an eGFR above 20 ml/min/1.73 m 2 could still be offered an SGLT-2 inhibitor to reduce the risk of end-stage renal events and cardiovascular events (including cardiovascular mortality, myocardial infarctions, non-fatal strokes and hospitalisations for heart failure) from type 2 diabetes. The committee recommended dapagliflozin and empagliflozin because these are the 2 SGLT-2 inhibitors that are licensed 98 of for use in this population. The committee noted that while the cardiovascular and renal protection provided by SGLT-2 inhibitors are retained at eGFR values below 30 ml/min/1.73 m 2, the glycaemic benefits may reduce or be absent.
