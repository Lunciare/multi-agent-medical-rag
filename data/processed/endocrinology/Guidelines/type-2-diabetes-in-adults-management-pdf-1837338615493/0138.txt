The use of more intensive earlier treatment may lead to a reduction in the use of insulin, which may offset costs in the long term. 112 of Return to recommendations Further treatment: people living with obesity Recommendations 1.29.1 to 1.29.4 Why the committee made the recommendations GLP-1 receptor agonists or tirzepatide The committee agreed GLP-1 receptor agonists and tirzepatide should be considered for people living with obesity because of the medicines' glycaemic reduction properties. GLP-1 receptor agonists also have the cardiovascular and renal benefits. These may also reduce weight, which may be an important side effect for the person with type 2 diabetes and their healthcare professionals. However, if weight reduction is the primary aim of the treatment, the committee agreed that guidance should be sought within NICE's guideline on overweight and obesity management instead of this guideline. Evidence showed that out of the GLP-1 receptor agonists, only liraglutide reached the cost-effectiveness threshold in the base-case analysis for people living with obesity.
