Return to recommendations Further treatment: people with type 2 diabetes and no relevant comorbidities Recommendation 1.25.1 Why the committee made the recommendations Most of the evidence used an additive strategy (where additional treatment was provided) rather than a switching strategy (where 1 treatment was stopped and another was started). This appeared to provide effective results. The committee agreed that this is 104 of likely to be effective for most people.
