The evidence evaluated for tirzepatide did not show cardiovascular benefits, which leaves some uncertainty about its use for this purpose. However, the committee acknowledged the benefits in reducing HbA1c and weight, and how this could lead to beneficial cardiovascular outcomes in the long term. The committee agreed that GLP-1 receptor agonists and tirzepatide should be considered in addition to metformin and SGLT-2 inhibitors, given the: • relatively small size of this group and • challenges in identifying appropriate data. The committee also made a recommendation for research on treatments for people with early onset diabetes. The committee noted potential serious side effects with GLP-1 receptor agonists and tirzepatide, and a potential for misuse. However, they did not make a recommendation for monitoring because it is covered by MHRA drug safety updates.
