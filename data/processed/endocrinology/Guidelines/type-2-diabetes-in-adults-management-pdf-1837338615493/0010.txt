when discussing HbA1c targets with patients. 1.5.5 Discuss and agree an individual HbA1c target with adults with type 2 diabetes (see recommendations 1.5.6 to 1.5.10). Encourage them to reach their target and maintain it, unless any resulting adverse effects (including hypoglycaemia), or their efforts to achieve their target impair their quality of life. Think about using the NICE patient decision aid on weighing up HbA1c targets to support these discussions.
