Insulin-based treatments Evidence showed that insulin-based treatments are less effective than most other antidiabetic therapies at reducing harm from adverse events because it can increase the risk of hypoglycaemic events and weight gain. There are 2 main scenarios in which insulin is an effective treatment: • managing acute hyperglycaemia • managing long-term worsening hyperglycaemia that does not respond to other treatments.
