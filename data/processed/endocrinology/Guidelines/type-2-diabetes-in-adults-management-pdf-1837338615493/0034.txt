For example, people who are following a very low carbohydrate or ketogenic diet may need to delay treatment until they have changed their diet. 1.21.3 Advise adults with type 2 diabetes who are taking an SGLT-2 inhibitor that a very low carbohydrate or ketogenic diet would increase their risk of DKA and so: • they should speak with their healthcare professional before starting such a diet, and • their SGLT-2 inhibitor treatment may need to be suspended for the duration of the diet. For a short explanation of why the committee made the 2022 recommendations and how they might affect practice, see the rationale and impact section on how to introduce medicines.
