It showed that, at list price, the acquisition cost of ertugliflozin is lower than that of canagliflozin, dapagliflozin and empagliflozin. The committee concluded that ertugliflozin, both as monotherapy and in dual therapy, meets the criteria for recommendation on the basis of a cost comparison, because: • the overall health benefits are similar to those of its comparators • the acquisition costs are similar to or lower than those of its comparators. Ertugliflozin is therefore recommended as monotherapy or dual therapy with metformin, as an option for treating type 2 diabetes in adults.
