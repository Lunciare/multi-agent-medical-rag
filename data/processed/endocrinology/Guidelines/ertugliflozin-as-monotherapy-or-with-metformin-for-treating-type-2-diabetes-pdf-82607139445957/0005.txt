Clinical effectiveness Ertugliflozin monotherapy is clinically effective compared with placebo 3.2 The company presented the results of the VERTIS-MONO trial. This compared ertugliflozin monotherapy at the licensed dosages (5 mg and 15 mg) against placebo in 461 people aged 18 years or older with inadequate glycaemic control (defined as HbA1c 7.0% to 10.5% [53 to 91 mmol/mol]) despite diet and exercise. The primary outcome was change in HbA1c (measured as change in least- squared means from baseline to week 26). Both doses of ertugliflozin showed a statistically significant improvement compared with placebo in the full analysis set, which included all randomised patients who took at least 1 dose of study medication and had at least 1 measurement of the outcome variable (at baseline or after baseline).
