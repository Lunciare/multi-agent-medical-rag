Measurement of serum 1,25 (OH)2D concentration during burosumab treatment is debated (14). A Consensus Statement recommended monitoring 1,25(OH)2D during burosumab treatment to assess the increment of this vitamin D metabolite (101), but its role in adjusting burosumab dose has not been documented. In the opinion of other authors, measurement of serum 1,25(OH)2D concentrations during burosumab adds cost without clear beneﬁt, so it is not recommended (14).
