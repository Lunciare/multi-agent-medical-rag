Serum phosphate concentration represents a main biochemical parameter to titrate the dose of burosumab. Dose adjustments should be continued until the target low-normal serum phosphate concentration is reached or no further increase is observed after dose escalation (101, 135). Likely, targeting fasting serum phosphate at the lower end of the normal reference range for age is the safest approach to reduce the risk of ectopic mineralization (101). Serial measurement of alkaline phosphatase activity may also have a pivotal role to assess the progression of disease (135).
