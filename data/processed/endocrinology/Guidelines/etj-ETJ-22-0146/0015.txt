The strength of each statement was scored as strong (S, a recommendation) or weak (W, a suggestion – not a recommendation), depending on the clinical significance and weight of opinion favoring the statement. Strong recommendations are clinically important best practice and should be applied to most patients in most circumstances. In contrast, weak statements should be considered by the clinician and will be applicable to best practice only to certain patients or under certain circumstances.
