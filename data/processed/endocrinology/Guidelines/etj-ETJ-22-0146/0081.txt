I-131 activity The question of I-131 activity selection, that is the ‘optimal’ dosage, is of ongoing relevance since very limited evidence is available in the literature. The expert panel questioned what the most optimal dose effect curve is of radioiodine with least side effects calculated by body weight/fixed activity or dosimetry; however, no literature was found (Appendix A [Q14], B). As a theoretical construct, the activity which delivers an absorbed dose of radiation is sufficient to destroy a lesion/metastasis while low side effects should be chosen. As a standard of care empirically derived, fixed activities are used, and therapy is repeated as deemed necessary.
