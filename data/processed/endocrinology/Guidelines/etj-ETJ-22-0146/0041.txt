Recommendation 3A: We recommend to undergo thyroid US to assess the risk of cancer in a thyroid nodule, based on multiple US characteristics. However, US alone cannot definitively distinguish a benign thyroid nodule from thyroid cancer. For this reason, in suspect nodules, FNB is recommended (2S) (Fig. 1).
