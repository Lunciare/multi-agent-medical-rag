Quality of evidence was scored as level 1: high (randomized controlled trial (RCT) evidence/ meta-analysis – high-quality evidence (⊕⊕⊕⊕)); level 2: moderate (intervention short of RCT or large observational studies –moderate-quality (⊕⊕⊕⊖)); level 3: low quality (case–control studies, case series – low quality (⊕⊕⊖⊖)); level 4: very low quality (case reports, expert opinion – very low quality (⊕⊖⊖⊖)) (9). If all expert panel members agreed on a recommendation of the 2015 ATA Pediatric Guideline (8), no specific search was performed. The grade of quality of evidence, as had been assigned by the ATA working group, was assumed. The statements based on recommendations of the 2015 ATA Pediatric Guideline are considered as ‘expert opinion’ (level 4). The expert panel members discussed the evidence and formulated statements based on the best available evidence and expert opinion (Table 1).
