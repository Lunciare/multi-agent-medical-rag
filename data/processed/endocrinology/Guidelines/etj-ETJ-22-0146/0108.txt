If no residual or recurrent disease is found, serum Tg on LT4 and serum TgAbs must be followed every 3–6 months (wait-and-see). In case of consistent rising Tg on LT4 or TgAbs, neck US is recommended, and a I-123 scan and/or FDG PET/CT may be considered to locate the origin of persistent/recurrent disease. The expert panel agreed to not define an exact value for high or rising Tg, as this is dependent on the assay and the course over time. The definition of consistent rising Tg on LT4 is debatable; the levels of Tg as well as the doubling time should be taken into account and weighted in the individual patient. Considering the excellent outcome of childhood DTC and the risk for adverse late effects of I-131, empiric treatment may only be considered after the abovementioned diagnostic modalities have failed to identify a source of rising Tg on LT4 or rising TgAbs.
