However, the TNM staging system comes with limitations regarding the assessment of the prognosis of DTC in pediatric patients: only two disease stages can be identified (stage I: no distant metastasis; stage II: distant metastasis), since all pediatric patients are <45 years of age and secondly, the disease-specific mortality is extremely low. Despite this, the TNM staging system seems to be the most preferred system to be used for staging pediatric DTC. Recommendation 13A: We recommend that post-operative staging is done using the surgical report, histological report, measurement of Tg, and I-131 post-therapy scintigraphy (4S). Suggestion 13B: We suggest that the AJCC TNM classification system should be used to describe the extent of disease in pediatric DTC (4W). C6. I-131 therapy I-131 therapy for benign and malignant thyroid disease has been successfully used for 80 years. Over this time, it has been applied extensively in adult and in pediatric patients alike and has contributed to a normalization of life expectancy in DTC.
