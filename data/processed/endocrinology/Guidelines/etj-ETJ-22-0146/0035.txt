We suggest that all pediatric DTC patients receive additional calcium and vitamin D supplementation therapy for optimal bone mineralization during follow-up (4W). We suggest that all patients with pediatric DTC should be offered psychosocial support (4W). We suggest that future studies should further evaluate the prevalence and clinical significance of diastolic dysfunction in survivors of pediatric DTC after prolonged TSH suppressive therapy (4W). [D9] Follow-up scheme and transition to adult care We suggest to continue follow-up of children with DTC for at least 10 years; thereafter, the follow-up strategy should be the result of shared decision-making between the physician and the patient (4W).
