A second cornerstone of preparation for I-131 treatment generally consists of a low-iodine diet for a period of 1–2 weeks before administration; however, a recent study showed that a 4-day low-iodine diet seems to be sufficient in areas with iodine-sufficient intake (40, 114, 115). Although in use for many years, its effect on therapy outcome is not uniformly accepted; here, local iodine sufficiency status might also play a role. Although its merit is still subject to some scientific debate, a low- iodine diet before I-131 therapy may favorably influence the uptake of I-131 in DTC during therapy and will do no harm. Therefore, the expert panel agreed to recommend a low-iodine diet for at least 4 days before the administration of I-131 treatment (115). Recommendation 16A: We recommend that TSH stimulation (>30 mI/L) is induced before I-131 therapy in order to facilitate I-131 uptake (4S).
