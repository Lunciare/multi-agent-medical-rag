What is the difference in outcome of DTC in children treated with a (prophylactic) central lymph node dissection vs no central lymph node dissection? Conclusion Conflicting results were found. One study suggests that an aggressive surgical approach may both simultaneously decrease the risk of recurrence and improve prognostication in patients with more advanced or aggressive disease. Another study showed no difference in recurrence-free survival between patients treated with LND compared to limited node excision of no LND. However, location of LND was not specified. It remains unclear if these patients underwent prophylactic central lymph node dissection. ⊕⊖⊖⊖ 13. Is outcome of microcarcinoma worse in children treated with I-131 vs those not treated with I-131?
