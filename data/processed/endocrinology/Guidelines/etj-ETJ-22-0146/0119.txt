However, calcium–vitamin D3 medication had a beneficial effect on BMD. TSH- suppressive therapy does not seem to affect BMD in women treated for DTC at young age, at least after 10 years of follow-up. Only one study was found that reported on female fertility in survivors of pediatric DTC (172). In this study, neither major abnormalities in reproductive characteristics nor in predictors of ovarian failure in female survivors of DTC, who received I-131 treatment during childhood, were found. In conclusion, hypoparathyroidism, cardiac dysfunction, and salivary gland dysfunction occur in 23.8, 21.2, and 1.9-47.6% respectively. No significant effect of treatment for pediatric DTC was found on overall quality of life, bone mineral density, or female fertility. Suggestion 28A: We suggest counseling pediatric DTC patients about the risk of developing recurrent laryngeal nerve injury or hypoparathyroidism after thyroid surgery and salivary gland dysfunction after exposure to I-131.
