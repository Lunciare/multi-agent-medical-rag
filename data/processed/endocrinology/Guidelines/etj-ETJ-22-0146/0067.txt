Surgical approach for co-incidentally found, very small DTC (exception) Due to the fact that the diameter of thyroid cancer is not related to the presence of cervical lymph node metastases (87), total thyroidectomy is recommended in all children with DTC regardless of the size of the nodule. However, as mentioned above, considering the excellent prognosis of DTC, the expert panel questioned whether children with very small lesions (found coincidentally) with no clinical signs could be treated differently (Appendix A [Q11]). A literature search was performed. No studies were found to evaluate differences in outcome between patients with DTC < 1 cm (also defined in literature as thyroid microcarcinoma (TMC)) treated with total thyroidectomy vs hemithyroidectomy or subtotal thyroidectomy.
