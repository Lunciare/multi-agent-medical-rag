In SURPASS-2, -3 and -5, people had to have HbA1c levels of 53 mmol/mol (7.0%) or more to 91 mmol/mol (10.5%) or less. People also had to have had a stable weight for 3 months, and a BMI of 25 kg/m 2 or more in SURPASS-2, -3 and -4, and a BMI of 23 kg/m 2 or more in SURPASS-5.
