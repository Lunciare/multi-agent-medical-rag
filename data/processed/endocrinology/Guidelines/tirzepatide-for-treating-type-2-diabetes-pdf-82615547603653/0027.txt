After consultation, the company provided 2 scenario analyses using lower baseline utilities for people with type 2 diabetes: 0.785 from Clarke et al. (2002) and 0.772 from Redenz et al. The EAG noted that both scenario analyses resulted in cost-effectiveness estimates slightly lower than the company's base-case results. The committee concluded that it preferred to use the lower baseline utility value identified by the EAG. But it acknowledged that changing the value had limited impact on the cost-effectiveness estimates.
