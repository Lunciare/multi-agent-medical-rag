Clinical-effectiveness evidence: SURMOUNT trials 3.6 In its second meeting, the committee considered whether SURMOUNT-2 and the SURMOUNT-CN trials should have been included in the company's submission. The company noted that the SURMOUNT trials focused on a different indication (weight loss) and were not relevant to this appraisal. Only SURMOUNT-2 included people with type 2 diabetes, but it would not have been included in the company's network meta-analysis (NMA) for the current appraisal. This was because the definition of background therapies allowed was not directly relevant to the current appraisal's decision problem. The EAG noted substantial differences between SURMOUNT-2 and the SURPASS-2, -3, -4 and -5 trials, and that direct comparison was not advisable.
