BMI is weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared. 1.3 Indications for nutrition support in hospital and the community 1.3.1 Nutrition support should be considered in people who are malnourished, as defined by any of the following: • a BMI of less than 18.5 kg/m • unintentional weight loss greater than 10% within the last 3 to 6 months • a BMI of less than 20 kg/m 2 and unintentional weight loss greater than 5% within the last 3 to 6 months. 1.3.2 Nutrition support should be considered in people at risk of malnutrition who, as defined by any of the following: • have eaten little or nothing for more than 5 days and/or are likely to eat little Page 10 of or nothing for the next 5 days or longer • have a poor absorptive capacity, and/or have high nutrient losses and/or have increased nutritional needs from causes such as catabolism. 1.3.3 Healthcare professionals should consider using oral, enteral or parenteral nutrition support, alone or in combination, for people who are either malnourished or at risk of malnutrition, as defined in recommendations 1.3.1 and 1.3.2.
