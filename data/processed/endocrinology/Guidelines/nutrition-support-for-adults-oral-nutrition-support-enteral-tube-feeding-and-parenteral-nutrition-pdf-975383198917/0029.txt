Route of access 1.7.6 People in general medical, surgical and intensive care wards who meet the criteria in recommendation 1.7.1 should be fed via a tube into the stomach unless there is upper gastrointestinal dysfunction. 1.7.7 People who meet the criteria in recommendation 1.7.1, with upper gastrointestinal dysfunction (or an inaccessible upper gastrointestinal tract) should be considered for post-pyloric (duodenal or jejunal) feeding. 1.7.8 Gastrostomy feeding should be considered in people likely to need long-term (4 weeks or more) enteral tube feeding. 1.7.9 Percutaneous endoscopic gastrostomy (PEG) tubes that have been placed without apparent complications can be used for enteral tube feeding 4 hours after insertion.
