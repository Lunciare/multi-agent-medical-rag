Page 6 of Key organisational priorities • All healthcare professionals who are directly involved in patient care should receive education and training, relevant to their post, on the importance of providing adequate nutrition. • Healthcare professionals should ensure that all people who need nutrition support receive coordinated care from a multidisciplinary team. The composition of this team may differ according to setting and local arrangements. • All acute hospital trusts should employ at least 1 specialist nutrition support nurse. • All hospital trusts should have a nutrition steering committee working within the clinical governance framework.
