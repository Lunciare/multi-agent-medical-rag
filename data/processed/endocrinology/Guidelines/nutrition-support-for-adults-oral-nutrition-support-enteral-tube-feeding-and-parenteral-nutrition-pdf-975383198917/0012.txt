It should be started at no more than 50% of the estimated target energy and protein needs. It should be built up to meet full needs over the first 24 to 48 hours according to metabolic and gastrointestinal tolerance. Full requirements of fluid, electrolytes, vitamins and minerals should be provided from the outset of feeding. 1.4.5 People who have eaten little or nothing for more than 5 days should have nutrition support introduced at no more than 50% of requirements for the first monitoring reveals no refeeding problems. 1.4.6 People who meet the criteria in box 1 should be considered to be at high risk of developing refeeding problems.
