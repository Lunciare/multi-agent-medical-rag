Offer a renal assessment for women with pre-existing diabetes, if they have not had 1 in the last 3 months. Arrange contact with the joint diabetes and antenatal clinic every 1 to 2 weeks throughout pregnancy for all women with diabetes. Measure HbA1c levels for women with pre-existing diabetes to determine the level of risk for the pregnancy. Offer self-monitoring of blood glucose or a 75-g 2-hour oral glucose tolerance test (OGTT) as soon as possible for women with previous gestational diabetes who book in the first trimester. Confirm the viability of the pregnancy and gestational age at 7 to 9 weeks.
