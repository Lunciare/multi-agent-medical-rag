Target blood glucose and HbA1c levels before pregnancy 1.1.16 Agree individualised targets for self-monitoring of blood glucose with women who have diabetes and are planning a pregnancy, taking into account the risk of hypoglycaemia. 1.1.17 Advise women with type 1 diabetes who are planning a pregnancy to aim for the normal capillary plasma glucose target ranges: • a fasting plasma glucose level of 5 mmol/litre to 7 mmol/litre on waking and • a plasma glucose level of 4 mmol/litre to 7 mmol/litre before meals at other times of the day. For more information, see the section on blood glucose targets in the NICE guideline on type 1 diabetes in adults.
