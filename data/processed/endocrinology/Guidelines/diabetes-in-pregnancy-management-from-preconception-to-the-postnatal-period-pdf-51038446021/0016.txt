Target blood glucose levels 1.3.4 Agree individualised targets for self-monitoring of blood glucose with pregnant women with diabetes, taking into account the risk of hypoglycaemia. 1.3.5 Advise pregnant women with any form of diabetes to maintain their capillary plasma glucose below the following target levels, if these are achievable without causing problematic hypoglycaemia: • fasting: 5.3 mmol/litre and • 1 hour after meals: 7.8 mmol/litre or • 2 hours after meals: 6.4 mmol/litre. 1.3.6 Advise pregnant women with diabetes who are taking insulin to maintain their capillary plasma glucose level above 4 mmol/litre.
