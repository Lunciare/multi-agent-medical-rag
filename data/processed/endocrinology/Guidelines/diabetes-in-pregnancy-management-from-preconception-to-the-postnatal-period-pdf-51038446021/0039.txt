UK population studies are needed to establish the incidence of glucose intolerance in women in the first trimester. Why this is important It is vital for normal fetal development in the first trimester that women with pre-existing diabetes achieve good blood glucose control both before and during pregnancy. Good control also helps to prevent macrosomia and other complications in the third trimester in women with pre-existing or gestational diabetes.
