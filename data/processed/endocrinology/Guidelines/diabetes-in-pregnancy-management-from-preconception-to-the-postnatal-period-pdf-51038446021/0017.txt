Managing diabetes during pregnancy Insulin treatment and risks of hypoglycaemia A 2020 Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency drug safety update highlights the need to rotate insulin injection sites within the same body area to avoid cutaneous amyloidosis. 1.3.12 Consider rapid-acting insulin analogues (aspart and lispro) for pregnant women with diabetes.
