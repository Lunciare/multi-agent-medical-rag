Diabetes in pregnancy:
KEYWORDS: diabetes, women, glucose, pregnancy, blood, women diabetes, blood glucose, gestational, gestational diabetes, offer, care, type, type diabetes, insulin, risk

1.1.23 Use isophane insulin (also known as NPH insulin) as the first choice for long-acting insulin during pregnancy. Consider continuing treatment with long-acting insulin analogues (insulin detemir or insulin glargine) for women with diabetes who have established good blood glucose control before pregnancy. Levemir insulin (insulin detemir), in both its Penfill and Flexpen presentations, is to be discontinued, with an anticipated supply end date of December 2026. A Medicine Supply Notification (MSN/2025/036U) has been issued about the discontinuation. For information on alternative basal insulins, see the Association of British Clinical Diabetologists and the Primary Care Diabetes & Obesity Society guidance on discontinuation of Levemir (insulin detemir). NICE is assessing the impact of the discontinuation of insulin detemir on recommendations in this guideline.
