Safety of medicines for diabetes before and during pregnancy 1.1.21 Women with diabetes may be advised to use metformin as an adjunct or alternative to insulin in the preconception period and during pregnancy, when the likely benefits from improved blood glucose control outweigh the potential for harm. Stop all other oral blood glucose-lowering agents before pregnancy, and use insulin instead. 1.1.22 Be aware that the available evidence on rapid-acting insulin analogues (aspart and lispro) does not show an adverse effect on the pregnancy or the health of baby. 1.1.23 Use isophane insulin (also known as NPH insulin) as the first choice for long-acting insulin during pregnancy.
