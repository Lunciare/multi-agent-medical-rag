At the booking appointment, check for the following risk factors: • BMI of 30 kg/m 2 or over • previous macrosomic baby weighing 4.5 kg or more • previous gestational diabetes • family history of diabetes (first-degree relative with diabetes) • an ethnicity with a high prevalence of diabetes. Offer women with any of these risk factors testing for gestational diabetes (see recommendations 1.2.5 to 1.2.7).
