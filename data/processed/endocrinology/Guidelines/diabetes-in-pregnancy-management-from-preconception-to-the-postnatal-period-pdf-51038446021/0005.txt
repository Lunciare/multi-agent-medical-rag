individualised dietary advice to women with diabetes who are planning a pregnancy. 1.1.10 For women with diabetes who are planning a pregnancy and who have a body mass index (BMI) above 27 kg/m 2, offer advice on how to lose weight, in line with the NICE guideline on overweight and obesity management (this includes guidance on BMI and using variations on the BMI cut-off, based on the risk for different ethnic groups). Page 7 of 1.1.11 Advise women with diabetes who are planning a pregnancy to take folic acid (5 mg/day) until 12 weeks of gestation to reduce the risk of having a baby with a neural tube defect. Monitoring blood glucose and ketones before pregnancy 1.1.12 Offer up to monthly measurement of HbA1c levels for women with diabetes who are planning a pregnancy. [2008, amended 2020] 1.1.13 Offer blood glucose meters for self-monitoring to women with diabetes who are planning a pregnancy. 1.1.14 If a woman with diabetes who is planning a pregnancy needs to intensify blood glucose-lowering therapy, advise her to monitor her blood glucose more often, to include fasting levels and a mixture of pre-meal and post-meal levels. 1.1.15 Offer blood ketone testing strips and a meter
