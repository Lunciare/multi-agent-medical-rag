Risk factors and risk markers for GDM include age (the older a woman of reproductive age is, the higher her risk of GDM); overweight or obesity; excessive weight gain during pregnancy; a family history of diabetes; GDM during a previous pregnancy; a history of stillbirth or giving birth to an infant with congenital abnormality; and excess glucose in urine during pregnancy (24). Diabetes in pregnancy and GDM increase the risk of future obesity and type 2 diabetes in offspring.
