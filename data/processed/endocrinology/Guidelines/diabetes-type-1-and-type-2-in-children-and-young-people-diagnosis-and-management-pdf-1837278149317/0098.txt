Given the relatively small number of children and young people with type 2 diabetes in England and Wales (1,560 in all NHS settings as of 2019/20, according to the NHS Digital report on Young People with type 2 diabetes, 2019-20), the provision of equipment for both capillary blood glucose monitoring and insulin is not expected to have a significant resource impact.
