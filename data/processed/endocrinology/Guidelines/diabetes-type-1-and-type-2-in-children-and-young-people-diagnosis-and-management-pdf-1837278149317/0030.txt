Surgery 1.2.100 Only offer surgery in centres that have dedicated paediatric facilities for children and young people with diabetes. 1.2.101 All centres caring for children and young people with type 1 diabetes should have written protocols on safe surgery for children and young people. The protocols should be agreed between surgical and anaesthetic staff and the diabetes team. 1.2.102 Surgical, anaesthetic and diabetes teams should discuss care for children and young people with type 1 diabetes before they are admitted to hospital for elective surgery, and as soon as possible after they are admitted for emergency Page 25 of surgery.
