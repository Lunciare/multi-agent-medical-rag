Take into account factors such as their daily activities, individual life goals, complications, comorbidities and the risk of hypoglycaemia. 1.2.79 Support children and young people with type 1 diabetes and their families or carers to safely achieve and maintain their individual agreed HbA1c target level. 1.2.80 Measure HbA1c level 4 times a year in children and young people with type 1 diabetes. Think about more frequent testing if they are having difficulty with blood glucose management.
