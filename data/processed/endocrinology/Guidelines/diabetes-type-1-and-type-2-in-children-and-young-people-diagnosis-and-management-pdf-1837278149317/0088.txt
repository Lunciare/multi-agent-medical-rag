They therefore agreed that isCGM should be who would prefer isCGM. Children and young people who prefer isCGM are likely to have better adherence with this type of device, so it would provide more benefit. The recommendation limits isCGM to children aged 4 and over because no isCGM devices are licensed in children under 4. The committee did not make a recommendation on using specific devices because CGM technologies are changing very quickly and this recommendation would soon be out of date. Local healthcare services are better placed to assess which devices are evidence- based and suitable for use at any given time.
