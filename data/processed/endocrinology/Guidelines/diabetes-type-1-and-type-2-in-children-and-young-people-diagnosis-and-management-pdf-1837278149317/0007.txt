Immunisation 1.2.16 Explain to children and young people with type 1 diabetes and their families or carers that the UK Health Security Agency Green Book recommends they have: • annual immunisation against influenza, starting when they are 6 months old • immunisation against pneumococcal infection if they are taking insulin or oral hypoglycaemic medicines.
