The committee used their expertise and their understanding of the evidence on the pathophysiology of DKA to update the recommendation. They added more detail about how to care for children and young people with anuria or potassium levels above the normal range. It is essential to not delay adding potassium to fluids because insulin can cause hypokalaemia in this population, which can lead to cardiac arrhythmias and death.
