[2004, amended 2015] 1.3.68 Offer children and young people with type 2 diabetes and their families or carers timely and ongoing access to mental health professionals with an understanding of diabetes. This is because they may experience psychological problems (such as anxiety, depression, behavioural and conduct disorders, and family conflict) or psychosocial difficulties that can impact on the management of diabetes and wellbeing.
