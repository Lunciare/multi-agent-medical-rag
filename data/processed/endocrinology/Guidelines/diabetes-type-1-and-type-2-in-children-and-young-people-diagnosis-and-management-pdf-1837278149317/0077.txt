Monitoring during therapy 1.4.46 Monitor and record the following at least hourly in children and young people with DKA: • capillary blood glucose • heart rate, blood pressure, temperature, respiratory rate (look for Kussmaul breathing) • fluid balance, with fluid input and output charts • level of consciousness (using the modified Glasgow Coma Scale).
