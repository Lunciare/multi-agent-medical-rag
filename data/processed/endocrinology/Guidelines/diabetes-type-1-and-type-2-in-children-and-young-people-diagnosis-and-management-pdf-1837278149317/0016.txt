Blood glucose and HbA1c targets and monitoring Blood glucose targets 1.2.55 Explain to children and young people with type 1 diabetes and their families or carers that the optimal target ranges for short-term plasma glucose management are: • fasting plasma glucose level of 4 mmol/litre to 7 mmol/litre on waking • a plasma glucose level of 4 mmol/litre to 7 mmol/litre before meals at other times of the day • a plasma glucose level of 5 mmol/litre to 9 mmol/litre after meals • a plasma glucose level of at least 5 mmol/litre when driving (see the Driver and Vehicle Licensing Agency [DVLA] guidance for people with diabetes for further details about driving). 1.2.56 Explain to children and young people with type 1 diabetes and their families or carers that maintaining blood glucose levels at the lower end of the target ranges will help them achieve the lowest possible HbA1c. 1.2.57 If children and young people with type 1 diabetes experience problematic hypoglycaemia or undue emotional distress while attempting to
