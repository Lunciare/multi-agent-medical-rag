Return to recommendations Continuous glucose monitoring Recommendations 1.3.38 to 1.3.47 Why the committee made the recommendations CGM is already recommended for everyone with type 1 diabetes and in some adults with type 2 diabetes, and the committee agreed that children and young people with type 2 Page 78 of diabetes should be offered the same.
