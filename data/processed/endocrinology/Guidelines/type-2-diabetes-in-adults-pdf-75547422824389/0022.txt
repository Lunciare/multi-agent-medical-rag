Integrated care systems ensure that services offer intermittently scanned CGM (isCGM), or real-time CGM (rtCGM) if it is available for the same or lower cost as isCGM, to adults with insulin-treated type 2 diabetes who would need help from a care worker or healthcare professional to monitor their blood glucose. They should also address inequalities in CGM access and uptake by commissioning services that monitor who is using CGM, identify groups who are eligible but who have a lower uptake and make plans to engage with these groups to encourage them to consider CGM.
