The use of CGM by adults with type 2 diabetes who need help to monitor their blood glucose and administer insulin injections will help care workers to record blood glucose levels quickly. For people who have multiple visits per day, blood glucose levels can be recorded at each visit. This can be used to adjust their insulin levels to reduce the risk of hypoglycaemic events between home visits and may also reduce the number of hospital admissions. Quality measures The following measures can be used to assess the quality of care or service provision specified in the statement. They are examples of how the statement can be measured, and can be adapted and used flexibly. The quality measures focus on use of CGM by adults who need help from a care worker or healthcare professional to monitor their blood glucose. The measures aim to reduce health inequalities in this group. Local services may want to identify other groups to focus measures on, for example, by reporting data for other groups that need help to monitor their blood glucose, by age or indices of deprivation.
