Healthcare professionals (members of the foot care service, nurses, doctors and podiatrists) are trained to provide foot assessments to adults with type 2 diabetes when they are admitted to hospital. They can refer to and work with the multidisciplinary foot protection service if any foot problems are identified on assessment. Integrated care systems ensure that multidisciplinary foot care services and foot protection services are available to provide management of foot problems including high- risk and active foot problems identified from foot assessments for adults with type 2 diabetes when they are admitted to hospital. Adults with type 2 diabetes who are admitted to hospital have a check of their feet for any problems that may be related to their diabetes. Source guidance Diabetic foot problems: prevention and management.
