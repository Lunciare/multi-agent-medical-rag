NICE guideline NG28 (2015, updated 2026), recommendations 1.7.2 and 1.7.3 Definitions of terms used in this quality statement Continuous glucose monitoring A CGM is a device that measures blood glucose levels and sends the readings to a display device or smartphone. rtCGM and isCGM, commonly referred to as 'flash', automatically measure glucose levels through a sensor applied under the skin. This allows patterns of glucose levels to be seen, which can be used to plan insulin treatment. rtCGM can be considered as an alternative to isCGM if it is available for the same or lower cost.
