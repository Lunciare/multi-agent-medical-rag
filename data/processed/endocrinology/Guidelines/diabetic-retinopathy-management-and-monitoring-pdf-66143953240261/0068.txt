The committee discussed how, ideally, there would be a list of biomarkers that can be Page 53 of used to define responsiveness to anti-VEGF therapy to help determine whether to continue, switch or stop treatment. Therefore, they made a recommendation for research on the effectiveness of clinical features or factors that suggest treatment for diabetic macular oedema should be switched or stopped. Although the committee did not think they could recommend a specific switching criteria, they thought it important to highlight when a decision about switching or changing treatments should be made.
