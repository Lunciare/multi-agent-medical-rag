Full details of the evidence and the committee's discussion are in evidence review J: effectiveness of different monitoring frequencies. 1.5 Proliferative diabetic retinopathy Treatment strategies for proliferative diabetic retinopathy 1.5.1 Discuss with the person with proliferative diabetic retinopathy the benefits and potential side effects of each of the following 3 options: panretinal photocoagulation, anti-vascular endothelial growth factor medicines (anti- VEGFs), and no treatment (observation). As part of this discussion, explain: • what proliferative diabetic retinopathy is, and whether they have high-risk Page 11 of characteristics • that panretinal photocoagulation is usually the first treatment for most people with proliferative diabetic retinopathy • which treatment is likely to work best for them.
