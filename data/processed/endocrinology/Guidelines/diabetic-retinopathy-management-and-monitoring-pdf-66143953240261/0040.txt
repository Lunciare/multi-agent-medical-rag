The committee thought it was important to identify prognostic factors. Identifying people who are at risk of progression will mean their condition can be closely monitored and they can receive early treatment to avoid or reduce the complications associated with progression. The committee therefore made a recommendation for research aimed at identifying other prognostic factors.
