With no evidence on timing of vitrectomy for this group, the committee did not think they could specify when this should be done. However, they said this should be done early enough after a person's condition shows no response to anti-VEGF treatment to ensure that the eye does not incur any permanent damage. Although there is limited evidence for people with vitreoretinal traction or epiretinal membrane secondary to diabetic macular oedema, the committee did not make a recommendation for research on this topic because the small number of people in the group has made it hard to meet targets for trial recruitment in the past (see evidence review F). How the recommendations might affect practice These recommendations are in line with current practice and so should not have any resource impact on the NHS.
