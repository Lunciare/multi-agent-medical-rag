Therefore, as this recommendation is for a population not included in NICE technology appraisal guidance on anti-VEGFs, the mandatory funding status does not apply to this recommendation. Macular laser was recommended as an alternative treatment option because the evidence and committee's experience indicated that this can also be effective and is current practice for many people in this group. It also has the benefit of delaying the need for anti- VEGF treatment for some people.
