(Good clinical practice) • R 2.3 We recommend consideration of glucocorticoid withdrawal syndrome that may occur during glucocortic­ oid taper. When glucocorticoid withdrawal syndrome is severe, glucocorticoid dose can be temporarily increased to the most recent one that was tolerated, and the dur­ ation of glucocorticoid taper could be increased. (Good clinical practice) • R 2.4 We recommend against routine testing for adrenal insufficiency in patients on supraphysiologic doses of glu­ cocorticoids, or if they are still in need of glucocorticoid treatment for the underlying disease.
