General remarks: Patients with glucocorticoid-induced adrenal insufficiency may be asymptomatic at baseline conditions but can develop symptoms – from mild to life-threatening adrenal crisis – when exposed to potential triggers (see Table 9). When present, symptoms of adrenal insufficiency are often non-specific and can overlap with those of the disease for which glucocorticoids are prescribed. Recurrence of underlying autoimmune diseases can occur during tapering of exogenous glucocorticoids. Signs and symptoms of adrenal insufficiency can overlap with those of glucocorticoid withdrawal syndrome, which arises from the discontinuation of rapid tapering of glucocorticoid therapy in patients who developed a tolerance to supraphysiologic glucocorticoid levels.
