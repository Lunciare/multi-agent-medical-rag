(Good clinical practice) Rationale Glucocorticoid withdrawal syndrome occurs due to depend­ ence on supraphysiologic glucocorticoids while decreasing the dose of glucocorticoids64–66. Patients should be informed that glucocorticoid withdrawal symptoms are expected to occur during the glucocorticoid dose reduction and what the differ­ ences are between glucocorticoid withdrawal syndrome, ad­ renal insufficiency, and underlying disease flare. It should be emphasized that an insufficient glucocorticoid supply is not ex­ pected to occur when the glucocorticoid dose is greater than the physiologic daily dose equivalent.
