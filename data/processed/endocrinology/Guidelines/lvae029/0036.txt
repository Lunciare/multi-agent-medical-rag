General recommendations for glucocorticoid therapy of non-endocrine conditions and recommendations regarding patient education • R 1.1 We recommend that, in general, patients on, or ta­ pering off glucocorticoids for non-endocrine conditions do not need to be evaluated by an endocrinology specialist.
