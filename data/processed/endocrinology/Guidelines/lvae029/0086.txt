Some data suggest that the risk is low considering the relatively small number of hospital admissions for adrenal crisis recorded in patients on long-term glucocorticoids.124 The preserved aldosterone production and some residual corti­ sol production in glucocorticoid-induced adrenal insufficiency may explain these observations. One study found a higher inci­ dence of adrenal crisis – precipitated by infections in about half of cases – in patients with established glucocorticoid-induced adrenal insufficiency compared to other causes of adrenal insuf­ ficiency,22 but this was not observed in other studies.21 A significant proportion of patients with glucocorticoid- induced adrenal insufficiency may be undiagnosed; as such, adrenal insufficiency symptoms and adrenal crisis can be missed.
