Although glucocorticoid dose and treat­ ment duration are associated with the development of adrenal insufficiency, predicting the risk of adrenal insuffi­ ciency remains challenging. A uniform approach to tapering the glucocorticoid dose has not yet been established and there is a lack of sufficient data on this topic (see Clinical question II). While some authors recommend a rapid reduction of the glucocorticoid dose to slightly above physiologic daily dose equivalent (e.g. 7.5 mg prednisone), followed by a further re­ duction in smaller steps, others prefer testing of HPA axis to guide further tapering or immediate discontinuation, if nor­ mal adrenocortical function is demonstrated.
