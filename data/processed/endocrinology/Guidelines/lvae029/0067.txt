(⊕○○○) Rationale Morning cortisol measurement can serve as a simple ap­ proach to HPA axis assessment, obviating the need for other tests in many patients (see recommendation 2.7)94–96. However, if cortisol remains indeterminate (see 2.7), dynamic testing can be considered. The decision to carry out dynamic testing should consider the test’s availability, feasibility, costs and regional accessibility. There is no evidence that a specific test in the context of glucocorticoid treatment is superior. Dynamic testing options include 250 µg ACTH (1-24) and, less commonly, overnight metyrapone97 and insulin tolerance tests. The 250µg ACTH (1-24) test only examines the direct re­ sponse of the adrenal gland to supraphysiologic ACTH stimu­ lation.
