Patients with very low morning cortisol levels (as a guide: < 150 nmol/L (5μg/dL)) are very likely to have persistent adrenal insufficiency.83 In such cases, dynamic testing is unlikely to be useful. We recommend that these patients continue with physiologic daily dose equivalent glucocorticoid replacement aiming for the lowest safe dose and undergo repeat morning cor­ tisol testing until recovery occurs. As a general guide, the gluco­ corticoid dose should provide sufficient replacement, but also a sufficient stimulus for recovery (meaning avoiding any over- replacement). Frequency of repeat measuring may range be­ tween 1 to 6 months, depending on the dose and length of gluco­ corticoid therapy and the prior trajectory of cortisol values.
