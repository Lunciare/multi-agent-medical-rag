Management of non-attendance Transition is a high-risk period for non-attendance in care. To prevent patients dropping out, we recommend educating both patients and their parents within paediatric care, on the signifi­ cance of ongoing care and the risks of care discontinuation. We suggest that paediatric professionals and/or the transition coordinator should facilitate this continuity by scheduling and confirming attendance at the first appointment in adult care. Subsequently, adult care providers and/or the transition coord­ inator should monitor attendance at the following appointments until the transition process is fully completed. We suggest that paediatric and adult health care providers in­ clude in their transition programme methods to secure information on follow-up in adult care, such as interviews, questionnaires, and automated appointment reminders to enhance attendance and to identify any barrier. G7 While we recommend these core tracking methods, the specif­ ic tools and protocols may be adapted locally based on available resources and healthcare system infrastructure.
