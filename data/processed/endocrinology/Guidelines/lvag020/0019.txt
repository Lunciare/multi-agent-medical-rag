Multidisciplinary team organization We recommend adopting a comprehensive, holistic, coordi­ nated, and team-based approach to patient care. This approach should establish clearly defined roles for all providers and ensure regular communication and collaboration both within and be­ tween paediatric and adult care providers. We recommend assigning a dedicated transition coordinator mediates organization and communication among stakeholders, and maintains a mutually trusting relationship with the patient. At the relevant time, this multidisciplinary team should ideally in­ clude fertility specialists (gynaecologists and/or andrologists) and, who can provide early counselling about reproductive options and risks.
