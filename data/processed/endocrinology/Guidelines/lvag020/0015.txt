We suggest that healthcare professionals engage in specialized training in adolescent and young adult care, create welcoming care environments, identify a transition coordinator for each patient, establish accessible communication channels with patients, and im­ plement digital health solutions including patient communication apps and telemedicine platforms. Additionally, programmes should incorporate feedback mechanisms from patients and families.
