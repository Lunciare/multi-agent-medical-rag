Synthesis of recommendations The 351 extracted recommendations were thematically classified by 5 core group members (E.L., S.N., K.S., M.S., and S.C.) working in pairs. When multiple articles provided overlapping recommen­ dations, we synthesized them into unified statements while pre­ serving nuanced differences and ensuring comprehensive G3 coverage. Through virtual meetings the entire core group re­ viewed the statements, and divided them into 11 domains, here­ by creating a draft guidance. Expert panel selection and focus groups The draft guidance was presented to expert focus groups for val­ idation and refinement. Healthcare professionals with expertise in transition care, research, and/or policy development were re­ cruited through ESE and ESPE networks. The expert panel in­ cluded 18 healthcare professionals from 10 European countries (France, Italy, Lithuania, Denmark, Belgium, The Netherlands, Sweden, Ireland, Greece, and Germany), representing diverse per­ spectives in endocrine care transition. The panel included both paediatric (n = 5) and adult (n = 8) endocrinologists, nurses (n = had extensive experience in transition care, with several leading dedicated transition clinics at their institutions.
