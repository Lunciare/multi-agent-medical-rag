Setmelanotide for treating obesity and hyperphagia in Bardet-Biedl syndrome (HST31) Page 15 of 41 At the second committee meeting, the company explained that, although its response rates used data from week 52 of RM-493-023, response to setmelanotide would be assessed earlier in clinical practice. This was because there was a delay between response in hyperphagia and weight. The clinical experts explained that changes in hyperphagia indicating a treatment response would be evident after 14 weeks. This was based on data from RM-493-023, in which a marked decrease in hunger scores could be seen by 14 weeks of setmelanotide in people whose condition responded to treatment. The company stated that people without a hyperphagia response within the first 3 months were unlikely to have a change in eating habits to allow a reduction in BMI after 52 weeks of treatment.
