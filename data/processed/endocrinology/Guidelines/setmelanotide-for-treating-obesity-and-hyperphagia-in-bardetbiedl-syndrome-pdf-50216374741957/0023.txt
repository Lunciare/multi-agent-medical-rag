Economic model Company's modelling approach 3.13 The company developed a lifetime model based on UK life tables to estimate the cost effectiveness of setmelanotide. Health states in the model included 7 BMI-Z classes (0 to 1, over 1 to 2, 2 to 4 in increments of 0.5 and over 4) for children and young people, 7 BMI classes (25 to 50 in increments of 5 and over 50) for adults, and death.
