See the committee papers for full details of the evidence. The condition Bardet-Biedl syndrome (BBS) 3.1 BBS is a rare genetic disorder that results in obesity. It is caused by mutations in 1 or more of the BBS genes, of which 22 have been identified to date. These genes are involved in signalling through the melanocortin-4 receptor (MC4R) neuroendocrine system in the hypothalamus. This system regulates hunger, satiety (a feeling of fullness) and energy expenditure.
