Modelling treatment effect on hyperphagia 3.17 At 14 weeks, the company assumed that all people whose condition responded to setmelanotide transitioned to mild hyperphagia. This was regardless of their hyperphagia severity when they started in the model and independent of change in BMI-Z or BMI. It was based on feedback from people who met the primary outcome in RM-493-023.
