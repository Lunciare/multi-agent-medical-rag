Cost-effectiveness estimates Company and EAG cost-effectiveness estimates 3.25 The company's base case showed that setmelanotide was associated with an ICER of £169,658 per QALY gained compared with best supportive care. This was in a mixed population of children (60%) and adults (40%; see section 3.14), and assuming 84% of people with BBS would have severe hyperphagia and 16% moderate hyperphagia at baseline (see section 3.15).
