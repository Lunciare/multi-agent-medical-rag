In some areas, the recommendations may have small resource implications relating to the additional healthcare professional time needed to discuss healthy eating and drinking for children, particularly with families from low income or disadvantaged backgrounds. Interventions to improve people's skills and confidence related to healthy eating, such as local cookery classes or groups, may not be available in all areas so establishing these may have resource implications but promoting healthy eating can, in turn, bring long-term benefits.
