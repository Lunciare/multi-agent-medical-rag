Gestational diabetes The recommendations in this section should be read in conjunction with NICE's guideline on diabetes in pregnancy. 1.2.18 When a person is diagnosed with gestational diabetes, ask about their usual diet and physical activity in order to provide individualised advice. 1.2.19 Advise people with gestational diabetes that there is currently no convincing evidence that a particular diet (for example, a low-glycaemic-index diet, low- carbohydrate diet, low-fat diet, or high-fibre diet) is better than the other. Discuss a healthy diet for gestational diabetes that is the most preferable and appropriate for the person. See NHS advice on a healthy diet for gestational diabetes.
