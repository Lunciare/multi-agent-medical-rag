Physical activity in pregnancy 1.2.5 Discuss the importance of physical activity with anyone who is pregnant (see the UK Chief Medical Officers' guidance on physical activity in pregnancy and the NHS Start for Life advice on exercising in pregnancy). Ask people about their usual physical activity and exercise habits and preferences, and provide information on the following that is in the person's preferred format and relevant to their individual circumstances: • How to safely continue with physical activity. • How to gradually increase physical activity during pregnancy if they are not already physically active. • The importance of minimising sedentary time, such as sitting for long periods.
