How the recommendations might affect practice The recommendations reinforce current best practice. In some areas, the recommendations may have small resource implications relating to the additional healthcare professional time needed to discuss healthy eating in pregnancy, particularly with young people and those from low income or disadvantaged backgrounds. Commissioners and service providers in some areas may need to improve training for healthcare professionals about healthy eating in pregnancy. Interventions to improve people's skills and confidence related to healthy eating, such as local cookery classes or groups, may not be available in all areas so establishing these may have resource implications but promoting healthy eating can, in turn, bring long-term benefits.
