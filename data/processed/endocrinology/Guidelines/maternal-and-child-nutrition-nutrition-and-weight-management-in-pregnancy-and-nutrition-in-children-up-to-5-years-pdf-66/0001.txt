nutrition: nutrition and weight management in pregnancy, and nutrition in children up to 5 years Overview This guideline covers nutrition and weight management in pregnancy for anyone who may become pregnant, is planning to become pregnant or is already pregnant, and nutrition in children up to 5 years. Care of babies and children born preterm or with low birth weight is not covered. The guideline does not give detailed advice on what constitutes a healthy diet. Who is it for? • Healthcare professionals working in the NHS responsible for maternal and child nurses, dentists and dental professionals, and pharmacists • Commissioners and providers of antenatal and postnatal care services, weight management and activity or exercise services, and primary care services • Providers of preschool education and care services and early years settings • Employers and staff in education settings • People planning a pregnancy or who are pregnant, and their families • Families, carers, partners, people who look after babies and children up to 5 years, and the public Page 5 of Recommendations People have the right to be involved in discussions and make informed decisions about their care, as described in NICE's information on making decisions about your care.
