This is because of the following: • There are different factors that can affect weight change during pregnancy, for example, weight of the baby, weight of the placenta, maternal increase in blood volume, amniotic fluid, breast tissue expansion and body fat, and how these (especially the weight of the baby) vary between individuals and affect weight differently.
