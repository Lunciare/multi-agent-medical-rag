The committee agreed that starting or maintaining moderate physical activity during pregnancy is important for both the pregnant person and their unborn baby. They recommended that discussion around physical activity is individualised and based on a discussion about the person's usual habits and preferences, because this will help encourage physical activity during pregnancy. How the recommendation might affect practice The recommendation reinforces current best practice. In some areas, the recommendation may have small resource implications relating to the additional healthcare professional time needed to discuss physical activity in pregnancy.
