Short term Most patients will be biochemically euthyroid within 4–6 weeks, although the timeline will depend on disease severity, ATD dose and compliance. Patients with higher baseline thyroid hormone concentrations may take a longer time to normalize. Moreover, ATDs build up in the thyroid over a few weeks, and improvement in symptoms may not be immediate. Longer term Although most patients have a BMI SD score within normal limits at diagnosis, excessive weight gain can occur after euthyroidism is restored (38).
