Genetically determined immunological susceptibility appears to interact with environmental insults (e.g. cigarette use, infection, stress and gut microbiota). Genetic susceptibility is linked to the HLA locus and other immune-related genes (e.g. CTLA4, IL-2RA and PTPN22) and thyroid-specific genes (e.g. TG and TSHR) (13, 14, 15). HCP5 polymorphisms have been associated with a younger age at GD onset (16, 17). GD is associated with the occurrence of other autoimmune disorders such as type I diabetes mellitus, celiac disease and vitiligo and is more common in Down syndrome (4, 18). In approximately 15% of cases, there is a first-degree relative with autoimmune thyroid disease. GD can also occur following bone marrow transplantation and HIV therapies (19).
