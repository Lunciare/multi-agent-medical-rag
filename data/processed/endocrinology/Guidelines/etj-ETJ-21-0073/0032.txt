Patients and families should be familiar with the manifestations of thyrotoxicosis, and patients should have thyroid function checked if relapse is suspected, or every 3–4 months after stopping ATD in the first year, every 6 months in the second year and annually thereafter; not only because of the risk of relapse but also of autoimmune hypothyroidism (see prognosis) (51). Patients can be referred back to their family doctor after 12–24 months, with advice to monitor thyroid function for at least years or in case of symptoms or signs of hyperthyroidism. The possibility of TSHRAb crossing the placenta and affecting the health of the fetus years later should be discussed with females pre-discharge and reinforced by pertinent text in associated correspondence. Evaluation of thyroid function pre-conception and TSHRAb levels when pregnant (especially after definitive treatment for GD) is needed.
