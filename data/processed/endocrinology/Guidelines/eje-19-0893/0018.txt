Overall, 3819 potentially relevant papers were considered; 68 were included (two studies assessed both hypothyroidism and hypercortisolism (16)). 3.7. Review process and endorsement of other societies A draft of the guideline was reviewed by the experts in the field; it was also submitted for comments by ESE members.
