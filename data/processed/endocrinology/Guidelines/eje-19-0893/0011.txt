Target group This guideline was developed for healthcare providers involved in the care of patients with obesity, which covers a broad range of doctors. In line, the guidelines were not developed with the specific aim to cover rare forms of obesity. 3.3. Aims The overall purpose of this guideline is to provide clinicians with practical guidance for the endocrine work-up in decisions should take into account the recommendations but also the clinical judgment of the treating physician. Recommendations are thus never meant to replace clinical judgment. 3.4. Summary of methods used for guideline development The methods used have been described in more detail previously (11). In short, the guideline used GRADE (Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation) as a methodological base. The first step was to define the clinical questions (see Section 3.5), the second a systematic literature search (see Section 3.6).
