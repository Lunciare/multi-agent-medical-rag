Clearly, this has the potential to reduce bias and stigma and to promote access to evidence-based care. Conversely, stigma and IWB can be perpetuated by the sole use of BMI as the deﬁning characteristic in the public forum and for diagnosis in adiposity on health or a comprehensive understanding of disease pathophysiology. In contrast, ABCD indicates what clinicians are treating (adiposity-based ¼ abnormalities in the mass, distribution, and function of adipose tissue) and why they are treating it (chronic disease associated with complications).
