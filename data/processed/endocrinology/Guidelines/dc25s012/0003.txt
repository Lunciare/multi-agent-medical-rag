Screening Recommendations 12.3 Adults with type 1 diabetes should have an initial dilated and comprehen- sive eye examination by an ophthal- mologist or optometrist within 5 years after the onset of diabetes. B 12.4 People with type 2 diabetes should have an initial dilated and comprehen- sive eye examination by an ophthal- mologist or optometrist at the time of the diabetes diagnosis.
