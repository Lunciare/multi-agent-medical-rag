Type 1 Diabetes Because retinopathy is estimated to take at least 5 years to develop after the on- set of hyperglycemia, people with type 1 diabetes should have an initial dilated and comprehensive eye examination within 5 years after the diagnosis of di- abetes (19). Type 2 Diabetes People with type 2 diabetes who may have had undiagnosed diabetes for years and have a signiﬁcant risk of prevalent diabetic retinopathy at the time of diag- nosis should have an initial dilated and comprehensive eye examination at the time of diagnosis.
