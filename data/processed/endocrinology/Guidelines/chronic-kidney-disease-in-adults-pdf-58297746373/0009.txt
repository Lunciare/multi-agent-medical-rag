Frequency of monitoring is determined by the stability of kidney function and the ACR level, and tailored to the individual according to: • the underlying cause of CKD • the rate of decline in eGFR or increase in ACR (but be aware that CKD progression is often non-linear) • other risk factors, including heart failure, diabetes and hypertension • changes to their treatment (such as renin–angiotensin–aldosterone system [RAAS] antagonists, NSAIDs and diuretics) • intercurrent illness (for example, acute kidney injury) • whether they have chosen conservative management of CKD.
