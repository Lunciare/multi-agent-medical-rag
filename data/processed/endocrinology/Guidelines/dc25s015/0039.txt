In systematic re- views and meta-analyses, compared with insulin or metformin, glyburide was as- sociated with a higher rate of neonatal hypoglycemia and macrosomia and an increased neonatal abdominal circumfer- ence (103,104). Glyburide was not found to be nonin- ferior to insulin based on a composite outcome of neonatal hypoglycemia, mac- rosomia, and hyperbilirubinemia (105). Long-term safety data for offspring ex- posed to glyburide are not available (105). Metformin Metformin was associated with a lower risk of neonatal hypoglycemia and less mater- nal weight gain than insulin in systematic reviews and RCTs for GDM treatment, but treatment monotherapy failure occurred in 14–46% of individuals (103,106–109). A meta-analysis of 11 RCTs demonstrated that metformin treatment in pregnancy does not reduce the risk of GDM in high- risk individuals with obesity, polycystic ovary syndrome, or preexisting insulin re- sistance (110).
