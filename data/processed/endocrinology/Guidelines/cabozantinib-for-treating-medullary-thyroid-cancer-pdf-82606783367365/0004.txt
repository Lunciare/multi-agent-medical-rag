Furthermore the toxicity profile of cabozantinib differs to that of vandetanib, so some people who can have cabozantinib may not be able to have vandetanib. The committee agreed that the relevant comparators were therefore vandetanib and best supportive care. It concluded that there is a clinical need for active treatment options for unresectable, locally advanced or metastatic medullary thyroid cancer.
