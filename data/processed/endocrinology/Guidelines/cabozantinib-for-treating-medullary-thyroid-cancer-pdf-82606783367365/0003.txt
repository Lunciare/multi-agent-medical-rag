Cabozantinib for treating medullary thyroid cancer (TA516) Page 5 of Committee discussion The appraisal committee (section 5) considered evidence from a number of sources. See the committee papers for full details of the evidence. The condition and current treatment locally advanced or metastatic medullary thyroid cancer 3.1 Medullary thyroid cancer is rare and around 25% of cases are hereditary. The most common symptoms, such as diarrhoea and fatigue, can significantly affect patients' quality of life and wellbeing. The patient experts commented that in the absence of a cure, patients would welcome treatments that delay disease progression and control symptoms. The committee noted that cabozantinib and vandetanib are the only systemic treatment options for unresectable, locally advanced or metastatic medullary thyroid cancer, and are only available through the Cancer Drugs Fund for people with progressive and symptomatic disease. The clinical experts explained that both treatments are associated with side effects, so not all patients can tolerate them. The only alternative for these people is best supportive care.
