The patient expert described side effects such as frequent diarrhoea, rash and fatigue, but considered that the disease would have had a more severe effect without treatment. The clinical experts explained that treatment toxicities tend to occur soon after treatment starts, and that for most patients the dosage is reduced after the initial treatment period. The experts explained the importance of balancing the risks and benefits when considering starting treatment with cabozantinib, and that treatment is usually started only when the disease becomes symptomatic to the extent that the benefits of treatment outweigh the burden of side effects.
