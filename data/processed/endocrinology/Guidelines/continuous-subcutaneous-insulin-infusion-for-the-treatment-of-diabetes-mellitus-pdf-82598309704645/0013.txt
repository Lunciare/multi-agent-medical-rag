Twenty-six observational studies compared the rate of severe hypoglycaemic episodes in people receiving CSII and MDI therapy. Of the 10 studies in the adult/mixed age groups, 8 reported statistically significant decreases in the rate of severe hypoglycaemic episodes after starting CSII therapy.
