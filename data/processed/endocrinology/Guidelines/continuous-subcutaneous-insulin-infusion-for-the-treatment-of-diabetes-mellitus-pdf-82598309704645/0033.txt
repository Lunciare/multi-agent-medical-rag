This recommendation is consistent with NICE guideline on the diagnosis and management of type 1 diabetes in children, young people and adults. 4.3.9 In developing the recommendations for children, the committee considered that children 12 years and older would normally be competent to self-inject an afternoon dose of insulin at school, which would allow for a proper trial of MDI therapy.
