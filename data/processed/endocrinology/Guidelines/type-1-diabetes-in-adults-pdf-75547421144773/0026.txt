Definitions of terms used in this quality statement Key care processes The care processes are: • urine ACR measurement • HbA1c measurement • blood pressure measurement • foot surveillance • serum creatinine measurement • serum cholesterol measurement • BMI measurement • smoking status • retinal screening. [National Diabetes Audit] Equality and diversity considerations Appointments for completion of 9 key care processes should be offered at times, and in locations, that meet the needs of adults with type 1 diabetes. Appointments should be appropriate and age appropriate. Adults should have access to an interpreter or advocate if needed.
