[Adapted from NICE's guideline on diabetic foot problems, recommendations 1.3.4 to 1.3.6 and expert opinion] 29 of Quality statement 6: Support to self- manage diabetes during inpatient admissions Quality statement Adults with type 1 diabetes admitted to hospital are supported to self-manage their diabetes. [2011, updated 2023] Rationale Adults with type 1 diabetes may be admitted to hospital for conditions related or unrelated to diabetes. This can disturb daily routines, affecting carbohydrate intake and insulin therapy, and special regimens may be needed in preparation and response to procedures that affect the usual management of diabetes. A specialist multidisciplinary team with expertise in diabetes can help the person and the team caring for them to adapt the management of their diabetes when in hospital. They should do this while still respecting the person's expertise in managing their own diabetes. The specialist multidisciplinary team should support the person to continue to self-manage their diabetes and administer their own insulin if they are willing and able and it is safe.
