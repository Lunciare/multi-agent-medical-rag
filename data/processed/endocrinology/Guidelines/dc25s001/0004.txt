State of Diabetes Care The proportion of people with diabetes who achieve recommended A1C, blood pressure, and LDL cholesterol levels has ﬂuctuated over the years, with some improvement over time (4,5). In 2015– 2018 (the most recent time period with population-level data available), just 50.5% of U.S. community-dwelling adults with di- abetes achieved A1C <7% and 75.4% achieved A1C <8% (5). The goal blood pressure of <130/80 mmHg was achieved by just 47.7% of adults with diabetes, while 70.4% achieved blood pressure <140/90 mmHg (5).
