Public Health Association deﬁnes a CHW as a “frontline public health worker who is a trusted member of and/or has an unusually close understanding of the community served” (163). CHWs can be part of an evidence- based strategy to improve the manage- ment of diabetes and cardiovascular risk factors in underserved communities and health care systems (164). The CHW scope of practice in areas such as outreach and communication, advocacy, social support, nity clinics, and other services has success- fully provided social and primary preventive services to underserved populations in ru- ral and hard-to-reach communities.
