Regression analyses of baseline HbA1c compared with change in HbA1c 3.6 The EAG did some exploratory regression analyses comparing baseline HbA1c with HbA1c change. This is because people with a higher baseline HbA1c are expected to have a greater reduction in HbA1c after using an HCL system than people with a lower baseline HbA1c. Two analyses were done: 1 for HCL systems compared with CSII plus rtCGM and 1 for CSII plus rtCGM compared with CSII plus isCGM. The effects from each of these were then coupled together to estimate the effect of HCL systems on HbA1c from baseline compared with CSII plus isCGM.
