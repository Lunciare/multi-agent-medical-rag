The EAG said that the regression analyses were highly uncertain because only 5 studies were included in the regression that provided the slope parameter. The studies were heterogeneous in terms of their design, duration, and age range of participants. The committee said that although the regression analyses were uncertain, they indicated a greater HbA1c effect size as the in practice. It concluded that the regression analyses should be included in the economic modelling.
