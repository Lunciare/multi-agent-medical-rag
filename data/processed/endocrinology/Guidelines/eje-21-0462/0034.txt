An endocrine work-up including all anterior pituitary functions should be performed (see Table 3), with special attention to possible associated metabolic or cardiovascular diseases. R.1.3. We recommend that management of women of reproductive age with a large pituitary adenoma (>1 cm), Cushing’s disease or acromegaly, who consider pregnancy, be discussed in a multidisciplinary team.
