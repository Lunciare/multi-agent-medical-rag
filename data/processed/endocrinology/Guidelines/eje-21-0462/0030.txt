In acromegaly, symptomatic tumour growth during pregnancy was seen in nine patients (7.0%, 95% CI: 3.3–12.9%) while radiological tumour growth occurred in six (5.4%, 95% CI: 2.0–11.4%). For GH-producing microadenomas, three patients exhibited symptomatic tumour growth (30.0%, 95% CI: 6.7–65.2%), and two only radiological tumour growth (25.0%, 95% CI: 3.2–65.1%); however, the low number of patients precludes a firm conclusion regarding the true risk as can be seen from the wide CIs.
