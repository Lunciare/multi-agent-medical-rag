Introduction: The pituitary gland and endocrine milieu in pregnancy Pregnancy changes the morphology and function of the pituitary gland. The gland size increases and reaches its maximal volume in late pregnancy and the first days post- partum with a height of up to 12 mm followed by a gradual decline to normal size within 6 months after delivery (1, 2, 3).
