In women with ACTH deficiency, hydrocortisone stress dosing is recommended during labour, similar to that used in major surgical stress. Hydrocortisone 100 mg intravenously should be initiated at the onset of active labour for vaginal delivery or pre-operatively for a Caesarean section. While it has been suggested that G15 this should be followed immediately by a continuous intravenous infusion of hydrocortisone 200 mg/24 h throughout labour for both vaginal delivery or Caesarean Guideline Committee are using lower doses. After delivery, the pregnancy hydrocortisone dose should be gradually tapered down to the pre-pregnancy dose (200, 210, 214, 217). R.3.2.
