Glucose-Lowering Therapy for People With Metabolic Comorbidities Many adults with diabetes, either type 2 diabetes or type 1 diabetes, with obesity are at high risk of developing MASLD or MASH as well as MASH cirrhosis. Hence, the presence of MASLD or MASH should be a consideration when choosing glucose- lowering therapies. Accruing randomized GLP-1 RA, and a dual GIP and GLP-1 RA have potential beneﬁts in terms of decreas- ing hepatic steatosis and in the resolution of MASH without worsening of ﬁbrosis in individuals with biopsy-proven MASH or those at higher risk of clinically signiﬁcant liver ﬁbrosis identiﬁed with noninvasive tests (146–153).
