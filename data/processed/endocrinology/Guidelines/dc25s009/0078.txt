E Therapeutic Strategies With Medication Unavailability Health care professionals and people with diabetes struggle when medication sup- plies are insufﬁcient to meet the demand. Recent examples of such circumstances include recalls involving a number of met- formin products and the marked increase in demand for agents from the GLP-1 RA and dual GIP and GLP-1 RA classes. The latter circumstance led to such a low level of availability that products were deter- mined by the FDA to be in shortage (204).
