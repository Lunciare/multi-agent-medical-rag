R.2.4 We suggest aiming for phosphate levels within the reference range. (⊕OOO) Reasoning: HypoPT is characterised by a relatively high circulating phosphate level and a higher-than-normal calcium- phosphate product. This is attributable to the lack of the phos­ phaturic effect of PTH, as well as an enhanced intestinal absorption of phosphorus, due to activated vitamin D treat­ ment (conventional treatment)25,121. The risk of extra-skeletal calcifications, including nephrocalcinosis and cataracts, is in­ creased in HypoPT.
