Other rare causes of HypoPT include, for example, parathyroid gland radi­ ation injury, haemochromatosis, granulomatous diseases, or metastatic cancers25. 2.2 Postsurgical Hypoparathyroidism Postsurgical HypoPT persisting for more than 6–12 months in adults is usually secondary to thyroid surgery but may occur following parathyroidectomy or other operations on the neck26. Overall, between 2-10% of patients undergoing total thyroidectomy, particularly when combined with central/lat­ eral lymph node dissection for thyroid cancer, will develop permanent HypoPT27,28. Postsurgical HypoPT is associated with young age, female sex, underlying Graves’ disease, unilateral or bilateral lympha­ denectomy, gross extrathyroidal extension of thyroid cancer, early postoperative hypocalcaemia (within 24 hours of thyroi­ dectomy), accidental parathyroidectomy, intraoperative para­ thyroid biopsies during surgery for hyperparathyroidism, the presence of parathyroid tissue in the operative specimen, and parathyroid auto-transplantation29.
