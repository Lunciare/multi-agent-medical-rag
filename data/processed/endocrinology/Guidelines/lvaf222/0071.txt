For the purposes of the above, frequent fluctuations in cal­ cium levels are considered if 3 or more adjustments per year are needed in doses of activated vitamin D and/or calcium sup­ plements to maintain a calcium concentration in the target range. Such patients are considered inadequately controlled by conventional therapy and usually suffer from a significant disease burden with likewise frequent and costly demands on healthcare resources175,176. These patients might benefit from PTH replacement therapy.
