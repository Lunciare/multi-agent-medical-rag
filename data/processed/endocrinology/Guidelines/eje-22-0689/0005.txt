Whether patients with immune-related adverse events have better overall oncologic response rates to ICIs is still under intensive investigation. Recently published studies and systemic meta-analyses found that anti-PD-1 inhibitor- induced immune-related adverse events (including endocrine AEs) were strongly correlated with improved progression-free and overall survival in patients with non- small cell lung cancer (13), hepatocellular cancer (14), and other types of cancer (15).
