There is a surprising lack of reports on endocrinopathies related to ICI therapy in large patient databases which include data on adequately diagnosed ICI-induced endocrinopathies in patients followed up over time. As the range of ICIs is expanding, there is a need to establish registries with long-term follow-up, not only to register side-effects but also the outcome of oncologic and endocrine treatments.
