developed for health care professionals who may see patients with endocrine-related adverse conditions during or after ICI treatment and who seek guidance for the treatment and follow-up. General practitioners, nurses, and patients might also find the guideline useful. The guideline can serve as a source document for the preparation of patient information leaflets and educational materials. Aims The purpose of this guideline is to provide clinicians with practical guidance on the management of patients with ICI-related endocrine conditions. In clinical judgment of the treating physician should be taken into account. Recommendations are not meant to replace clinical acumen and may need adaptation to local circumstances.
