ICM administration may cause transient changes in serum TSH, T4, and T3 levels in healthy individuals, usually within the reference values. While interpreting thyroid function tests, we suggest taking ICM expo- sure (within the last 1–3 months) into consideration. 2, ØØØO Should Thyroid Function Be Monitored after ICM Administration? There is no indication for routine monitoring for ICM-induced TD because this would not be cost-effective . Therefore, all patients should be informed that signs and symptoms of Hyper or Hypo may occur after ICM administration and should be advised to alert the medical team if such develop. Routine thyroid function testing is favored by a few experts in older patients with a high risk of ICM-induced Hyper and patients with an underlying unstable cardio- vascular disease.
