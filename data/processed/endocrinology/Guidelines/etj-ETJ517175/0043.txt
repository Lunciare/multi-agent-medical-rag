Premature infants and neonates might be particularly susceptible to the development of ICM-induced Hypo be- cause the immature gland may not be able to fully escape from the acute Wolff-Chaikoff effect . In 2015, the US Food and Drug Administration issued a safety announce- ment regarding 10 cases of ICM-induced Hypo in infants younger than 4 months, reported between 1969 and 2012. In all cases, the infants were either premature or had seri- ous cardiac abnormalities. Hypo occurred within 1 month after receiving ICM.
