Al- though ICM-induced Hyper may occur up to 1 year after a radiological examination with ICM , the major- ity of patients develop symptoms and/or signs of Hyper within 3–10 weeks after exposure . The severity of ICM-induced Hyper is unpredictable. The majority of patients remain oligo- or asymptomatic. However, the consequences of even mild ICM-induced Hyper should not be underestimated in geriatric patients and in pa- tients with unstable cardiovascular status; it can aggravate pre-existing cardiac diseases leading to atrial fibrillation, congestive heart failure, worsening of angina pectoris, and thromboembolism [57–59, 92–98]. Finally in rare cases, ICM exposure may lead to thyroid storm [36–38] (online suppl. Table 2). The diagnosis of Hyper is confirmed by thyroid func- tion tests (TSH, FT4 and TT3 or FT3).
