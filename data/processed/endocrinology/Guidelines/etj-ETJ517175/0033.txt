The induction of OHypo appears to be rare and the exact timing of the event is unpredictable, ranging from days to up to 2 years after ICM exposure . Because of the wide vulnerable time window (from ICM exposure to develop- ment of Hypo), general monitoring of thyroid function tests in high-risk patients for Hypo is not advisable. Therefore, all patients should be informed to alert the medical team if signs and symptoms of Hypo occur after ICM administration.
