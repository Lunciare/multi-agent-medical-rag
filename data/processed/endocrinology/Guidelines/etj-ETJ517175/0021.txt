The Austrian Thyroid Association recommends using perchlorate as preventive treatment (600 mg prior to ex- amination and continuation with 3 × 300 mg daily for 7–14 days) in high-risk patients before emergency studies . Prior to ICM exposure, a consultation in a Thyroid regimen is used: methimazole 20–30 mg daily, starting the day before ICM administration and continuing for 14 days .
