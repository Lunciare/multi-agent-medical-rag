Additional risk factors associated with type 2 diabetes in youth include obesity and excess adi- posity (182), family history of diabetes possibly mediated by shared genetics, lifestyle, and environmental factors (183), female sex, maternal gestational diabetes mellitus (184), and adverse social determi- nants of health (176). As with type 1 diabetes, youth with type 2 diabetes spend much of the day in school. Therefore, close communica- tion with and the cooperation of school personnel are essential for optimal dia- betes management and safety and max- imal academic opportunities.
