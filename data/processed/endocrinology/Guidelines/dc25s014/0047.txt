B Retinopathy (like albuminuria) most com- monly occurs after the onset of puberty and after 5–10 years of diabetes duration (166). It is currently recognized that there is a low risk of development of vision- threatening retinal lesions prior to 12 years of age (167,168). A 2019 publication based on the follow-up of the DCCT adolescent cohort supports a lower frequency of eye examinations than previously recom- mended, particularly in adolescents with A1C closer to the goal range (169,170).
