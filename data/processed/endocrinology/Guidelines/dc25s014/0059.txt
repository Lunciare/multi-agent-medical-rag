Self-management in pediatric diabetes involves both the youth and their parents or adult caregivers. Individuals and their families should receive education and sup- port for healthful nutrition and physical activity, such as a balanced meal plan, achieving and maintaining a healthy weight, and regular physical activity. Physical activity should include aerobic, muscle-strengthening, and bone-strength- ening activities (24).
