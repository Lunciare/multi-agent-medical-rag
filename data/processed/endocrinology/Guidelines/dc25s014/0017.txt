School and Child Care As a large portion of a youth’s day is spent in school and/or daycare, training of school or daycare personnel to provide care in accordance with the child’s indi- vidualized diabetes medical management plan is essential for optimal diabetes management and safe access to all school- or daycare–sponsored opportuni- ties (11,37,38). In addition, federal and state laws require schools, daycare facili- ties, and other entities to provide needed diabetes care to enable the child to safely access the school or daycare envi- ronment. Refer to the ADA position statements “Diabetes Care in the School Setting” (11) and “Care of Young Chil- dren With Diabetes in the Childcare and Community Setting” (38) and the ADA’s Safe at School website (diabetes.org/ resources/know-your-rights/safe-at-school- state-laws) for additional details.
