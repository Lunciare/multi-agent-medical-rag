B Diabetic neuropathy rarely occurs in pre- pubertal children or after only 1–2 years of diabetes (166), although data suggest a prevalence of distal peripheral neuropathy of 7% in 1,734 youth with type 1 diabetes and association with the presence of CVD risk factors (172,173). A comprehensive foot exam, including inspection, palpation of dorsalis pedis and posterior tibial pulses, and determination of proprioception, vi- bration, and monoﬁlament sensation, should be performed annually along with an assessment of symptoms of neuro- pathic pain (173). Foot inspection can be performed at each visit to educate youth regarding the importance of foot care (see Section 12, “Retinopathy, Neuropathy, and Foot Care”).
