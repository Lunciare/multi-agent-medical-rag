Glucose levels for all children and adolescents with type 1 diabetes should be monitored multiple times daily by blood glucose monitoring and/or CGM. Recent data on children and adults sug- gest that use of CGM soon after type 1 diabetes diagnosis is associated with improved A1C (84,85,113). In the U.S., real-time CGM is approved for nonad- junctive use in children aged 2 years and older, and intermittently scanned CGM is approved for nonadjunctive use in children aged 4 years and older.
