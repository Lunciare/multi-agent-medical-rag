Thyroid function tests may be misleading (euthyroid sick syndrome) if performed at the time of diagnosis owing to the effect of previous hyperglycemia, ketosis or ketoacidosis, weight loss, etc. Therefore, if performed at diagnosis and slightly abnormal, thyroid function tests should be repeated soon after a period of metabolic stability and achievement of glycemic goals. Subclinical hypothyroidism may be associated with an increased risk of symptomatic hypoglycemia and dyslipi- demia (129,130) and a reduced linear growth rate. Hyperthyroidism alters glu- cose metabolism and usually causes dete- rioration of glycemia. Celiac Disease Recommendations 14.31 Screen youth with type 1 diabe- tes for celiac disease by measuring IgA tissue transglutaminase (tTG) antibod- ies, with documentation of normal to- tal serum IgA levels, soon after the diagnosis of diabetes, or IgG tTG and deamidated gliadin antibodies if IgA is deﬁcient.
