All these medications are contraindicated in in- dividuals who are pregnant or actively trying to conceive and are not recommended for use in individuals who are nursing. Indi- viduals of childbearing potential should receive counseling regarding the use of reliable methods of contraception while using weight loss medications.Tirzepatide in particular may reduce the efﬁcacy of oral hormonal contraceptives due to de- layed gastric emptying, an effect that is largest after the ﬁrst dose and diminishes over time. Individuals using oral hor- monal contraceptives should switch to a nonoral contraceptive method or add a barrier method of contraception for 4 weeks after initiation and for 4 weeks af- ter each dose escalation.
