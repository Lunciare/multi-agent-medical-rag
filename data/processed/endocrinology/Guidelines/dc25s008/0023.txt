In the Efﬁcacy and Safety of Tirzepatide Once Weekly in Participants With Type 2 Diabe- tes Who Have Obesity or Are Overweight: A Randomized, Double-Blind, Placebo- Controlled Trial (SURMOUNT-2), tirzepa- tide resulted in body weight loss of 9.6% and 11.6% more than placebo and A1C lowering of 1.55% and 1.57% more than placebo after 72 weeks of treatment with the 10 mg and 15 mg doses, respectively, with adverse effects similar to those seen with the GLP-1 RA class (89). The observed weight loss with weight management pharmacotherapy is lower in people with diabetes than in those of similar baseline weight without diabetes; therefore, it is important to appropriately manage ex- pectations of individuals with diabetes and health care professionals. Weight management pharmacotherapy has demonstrated multiple additional ben- eﬁts beyond weight loss and improvement in glucose management. Some such exam- ples include improvements or potential improvements in cardiovascular risk factors (e.g., blood pressure and lipids), inﬂammation, obstructive sleep apnea, MASLD and MASH, and symptoms re- lated to heart failure with preserved ejection fraction (90–93).
