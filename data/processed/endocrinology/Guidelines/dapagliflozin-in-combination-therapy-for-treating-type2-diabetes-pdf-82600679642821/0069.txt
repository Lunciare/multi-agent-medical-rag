However, it also recognised that a new drug therapy that was associated with a lower risk of hypoglycaemia than some other existing therapies would also be valued by patients for whom driving might be a significant factor in their lifestyle or livelihood. The Committee concluded that the adverse-events profile of dapagliflozin was different from those of other antidiabetic therapies and that it was important to examine these adverse events when considering the manufacturers' economic model.
