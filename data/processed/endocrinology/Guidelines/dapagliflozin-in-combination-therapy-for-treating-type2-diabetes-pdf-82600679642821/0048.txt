For the dual therapy analyses, the CORE model produced an ICER of £8,879 per QALY gained for the comparison of dapagliflozin with sulfonylureas and ICERs of £2,014 and £7,093 per QALY gained for the comparisons of dapagliflozin with DPP-4 inhibitors and with thiazolidinediones. For the insulin add-on analyses, the CORE model resulted in an ICER of £1,675 per QALY gained for dapagliflozin compared with DPP-4 inhibitors. For the triple therapy analyses, the CORE model produced ICERs of £1,759 per QALY gained for the comparison of dapagliflozin with DPP-4 inhibitors and £16,054 per QALY gained for the comparison of dapagliflozin with thiazolidinediones. The CORE model also produced an ICER of £32,243 per QALY lost for the comparison of dapagliflozin with GLP-1 analogues. 3.54 Both the ERG and the DSU reviewed the manufacturers' revised economic model and analyses provided in response to the appraisal consultation document.
