There are neither prospective randomized nor pro- pensity score-matched trials evaluating the efficacy and tolerability of HIFU in comparison with the other TA techniques. In a small uncontrolled series of patients, HIFU, RFA, and MWA showed a comparable nodule vol- ume reduction at 3 months (48, 50, and 44%, respectively) .
