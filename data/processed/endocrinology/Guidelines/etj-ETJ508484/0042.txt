Therefore, TA could be used for selected large-size AFTNs to obtain a volume reduction prior to RAI treatment [94, 98]. A prospective study employing a 12-item Short Form Health Survey questionnaire demonstrated a comparable improvement in HRQoL in patients with AFTN offered either lobectomy or LTA followed by RAI . High-Intensity Focused Ultrasound HIFU is less effective than RAI for control of hyper- functioning nodules. A prospective trial in 2 cohorts treated either with HIFU or RAI demonstrated that eu- thyroidism at 12 months was obtained in 94% of the RAI- treated versus 53% of the HIFU-treated patients .
