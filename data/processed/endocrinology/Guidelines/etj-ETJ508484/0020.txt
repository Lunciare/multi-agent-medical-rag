Procedure and Post-Procedural Management Procedure The operators performing thyroid TA need appropri- ate experience in cervical US anatomy and US-guided di- agnostic procedures and a dedicated training in image- guided therapeutic procedures. During the TA procedure, frequent swallowing and head movements should be avoided. Hence, a mild con- scious sedation (e.g., midazolam, 1–3 mg intravenously) may be helpful for keeping the patient relaxed throughout the procedure [9–12].
