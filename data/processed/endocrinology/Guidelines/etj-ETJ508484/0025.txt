Physicians who perform thyroid US examinations during follow-up after TA procedures should be aware of the timing and characteristics of structural modifications induced by the intervention. This is to avoid misinterpre- tations of late, potentially worrisome, morphological changes (e.g., development of a hypoechoic lesion with intranodular calcifications) . Recommendation 6. Local subcutaneous and pericapsular anes- thesia is recommended before TA procedures. Mild conscious se- dation may be considered, especially in case of HIFU treatment (1, ØØOO). US evaluation is recommended in order to rule out potential early adverse effects and define the extent of the ablated area. Patients should be instructed to present for clinical and US assessment if severe pain, local swelling, or fever occur within the first week after TA (1, ØØOO). Recommendation 8. Early-term (e.g., 3 months) and interme- evaluations are recommended. Long-term follow-up monitoring is suggested, in the absence of symptoms every 1–2 years, in order to reveal regrowth (1, ØØOO).
