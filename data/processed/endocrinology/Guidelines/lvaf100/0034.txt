Therefore, the consensus group suggests that post­ operative MRI imaging for APTs or PC should be performed within 3 months after surgery and at least every 6 months to ensure timely detection of progression. The prior rate of growth and anatomical considerations, such as compression of the optic nerve/chiasm, may necessi­ tate more frequent imaging.
