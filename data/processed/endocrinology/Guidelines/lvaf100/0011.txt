The working group had in-person meetings in June 2023 and February 2024. All par­ ticipants completed conflict of interest forms (see Table S1). Prior to publication, a draft of the guideline was reviewed by two patient representatives and four experts in the field (see Acknowledgments). Revision of the guideline was based on feedback from ESE members and following presentation at the European Congress of Endocrinology (Stockholm). All comments and suggestions were discussed and implemented as deemed appropriate by the working group (see Table S14). Target group This document was developed for healthcare providers of patients with APT and PC, and served as a source document for the prep­ aration of educational material published on the ESE website, to empower patients with APT and PC and their clinicians. Endorsement by other societies To achieve wide acceptance of the guidelines within the clinical community of the different disciplines involved in the management of APT and PC, the draft of the guideline document was submitted to several other professional/learned societies.
