In eight patients with Nelson’s syndrome pasireotide had minimal effects on tu­ mour volume, despite reductions in Adrenocorticotropic hormone (ACTH) levels in most patients.195 In three patients with aggressive corticotroph macroadenomas (1 PC) pasireo­ tide was not clinically useful,196 and in three patients with re­ current corticotroph tumour after discontinuation of temozolomide, pasireotide had no effect.15 Hyperglycaemia is a common side effect of pasireotide treatment and should be considered before starting this treatment, given the limited benefit in the context of aggressive pituitary tumours.
