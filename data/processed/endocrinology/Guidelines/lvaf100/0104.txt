An in­ crease in circulating hormone concentrations may prompt in­ vestigation for disease progression and/or metastatic disease. In addition, given treatment-related hypopituitarism, includ­ ing radiation effects on pituitary endocrine function which can occur many years following therapy, we recommend a complete endocrine evaluation to monitor adrenal, thyroid, and sex steroid function at least yearly or more often if clinical symptoms suggest dysfunction.243,294 R 4.3 We recommend life-long follow-up of patients with aggressive pituitary tumours.
