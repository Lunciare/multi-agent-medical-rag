In addition, possible predictors of treatment response were reviewed. It was also acknowledged that predicting clinical behaviour in pituitary tumours remains challenging. It was decided to system­ atically review literature to try to estimate the average growth rate in pituitary tumours, to identify those with aggressive growth, and possible predictors of clinically aggressive behaviour.
