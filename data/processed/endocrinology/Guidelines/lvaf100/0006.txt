(⊕⊕○○) 3.4 Chemotherapies R 3.4.1 We recommend use of temozolomide monotherapy as first line chemotherapy for aggressive pituitary tumours and pituitary carcinomas, following documented tumour progression. (⊕⊕○○) R 3.4.2 We recommend use of temozolomide standard dosing regimen: 150-200 mg/m2 for 5 consecutive days every 28 days. (⊕○○○) R 3.4.3 We suggest to consider concurrent temozolomide and a course of radiotherapy in cases of rapid tumour pro­ gression of a large residual, inaccessible to additional sur­ gery, particularly in the presence of high proliferative markers and/or somatic mutations suggestive of a poor prognosis (see R 2.2.4), or when a rapid tumour response is required. (⊕○○○) R 3.4.4 We recommend first evaluation of temozolomide treatment response after 3 cycles. If tumour progression is demonstrated, temozolomide treatment should be ceased. (⊕⊕○○) R 3.4.5 We recommend monitoring of haematological tion for potential adverse effects during treatment.
