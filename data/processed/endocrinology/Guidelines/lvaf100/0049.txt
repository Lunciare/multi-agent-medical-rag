Role of radiotherapy R 3.2.1 We recommend radiotherapy to improve tumour control in patients with clinically relevant tumour progres­ sion despite surgery and standard medical treatment. (⊕⊕○○) Reasoning Radiation therapy may offer the possibility of long-term con­ trol of tumour growth and should be discussed in all patients with an APT. Both fractionated external beam radiation ther­ apy (EBRT) and stereotactic radiosurgery (SRS) are highly ef­ fective in pituitary tumours, although few data are available in more aggressive phenotypes.
