All studies obtained from the searches were entered into ref­ erence manager software (EndNote X20, Clarivate Analytics, Philadelphia, PA), and titles and abstracts were screened. Potentially relevant studies were retrieved for detailed assess­ ment. References of included studies were assessed for add­ itional relevant articles. The literature search for clinical question I (growth velocity in pituitary tumours) resulted in 485 papers. After assessment, 11 studies were included (see Table 1). For clinical question II (predictors of aggressive behaviour), 428 papers were identi­ fied, of which 4 were included.
