Summary of methods used for guideline development The methods used have been described in more detail previously.17,18 In short, the guideline used GRADE (Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation) as a methodological base. The first step was being a systematic literature search (see Section 2.5). After in­ cluding relevant articles, we (1) estimated an average effect for specific outcomes (if possible), and (2) rated the quality of the evidence.
