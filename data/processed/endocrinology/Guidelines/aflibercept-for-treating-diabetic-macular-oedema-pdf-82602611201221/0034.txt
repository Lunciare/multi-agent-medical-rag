Macular oedema (diabetic) - aflibercept (TA346) Page 21 of 4 Consideration of the evidence 4.1 The Appraisal Committee reviewed the data available on the clinical and cost effectiveness of aflibercept, having considered evidence on the nature of diabetic macular oedema (DMO) and the value placed on the benefits of experts. It also took into account the effective use of NHS resources. 4.2 The Committee heard from the clinical and patient experts about the impact of living with visual impairment caused by DMO. It heard from patient experts that sight loss from DMO can result in significant life changes including: loss of physical independence and reduced capacity for self-care (including diabetes management); loss of financial independence (because of forced early retirement, loss of income and dependence on benefits); reduced emotional wellbeing (depression); and loss of driving licence.
