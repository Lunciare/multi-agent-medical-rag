The ERG commented that the concerns it had about the validity of the results from the 10 UK ophthalmologists for the number of aflibercept injections in year 1 also raised concerns about the reliability of the mean estimate of 4.0 aflibercept injections for the second year of the online survey. However, the ERG acknowledged that it had questioned to what degree the dosing of aflibercept might be protocol driven in its original report, and therefore the ERG applied 4.0 aflibercept injections in year 2 within a sensitivity analyses.
