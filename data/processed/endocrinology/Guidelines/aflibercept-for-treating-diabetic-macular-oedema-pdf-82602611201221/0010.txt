This approach needed 36 utility values to account for every possible combination of the better seeing eye and worse seeing eye. The company used 4 sources of health-related quality-of-life data in its cost-effectiveness analyses. For its base-case analyses, the company used utility values from Czoski-Murray et al. (2009).
