It was aware that the study compared the relative efficacy and safety of aflibercept with bevacizumab and ranibizumab and that it provided some evidence for the relative effectiveness of aflibercept compared with bevacizumab and with ranibizumab in people with a CRT less than 400 micrometres. The Committee also noted the comments from one of the comparator companies and heard from the ERG that the evidence from the trial for the comparison of aflibercept with ranibizumab was not relevant for this appraisal because the ranibizumab treatment arm was dosed at 0.3 mg pro re nata (PRN), which is not consistent with the dose specified in the summary of product characteristics for ranibizumab (0.5 mg PRN). The Committee acknowledged the limitations of the subgroup analysis on CRT from the Protocol T study and agreed that the results could not be considered in its decision-making.
