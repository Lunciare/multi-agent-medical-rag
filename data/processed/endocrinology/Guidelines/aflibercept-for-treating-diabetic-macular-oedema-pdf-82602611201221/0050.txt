The ICER for the whole trial population had suggested that aflibercept compared with laser in this group was not a cost-effective use of NHS resources. The Committee noted that the ICER for the less than 400 micrometres CRT subgroup was £49,400 per QALY gained for the comparison of aflibercept with laser. The Committee noted that the company had not submitted any new evidence for this subgroup in its response to consultation on the appraisal consultation document. It considered the revised ICER using the increased cost of laser administration, which was £48,300 per QALY gained (see section 3.30).
