Full details of all cost evidence considered by the Committee are available in the assessment report overview. 5.2 The decision tree model submitted had 2 arms: a current practice and an intervention arm, in which a proportion of patients were tested with VibraTip and current practice was used for the remainder. The current practice arm used either the 10 g monofilament or the tuning fork. In the base case it was assumed that 40% of the patients in the intervention arm were tested with VibraTip. Over the 3-year time horizon, patients in the pathway had a risk of ulceration, repeat ulceration and amputation. The parameters and transition probabilities which impacted on the patient pathway included the probability of developing diabetic peripheral neuropathy, risk of foot ulceration and risk of amputation. These values did not influence the base case results because the costs and transition probabilities were identical for both arms.
