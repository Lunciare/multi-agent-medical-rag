If a cost minimisation analysis were adopted, including the per-examination device costs only, VibraTip might be more expensive than either comparator under heavy usage, with a plausible per-examination cost of between £0.0398 (for 4 sites per examination) and £0.0995 (for 10 sites per examination). This compares with a per-examination cost of £0.076 for the 10 g monofilament assuming 10 sites per examination. The External Assessment Centre concluded that the economic case for the adoption of VibraTip had not been demonstrated robustly. 18 of Committee considerations 5.11 The Committee agreed with the External Assessment Centre's opinion that the economic model submitted did not fully address the costs and resources associated with the adoption of VibraTip.
