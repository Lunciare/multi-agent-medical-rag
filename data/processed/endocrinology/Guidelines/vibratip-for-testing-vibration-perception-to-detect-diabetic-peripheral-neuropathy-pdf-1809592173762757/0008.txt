The first part of the study tested intra-rater reliability of VibraTip in a population of 18 people with diabetes and at high risk of diabetic peripheral neuropathy (note: inter-rater reliability was the outcome specified in the scope). Results from successive readings, taken 2 to 3 weeks apart, demonstrated good intra-rater reliability (Cronbach's alpha = 0.88, no CI given). The main part of the study tested the comparative diagnostic accuracy of the 4 index tests compared with 9 of the neurothesiometer. Of the 141 people recruited from a secondary care setting, 89% reported having no history of foot ulcers. The authors performed a receiver-operator characteristic analysis to find the optimum number of insensate sites which gave the best diagnostic accuracy for each test, and found that 2 or more out of 10 were optimal for VibraTip, 10 g monofilament and NeuroTip, while 1 or more was optimal for the tuning fork. From the results provided, the External Assessment Centre calculated VibraTip's sensitivity as 0.79 (95% CI 0.69 to 0.90) and its specificity as 0.82 (95% CI 0.74 to 0.90).
