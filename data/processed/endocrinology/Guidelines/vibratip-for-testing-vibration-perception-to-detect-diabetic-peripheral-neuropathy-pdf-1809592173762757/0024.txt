The External Assessment Centre recalculated the per-examination costs for VibraTip based on the battery life evidence in Horsfield and Levy (2013) and obtained a range of values from £0.02 to £0.0995 depending on the number of sites (between 1 [1 foot] and 10 [5 per foot]) used per examination. For the Bailey's 10 g monofilament (a brand widely used in the NHS), the External Assessment Centre recalculated the per-examination costs as £0.076 based on 10 sites per examination, from monofilament useable lifetime data in a published technical study (Lavery et al. 2012).
