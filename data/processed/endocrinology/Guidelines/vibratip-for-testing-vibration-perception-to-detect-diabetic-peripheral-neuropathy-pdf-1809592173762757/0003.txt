NICE guidelines on diabetic foot problems, type 1 diabetes in adults and type 1 and type 2 in diabetes in children and young people, recommend a structured programme of regular (annual) foot surveillance, risk assessment and education by trained personnel to raise awareness of the condition. The annual foot examination should include a visual check, palpation of pulses and assessment of foot sensory nerve function.
