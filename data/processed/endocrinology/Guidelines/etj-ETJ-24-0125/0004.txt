Differential diagnosis of raised thyroid hormones and non- suppressed TSH The finding of thyroid hormones {(free) T4 and/or (free) T3} above the reference interval with non-suppressed thyrotropin (TSH) can be one of the most challenging patterns of discordant thyroid function tests (TFTs) to resolve. A systematic approach (Fig.1) is required to prevent inappropriate further investigations and unnecessary treatments, while at the same time ensuring rare genetic and acquired disorders are diagnosed in a timely manner.
