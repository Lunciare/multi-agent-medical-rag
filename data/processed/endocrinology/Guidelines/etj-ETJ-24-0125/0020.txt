Box 4. Summary of key recommendations – Disorders of thyroid hormone metabolism. Recommendations in selenoprotein deficiency Diagnosis • We recommend measurements to identify raised serum FT4, normal or low FT3, raised reverse T3, and low plasma selenium concentrations (Recommendation: S, Quality of evidence: ØØOO). • When making a genetic diagnosis, we recommend next-generation (whole exome or genome) sequencing of SECISBP2, enabling identification of intronic mutations as recorded in several cases. In cases of proven selenoprotein deficiency with an apparent, monoallelic, gene defect, we recommend analysis of SECISBP2 cDNA to identify missplicing events due to cryptic intronic mutations involving the other allele.
