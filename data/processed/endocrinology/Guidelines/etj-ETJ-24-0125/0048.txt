Iodothyronine deiodinase defects Background • The search for mutations in the deiodinase (DIO) genes has intensified after the generation of mice deficient in each of the three deiodinases (DioKOs) (139, 140, 141, 142) (Supplementary Table 3). Until 2021, the only genetic conditions affecting deiodinases were SECISBP2 and TRU-TCA1-1- dependent defects in selenoprotein synthesis (see disorders of thyroid hormone metabolism due to selenoprotein deficiency). • Only three families with pathogenic DIO1 mutations (OMIM 619855: Thyroid hormone metabolism abnormal 2; ORPHA:171706) have been reported (143, 144).
