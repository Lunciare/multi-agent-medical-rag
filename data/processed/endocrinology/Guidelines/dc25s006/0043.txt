Individuals at risk for hypoglycemia should also be screened for impaired hy- poglycemia awareness (also called hypo- glycemia unawareness or hypoglycemia- associated autonomic failure) at least yearly. Impaired hypoglycemia awareness is deﬁned as not experiencing the typical counterregulatory hormone release at low glucose levels or the associated symp- toms, which often occurs in individuals with long-standing diabetes or recurrent hypoglycemia (109). Individuals with im- paired hypoglycemia awareness may ex- perience confusion as the ﬁrst sign of hypoglycemia, which can create fear of hypoglycemia and severely impact qual- ity of life (110). Impaired hypoglycemia awareness dramatically increases the risk for level 3 hypoglycemia (111).
