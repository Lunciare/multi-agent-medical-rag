Most recently available data for inpatient mortality during hospital admis- sion for DKA ranges from 0.2% in type 1 di- abetes (163) to 1.0% in type 2 diabetes (156,164). Inpatient mortality among peo- ple with type 2 diabetes hospitalized for HHS decreased from 1.44% in 2008 to 0.77% in 2018 (165). The only study to have examined inpatient mortality for mixed DKA-HHS found it to be higher than mortality for HHS or DKA alone (154). Mor- tality rates reported in low- and middle- income countries are much higher than those in developed countries, potentially because of delayed diagnosis and treat- ment (151).
