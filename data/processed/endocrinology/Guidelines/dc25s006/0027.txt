As discussed above, these landmark studies in individuals with type 2 diabetes need to be considered with the important caveat that GLP-1 RAs and SGLT2 inhibi- tors were not yet in clinical use. These agents with established cardiovascular and kidney beneﬁts appear to be safe and beneﬁcial in this group of individuals at high risk for cardiovascular and kidney complications.
