Clini- cians should also consider medication bur- dens other than hypoglycemia, including tolerability, difﬁculties of administra- tion, impact on education or employ- ment, and ﬁnancial cost. These factors should be balanced against beneﬁts from glycemic lowering and disease-speciﬁc beneﬁts of newer medications that may be independent of glycemic lowering (Section 9, “Pharmacologic Approaches to Glycemic Treatment”). Multiple trials have shown that deintensiﬁcation of diabetes treatment can be achieved successfully and safely (68–70). It is im- portant to partner with people with dia- betes during the deintensiﬁcation process to understand their goals of diabetes treat- ment and agree upon appropriate glycemic monitoring, glucose levels, and goals of care (71).
