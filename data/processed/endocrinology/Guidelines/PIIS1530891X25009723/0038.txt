Information on the effects of specific antiretroviral drugs on lipid metabolism and specific statin-ART adverse interactions is available, 92,93 and should be incorporated into decision making, particularly if a statin other than pitavastatin is being entertained and/or if high- intensity statin is indicated because of underlying ASCVD or a high ASCVD risk estimate. Individuals should receive close monitoring where there may be potential drug-drug interactions and re- evaluation any time ART changes are made. In persons under 40 years of age, primary prevention de- cisions should be individualized. 92 Pitavastatin is preferred over other statins in persons with HIV infection who are found to be at low/moderate ASCVD risk on traditional risk calculators. Pit- avastatin effectively decreases ASCVD events 94 and avoids the risk of adverse interactions with ART since it is metabolized via glucuronidation. 95 Rosuvastatin (≤10 mg/day) is a good alter- native and atorvastatin (≤10-20 mg/day) may be used with certain antiretroviral drugs. Pravastatin should not be prescribed in individuals on darunavir but is otherwise safe, although its LDL-C lowering effect is more modest compared with rosuvas- tatin.
