Management of Hypercholesterolemia in Pregnant and Lactating Individuals Hypercholesterolemia management during pregnancy requires both the evaluation of the individual's cardiovascular risk and consideration of treatment effects on embryonic and fetal devel- opment (Algorithm Fig. 9). Information on the effects of choles- terol-lowering medications on pregnancy outcomes is limited. In individuals at very high risk of ASCVD, such as those with homo- zygous FH or with severe heterozygous FH and/or a history of underlying ASCVD who are on cholesterol-lowering drugs, a pre- conception discussion with multidisciplinary input regarding the risks and benefits of available treatment options is recommended. Shared decision making should involve the individual, maternal- fetal medicine/obstetrics, and lipidology and cardiology special- ists.
