If further LDL-C reduction is needed, consider adding bempedoic acid (BA) or a PCSK9 inhibitor. 3 Approach to Statin-Associated Muscle Symptoms (SAMS in Adults) For most individuals, statins are safe and well tolerated, and the benefits of therapy clearly outweigh the risk for adverse effects, including the potential development of diabetes. A subset of in- dividuals may experience SAMS or statin intolerance (Algorithm Fig. 6). SAMS are characterized by muscle complaints such as myalgia, weakness, or tenderness after starting statins, which improve or resolve after dose reduction or discontinuation.
