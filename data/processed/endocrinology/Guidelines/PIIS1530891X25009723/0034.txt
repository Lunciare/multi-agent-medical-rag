Older Adults More than 80% of ASCVD deaths occur in individuals 65 years of age and older. 73 Older adults face higher risks of recurrent myocardial infarction, stroke, and death, highlighting a heavy ASCVD burden. The strongest evidence for cholesterol- lowering benefits in older adults comes from secondary pre- vention studies.
