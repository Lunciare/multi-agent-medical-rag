Caution must be exercised and include shared decision making with the patient and maternal-fetal/obstetric input. It must be acknowledged that the effect of fibrates on TG levels is not immediate and that in individuals with FCS fibrates may not result in TG lowering. 148 The risk of pancreatitis is very high when TG levels reach 885 to 1000 mg/dL (10-11.3 mmol/L) and imminent when approaching 2000 mg/dL (22.6 mmol/L). 48,136 Supervised dietary management in the hospital setting with <20 grams of fat is recom- mended. 48,136,143 Parenteral nutrition without lipid infusion or with controlled amounts of lipids, as TG levels allow, may be used to support the mother’s nutritional status and prevent the risk of low birthweight or premature birth. 136,143,149-151 Plasmapheresis is Algorithm Fig. 10.
