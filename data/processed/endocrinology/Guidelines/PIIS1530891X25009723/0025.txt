Diets with fat content of <15 to 20 grams of fat per day are needed in individuals with persisting chylomicro- nemia who have FCS or a refractory form of MCS. Targeting LDL-C with Pharmacologic Therapy for ASCVD Prevention When managing dyslipidemia with pharmacologic therapy to lower ASCVD risk (Algorithm Fig. 5), it is important to determine whether the approach involves primary or secondary prevention; the latter involves individuals with known clinical ASCVD. For secondary prevention, LDL-C lowering is standard of care. 3,4,51,52 Some primary prevention categories include individuals at mod- erate to high ASCVD risk, who also warrant LDL-C—lowering therapy.
