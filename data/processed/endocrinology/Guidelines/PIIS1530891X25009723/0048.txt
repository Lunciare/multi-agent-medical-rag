There are insufficient data to support its use in pregnancy. 121 The car- diovascular benefits of niacin are questionable, and it crosses the placenta. Its use in pregnancy should be avoided. 121 PCSK9 mAbs cross the placenta more readily as pregnancy progresses. 131,132 A recent Mendelian randomization study modeling fetal LDL- C—lowering through PCSK9 drug targets showed higher odds of fetal malformations. 133 Until future information proves that PCSK9 mAbs are safe in pregnancy, it is recommended that their use be held at least 4 weeks before conception. Lack of outcome data on the embryo-fetal effects of BA and inclisiran preclude their use in pregnancy. However, inclisiran is a liver-directed small-interfering RNA molecule that targets PCSK9 mRNA and is undetectable in circulation 48 hours after administration, 134 providing months of effective LDL-C lowering 135 and making its use in the preconcep- tion period attractive. Further research confirming the safety of this approach for the treatment of hypercholesterolemia during pregnancy is awaited.
