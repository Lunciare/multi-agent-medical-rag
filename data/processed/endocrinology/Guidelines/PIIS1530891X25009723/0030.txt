For moderate to severe symptoms, it is advisable to check creatine kinase (CK) levels. If CK levels are elevated but remain <3 times the upper limit of normal (ULN), individuals may be managed similarly to those with mild symptoms as detailed above. However, in more severe cases where CK levels are >5 times the ULN, in the presence of rhabdomyolysis (a rare condition, approximately 1 in 10 000 individuals), or in the extremely rare case of statin-associated autoimmune myopathy, statin therapy should be immediately discontinued. 64,65 After appropriate treat- ment, non-statin therapies should be explored. For CK elevations between 3 and 5 times the ULN, evidence is limited. Clinicians may choose to rechallenge individuals with a lower dose or switch to a different statin or opt for non-statin therapy after shared decision making with the individual.
