No differences were seen in renal function or cardiac myopathy, although 1 re- view observed an increase in rhabomyolysis. 116 The use of PCSK9 inhibitors has not been extensively evaluated in individuals after organ transplant. A systematic review of 6 studies (97 individuals) saw no serious adverse drug reactions and stable coronary plaque thickness after heart transplant. 119 A case series of 12 individuals treated with either PCSK9 mAb after kid- ney transplant showed safety and tolerability with these medica- tions.
