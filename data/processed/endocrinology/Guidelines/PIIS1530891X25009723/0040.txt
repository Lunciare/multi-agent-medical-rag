Individuals with psoriasis and RA, irre- spective of disease severity, may be considered at increased ASCVD risk. 96-98 In RA, studies show that statins significantly lower all- cause mortality and reduce the progression of atherosclerosis. 96-98 Large prospective trials with statin therapy in SLE are lacking; smaller studies (the Lupus Atherosclerosis Prevention Study and Atherosclerosis Prevention in Pediatric Lupus Erythematosus studies) did not show benefit in terms of progression of subclinical measures of atherosclerosis. 99,100 Post hoc analysis of the Treating to New Targets and Incremental Decrease in End Points Through Aggressive Lipid Lowering trials show a similar decrease in event rates in individuals with and without psoriasis when statin was used for secondary prevention. 101 A meta-analysis of mostly non- randomized studies involving 148 722 individuals with autoim- mune conditions, including SLE, RA, and T1D, showed that statin use for primary prevention was associated with lower risks of mortality and cardiovascular events. 102 Despite limited studies, an Algorithm Fig. 8.
