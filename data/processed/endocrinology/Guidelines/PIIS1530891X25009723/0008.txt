Risk assessment and testing for dyslipidemia. small improvements in risk assessment and remains an option for additional risk assessment. 13-15 ASCVD risk assessment is critical in the management of adults with dyslipidemia. It allows for the prevention of ASCVD and the definition of individualized treatment goals which include shared decision making and promote risk behavior modification. Once a 10-year ASCVD risk is estimated, individuals are classified as fall- ing within a low (<7.5%), intermediate (7.5-20%), or high (>20%) risk category. The main treatment recommendation for individuals at low risk is lifestyle intervention that includes diet and physical activity modifications (See Algorithm Fig. 4 below). Pharmacologic lipid management is not recommended for individuals at low risk for ASCVD. In individuals whose lipid levels are not at goal, shared decision making on pharmacologic intervention, with discussion of risks and absolute benefits, is recommended. 3 For individuals at high risk, pharmacologic therapy should be added to lifestyle interventions (Algorithm Fig. 5). For individuals at intermediate risk, clinicians should discuss benefits and harms of treatment options (See Algorithm Fig. 5 below).
