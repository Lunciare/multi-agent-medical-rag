This 2025 algorithm emphasizes lifestyle modification, the importance of managing atherosclerotic risk factors, and provides a framework regarding pharmacologic choices for the treatment of individuals with dyslipidemia, in alignment with the 2025 AACE Clinical Practice Guideline on Management of Adults with Dyslipi- demia. 3 The task force acknowledged that health care disparities and lack of access to newer medications remain significant bar- riers. In addition, task force members included information on new topics such as the management of dyslipidemia in special populations and genetic dyslipidemias. The algorithm is divided into specific sections with graphics that outline the guiding principles of person-centered management (Algorithm Fig. 1), appropriate evaluation and ASCVD risk assess- ment (Algorithm Figs. 2 and 3), lifestyle modifications specific for individuals with dyslipidemia (Algorithm Fig. 4), risk-stratified treatment of low-density lipoprotein cholesterol (LDL-C) and statin-associated muscle symptoms (SAMS) (Algorithm Figs. 5 and 6), and treatment of adults with hypertriglyceridemia (HTG) (Algorithm Fig. 7).
