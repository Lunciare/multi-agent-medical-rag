In individuals with homozygous FH, an assessment of coro- nary status and severity of aortic stenosis is necessary. 14 Preconception genetic counseling may be advisable for individuals with FH who are contemplating pregnancy. A lipid profile before pregnancy or when pregnancy is first identified will inform the treatment plan. If dyslipidemia is newly diagnosed, exclusion of secondary causes is necessary. Adherence to a healthy lifestyle with dietary modifications that reduce intake of saturated and trans fats is recommended. Physical activity may need to be adjusted according to pregnancy status. 121 Statin use among adults of reproductive age has increased. In 2021, the FDA removed its strongest warning against the use of statins in pregnancy considering that these medications may help reduce serious or potentially fatal events in a small group of high- risk pregnant individuals, including those with FH and/or a history of previous cardiovascular events. 122 However, they clarify that statins should be discontinued in most individuals during preg- nancy and not prescribed during lactation.
