Clinicians should also be aware of the increased risk resulting from common drug interactions (Algorithm Fig. 6), substance use dis- orders, advanced age, certain ethnicities, and the presence of CKD. 61,62 After ruling out secondary causes, the next step in managing SAMS depends on symptom severity. For mild symptoms, clini- cians may consider rechallenging with a lower statin dose or a hydrophilic statin (which may have a lower incidence of myop- athy), along with cautious dose up-titration. Alternative strategies, such as less frequent dosing (e.g, 1-3 times per week) or substi- tution of nonstatin lipid-lowering medications, may also alleviate symptoms. 63 If a rechallenge fails or the LDL-C remains above target levels, nonstatin therapies should be considered.
