Identifying Genetic Dyslipidemias In all individuals at initial assessment, an elevated LDL-C is defined as >160 mg/dL, an elevated TG (fasting) as >150 mg/dL, and a low high-density lipoprotein cholesterol (HDL-C) is defined as <45 mg/dL for men and <55 mg/dL for women.
