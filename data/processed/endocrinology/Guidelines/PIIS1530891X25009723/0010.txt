Regardless of the tool used, risks and benefits, as well as harms of treatment compared with no treatment should be discussed with the individual and/or caregivers to underscore the importance of both lifestyle changes and pharmacologic treatment. Tools and algorithms used to estimate ASCVD risk include 16 : • Pooled Cohort Equations ASCVD Risk Calculator: Estimates the 10-year and lifetime risk of cardiovascular disease (https:// tools.acc.org/ascvd-risk-estimator-plus/#!/calculate/estimate/). • PREVENT Calculator: Designed to estimate the 10-and 30-year ASCVD risk for individuals 30-79 years of age. Includes ZIP code as a measure of social deprivation index in lieu of race (https:// professional.heart.org/en/guidelines-and-statements/prevent- calculator). • Framingham Risk Score: Estimates 10-year cardiovascular risk based on data from the Framingham Heart Study ( mdcalc.com/calc/38/framingham-risk-score-hard-coronary- heart-disease). • SCORE2 and SCORE2-OP: Provides risk estimates based on Eu- ropean populations, particularly informative for individuals >79 years of age ( Practice-Tools/CVD-prevention-toolbox/SCORE-Risk-Charts). • PAHO Cardiovascular Risk Calculator: Developed to estimate the risk of developing ASCVD within the next 10 years among Latin American and Caribbean populations ( org/en/hearts-americas/cardiovascular-risk-calculator-app). • QRISK: Developed using large data sets within the UK National years of age (including those with T2DM).
