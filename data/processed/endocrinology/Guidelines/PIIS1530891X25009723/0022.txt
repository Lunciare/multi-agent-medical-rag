HTG is linked to an increased risk of ASCVD. 46 In instances of mild/moderate HTG (TG 150-499 mg/dL or 1.7-5.6 mmol/L), the treatment priority is to reduce ASCVD risk; in that sense, LDL-C must be the primary target based on the person’s individual risk. It is important to emphasize that the TG-carrying particles in this range are predominantly derived from very low-density lipopro- tein (hepatic origin) or their remnants and focuses attention on the metabolic pathways that impact increased very low-density lipo- protein secretion and/or clearance issues.
