If TG levels remain above 500 mg/dL (5.6 mmol/L) despite dietary fat restriction and omega-3 FA treatment (prescription grade) in an individual who is pregnant, a brief hospitalization to optimize and support dietary management, including dietary fat restriction under medical observation, may be necessary.
