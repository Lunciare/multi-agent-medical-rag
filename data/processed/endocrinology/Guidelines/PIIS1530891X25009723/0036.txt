As in the general population, most individuals receiving gender-affirming care fall into the primary prevention category and need only to have conventional risk factors assessed (Algorithm Fig. 8). The use of HRT may cause small changes in lipid levels. 85,86 There are no prospective high-quality studies to form a robust opinion on whether these changes will lead to an increase in ASCVD. 87,88 Risk assessment must be nuanced as high-risk be- haviors, including continued use of tobacco products, may in- crease ASCVD risk significantly. In 2022, transgender individuals represented 2% of new HIV diagnoses, with transgender women accounting for the majority of diagnosis of HIV. 89 HIV increases the risk of ASCVD, and statin use may be indicated at a higher rate for transgender women.
