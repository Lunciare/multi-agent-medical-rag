Competing risks and life expectancy are crucial for older adults. Muscle-related side effects do not appear to be more common in these individuals at higher doses. 82 However, when muscle symptoms occur, strategies like dose reduction, agent switching, or interval dosing may help. In individuals taking other medications metabolized by the CYP450 system, pravastatin or rosuvastatin (partially metabolized by CYP450) are preferred. 83 For older individuals with ASCVD or at increased risk for ASCVD, ezetimibe and PCSK9 inhibitors, may be added to statin therapy if the LDL-C goal is not reached with statin therapy alone. 3-5,84 However, data regarding the safety and efficacy of these drugs in older adults are currently limited.
