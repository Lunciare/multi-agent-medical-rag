Algorithm for Management of Adults with Dyslipidemia
KEYWORDS: risk, individuals, ascvd, algorithm, adults, levels, management, treatment, dyslipidemia, ldl-c, htg, use, ascvd risk, mmol, pregnancy

Key words: atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease cardiovascular risk cholesterol dyslipidemia guideline Objective: To provide visual guidance in concise algorithms and tables to assist with clinical decision making in the management of adults with dyslipidemia to reduce risk of ASCVD and triglyceride- induced pancreatitis. Methods: An international multidisciplinary task force of clinical experts was convened to update the 2020 AACE algorithm for dyslipidemia. Literature searches informed the creation of visual guidance graphics and supporting narratives that reflect consensus of the task force.
