For assessment of risk in individuals receiving gender-affirming care, particularly hormone therapy, clinicians may use conventional risk calculators for both sex at birth and patient self-identified gender and then use the highest risk for shared decision making about the use of pharmacologic therapy. Adults Living with HIV People living with HIV have a higher risk of acute coronary syndromes and unfavorable post-event outcomes than those without HIV. 90,91 Factors explaining their increased cardiovascular risk and the early occurrence of ASCVD include, in addition to traditional risk factors such as smoking, low-grade inflammation, immune activation, and the dysmetabolic effects associated with antiretroviral therapy (ART). Algorithm Fig. 7. Management of adults with hypertriglyceridemia. Adults with HIV should receive appropriate lifestyle counseling (Algorithm Fig. 8), including smoking cessation. Given the variable effects of antiretroviral agents on lipids and their drug-drug in- teractions, prescribing physicians should work closely with an infectious disease specialist to choose ART that achieves effective viral control while minimizing the adverse metabolic effects of these medications, including dyslipidemia.
