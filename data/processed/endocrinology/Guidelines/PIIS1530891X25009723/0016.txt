Algorithms for determining FCS probabilities are available. 30,31 Furthermore, some therapies approved by the FDA are now available for treatment of FCS 33 and others may be available in the near future for FCS and MCS. A common reason to pursue genetic testing with the suspicion of FCS would be the observation of otherwise unexplained acute pancreatitis in the setting of severe HTG. Genetic testing panels include LPL, GPIHBP1, APOA5, APOC2, and LMF1; biallelic pathogenic variants are diag- nostic for FCS while single heterozygous variants are inconsis- tently seen in MCS. Dysbetalipoproteinemia Dysbetalipoproteinemia should be suspected if there is a near- equimolar rise in TG and total cholesterol. Simultaneous observa- tion of tuberoeruptive xanthomas on elbows, knees, or back of the hands is pathognomonic. Typical for this condition is that it can fluctuate between a seemingly normal lipid profile and extreme elevations of both cholesterol and TG. At times of extreme TG and cholesterol elevations there is often an identifiable precipitant, such as weight gain, persistent hyperglycemia in individuals with diabetes, or a new medication.
