Adults who are Transplant Recipients Individuals who have undergone whole organ transplantation have an increased prevalence of dyslipidemia and are at increased risk of ASCVD. They should receive lifestyle counseling and phar- macologic therapy accordingly. Underlying causes for dyslipidemia and possible side effects related to drug-drug interactions should be considered when discussing treatment options (Algorithm Figs. 6 and 8). 9,114 Drug-drug interactions include statins and some immunosuppressive agents like cyclosporine and tacrolimus that are metabolized by the same CYP enzymes.
