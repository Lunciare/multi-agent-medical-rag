A multidisciplinary task force with representatives from AACE and several international cosponsoring organizations (Canadian Cardiovascular Society, European Association for the Study of Dia- betes, and the Latin American Academy for the Study of Lipids and Cardiometabolic Risk) were appointed to determine the scope and breadth of the algorithm and the areas in need of updated guidance. Searches were conducted by a methodology fellow to identify peer- reviewed literature in response to specific questions from the task force. Clinical practice guidelines and systematic reviews of sufficient quality were prioritized followed by randomized controlled trials and observational studies. Where data were limited or unavailable, the task force used their judgment and expertise. The process for updating the algorithm involved multiple task force meetings be- tween January 2024 and February 2025. Smaller groups focused on specific sections, which were then brought to the complete task force for discussion and review. A proportion of algorithm task force members overlapped with the 2025 dyslipidemia clinical practice guideline to ensure continuity and content alignment.
