Individuals should be counseled to limit alcohol and sugary drinks. For individuals with fasting TG levels in the 500 to 884 mg/dL range (5.6-9.9 mmol/L), ASCVD prevention remains a priority, but attention should be given to the reduction of TG levels to decrease the individual’s risk of HTG-induced acute pancreatitis. This is particularly true when TG levels reach the 885 to 1000 mg/dL (10- 11.3 mmol/L) threshold. At these extreme TG levels, the primary focus shifts to the prevention of HTG-induced pancreatitis. Elim- inating underlying causes for HTG remains critical. An immediate reduction in dietary fat intake is imperative. By doing so, the load of postprandial TG will be decreased with less competition in overall plasma TG clearance. Alcohol should be avoided in in- dividuals with TG ≥500 mg/dL (5.6 mmol/L).
