Introduction Management of dyslipidemia is key to reducing atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease (ASCVD) and triglyceride (TG)-induced pancreatitis risk. The global impact of ASCVD is high, with more than half a billion people affected. 1 The prevalence of CVD varies published an update of the 2017 Guidelines for Management of Dyslipidemia and Prevention of Cardiovascular Disease, providing evidence-based recommendations for the pharmacologic man- agement of adults with dyslipidemia by multidisciplinary care teams. 3 As a companion to the guideline, AACE subsequently developed this algorithm as an update of the previous iteration published in 2020. 4 This updated algorithm provides visual guid- ance for the management of adults with dyslipidemia to help cli- nicians navigate the complexities of screening, diagnostic testing, and treatment of lipid disorders. While differences in prevalence, were discussed in detail in the 2025 AACE Clinical Practice Guideline for Pharmacologic Management of Adults with Dyslipidemia, 3 op- tions in clinical management for different groups of individuals and locations were also considered in the visual guidance and supporting text below.
