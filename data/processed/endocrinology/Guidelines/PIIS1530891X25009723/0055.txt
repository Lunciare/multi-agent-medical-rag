Algorithm for Management of Adults with Dyslipidemia
KEYWORDS: risk, individuals, ascvd, algorithm, adults, levels, management, treatment, dyslipidemia, ldl-c, htg, use, ascvd risk, mmol, pregnancy

Insulin helps activate lipoprotein lipase and enhances TG clearance. Plasmapheresis is a therapeutic alternative in the management of TG-induced pancreatitis during pregnancy, particularly if TG levels are ≥2000 mg/dL (22.6 mmol/L) or if pancreatitis is severe. 152,153 Prospective studies in TG-induced pancreatitis documenting benefits from plasmapheresis are lack- ing; there does not appear to be a mortality benefit 152,153 but a small observational study in pregnancy suggests that it may reduce length of hospitalization. 154 Consideration may be given to intravenous fluids with glucose to provide calories or to parenteral nutrition (lipid-deplete or with low fat content as TG levels allow) to support the nutritional needs of the mother and fetus while fasting is in place. 149-151 In HTG-induced pancreatitis, oral intake may be reinstituted once TGs are <750 mg/dL (8.5 mmol/L) with adherence to strict dietary fat restrictions (≤20 g fat/day) that may be further relaxed (30-40 g/day) when TG decline to <500 mg/dL
