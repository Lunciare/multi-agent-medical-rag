Guiding Principles of Algorithm Guiding principles are provided to highlight the foundation for patient-centered management of adults with dyslipidemia (Algorithm Fig. 1). Cardiovascular Risk Assessment and Testing for Dyslipidemia For primary ASCVD prevention, AACE recommends that adults with dyslipidemia be assessed using a validated risk assessment tool (Algorithm Fig. 2). 3 In addition, we recommend that all in- dividuals (primary and secondary prevention) should be coun- seled on enduring lifestyle changes to address lipid and nonlipid ASCVD risk factors. Adults with ASCVD do not need risk assessment since they are already considered to be at high risk and should receive treatment for secondary prevention.
