Diabetes, obesity, and alcohol commonly un- derlie HTG and must be appropriately managed, ideally in the preconception period, with alcohol discontinuation if applicable. Medications that increase TG levels should be discontinued when possible. Individuals with HTG planning pregnancy should implement healthy lifestyle modifications that will improve TG levels, changes that they will need to sustain and maximize while pregnant to keep their plasma levels below the 500 mg/dL (5.6 mmol/L) range.
