For individuals at intermediate risk, clinicians should discuss benefits and harms of treatment options (See Algorithm Fig. 5 below). AACE recommends an LDL-C treatment target of <70 mg/dL for individuals with ASCVD or at increased risk of ASCVD including those with diabetes mellitus. 3 Some individuals at intermediate risk may benefit from additional testing for risk enhancers to aid in shared decision making. However, these tests have limited evidence to support wide adoption, and many individuals may have barriers with access. 3 There are several risk assessment tools available for use, and while all these share core risk factors, they all also have subtle differences. Risk category cut points may differ across assessment tools. Currently, there is no evidence to support the preferential use of any particular risk calculator. Clinicians should recognize the limitations of risk calculators as they may not have been validated in all populations and may not appropriately account for diversity and risk related to current or previous health conditions. 3 There are some tools that are preferred in specific regions of the world.
