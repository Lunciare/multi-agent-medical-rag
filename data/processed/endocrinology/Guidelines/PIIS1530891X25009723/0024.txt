If this degree of reduction in dietary fat intake is needed to chronically maintain TGs <885 mg/dL (10 mmol/L), and it is proving difficult to maintain adequate calorie intake, medium-chain TGs (MCT) and essential fatty acid supplements can be added to the diet to avoid nutritional deficiencies. Effective implementation of lifestyle measures aimed at lowering TG will have a very noticeable effect in a short space of time (days to weeks). 50 It should be noted that most adults with TG levels ≥885 to 1000 mg/dL present with the polygenic form of the chylomicronemia syndrome (MCS) and retain partial or full LPL activity. These individuals will be able to maintain TG levels <250 mg/dL by effectively addressing second- ary factors, a moderate restriction of dietary fat (25-30% calories from fat, ideally ≤40-50 grams of fat/day) and the use of TG- lowering medications.
