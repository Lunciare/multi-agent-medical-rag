Hypercholesterolemia For all adults, managing hypercholesterolemia effectively in- volves addressing underlying health conditions that may increase LDL-C and selecting diet patterns to support healthy cholesterol levels and reduce ASCVD risk (Algorithm Fig. 4). Likewise, medi- cations known to increase LDL-C should be avoided where possible. 29,42,43 Hypertriglyceridemia The plasma TG concentration varies substantially within a per- son and recently consumed foods will have a significant influence. It is therefore helpful to base recommendations on more than one determination and ideally measure fasting TG levels at least once. Addressing underlying causes for HTG is key to the treatment of these individuals. A review of medications that may contribute to HTG is warranted. 44 Lifestyle interventions aimed at correcting underlying medical conditions such as obesity 44 and persistent hyperglycemia in individuals with diabetes will reduce plasma TG (Algorithm Fig. 4).
