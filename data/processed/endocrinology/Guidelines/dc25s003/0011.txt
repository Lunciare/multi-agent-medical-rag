In addition to aerobic ac- tivity, a physical activity plan designed to prevent diabetes may include resistance training (12,29). Breaking up prolonged sedentary time may also be encouraged, as it lowers postprandial glucose levels (30). Sleep Characteristics Associated With Increased Risk of Type 2 Diabetes Sleep occupies approximately one-third of the day for most people and modu- lates a variety of metabolic, endocrine, and cardiovascular processes (32). The latest ADA-EASD consensus report on management of hyperglycemia highlights sleep as a central component in the management of prediabetes and type 2 diabetes, placing it, for the ﬁrst time, on the same level as other lifestyle behav- iors (e.g., physical activity and nutrition) (33). Sleep can be characterized using three key constructs: quantity, quality, and timing (i.e., chronotype).
