The DPP lifestyle intervention was a goal-based intervention. All participants were given the same weight loss and physical activity goals, but individualiza- tion was permitted to achieve the goals (12). The two major goals of the DPP in- tensive lifestyle intervention were to achieve and maintain a minimum of 7% weight loss and to partake in 150 min of moderate-intensity physical activity per week, such as brisk walking. Although weight loss was the most important factor in reducing the risk of incident diabetes, achieving the behavioral goal of at least 150 min of physical activity per week, even without achieving the weight loss goal, reduced the incidence of type 2 dia- betes by 44% (13).
