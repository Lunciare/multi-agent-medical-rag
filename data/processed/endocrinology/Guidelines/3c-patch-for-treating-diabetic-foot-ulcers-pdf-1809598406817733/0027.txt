It also noted that because the EAC analysis included no discontinuation of treatment at all, it was unlikely to provide a true estimate of the cost impacts of 3C Patch. The committee concluded that the lack of direct clinical trial evidence for the company's proposed treatment pathway is a major limitation of the economic analysis. The EAC and company used different data sources in the cost modelling, which changed the direction of the cost case for 3C Patch 4.8 The committee heard that EAC changes to the data sources used in the cost modelling meant that the overall cost of 3C Patch was increased by around £800 in the EAC's model A (a model without a separate infection state).
