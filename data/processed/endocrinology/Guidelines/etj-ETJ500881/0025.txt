How Frequently Should Thyroid Function Be Tested after IRT? Considering the high incidence of TD, the European Public Assessment Report of the European Medicines Agency about Lemtrada’s use in MS patients recommends testing thyroid func- tion every 3 months for 4 years after the last alemtuzumab dose .
