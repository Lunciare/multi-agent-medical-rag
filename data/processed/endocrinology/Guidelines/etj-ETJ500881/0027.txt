For How Long post-IRT Routine Thyroid Function Testing Should Be Performed? The TD onset has been described 32 months (average) or 16–23 months (range 2–107 months) after the first and last alemtuzumab treatment for MS, respectively [12–14], with frequent episodes of transient autoimmune thyroiditis reported 6–12 months before GD onset (panel member’s personal experience). In the extension studies of alemtuzumab for MS (5 years follow-up), TD peaked at year 3, declining thereafter . However, late cases (rarely) of TD have been reported up to 9 years after the last alemtuzumab dose . The European Medicines Agency’s Lemtrada-European Public Assessment Report recommends testing thyroid function every 3 months for 4 years after the last alemtuzumab dose . Among HIV patients GD occurred 8–33 months (mean 17 months) after HAART initiation [15–21]. Recommendations 6a.
