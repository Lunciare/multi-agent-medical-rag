How Should post-IRT Hypothyroidism Be Managed? with LT4 is indicated according to the general guidelines . In contrast to the usually irreversible hypothyroidism of clas- sical Hashimoto’s thyroiditis disease, a higher frequency of po- tential reversibility needs to be considered in hypothyroidism de- veloping post-IRT, as a higher frequency of atypical autoimmune thyroid disease is reported . This finding is likely related to TRAb found positive in about 70% of hypothyroid cases and with described blocking activity (TBAb) . TRAb mea- surement should be considered in cases of post-IRT hypothy- roidism; TRAb presence indicates potential later recovery from hypothyroidism since TBAb usually do not persist for a long time .
