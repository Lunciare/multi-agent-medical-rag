For the reasons outlined above, these guidelines will therefore predominantly refer to TA in- duced by alemtuzumab in the context of MS treatment, or HIV/HAART. Note that BMT/HSCT will not be dis- cussed in detail since mainly associated with thyroid dis- orders, including TA, secondary to the irradiation of the thyroid gland, and are unlikely related to the immune re- constitution alone. TA caused by IRT is predominantly humoral, induc- ing a rise of both thyroid-stimulating hormone receptor (TSHR) autoantibodies (TRAb) and autoantibodies to thyroid peroxidase (TPOAb) ; in particular, GD is the most common manifestation . Recent studies have demonstrated that TRAb with TSHR-blocking ac- tivity (TBAb) are responsible for 30–50% of all cases of IRT-induced hypothyroidism , which differs from the spontaneous form of autoimmune hypothyroidism where TBAb account for only 10% of cases . The frequency of secondary TA among MS patients treated with alemtuzumab in clinical trials has been reported as between 34 and 41% , of which 63 – 5% are cases of GD, 21 – 34% hypothyroidism, and 3 – 12% subacute/transient thyroiditis. TA occurring in HIV/HAART patients is less com- mon, with a GD incidence of 3.0% in women and 0.2% in men
