A GO-focused cross-sectional study identified 3/11 (27%) pa- tients with HIV/HAART-induced GD developing GO, character- ized by different EUGOGO severity: 1 mild, 1 moderate-severe, 1 sight threatening . There is no evidence suggesting that the clinical management of GO in post-IRT GD should be different from the spontaneous form . The clinical management of GO in HIV patients involves additional challenges, since HAART con- flicts with ATD, immunosuppressive treatments and analgesics for orbital decompression surgery often used in GO treatment .
