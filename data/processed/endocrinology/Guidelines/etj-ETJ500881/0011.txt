Should Thyroid Autoantibodies (TPOAb and TRAb) Be Tested before IRT? In one study, 69% of MS patients who had positive TPOAb be- fore receiving alemtuzumab developed TD, compared to 31% of those with negative TPOAb. However, the number of patients with baseline positive TPOAb levels was small (n = 16), and 85% of those developing TD were TPOAb negative at baseline . Hence, while pretreatment TPOAb levels may help to identify patients at particular risk of post-alemtuzumab TD, a surveillance program directed at only these individuals would not detect the majority of TD.
