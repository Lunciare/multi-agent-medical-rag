LT3 pills with a pill cut- ter would allow for twice-daily dosing such as 2.5 µg twice daily. At the start, the LT4 dose is usually reduced by 12.5 or 25 µg to accommodate the addition of LT3. The thera- peutic substitution of LT3 for LT4 has previously been calculated to approximate a 1: 3 ratio . In theory, the ratio of T4:T3 of 13: 1 to 20: 1 could also be achieved by a combination of DTE and LT4, an ap- proach that has been suggested by some patient groups, possibly based on the premise that there are additional salutary effects of DTE. Monitoring Monitoring is challenging due to the variation in se- rum T3 levels with current preparations. If peak serum T3 levels (2–4 h postdose) are within the reference range and TSH is not suppressed, this is likely to be a safe dose range, although not all patients are satisfied in this range.
