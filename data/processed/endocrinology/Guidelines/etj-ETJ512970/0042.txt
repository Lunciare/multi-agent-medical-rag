These include the hypothyroid-related QoL , the Thyroid Symptoms Questionnaire (TSQ) , and the Thyroid-specific Patient Reported Outcome (ThyPRO) measure . For example, a score > 4 in the 12-question TSQ, a validated instrument used to assess hypothyroid symptomatology, could be used as the threshold to define dissatisfaction with the current therapy . Potentially, an overall health-related QoL score using the ThyPRO-39 composite score of, for example, > 32 could be used for inclusion (see also Topic 7). The score of 32 represents a score sufficiently above a previously found mean among patients with hypothyroidism that these individuals would be expected to have worse QoL than average . Candidate measures of patient-reported outcomes (PROs) should include the complaint that is most impor- tant to the patient (e.g., fatigue, weight gain, and brain fog – also described as mental cloudiness or lack of men- tal alertness).
