Body weight was slightly decreased in the combination therapy arm in two trials , but although statisti- cally significant, it is unclear whether the weight loss would be considered clinically significant in terms of a percentage decrease in body weight with associated health benefits. One of these two trials was the trial of DTE and the other was the arm of the trial by Appelhof et al. that used synthetic combination therapy at a 5: 1 ratio of LT4 to LT3. Two trials showed a decreased cholesterol in the combination therapy arm , while a third trial showed an increased cholesterol in the combination therapy group, but this group also had a higher mean TSH value .
