For some of these secondary outcomes, a reasonable approach might be a consideration of “proof-of-concept” studies to assess the preliminary point estimates of the intervention and to characterize the effects on secondary endpoints (online suppl. Table 6). Such pilot trials needed to explore addi- tional outcomes of secondary importance as well as rela- tionships between variables could either be conducted as separate pilot studies or could be conducted within a larg- er trial. The advantage of inclusion as a nested study with- in a larger study is that this could avoid delay of the larger study.
