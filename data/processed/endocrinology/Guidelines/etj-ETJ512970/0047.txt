T3 Levels during LT4 Monotherapy versus LT4/LT3 Combination Therapy As previously mentioned, treatment of hypothyroid- ism with LT4 as an exogenous prohormone does not re- capitulate the serum TH profiles seen in individuals with native euthyroidism . Although serum TSH levels can be normalized, this occurs at the expense of elevated fT4 levels, an elevated fT4/T3 ratio, and low/lower T3 lev- els [10–12]. Despite the low T3 levels seen with LT4 monotherapy, the T3 levels achieved are, in fact, stable, presumably reflecting that conversion is a regulated step . With combined therapy with LT4 and LT3, the en- dogenous conversion step is partially bypassed. However, the amount of LT3 administration that results in no de- iodination of T4 to T3 is not known. When therapy with LT3 is added to LT4 therapy, measured T3 levels fluctuate depending on the dosage regimen (dose, timing, and fre- quency) used.
