The Requirement for Use of a Slow-Release Preparation in Future Trials of T4+T3 For the reasons already given, it is argued that to both monitor T3 dosing and use TSH as a reliable measure of appropriate dosing, a slow-release T3 preparation is re- quired and should be the goal of future trials. Recently, Santini et al. demonstrated using T3 sulfate as a slow- release preparation that combination T4/T3 therapy could restore a near-normal fT3:fT4 ratio with reference range TSH levels.
