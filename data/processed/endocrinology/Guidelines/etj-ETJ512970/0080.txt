Maintenance of blinding, either through generation of identical placebos or overencapsulation, is important for maintaining the study integrity. In addition, both participant and investigator would need to be blinded to the participant’s serum fT4 and T3 concentrations. Blinding would be confounded if par- ticipants experienced manifestations of high serum T3 levels such as palpitations or insomnia. In addition to randomiza- tion, placebo control, and blinding, a key element in a rigor- ous trial would be standardization of the assays used to mea- sure TSH, fT4, and T3.
