With respect to the issue of patient preference, al- though current data show that patient preference is not different from that expected by chance , the trials conducted thus far did not specifically seek out patients who were dissatisfied while taking their usual therapy. These trials recruited hypothyroid patients regardless of whether they were symptomatic or not. This could poten- tially lead to a dilution of patient preference that might have reached statistical significance if only patients dis- satisfied with their therapy were recruited. It is also pos- sible that if there is failure to show mood or cognitive benefits in patients who nevertheless prefer combination therapy, this could be due to our lack of knowledge about what is causing the preference and an associated lack of knowledge about how to measure this factor.
