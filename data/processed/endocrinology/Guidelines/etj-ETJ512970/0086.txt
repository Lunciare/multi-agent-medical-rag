Including both patients with auto- immune thyroid disease and those with hypothyroidism due to removal or destruction of the thyroid would be ide- al, with stratification of the analysis by group. Patients with thyroid cancer requiring TSH suppression would likely need to be studied separately due to the additional consideration of their iatrogenic hyperthyroidism. Al- though subclinical hypothyroidism is more common than overt hypothyroidism, participants with this diagnosis should probably be excluded from an initial future trial due to their retention of endogenous thyroid function and possible difficulty attributing their symptoms to thyroid disease. However, this too would reduce the generalizabil- ity of the findings, as the majority of patients taking LT4 are being treated for subclinical hypothyroidism .
