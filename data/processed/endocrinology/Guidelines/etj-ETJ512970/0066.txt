The second domain to consider is “responsiveness,” that is the “the extent to which a PRO measure can detect changes in the construct being measured over time.” This has a signifi- cant impact on the power of the study (see section on powering studies on the next page). Prior Use of PROs in Studies of LT4 or LT4/LT3 Therapy PROs used in prior randomized trials of combination therapy have mostly been generic PROs, such as the BDI, the Profile of Mood States, symptom checklist-90, and the SF-36 in four trials each, and the GHQ in three trials. Three trials of synthetic combination therapy and one of DTE have used thyroid-specific PROs. ThyPRO measures tiredness using seven items each scored on a 5-point scale, all subsequently reflected in the tiredness scale. When patients with hypothyroidism were compared with the general population, tiredness was one of the parameters that showed the most difference between the populations, with a large effect size .
