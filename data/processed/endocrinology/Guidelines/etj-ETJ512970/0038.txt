An ear- lier meta-analysis of the crossover studies suggested that 27% of patients preferred monotherapy, 25% had no pref- erence, and 48% preferred combination therapy . Sub- jective outcomes such as patient preference are likely to be influenced by the quality of blinding and ability to main- tain euthyroidism in study participants. In trials in which some participants had iatrogenic hyperthyroidism, this could either drive patient lack of preference or patient preference for combination therapy.
