These same studies have shown that the half-life of LT3 is 22 h or 22.9 h , clearly supporting the need to administer LT3 more than once a day to maintain reasonably stable serum T3 con- centrations. Of the 14 combination therapy trials that have been completed, 10 employed once-daily LT3 administration , 4 employed twice- daily LT3 therapy , and none used three times a day LT3 therapy. Assuming that three times daily LT3 administration is too onerous to be used in future trials , and considering that a sustained-release twice a day therapy may be the most reasonable option. Given that twice-daily LT3 therapy, although not ideal with respect to the T3 fluctuations produced, is likely to be the best compromise, modeling studies provide infor- mation about potential twice-daily LT3 doses that could be utilized .
