Reassessment 1.6.14 When microbiological results are available: • review the choice of antibiotic and • change the antibiotic according to results, using a narrow-spectrum antibiotic, if appropriate. 1.6.15 Reassess people with a suspected diabetic foot infection if symptoms worsen the person becomes systemically very unwell or has severe pain out of proportion to the infection. Take account of: • other possible diagnoses, such as pressure sores, gout or non-infected ulcers • any symptoms or signs suggesting a more serious illness or condition, such as limb ischaemia, osteomyelitis, necrotising fasciitis or sepsis • previous antibiotic use.
