Treatment 1.5.4 Offer 1 or more of the following as standard care for treating diabetic foot ulcers: • Offloading. • Control of foot infection. • Control of ischaemia. • Wound debridement. • Wound dressings. 1.5.5 Offer non-removable casting to offload plantar neuropathic, non-ischaemic, uninfected forefoot and midfoot diabetic ulcers.
