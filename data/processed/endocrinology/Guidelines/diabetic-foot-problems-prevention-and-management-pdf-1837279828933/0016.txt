Treatment 1.6.6 Start antibiotic treatment for people with suspected diabetic foot infection as soon as possible. Take samples for microbiological testing before, or as close as possible to, the start of antibiotic treatment. Page 16 of 1.6.7 When choosing an antibiotic for people with a suspected diabetic foot infection (see recommendations 1.6.8 and 1.6.9), take account of: • the severity of diabetic foot infection (mild, moderate or severe) • the risk of developing complications • previous microbiological results • previous antibiotic use • patient preferences.
