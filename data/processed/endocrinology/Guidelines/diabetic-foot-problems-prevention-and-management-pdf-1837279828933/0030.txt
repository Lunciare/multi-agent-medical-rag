Diabetic foot problem 'Diabetic foot problem' refers to any problem affecting the feet in people with diabetes that is caused by loss of sensation (peripheral sensory neuropathy) and/or circulation problems (peripheral arterial disease). Diabetic foot problems include: • diabetic foot ulcers • soft tissue infection • destruction of the deep tissues such as heel pressure sores • osteomyelitis (bone infection) • Charcot arthropathy. This guideline uses 'diabetic foot problem' throughout, because this is the term healthcare professionals will most commonly recognise for foot problems in people with diabetes.
