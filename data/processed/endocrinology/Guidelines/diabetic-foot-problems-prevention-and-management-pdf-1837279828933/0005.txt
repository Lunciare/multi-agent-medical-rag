Assessing the risk of developing a diabetic foot problem 1.3.4 When examining the feet of a person with diabetes, remove their shoes, socks, bandages and dressings, and examine both feet for evidence of the following risk factors: • neuropathy (use a 10 g monofilament as part of a foot sensory examination) • limb ischaemia (see the NICE guideline on peripheral arterial disease) • ulceration • callus • infection and/or inflammation • deformity • gangrene • Charcot arthropathy. 1.3.5 Use ankle brachial pressure index in line with the NICE guideline on peripheral arterial disease.
