prevention and management Overview This guideline covers preventing and managing foot problems in children, young people and adults with diabetes. It aims to reduce variation in practice, including antibiotic prescribing for diabetic foot infections. See a 3-page visual summary of the antimicrobial prescribing recommendations, including tables to support prescribing decisions.
