Chapter-Specific Recommendations/Suggestions We recommend LT4 treatment in women with TAI and TSH levels >4.0 mIU/L/ULRR to keep TSH levels <2.5 mIU//L (1, ØOOO). OS induces a rapid and supraphysiologic increase of serum E2 (4,000–6,000 ng/L) that reaches levels similar to those observed in the second part of pregnancy.
