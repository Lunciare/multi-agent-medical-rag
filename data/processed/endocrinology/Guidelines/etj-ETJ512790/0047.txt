Conclusions and Perspectives Thyroid dysfunction and autoimmunity are associated with female and idiopathic subfertility and many aspects of the subsequently applied ART treatment. Since the ev- idence on the treatment of subfertile women with SCH with LT4 is scarce and based on small or low-quality stud- ies, many questions remain unanswered, and more RCTs are necessary.
