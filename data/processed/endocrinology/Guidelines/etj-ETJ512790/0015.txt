Chapter-Specific Recommendations/Suggestions We recommend that all women seeking medical ad- vice for subfertility should be screened for serum TSH and TPOAb. TgAb can be added systematically according to the local regulatory authority rules (1, ØØØO). We suggest that subfertile women with TSH levels >2.5 mIU/L and without increased TPOAb levels (according to the lo- cal reference range) should be screened for the presence of increased TgAb levels if not yet done at initial workup (2, ØØOO). We recommend LT4 treatment should be started promptly in case of overt thyroid dysfunction (1, ØØØO). We recommend LT4 treatment when TSH val- ues are above 4.0 mIU/L or ULRR (1, ØØOO). Thyroid Disorders and Male Subfertility Benign thyroid diseases (namely, hyperthyroidism, hypothyroidism, and TAI) have all been associated with adverse effects on male fertility. In case of thyroid malig- nancies, treatment with radioactive iodine (RAI) may have adverse effects on male fertility, and it has been sug- gested that thyroid malignancies per se might be more prevalent in men with azoospermia.
