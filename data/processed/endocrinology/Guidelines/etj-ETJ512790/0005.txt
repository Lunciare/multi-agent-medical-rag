Hence, here, LT4 is of no benefit to increase the oocyte retrieval rate. It is impor- tant to notice that age (>30 years) is the strongest predic- tor of a low oocyte retrieval rate . Fertilization rates and embryo quality might be impaired in women with TSH levels >4.0 mIU/L and may be improved with LT4 treatment . The impact of thyroid dysfunction before ART on the live birth rate (LBR) seems to emerge at TSH levels >3.5 mIU/L and upwards . In a meta-analysis mainly in- cluding women with TSH levels >4.0 mIU/L, LT4 treat- ment increased LBR to comparable rates to those in wom- en without elevated TSH . In a recent meta-analysis investigating the impact of euthyroid TAI on ART pregnancy outcomes, no de- creased LBR was observed . This discrepancy with previous meta-analysis concluding a detrimental impact of TAI could be due to the inclusion of women with low- er TSH levels (<3.0 mIU/L) and the use of ICSI as the main type of ART .
