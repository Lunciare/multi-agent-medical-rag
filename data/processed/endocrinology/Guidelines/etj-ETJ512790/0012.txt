How- ever, based on these studies, association with adverse fer- tility outcomes seems to surface at TSH levels >4.0 mIU/L . To date, there are no randomized controlled trials (RCTs) evaluating the benefit of LT4 on spontaneous pregnancy rates in women with SCH/TAI. Among sub- fertile women with TAI, a meta-analysis of 3 RCTs, in- cluding 2 studies that used TSH levels >4.0 mIU/L to de- fine SCH, found a beneficial effect of LT4 on pregnancy after ART . Subclinical and Overt Hyperthyroidism and Female Subfertility Because of limited data in the literature, the degree to which hyperthyroidism is linked to fertility issues is not well known. Nevertheless, increased levels of SHBG and E2 have been reported in patients with thyrotoxicosis compared to euthyroid women. In addition, in patients with Graves’ disease, LH secretion is increased compared to euthyroid patients. Menstrual irregularities have been identified in up to 65% of hyperthyroid women compared to 17% in healthy controls .
