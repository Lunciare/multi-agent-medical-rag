Thyroid Disorders and Ovarian Retrieval Rate/ Fertilisation/Embryo Quality An increased prevalence of TAI (mainly TPOAb) is reported in women with recurrent pregnancy loss and subfertility and associated with lower AMH levels [16, 64]. This relationship raises the possibility that thyroid disease may negatively affect ovarian function, fertilisa- tion potential, and embryo development. One of the ma- jor in vitro outcomes of an ART procedure is the NOR that closely reflects a woman’s ovarian reserve.
