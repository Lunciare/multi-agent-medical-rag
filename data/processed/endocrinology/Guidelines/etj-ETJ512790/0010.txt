Various mechanisms for the relationship between thyroid disorders and subfertility have been suggested. Indeed, TH appears to play an im- portant role in the regulation of ovarian function, and consequently thyroid dysfunction may lead to menstrual and ovulatory disturbances . Subfertility is a disease of the reproductive system de- fined by the failure to achieve a clinical pregnancy after 12 months or more of regular unprotected sexual inter- course . Female-related subfertility accounts for 35%, male factors for 30%, and combined male and female fac- tors for 20% of the causes of subfertility. In 15% of the cases, the cause remains unknown .
