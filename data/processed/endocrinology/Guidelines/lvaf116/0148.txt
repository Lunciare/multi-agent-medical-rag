Users of HCL during these critical times spent 82% of their time in TIR during labor and delivery with a mean ± SD glucose level of 124 ± 36 mg/dL (6.9 ± 2.1 mmol/L). Individuals in the HCL group maintained this level of glycemia regardless of the delivery method including vagi­ nal, elective cesarean, and emergency cesarean delivery.
