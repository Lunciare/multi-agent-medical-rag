Should a single continuous glucose monitoring target of <140 mg/dL (7.8 mmol/L) be used vs standard- of-care pregnancy glucose targets of fasting <95 mg/dL (5.3 mmol/L), 1-hour postprandial <140 mg/dL (7.8 mmol/L), and 2-hour postprandial <120 mg/dL (6.7 mmol/L) in individuals with preexisting diabetes melli­ tus using continuous glucose monitoring? Recommendation 7 In individuals with preexisting diabetes mellitus (PDM) using a continuous glucose monitor (CGM), we suggest against the use of single 24-hour CGM target <140 mg/dL (7.8 mmol/ L) in place of standard-of-care pregnancy glucose targets of fasting <95 mg/dL (5.3 mmol/L), 1-hour postprandial <140 mg/dL (7.8 mmol/L), and 2-hour postprandial <120 mg/dL (6.7 mmol/L) (2 | ⊕OOO).
