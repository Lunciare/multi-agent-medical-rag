Ozaslan et al evaluated the feasibility of using closed-loop insulin delivery with a zone model predictive control (MPC) algorithm designed to be used by women with PDM in preg­ nancy268. Eleven pregnant women already using SAPT were enrolled in and completed this 2-day study, which demon­ strated that a customized closed-loop control system tailored to pregnancy glucose targets is feasible for women previously using SAPT.
