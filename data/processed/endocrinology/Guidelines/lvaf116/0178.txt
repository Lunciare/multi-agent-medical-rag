This team should also support ongoing, long-term established follow-up. • In many cases, postpartum care also serves as pre­ conception care (PCC) for a future pregnancy. Approximately half of all deliveries occur among individuals who already have at least one child, highlighting the opportunity for postpartum care to contribute meaningfully to PCC. There is strong evidence that preconception care improves several pregnancy outcomes in individuals with PDM.
