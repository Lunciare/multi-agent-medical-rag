Some adverse outcomes, including maternal hyper­ tensive disorders of pregnancy, delivery-related morbidity, and stillbirth, may be prevented by delivery before 39 weeks130. However, this is associated with an increased likelihood of neo­ natal intensive care unit (NICU) admission and other neonatal morbidities. Optimal delivery timing balances the known mor­ bidity associated with preterm delivery with the risks for stillbirth and maternal complications in ongoing pregnancy.
