The mean age of participants was 34.8 years, with HbA1c levels from 6.3% to 7.7%. Both groups had increased TIR (63-140 mg/dL/ 3.5-7.8 mmol/L) and decreased TAR (>140 mg/dL/7.8 mmol/L) at the second and third trimester with respect to first trimester. The HCL group spent less TBR (<63 mg/dL/3.5 mmol/L) com­ pared to MDI group (HCL 3% vs 2% vs 1% from first to second and third trimester; MDI 4% vs 3% vs 2% from first to second and third trimester, P < .05). The HCL group gained more weight and had a higher prevalence of fetal macrosomia.
