Metformin given maternally at doses that achieve equivalent lev­ els in humans resulted in decreased fetal weight and skeletal muscle mass and bioaccumulated in the fetal kidney and liver141. Considering the increasing prevalence of metformin use during pregnancy, the need for insulin therapy in most people with pre­ existing T2DM, and the concerns of intrauterine metformin ex­ posure to the fetus, the Guideline Development Panel (GDP) prioritized evaluation of metformin added to insulin compared to insulin alone in preexisting T2DM.
