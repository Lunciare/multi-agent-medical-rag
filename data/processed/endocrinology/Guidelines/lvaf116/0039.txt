No studies were identified that compared a screening ap­ proach to a nonscreening approach for pregnancy intention in individuals with diabetes. Therefore, the GDP’s approach to this question followed a framework proposed by Murad Wyckoff et al. G9 and colleagues45. This framework includes a series of factors that need to be considered to support screening: • Importance: The condition is an important health prob­ lem in terms of prevalence and/or consequences. ○The GDP noted that lack of pregnancy planning has been linked to important health problems in individu­ als with PDM. • Natural history: The condition for which screening is being performed has a well-understood natural history that includes a latent (preclinical) phase. ○The GDP agreed that the adverse effects of lack of pregnancy planning might manifest only after a latency period and that early intervention could improve these outcomes. • Difference in management and treatment availability: Those with positive screening test results would be man­ aged
