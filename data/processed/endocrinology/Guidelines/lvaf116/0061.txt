Comments Among individuals with diabetes who could become pregnant, there are 3 important subgroups to consider: (i) adolescent girls/young women; (ii) women with T2DM, as previously dis­ cussed; and (iii) women with diabetes and obesity. Women with diabetes and accompanying obesity require special con­ sideration, due to the high prevalence of the association and the potential impact of obesity on effectiveness and undesir­ able effects of contraceptive methods. Universally, clinician counseling about contraception should be noncoercive and patient centered. Shared decision making should prioritize an individual’s autonomy and be in­ formed by the clinician’s expertise36,104.
