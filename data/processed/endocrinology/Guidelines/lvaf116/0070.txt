The panel believed that the lack of information on level of glycemia when GLP-1RAs were stopped in early gestation was a significant limitation. The limited available evidence supports a likely low risk in humans of congenital anomalies, most likely due to GLP-1RAs being too large to cross the placenta. The indir­ ect effects of GLP-1RAs affecting placental function are not known. The risk of spontaneous abortion does not appear to be increased in the infertility trials106,119. The case reports were not consistent on effects of GLP-1RAs on GWG or al­ tered fetal growth. However, the animal and pharmacovigi­ lance data are concerning.
