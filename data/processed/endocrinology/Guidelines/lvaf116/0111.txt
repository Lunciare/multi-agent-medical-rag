As a result, improving pregnancy outcomes in T2DM may require opti­ mizing numerous factors in addition to improving the degree of glycemia and may require different targets for glycemia compared with T1DM20,191–197. Frequent glucose monitoring is critical in T2DM in preg­ nancy to appropriately modify treatment regimens with the in­ creasing insulin resistance of pregnancy. Although continuous glucose monitor (CGM) use in T1DM is considered standard of care4, the current standard in T2DM is self-monitoring of blood glucose (SMBG) (fasting and either 1 or 2 hours post­ prandial). Ease of use and increasing access to CGM technol­ ogy means that more individuals with T2DM are interested in using CGM during pregnancy. The American Diabetes Association (ADA) recommends that CGM in pregnancy should be combined with SMBG to achieve optimal pre- and postprandial targets4,198.
