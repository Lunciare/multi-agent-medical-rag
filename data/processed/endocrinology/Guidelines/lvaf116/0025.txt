Following the GRADE methodology, the panel considered all elements of the EtD framework, including stakeholder val­ ues and preferences (with input from clinical experts and 2 pa­ tient representatives), costs and resource requirements, cost-effectiveness, acceptability, feasibility, and potential im­ evidence for most of these EtD factors in relation to the clinical questions, highlighting the need for research in these areas.
