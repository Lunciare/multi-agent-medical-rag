Recommendation 3 In individuals with type 2 diabetes mellitus (T2DM), we suggest discontinuation of glucagon-like peptide-1 re­ ceptor agonist (GLP-1RA) before conception rather than discontinuation between the start of pregnancy and the end of the first trimester (2 | ⊕OOO).
