Preexisting diabetes (PDM) is defined as any type of diabetes diagnosed prior to the current pregnancy. The prevalence of PDM has doubled in the last 20 years and now complicates about 1% of pregnancies worldwide, ranging from 0.5% in Europe, to 2% in the United States, and up to 2.4% in Africa and the Middle East1. PDM significantly increases the risk of adverse pregnancy outcomes, as shown in Tables 1 and 2. Specialized diabetes care throughout all stages of the reproduct­ ive cycle is needed to improve pregnancy outcomes (Fig. 1).
