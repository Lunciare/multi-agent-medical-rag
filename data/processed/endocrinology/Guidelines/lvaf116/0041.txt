Comments Two subgroups need additional consideration: adolescent girls/ young individuals with T1DM and women with T2DM. Serious adverse pregnancy outcomes can be especially high among adolescents and young women. The early and unpre­ dictable timing of sexual debut, the high percentage of individ­ uals who have at least one episode of unprotected sex46, the higher occurrence of major malformations (20.5%), and the high prevalence of serious adverse pregnancy and neonatal out­ comes support early intervention to reduce risks47.
