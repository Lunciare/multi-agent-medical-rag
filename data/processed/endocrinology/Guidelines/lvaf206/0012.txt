The term is often used inter-changeably with Menopause Transition (see Figure 1) al­ though the precise definition of this can vary as it does not in­ clude one year post menopause in the STRAW criteria. Premature Ovarian Insufficiency (POI) is the onset of meno­ pause under the age of 40 years with amenorrhoea or irregular menstrual cycle for 4-6 months, in combination with low oes­ tradiol and elevated gonadotrophins. Menopausal Hormone Therapy (MHT) is hormone ther­ apy given to women around the age of the natural menopause to treat symptoms of menopause. Both unopposed and op­ posed oestrogen are used depending on absence or presence of a uterus. It may be started prior to the menopause and be continued for many years after it. Hormone Replacement Therapy (HRT) (also known as Hormone Therapy) is hormone replacement given to young women with POI or those with early menopause who have be­ come menopausal.
