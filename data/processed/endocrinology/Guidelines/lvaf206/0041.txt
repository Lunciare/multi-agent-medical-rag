It is important to avoid delay in diagnosis (see R 1.5) and start appropriate treatment early.58 HRT should aim at restoring oestrogen levels to the preme­ nopausal state and should be prescribed to all women with POI irrespective of the presence of menopausal symptoms un­ less there is a contraindication for HRT such as hormone de­ pendent cancer. The HRT regimen should be determined based on clinical history and comorbidities. Vaginal oestro­ gens may also be needed for women who still have symptoms of vulvovaginal atrophy while on systemic HRT.
