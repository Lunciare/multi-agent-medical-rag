(Good Clinical Practice) R 1.8 We recommend that all women with POI are referred to a menopause expert and where possible, a multidisciplinary team. (Good Clinical Practice) R 1.9 We suggest that where possible, women in perimeno­ pause, who are under the age of 40 and have apparent contra­ indications for hormone replacement therapy, are referred to a unit with particular expertise in managing such women. These include, for example, women with a history or high risk of a hormone dependent cancer, thrombotic or cardiovascular high risk, women who have an inadequate response to hormone therapy or those who have significant adverse effects. Management R 2.1 We recommend that a holistic approach is taken for women during perimenopause and menopause, not having a sole focus on hormone replacement therapy (HRT)/ meno­ pausal hormone therapy (MHT). (Good Clinical Practice) R 2.2 We recommend that women in peri- and post- menopause who are appropriate candidates for MHT can be managed in primary care according to recognized guidelines.
