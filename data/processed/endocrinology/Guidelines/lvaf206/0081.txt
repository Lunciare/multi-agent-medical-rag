Compared to pla­ cebo, MHT did not show any significant effect on non-fatal MI (7 RCTs), CVD death (6 RCTs) or stroke (5 RCTs).211 However, these data were of low quality and insufficient to rec­ ommend the use of MHT for secondary CVD prevention. Cardiovascular risk factors such as elevated blood pressure, lipid levels and glucose levels should always be optimized ac­ cording to CVD guidelines before systemic MHT can be con­ sidered.140,214 Transdermal low dose MHT is preferred when cardiovascular risk factors are present. Diabetes R 3.4 We recommend that well-controlled diabetes is not considered a contraindication for MHT use; transdermal oestrogen is the preferred choice. (⊕⊕⊕○).
