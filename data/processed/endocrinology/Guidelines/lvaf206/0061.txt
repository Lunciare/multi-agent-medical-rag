R 2.8 We recommend HRT in POI irrespective of the presence of vasomotor—or other climacteric symptoms as the multimodal benefits clearly exceed the risk. HRT should be continued until the anticipated age of natural menopause and then the ongoing prescription reevaluated periodically.
