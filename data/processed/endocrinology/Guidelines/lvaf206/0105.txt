Studies are needed to show if hormone therapy directly or indirectly affects cognition and risk for dementia. • RCT data are needed to determine the effects of MHT on other age-related disorders (sarcopenia, joints, auto- immune disease). • Longitudinal data are needed on the risks and benefits of the use of supraphysiologic dosing, such as that seen with pellets, regarding risks and benefits regarding breast, bone, cardiovascular, and cognitive effects. • The impact of the use of testosterone for effects other than on libido needs to be studied.
