These include, for example, women with a history or high risk of a hormone dependent cancer, thrombotic or cardiovascular high risk, women who have an inadequate response to hormone therapy or those who have significant adverse effects Women with POI or apparent contraindications for HRT, should be referred to an appropriate unit with particular ex­ pertise. Women experiencing inadequate response and/or adverse effects related to HRT should also be referred to menopause specialists. In most cases, changing the HRT route of adminis­ tration and/or dosage is sufficient to improve symptoms in the long-term (see also 2.6). Women should be supported to make a personalised decision on managing their menopause symp­ toms.
