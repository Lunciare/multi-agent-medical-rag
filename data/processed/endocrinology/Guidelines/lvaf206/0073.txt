However, further discussion is warranted as described in the sections below. Age R 3.1 We suggest a targeted approach to MHT continuation in women over 60 yrs. We suggest taking into account the effect on VMS and/or other climacteric symptoms, the changing benefit-risk profile with age and effect on bone and personal preferences. (Good Clinical Practice).
