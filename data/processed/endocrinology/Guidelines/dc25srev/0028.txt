Section 11. Chronic Kidney Disease and Risk Management ( Recommendation 11.3 was amended for clarity about optimizing blood pres- sure management goals. Recommendation 11.4a was revised to clarify that ACE inhibitors or ARBs should be titrated to the maximally tolerated dose to prevent the progression of CKD and re- duce cardiovascular events in nonpregnant individuals with diabetes and hypertension. Recommendation 11.4b was modiﬁed to specify appropriate monitoring for in- creased serum creatinine levels, serum potassium levels, and hypokalemia when ACE inhibitors, ARBs, or mineralocorti- coid receptor antagonists are used.
