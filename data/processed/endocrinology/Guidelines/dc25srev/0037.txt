Diabetes Care in the Hospital ( Diabetes care in the hospital stresses identi- ﬁcation and treatment of dysglycemia and provides glycemic goals. For the treatment of persistent hyperglycemia starting at a threshold of $180 mg/dL ($10.0 mmol/L), Recommendation 16.4a was amended to reﬂect that insulin should be initiated or intensiﬁed for the majority of critically ill individuals, and Recommendation 16.4b was added to state that insulin and/or other glucose-lowering therapies should be initiated or intensiﬁed for the majority of noncritically ill individuals. Recommendation 16.5a was updated to state that a glycemic goal of 140–180 mg/dL (7.8–10.0 mmol/L) is recommended for most critically ill individuals, but more stringent individualized glycemic goals may be appropriate if they can be achieved without signiﬁcant hypoglyce- mia. Recommendation 16.5b was up- dated to recommend a glycemic goal of 100–180 mg/dL (5.6–10.0 mmol/L) for most noncritically ill individuals if it can be achieved without signiﬁcant hypoglycemia.
