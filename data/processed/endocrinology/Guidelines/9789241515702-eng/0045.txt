T2DM with onset in youth occurs most often during the second decade of life and there is usually a strong family history of T2DM in first- and second-degree relatives. While all ethnic groups can be affected, prevalence is much lower in populations of European descent. It is the predominant form of youth- onset diabetes in some countries – 90% in Hong Kong, 60% in Japan and 50% in Taiwan. The presence of obesity varies with ethnicity and is found in virtually all people with youth-onset T2DM in the USA and Europe; by contrast, half of Asian children with T2DM have a normal weight. The presentation of youth-onset T2DM can vary from asymptomatic hyperglycaemia detected through screening or during routine physical examination, to ketoacidosis in up to 25% of patients (123) or hyperglycaemic hyperosmolar state (124).
