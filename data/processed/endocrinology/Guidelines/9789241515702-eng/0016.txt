With this in mind, the Expert group considered it best to define a classification system that prioritizes start treatment with insulin, particularly at the time of diagnosis. The group considered that the prerequisites of a clinically based classification system include being internationally applicable and using easy and readily available clinical parameters and resources; being reliable and equitable; and feasible to implement. The only classification system which could currently go some way towards achieving this is one based on clinical parameters to identify diabetes subtypes.
