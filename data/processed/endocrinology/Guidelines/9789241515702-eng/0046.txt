Clinical prediction models have been developed that can be used to discriminate between monogenic diabetes and T1DM and T2DM in individuals diagnosed between the ages of 1 and 35 years (126). It is found in around 10% of people presenting as T2DM in Europe, North America, and Asia. Pancreatic autoantibodies (especially GAD autoantibodies) are a feature.
