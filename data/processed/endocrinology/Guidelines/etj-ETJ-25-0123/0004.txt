Methodology used in this guideline is provided as Supplementary Material (see section on Supplementary materials given at the end of the article) Issues about the drug The pharmaceutical form of thyroxine available is the levothyroxine sodium salt pentahydrate (LTSS), introduced in 1949 (16). Levothyroxine sodium shows a markedly increased solubility and more reliable intestinal absorption compared to the free acid form of T4, and it is now the active pharmaceutical ingredient (API) of T4 tablets (17).
