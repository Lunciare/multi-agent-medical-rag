Recommendation 2 2a) When the dose of levothyroxine needed to restore serum TSH within the reference interval exceeds 1.5–1.7 μg/kg/day in adult hypothyroid patients with thyroid in situ or 1.8–2.0 μg/kg/day in thyroidectomized patients, always explore low adherence to treatment or even pseudomalabsorption.
