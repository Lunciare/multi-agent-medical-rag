There was some variability about the procedures for LT4 absorption test; namely the dose of levothyroxine and duration of sampling, the frequency of blood collection and the interpretation of the results (32). In fasting patients, 600–2,000 μg are given and the absorption proﬁle via measurement of FT4 level was observed at hourly intervals up to a 6 h period (32). In a cross-sectional study, a total of 100 patients (25 euthyroid, 25 newly diagnosed hypothyroid, 25 treated hypothyroid with TSH within the reference intervals, and 25 hypothyroid subjects with elevated TSH despite an adequate dose of LT4 over more than 6 months) and ten euthyroid subjects with true malabsorption were administered LT4 (10 μg/kg or maximum 600 μg) (33). These authors reported that free T4 increment at 3 h above 5.15 pmol/L had a sensitivity of 97% and a speciﬁcity of 80% in excluding true malabsorption.
