Although most studies investigating the impact of this issue showed improved TSH control after switching patients from tablet to LS or SG LT4, no prospective studies have systematically compared liquid gel formulations of levothyroxine with solid formulations in relation to clinical outcomes. 16b) Consider using LT4 liquid solution or softgel capsule formulations as ﬁrst-line in patients at risk of malabsorption of LT4 tablet preparations. Strong recommendation. Quality of evidence (ØØOO). Agreement 8/8 Is there a speciﬁc group of patients that might beneﬁt from the use of non-tablet LT4 preparation? Based on the features of non-tablet LT4 preparations and on the available literature, four speciﬁc categories of hypothyroid patients can be identiﬁed that might beneﬁt from the use of non-tablet LT4 medications. First, hypothyroid patients that have undergone bariatric surgery. These patients have gastrointestinal malabsorption with some differences according to the surgical therapy. As a consequence, they are at a higher risk of malabsorption of tablet LT4. These patients have been shown to have an improvement of TSH values after switching from tablet to LS (131).
