Eighteen of the 25 ulcers healed within 16 weeks (study end point), 2 needed surgery and 5 did not heal. 3.16 Skovgaard-Holm and Simonsens (2012) described a study of 10 patients that was completed by homecare nurses. Debridement using the Debrisoft pad was performed 3 times a week over a 2-week period. The efficacy rate of the Debrisoft pad was found to depend on the thickness and adherence of the slough and the thickness of the hyperkeratotic layer. Debridement reduced the area of thin slough by an average of 24% in 3 patients. In 6 patients, an adherence layer of slough was reduced by an average of 7%. The Debrisoft pad reduced a thick soft layer of slough by 10% in 1 patient. Three patients did not feel increased pain during treatment, but 3 experienced severe pain (VAS scores of 8, 7 and 6).
