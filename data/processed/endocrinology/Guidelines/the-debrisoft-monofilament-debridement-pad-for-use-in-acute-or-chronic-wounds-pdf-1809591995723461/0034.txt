Page 24 of 6 Conclusions 6.1 The Committee concluded that there is sufficient evidence to support the use of the Debrisoft pad in the debridement of wounds in a community setting. The Committee noted that the available evidence is mainly in adults with chronic wounds and accepted that there is little evidence specific to children or the debridement of acute wounds. The Committee also noted, from the limited available evidence, that the Debrisoft pad is particularly suited to the debridement of sloughy wounds with exudate and hyperkeratotic skin.
