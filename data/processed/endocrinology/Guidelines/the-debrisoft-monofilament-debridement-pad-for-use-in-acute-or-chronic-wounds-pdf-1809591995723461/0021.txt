The Committee considered that the role of gauze in clinical practice is particularly unclear, but it received expert advice that gauze is unlikely for the patient. 3.23 The Committee received expert clinical advice that the use of larvae is a valid comparator because they are now provided in bags and are regularly used in community wound management.
