(2012) retrospectively compared the debridement results using the Debrisoft pad in 15 patients with venous leg ulcers or diabetic foot ulcers with a sloughy wound bed with the results obtained with saline soaks used in a similar patient group. No quantitative results were reported. The Debrisoft pad was shown to deliver effective and fast debridement, but it was reported to be somewhat rigid when used on toes or cavity wounds. Patient-reported pain during the procedure was less than for those treated with saline soaks, especially for the patients with arterial ulcers. The slight discomfort reported with the Debrisoft pad seemed to be better tolerated than debridement using saline soaks.
