Although it was not stated explicitly, it appears from the results that each wound was treated once using the Debrisoft pad. Patients found the treatment very acceptable with minimal pain reported in 95% of cases. The reported time to debridement was 2–4 minutes for 10 patients, 5–7 minutes for 5 patients and more than 7 minutes for 5 patients. Skin condition after Debrisoft pad use compared with a previous hyperkeratosis method was rated for 8 patients and was 'much better' for 6 patients, 'good' for 1 patient and 'very good' for 1 patient.
