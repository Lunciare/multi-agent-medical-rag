Debridement was effective in 93.4% (142/152) of the sessions. During the debridement procedure 45% (n=26) of patients reported that they experienced no pain, 50.4% (n=29) reported slight discomfort of short duration (mean 2 minutes) and 4.6% (n=2) reported moderate pain of short duration (mean 2.4 minutes). No side effects after the procedure were reported by 56 out of 57 patients. No serious adverse events or adverse events were reported. Clinicians reported that the Debrisoft pad removed debris, slough, dried exudate and crusts efficiently, without damaging the fragile skin surrounding the wound.
