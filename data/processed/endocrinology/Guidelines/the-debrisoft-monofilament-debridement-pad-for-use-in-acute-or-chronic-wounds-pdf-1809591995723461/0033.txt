Revisions to cost model for 2019 guidance review 5.18 For the guidance review, the External Assessment Centre revised the cost model parameters to reflect 2018 values (original guidance values given in brackets). Nurse costs were inflated using the 2015/16 pay and price series. The main parameter changes were the unit costs of Debrisoft at £6.55 (£6.19, 10 cm × 10 cm), Hydrogel at £1.41 (£1.02), gauze at £0.42 (£0.39) and bagged larvae at £319 (£295). In addition, analysis was done with the larger Debrisoft pad at £16.38 (13 cm × 20 cm) and Debrisoft Lolly at £5.88.
