Clinical evidence 4.4 The committee was aware that the clinical trial evidence had demonstrated that dapagliflozin in triple therapy is more effective than placebo in reducing HbA1c and weight. In comparison with the other SGLT-2 inhibitors and the DPP-4 inhibitors, the company's network meta-analyses demonstrated that dapagliflozin had a similar effect on HbA1c as the other SGLT-2 inhibitors and DPP-4 inhibitors, but that the SGLT-2 inhibitors produced more weight loss than the DPP-4 inhibitors. The committee heard from the clinical experts that although there are subtle pharmacological differences between the individual SGLT-2 inhibitors, they can generally be considered as a class in terms of effectiveness. Patients with diabetes that does not respond to one SGLT-2 inhibitor will probably not respond to another.
