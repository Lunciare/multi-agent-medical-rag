The committee concluded that the company and the ERG's base cases had not fully captured the health-related quality of life benefits of dapagliflozin. Clinical effectiveness and cost assumptions 4.9 The committee noted that the company had used older UK prospective diabetes study equations (UKPDS 68) to predict the incidence of diabetes-related complications in the model. However, it heard from the ERG that newer equations are available (UKPDS 82), and these should have been used because they are more up to date. The ERG explained that the newer UKPDS 82 equations predict a lower incidence of diabetes-related myocardial infarction, renal failure and death, and that the use of the older UKPDS 68 equations in the company's cost- effectiveness analysis was beneficial for dapagliflozin. In particular, the committee noted that the main differences in modelled costs between dapagliflozin and DPP-4 inhibitors in the company's base case were differences related to the incidence of nephropathy.
