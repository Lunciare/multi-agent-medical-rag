For full details of the EAC's changes to the company's economic model, see sections 4.2 and 4.3 of the assessment report. 3.8 Results from the EAC's revised model showed that Neuropad testing incurs additional cost over a 10-year time horizon compared with all other comparators: • £775 per patient compared with 10 g monofilament testing • £1,075 per patient compared with Neuropad testing then 10 g monofilament testing • £1,792 per patient compared with no testing.
