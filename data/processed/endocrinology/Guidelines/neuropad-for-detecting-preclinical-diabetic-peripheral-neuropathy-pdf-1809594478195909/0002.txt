Page 5 of Current management 2.4 NICE's guideline on diabetic foot problems recommends that adults with diabetes should have a risk assessment for diabetic foot problems at diagnosis, at least every year thereafter, whenever foot problems arise and at the time of any for any risk factors, including manifestations of DPN, which should be tested using a 10 g monofilament as part of a foot sensory examination. If DPN is detected, a person's risk is classified as being moderate or high (depending on the presence or absence of other comorbidities).
