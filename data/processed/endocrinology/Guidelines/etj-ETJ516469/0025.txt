Neck Recurrences of Thyroid Cancer The majority of DTCs are successfully cured with ini- tial surgical treatment, followed, if needed, by RAI abla- tion . Even after apparently successful treatment, re- currence can occur in the thyroid bed and/or as nodal metastases. Repeat compartment-oriented surgery is the established therapeutic approach for DTC recurrences but lymphadenectomy results in biochemical and struc- tural remission only in some of these patients . Thus, AS and MIT can be considered an alternative to surgery for selected patients with cervical DTC metastases . EA has been reported as effective in local disease con- trol in a few single-center case series . The larg- est retrospective study, including 109 neck lymph node metastases, with a 38-month median follow-up, demon- strated complete response in 84% . Up to 5 EA ses- sions were needed for complete ablation and, despite re- porting no major complications, local pain and side ef- fects may occur frequently. The major limitations of EA are the uneven and unpredictable area of ablation and risk of ethanol seeping into neck tissues .
