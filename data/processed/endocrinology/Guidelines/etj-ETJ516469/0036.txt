Liver Metastases There is considerable experience with TA of liver le- sions, including metastases from different tumors [88, 89]. After RFA in 512 colorectal liver metastases, local tumor progression-free and overall survival were 72 and 30% at 10 years, and 72 and 28% at 10 and 15 years, re- spectively . Small ablation margins (<5 mm) and large tumor size (>2 cm) were factors affecting complete abla- tion and local tumor progression . Importantly, major complications following MIT were rare (1–7%) , and percutaneous TA was safely combined with systemic treatments.
