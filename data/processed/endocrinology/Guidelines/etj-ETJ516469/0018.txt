Since the first feasibility study using laser for nonsurgical management of PTMC , US-guided treatments with laser, RFA, and MWA have been documented by several centers [40, 44, 50–54]. Three systematic reviews and meta-analyses have provid- ed high-quality evidence of the efficacy and safety of RFA, MWA, and LA. The first study, based on 503 PTMCs with follow-up ranging from 8 to 53 months, demonstrated neither local tumor recurrence nor distant metastases af- ter TA . Cervical lymph node metastases were detect- ed, at 24 and 30 months, respectively, in 2 patients (0.4%). There were no major complications, and minor compli- cations (transient dysphonia, intrathyroidal bleeding, and parenchymal edema) ranged from 3.4 to 19.0% . A second meta-analysis, including 715 patients with follow- up ranging from 8 to 26 months, registered complete dis- appearance in 57.6% of treated PTMC. Scar-like rem- nants, devoid of viable cancer cells at FNA, were observed in the remaining cases.
