Concluding Remarks The major hindrances before implementing these tech- nologies in the treatment of malignant thyroid disease are the same as those for benign thyroid disease, which are primarily, lack of guidelines and access to these technolo- gies for thyroid disease . Realizing this has been among the main drivers of the present guideline and the ambition to establish teaching courses in the near future.
