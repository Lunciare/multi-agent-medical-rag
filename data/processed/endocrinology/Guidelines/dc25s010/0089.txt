Based on collective evidence, consider screening asymptomatic adults with dia- betes for the development of cardiac structural or functional abnormalities (stage B heart failure) by measurement of natriuretic peptides, including BNP or NT-proBNP levels. The biomarker thresh- old for abnormal values is BNP level $50 pg/mL and NT-proBNP level $125 pg/mL. Abnormal levels of natriuretic peptide will need to be evaluated in the ment, in the absence of any possible com- peting diagnoses, particularly recognizing conditions that may lead to increased lev- els of natriuretic peptide, including renal insufﬁciency, pulmonary disease including pulmonary hypertension and chronic ob- structive lung disease, obstructive sleep apnea, ischemic and hemorrhagic stroke, and anemia. Conversely, natriuretic peptide levels may be decreased in the population with obesity, which impairs sensitivity of testing. In people with diabetes and an abnor- mal natriuretic peptide level, echocardiog- raphy is recommended as the next step to screen for structural heart disease and echocardiographic Doppler indices for evidence of diastolic dysfunction and in- creased ﬁlling pressures (229).
