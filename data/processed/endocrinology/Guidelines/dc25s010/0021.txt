Pregnancy and Antihypertensive Medications There are few randomized controlled tri- als of antihypertensive therapy in preg- nant individuals with diabetes. A 2018 Cochrane systematic review of antihy- pertensive therapy for mild to moderate chronic hypertension included 63 trials and over 5,909 women and suggested that an- tihypertensive therapy probably reduces the risk of developing severe hypertension but may not affect the risk of fetal or neo- bies, or preterm birth (52).
