and Cardiovascular Outcomes In 2008, the U.S. Food and Drug Adminis- tration (FDA) issued guidance for industry to perform cardiovascular outcomes trials for all new medications for the treatment of type 2 diabetes amid concerns of in- creased cardiovascular risk (253). Previ- ously approved diabetes medications were not subject to the guidance. Recently pub- lished cardiovascular outcomes trials have provided additional data on cardiovascular and renal outcomes in people with type 2 diabetes with cardiovascular disease or at high risk for cardiovascular disease. Cardiovascular outcomes trials of dipep- tidyl peptidase 4 (DPP-4) inhibitors have all, so far, not shown cardiovascular bene- ﬁts relative to placebo. In addition, the CAROLINA (Cardiovascular Outcome Study of Linagliptin Versus Glimepiride in Type 2 Diabetes) study demonstrated noninferior- ity between a DPP-4 inhibitor, linagliptin, and a sulfonylurea, glimepiride, on cardio- vascular outcomes despite lower rates of hypoglycemia in the linagliptin treatment group (254).
