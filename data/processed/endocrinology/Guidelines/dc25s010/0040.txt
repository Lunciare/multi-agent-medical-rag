Initiating Statin Therapy People with type 2 diabetes have an in- creased prevalence of lipid abnormali- ties, contributing to their high risk of ASCVD. Multiple clinical trials have dem- onstrated the beneﬁcial effects of statin therapy on ASCVD outcomes in subjects with and without coronary heart dis- ease (CHD) (99,100). Subgroup analyses of people with diabetes in larger trials (101–105) and trials in people with diabe- tes (106,107) showed signiﬁcant primary and secondary prevention of ASCVD events and CHD death in people with dia- betes.
