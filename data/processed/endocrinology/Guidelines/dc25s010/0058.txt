This is likely related to concerns and lack of knowledge related to use of lipid-lowering agents during pregnancy.The trials evaluat- ing the efﬁcacy and safety of lipid-lowering medications exclude individuals who are pregnant and require individuals of child- bearing potential to use contraception (some requiring two forms). Therefore, for many pregnant individuals, it is recom- mended that they discontinue lipid-lowering therapies during gestation.
