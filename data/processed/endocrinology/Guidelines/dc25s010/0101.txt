Early primary prevention strategies and treatment of associated risk factors re- duce incident, symptomatic heart failure and should include lifestyle intervention with nutrition, physical activity, weight management, and smoking cessation (271–274) (Fig. 10.6). The vast majority of incident heart failure is preceded by hyper- tension; up to 91% of all new heart failure development in the Framingham cohort occurred in people with a previous diagno- sis of hypertension (275). Therefore, man- agement of hypertension constitutes a key goal in people with diabetes and stage A or B heart failure. For example, in the UK Prospective Diabetes Study (UKPDS) trial, intensive blood pressure management in people with type 2 diabetes reduced the risk for heart failure by 56% (276).
