Screening for Asymptomatic Heart Failure in People With Diabetes People with diabetes are at an increased risk for developing heart failure, as shown in multiple longitudinal, observational stud- ies (216,217). This association is not only observed in people with type 2 diabetes but also evident in people with type 1 dia- betes (216,217). In a large multinational cohort of 750,000 people with diabetes without established cardiovascular disease, heart failure and CKD were the most fre- quent ﬁrst manifestations of cardiovascular or kidney disease (218).
