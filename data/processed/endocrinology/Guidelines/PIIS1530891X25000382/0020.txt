Endoscopic endonasal transsphenoidal resection remains the ﬁrst-line therapy for all pitNETs requiring treatment except for prolactinomas.44 Patients diagnosed with large pitNETs causing mass effect, acromegaly, or Cushing disease require surgery. Nonfunctional macroadenomas conﬁned to the sella and most microadenomas may be managed conservatively with serial imaging. Medical therapy is ﬁrst-line treatment in prolactinoma and is an alter- native therapy for other functional pituitary adenomas not cured by surgery or for whom surgery is not an option (Table 2).
