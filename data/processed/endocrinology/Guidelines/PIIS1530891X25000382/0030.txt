Although the aforementioned thera- peutic modalities have proven beneﬁcial in MEN1-associated NETs, treatment options that provide improved progression-free survival over extended periods of time are limited.51,72 Furthermore, treat- ment of MEN1-related NETs is more complicated compared with sporadic NETs because of the presence of multifocal tumors within multiple concurrent organs and metastatic disease that may be challenging to deﬁnitively trace back to a single primary tumor.73 Treatment Risks and Beneﬁts Treatment for advanced dpNETs in patients with MEN1 is based on the continuously evolving treatment guidelines for sporadic advanced dpNETs.
