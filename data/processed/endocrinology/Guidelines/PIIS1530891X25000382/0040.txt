Thymic NETs have been reported to be almost exclusively in male pa- tients with MEN1,87 frequently associated with smoking, with a prevalence of 3.7% and a 10-year survival rate of 30%.88 While commonly a disease found in males, Sakurai et al89 found a prevalence of thymic NETs in men of 7.6% compared with 3.2% in women, a majority of whom were nonsmokers, suggesting an increased risk of thymic NET in women with MEN1.
