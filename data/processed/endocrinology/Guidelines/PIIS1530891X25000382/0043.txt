Studies of patients with MEN1 have revealed that the occurrence of multiple facial angioﬁbromas may range from 22% to 88%, and the occurrence of collagenomas may range from 0% to 72%. Breast cancer can occur in 7% (considered at moderately elevated risk) of female patients with MEN1.96,97 Screening While MEN1-associated thymic tumors are usually asymptom- atic, they are a signiﬁcant determinant of survival because of their high malignant potential and accompanying recurrence and distant metastases.89 Screening should begin at 8 to 15 years of age and should be repeated every 1 to 3 years with low-dose CT or MRI.5 CT or MRI of the chest every 1 to 3 years is recommended for the screening of thymic and bronchopulmonary tumors.5,98 For screening of patients with MEN1 for abdominal tumors, such as adrenal nodules, cross-sectional abdominal imaging by CT or MRI should be considered every 1 to 3 years.98 The evaluation of adrenal masses regarding the risk of ma- lignancy represents an essential component in the management of adrenal tumors.
