Importantly, some agents belonging to 2 of the newer classes of antihyperglycemic agents, GLP-1 RA and SGLT2i, have been demonstrated in large, interna- tional, multicenter randomized, controlled trials to reduce ASCVD risk in persons with T2D and established ASCVD as well as in those at high risk for ASCVD.
