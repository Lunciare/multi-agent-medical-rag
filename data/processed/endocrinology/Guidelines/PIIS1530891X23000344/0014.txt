Get to goal as soon as possible (adjust 3 months). Therapeutic inertiadfailure of clinicians to escalate therapy or initiate new therapiesdis a major threat to achieving improved health outcomes in persons with overweight/obesity, prediabetes, and T2D.22,23 Clini- cians should continuously evaluate treatment goals at each visit, ideally 3 months, and consider making therapeutic changes to more rapidly achieve targets for glucose, lipids, and BP. Avoid hypoglycemia. Hypoglycemia, deﬁned as blood glucose (BG) <70 mg/dL, is associated with an increased risk for adverse out- comes including mortality.14,16 Therefore, the optimal treatment for T2D should take into account the risk of hypoglycemia. Anti- hyperglycemic agents and A1C goals should be chosen to avoid hypoglycemia.9 Agents such as DPP-4i, GLP-1 RA, and SGLT2i have a lower risk of hypoglycemia compared with that of insulin and SUs and are preferred to achieve optimal glycemic goals.
