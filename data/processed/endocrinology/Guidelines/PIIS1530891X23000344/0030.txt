Ancillary apo B measurement is recommended to assess for residual ASCVD risk from remnant and small dense lipoproteins not revealed with a standard lipid panel.75-78 Apo B is superior for predication of ASCVD risk over LDL-C and is more accurate than non-HDL-C.79,80 There are additional biomarkers, including high sensitivity C-reactive protein,81 lipoprotein(a) (Lp[a]),82 coronary artery calcium score,83-86 and ankle-brachial index (ABI)87 that are independently associated with increased risk of ASCVD events, and these may be helpful when the lipid management goal is unclear.88 Algorithm Fig. 4.
