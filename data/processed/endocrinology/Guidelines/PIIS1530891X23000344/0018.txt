Previous AACE guidance documents have utilized stages 0, 1, and 2 for ABCD severity, but a recent AACE consensus statement centered on obesity stigma and weight bias28 recom- mends a shift to stages 1, 2, and 3 to avoid inviting treatment inertia at stage 0, where primary prevention must still be in place. The revised staging also incorporates weight bias/stigma and mental health as key components of ABCD that should be addressed in addition to the cardiometabolic and biomechanical complications which can be improved by treatment of obesity. Patients may have an elevated BMI but lack physical complica- tions (Stage 1). Alternatively, patients without a substantially elevated BMI may already have manifested components of ABCD, and action is required.
