The use of CGM is recommended for persons treated with insulin to optimize glycemic control while minimizing hypoglycemia.20 Symptomatic Hyperglycemia Basal with or without prandial insulin treatment may be needed as initial therapy if the A1C is >10% and/or glucose values are >300 mg/dL, combined with catabolic symptoms, such as weight loss. If symptomatic hyperglycemia is present, a GLP-1 RA alone is not recommended as it requires titration and may delay glucose con- trol. The goal of initial intensive insulin therapy for symptomatic hyperglycemia is to reduce glucose levels safely and promptly. After improved glycemic control is achieved with short-term insulin therapy, especially with a new diagnosis of DM,157 a role for non- insulin antihyperglycemic agents could be considered.
