The dihydropyridine calcium channel blockers amlodipine or nifedipine also can be considered as add-on therapies. Understanding that many persons with HTN can require mul- tiple antihypertensive medications to achieve their goal BP, addi- tional therapies can include b-blockade. Notably, b-blockers can be associated with weight gain, thought to be secondary to decreased energy expenditure.116,117 This may be more pronounced with older agents such as atenolol or metoprolol, so the use of newer a-b blockade (carvedilol, labetalol, dilevalol) or b1-selective agents (nebivolol or betaxolol) may be more weight sparing. The central a2 agonist (clonidine) or a peripheral a1 RA (eg, doxazosin, prazosin, terazosin) may be needed for persons who are still hypertensive despite multiple therapies. Hydralazine also may be an effective adjunct therapy but requires multiple doses throughout the day.
