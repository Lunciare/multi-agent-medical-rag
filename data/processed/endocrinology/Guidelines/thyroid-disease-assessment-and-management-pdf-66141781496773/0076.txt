In addition, guidance on optimal treatment and follow- up strategies is needed for managing thyrotoxicosis, which is usually done by shared care between primary and secondary care. Opinions regarding the need to treat subclinical thyroid dysfunction, especially in older people, vary widely. This guideline also aims to improve the diagnosis, management and follow-up of non- malignant thyroid enlargement associated with normal thyroid function.
