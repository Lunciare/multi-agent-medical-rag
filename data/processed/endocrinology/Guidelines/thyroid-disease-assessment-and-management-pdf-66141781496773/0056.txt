These findings were consistent with the committee's own experience. The committee agreed to recommend total thyroidectomy for adults with Graves' disease having surgery. This was based on their experience that people opting for surgery are generally seeking a definitive treatment. How the recommendations might affect practice The committee was aware that the recommendations would result in radioactive iodine being offered as first-line definitive treatment to more people than currently.
