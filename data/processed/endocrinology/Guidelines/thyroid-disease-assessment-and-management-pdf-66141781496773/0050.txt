The committee agreed that based on their experience, TPOAbs testing alone is not likely to be as useful as TRAbs testing for the diagnosis of Graves' disease, but it could be used in children and young people where the absence of TRAbs but presence of TPOAbs indicates that thyrotoxicosis is more likely to resolve spontaneously. How the recommendations might affect practice Over recent years, TRAbs testing has become more widely available and more centres in the UK are using it to confirm the diagnosis of Graves' disease.
