Importantly, while the classical variant of papillary thyroid carcinoma (PTC) has demonstrated high stiffness, other variants of PTC and follicular thyroid carcinoma (FTC) may show a normal stiffness (44). Thus, the contribution of this method to the standard US imaging does not justify its routine use and inclusion in the risk- stratification systems (RSSs). Contrast-enhanced ultrasound A few meta-analyses report that contrast-enhanced ultrasound (CEUS) has a rather high positive and negative predictive value for the assessment of the ROM (45, 46). However, authors have reported inconsistency across studies and publication bias. Thus, even if these methods may add some information to gray-scale US examination, their use is limited because contrast agents are expensive, invasive, and not universally licensed for this purpose. However, CEUS provides a clear depiction of the ablated areas after thermal ablation (TA) of thyroid nodules and offers an advantage for guiding the need for repeat treatments (18).
