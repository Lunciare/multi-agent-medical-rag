Diagnostic thyroid and neck US should be performed in all patients clinically suspected of having nodular thyroid disease, or if a nodule is incidentally detected using another imaging modality (e.g. US scan of carotid arteries, computed tomography (CT) of the neck) (33). Anatomical regions to be evaluated • The thyroid bed and anterior neck from hyoid bone to sternal notch and below, if accessible. • From levels II to V in the lateral neck and level VI in the central neck for lymph nodes (Fig. 3). A high-frequency linear probe (e.g. up to 14 MHz) is adequate for exploring these regions. To visualize the inferior pole of an intrathoracic thyroid a convex probe (frequency range: 2.5–5.0 MHz) may be useful (34). Applying thyroid ultrasound in the evaluation of nodular thyroid disease First examination • Assess the presence, location, size, and features of nodule(s) and thyroid lobes.
