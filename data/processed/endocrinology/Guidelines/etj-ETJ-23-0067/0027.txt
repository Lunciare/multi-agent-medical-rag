Even if TIRADS scores provide quantitative malignancy risk stratification, users should be aware of the following: (a) TIRADS have been designed and mainly tested for PTCs, although they are proposed to estimate the malignancy risk of any thyroid neoplasm. The sensitivity for the detection of the classical variant is excellent but decreases substantially for the follicular variant and even more for FTCs (52, 53). Accuracy in identifying medullary thyroid carcinomas is debated (54). (b) Misdiagnoses may occur, especially in cystic nodules and in sub-acute as well as chronic thyroiditis (55, 56). (c) Composition of the nodule is not included in the cardinal features of the EU-TIRADS. However, the users should consider that the ROM is higher in completely solid than mainly cystic ones (57).
