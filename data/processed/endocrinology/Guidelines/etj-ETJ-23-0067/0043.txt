Other imaging modalities While there is no indication for initial evaluation of nodular thyroid disease using cross-sectional (i.e. contrast- enhanced CT and magnetic resonance imaging (MRI)) or functional (e.g. [18F]FDG-PET/CT) studies, incidentally detected thyroid lesions do have a ROM of 5–13% when using CT and MRI and of about 35% of high activity lesions when using [18F]FDG-PET/CT (33).
