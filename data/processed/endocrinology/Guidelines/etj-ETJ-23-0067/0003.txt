A more conservative approach results in reduced cost as well as a lower risk of complications (8, 9). Implementation of active surveillance and minimally invasive techniques (MITs) has been limited and measured (10). Overall, these observations cause concern for unwarranted expense and excess morbidity associated with thyroid nodule over-diagnosis and -treatment. This is exemplified by the abundantly demonstrated overdiagnosis of benign and malignant thyroid lesions without this leading to a significant lowering of thyroid cancer mortality (11, 12, 13). There is a pronounced need for more cost-effective, risk-adapted approaches to the management of this highly prevalent condition, taking the wishes of the patient into consideration. Based on the aforementioned, ultrasound (US) screening of asymptomatic adults is discouraged. Thus, the present guidelines aim at providing a clinical practice guide for the initial workup and the subsequent management of adult individuals harboring thyroid nodules.
