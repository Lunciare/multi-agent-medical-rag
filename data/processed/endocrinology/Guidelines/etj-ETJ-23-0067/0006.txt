The statements that panelists considered worthy of being presented, but inappropriate for rating the evidence despite a considerable and compelling amount of indirect evidence, are described as ‘ungraded good practice statement’ (20). Consensus on all the recommendations was reached following a modified Delphi process, which involved two rounds of voting using an online survey tool (Google Forms platform). Panelists rated each recommendation on a 5-point scale: strongly agree, agree, neutral, disagree, or strongly disagree. Consensus was defined as >80% of voters (that is a minimum of eight out of nine voters) strongly agreeing, agreeing or being neutral with a statement.
