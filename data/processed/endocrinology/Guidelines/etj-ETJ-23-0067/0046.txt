Therapeutic options: non-surgical approaches Clinical surveillance Most benign thyroid nodules are incidentally diagnosed and asymptomatic (4). In the absence of elevated TSH, the use of thyroid hormone in order to decrease TSH should be discouraged in order to limit the increased morbidity and mortality seen with such therapy (101, 102), but mainly because of its lack of efficacy in adequately decreasing size in symptomatic nodules (103).
