Older Adults With Good Functional Status and Without Complications There are few long-term studies in older adults demonstrating the beneﬁts of in- tensive glycemic, blood pressure, and lipid management. Older adults who can be expected to live long enough to realize the beneﬁts of long-term inten- sive diabetes management, who have good cognitive and physical function, and who choose to do so via shared deci- sion-making may be treated using thera- peutic interventions and goals similar to those for younger adults with diabetes (Table 13.1). As for all people with diabetes, diabe- tes self-management education and on- going diabetes self-management support are vital components of diabetes care for older adults and their caregivers. Self- management knowledge and skills should be reassessed following a signiﬁcant clini- cal change or hospitalization, when treat- ment plan changes are made, or when an individual’s functional abilities diminish.
