Additional research is needed to develop a reproducible classiﬁcation scheme to distinguish the natural history of disease as well as differential response to glucose management and speciﬁc glucose-lowering agents (69). Vulnerable Older Adults at the End of Life For people with diabetes receiving pallia- tive care and end-of-life care, the focus should be to avoid hypoglycemia and symptomatic hyperglycemia while reduc- ing the burdens of glycemic management. Thus, as organ failure develops, the treat- ment plan will have to be deintensiﬁed and one or more agents will need to be discontinued. At the end of life, most agents for type 2 diabetes may be re- moved (70). There is, however, no consen- sus for the management of type 1 diabetes in this scenario (71).
