C 13.8c Older adults with very complex or poor health receive minimal beneﬁt from stringent glycemic goals. Clinicians should focus on avoiding hypoglycemia and symptomatic hyperglycemia rather than achieving stringent glycemic goals. C 13.9 Screening for diabetes compli- cations should be individualized in older adults with diabetes. Particular attention should be paid to compli- cations that would lead to impair- ment of functional status or quality of life. C 13.10 Treatment of hypertension to individualized goal levels is indicated in most older adults with diabetes. B 13.11 Treatment of other cardiovas- cular risk factors should be individu- alized in older adults with diabetes, considering the time frame of bene- ﬁt. Lipid-lowering therapy and anti- platelet agents may beneﬁt those with life expectancies at least equal to the time frame of primary preven- tion or secondary intervention trials.
