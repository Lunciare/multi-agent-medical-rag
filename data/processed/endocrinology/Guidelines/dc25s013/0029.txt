Recommend reg- ular exercise, including aerobic activity, weight-bearing exercise, and/or resis- tance training as tolerated in those who can safely engage in such activi- ties. B 13.13 For older adults with type 2 diabetes, overweight or obesity, and capacity to exercise safely, an inten- sive lifestyle intervention focused on dietary changes, physical activity, and modest weight loss (e.g., 5–7%) should be considered for its beneﬁts on qual- ity of life, mobility and physical func- tioning, and cardiometabolic risk.
