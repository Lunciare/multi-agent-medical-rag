The study designs, the quality and con- sistency of the results, and the statistical analysis used to assess the effects of CeH treatment were carefully con- sidered. It was appreciated that only 1 randomized con- trolled trial (RCT) was available and very few reports fulfilled the established criteria. Retrospective studies and expert opinions were also considered. For this rea- son, this document should be considered as an “expert guidance” for clinical endocrinologists. The task force rated the recommendations according to the GRADE system . The strength of each statement was clas- sified as strong (1, a recommendation) or weak (2, a sug- gestion – not a recommendation), depending upon the clinical significance and weight of opinion favoring the statement. Strong recommendations are clinically im- portant best practice and should be applied to most pa- tients in most circumstances. In contrast, weak state- ments should be considered by the clinician and will be applicable best practice only to certain patients or under certain circumstances.
