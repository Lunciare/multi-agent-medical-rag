The text and recom- mendations were then verified according to the AGREE II instrument . Which Patients Are at Risk of CeH? The existence of CeH should be suspected in all sub- jects with a subnormal circulating concentration of FT4 together with an inappropriately low serum TSH. Im- portantly, thyroid hormone levels change markedly during childhood and adult reference intervals are not universally applicable to children . Therefore, the establishment of the reference interval of TSH and FT4 is critical in the diagnosis of CeH as these values can be affected by age, gender, iodine nutrition, and ethnicity .
