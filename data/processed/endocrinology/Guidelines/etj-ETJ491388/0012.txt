Heritable forms of CeH due to biallelic TSHβ muta- tions are associated with severe neonatal onset and char- acterized by the typical manifestations of congenital primary hypothyroidism (e.g., jaundice, macroglossia, hoarse cry, failure to thrive and retarded growth, umbili- cal hernia, hypotonia). If untreated within a few weeks of postnatal life, these patients develop cretinism compara- ble to patients with severe primary congenital hypothy- roidism . Therefore, CeH must be ruled out in all infants with manifestations of congenital hypothyroid- ism and inappropriately low TSH concentrations. Defective TRH action due to biallelic mutations in the TRHR gene has, to date, been described in few families [11, 28–30]. Though prolonged neonatal jaundice was re- ported in 1 female, even complete TRH resistance does not cause severe neonatal hypothyroidism.
