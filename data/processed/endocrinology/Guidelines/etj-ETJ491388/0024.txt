The determination of circulating free thyroid hor- mone concentrations is of major significance in monitor- ing L-T4 treatment in CeH patients [1, 24, 25, 49, 56–58]. Blood should be withdrawn before or at least 4 h after the L-T4 administration . The determination of FT4 ac- quires a more relevant role in the evaluation of replace- ment therapy than in primary hypothyroidism. Several groups reported that concentrations of FT4 in the upper part of normal range might represent an appropriate target in most treated CeH patients (Recom- mendation 26).
