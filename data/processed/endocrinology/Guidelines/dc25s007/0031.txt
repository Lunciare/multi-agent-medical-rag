In this study, rtCGM beneﬁts were observed at 6 month and 12 months versusbaseline, with reduction of mean glu- cose levels, reduction of GMI, increase in TIR, increase in time in tight target range (70–140 mg/dL [3.9–7.8 mmol/L]), and re- duction in TAR >180 and >250 mg/dL (88).
