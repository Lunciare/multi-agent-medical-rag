There are two basic types of CGM devices. The ﬁrst type includes those that are owned by the user, unblinded, and intended for frequent or continuous use, including real-time CGM (rtCGM), intermittently scanned CGM (isCGM), and over-the-counter CGM devi- ces. The second type is professional CGM devices that are owned by practices and ap- are blinded or unblinded for a discrete pe- riod of time.
