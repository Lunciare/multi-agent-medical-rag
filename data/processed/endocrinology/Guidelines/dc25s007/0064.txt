Similar to data on insulin pump use in people with type 1 diabetes, reductions in A1C levels have been reported in some studies (243,246). More recently, real-world re- ports have shown reduction of A1C levels and reduction of total daily insulin dose in individuals with type 2 diabetes initiat- ing insulin pump therapy (247). Use of in- sulin pumps in insulin-requiring people with any type of diabetes may improve user satisfaction and simplify therapy (171,241).
