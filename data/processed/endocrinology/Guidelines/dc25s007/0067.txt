B Open-source automated insulin dosing (OS-AID) algorithms provide the precise code that governs their operation, so health care professionals and people with diabetes can have a more com- plete understanding of risks and bene- ﬁts (254). Any commercial entity could provide the source code for their inter- operable automated glycemic controller, but most choose not to. OS-AID algo- rithms are largely designed, maintained, and curated by people with diabetes and their loved ones. Thousands of peo- ple with diabetes use these algorithms with cleared CGM systems and insulin pump components. The information on how to set up and manage these sys- tems is freely available online. OS-AID is the preferred term when re- ferring to any open-source system (com- mercial or otherwise). It is important to note that the term “DIY” is not reﬂective of any aspect of these community-driven systems.
