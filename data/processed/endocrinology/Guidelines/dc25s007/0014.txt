For many individuals using BGM, this requires checking up to 6–10 times daily, although individual needs may vary. A database study of almost 27,000 children and adolescents with type 1 diabetes showed that, after ad- justing for multiple confounders, increased daily frequency of BGM was signiﬁcantly as- sociated with lower A1C levels (0.2% per additional check per day) and with fewer acute complications (29).
