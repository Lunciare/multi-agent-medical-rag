of Care in Diabetes—2025 Recommendations 7.1 Diabetes devices should be offered to people with diabetes. A 7.2 Initiation of continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) should be offered to people with type 1 diabetes early in the disease, even at time of diagnosis. A 7.3 The type(s) and selection of devices should be individualized based on a person’s speciﬁc needs, circumstances, preferences, and skill level. In the setting of an individual whose diabetes is partially or wholly managed by someone else (e.g., a young child or a person with cognitive impairment or dexterity, psychoso- cial issues, and/or physical limitations), the caregiver’s skills and preferences are integral to the decision-making process. E 7.4 When prescribing a device, ensure that people with diabetes and caregivers receive initial and ongoing education and training, either in person or remotely, and ongoing evaluation of technique, results, and the ability to utilize data, includ- ing uploading or sharing data (if applicable), to monitor and adjust therapy.
