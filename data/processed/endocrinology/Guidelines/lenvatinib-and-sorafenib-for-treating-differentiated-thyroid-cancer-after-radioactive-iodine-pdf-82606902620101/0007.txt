Progression-free survival results from SELECT and DECISION Both treatments improve progression-free survival compared with placebo 3.3 In SELECT, lenvatinib statistically significantly improved median investigator- assessed progression-free survival compared with placebo (16.6 months for lenvatinib compared with 3.7 months for placebo; hazard ratio [HR] 0.24, 95% confidence interval [CI] 0.16 to 0.35). Similar results were reported for independently-assessed progression-free survival. In DECISION, sorafenib statistically significantly improved median investigator-assessed progression-free survival compared with placebo (10.8 months for sorafenib compared with Page 10 of 5.4 months for placebo; HR 0.49, 95% CI 0.39 to 0.61). Similar results were reported for independently-assessed progression-free survival. The committee concluded that there was evidence to show that both treatments are clinically effective in improving progression-free survival compared with placebo. Overall survival results from SELECT and DECISION Lenvatinib and sorafenib improve overall survival but there is uncertainty from the crossover adjustment and anticancer treatment after progression 3.4 The proportion of people crossing over from placebo to active treatment after disease progression was 88% in SELECT and 75% in DECISION.
