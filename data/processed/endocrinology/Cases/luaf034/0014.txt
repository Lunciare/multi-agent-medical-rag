Case Report
KEYWORDS: hypoglycemia, patient, tramadol, glucose, mmol, insulin, insulinoma, hours, did, days, glucagon, following, nmol, plasma, fluids

A detailed history including any new medication use will likely be cost-effective and can avoid prolonged hospitalizations and expensive testing. Learning Points • Increased awareness of tramadol-induced hypoglycemia is important. • The degree of C-peptide, insulin elevation, in the correct clinical setting of the acute hypoglycemia may serve as a clue to tramadol-induced hypoglycemia. • Blunted post-glucagon increase in glucose levels is incon­ sistent with insulinoma and may serve as another clue, in­ dicating that other acute etiologies should be investigated.
