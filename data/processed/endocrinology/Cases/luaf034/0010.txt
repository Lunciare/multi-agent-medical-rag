Discussion Tramadol is approved by the US Food and Drug Administration for moderate to severe pain. Tramadol is a commonly prescribed pain medication with 15.5 million pre­ scriptions written in 2021 in the United States. The liver en­ zyme CYP2D6 converts tramadol to its active metabolite M1 (O-demethyl tramadol), which has a stronger affinity for the μ receptor than the inactive form. The M1 metabolite demonstrates analgesic potency up to 6 times greater than tra­ madol .
