Insulinoma Mimic: Tramadol-induced Hypoglycemia Abstract Endogenous hyperinsulinemia hypoglycemia has numerous etiologies. The objective of this report is to describe a patient with severe hyperinsulinemic hypoglycemia with no known history of diabetes or hypoglycemia who presented with acute altered mental status. Blood glucose was noted to be 40 mg/dL (2.22 mmol/L) (reference range 65-125 mg/dL; 3.61-6.94 mmol/L). The patient’s sulfonylurea screen was negative. Following 1 mg glucagon injection, the patient’s glucose did not improve, a response inconsistent with insulinoma. Imaging studies of the pancreas did not show pancreatic mass.
