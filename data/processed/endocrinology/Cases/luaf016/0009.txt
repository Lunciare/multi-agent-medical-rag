This frameshift variant results in prema­ ture termination of translation within exon 1, causing signifi­ cant abnormalities in protein structure or loss of protein expression. In silico analysis revealed that this variant was disease-causing. According to the American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics guidelines , this variant is categorized as pathogenic; it has been found exclusively in patients who developed PHPT and jaw tumors, whereas other family members without the variant have no history of these diseases, supporting its pathogenic nature. CDC73-related disorders are inherited in an autosomal dominant manner, although cases of de novo variants have also been reported ; however, the proportion of de novo pathogenic variants remains unknown.
