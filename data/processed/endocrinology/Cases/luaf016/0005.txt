With written informed consent, we extracted germline DNA from her peripheral blood and performed next-generation sequencing using a genetic panel including MEN1, CDKN1B, RET, CASR, GNA11, AP2S1, CDC73, and GCM2. A novel frameshift variant, c.93del p.Trp32GlyfsTer5, in exon 1 of the CDC73 gene was identi­ fied that has not been previously reported in Clin Var ( Genome Aggregation Database ( or the Human Genome Mutation Database ( index.php). The databases were accessed on October 2, 2024.
