Introduction The differential diagnosis of elevated thyroid hormones in the con­ text of nonsuppressed thyroid stimulating hormone (TSH), once laboratory and medication interference have been excluded, is a TSH-secreting pituitary adenoma (TSH-oma), or resistance to thyroid hormone β (RTHβ). However, differentiation between these 2 diagnoses may be challenging, even with thorough bio­ chemical, genetic and radiological investigation. TSH-secreting adenomas are rare, comprising less than 1% of all functioning pituitary adenomas, with an estimated inci­ dence of 0.15 cases per million people per year .
