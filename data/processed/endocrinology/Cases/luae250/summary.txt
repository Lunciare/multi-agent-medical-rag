Ectopic Thyrotropin-Secreting Tumor in the Nasopharynx Causing Central Hyperthyroidism 5Department of Endocrinology, Ashford and St. Peter’s NHS Foundation Trust, Ashford TW15 3AA, UK Abstract We report a 31-year-old man with diarrhea and tachycardia. Diagnostic workup confirmed raised free thyroid hormones with unsuppressed thyroid stimulating hormone (TSH). Laboratory assay and medication interference were excluded. Consistent with a high glycoprotein hormone α-subunit (α-GSU), the α-GSU:TSH molar ratio was increased. However, anterior pituitary panel testing also confirmed an isolated, raised follicle stimulating hormone (FSH) (17.3 IU/L; reference range, 1.7-8.0).
