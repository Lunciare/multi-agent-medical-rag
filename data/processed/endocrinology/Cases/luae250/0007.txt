A pituitary magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) with contrast and a subsequent dynamic MRI did not reveal a pituitary lesion (Fig. 2A and 2D). A thyrotropin-releasing hormone (TRH) stimulation test showed a 232%-fold-change in TSH from baseline: TSH at t = 0 6.9 mU/L, at t = 20 minutes 12.9 mU/L, and at t = 60 minutes 16 mU/L (Fig. 1B), suggestive of RTHβ. However, fluorescent sequencing analysis of the hormone binding domain (exons 710) of the thyroid hor­ mone receptor β gene did not detect any pathogenic var­ iants. A 10-day T3 suppression test was offered to the patient, but he declined this due to the intensive testing schedule.
