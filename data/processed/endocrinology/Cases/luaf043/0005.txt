Case 2 The electrocardiogram showed a recurrence of atrial fibrilla­ tion with a rapid ventricular response. Laboratory tests re­ vealed hyperthyroidism, with fT4 at 40.8 pmol/L (31.6 pg/ mL) (normal reference range, 8.6-25 pmol/L, 9-20 pg/mL), free triiodothyronine (fT3) at 10.4 pmol/L (2.80 pg/mL) (nor­ mal reference range, 4.26-8.10 pmol/L, 2.3-4.2 pg/mL), and undetectable TSH (normal reference range, 0.4-4 mUI/L). Antibodies were negative for anti-thyroid peroxidase and anti-TSH receptor antibodies. The thyroid ultrasound de­ scribed a goiter with a left EU-TIRADS 3 nodule. A thyroid scan indicated asymmetrical lobes favoring the right lobe and did not show scintigraphic evidence supporting Graves disease or a hot nodule.
