Case 2 A 47-year-old man with hypertrophic cardiomyopathy and re­ duced left ventricular ejection fraction was equipped with a secondary prevention defibrillator. He developed syncopal episodes due to atrial fibrillation, which did not respond to electrical cardioversion and radiofrequency ablation. An anti­ arrhythmic drug (amiodarone 200 mg) was prescribed. Two years after starting amiodarone, the patient presented to the tightness, palpitations, dyspnea, hot flashes, and intermittent tremors in the extremities. Diagnostic Assessment Case 1 The electrocardiogram showed atrial fibrillation with a rapid ventricular response. The echocardiogram revealed left ventricular dilation with severe impairment of the ejection fraction, estimated at 15%.
