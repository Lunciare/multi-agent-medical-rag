Testicular ultrasound confirmed reduced testicular volumes (left: 4.5 cm3 [3.6 × 1.8 × 1.4 cm]; right: 5.0 cm3 [3.7 × 1.9 × 1.4 cm]), both below the adult reference range (>9 cm3). Magnetic resonance imaging of the superior abdomen did not ini­ tially identify the adrenal glands; subsequent imaging identified a hypoplastic left adrenal gland with a small adjacent cystic le­ sion, while the right adrenal gland remained nonvisualized. Treatment The patient is currently treated with prednisone 7.5 mg/day (5 mg in the morning and 2.5 mg in the afternoon) and fludrocor­ pressure and electrolytes. He also supplements 7000 IU vitamin D3 per week. Outcome and follow-up The patient remains clinically stable on his current adrenal re­ placement regimen, with no further adrenal crises. He maintains resistance training 3 times per week and reports adequate diet­ ary calcium intake. Persistent azoospermia prompted referral to urology for fertility evaluation. Long-term follow-up focuses on monitoring glucocorticoid and mineralocorticoid adequacy on­ going surveillance of gonadal function, reassessment of bone genetic counseling for at-risk relatives.
