In view of reduced testicular volume, semen analyses at ages 22 and 24 were performed and azoosper­ mia was detected in both samples. Given the combination of early-onset adrenal insufficiency and azoospermia, a genetic etiology was suspected. Whole-exome sequencing identified a hemizygous pathogenic nonsense NR0B1 variant (c.625C>T; p.Gln209), introducing a premature stop codon predicted to result in a truncated or aberrant protein with loss of function. The variant was confirmed by Sanger se­ quencing (Fig. 2). Familial segregation analysis showed maternal heterozygosity, consistent with X-linked inheritance, while the fa­ ther and brother tested negative. This variant was absent from Genome Aggregation Database and Brazilian Online Archive of Mutations but reported in ClinVar (Variation ID: 586188, SCV000842942) without phenotype description. Based on the American College of Medical Genetics and Genomics criteria (PM2, PP4, PVS1), this variant is classified as pathogenic .
