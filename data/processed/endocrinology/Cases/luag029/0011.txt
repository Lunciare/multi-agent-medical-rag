Late-onset childhood presentations of AHC can complicate diagnosis, particularly in milder cases . In our patient, recur­ rent dehydration and infections during early childhood suggest residual adrenal function before the adrenal crisis at age 7, illus­ trating variability of adrenal dysfunction in X-linked AHC . His phenotype—childhood adrenal insufficiency, normal puber­ tal androgenization, and adult azoospermia—represents an un­ common but increasingly recognized presentation within the NR0B1 spectrum.
