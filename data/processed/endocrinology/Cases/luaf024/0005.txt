A follow-up Tc-99 m methoxy isobutyl isonitrile myocardial per­ fusion imaging scan pre- and postadenosine infusion showed a dilated LV cavity and global hypokinesia without any evidence of stress-induced ischemia or scar in any of the LV myocardial segments. There was septal hypertrophy.
