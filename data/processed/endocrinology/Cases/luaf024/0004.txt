Case 3 A 26-year-old male with severe hypertension since 24 years of age was suspected of having primary hyperaldosteronism due to ongoing hypokalemia. His initial blood pressure was 180/130 mm Hg. He developed sudden chest pain in the hos­ pital ward during evaluation of hypertension. ECG showed ex­ tensive anterior wall ST elevation (V1-V5 chest leads), and echocardiography showed severe left ventricle (LV) global hy­ pokinesia with LVEF of 20%.
