Treatment Case 1 At the initial presentation of MINOCA, the patient was throm­ bolyzed in the ER with a diagnosis of acute ST-segment eleva­ tion MI, and the ECG changes were normalized after 1 hour. She underwent transsphenoidal removal of the microadenoma after stabilization. Histopathology was consistent with aden­ oma, which stained diffusely positive for ACTH. Case 2 At the initial presentation, the patient was managed medically with low molecular weight heparin, dual antiplatelet (aspirin and clopidogrel), statins (atorvastatin 80 mg), β-blockers, and antihypertensives.
