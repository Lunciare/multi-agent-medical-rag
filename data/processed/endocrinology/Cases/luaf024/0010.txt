His latest echocardiog­ raphy was normal, and he was clinically stable at 6 months of follow-up. Discussion Here we describe 3 cases of MINOCA presented to our hos­ pital within a span of 2 years. The workup revealed 3 different endocrine disorders: ACTH-dependent Cushing’s disease due to pituitary microadenoma, adrenal pheochromocytoma, and primary hyperaldosteronism due to unilateral adrenal hyper­ plasia.
