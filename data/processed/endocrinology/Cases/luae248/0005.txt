Combined with high calcium, low phosphorus, and elevated parathy­ roid hormone (PTH), Doppler ultrasound indicated parathy­ roid adenoma, which was diagnosed with primary hyperparathyroidism. Meanwhile, quantitative computed tomography (QCT) bone mineral density was 69.7 mg/cm³, suggesting osteoporosis. Abdominal CT showed multiple stones in both kidneys and ureters with hydronephrosis. The level of serum creatinine was 130.9 μmol/L (1.48 mg/dL) (reference range, 49-90 μmol/L [0.55-1 mg/dL]).
