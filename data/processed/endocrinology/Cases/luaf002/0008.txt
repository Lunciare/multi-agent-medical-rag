Consistent with prior reports of this rare pathogenic variant , MTC presented in mid- and later-life decades in affected heterozy­ gous individuals without additional features of MEN2 syn­ drome. This disease pattern is suggestive of age-dependent expressivity and perhaps incomplete penetrance.
