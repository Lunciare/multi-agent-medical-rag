In a large retrospective study, Crino et al compared the out­ come of 89 patients with pancreatic insulinoma treated with EUS-RFA against 89 patients who underwent surgical resec­ tion, matched by propensity scoring for age, lesion size, site, grade. and distance to main pancreatic duct . Fifteen of 89 patients (16.9%) treated with EUS-RFA experienced symp­ tom relapse, 12 within 1 year. Four of these patients underwent uneventful patient-preferred surgical resection, whereas 11 were successfully retreated with a repeat EUS-RFA session with complete symptom resolution lasting for an additional mean follow-up period of 23 months. Evidence of residual vas­ cularized tissue was more frequent in patients with symptom re­ currence than in patients without (8/15; 53.3% vs 5/74; 6.8%, P < .0001). Our patient was informed of her subtotal ablation and was receptive to a repeat EUS-RFA should her symptoms recur.
