She had a Clinical Frailty Score of 5, was homebound ambulatory with a rollator frame, and used a wheelchair in the community. Diagnostic Assessment She weighed 61 kg, her height was 150 cm, and her body mass index was elevated at 27.1 kg/m2. Physical examination was unremarkable. She had recurrent episodes of hypoglycemia both on point-of-care testing and plasma samples during her in­ patient stay (Fig. 1). CT of the thorax, abdomen, and pelvis by the primary team revealed an enhancing nodular focus measuring 1.36 cm × 0.93 cm in the head of pancreas (Fig. 2). No other focal lesion was seen. Her thyroid function, 8 AM cor­ tisol, and renal and liver function results were all normal (Table 2). A second supervised fast at our institution terminated at 48 hours with severe weakness and inattention, hypogly­ cemia blood glucose level (BGL) 2.4 mmol/L (43 mg/dL), sup­ pressed serum β-hydroxybutyrate (BOHB) levels, and borderline nonsuppressed C-peptide levels (Table 3). Serum in­ sulin levels were again discordantly suppressed. Sulfonylurea and insulin antibody screened as negative.
