The documen­ tation of high calcitriol in our case carried a 2-fold significance. First, it confirmed that the source of hypercalce­ mia in our patient was due to both HCM mediated by calci­ triol from the renal mass and vitamin D intoxication. Second, the documentation of high calcitriol levels carried therapeutic significance by facilitating the use of glucocorti­ coids, which specifically act by inhibiting 1α-hydroxylase ac­ tivity, leading to prompt control of hypercalcemia in our patient, as shown in Table 2 .
