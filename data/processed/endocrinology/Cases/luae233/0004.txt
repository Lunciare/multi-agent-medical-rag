Diagnostic Assessment Her physical examination revealed signs of dehydration; however, her vitals were stable at the time of presentation. Initial laboratory investigations showed elevated serum calcium levels (16.2 mg/dL; 4.0 mmol/L) (reference range: 8.5-10.5 mg/dL; 2.1-2.6 mmol/L) and normal inorganic phos­ phate levels (3.8 mg/dL; 1.2 mmol/L) (reference range: 2.5-4.5 mg/dL; 0.8-1.5 mmol/L). Other baseline investigations were normal. A workup to identify the cause of hypercalcemia was initiated.
