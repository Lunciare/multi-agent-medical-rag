This case represents the first report of probable or definitive LINH with anti-rabphilin-3A antibody positivity in a 4-year-old child, making it the youngest positive case reported to date. Our case highlights the importance of noninvasive approaches and careful follow-up to avoid invasive interventions for children with LINH.
