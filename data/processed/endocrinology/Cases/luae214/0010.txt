However, pituitary bi­ opsies are rarely performed because of their invasiveness and the risk of inducing hypopituitarism. The average patient age at the onset of LINH is reportedly 42 years , and cases in­ volving children are considered rare . However, this may be an underestimate because pituitary biopsies are more diffi­ cult and challenging to perform in children than in adults. At first, anti-rabphilin-3A antibody was reported to be highly sensitive, with a sensitivity of 100% in biopsy-confirmed LINH patients and 76% in clinically diagnosed LINH patients , indi­ cating that the test has high-level clinical utility in determining the etiology of AVP-D . Recently, the sensitivity and specificity of anti-rabphilin-3A antibody, where a histological diagnosis was made, was summarized in a recent publication . These cases revealed a sensitivity of LINH was 100.0%, 80.0% for LPH causing AVP-D, and 88.9% for LINH and LPH causing AVP-D; specificity was 97.4% for distinguishing sellar/suprasel­ lar masses.
