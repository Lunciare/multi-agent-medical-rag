A combination of SVS and 68Ga-DOTATOC PET/CT was useful in the present case for detecting a small-sized FGF23- producing tumor. Careful interpretation of scintigraphy is needed for precise localization of the tumor because the physiological uptake of radionuclides could lead to misdiagnosis.
