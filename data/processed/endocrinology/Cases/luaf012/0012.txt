The reported accuracy (sensitivity, specificity) varies, but 68Ga-DOTATOC PET/CT tends to have a higher sensitivity and specificity (60%-91%, 66%-95%) than those of 111In-pentetreotide scintigraphy (36%-95%, 64%) (Table 2). A systematic review of 1725 TIO cases from Italy reported that 68Ga-DOTATOC PET/CT had a higher sensitivity than that of 111In-pentetreotide scintigraphy or 18F-FDG PET/CT .
