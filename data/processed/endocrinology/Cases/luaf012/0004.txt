. Case Presentation A 74-year-old woman had recurrent fractures and bone pain throughout the body. She never smoked or drank alcohol and had no abnormal eating behavior. She had chronic back pain from approximately 50 years old and reached meno­ pause at 51 years old. Annual physical examination revealed high levels of serum alkaline phosphatase (ALP), but no add­ itional work-up had been performed. She had a fall at age 55 years and began to feel pain in her left foot. A nearby hospital diagnosed left third metatarsal fracture. One month later, follow-up radiography revealed additional fractures of the first, second, and fourth metatarsal bones.
