Case Presentation Case 1: An 11-year-old male patient presented to medical at­ tention with left knee pain and swelling after a minor water tubing accident. The patient was tall for his age (91st centile) and lean, with a body mass index of 16.36 kg/m². His head cir­ cumference was at the 30th centile for age. There were no rashes, puberty delay, or any physical abnormalities apart from asymptomatic cutaneous calcifications on his left hand, and right knee. The patient was born to non-consanguineous parents from an uncomplicated, full-term pregnancy with a birth weight of 5 lb 4.0 oz and birth length of 43.2 cm. The paternal side was of mixed European and Native American ancestry, and the maternal side was of mixed European ancestry. During infancy, the patient suffered from colic. He was also described as slightly smaller in size than his siblings and manifested mild language development delays that might have been related to chronic otitis media.
