All other endocrine func­ tions evaluated were normal Treatment Case 1: Upon diagnosis of POH, the patient was advised against any surgeries to remove the foci of ossification. The patient was counseled to reduce physical activities that could increase the likelihood of physical injuries. He utilizes anal­ gesic and anti-inflammatory medications to ease his knee discomfort. Case 2: The patient received vitamin D supplementation (200 to 1000 units per day). He was counseled to reduce phys­ ical activities that could increase the likelihood of physical injuries. Outcome and Follow-Up Case 1: No limitation of walking or running abilities have been observed over the past 2 years since diagnosis. Future follow-up to examine the progress of heterotopic ossification with sodium 18F-fluorine PET-CT-scan is planned. He has been otherwise healthy with normal growth.
