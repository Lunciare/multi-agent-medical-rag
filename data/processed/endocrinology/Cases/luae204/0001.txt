Advance access publication 25 February 2025 Case Report Late-Onset Progressive Osseous Heteroplasia: 2 Unrelated Cases and Use of Positron Emission Tomography for Diagnosis. Abstract Progressive osseous heteroplasia (POH) is a rare autosomal-dominant hereditary bone disorder caused by inactivating pathogenic variants in GNAS1. POH is characterized by progressive cutaneous ossification and heterotopic ossification in skeletal muscles and subdermal connective tissues. Understanding of the natural history and phenotypic heterogeneity of the illness is incomplete. We report 2 affected male positron emission tomography (PET) scanning as a sensitive technique for POH diagnosis and management.
