Case 2: This now 12-year-old male patient was the 6 lb 0 oz product of a 40-week gestation born by spontaneous vaginal delivery to a 33-year-old G4 P2 AB2 mother. The pregnancy was complicated by small-for-dates fetal measurements and a nuchal cord. He was jaundiced and required phototherapy briefly. There were no other postnatal problems, and he was discharged home at 3 days of age being breastfed. Subsequently, he received treatment for attention-deficit/ hyperactivity disorder and has had no other significant medic­ al issues. He was noted at age 4 with a small (3-4 mm) nodule near his right foot that was surgically removed and found to be calcified. There was no history of prior trauma.
