The Q12X nonsense variant itself has been reported in only one previous case . In classic POH, maculopapular skin lesions are not uncom­ mon early in life and may advance to cause progressive limb deformities. We consider our cases to be milder forms of the disease due to the absence of this early presentation. In terms of phenotype-genotype correlations, Case 1 was less se­ verely affected compared to previously reported subjects with the 565-568delGACT GNAS1 variant, although intrafamilial variability has been documented in at least one family . In contrast, the sole previously reported subject with the Q12X GNAS1 variant was also described as mildly affected . Current methods of clinical detection of ectopic calcifica­ tion are limited. Heterotopic ossification plates found radio­ logically may be diagnosed as non-hyperphosphatemic tumoral calcinosis, which can lead to physicians ordering un­ necessary surgeries to extract these ossification plates.
