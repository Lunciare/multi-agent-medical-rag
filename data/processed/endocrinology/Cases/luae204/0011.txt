Discussion The diagnosis of POH is often challenging due to difficulties in visualizing small bone plates in deep connective tissues. A metabolic panel is often performed to rule out metabolic dis­ orders such as hyperphosphatemic tumoral calcinosis. A bone dysplasia gene panel is necessary to confirm a heterozygous GNAS1 variant. Loss of function GNAS1 variants are typical­ ly small indels that disrupt the translational reading frame, or canonical splice junction changes that perturb proper mRNA splicing .
