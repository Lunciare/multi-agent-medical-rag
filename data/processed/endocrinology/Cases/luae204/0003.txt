Characteristic symptoms of AHO are brachydactyly, obesity, short stature, dental abnormal­ ities, and superficial ectopic ossification. Patients with AHO are classified as pseudohypoparathyroidism or pseudopseudopara­ thyroidism based on whether they have hormone resistance. pseudohypoparathyroidism is linked to inactivation of the ma­ ternal GNAS1 allele, while pseudopseudoparathyroidism is associated with inactivation of the paternal GNAS1 allele, the same pathogenic variants that can cause POH. GNAS1 encodes for multiple transcripts, the most promin­ ent of which is the G-protein subunit alpha (Gsα). Apart from Gsα, 4 other transcripts are encoded by the GNAS1 locus: NESP33, XLαs, and untranslated transcripts A/B and AS (antisense). In parental imprinting of GNAS1, Gsα is ex­ pressed from both paternal and maternal alleles with preferen­ ces to maternal alleles in hormone-sensitive tissues such as renal proximal tubules, thyroid, ovaries, and pituitary. Additionally, while NESP55, a neuroendocrine secretory pro­ tein, is only expressed from the maternal allele, XLαs, a larger version of Gsα, and the untranslated transcripts A/B and AS are only transcribed from the paternal allele .
