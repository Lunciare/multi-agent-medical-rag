Discussion ICI are established therapies for metastatic clear-cell RCC and other malignancies due to their ability to elicit robust antitumor immune responses . However, immune activation is associ­ ated with a tendency for immune-mediated destruction of healthy tissues, leading to a range of irAEs. Endocrine irAEs are among the most clinically significant due to their potential for morbidity and chronicity [3-5].
