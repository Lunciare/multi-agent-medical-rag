Significant examination findings in­ cluded generalized obesity (BMI 30.2 kg/m2, > 97th centile), mild acanthosis nigricans, and Tanner 2 stage of puberty. A follow-up screening oral glucose tolerance test (OGTT) was performed the following month: his blood glucose levels were 6.4 mmol/L (at 0 minutes) (normal 3.9-6.0 mmol/L) and 14.7 mmol/L (120 minutes) (normal 3.9-7.7 mmol/L) with glycated hemoglobin (HbA1c) of 6.9% (normal < 6.5%). Lifestyle and dietary modifications were advised. At 3 months after diagnosis, his repeat HbA1c improved to 6.6%, pharmacotherapy was withheld. Diagnostic Assessment He developed symptomatic hyperglycemia 8 months after diagnosis and a repeat HbA1c level rose to 12.9%. Insulin and C-peptide levels were 29.3 mIU/L (203 pmol/L) (normal reference range, 2.40-24.9 mIU/L; 16.7-172.9 pmol/L) and 2.39 µg/L (791 pmol/L) (normal reference range, 1.10- 5.00 µg/L; 364-1655 pmol/L), respectively. Autoantibodies for anti-islet cell, glutamic acid decarboxylase, tyrosine phosphatase-related islet antigen 2, and zinc transporter 8 were negative. He was diagnosed as having T2DM.
