Discussion We present a patient with adolescence-onset diabetes with moderate obesity, negative for multiple autoantibodies and preserved C-peptide levels, who was initially diagnosed T2DM, but subsequent molecular gene testing found INS-MODY due to a novel missense variant (p.His29Tyr).
