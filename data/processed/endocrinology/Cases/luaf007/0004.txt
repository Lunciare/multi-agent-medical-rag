There have been no randomized controlled trials evaluating adjuvant radiotherapy, and retrospective studies have not shown bene­ fit in survival . The use of chemotherapy or immunotherapy is not part of the standard treatment of PC, as these have not been shown to be effective in either local or metastatic PC . The molecular pathogenesis of PC is complex, with genetic analyses painting a highly heterogenous picture. One of the more commonly identified genetic mutations is CDC73, a tumor suppressor gene. This can be seen both as a germline mutation and, less commonly, as a somatic mutation. As a germline mutation, CDC73 is also associated with fibro-osseous jaw tumors and renal or uterine masses, collect­ ively known as hyperparathyroidism-jaw tumor syndrome . Another more frequently identified mutation is in the TP53 gene, responsible for the well-known tumor suppressor protein p53 . To date, neither of these mutations has tar­ geted therapies available. therapies available.
