In conclusion, our case report illustrates a complicated case of severe and rapidly progressive hypercortisolism due to EAS. In the context of ineligibility for BAL, use of osilodrostat at initially high doses quickly reduced cortisol levels and im­ proved comorbidities of hypercortisolism. Hypocalcemia is very rare in severe hypercortisolism, which can be due to accelerated renal calcium loss, and the most effective treat­ ment appears to be effective reduction of cortisol levels.
