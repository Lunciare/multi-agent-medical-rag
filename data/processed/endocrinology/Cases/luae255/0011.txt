Once hypercortiso­ lism has improved, dose reduction or discontinuation of the mineralocorticoid receptor antagonist can be considered. During maintenance therapy with osilodrostat, her blood pressure and potassium level remained normal, possibly due to only mildly elevated 11-deoxycorticosterone level. In our case, since the metastatic NET was not feasible for removal, effective therapy was aimed at gaining prompt and effective control of excessive cortisol. Several treatment op­ tions were considered. BAL was not considered feasible given her complicated medical condition and very high surgical risks. We chose to use adrenal steroidogenesis inhibitor so that cortisol levels can be monitored to guide our therapy. Etomidate inhibits cortisol production and can be used intra­ venously in the intensive care setting to gain rapid control
