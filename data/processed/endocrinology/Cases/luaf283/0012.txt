In our experience, we have used etomidate outside of the intensive care unit with lower doses as reported in the literature . Bilateral adrenalectomy is recommended for patients with active disease when all other treatment options have been sub­ optimal or have failed. In our case, the procedure was per­ formed due to the severity of the clinical and biochemical behavior of CS, and the impossibility to achieve an R0 resec­ tion of the tumor.
