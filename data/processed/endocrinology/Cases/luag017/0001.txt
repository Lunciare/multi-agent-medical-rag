An 11-year-old girl presented with short stature and lower-limb bowing for more than 3 years. She was the firstborn from a third- degree consanguineous marriage and had a history of delayed motor milestones. Her height was 128.4 cm (< third percentile), and she had arachnodactyly, a single transverse crease, pectus excavatum, kyphoscoliosis, pes planus, and genu valgum in the right and genu varum in the left leg (Fig. 1A). The metabolic panel showed calcium 9.1 mg/dL (SI: 2.27 mmol/L) (reference range, 8.5-10.5 mg/dL; 2.12-2.62 mmol/L), phosphate 4.8 mg/dL (SI: 1.55 mmol/L) (reference range, 2.5-4.9 mg/dL; 0.81-1.58 mmol/L), alkaline phosphatase 264 U/L (SI: 4.4 µkat/L) (reference range, 45-145 U/L; 0.7-2.5 µkat/L), and low 25-hydroxyvitamin D of 17.6 ng/mL (SI: 44 nmol/L) (reference range, 30-50 ng/mL; 75-125 nmol/L). The skeletal survey revealed windswept deformity (Fig. 1B), kyphoscoliosis (Fig. 1C), Swedish key appearance of the femur (Fig. 1D), and bone age of 10 years.
