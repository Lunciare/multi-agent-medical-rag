Molecular analysis revealed a homozygous 1-bp deletion c.893T > C (p.Leu298Pro) in the calcium activated nucleotidase 1 gene (CANT1), confirming the diagnosis of Desbuquois dysplasia 1 (DBQD1). The child was given vitamin D replacement and is being monitored for pubertal development. DBQD1 and DBQD2 are auto­ somal recessive osteochondrodysplasias caused by pathogenic variants in the CANT1 and XYLT1 genes respectively on chromo­ some 17q25 .
