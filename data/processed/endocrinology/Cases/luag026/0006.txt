TT4 was markedly elevated (10.8-13-fold) on Roche COBAS, Beckman ACCESS, and Ortho VITROS. fT4 was elevated (3.2-5.4-fold) on Roche COBAS and Beckman ACCESS but normal on Ortho VITROS. Abbott ARCHITECT showed substantially lower elevations (TT4 ∼2-fold; fT4 ∼1.2-fold). Treatment Both probands and the affected children were counseled about the benign nature of FDH and the assay-related false elevations of TT4 and fT4. Outcome and follow-up One year later, Case 1 received an urgent laboratory alert for a “critically high” TT4 value. Knowing her diagnosis, she reassured the laboratory and continued her routine care without unneces­ sary interventions. Discussion We describe 2 Indian cases of FDH, including a novel likely patho­ genic ALB variant, p.Asn415Ile, in Case 1.
