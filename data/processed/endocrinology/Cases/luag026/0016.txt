Case Report
KEYWORDS: nmol, pmol, pmol pmol, fdh, fold, nmol nmol, case, variant, architect, alb, abbott, abbott architect, albumin, beckman, ortho vitros

Learning points • We report a novel ALB variant, p.Asn415Ile, associated with the FDH phenotype, thereby expanding the known genetic spectrum of FDH. • Individuals carrying the p.Asn415Ile variant show intermedi­ ate elevations of TT4 and fT4 compared with those harboring p.Arg242His and p.Arg242Ser. • In patients with euthyroid hyperthyroxinemia, the combin­ ation of elevated fT4 in most immunoassays but low-normal or low fT4 on the Ortho VITROS platform strongly suggests FDH and helps distinguish it from thyroid hormone resist­ ance and TSH-secreting pituitary adenoma. • The degree of TT4 and fT4 elevation in FDH is influenced by both the underlying genotype and the immunoassay plat­ form, with the Abbott ARCHITECT platform showing the least assay interference.
