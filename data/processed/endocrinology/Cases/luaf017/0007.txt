A pituitary MRI scan showed a mass-like enhancement in­ volving the hypothalamus and infundibulum, and an enhancing lesion in the parietal calvaria, right mastoid, and external audi­ tory canal with extension and erosion of the sigmoid plate (Fig. 3A-C and Fig. 4A and C). FDG-PET-CT showed hyperme­ tabolic lytic lesion in the right parietal bone and right mastoid (Fig. 4B and D). Treatment Patient 1 After histopathological confirmation of LCH, cytarabine 100 mg/m2 5 days/month was administered for 12 months. Patient 2 Patient saw reproductive urology, which assisted with sperm preservation. Hematology evaluated the patient and started cytarabine 150 mg/m2 5 days/month. Three months later, FDG-PET-CT showed complete resolution of metabolic activ­ ity in the right parietal bone and decreased metabolic activity in the right mastoid (Fig. 4F and 4H). MRI showed decreased size of the HP mass from 9 × 7 × 6 mm to 9 × 3.5 × 3.5 mm (not shown). Free thyroxine became low and levothyroxine 75 mcg daily was started.
