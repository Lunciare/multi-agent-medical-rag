Only a few such cases were reported with onset in adulthood [7-11]. In a case series of 12 children and adults with HP LCH and AVP-D, 3 adults had had bone involvement on presenta­ tion without other soft-tissue disease . In a case series of 10 patients with LCH and AVP-D, among the 5 adults, 1 had a calvarial lesion and another a right temporal bone le­ sion. The remaining 3 patients only had HP lesions . Similar with our cases, the most common hormone abnor­ mality in HP LCH in the literature is AVP-D (up to 55%), with onset usually before LCH diagnosis .
