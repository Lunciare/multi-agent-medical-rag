Introduction Langerhans cell histiocytosis (LCH) is a rare granuloma­ tous disorder characterized by an abnormal proliferation of histiocyte-like dendritic cells, classified as a neoplastic disease after identification of BRAF-V600E pathogenic variant . Most patients are diagnosed during early childhood. A national registry study in England estimated an incidence of 1.06/million/year after age 15 years, and a prevalence of 6.03 (95% CI, 5.34-6.78)/million . United States Surveillance, Epidemiology, and End Results data­ base suggests a lower incidence of 0.07/million (95% CI, 0.05-0.10) . LCH can harbor a variety of possible clinical manifesta­ tions and its classification has changed over the years.
