In patients with BRAF-V600E pathogenic variants and critical organ involve­ ment, targeted therapy such as BRAF inhibitors can be consid­ ered and holds promise. There remains some debate regarding when to utilize BRAF inhibitors as they require lifelong treat­ ment, whereas cytotoxic chemotherapy for a limited period may produce prolonged remission and possible cure . In summary, diagnosis of LCH usually entails histopatho­ logical confirmation. Treatment of HP LHC entails collabor­ ation between endocrinology, which manages the pituitary hormone abnormalities, and hematology, which performs treatment and surveillance of LCH lesions. Neurosurgery and radiation oncology involvement may be required. Long-term follow-up is essential to evaluate for recurrence or new lesion development. Given the heterogenous presenta­ tion and rarity in adults, a multicenter registry would yield valuable information. Learning Points • LCH should be considered in patients with infundibular thickening or hypothalamic enhancement. • Diagnosis is achieved by histopathological confirmation entailing biopsy of the HP lesion or other affected organs. • After LCH diagnosis, FDG-PET-CT scans are recom­ mended for detection of other organ involvement. • Multidisciplinary long-term treatment and surveillance are necessary.
