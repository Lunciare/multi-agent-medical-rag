Discussion Our 2 cases presented with AVP-D and radiological evidence of infiltrative lesions of the hypothalamus and infundibulum. Although rare, their etiological spectrum is wide, including primary hypophysitis, multisystemic disorders (IgG4-related disease, LCH, Erdheim-Chester disease, sarcoidosis), infec­ tions (tuberculosis, syphilis, HIV, fungal), and rarely malig­ nancies (lymphoproliferative disorders, metastases) .
