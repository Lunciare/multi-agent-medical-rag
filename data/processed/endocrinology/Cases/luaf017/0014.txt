Another abnormality that can be encountered is stalk-effect hyperprolactinemia. According to the LCH international expert consensus, treat­ ment depends on LHC organ involvement . For asymptomat­ ic patients without critical organ involvement, close observation is considered . Otherwise, treatment is recommended; how­ ever, no standardized treatment exists in adults. Options include vinca alkaloid/glucocorticoid and antimetabolite-based (cladri­ bine/cytarabine) regimens. In the HP/bone cases reviewed, pa­ tients received a combination of surgery, radiation (reported mostly in older publications), vinblastine/prednisone, dabrafe­ nib and trametinib, and cyclosporine/etoposide [7-10]. In re­ ported adult-onset cases, cytarabine was associated with relatively high response rates, whereas vinblastine and prednis­ one regimen had a higher relapse risk . Our 2 patients had an excellent response to 12-month cytarabine therapy, which was maintained at 1 and 4 years respectively. Recent studies found that 27.4% to 28.1% biopsy specimens in adult LCH patients were positive for BRAF V600E mutation . Other pathogenic variants encountered in unifocal LCH were MAP2K1 and PIK3CA .
