Learning points • Hemoglobin A1c may not reliably reflect glycemic status in patients with glycemic variability, systemic inflammation, or altered red blood cell turnover. • CGM and OGTT are essential tools for detecting postprandial hyperglycemia when HbA1c appears normal. • Pancreatic cystic lesions, such as pNETs or IPMNs, can impair glucose regulation even without overt diabetes and should prompt metabolic evaluation. • CHIP may contribute to inflammation-related insulin resist­ ance or metabolic dysfunction.
