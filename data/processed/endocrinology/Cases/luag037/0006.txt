Surgical consultation favored surveillance over intervention, as lesions ap­ peared nonfunctioning and indolent. Treatment Antidiabetic medications were temporarily held to allow physiologic assessment of her endogenous glucose regulation, given the marked discordance between CGM findings and her normal HbA1c. Metformin had previously been discontinued due to intolerance, and semaglu­ tide was avoided because of underlying pancreatic cystic lesions. An OGTT was performed off therapy to characterize the degree and pattern of dysglycemia without pharmacologic influence. After con­ firming significant hyperglycemia despite a low HbA1c, empagliflozin was resumed as monotherapy during the evaluation period. Outcome and follow-up Follow-up CGM showed an average glucose of 198 mg/dL (11.0 mmol/L) with a nadir of 120 mg/dL (6.7 mmol/L).
