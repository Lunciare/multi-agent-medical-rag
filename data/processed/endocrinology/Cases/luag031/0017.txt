Ethiodized oil interference of planned radioiodine
KEYWORDS: rai, tde, oil, weeks, therapy, neck, ethiodized oil, ethiodized, case, rai therapy, uptake, post, iodine, disease, lymph

ethio­ dized oil. Learning points • A CL resulting from neck dissection may require treatment with TDE following lymphangiography. Both procedures util­ ize ethiodized oil, an oil-based iodinated contrast agent. • Nonradioactive iodine from ethiodized oil can result in a pro­ longed, excess UIC and can reduce RAI uptake for several months and beyond, potentially impacting the diagnostic and therapeutic efficacy of RAI for DTC management. • Alternatives to TDE and lymphangiography should be consid­ ered in patients planned to undergo evaluation for RAI ther­ apy. Otherwise, RAI therapy may not be effective.
