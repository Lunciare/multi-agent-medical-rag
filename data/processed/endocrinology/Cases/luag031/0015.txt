Ethiodized oil is also indicated for hysterosalpingography. Studies have shown prolonged elevations of serum iodine concentration and UIC from baseline up to 24 weeks in women who underwent this pro­ cedure . Excess UIC observed in these cases is consistent with ethiodized oil exposure with hysterosalpingography. In case 1, there was no evidence of iodine-avid disease. BRAFV600E mutation and FDG-avidity raise the likelihood that PTC was dedifferentiated and noniodine-avid and therefore would not have exhibited RAI uptake independent of excess iod­ ine present. BRAFV600E mutation can suppress the expression of genes necessary for iodine uptake, organification, and retention. Furthermore, FDG-avidity may suggest more aggressive, dediffer­ entiated disease . Nevertheless, the potential interference of RAI uptake from excess iodine complicated management.
