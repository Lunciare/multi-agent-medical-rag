In both cases, UIC was higher than ex­ pected after a typical 4- to 6-week washout following iodinated contrast administration. The cases presented suggest that ethiodized oil can have sig­ nificant implications on RAI therapy and DTC management. We recommend that all conservative measures for CL treatment should be trialed if possible. If refractory to conservative measures or chylous output is high, we suggest prioritizing surgical management or TDL prior to lymphangiography and TDE. In conclusion, RAI therapy may need to be deferred or may not be effective for patients who undergo standard TDE and lymphangiography due to iodine interference from ethio­ dized oil. Learning points • A CL resulting from neck dissection may require treatment with TDE following lymphangiography. Both procedures util­ ize ethiodized oil, an oil-based iodinated contrast agent.
