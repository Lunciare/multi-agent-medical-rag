Case 2 Neck ultrasound, 6 months after RAI therapy, demonstrated nonaggressive-appearing, likely reactive lymph nodes and hypoe­ choic nodular areas within the thyroid fossae bilaterally. The pa­ tient was classified as having a structurally incomplete response with remnant disease in the right neck and ultimately underwent right levels II-IV neck dissection for treatment of lymphadenopathy concerning for locoregional disease 36 weeks post TDE. Discussion We report 2 cases of patients with PTC who, following total thy­ roidectomy and neck dissection, had a postoperative CL, which was treated with TDE following lymphangiography. Both cases were initially evaluated for RAI therapy 12 weeks after TDE; however, each case was managed differently despite similar challenges.
