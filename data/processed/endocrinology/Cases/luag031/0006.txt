Case 2 A 21-year-old woman diagnosed with well-differentiated PTC underwent total thyroidectomy with central and bilateral lateral neck dissection. Surgical pathology noted PTC centered in the isthmus (tumor size 4.8 × 3.2 × 1.3 cm) with multifocal involve­ ment in bilateral lobes, extensive extrathyroidal extension (microscopic), no capsular invasion, negative margins, and lymphatic invasion (22 lymph nodes positive for metastatic dis­ ease of 31 examined, with many demonstrating extranodal ex­ tension). Concern for a low-grade CL in the left lateral neck was noted during the procedure. Chylous output ranged from 100 to 300 mL post-op day 1 through post-op day 8. A no-fat diet and octreotide were implemented during this timeframe with no significant improvement. On post-op day 9, TDE was suc­ cessfully performed with coils and a mixture of n-BCA glue and ethiodized oil following lymphangiography (15 mL of ethiodized oil 48% and 25 mL of iopamidol 61% were administered). Chylous output diminished, and the patient was discharged post- op day 12. A neck CT was performed 2 weeks after TDE (75 mL of iopamidol 76% was administered).
