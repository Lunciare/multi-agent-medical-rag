Treatment Case 1 RAI therapy was not administered. The patient was referred to endocrinology and oncology. He was prescribed levothyroxine 175 mcg (1 tablet by mouth daily Monday-Saturday and 1.5 tab­ lets on Sunday) for TSH suppression with a goal of TSH <0.1 µIU/ mL (<0.1 mIU/L). Disease progression was monitored with inter­ val chest and neck CT. Case 2 Given a mildly iodine-avid lymph node in the right neck, 125 mCi (4.625 GBq) of I-131 NaI was administered. Posttherapy whole- body scan was performed 48 hours later, demonstrating similar subtle activity in the right neck lymph node and new faint activity in the left thyroidectomy bed (Fig. 3). Revisit with surgery for dis­ section of FDG-avid lymph nodes was recommended.
