In affected patients, the diag­ nosis of MRKH makes conception impossible, leading to fer­ tility issues. This condition often affects patients’ emotional well-being and personal relationships. Thus, integrating awareness of MRKH syndrome into the clinical management of patients with Sotos syndrome will allow for comprehensive care and support of affected individuals and their families.
