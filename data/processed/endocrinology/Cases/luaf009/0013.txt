However, the presence of multiple familial cases and recent re­ search indicating a heterogeneous etiology suggest a potential genetic component . The reported association between MRKH and Sotos syndrome, although rare, raises the possi­ bility of a shared genetic or developmental pathway that war­ rants further exploration. Given the lack of a known genetic cause for MRKH and the emerging evidence of familial clus­ tering, future research, particularly with whole-genome se­ quencing, could provide valuable insights into the genetic underpinnings of both conditions and inform the evaluation and management of individuals with Sotos syndrome who present with primary amenorrhea.
