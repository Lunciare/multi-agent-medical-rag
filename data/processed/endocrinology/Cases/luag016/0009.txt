To investigate whether the hypoglycemia observed in this child was likely related to the CHD7 disorder or was coincidental, next-generation sequencing of the known HI genes was per­ formed at the Exeter Genomics Laboratory (UK) using previously described methods . This analysis excluded single-nucleotide variants and copy number variants in 22 HI genes (ABCC8, AKT2, CACNA1D, CREBBP, EP300, FOXA2, GCK, GLUD1, GPC3, HADH, HNF1A, HNF4A, INSR, KCNJ11, KDM6A, KMT2D, MAFA, NSD1, PHOX2B, PMM2, SLC16A1, and TRMT10A).
