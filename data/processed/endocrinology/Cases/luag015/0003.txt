Case presentation Here, we describe a 31-year-old female evaluated for a 4-year his­ tory of asymptomatic hypercalcemia. Physical examination was un­ remarkable. Family history was notable for pHPT in her mother, who had undergone parathyroidectomy. Laboratory examinations showed serum parathyroid hormone (PTH) of 74.8 pg/mL (International System of Units [SI]: 7.9 pmol/L) (reference range, 8.7-77.1 pg/mL [SI: 0.92-8.17 pmol/L]) with an elevated calcium lev­ el of 10.5 mg/dL (SI: 2.6 mmol/L) (reference range: 8.4-10.2 mg/dL [SI: 2.10-2.55 mmol/L]).
