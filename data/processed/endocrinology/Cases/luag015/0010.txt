This case provides strong clinical evidence supporting the pathogenicity of the MEN1 (NM_130799.2): c.652C>T (p.Arg218Trp) variant, which has con­ flicting classifications in ClinVar. Although 4 laboratories, includ­ ing the performing laboratory, classified it as a VUS, the presence of multiple MEN1-related endocrine neoplasms, including bilat­ eral parathyroid adenomas and a pNET, along with a family his­ tory of parathyroid disease, strongly supports its causal role in MEN1 syndrome.
