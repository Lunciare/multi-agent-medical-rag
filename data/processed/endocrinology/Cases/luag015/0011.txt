Currently, 7 laboratories have reported this variant, excluding our evaluation: 4 classify it as pathogenic or likely pathogenic, and 3 as a VUS. Missense pathogenic variants affecting critical residues have been previously implicated in MEN1-related tumorigenesis . Given this patient’s clinical findings, this variant likely disrupts menin function, leading to early-onset, multiglandular endocrine involvement consistent with MEN1 syndrome. Although genet­ ic testing was not performed in other family members, testing could provide further insight into segregation of this variant and help guide clinical management for at-risk relatives. Most experts recommend bilateral cervical exploration with subtotal parathyroidectomy and cervical thymectomy as the pre­ ferred initial surgical approach in MEN1 . In this case, focused resection of 2 dominant adenomas normalized calcium and PTH levels. The high recurrence rate of pHPT in MEN1 distinguishes it from sporadic disease and necessitates lifelong surveillance .
