Outcome and follow-up Regarding the well-differentiated pNET, given the size, lack of metastatic features, and absence of biochemical evidence of it being functional, our tumor board panel opted for a conservative approach and ongoing surveillance with periodic imaging. Follow-up abdominal imaging is planned 1 year after initial im­ aging. Serum gastrin and chromogranin A levels remained within normal limits.
