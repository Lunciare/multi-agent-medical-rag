Sequence and copy number variation ana­ lysis of NR3C1 using proprietary software (Blueprint Genetic Glucocorticoid Deficiency Panel) demonstrated the patient to be heterozygous for a variant in the NR3C1 gene c.1322G > C, p.(Cys441Ser). This missense variant results in a substitution of cysteine to serine at nucleotide position 1322, a highly conserved amino acid in the zinc finger DNA-binding domain.
