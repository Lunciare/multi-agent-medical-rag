Introduction Glucocorticoid resistance syndrome (GRS) is a rare genetic disorder characterized by diminished sensitivity of target tis­ sues to cortisol due to impaired glucocorticoid receptor (hGR) function . It is caused by inactivating pathogenic variants in the hGR gene, NR3C1, located on chromosome 5 (5q31.3) . To date, there have been 33 pathogenic var­ iants in NR3C1 identified in the Human Gene Mutation Database 2021.3. Cases of GRS without a recognizable hGR pathogenic variant have also been reported [3-5]. Defects in glucocorticoid receptor function result in decreased negative feedback inhibition of the hypothalamic-pituitary- adrenal (HPA) axis leading to increases in CRH, ACTH, and cortisol levels.
