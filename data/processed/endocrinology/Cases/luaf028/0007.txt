Our patient returned to the ED 8 hours after initial discharge with heart rate 130 beats per minute (reference range, 60-100 beats per minute), oxygen saturation 90% (reference range, ≥95%), WBC 11 200 cells/µL, serum bicarbonate 12.3 mmol/L (12.3 mEq/L), AG 24 mmol/L (24 mEq/L), blood glucose 72 mg/dL (4.0 mmol/L), and HbA1c 6.9%. A venous blood gas demonstrated acidosis with a pH 7.17, and repeat labora­ tory tests several hours later showed severe metabolic acidosis with serum bicarbonate 8.2 mmol/L (8.2 mEq/L).
