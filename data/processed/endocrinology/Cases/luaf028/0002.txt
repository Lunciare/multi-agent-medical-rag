Introduction Diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA) is a well-known, life-threatening complication of type 1 and type 2 diabetes mellitus, classically characterized by severe hyperglycemia and ketone body pro­ duction leading to systemic acidosis, dehydration, and end-organ damage.
