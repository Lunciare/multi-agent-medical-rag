Introduction Growth hormone (GH) secretion by anterior pituitary gland is finely regulated by a stimulatory pathway led by growth hor­ mone releasing hormone (GHRH) and by an inhibitory path­ way led by somatostatin . In addition, these main control pathways of GH secretion are modulated by different neurotransmitters acting at the hypothalamic or pituitary level, influencing GHRH and somatostatin secretion . Furthermore, it has been demonstrated, first in animal models and then also in humans, that melatonin plays a role in the regulation of GH secretion .
