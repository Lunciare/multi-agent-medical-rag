Case Presentation A 35-year-old woman of Windish ancestry, present-day Slovenia, first presented in 1989 for subacute thyroiditis, which resolved. Her occupational, environmental, drug, and alcohol history were unremarkable. Family history was positive for dia­ betes mellitus, breast cancer, and pancreatic cancer. She has been routinely evaluated and has no other pertinent medical history.
