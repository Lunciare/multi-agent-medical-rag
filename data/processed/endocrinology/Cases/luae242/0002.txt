Introduction Elevated glycated hemoglobin A1c (HbA1c) levels generally indicate increased glucose binding to hemoglobin (Hb), im­ plying hyperglycemia and risk for diabetes. Normal HbA1c levels are defined as less than 5.7% (<39 mmol/mol); predia­ betes between 5.7% and 6.4% (39 to 46 mmol/mol); and dia­ betes greater than or equal to 6.5% (≥48 mmol/mol) .
