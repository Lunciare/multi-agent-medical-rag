Moderate weight loss decreases lipedema-affected body
KEYWORDS: weight, body, fat, loss, weight loss, mass, glucose, lipedema, mmol, total, body fat, normal, tissue, relative, subcutaneous

evaluated the effect of moderate (∼11%) diet-induced weight loss on body composition and metabolic function in a woman with lipedema who was not overweight/obese and had a normal body mass index. The assessment of subcutaneous fat in differ­ ent depots, measured by using DXA and magnetic resonance im­ aging, demonstrated that the relative decrease in body fat was ∼2.5 fold greater than the relative decrease in body weight.
