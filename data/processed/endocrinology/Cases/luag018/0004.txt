At baseline, the participant presented with disproportionate in­ creases in both arm and leg fat masses, bilateral fat nodules in both legs, bilateral ankle cuffing, and nonpitting edema in both thighs and arms. In addition, the participant reported leg tender­ ness and pain and easy bruising in lipedema-affected areas. The participant completed a screening evaluation that included a medical examination and standard blood tests. The participant did not have any medical illnesses, other than lipedema, and was not taking any medications. Written, informed consent was obtained from the participant before she participated in Institutional Review Board and registered in ClinicalTrials.gov (NCT03271034).
