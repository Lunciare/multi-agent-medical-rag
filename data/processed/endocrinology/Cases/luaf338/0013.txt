Discussion Insulin treatment in NDM is uniquely challenging, with mi­ nute insulin doses and highly variable feeding patterns. Additionally, diabetes technologies considered standard of care in older people have limited utilization in this age group due to a lack of safety data and technical concerns. This case details several adaptations of an existing HCLS for NDM in­ cluding omission of carbohydrate dosing, usage of dilute insu­ lin, and modulation of the glycemic target in the insulin delivery algorithm. Hypoglycemia is the most serious medical and developmen­ tal risk in NDM.
