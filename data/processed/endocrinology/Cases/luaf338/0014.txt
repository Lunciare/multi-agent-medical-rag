In our case, significant re­ ductions in delivered insulin preceded overt β-cell recovery in our patient and allowed for safe transition off insulin therapy. Safe, equitable adoption of HCLS in NDM requires sub­ stantial caregiver education. In this case, education by trained diabetes education staff was completed while the infant was hospitalized. Education was modified from clinic standard curricula for patients with new onset of diabetes and those in­ terested in pump initiation. Our team chose to initiate the HCLS inpatient and ensure caregivers had ample opportunity to physically use the technology prior to home use while under trained supervision, likely contributing to successful use in this case. Speculatively, limitation of both hyperglycemia and hypo­ glycemia through HCLS in NDM have the potential to mean­ ingfully improve neurodevelopmental outcomes . This and several other reports [5, 7-9] of HCLS use in this popula­ tion with clear benefits over conventional management strongly support routine use of HCLS and other diabetes tech­ nology in this age population.
