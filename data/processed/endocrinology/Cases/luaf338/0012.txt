The patient was discharged from the hospital on an HCLS with U10 insulin lispro Outcome and Follow-up Fig. 2 displays a 7-day average of CGM data during each treat­ ment modality. Consistent with prior studies , HCLS improves average glucose, glycemic variability (as measured by SD and coefficient of variation). The patient’s mean glucose of 180 mg/dL [SI: 10 mmol/L] is slightly above the American Diabetes Associate Guidelines recommendation; however, this aligned with recommendations to maximize glycemic con­ trol while minimizing hypoglycemia to avoid adverse neuro­ developmental effects in this age group. U10 insulin lispro specifically avoided nearly all hypoglycemia, and ongoing out­ patient insulin dose titration yielded a glycated hemoglobin measurement of 4.8% (reference range <6.5%) at 2 months after hospital discharge. We specifically highlight the omission of any insulin dosing for carbohydrate consumption including via insulin pump. This infant continued to fed on-demand, without diabetes- related restrictions on feeding volume/selection or carbohy­ drate quantification. Although this is a single case, the parents reported satisfaction with this selection and felt the patient was less medicalized overall.
