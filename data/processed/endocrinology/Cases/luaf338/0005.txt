With negative gen­ omic origin of NDM, we ordered a methylation-specific multi­ plex ligation assay on the 14th day of life as 70% of the transient subtype of NDM can be attributed to chromosome 6 methylation anomalies. During the sixth week of life, mater­ nal hypomethylation of 6q24 region was confirmed. Treatment Substantial time is required for even expedited genomic/epige­ nomic diagnostics, and empiric treatment of NDM is re­ quired. Approximately 40% of NDM patients respond to sulfonylurea medications, and we initially trialed glyburide (1 mg/kg/day) to possibly avoid insulin treatment. After 48 hours, this was discontinued for inefficacy, leaving insulin as the only treatment option. Insulin treatment was initiated on the seventh day of life, initially via continuous IV infusion of regular insulin. This is conventionally the treatment of choice for NDM; however, we experienced technical challenges with this delivery, as well as significant glucose variability. Our patient experi­ enced several episodes of hyperglycemia seemingly unre­ sponsive to increased IV delivery.
