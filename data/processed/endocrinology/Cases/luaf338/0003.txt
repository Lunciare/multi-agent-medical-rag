There are several rare reports of HCLS in NDM [6-8], and slightly more reports of insulin pump use , although diabetes technology remains underused due to a lack of regu­ latory studies regarding safety/efficacy in this population, technical challenges related to delivery of very small insulin doses, and decreased access to technology due to insurance coverage. However, the adaptability of insulin delivery in HCLS is uniquely suited to improve glycemic control and caregiver quality of life in this age group. We describe use of HCLS in a 29-day-old term infant diag­ nosed with NDM due to chromosome 6q24 hypomethylation. To our knowledge, this is the youngest patient to use HCLS to date.
