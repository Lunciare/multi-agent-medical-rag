We also noted in­ creased hypoglycemia frequency (5.75% on HCLS vs 0.36% with IV and injection regimen) (Table 3). After review, we felt this was most likely because the lowest programmable in­ sulin sensitivity factor exceeded the true needs of the patient. In response to this hypoglycemia, we transitioned to diluted U10 insulin lispro in the same HCLS, with a programmed basal rate of 0.03 units/hour (programmed on the pump as 0.3 units/h) with a new effective insulin sensitivity of 1 unit:3200 mg/dL (programmed as 1:320 mg/dL on the pump). U10 insulin lispro was compounded from U100 insu­ lin lispro by a licensed pharmacy and has a refrigerated shelf life of 28 days. Carbohydrate-related insulin dosing continued to be omitted and the Control-IQ automated insulin delivery system in exercise mode was implemented to allow for auto­ mated correction doses of insulin while maintaining a slightly higher target glucose of 140 to 160 mg/dL [SI: 7.8-8.9 mmol/ L] to limit hypoglycemia.
