To dif­ ferentiate hypophysitis from pituitary adenoma, 2 scoring sys­ tems have been described in the literature. Gutenberg et al developed a score based on MRI features of the mass, with a sensitivity of 92% and a specificity of 99% for diagnosing autoimmune hypophysitis. This score includes variables such as volume, symmetry, signal intensity, and homogeneity postgadolinium; presence of the posterior pituitary bright spot; stalk size; mucosal swelling; age; and relation to preg­ nancy.
