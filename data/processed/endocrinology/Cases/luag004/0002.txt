Graves disease could result from hemiagene­ sis, differential expression of sodium-iodide symporters, or a history of unilateral thyroiditis . The vascular supply of the thyroid gland includes the superior and inferior thyroid ar­ teries, which are branches of the external carotid and thyro­ cervical trunk, respectively .
