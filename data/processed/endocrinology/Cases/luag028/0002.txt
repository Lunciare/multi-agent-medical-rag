This case highlights the diagnostic complexities of non-PTH-mediated hypercalcemia and underscores the importance of a comprehensive workup, including medication review, laboratory tests, radiography, and biopsy, with consideration for excisional biopsy. Key Words hypercalcemia, parathyroid hormone, sarcoidosis, retroperitoneal Non-parathyroid hormone (PTH)-mediated hypercalcemia includes a broad set of etiologies, most commonly caused by hypercalcemia of malignancy. However, calcitriol overproduction from either ma­ lignancy or granulomatous processes is a possibility. Sarcoidosis is a known etiology of hypercalcemia, typically presenting with lung lesions, though rarely presenting without pulmonary manifes­ tations. Here, we report a case of nonpulmonary sarcoidosis pre­ senting with severe, non-PTH-mediated hypercalcemia. Case presentation A 44-year-old man with a history of hypertension and type 2 dia­ betes presented with malaise, polyuria, and polydipsia for 2 weeks.
