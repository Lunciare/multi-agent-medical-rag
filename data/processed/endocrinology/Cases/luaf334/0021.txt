The dose and therapeutic concentrations of MTT required for EAS-related hypercortisolism are suggested to be lower than those for adrenocortical carcinoma (14-20 mg/L). Only quite few EAS cases have reported the plasma concentration of MTT, and the mean value was 10.4 ± 6.5 mg/L (range, 2-15.5 mg/L), with a mean daily dose of 3.3 ± 1.2 g/d . Similar dose and plasma concentrations were observed in pa­ tients with CD . Measurement of plasma MTT concentra­ treatment with MTT, compared to plasma concentration of MTT, a decrease in cortisol levels might be more sensitive and help guide optimal dose titration. Patients with EAS generally tolerate MTT well. The most common side effects were mild gastrointestinal and neuro­ logical symptoms, which occurred in 65% and 26% patients, respectively . Tolerance is dependent not only on the dose, but also on the duration of treatment, and side effects are re­ versible when discontinuing the drug or reducing the dose [3, 10].
