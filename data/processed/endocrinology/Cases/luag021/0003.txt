Although very rare, less than 20% of cases have been reported in patients without dialysis-dependent chronic kidney disease (CKD), a presentation known as non­ uremic calciphylaxis (NUC) . One of the underlying condi­ tions associated with NUC is hyperparathyroidism, both primary and secondary . NUC is associated with poorer prognosis, with a high mortality rate of 52%, with 50% of cases due to sepsis . NUC in the context of primary hyper­ parathyroidism has rarely been documented, with only 53 re­ ported cases globally . Currently, evidence-based management options for NUC are limited due to the rarity of the condition. Treatment pri­ marily involves wound care with or without surgical debride­ ment .
