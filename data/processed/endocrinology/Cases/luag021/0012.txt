JCEM Case Reports, 2026, Volume 4, Issue 3 a review of 7 cases over nearly 30 years, in which parathyroidec­ tomies were performed for indications related to ESRD, calciphy­ laxis developed between 2 weeks and 2 years postoperatively, with most cases occurring within the first 3 months . In an­ other series of 30 patients with ESRD, the time of onset of calci­ phylaxis from commencement of renal replacement therapies ranged from 5 days to 18 years, with nearly half presenting within 2 years . The pathogenesis of calciphylaxis involving calcifica­ tion of small arterioles of the dermis and hypodermis resulting in thrombosis causes soft tissue and skin necrosis.
