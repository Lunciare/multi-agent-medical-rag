Case Report
KEYWORDS: pembrolizumab, response, acc, patients, nmol, mitotane, therapy, nmol nmol, pd-, patient, pem, cycles, advanced, pmol, edp-m

The patient underwent complete surgical resection, achieving full remission. More than 1 year later, she remains disease free.
