The patient received a single dose of 140 mg in December 2023. Unfortunately, the treatment was complicated by the onset of grade 3 immune-mediated hepatitis, resulting in prolonged hospitalization and the discontinuation of mitotane and pembrolizumab. Outcome and Follow-up After pembrolizumab, the patient’s blood pressure returned to normal, but hyperkalemia developed despite antihypertensive medication being stopped. The immune-mediated hepatitis resolved entirely after 2 months of mycophenolate mofetil and continuous tapering of prednisone. In March 2024, a CT scan showed a 60% reduction of the adrenal mass (4.1 × 7.2 cm) and stability of the renal lesion and teratoma. Furthermore, the CT scan showed complete resolution of the pulmonary micronodules (Fig. 1C, 2B, and 3B). Mitotane levels remained within the therapeutic range (15.1 mg/L) six weeks after discontinuation. Mitotane was progressively re-introduced in March 2024 and titrated up to 3 g/day, with no adverse reaction. A subsequent 18-FDG-PET scan confirmed regression of the adrenal mass (3.5 × 5.4 × 3.3 cm, SUV 18.6) (Fig. 3B), indicating continued response to immunotherapy.
