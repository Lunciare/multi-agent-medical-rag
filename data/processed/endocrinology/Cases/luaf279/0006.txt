A week later, EDP-M was initiated and after 6 cycles, the disease was considered sta­ ble according to the Response Evaluation Criteria in Solid Tumors (RECIST) v1.1 criteria . A consensus was reached to extend EDP-M to 8 cycles. After 8 cycles of EDP-M, the pa­ tient’s plasma androgens levels partly improved, and her corti­ sol levels were in the normal range (see Table 1). However, subsequent imaging revealed a progression of the adrenal tu­ mor (9.2 × 18.2 cm), with stability of the pulmonary micronod­ ules. A biopsy of the lesion was performed for therapeutic purposes and showed low programmed-death-ligand 1 (PD-L1) expression (<1%), low tumor mutation burden (TMB) (5.04 mutations/mB), a T-cell score of 3.43 (88th per­ centile) (hot immune phenotype), and a microsatellite stability status (MSS) tumor. A TP53 and a CTNNB1 somatic patho­ genic variant were also identified in the tumor cells. Germline mutation testing confirmed a heterozygous missense TP53 c.638G>A, p.
