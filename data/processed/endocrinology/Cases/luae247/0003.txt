Introduction Early cataract formation has been described as an uncommon complication of type 1 diabetes in children and adolescents, occurring in an estimated 0.1% to 3.4% of pediatric patients [1-6]. In this report, we describe a case of a 15-year-old patient who presented with severe bilateral vision loss secondary to rapid formation of cataracts and was found to be in diabetic ketoacidosis secondary to new-onset type 1 diabetes. After im­ proved glycemic control, the patient underwent cataract sur­ gery with successful return of 20/20 vision. In this case report, we detail the pathophysiology of pediatric cataracts in diabetes, as well as discuss prevalence, presentation, treat­ ment, and screening guidelines. Her vision started as the inability to see far objects but rapidly deteriorated over the previous month, prompting a visit to an optometrist.
