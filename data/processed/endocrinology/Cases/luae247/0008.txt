Children with cataracts typically first present with de­ creased vision—in younger children, parents often report in­ ability to maintain gaze, lack of eye contact, or frequent eye straining. In older children, patients report progressive vision deficits—first an inability to see far objects, and then later, a decreased ability to see near objects [8-10]. Leukocoria (a white/opacified lens) or lack of red reflex are classic signs seen on examination . Treatment for cataracts involves surgical removal of the cataracts, with most patients having resolution of symptoms and improvements in vision . Given the severity of vision loss in this case, and the unlikely change of spontaneous resolution over months to years, sur­ gery was recommended in our patient.
