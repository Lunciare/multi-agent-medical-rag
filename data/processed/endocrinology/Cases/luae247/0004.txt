Diagnostic Assessment The patient’s visual acuity on presentation was notable for “hand motion” vision—she could visualize hand motion in front of her but was unable to count how many fingers were present on the hand. The eye examination was significant for white hypermature bilateral cataracts (Fig. 1), which pre­ vented a visualization of the retina, but a posterior segment ultrasound was unremarkable. A laboratory workup by ophthalmology included a normal complete blood count, a comprehensive metabolic panel significant for hyperglycemia with glucose of 668 mg/dL (37.07 mmol/L) (normal reference range: 70-110 mg/dL; 3.9-6.1 mmol/L), mild acidosis of HCO3 15 mEq/L (15 mmol/L) (normal reference range: 21-28mEq/L; 21-28 mmol/L), and an elevated hemoglobin A1c of >20% (normal reference range <5.7%).
