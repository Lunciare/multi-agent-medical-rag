She was immediately referred to the emergency room but presented the next day (due to social barriers) and was found to be in mild diabetic ketoacidosis with a venous pH of 7.285 (normal reference range 7.35-7.45), beta-hydroxybutyric acid 5.41 mmol/L (31.43 mg/dL) (normal reference range: <0.4 mmol/L; 0.21-2.81 mg/dL), glucose 480 mg/dL (26.64 mmol/ L), and insulin c-peptide 0.32 ng/mL (0.106 nmol/L) (normal reference range 0.5-3.5 ng/mL; 0.17-1.17 nmol/L). Pancreatic antibodies were significant for positive glutamic acid decarb­ oxylase and zinc transporter 8 antibodies, confirming type 1 diabetes. Treatment The patient was admitted to the intensive care unit and started on insulin infusion at 0.1u/kg/hour; she transitioned quickly to subcutaneous insulin. She underwent cataract surgery for her left eye 1 week after presentation. Within 6 months of diagnosis, she completed cataract surgery for her right eye. Outcome and Follow-up After bilateral cataract surgery, her corrected visual acuity was 20/20 in both eyes, and she demonstrated no signs of dia­ betic retinopathy.
