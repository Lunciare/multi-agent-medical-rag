Current International Society for Pediatric and Adolescent Diabetes clinical guidelines regarding microvascular compli­ cations, including ophthalmological concerns, are mainly focused on diabetic retinopathy. The guidelines briefly men­ tion diabetic cataracts, focusing on a comprehensive initial eye examination that should include evaluation for cataracts. However, afterward, they recommend cataract monitoring should be concurrent with retinopathy evaluation—in pa­ tients with less than 10 years of diagnosis and good glycemic control, this is only once every 2 years. Given the possibility of a rapidly progressive symptomatic cataract, pediatric endocri­ nologists should consider continually screening for visual symptoms at each visit (typically every 3-4 months) to moni­ tor for symptoms concerning for cataracts and refer to oph­ thalmology for further evaluation if there are positive findings on the screening.
