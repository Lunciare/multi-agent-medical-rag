In diabetic cataracts the hypothesized pathophysiology involves hyperglycemia leading to defect in the polyol pathway. This increases activation of aldose reductase, which converts glucose into sorbitol. Having more sorbitol leads to fluid accumulation into the lens, creating os­ motic damage. In addition, there is increased nicotinamide adenine di­ nucleotide phosphate hydrogen oxidase activity in the polyol pathway leading to increased free radicals; this, combined with lens glycosylation, creates oxidative stress.
