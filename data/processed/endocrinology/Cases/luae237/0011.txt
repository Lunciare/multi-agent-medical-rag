Eighty percent of individuals with nonsyndromic 46,XX tes­ ticular DSD are SRY-positive . The proximity of SRY to pseudoautosomal region 1 makes it susceptible to transloca­ tion to the X chromosome during paternal sperm meiosis and results in X-type sperm containing the SRY gene.
