The patient was informed that he would be unable to father a biological child and was offered options of either adoption or in vivo fertilization with donor sperm. The presence of the Y chromosome in DSD patients in­ creases the risk for a gonadal tumor. A gonadal tumor in 46,XX testicular DSD without the Y chromosome is extreme­ ly rare and is limited to isolated case reports of germ cell tumor and Leydig cell tumor . Testicular ultrasound in our patient revealed a 0.2-cm hypoechoic focus within the right testis, which subsequently reduced in size on repeat scan. Outcome and Follow-up Our patient will be continued on lifelong testosterone replace­ since diagnosis, he is currently maintained on IM testosterone cypionate 150 mg twice weekly, with serum testosterone lev­ els fluctuating between 8.0 and 26.0 nmol/L.
