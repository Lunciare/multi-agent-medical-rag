Discussion Malignant insulinomas present 2 major obstacles: refractory hypoglycemia and metastatic disease limiting the curative role of surgery. We present a rare case of a malignant insulinoma with unique management including chemotherapy, 2-month course of diazoxide, lanreotide, and CGM.
