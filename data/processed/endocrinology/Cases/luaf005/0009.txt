Other case studies describe timely treatment of asymptomatic hypoglycemia or prevention of hypoglycem­ ic episodes with use of CGM . However, most published cases are limited to several weeks of CGM use until definitive treatment was provided. A case of a 70-year-old individual with an unresectable insulinoma highlights similar benefits of a CGM as found in our patient, but given the difference in age and activity level, CGM in our patient has opened fur­ ther benefits of live monitoring of glucose levels . Our pa­ tient was dependent on the CGM for months to self-titrate D10 and diazoxide dosing, until the stabilizing effect of som­ atostatin and chemotherapy agents took place. This allowed her to minimize the side effects of diazoxide while effectively mitigating hypoglycemia episodes prior to their occurrence, thus avoiding recurrent emergency department visits.
