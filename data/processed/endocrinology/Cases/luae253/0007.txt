The CGM sensors used were noted to have signal loss and then re­ moved, which revealed that the sensor wire had detached . As technology advances, sensor wires have become thinner over time, reducing pain with insertion but also potentially in­ creasing the risk of breaking . Currently, the FreeStyle Libre 3 sensor wires are <0.4 mm in diameter and are inserted to a depth of 5 mm under the skin . Despite theoretical in­ creased risk of breaking with thinner and smaller sensor wires, a recently published review has only noted 2 prior cases of re­ tained CGM sensor wires and another reports 0.03% risk of detached sensor wires in all patients , suggesting a lower risk of the complication retained or broken sensor wires. When considering evaluation for retained CGM sensor wires, X-ray is an optimal, first-line choice. In our patient, US did not identify the retained sensor wires that were subsequently visual­ ized on X-ray.
