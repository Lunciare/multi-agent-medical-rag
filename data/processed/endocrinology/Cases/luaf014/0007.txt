Following the establishment of targeted therapy for NS, alongside metabolic interventions with metformin 850 mg twice daily and atorvastatin/ezetimibe 40/10 mg once daily, the patient showed improvement in metabolic parameters, weight reduction, and decreased hyperphagia. Imaging studies (Fig. 1B-1D) also revealed significant lesion reduction. However, there was no recovery of the affected hormonal axes.
