Glucocorticoids are typically used as the first-line therapy, as highlighted by Fritz and colleagues in a 2016 systematic review. In 27% of cases, second-line treatments were initiated, which included metho­ trexate, azathioprine, chloroquine, mycophenolate, and cyclo­ sporin A. Furthermore, 9% of cases required third-line treatments, such as cyclophosphamide, rituximab and tumor necrosis factor-α antagonists. Our patient initially demonstrated a partial response to glu­ cocorticoids. However, based on recent reports of positive out­ comes in patients with systemic sarcoidosis and neurological involvement treated with rituximab, we initiated this therapy as a second-line treatment. The patient exhibited an excellent response to rituximab , underscoring its potential efficacy in cases of NS that do not fully respond to glucocorticoids.
