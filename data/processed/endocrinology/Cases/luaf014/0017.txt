Additionally, 5 patients developed new hormo­ nal deficiencies during follow-up. Our patient exhibited an uncommon presentation of the disease, manifesting as isolated NS confirmed through histo­ pathology. Her clinical picture was primarily characterized by endocrinological disturbances indicative of pituitary and hypothalamic involvement, including multiple hormonal defi­ ciencies and symptoms related to hypothalamic dysfunction. She demonstrated an excellent response to second-line treat­ ment with rituximab. Learning Points • Isolated NS is a rare entity that should be included in the differential diagnosis of patients with hypothalamic- pituitary lesions, particularly when arginine-vasopressin deficiency is among the initial clinical manifestations. • Histopathological confirmation is crucial in patients with­ out identifiable extraneural lesions suggestive of systemic sarcoidosis, as it may influence patient outcomes and re­ duce mortality through the establishment of timely and appropriate treatment. • Rituximab may be considered as a second- or third-line treatment option in patients with NS who demonstrate partial or no response to glucocorticoid therapy.
