A follow-up imaging study conducted 6 months later re­ vealed remission of abnormalities in the pituitary gland and stalk; however, persistent and more extensive changes were observed at the suprasellar level. Given the lack of response to prior therapies, treatment was transitioned to rituximab, initiated at 500 mg intravenously every 2 weeks for the first 4 months, resulting in an excellent response (Fig. 1D). This was followed by monthly infusions for an additional 6 months. Outcome and Follow-up During the course of her illness, the patient likely developed obesity, characterized by hyperphagia and impaired satiety, alongside metabolic complications associated with consider­ able weight gain. The patient experienced insulin resistance, dyslipidemia, and an 11.8-kg weight increase from her initial weight of 69.9 kg. Additionally, persistent hyperprolactinemia and galactor­ rhea required initiation of cabergoline at 1 mg weekly, which successfully normalized prolactin levels and resolved the galactorrhea.
