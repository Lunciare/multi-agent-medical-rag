Case Presentation In 2021, a 20-year-old woman sought medical consultation due to a 4-month history of polyuria, nausea, vomiting, a 7-kg weight loss, blurred vision, galactorrhea, and amenorrhea. Her family history was negative for pituitary or parathyroid tumors. Clinically, the patient presented with a body mass index of 27.3 and blood pressure of 90/60 mm Hg. On detailed question­ ing, she reported symptoms of asthenia, adynamia, fatigue, dry skin, thirst, and polyuria without a defined diurnal pattern. She denied experiencing orthostatic hypotension, fever, or flush­ ing and reported no use of medications or dietary supplements.
