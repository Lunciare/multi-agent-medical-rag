Learning Points • Thyroid schwannomas are extremely rare and present diagnostic challenges due to their overlapping clinical and imaging features with more common thyroid tumors. • FNA often provides inconclusive results or demonstrates spindle cells, and histopathological examination remains essential for definitive diagnosis. • If spindle cells are noted on FNA, it is important to con­ sider medullary and anaplastic thyroid carcinomas as a part of the differential diagnosis. • Surgical excision is generally curative, with an excellent prognosis and no recurrence reported in the literature. • Further studies are needed to deepen understanding of the molecular and genetic underpinnings of these rare thyroid tumors.
