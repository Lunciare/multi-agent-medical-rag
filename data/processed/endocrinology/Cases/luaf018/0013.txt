A lobectomy could have been an option had we known the schwannoma diagnosis beforehand; due to the extreme rarity of thyroid schwannomas or TMTs in general, these tu­ mors were not on our preoperative differential diagnosis, which further emphasizes the need to raise awareness of this elusive group of thyroid neoplasms. Postoperative pathology results, which confirmed the benign nature of the tumor and a low proliferation index, suggest that hemithyroidectomy or a lobectomy could have been a sufficient treatment modal­ ity in this case.
