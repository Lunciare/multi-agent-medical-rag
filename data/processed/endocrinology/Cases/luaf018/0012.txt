On the other hand, thyroid schwannomas can be differentiated from extra­ thyroidal schwannomas by their imaging, surgical, and histo­ pathological characteristics . On imaging, thyroid schwannomas appear as well-circumscribed lesions within the thyroid parenchyma, whereas extrathyroidal schwanno­ mas are located outside the thyroid capsule, often near neural structures. Surgically, the tumor’s location relative to the thy­ roid capsule and nearby neural structures helps determine its origin. Histopathologically, thyroid schwannomas exhibit direct involvement of the thyroid parenchyma or capsule without evidence of neural tissue, while extrathyroidal schwannomas are surrounded by neural tissue and lack thy­ roid glandular elements. Treatment is usually either hemi-thyroidectomy or total thyroidectomy . The choice depends on ultra­ sound findings, FNA diagnosis, and the presence of compres­ sive symptoms, following guidelines for thyroid nodule management .
