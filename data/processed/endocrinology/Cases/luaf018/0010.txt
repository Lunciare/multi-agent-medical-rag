Histopathology reveals two distinct pat­ terns: Antoni A (cell-rich areas with palisading nuclei and Verocay bodies) and Antoni B (paucicellular areas with cystic degeneration or xanthomatous changes) . Both patterns occur in approximately 60% of cases, while Antoni A alone is seen in approximately 20%; Antoni B alone has not been reported . Antoni A or Antoni B patterns identified on the eventual histological examination do not seem to demonstrate any form of correlation with the inconclusive/spindle cell/ schwannoma diagnoses made on FNA samples .
