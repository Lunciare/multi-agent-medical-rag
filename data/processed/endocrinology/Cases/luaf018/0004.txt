She also denied hearing loss, neurological abnormal­ ities, visual disturbances, or history of exposure to head and neck radiation. There was no family history of thyroid cancer, neurofibromatosis, ocular or nervous system tumors. On physical examination, a firm, nontender, 3-cm nodule was palpated in the right thyroid lobe. There was no evidence of cervical lymphadenopathy. Pemberton sign was negative. Skin examination did not reveal any café-au-lait spots or cuta­ neous neurofibromas.
