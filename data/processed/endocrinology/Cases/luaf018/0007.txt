Discussion PNSTs are neoplasms that originate from the sheaths of per­ ipheral nerve fibers, primarily derived from Schwann cells or neural crest-derived pluripotent cells . PNSTs within the thyroid gland are exceptionally rare, occurring sporadically or as part of genetic syndromes such as Von Recklinghausen neurofibromatosis (NF-1) . The two main types of be­ nign PNSTs are schwannomas (neurilemmomas) and neuro­ fibromas, with thyroid schwannomas being among the tures, diagnostic challenges, and management of thyroid schwannomas. Thyroid schwannomas are rare, with approximately 39 cases reported to date [7-9, 11]. The first case was described in 1964 by Delaney and Fry . They are most often diag­ nosed in individuals aged 40 to 60 years but have been ob­ served in patients as young as 11 years and as old as 70 years .
