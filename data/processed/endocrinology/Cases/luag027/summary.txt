Fatal tracheal perforation following Dabrafenib- Trametinib therapy in BRAF V600E-mutant mixed anaplastic thyroid cancer Abstract Anaplastic thyroid cancer (ATC), pure or mixed, is the most aggressive form of thyroid cancer that leads to rapid death if left untreated. BRAF V600E is the most frequent pathogenic variant of ATC. Dabrafenib and Trametinib (D+T) have shown promising efficacy and are approved as first-line therapy for unresectable BRAF-mutant ATC. We report a case of fatal aero-digestive perforation in a patient treated with D+T. A 56-year-old man presented with an enlarging cervical mass and airway compromise. BRAF V600E-mutant high-grade papillary thyroid carcinoma with an anaplastic component and tracheal invasion was diagnosed.
