Discussion We described a case of a relatively young patient with progres­ sive symptoms caused by extrinsic compression of neck struc­ tures by a rapidly enlarging thyroid mass. A high-grade PTC with anaplastic components was diagnosed through core biopsy. Interestingly, molecular analysis confirmed the presence of the BRAF V600E mutation in both tumor components, indicating a single tumor progressing to cellular anaplasia. This supports the widely accepted theory that most PDTC and ATC originate from pre-existing PTC, as they often coexist within the same tu­ mor specimen and share a common driver mutation .
