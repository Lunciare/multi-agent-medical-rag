Outcome and follow-up A few days after starting therapy, the patient reported relief from neck tension and dyspnea. However, 2 weeks later, he experi­ enced a new exacerbation of dyspnea, leading to acute respira­ tory failure. The CT scan of the neck showed a significant decrease in the size of the thyroid mass (6.6 cm vs 10 cm before therapy) and an improvement in tracheal deviation (Fig. 4A). However, it also revealed a large tracheal perforation with air en­ tering the subcutaneous tissue (Fig. 4B).
