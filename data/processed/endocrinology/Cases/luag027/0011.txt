In a phase 3 trial comparing the efficacy of cabozantinib vs placebo in patients with metastatic medullary thyroid cancer, the incidence of gastrointestinal fistulas of any grade was 0.9%. In contrast, the incidence of non-gastrointestinal fistulas was 3.7% . A few cases of aerodigestive fistula formation have been reported during sorafenib treatment , whereas no cases have been described with vandetanib. The main risk factors asso­ ciated with organ perforation include tumor infiltration of aero­ digestive structures (such as the trachea and esophagus), previous radiotherapy, an aggressive histological subtype, and extensive necrosis induced by a rapid therapeutic response . The neoadjuvant setting may carry a higher risk of perfor­ ation due to extensive infiltration of neck structures, which is often the primary reason the tumor is considered inoperable and managed with neoadjuvant treatment using multikinase in­ hibitors . Managing perforations involves immediately stopping multiki­ nase inhibitor therapy, providing nutritional and respiratory sup­ port, and engaging a multidisciplinary team.
