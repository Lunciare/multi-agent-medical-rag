His medical history revealed that the compressive symptoms started in November 2023, when he underwent an otolaryngo­ logical examination for dysphonia and neck tightness, which had progressively worsened. At that time, he was diagnosed with gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD) and prescribed pro­ ton pump inhibitor therapy; however, no additional diagnostic investigations were performed. Diagnostic assessment Due to worsening symptoms, a new videolaryngoscopic examination was conducted in April 2024, revealing left vocal cord paralysis. Subsequently, the patient underwent esophago­ gastroscopy, which demonstrated deformation of the cervical esophagus lumen due to extrinsic compression. A contrast- enhanced whole-body computed tomography (CT scan) revealed an 8 cm lesion in the left thyroid lobe, adjacent to the trachea and esophagus, resulting in airway narrowing and compression. The left common carotid artery was also enveloped. Multiple bilateral metastatic lymph nodes (up to 8 mm) and pulmonary micronodules (6-7 mm) were detected, raising suspicion for sec­ ondary lesions.
