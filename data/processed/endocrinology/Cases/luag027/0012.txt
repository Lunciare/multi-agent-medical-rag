Treatment options may include stent placement, surgical intervention, and antibiot­ ic therapy for secondary infections. Therefore, prevention and screening of cases at higher risk of perforations should be performed. Next, a thorough evaluation, is crucial, along with multidisciplinary consultation. In such cases, careful monitoring is necessary, and starting treatment with a reduced dose of multikinase inhibitor should be consid­ ered to reduce complications related to rapid tumor shrinkage. While tracheal perforation has been documented with multiki­ nase antiangiogenic drugs such as lenvatinib, to date, no cases of tracheal perforation have been reported with D+T. This AE was particularly relevant in our cases due to its rapid onset, within 14 days of starting therapy, leading to death. This case is particularly relevant because the use of these se­ lective drugs, when feasible based on the type of mutation, has recently been recommended as neoadjuvant treatment for cases deemed inoperable due to invasion of neck structures.
