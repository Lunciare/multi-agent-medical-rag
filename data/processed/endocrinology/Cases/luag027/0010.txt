Thanks to early histological and molecular diagnosis, targeted treatment with D+T was started, resulting in a significant reduc­ relief from compressive symptoms affecting the airway and neck. However, the tumor had already infiltrated the membran­ ous wall of the trachea. Tracheal perforation has been reported with the initiation of multikinase antiangiogenic inhibitors. In the phase 3 Study of (E7080) Lenvatinib in Differentiated Cancer of the Thyroid (SELECT), gastrointestinal fistulas of any grade occurred in 1.5% of cases treated with lenvatinib . Subsequently, we con­ ducted a retrospective study of 95 patients with radioiodine- refractory DTC treated with lenvatinib and found that 14.7% of patients developed aerodigestive fistulas or organ perforation .
