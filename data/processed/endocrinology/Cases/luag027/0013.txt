Wang et al reported a series of six patients with BRAF V600E-mutant ATC and locoregionally advanced disease who were treated with neoadjuvant D+T. All patients achieved complete surgical resection without requiring a tracheostomy . Similarly, other case reports have suggested initiating D+T instead of antiangiogenic multikinase inhibitors due to its lower risk of bleeding and organ perforation . The occurrence of organ perforation in this context is especial­ ly concerning, as, to our knowledge, such events have not been reported as side effects of D+T therapy. These drugs specifically target tyrosine kinases and do not directly affect angiogenesis or cause necrosis, both of which are known to contribute to organ perforation. However, we hypothesize that the rapid tumor shrinkage, which had already invaded the membranous wall of the trachea, may have caused structural weakening and rupture. This indicates that while these drugs are highly effective at redu­ cing tumors, they may carry unexpected risks when tumors ex­ tensively invade nearby vital structures. The lack of similar reported cases emphasizes the importance of real-world experience in recognizing and documenting new or unexpected adverse events. Close
