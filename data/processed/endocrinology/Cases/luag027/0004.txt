Furthermore, in patients with ATC who often have invasion of neck structures, mainly the trachea and esophagus, multikinase inhibitors, because of their strong antiangiogenic effect, can cause potentially fatal adverse events like the development of aero-digestive perforations . So far, no data has been re­ ported regarding the risk of perforation in ATC patients treated with D+T. We reported a case of a patient with a BRAF V600E mutant ATC treated with D+T treatment who developed a fatal aero-digestive perforation.
