A pre­ operative computed tomography (CT) scan disclosed several paraaortic lymph nodes measuring < 8 mm. Following cryo­ preservation of sperm, a unilateral right-sided orchidectomy and a contralateral testicular biopsy were performed. Histological analysis showed a NSGCT with components of embryonal carcinoma, yolk sac tumor, and choriocarcinoma (Fig. 1). It was radically removed, showing growth into rete testis and lymphatic vessel invasion. The contralateral testis biopsy was normal. Treatment In accordance with the national Swedish guidelines for TC , our patient was promptly referred post-surgery to an on­ cology clinic for specialized care. The patient received a single dose of adjuvant chemotherapy (bleomycin, etoposide and platinol/cisplatin [BEP]).
