Concomitant Exacerbation of Graves Orbitopathy and Double-Seronegative Myasthenia Gravis after SARS-CoV-2 Infection Abstract SARS-CoV-2 infection could trigger autoimmune disease. We report a case of concomitant exacerbation of Graves orbitopathy (GO) and myasthenia gravis (MG) after SARS-CoV-2 infection. A 43-year-old woman had diplopia, proptosis, and swollen eyelids. Blood tests showed thyrotoxicosis and positive thyroid-stimulating hormone receptor antibodies, and orbital magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) showed enlarged extraocular muscles. She was therefore referred to our hospital with diagnosis of GO. Methylprednisolone pulse therapy (MPT) in combination with orbital radiotherapy were performed for 3 weeks, and ocular symptoms improved.
