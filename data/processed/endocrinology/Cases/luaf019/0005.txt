On her second admission, blood tests showed that she was euthyroid and TSAb levels were 917% (see Table 1). Orbital MRI showed predominant enlargement of right inferior rectus muscles with high signal intensity on STIR image (Fig. 2C), compatible with the exacerbation of GO. The second course of MPT and orbital radiotherapy was effective for the enlarge­ ment and inflammatory MRI findings of the ocular muscles (Fig. 2D), but the patient reported continued ptosis, which was especially enhanced in the afternoon. Diurnal fluctuation of the symptoms is characteristic of MG, so we evaluated anti– muscle-specific tyrosine kinase (MuSK) antibodies and anti-AChR antibodies: Both were negative (see Table 1). To further evaluate for a neuromuscular junction disorder, we performed a repetitive nerve stimulation test, which re­ vealed waning at low frequent repetitive stimulation, and the ice pack test, which showed improvement of ptosis.
