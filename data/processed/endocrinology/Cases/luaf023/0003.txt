Case Presentation This is the case of a 48-year-old female, with a complex onco­ logical history of recurrent metastatic melanoma dating from 2004. She initially presented with choroidal melanoma with metastases to the retro-orbital soft tissue and right lung, and she underwent a left orbital exenteration and lung wedge resec­ tion. In 2007-2008, there was evidence of recurrence to the left orbit and the right lower lobe of the lung, leading to tumor re­ section and adjuvant local radiation.
