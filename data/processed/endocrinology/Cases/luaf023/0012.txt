Learning Points • Panniculitis may be an early feature of ICI-associated AGL which may be incidentally reported on PET-CT whole-body scan imaging. • Hypoleptinemia may support the diagnosis of AGL. • Although there may be a strong association with PD-1 in­ hibitors and ICI-induced AGL, it is possible that combin­ ation immunotherapy increases the risk of AGL, as with other ICI related endocrinopathies.
