Further work up included leptin and adiponectin levels which were < 0.5 ng/mL (SI < 0.5 µg/L) (normal reference, 2.8-15.6 ng/mL; SI 2.8-15.6 µg/L for BMI 21 kg/m2; patient BMI 20.65) and < 0.2 µg/mL (normal reference, 5-37 µg/mL for BMI < 25 kg/m2; patient BMI 20.65) respectively, and a lipoprotein(a) of 40.6 nmol/L (SI 0.0406 µmol/L) (normal ref­ erence, < 75 nmol/L; SI < 0.075 µmol/L). An excisional skin biopsy of the right leg was performed which showed changes consistent with involutional lipoatrophy/lipodystrophy (Fig. 3). PET-CT imaging performed for melanoma surveil­ lance reported diffuse hepatic steatosis and paucity of mesen­ teric fat. Treatment For the treatment of diabetes and hypertriglyceridemia, glar­ gine insulin was commenced with subsequent addition of ro­ suvastatin 5 mg daily.
