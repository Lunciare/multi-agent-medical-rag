Fertility data in 3βHSD2D are very limited but 46, XY pa­ tients seem to be infertile due to abnormal testis development and arrested spermatogenesis or azoospermia . Approximately 20% to 30% of 46, XY patients with 3βHSD2D have been described to have testicular adrenal rest tumors but the risk for malignancies seems to be low . In conclusion, we discuss the pitfalls and challenges in diagnosis and management of a 46, XY 3βHSD2D patient who presented at birth with ambiguous genitalia, without adrenal insufficiency, was registered with female sex, and was late diagnosed at age 5 years by steroid profiling and genetic analysis.
