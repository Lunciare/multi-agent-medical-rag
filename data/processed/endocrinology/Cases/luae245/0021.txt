The mutant (Mut) c.307 + 1G > A minigene was then generated using site-directed mutagenesis following the QuikChange protocol by Stratagene (Agilent Technologies Inc). Correct sequences of plasmids were confirmed by direct sequencing (MicroSynth AG). HEK293T cells were transiently transfected with the WT and Mut minigenes (Lipofectamine 2000, 1 µg of DNA/well in 6-well plates). Total RNA was extracted and RT-PCR for HSD3B2 cDNA was performed.
