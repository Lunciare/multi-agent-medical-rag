Introduction Primary acquired adrenal insufficiency (AI) stems from deficient adrenal production of glucocorticoids and mineralocorticoids and manifests clinically and biochemically after destruction of more than 90% of the adrenal cortex . Autoimmune adrena­ litis accounts for the majority of cases of AI in developed coun­ tries, followed by infections such as tuberculosis, fungal, and viral infections. Ischemic insult to the adrenal glands is an ex­ tremely rare cause of AI . Here we report a case of an 8-year- old girl who developed AI due to an ischemic insult to the adrenal glands in the setting of severe acute pancreatitis.
