Diagnostic Assessment The patient’s vital signs at presentation included blood pres­ sure of 88/52 mm Hg, heart rate of 89 beats per minute, respiratory rate of 22 breaths per minute, oxygen saturation of 94%, and temperature of 96.8 °F (37 °C). She did not have hyperpigmentation, acne, hirsutism, or clitoromegaly.
