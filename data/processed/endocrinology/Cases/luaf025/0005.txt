The patient was diagnosed with acute pancrea­ titis, attributed to viral infection. spiratory distress syndrome (ARDS), and acute renal failure. She required epinephrine infusion for 5 days for the manage­ ment of hypotension. She developed thrombocytopenia the third day of hospitalization (DOH) and anemia on DOH 4. Her prothrombin time-international normalized ratio (PT-INR) was 1.4 (0.8-1.1). She developed bleeding from the mouth, nasogastric tube, and right internal jugular central venous line, and received fresh frozen plasma, red blood cell, and platelet transfusions. CT of the abdomen obtained DOH 4 showed bilateral symmetrically thickened centrally hypoen­ hancing adrenal glands without focal lesion or calcification, and a nonocclusive splenic vein thrombosis (Fig. 1A). Anticoagulant therapy was not initiated due to active bleed­ ing, thrombocytopenia, and prolonged PT-INR. Metabolic evaluation on DOH 5 showed normal serum electrolytes and glucose levels (Table 1).
