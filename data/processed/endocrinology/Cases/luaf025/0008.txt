Magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) of the adrenal glands (Fig. 1C) obtained 8 months after the initial presentation showed bilaterally diminutive adrenal glands with marked decrease in size compared to her initial CT scan, suggesting involution following a substantial insult to the adrenal glands. Follow-up studies 2 months after hospitalization revealed persistent glucocorticoid and mineralocorticoid defi­ ciency with a morning cortisol of 4.7 µg/dL (129.7 nmol/L) (pri­ or to the first morning dose of hydrocortisone) and renin of 37.4 ng/mL/hour (37.4 µg/L/hour). The fludrocortisone dose was increased to 0.1 mg daily based on her elevated renin level. At 1 year after initial presentation, her morning cortisol was less than 1 µg/dL (27.6 nmol/L) and renin was 13.5 ng/mL/hour (13.5 µg/L/hour).
