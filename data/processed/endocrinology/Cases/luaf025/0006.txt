A high-dose cosyntropin (250 µg) stimula­ tion test confirmed the diagnosis of AI (cortisol 1.1 µg/dL [30.3 nmol/L] at 30 minutes and 1 µg/dL [27.6 nmol/L] at 60 minutes after administration of cosyntropin). Her 17-α-hydroxyprogesterone levels were not elevated (<10 ng/ dL [<0.30 nmol/L]), the very long-chain fatty acid levels were normal, and 21-hydroxylase autoantibody was negative. AI was thought to be due to an ischemic injury to the adrenal glands in the setting of acute severe pancreatitis with septic shock and multiorgan dysfunction. Treatment Hydrocortisone was initiated at 5 mg in the morning and 2.5 mg in the afternoon (8.6 mg/m2/day) with fludrocortisone 0.05 mg daily. Medications were administered via gastros­ tomy tube.
