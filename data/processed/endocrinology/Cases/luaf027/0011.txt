While pheochromocytoma is a rare type of neuroendocrine malignancy, it is well-established that in certain instances it is associated with ectopic ACTH production and subsequent CS. This phenomenon was documented as early as 1964. Furthermore, it has been estimated that about 5% of ectopic ACTH-syndrome cases are due to ACTH-secreting pheochro­ mocytomas .
