On post-operative day 2, the patient developed low-grade fe­ vers, with a maximum oral temperature of 100.7°F [38.2 °C], but his leukocyte count and lactate continued to downtrend to 15 × 103/µL (SI: 15 × 109/L) and 7.2 mg/dL (SI: 0.8 mmol/L), re­ spectively, normalizing by post-operative day 3. He remained and continued to improve clinically. During the patient’s hospitalization, GLP-1RA and prednisone were discontinued. Insulin was initiated on a sliding scale for diabetes management, and upon discharge, the patient resumed prednisone 5 mg without recurrence of similar symptoms. Discussion As previously described, PI is a radiographic finding, and evaluat­ ing the underlying cause is imperative to rule out time-sensitive, life-threatening etiologies such as bowel ischemia. Workup relies primarily on CT imaging and supportive laboratory markers .
