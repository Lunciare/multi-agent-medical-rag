Regarding possible causes of PI, in our case, an infectious eti­ ology is unlikely in view of the two sets of negative blood cul­ tures, negative peritoneal fluid cultures, and negative stool PCR and HIV testing. Additionally, this patient had a complex medical history; however, none of his chronic medical conditions are associated with PI. In the absence of other etiologies, medication-induced PI must also be considered. Prednisone has been associated with PI in prior case reports and, therefore, represents a possible contributor alone or in com­ bination with GLP-1RAs. Previous reports hypothesize that mech­ anical injury through atrophy of bowel lymphoid follicles contributes to PI .
