Point-of-care (POC) glucose monitoring at home taken 3 times a day before feeding (breast milk or formula) demonstrated blood glucose levels between 50 and 60 mg/dL (2.8-3.3 mmol/L) despite feeding every 3 hours. Diagnostic Assessment our suspicion for BWS-related hyperinsulinism was high, and the patient was readmitted at 2 weeks of age for a diagnostic fast. The patient was fed prior to the challenge and underwent a 12-hour fast with labs notable for a postprandial glucose of 58 mg/dL (3.2 mmol/L) on POC glucose and i-STAT measure­ ment while the rest of the glucose levels remained >60 mg/dL (>3.3 mmol/L) with low β-hydroxybutyrate (Table 1). After completion of the fast, the patient was fed fortified breast milk. Two hours postprandially, he was noted to have hypo­ glycemia with glucose of 58 mg/dL (3.2 mmol/L) and suppressed β-hydroxybutyrate at <1.8 mg/dL (<0.18 mmol/L) (normal reference: >3.6 mg/dL, >1.8 mmol/L) and an elevated insulin level of 3.9 μIU/mL (27 pmol/L) (normal reference range: 2-13 μIU/mL; 14-90 pmol/L) (Table 2). The patient re­ mained asymptomatic during the time of hypoglycemia. After completion of the hypoglycemia challenge and shared decision-making with the family, the patient was discharged home.
