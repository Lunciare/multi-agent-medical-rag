Parents continued to check glucose levels for 2 weeks. They reported consistent postprandial hypoglycemia <50 mg/dL (<2.8 mmol/L) that occurred 2 to 3 hours after a meal regardless of intake (fortified breast milk or formula). Sugar packets given to treat hypoglycemia also caused delayed hypoglycemia to 50 mg/dL (2.8 mmol/L), which improved only with feeding. Several treatment options to prevent post­ prandial hypoglycemia were considered including cornstarch, further fortification of feeds, and prolonging duration of feeds. Given the patient’s risk for overgrowth with BWS, we opted to hold off on further fortification of feeds. As the pa­ tient fed by mouth exclusively, prolongation of feeds was not have enzymatic activity to properly digest cornstarch , a trial of low-dose diazoxide with chlorothiazide was performed. Treatment This patient was readmitted at 4 weeks of age for initiation of diazoxide and chlorothiazide. On the first day of admission, baseline complete blood count, complete metabolic panel, and echocardiogram were obtained and resulted in normal range.
