Case Presentation A male infant was born to a mother with BWS at 36.2 weeks of gestation with a birth weight of 9 lb (4.04 Kg). Maternal med­ ical records were not available. The patient was born large for gestational age. Shortly after birth, he was found to have per­ sistent hypoglycemia from 32 to 55 mg/dL (1.8-3.1 mmol/L) (normal reference: 70-140 mg/dL, 3.9-7.8 mmol/L), requiring a glucose infusion rate (GIR) of 9.7 mg/kg/min and was admit­ ted to the neonatal intensive care unit. An initial physical exam showed features suggestive of BWS including macroglossia, multiple ear pits, and an umbilical hernia. Over time, his feed­ ing improved and his GIR was weaned. However, he continued to have intermittent hypoglycemia. The patient was discharged home after glucometer and glucagon teaching.
