A glucagon stimu­ lation test, an indication of glycogen reserves that serves as fur­ ther evidence for insulin excess, was not completed after shared decision-making with the family. The incidence of hypoglycemia in BWS is around 50% . Previously reported BWS cases demonstrate that 80% of hypoglycemia will be mild and transient, while 20% will per­ sist longer than a week and can be difficult to manage.
