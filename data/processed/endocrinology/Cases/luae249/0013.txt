Despite having classical clinical features and a positive fam­ ily history of BWS, this patient tested negative on a BWS gen­ etic panel. After a discussion with the genetics team, he was diagnosed with clinical BWS and further genetic testing was deferred. Learning Points • Patients with features of BWS should be screened for fast­ ing and postprandial hypoglycemia in the newborn period. • Patients with BWS and hyperinsulinism can be treated with diazoxide and chlorothiazide. • Patients with BWS may test negative on the available gen­ etic testing and can be diagnosed with clinical BWS.
