Postprandial hypoglycemia is defined as the occurrence of hypoglycemia 3 to 4 hours after a meal. The dif­ ferential diagnosis for postprandial hypoglycemia includes hy­ perinsulinism as well as hereditary fructose intolerance, galactosemia, and organic acidemia . Our index of suspi­ cion for hyperinsulinism was high based on a maternal diag­ nosis of BWS and physical exam features consistent with BWS in the index case.
