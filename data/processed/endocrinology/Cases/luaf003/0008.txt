Treatment of pituitary aden­ omas in patients with MEN-1 is the same as sporadic pituitary adenomas . Treatment includes surveillance, medical management (eg, dopamine agonists for prolactinoma), trans­ sphenoidal surgery, and radiation therapy . Both patients’ pituitary adenomas were treated as per guidelines . Duodenopancreatic NETs are seen in >80% of patients with MEN-1 by age 80 years . [PATIENT] developed a gastrinoma, the most common functioning-NET in MEN-1 . Developing dyspepsia and diarrhea highlights the importance of considering MEN-1 in those with other classical MEN-1 tri­ ad tumors. Suggested screening/monitoring includes pancreatic imaging, fasting glucose, and gastrin levels at the time of diagno­ sis with repeat biochemical testing annually and imaging every 2 to 3 years thereafter . More recently, screening with chro­ mogranin A, pancreatic polypeptide, or glucagon has been ques­ tioned . Treatment varies based on the type and staging of the NET.
