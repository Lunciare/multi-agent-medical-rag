In this patient, isolated anti-cyclic citrulli­ nated peptide positivity was not linked to clinical evidence of rheumatoid arthritis, although future development of this condi­ tion is possible . Patients with TBIRS usually present with hyperglycemia second­ ary to severe insulin resistance, they require exceptionally high doses of insulin to maintain euglycemia . Furthermore, they tend to have acanthosis nigricans distributed in peri-orbicular, peri-oral, and labial region . Our patient did not experience hyperglycemia or have acanthosis nigricans.
