Learning points • TBIRS is a rare autoimmune disorder that may present with hypoglycemia or hyperglycemia. • It must be considered in patients with a spectrum of unexplained hypoglycemia, insulin resistance, and hyperglycemia. • Management with dietary modification and pharmacother­ apy can be effective in inducing remission but long-term monitoring is necessary as patients may relapse. • Pharmacotherapy includes a combination of cyclophospha­ mide, rituximab, and glucocorticoids.
