Key Words hypoparathyroidism, hypocalcemia, hypercalcemia, HDR syndrome, vitamin D, enteral nutrition Abbreviations: BTF, blenderized tube feeding; HDR, hypoparathyroidism, sensorineural deafness, and renal dysplasia syndrome; PTH, parathyroid hormone. Introduction Hypoparathyroidism is a rare disorder characterized by parathy­ roid hormone (PTH) deficiency leading to hypocalcemia . Treatment of hypoparathyroidism with activated vitamin D and calcium carbonate is associated with increased risk of hypercal­ cemia, hypercalciuria, and nephrocalcinosis reported in more than a third of patients . These complications are usually due to inadvertent overdose of medications. We present a case of a 6-year-old boy with hypoparathyroid­ ism, sensorineural deafness, and renal dysplasia (HDR) syn­ drome, also known as Barakat syndrome, and feeding difficulties who developed severe hypercalcemia twice during 6 years of treatment, and it was caused not just by medication. Both episodes of calcium overload were related to excessive diet­ ary calcium intake from enteral formula. Calcium carbonate also played a role in the first episode of hypercalcemia; the second time hypercalcemia resulted entirely from dietary calcium excess.
