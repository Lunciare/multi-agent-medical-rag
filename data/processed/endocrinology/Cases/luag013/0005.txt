He missed blood calcium checks due to technical difficulties obtaining his blood samples, continued taking the same doses of medications, and presented 3 months later with blood calcium of 17.8 mg/dL (SI: 4.45 mmol/L). Hypercalcemia was corrected, and he was subsequently treated with calcitriol without calcium carbonate.
