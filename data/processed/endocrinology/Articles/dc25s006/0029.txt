Setting and Modifying Glycemic Goals Glycemic goals and management should be individualized and not one size ﬁts all. To prevent both microvascular and macro- vascular complications of diabetes, there is a major call to overcome therapeutic in- ertia and treat to individualized goals (56,67). Numerous factors must be considered when setting a glycemic goal. The ADA proposes general goals that are appropri- ate for many people but emphasizes the importance of individualization based on key person characteristics. Glycemic goals must be individualized in the context of shared decision-making to address indi- vidual needs and preferences and con- sider characteristics that inﬂuence risks and beneﬁts of therapy; this approach may optimize engagement and self- efﬁcacy.
