However, a meta- analysis of individual participant data from UKPDS, ACCORD, ADVANCE, and VADT demonstrated a signiﬁcant reduc- tion in myocardial infarctions and major CVD events but no difference in stroke, heart failure, or mortality between in- tensive and less intensive glycemic man- agement (57). Longer-term epidemiological follow-up has been performed in these studies, and a clear pattern of CVD beneﬁt has emerged (58–60). In the post-DCCT follow-up of the EDIC cohort, participants previously randomized to the intensive arm had a signiﬁcant 57% reduction in the risk of nonfatal myocardial infarction, stroke, or cardiovascular death compared with those previously randomized to the standard arm (58).
