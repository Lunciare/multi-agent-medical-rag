Glucagon was traditionally dispensed as a powder that requires reconstitution prior to injec- tion. However, intranasal and ready-to- inject glucagon preparations are now widely available and are preferred due to their ease of administration resulting in more rapid correction of hypoglycemia (135–137). Although the physical and chemical stability of glucagon has im- proved with newer formulations, care should be taken to replace glucagon products when they reach their expira- tion date and to store glucagon based on speciﬁc product instructions to ensure safe and effective use. For currently avail- able glucagon products and associated providers may prefer only select glucagon products, so it is important to check indi- viduals’ insurance coverage and prescribe formulary products whenever possible.
