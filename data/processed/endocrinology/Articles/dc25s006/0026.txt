The beneﬁt of inten- sive glycemic management in this cohort with type 1 diabetes has been shown to persist for several decades (59) and to be associated with a modest reduction in all-cause mortality (61). UKPDS post-trial monitoring, with 20 years of total follow-up, has shown reductions in myocardial infarctions and total mortality both in the group of over- weight individuals treated with metfor- min and in the group previously treated intensively with sulfonylureas or insulin (47). Shorter overall follow-up of the VADT (10 years) has shown a signiﬁcant reduction in the primary outcome of major CVD events, with myocardial infarctions and heart failure being the commonest outcomes (60). In contrast, shorter follow- up of the ADVANCE study in the Action in Diabetes and Vascular Disease Preterax and Diamicron MR Controlled Evaluation Post Trial Observational Study (ADVANCE- ON) demonstrated no signiﬁcant effect on CVD events (62).
