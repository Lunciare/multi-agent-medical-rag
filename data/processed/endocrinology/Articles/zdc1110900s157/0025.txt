Although the ﬁrst-phase insulin response may be de- creased in some, but not all, of these sub- jects, the ﬁrst phase consistently is increased in the NGT offspring of two type 2 diabetic parents and the total insu- lin response is increased in NGT offspring and in IGT subjects.
