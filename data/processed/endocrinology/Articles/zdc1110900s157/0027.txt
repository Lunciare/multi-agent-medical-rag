Initially, the hyperglycemia- induced increase in insulin secretion serves a compensatory function to main- tain near-NGT. Chronic hyperglyce- mia (glucose toxicity) and/or distur- bances in lipid metabolism (lipotoxicity) may contribute to the defect in insulin secretion. The resultant insulinopenia leads to the emergence/exacerbation of postreceptor defects in insulin action.
