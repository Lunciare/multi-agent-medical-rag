The correlation be- tween homeostatic model assessment and the insulin clamp also is less robust when analyzed in subgroups of glucose toler- ance (11). During an OGTT, signiﬁcant (30 – 40%) amounts of glucose are taken up by the splanchnic bed, and HGP is less completely suppressed than during the insulin clamp technique (12). As a re- sult, the plasma glucose concentration during OGTT is affected by both hepatic and peripheral (primarily muscle) insulin resistance. Therefore, indexes of insulin resistance from the OGTT, e.g., the Mat- suda index, reﬂect both hepatic and pe- ripheral insulin resistance and correlate well (R value 0.70) with insulin sensi- tivity measured with the euglycemic insu- lin clamp (9).
