When the insulin response be- comes deﬁcient, the activity of the glucose transport system becomes severely im- paired and a number of key intracellular enzymatic steps involved in glucose me- tabolism become depressed. Addition- ally, when severe insulinopenia ensues, plasma FFA levels rise, further contribut- ing to the defects in intracellular glucose disposal. This pathogenetic sequence can explain all of the clinical and laboratory features ob- served in type 2 diabetic patients.
