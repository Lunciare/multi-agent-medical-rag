Several cross‐sectional studies have shown an increased prevalence of MASLD in women with PCOS independent of BMI [106–109] and this has also been described in a large epidemiological study comparing 63,000 women with PCOS and 121,000 mat- ched controls . Serum T levels greater than 3.0 nmol/L were linked to a higher risk of MASLD, with the risk increasing further with worsening biochemical androgen excess.
