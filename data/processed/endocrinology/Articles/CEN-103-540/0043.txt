The 2023 PCOS evidence‐based guideline desig- nates PCOS as a CVD risk, albeit based on low‐quality evidence . A recent 22‐year prospective Finnish study with robust phenotyping reaffirmed this, showing higher cardio- vascular risks in women with PCOS versus controls . The majority of metabolic studies in CAH have focused on children or adolescents; however, a Swedish cohort of 588 patients with CAH of all ages showed an increased risk of CVD (OR 2.7) compared to the reference population; in particular women with non‐classic CAH were at increased risk .
