Androgen receptor (AR) gene mutations are the most frequent cause of 46XY DSD and encompass a spectrum of disease from complete (phenotypic female) to partial (assigned male or female sex depending on severity) . The 46XY DSD due to complete androgen insensitivity syndrome (CAIS) is the clinical consequence of a dysfunctional AR, which cannot mediate the genomic effects of androgen binding.
