There are IGF‐1 receptors on thecal cells, and IGF‐1 may directly stimulate the activity of 17‐hydroxylase and 17,20 lyase, upregulating ovarian androgen production . Insulin resist- ance can also cause increased adrenal androgen biosynthesis . Excess GH is associated with lower SHBG alongside preservation of total T levels.
