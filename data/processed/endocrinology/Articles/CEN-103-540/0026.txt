Due to their small size, ovarian androgen‐secreting tumours may not be detected on imaging by transvaginal ultrasound with colour doppler, or on CT/MRI/ FDG PET . Ovarian volumes ≥4.0 cm with asymmetry ≥2.0 cm are suggestive of VOT. Bilateral increased ovarian stroma on ultrasound is characteristic of OHT . Stromal content of SLCTs and Leydig cell tumours may be detected as signal intensity on T2‐weighted MRI .
