It is recommended that the COCP is stopped for a period of 3 months before undertaking biochemical investigations. 6.2 | Testosterone Validated, highly accurate tandem mass spectrometry (LC‐MS/ MS) assays are recommended to measure serum total testos- terone in women . However, across the UK, immunoassays remain the most accessible analytical technique (UK NEQAS, Table S1). Most T immunoassays display a bias relative to LC‐ MS/MS in female samples, which may be either positive or negative depending on the manufacturer and can also be concentration‐dependent. Further data on the performance of several commercially available immunoassays in the United Kingdom compared to gold standard LC‐MS/MS are provided in the UK NEQAS, Figure S1. This bias has been attributed to poor standardisation, poor sensitivity and nonspecific cross‐reactivity [148–150]. Bias may also exhibit temporal variation thereby reflecting reagent and calibrator reformulation (UK NEQAS, Figure S1).
