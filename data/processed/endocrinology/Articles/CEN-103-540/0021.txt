It should be noted that 17OHP levels may also be significantly elevated in patients with adrenocortical carcinoma and therefore adrenal imaging is recommended at diagnosis in adulthood. 3.3 | Ovarian Hyperthecosis (OHT) OHT results from hyperplasia of androgen‐secreting theca cells in the ovary. It is thought that some of the steroidogenically inactive ovarian stromal cells differentiate into theca‐like cells capable of producing androgens. That means they are spread through the ovarian stroma and not purely associated with the ovarian follicles. These theca cells produce androgens under the influence of LH. This condition is usually seen after menopause , where there are high concentrations of LH, after the ovary has few, if any, remaining follicles able to aromatise androgens to oestrogen . The cause of OHT is unknown, but genetic factors may be implicated. High insulin levels are also associ- ated with ovarian hyperthecosis.
