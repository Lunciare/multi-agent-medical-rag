The guideline scope was agreed at an early meeting and specific writing tasks were assigned to core group members based on their respective areas of expertise. A series of vir- tual meetings were held between March 2023 and September 2024. The group members performed narrative reviews of the literature in their assigned area and provided written reports in each case. Each report underwent internal peer review followed by subsequent revisions. In circumstances where consensus on content could not be reached a decision was made by the chair. An advanced draft of the manuscript was reviewed by the SfE Clinical Committee and underwent further revision before submission for publication.
