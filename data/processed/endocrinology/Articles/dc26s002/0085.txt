The diagnosis of GDM is made when at least two>† of the following four plasma glucose levels (measured fasting and at 1, 2, and 3 h during OGTT) are met or exceeded (Carpenter-Coustan criteria ): • Fasting: 95 mg/dL (5.3 mmol/L) • 1 h: 180 mg/dL (10.0 mmol/L) • 2 h: 155 mg/dL (8.6 mmol/L) • 3 h: 140 mg/dL (7.8 mmol/L) GDM, gestational diabetes mellitus; GLT, glucose load test; OGTT, oral glucose tolerance test. *American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists (ACOG) recommends any of the commonly used thresholds of 130, 135, or 140 mg/dL for the 1-h 50-g GLT (223). †ACOG notes that one elevated value can be used for diagnosis (223).
