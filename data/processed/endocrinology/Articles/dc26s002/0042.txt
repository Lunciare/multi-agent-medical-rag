Prediabetes is a significant risk factor for progression to diabetes as well as cardio­ vascular disease and several other cardio­ metabolic outcomes. Criteria for screening for diabetes or prediabetes in asymptom­ atic adults are outlined in Table 2.5. Prediabetes is associated with obesity (especially abdominal or visceral obe­ sity), dyslipidemia with high triglycerides and/or low HDL cholesterol, and hyperten­ sion. The presence of prediabetes should prompt a comprehensive screening for car­ diovascular risk factors.
