Screening and Testing for Prediabetes and Type 2 Diabetes in Asymptomatic Adults Screening for prediabetes and type 2 di­ abetes risk through a targeted assessment of risk factors (Table 2.5) or with an assess­ ment tool, such as the ADA risk test (diabetes.org/diabetes-risk-test), is recom­ mended to guide health care professionals on whether performing a diagnostic test (Table 2.1) is appropriate. Prediabetes and type 2 diabetes meet criteria for con­ ditions in which early detection via screen­ ing is appropriate. Both conditions are common and impose significant clinical and public health burdens.
