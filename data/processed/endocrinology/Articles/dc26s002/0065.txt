Cystic fibrosis–related diabetes (CFRD) is a common comorbidity in people living with cystic fibrosis that is distinct from type 1 or type 2 diabetes and is often accompanied by pancreatic exocrine in­ sufficiency (144). CFRD occurs in about 20% of adolescents and 50% of adults (145) and is associated with worse nu­ tritional status and increased morbidity and mortality (146).
