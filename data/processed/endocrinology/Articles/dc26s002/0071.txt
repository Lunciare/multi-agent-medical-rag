However, screening people with FPG and/or A1C can identify high-risk individuals who require further assessment and may reduce the number of overall OGTTs required. Few RCTs have reported on the short- and long-term use of glucose-lowering medications in the setting of PTDM (163,167,168). Most studies have re­ ported that transplant individuals with hyperglycemia and PTDM after trans­ plantation have higher rates of rejec­ tion, infection, and rehospitalization (161,163,169).
