As amenorrhea is no longer a diagnostic criterion for AN in DSM-5, the distinction between the two has become somewhat more difﬁcult to make, for the difference is now, besides behavioural observations where these are possible, partly a subjective psychological criterion subject to individuals’ adherence to telling their mind. It has been postulated that part of the statistical rise in AN is due to misdiagnoses of CT as AN since the implementa- tion of DSM-5 criteria.
