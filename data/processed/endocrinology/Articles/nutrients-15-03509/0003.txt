Methods This review article identiﬁed studies via PubMed using keywords and the criterion of associations between anorexia nervosa or underweight and endocrine alterations. In some cases, meta-analytic papers were cited instead of the original texts if they were either from the 20th century or their results had been already widely discussed. The following hormones were reviewed: pituitary hormones and the consequentially released peripheral hormones, as well as hormones regulating appetite. In order to distinguish between symptoms and alterations following restricted food intake as opposed to underweight due to other reasons, a second section concerning studies on constitutional thinness, anorexia nervosa, and their respective endocrine alterations, all found on PubMed, was reviewed.
