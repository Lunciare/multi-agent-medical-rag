Oxytocin Oxytocin (OXT) is a hormone associated with social bonding and reproduction, and plays a role during and after childbirth, as well as in anxiety and depression. OXT is altered in patients with AN, as its peripheral levels during the night are lower in patients with AN than those in the normal population, but higher postprandially or in weight- recovered patients with AN [26–29].
