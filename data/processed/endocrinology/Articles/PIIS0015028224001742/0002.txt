However, MFM fellowships are not shortening, and it could be argued that more active research is now coming from MFM and presented at national meetings than novel research in the ﬁeld of REI. Will current and future REI training prepare us for a role as the ‘‘expert’’? Is the answer a decrease in training, limited to IVF, and a reduction in endo- crine expertise and research acumen? Alongside the shifts in workforce demands, there is a growing emphasis on artiﬁcial intelligence (AI) and its antici- pated impact on our ﬁeld. This includes the ability to recognize patterns and establish correlations with clinical outcomes. Al- gorithms are being reﬁned to provide guidance on various as- pects of controlled ovarian stimulation such as the type of stimulation, dosing, and when to trigger. In the embryology laboratory, AI assistance for fertilization and embryo selection is already available and completely automated IVF is not far away.
