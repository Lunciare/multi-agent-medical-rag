In Milestones 2.0 MK is separated into three sub-competencies: MK1: soning; MK3: Therapeutics (Behavioral, Medications, Technology, Radiopharmaceuticals). New sub‑competency concepts Milestones 2.0 addresses several topics that were not emphasized in Milestones 1.0. SBP now includes a spe- cific sub-competency on population and community health that incorporates the concept of health dispari- ties. A separate SBP sub-competency focuses on patient safety, which was previously combined with medical errors and inter-professional teamwork in Milestones 1.0. Additionally, a new PROF sub-competency centers on the concept of well-being. This sub-competency does not evaluate a fellow’s personal well-being but instead recog- nizes and emphasizes the importance of understanding factors that affect fellow and physician well-being .
