Glucose-Lowering Therapies and Cardiovascular Outcomes In 2008, the U.S. Food and Drug Adminis- tration (FDA) issued guidance for industry to perform cardiovascular outcomes trials for all new medications for the treatment of type 2 diabetes amid concerns of in- creased cardiovascular risk (253). Previ- ously approved diabetes medications were not subject to the guidance. Recently pub- lished cardiovascular outcomes trials have provided additional data on cardiovascular and renal outcomes in people with type 2 diabetes with cardiovascular disease or at high risk for cardiovascular disease.
