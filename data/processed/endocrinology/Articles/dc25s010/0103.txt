In FIDELIO-DKD, ﬁnerenone was compared with placebo for the primary outcome of kidney failure, a sustained decrease of at least 40% in the eGFR from baseline, or death from renal causes in people with type 2 diabetes and CKD (285). A prespeci- ﬁed secondary outcome was death from cardiovascular causes, nonfatal MI, non- fatal stroke, or hospitalization for heart fail- ure, which was reduced by 13% in the ﬁ- nerenone group. The incidence of heart failure hospitalization occurred less in the ﬁnerenone-treated group, and only 7.7% of study participants had a prior history of heart failure. In the FIGARO-DKD trial, ﬁ- nerenone reduced the primary outcome of death from cardiovascular causes, nonfatal MI, nonfatal stroke, or hospitalization for heart failure (HR 0.87 [95% CI 0.76–0.98]; P = 0.03) in people with type 2 diabetes and CKD (248). Only 7.8% of all participants had a prior history of heart failure, and the incidence of hospitalization for heart failure was reduced in the ﬁnerenone-allocated treatment arm (HR 0.71 [95% CI 0.56– 0.90]).
