Individuals at increased ASCVD risk should receive statin, ACE inhibitor, or ARB therapy if the individual has hypertension, and pos- sibly aspirin, unless there are contraindica- tions to a particular drug class. Clear cardiovascular beneﬁt exists for ACE inhibitor or ARB therapy in people with diabetes. The Heart Outcomes Pre- vention Evaluation (HOPE) study random- ized 9,297 individuals aged $55 years with a history of vascular disease or dia- betes plus one other cardiovascular risk factor to either ramipril or placebo.
