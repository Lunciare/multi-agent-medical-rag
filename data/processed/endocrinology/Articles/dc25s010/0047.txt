Statins and PCSK9 Inhibitors The addition of the PCSK9 inhibitors evolocumab and alirocumab to maxi- mum tolerated doses of statin therapy in participants who were at high risk for ASCVD demonstrated an average reduc- tion in LDL cholesterol ranging from 36% to 59% (126,127). No cardiovascu- lar outcome trials have been performed to assess whether PCSK9 inhibitor therapy reduces ASCVD event rates in individuals at low or moderate risk for ASCVD (pri- mary prevention). The evidence on the effect of PSCK9 inhibition on ASCVD out- comes is from studies of treatment with the monoclonal antibodies alirocumab and evolocumab.
