People with diabetes and stage B heart failure who remain asymptomatic but have evidence of structural heart disease, including history of MI, acute coronary syn- drome, or left ventricular ejection fraction (LVEF) #40%, should be treated with ACE inhibitors or ARBs plus b-blockers accord- ing to current treatment guidelines (12). In the landmark Studies of Left Ventricular Dysfunction (SOLVD) study, in which 15% of people had diabetes, treatment with enalapril reduced incident heart failure in people with asymptomatic left ventricular dysfunction by 20% (278).
