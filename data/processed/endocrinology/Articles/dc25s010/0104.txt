Treatment of Symptomatic Heart Failure In general, current guideline-directed medical therapy for a history of MI and symptomatic stage C and D heart failure in people with diabetes is simi- lar to that for people without diabetes. At these advanced stages of heart fail- ure, a collaborative approach with a car- diovascular specialist is recommended. The treatment recommendations are detailed in current 2022 American Heart Association/American College of Cardi- ology/Heart Failure Society of America guidelines for the management of heart failure (12).
