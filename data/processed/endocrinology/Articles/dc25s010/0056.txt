Consideration of initiating or continuing statin therapy during pregnancy should occur with these high-risk individuals. Al- though the evidence is limited, statins did not increase teratogenic effects for indi- viduals with familial hypercholesterolemia (147,148), and a meta-analysis of prava- statin in pregnant individuals showed a re- duction in preeclampsia, premature birth, and neonatal intensive care unit admis- sions (149). There is limited information regarding the use of lipid-lowering thera- pies (other than bile acid sequestrants) during pregnancy.Thus, it is recommended that individuals of childbearing potential use a form of contraception when also us- ing lipid-lowering medications with un- known risks, limited evidence on safety, or known risks during pregnancy regardless of intention to become pregnant, as many pregnancies are unplanned, and precon- ception counseling should be part of the routine care of individuals with diabetes who have childbearing potential.
