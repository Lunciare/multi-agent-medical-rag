Screening for Asymptomatic Peripheral Artery Disease in People With Diabetes The risk for PAD in people with diabetes is higher than that in people without diabe- tes (230–232). In the PAD Awareness, Risk, and Treatment: New Resources for Survival (PARTNERS) program, 30% of people aged 50–69 years with a history of cigarette smoking or diabetes, or aged $70 years regardless of risk factors, had PAD (233).
