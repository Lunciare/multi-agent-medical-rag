Finerenone in People With Type 2 Diabetes and Chronic Kidney Disease As discussed in detail in Section 11, “Chronic Kidney Disease and Risk Mana- gement,” people with diabetes are at an increased risk for CKD, which increases cardiovascular risk (308). Finerenone, a selective nonsteroidal MRA, has been shown in the FIDELIO-DKD trial to im- prove CKD outcomes in people with type 2 diabetes with stage 3 or 4 CKD and severe albuminuria (281). In the FIGARO-DKD trial, 7,437 individuals with UACR 30–300 mg/g and eGFR 25–90 mL/ min/1.73 m2 or UACR 300–5,000 and eGFR $60 mL/min/1.73 m2 on maximum dose of renin-angiotensin system blockade were randomized to receive ﬁnerenone or placebo (240).
