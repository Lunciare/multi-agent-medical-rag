In the Dapagliﬂozin and Prevention of Adverse Outcomes in Chronic Kidney Disease (DAPA-CKD) trial (250), 4,304 individuals with CKD (UACR 200– 5,000 mg/g and eGFR 25–75 mL/min/ 1.73 m2), with or without diabetes, were randomized to dapagliﬂozin 10 mg daily or placebo.
