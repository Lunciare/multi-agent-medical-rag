Meta-analyses of Trials To clarify optimal blood pressure targets in people with diabetes, multiple meta- analyses have been performed. One of the largest meta-analyses included 73,913 people with diabetes. Compared with a less tight blood pressure control, allocation to a tighter blood pressure control signiﬁcantly reduced the risk of stroke by 31% but did not reduce the risk of MI (37). Another meta-analysis of 19 trials that included 44,989 individ- uals showed that a mean blood pres- sure of 133/76 mmHg is associated with a 14% risk reduction for major cardio- vascular events compared with a mean blood pressure of 140/81 mmHg (31).
