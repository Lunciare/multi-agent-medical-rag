Initiating Statin Therapy Based on Risk People with type 2 diabetes have an in- creased prevalence of lipid abnormali- ties, contributing to their high risk of ASCVD. Multiple clinical trials have dem- onstrated the beneﬁcial effects of statin therapy on ASCVD outcomes in subjects with and without CHD (93,94). Sub- group analyses of people with diabetes in larger trials (95–99) and trials in people with diabetes (100,101) showed signiﬁcant primary and secondary preven- tion of ASCVD events and CHD death in people with diabetes.
