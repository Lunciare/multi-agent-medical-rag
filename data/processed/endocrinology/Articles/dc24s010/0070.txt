In addition, there was an increase in serious adverse events associated with the gastrointestinal system, musculoskele- tal system, skin, and, unexpectedly, infec- tion and bleeding. Therefore, combination therapy with a statin and niacin is not recommended, given the lack of efﬁcacy on major ASCVD outcomes and increased side effects.
