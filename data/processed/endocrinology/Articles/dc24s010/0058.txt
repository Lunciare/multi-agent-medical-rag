There were 160 and 307 individuals in the GAUSS 1 and 2 trials, re- spectively, who were randomized to vari- ous doses of evolocumab plus ezetimibe 10 mg or ezetimibe 10 mg plus placebo injection for 12 weeks. Reductions in LDL cholesterol ranged from 41% to 63% (depending on the dose) for evolocumab/ ezetimibe compared with 15% to 18% for ezetimibe/placebo.
