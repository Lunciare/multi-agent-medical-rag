SGLT Inhibitors. As reviewed in detail in the following section, SGLT2 inhibitors constitute a key treatment approach to reduce cardiovascular disease and heart failure outcomes in people with diabe- tes. People with type 2 diabetes and increased cardiovascular risk or estab- lished cardiovascular disease should be treated with an SGLT2 inhibitor to pre- vent the development of incident heart failure.
