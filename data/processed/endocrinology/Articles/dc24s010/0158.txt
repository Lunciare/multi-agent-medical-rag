Adoption of these agents should be reasonably straightforward in people with established cardiovascular or kid- ney disease who are later diagnosed with diabetes, as the cardioprotective agents can be used from the outset of di- abetes management. On the other hand, incorporation of SGLT2 inhibitor or GLP-1 receptor agonist therapy in the care of individuals with more long-standing dia- betes may be more challenging, particu- larly if individuals are using an already complex glucose-lowering plan. In such individuals, SGLT2 inhibitor or GLP-1 receptor agonist therapy may need to replace some or all of their existing medi- cations to minimize risks of hypoglycemia and adverse side effects and potentially to minimize medication costs.
