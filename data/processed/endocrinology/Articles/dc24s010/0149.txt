Sotagliflozin Sotagliﬂozin, a dual SGLT1 and SGLT2 in- hibitor, was recently approved by the FDA in the U.S. to reduce the risk of cardiovascular death, hospitalization for heart failure, and urgent heart failure in people with heart failure or type 2 diabetes, CKD, and other cardiovascular risk factors. This drug is distinct from other SGLT inhibitors, as it lowers glu- cose via delayed glucose absorption in the gut via inhibition of the cotrans- porter SGLT1 in addition to increasing urinary glucose excretion; however, it is not currently approved by the FDA for glycemic management of type 1 or type 2 diabetes. Sotagliﬂozin was evalu- ated in the SCORED trial (281) and SOLOIST-WHF trial (297).
