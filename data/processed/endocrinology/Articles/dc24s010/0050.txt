Combination Therapy for LDL Cholesterol Lowering Statins and Ezetimibe The IMProved Reduction of Outcomes: Vy- torin Efﬁcacy International Trial (IMPROVE- IT) was a randomized controlled trial in 18,144 individuals comparing the addition of ezetimibe to simvastatin therapy ver- sus simvastatin alone (112). Individuals were $50 years of age, had experienced a recent acute coronary syndrome, and were treated for an average of 6 years. Overall, the addition of ezetimibe led to a 6.4% relative beneﬁt and a 2% absolute reduction in major adverse cardiovascular events (atherosclerotic cardiovascular events), with the degree of beneﬁt be- ing directly proportional to the change in LDL cholesterol, which was 70 mg/dL (1.8 mmol/L) in the statin group on av- erage and 54 mg/dL (1.4 mmol/L) in the combination group (112).
