Two large outcomes trials of the SGLT2 inhibitor canagliﬂozin have been conducted that separately assessed 1) the cardiovascular effects of treatment in individuals at high risk for major ad- verse cardiovascular events (12) and 2) the impact of canagliﬂozin therapy on cardiorenal outcomes in people with diabetes-related CKD (247). First, the CANVAS Program integrated data from two trials. The CANVAS trial that started in 2009 was partially unblinded prior to completion because of the need to ﬁle interim cardiovascular outcomes data for regulatory approval of the drug (248). Thereafter, the postapproval CANVAS- Renal (CANVAS-R) trial was started in 2014. Combining both trials, 10,142 participants with type 2 diabetes were randomized to canagliﬂozin or placebo and were followed for an average of 3.6 years.
