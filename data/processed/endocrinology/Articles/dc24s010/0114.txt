In the randomized controlled Viborg Vascular (VIVA) trial, 50,156 participants were ran- domized to combined vascular screening for abdominal aortic aneurysm, PAD, and hypertension or to no screening. Vascular screening was associated with increased pharmacologic therapy (antiplatelet, lipid- lowering, and antihypertensive therapy), reduced in-hospital time for PAD and CAD, and reduced mortality (235). There- fore, the committee recommends screen- ing for asymptomatic PAD using ankle- brachial index in people with diabetes at high risk for PAD, including any of the fol- lowing: age $50 years, diabetes with du- ration $10 years, comorbid microvascular cations, or any end-organ damage from diabetes. Therefore, the committee rec- ommends screening people with diabe- tes and high risk for PAD, including age $50 years, diabetes with duration $10 years, microvascular disease, clini- cal evidence of foot complications, or any end-organ damage from diabetes.
