Results from a moderate-sized trial of another GLP-1 receptor agonist, sema- glutide, were consistent with the LEADER trial (257). Semaglutide is a once-weekly GLP-1 receptor agonist approved by the FDA for the treatment of type 2 diabetes. The Trial to Evaluate Cardiovascular and Other Long-term Outcomes With Semaglu- tide in Subjects With Type 2 Diabetes (SUSTAIN-6) was the initial randomized trial powered to test noninferiority of sem- aglutide for the purpose of regulatory ap- proval (257). In this study, 3,297 people with type 2 diabetes were randomized to receive once-weekly semaglutide (0.5 mg or 1.0 mg) or placebo for 2 years. The pri- mary outcome (the ﬁrst occurrence of car- diovascular death, nonfatal MI, or nonfatal stroke) occurred in 108 individuals (6.6%) in the semaglutide group vs. 146 individu- als (8.9%) in the placebo group (HR 0.74 [95% CI 0.58–0.95]; P < 0.001).
