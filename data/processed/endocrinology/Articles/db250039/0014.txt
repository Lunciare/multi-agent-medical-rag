NIDDK required that translational cores provide consul- tative services to researchers, an enrichment program to promote scientific education and exchanges among in- vestigators, and a P&F program to provide seed funding, primarily for ESIs. Optional opportunities to support re- gional and national cores, and partnerships with teaching- intensive institutions with outreach to various patient communities, were also included. The initial CDTR struc- ture set the stage for two subsequent renewals that have further broadened the scientific reach and impact of the program. Since 2011, the CDTR program has continued to grow in scientific prominence and impact.
