MMPC-Live offers an extensive catalog of tests ( shared/orderTestSelection.aspx) with >150 options, fo- cused primarily on glucose metabolism and homeostasis, lipid and protein metabolism, energy balance, body composition, eating and other behaviors, physical activ- the microbiome. Resources for experiments include ex- tensive imaging technology and gnotobiotic facilities. Because many of the tests are relatively noninvasive, the same mouse can often be studied in several ways, and tissues can be harvested for further experiments. MMPC-Live also sponsors training in its rigorous, stan- dard methodologies. Two formal annual weeklong courses are held where hundreds of students have been trained in “Glucose Clamping the Conscious Mouse: a Laboratory Course,” now in its 21st year, and in “Isotope Tracers in Metabolic Research: Principles and Practice of Kinetic Ana- lysis,” in its 16th year ( aspx). Webinars are available on the technology for use of isotope tracers and energy expenditure.
