The DEM centers also foster partnerships with external organizations. For example, in 2023, the American Diabetes Association and NIDDK forged a partnership to expand the impact of the CDTR P&F program. As of November 2024, the American Diabetes Association has funded 23 pilot proj- ects led by CDTR ESIs totaling $1.2 million to support high- risk, high-reward science and ESI career development and progression.
