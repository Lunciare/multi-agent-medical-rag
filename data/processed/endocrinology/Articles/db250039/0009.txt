The DRCs’ P&F program has also had a long-lasting im- pact in promoting the careers of talented early-stage in- new to the field of diabetes and related areas. Several of the DRCs have expanded P&F programs providing outreach, support, and mentoring to non-DRC-affiliated regional hos- pitals and universities. The majority of DRCs report that 70%–90% of their P&F awardees are successful in obtaining further research funding and staying within the field of dia- betes/endocrinology/metabolic diseases. The requirement that a DRC allocate 25% of the awarded direct costs to the P&F program reflects NIDDK’s commitment to the success of the P&F program. P&F awardees from each DRC are highlighted yearly (found at pilotfeasibility).
