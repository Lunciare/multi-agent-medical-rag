Supported Research Centers Focused on Diabetes, Endocrinology, and Metabolic Diseases This year marks the 75th anniversary of the National Insti- tute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases (NIDDK) of the National Institutes of Health. NIDDK’s long history of research and innovation includes support of four types of collaborative research centers focused on diabe- tes, endocrinology, and metabolic diseases. The Diabetes Research Centers promote basic and clinical diabetes re- search, while the Centers for Diabetes Translation Re- search conduct diabetes research across the translation science spectrum.
