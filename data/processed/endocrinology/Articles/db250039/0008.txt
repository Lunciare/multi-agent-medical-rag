The biomedical research cores of the DRCs frequently leveraged institutional research cores by supporting use by the center members, as well as by pro- viding support to the cores to develop and disseminate technology. Miniaturized physiological and metabolic technology was starting to be incorporated into DRC cores as genetic mouse models for diabetes were emerg- ing, and the community felt that wider access to these methodologies was important for progress.
