With longer life spans, diseases of the NIDDK mission are becoming increasingly more prevalent in people with CF, including increased incidence of CF- related diabetes (CFRD); 40% of adults with CF will develop diabetes (12). A major question is what drugs beyond insulin, the current standard treatment, can be proposed to have efficacy in people with CFRD, including drugs in- dicated for type 2 diabetes.
