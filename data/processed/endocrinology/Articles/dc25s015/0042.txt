Pregnant people with type 1 diabetes should be advised to obtain ketone test strips and receive education on DKA prevention and detection. DKA carries a high risk of stillbirth. Those in DKA who are unable to eat often require 10% dextrose with an insulin drip to adequately meet the higher carbohydrate demands of the placenta and fetus in the third trimester to resolve their ketosis. Retinopathy is a special concern in preg- nancy.The necessary rapid implementation of euglycemia in the setting of retinopathy is associated with worsening of retinopathy (126). Meta-analyses have also demon- strated a high risk of new-onset retinopa- thy and progression of existing retinopathy in pregnant individuals with type 1 or type 2 diabetes (32,127). Therefore, it is recom- mended that individuals with preexisting diabetes have dilated eye examinations be- fore pregnancy, in each trimester of pregnancy, and for 1 year postpartum as indicated by the degree of retinopa- thy and as recommended by the eye care health care professional. Recommended weight gain during preg- nancy for people with overweight status is 15–25 lbs (6.8–11.3 kg)
