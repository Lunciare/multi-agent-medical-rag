A1C in Pregnancy In studies of individuals without preexisting diabetes, increasing A1C levels within the normal range are associated with adverse outcomes (43). In the Hyperglycemia and Adverse Pregnancy Outcome (HAPO) study, increasing levels of glycemia were also as- sociated with worsening outcomes (23).
