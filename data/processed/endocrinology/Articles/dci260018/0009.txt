Type 2 Diabetes and Diabetes Technology Until recently, individuals with type 2 dia­ betes had fewer diabetes technology op­ tions, but access to CGM technology is currently increasing, particularly for those who are insulin treated. A key driver for widening access to diabetes technology among people with type 2 diabetes is the growing evidence of benefit in this large patient group. Indications for CGM have been expanding, from people with type 2 diabetes who require multiple daily insu­ lin injections with frequent blood glucose monitoring (22) to those requiring less complex regimens, including basal insulin (23), and even those not on insulin ther­ apy (1,24–27).
