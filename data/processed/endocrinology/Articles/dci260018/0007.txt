Suggestions for Action Health care professionals should support people with diabetes to select optimal diabetes technology by: • Evaluating their willingness to engage in the selection of the technology (e.g., determine what their treatment goals are) • Providing access to or recom­ mending informative, accurate, and user-friendly videos and tutorials and training sessions on websites and social media • Raising awareness of the range of device options available, for example, by using a virtual showroom on social media • Facilitating access to health care professionals who can an­ swer questions and guide in­ dividual decision-making, for cians, diabetes educators, phar­ professionals • Giving people with diabetes the opportunity to try out advanced technology devices before committing to them in the long-term However, challenges remain, as ad­ vanced technologies have high acquisition costs (even if cost-effective) and are not accessible to all with type 1 diabetes, even in higher-income countries (16). This high­ lights the need for more affordable op­ tions and far-sighted decisions by health care systems (irrespective of their own specific funding models).
