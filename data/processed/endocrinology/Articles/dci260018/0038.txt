While the ultimate goal is to provide access to the most advanced and current diabetes technologies, it is critical to understand that there are settings, particularly in low- and middle-income countries, where individuals can only afford/be provided with test strips and a glucose meter. In these settings, it is not feasible to test blood glucose levels as often as would be optimal.
