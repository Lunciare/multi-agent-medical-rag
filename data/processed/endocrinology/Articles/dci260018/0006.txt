TYPES OF DIABETES AND DIABETES TECHNOLOGY Type 1 Diabetes and Diabetes Technology Diabetes technology has transformed the management of type 1 diabetes over the last 50 years, starting with the availability of personal blood glucose meters in the 1970s, followed by insulin pumps (continu­ ous subcutaneous insulin infusion or CSII) since the 1990s (13), continuous glucose monitoring (CGM) systems since the 2000s (14), and automated insulin delivery (AID) systems since 2016 (15), which use algo­ rithms to automatically adjust basal insulin delivery based on CGM measurements. These diabetes technologies have allowed people with diabetes who have access to appropriate devices to make data-driven decisions to improve glucose management and reduce the daily burden of diabetes.
