Table 3—Continued Factor Consideration Solution Caregiver management If caregivers are involved in patient care, they should have a role in choosing the appropriate diabetes technology Caregivers should be trained and have clear protocols for diabetes technology use. This is particularly important when caregivers change and when the care of an individual is provided by multiple caregivers. All caregivers should be trained in the use of each type of diabetes technology employed and understand how to troubleshoot and who to call if difficulties arise Psychosocial concerns/diabetes distress People with diabetes often have diabetes distress, anxiety, depression, and other psychosocial concerns, which can impact their use of diabetes technology and diabetes distress, at least annually and more often as needed. Ensure access to a behavioral health individual/team for assessment and treatment.
