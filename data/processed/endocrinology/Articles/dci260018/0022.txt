Islet Cell/Pancreas Transplantation β-Cell replacement (pancreatic islet/ pancreas transplantation) is indicated for people with type 1 diabetes with signifi­ cant glycemic variability and a higher risk of severe hypoglycemia despite optimized management, or who are unable to suc­ cessfully engage with diabetes technology (2,60). These procedures can provide a pe­ riod of insulin independence of varying, sometimes limited, duration. Following β-cell replacement failure, the use of AID may restore glucose management, but further studies are needed to es­ tablish whether AID may represent an alternative to retransplantation (61).
