Most of these drugs either reduce appetite or enhance satiety, except orlistat, which works by decreasing fat absorption . In patients with obesity with T2DM, glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonists (GLP-1 RA), like semaglutide and dulaglutide, help control hyperglycemia and reduce weight. GLP-1 RAs act on alpha cells of the pancreas to inhibit glucagon secretion, and have extra-pancreatic eﬀects, such as increased satiety and delayed gastric emptying .
