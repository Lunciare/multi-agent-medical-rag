Endocrine Causes of Obesity Obesity can be the result of structural lesions at the hypothalamic–pituitary region or a consequence of inherited and genetic syndromes. These abnormalities cause hypothalamic obesity. Alternatively, functional neuroendocrine hormone abnormalities can cause obesity .
