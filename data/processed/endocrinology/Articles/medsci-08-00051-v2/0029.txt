Impact of Bariatric Surgery on Islet Function, Insulin Secretion, and Glucose Control All bariatric surgery procedures modify the gastrointestinal tract in diﬀerent ways, but all procedures improve the status of T2DM. This reduction in hyperglycemia that is achieved post-surgery is superior to that achieved through medical and lifestyle management . Of the various bariatric procedures (Figure 4), studies show that biliopancreatic diversion (BPD) gives the most beneﬁts in T2DM remission, sustained weight loss, and improved lipid proﬁle. However, this is a complex procedure and has more post-operative complications than other procedures, and hence, is less used. Of the commonly used procedures, Roux-en-Y gastric bypass (RYGB) and sleeve gastrectomy (SG) provide similar beneﬁts for T2DM remission, metabolic proﬁle, and weight loss . The glucose control post-BPD, -RYGB, and -SG is independent of weight loss, but improves signiﬁcantly with loss of weight.
