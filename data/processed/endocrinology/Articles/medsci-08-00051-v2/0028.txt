Endocrine Impact of Bariatric Surgery The anatomic changes post-bariatric surgery result in reduced appetite, reduced food consumption, and change in gut hormone secretion. Together, these changes profoundly improve the overall systemic metabolic proﬁle . Apart from dramatic weight loss beneﬁts, bariatric surgery signiﬁcantly improves insulin resistance and leads to resolution of type 2 diabetes in many cases .
