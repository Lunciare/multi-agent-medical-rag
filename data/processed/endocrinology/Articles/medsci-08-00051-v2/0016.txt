Endocrine Impact of Obesity Management 5.1. Endocrine Impact of Non-Pharmacological Management of Obesity Non-pharmacological management of obesity includes lifestyle changes, such as diet and exercise. Stress in everyday life is known to be associated with endocrine responses that contribute to obesity. Hence, stress management is an important factor in the endocrine management of obesity. 5.1.1. Potential Endocrine/Metabolic Impact of Exercise Exercise impacts all the endocrine glands. During exercise, the pituitary releases growth hormones, the thyroid releases T4, and the adrenals release cortisol and aldosterone. All these hormones increase the body’s capacity to exercise, stay alert, focus on the high-intensity task, provide energy for the exercise, and prevent dehydration (especially aldosterone).
