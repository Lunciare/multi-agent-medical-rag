THR doses were reduced signiﬁcantly in the rest of the patients (p < 0.02) . Rudnicki et al. (2018) reported similar results in 90 patients treated with either LSG or RYGB. In their observational study of 1581 patients with hypothyroidism and obesity mainly treated with LSG and RYGB, Matute et al. The mean TSH measurement (p = 0.153) and the mean thyroxine measurement (p = 0.021) improved after one year post-surgery . Overt hypothyroidism (elevated TSH and decreased FT4) in patients with obesity is treated irrespective of the antibodies’ status. However, thyroid hormones should not be used for treating obesity if the thyroid function is normal. Hyperthyrotropinaemia (elevated TSH and normal FT4) in obesity should not be treated with the sole aim of reducing body weight.
