Impact of Bariatric Surgery on Adrenal Function Adrenal insuﬃciency is a common complication after bariatric surgery. Therefore, clinicians should be very vigilant about symptoms and signs of adrenal insuﬃciency after bariatric surgery, such as anxiety, sweating, transient loss of consciousness, hypoglycaemia, and conﬂicting symptoms of profound weight loss or weight regain . However, in patients who have rapid weight loss after bariatric surgery, these symptoms are often masked. The possible cause of post-bariatric surgery adrenal insuﬃciency could be malabsorption of factors that help in steroid biosynthesis such as bile, and therefore cholesterol levels, selenium, and other trace elements, and vitamin B5 and other vitamins.
