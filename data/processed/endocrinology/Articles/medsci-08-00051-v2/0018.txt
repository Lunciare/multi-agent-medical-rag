More recently, emerging literature has also highlighted the role of skeletal muscle as a secretory organ and its role in the regulation of body fat metabolism. Myokines induced by physical activity and muscle contraction have paracrine eﬀects on the muscle. They promote myogeneisis and muscle hypertrophy in the individual. Beyond the muscle, they also assist in promoting glucose uptake, lipolysis, β-oxidation of fat, angiogenesis, and revascularization. Muscular irisin, β-aminoisobutyric acid (BAIBA), and ﬁbroblast growth factor 21 (FGF21) may also lead to conversion of white adipose tissue to brown adipose tissue.
