Nutrient- stimulated hormone-based therapeutics can be considered as adjuvants after met- abolic surgery to augment initial weight loss either shortly after surgery or when weight loss has plateaued (157). Studies have also shown that GLP-1 RAs can effec- tively treat weight regain after metabolic surgery and therefore could be considered as an alternative to revisional surgery (158). Long-term outcomes, however, are lacking in terms of durability of weight loss, effect on weight regain when medica- tions are stopped, and long-term side effects with use and after discontinuation (159).
