Most attention over the last 5 years has focussed on sele- nium, especially as a possible therapeutic agent, the gut microbiota and drugs. Data on both the association of sele- nium intake with markers of autoimmune thyroiditis and the effects of selenium supplementation on such mark- ers have been conflicting but despite this, a survey of 881 members of the Italian Associazione Medici Endocrinologi found that almost 80% prescribed selenium for HT associ- ated with euthyroidism, despite around half of the respond- ents not actually believing that the evidence supported its use .
