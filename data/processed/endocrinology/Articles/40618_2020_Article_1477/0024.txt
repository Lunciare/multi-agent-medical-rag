Evidence quality was too low to perform a meta-anal- ysis. Another intriguing association is the finding of HT in 16% of a series of 1239 patients with allergic rhinitis whereas Graves’ disease was not found . Allergic rhinitis depends on a Th2 response while HT has been assumed to reflect more of a Th1 response, so these results add fur- ther weight to the increasing understanding that a Th1/Th2 dichotomy is far less clear in man than in the mouse. A role for prolactin in modulating autoimmune responses has been suggested previously by animal and human studies, although this is not always acknowledged. A prospective study of prolactinoma patients has found a three-fold excess of HT compared to controls, in line with the results of a recent retrospective survey .
