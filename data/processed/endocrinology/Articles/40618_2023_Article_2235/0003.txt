Indeed, unlike other medical domains, endocrinology is not related to a sin- gle organ structure but is a complicated biological system of hormones and metabolites, where hormones function within an elaborate network of local and remote actions involving receptors, signaling pathways, and intricate feedback mecha- nisms . These complex and interconnected systems are often beyond the comprehension and reasoning abilities of the human brain. The purpose of this study is to explore the diverse applications of AI in the field of endocrinology and metabolism, with a focus on its potential to enhance screening, disease diagnosis, risk prediction, prognosis, and medical research.
