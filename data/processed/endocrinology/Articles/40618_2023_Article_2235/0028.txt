Facilitation of the diagnostic workflow The facilitation of the diagnostic workflow is a crucial ele- ment in modern healthcare, as timely and accurate diagnoses play a vital role in effective treatment and patient care. AI systems contribute to streamlining the diagnostic process by analyzing large volumes of patient data, thereby assisting the decision-making process and reducing diagnostic uncer- tainty. Peng et al. developed a DL model named ThyNet to aid in the diagnosis and management of thyroid nodules . ThyNet utilized ultrasound image sets to differenti- ate between malignant and benign tumors, enabling a strat- egy for clinical decision-making. The results of this study showed advantages in improving the diagnostic accuracy of radiologists on thyroid nodule differentiation and could potentially decrease the number of unnecessary fine needle aspirations.
