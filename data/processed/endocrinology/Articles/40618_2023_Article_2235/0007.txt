All conducted research was restricted to studies published from 2017 to the present, with a focus on articles available in full-text English. In this procedure, we also con- sidered grey-literature sources, notably integrating selected with the aim of elucidating the characteristics of specific diseases and highlighting the innovative aspects of “pre- emptive medicine”.
