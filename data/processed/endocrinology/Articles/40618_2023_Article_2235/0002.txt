One notable example is the field of artificial intelligence (AI). The healthcare sector is currently undergoing an unprecedented transforma- tion due to AI, as it possesses the potential to enhance exist- ing clinical practices. The innovative aspects introduced by AI find ideal applications within the field of endocrinology, given its complex and interconnected nature.
