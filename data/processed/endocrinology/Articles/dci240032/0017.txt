Risk factors for DKA in individuals with T2D treated with SGLT2 inhibitors include very- low-carbohydrate diets and prolonged fast- ing, dehydration, excessive alcohol intake, and the presence of autoimmunity, in addi- tion to typical precipitating factors (94,95). Notably, in one series, 35% of people treated with SGLT2 inhibitors presenting with DKA had glucose levels <200 mg/dL (11.1 mmol/L) (96), and in another series, 71% of people treated with SGLT2 inhibi- tors presenting with DKA had glucose lev- els #250 mg/dL (13.9 mmol/L) (97).
