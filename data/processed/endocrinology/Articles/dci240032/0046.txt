Severe hypokale- mia #2.5 mmol/L during treatment of DKA and HHS has been reported to be associ- ated with a threefold increase in mortality (10). To avoid hypokalemia, we recom- mend measuring serum potassium 2 h after starting insulin administration and every 4 h thereafter until the resolution of DKA. Use of too low or too high doses of potas- sium compared with the recommended potassium replacement protocols in the management of DKA has been associated with longer hospital stays (148).
