Hypokalemia (24)  Hypokalemia is a common complication owing to intracellular shift of potassium following insulin treatment.  Hypokalemia (<3.5 mmol/L) occurred in 55% of DKA and 51% of HHS patients.  Severe hypokalemia <2.5 mmol/L occurs in 16% of people with DKA and 9% of people with HHS.  Severe hypokalemia #2.5 mmol/L was associated with increased inpatient mortality (adjusted OR 4.9; 95% CI 1.3–18.8).  Potassium should be carefully monitored every 4 h during treatment.  Potassium replacement should be added to ﬂuid resuscitation.
