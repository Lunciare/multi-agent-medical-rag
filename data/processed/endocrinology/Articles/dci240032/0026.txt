Diagnostic Criteria for HHS HHS is a state of signiﬁcant hyperglyce- mia and hyperosmolality in the absence of severe ketonemia and metabolic aci- dosis. The diagnosis of HHS should be based on the four criteria presented in Fig. 2B. All four components must be present to make the diagnosis (12,117). Clinical overlap between DKA and HHS has been reported in more than one-third of people with hyperglycemic crises (50).
