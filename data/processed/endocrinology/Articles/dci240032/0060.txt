In individuals with multiple episodes of DKA, intensiﬁed and multidisciplinary approaches such as psychological interven- tions, peer support, individual coaching, and behavioral family systems therapy have been reported to reduce DKA risk (156). In addition, the use of telemedicine and digital communication methods, as well as the provision of a 24-h emergency call service that offers medical advice for symptoms of DKA or when blood glucose or ketone concentrations are high, may re- duce the risk of DKA admissions (156).
