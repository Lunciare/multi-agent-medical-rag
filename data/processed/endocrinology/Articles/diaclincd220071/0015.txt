Despite vast differences in the number of people with type 1 diabetes seen at each given center (ranging from <125 to $250 per month), there were no signiﬁcant dif- ferences in overall QI culture according to center size (P = 0.537) (Figure 5). Similarly, there were no overall differences in QI culture according to the percentage of publicly insured patients with type 1 diabetes (P = 0.17) (Figure 6). However, QI successes were higher in centers where $50% of patients with type 1 diabetes were publicly insured (P = 0.02). Discussion The T1DX-QI LHS has grown tremendously over a short period of time, while simultaneously demonstrating many tangible improvements in care resulting from its QI efforts (10,13,15). Successful LHSs have demon- strated improvement in a number of health outcomes ative complications and improved remission rates among cancer patients (3). Understanding QI culture is crucial for an LHS to meet member centers where they are, build on existing capacity, and strive toward collec- tive improvement.
