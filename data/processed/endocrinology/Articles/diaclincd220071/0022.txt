The ongoing success of the collabora- tive as it grows and continues to expand membership to centers with less QI capacity speaks to the importance of supporting LHSs as drivers to improve clinical care and outcomes for people with type 1 diabetes. To improve care for all people with type 1 diabetes, it is imperative to increase the involvement of adult care centers, small- to medium-sized centers, and centers serving under- served, publicly insured people with type 1 diabetes. Data-sharing, benchmarking, and peer collaboration will drive the collection of data that can lead to policy change.
