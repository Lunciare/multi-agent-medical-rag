Of note, there has not been a direct comparison of MRAs and SGLT2 inhibi- tors. At this time, they can be used inter- changeably or together for the goal of slowing progression of CKD and providing cardiovascular protection. There have also been no studies directly comparing MRAs, care professionals should use their best judgement as to which medication to pre- scribe initially and in combination. As noted, all of these studies included participants taking either an ACE inhibitor or an ARB, often at maximally tolerated doses.
