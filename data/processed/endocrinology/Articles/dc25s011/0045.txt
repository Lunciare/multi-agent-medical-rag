The mean age of participants was 64.1 years (31% were female), and the median follow-up duration was 3.4 years. The median A1C was 7.7%, the mean sys- tolic blood pressure was 136 mmHg, and the mean GFR was 67.8 mL/min/1.73 m2. People with heart failure with a reduced ejection fraction and uncontrolled hyper- tension were excluded. The primary composite outcome was cardiovascular death, myocardial infarc- tion, stroke, and hospitalization for heart failure. The ﬁnerenone group showed a 13% reduction in the primary end point compared with the placebo group (12.4% vs. 14.2%; HR 0.87 [95% CI 0.76–0.98]; P = 0.03).
