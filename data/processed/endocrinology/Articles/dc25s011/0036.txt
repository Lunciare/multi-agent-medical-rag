The most recently published clinical trial was EMPA-KIDNEY (Study of Heart and Kidney Protection with Empagliﬂozin) (115). This study enrolled participants with kidney disease with an eGFR of at least 20 but less than 45 mL/min/1.73 m2 or who had an eGFR of at least 45 but less than 90 mL/min/1.73 m2 with a UACR of at least 200 mg/g creatinine.
