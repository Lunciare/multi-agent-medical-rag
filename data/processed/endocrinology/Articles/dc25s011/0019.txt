Compared with higher levels of dietary protein intake, this level slowed GFR de- cline with evidence of a greater effect over time. Higher levels of dietary protein intake (>20% of daily calories from protein or >1.3 g/kg/day) have been associated with increased albuminuria, more rapid kidney function loss, and CVD mortality and therefore should be avoided. Reduc- ing the amount of dietary protein below the recommended daily allowance of 0.8 g/kg/day is not recommended because it does not alter blood glucose levels, car- diovascular risk measures, or the course of GFR decline (46). Some organizations rec- ommend a lower protein intake (0.6–0.8 g/ kg/day).
