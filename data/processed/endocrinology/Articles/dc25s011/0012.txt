ACUTE KIDNEY INJURY Acute kidney injury (AKI) is diagnosed by a sustained increase in serum creati- nine over a short period of time, which is also reﬂected as a rapid decrease in eGFR (26,27). People with diabetes are at higher riskof AKI than those without diabe- tes (28).
