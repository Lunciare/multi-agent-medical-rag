Prevention The only proven primary prevention inter- ventions for CKD in people with diabetes are blood glucose (A1C goal of 7%) and blood pressure management. There is no evidence that renin-angiotensin-aldosterone system inhibitors or any other interventions prevent the development of CKD in the ab- sence of hypertension or albuminuria.Thus, the American Diabetes Association does not recommend routine use of these medica- tions solely for the purpose of prevention of the development of CKD.
