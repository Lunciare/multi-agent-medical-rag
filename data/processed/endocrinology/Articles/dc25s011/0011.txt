STAGING OF CHRONIC KIDNEY DISEASE Stage G1 and stage G2 CKD are deﬁned by evidence of high albuminuria with eGFR $60 mL/min/1.73 m2, and stages G3-G5 CKD are deﬁned by progressively lower ranges of eGFR (23) (Fig. 11.1). At any eGFR, the degree of albuminuria is associated with risk of cardiovascular dis- ease (CVD), CKD progression, and mortal- ity (6). Therefore, there is an additional subclassiﬁcation by level of urine albumin (Fig. 11.1).
