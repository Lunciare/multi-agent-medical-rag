Chronotype preference has been linked with many chronic diseases, including type 2 diabe- tes. For example, for those with a prefer- ence for evenings (i.e., going to bed late and getting up late), there was a 2.5-fold higher odds ratio for type 2 diabetes than for those with a preference for mornings (i.e., going to bed early and getting up early), independent of sleep duration and sleep sufﬁciency (37).
