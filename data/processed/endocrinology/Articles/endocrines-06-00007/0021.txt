Alex Vermeulen, the Administrator In terms of his personality, Alex described himself as rather reserved and believed that, like many introverted people, he could be perceived as aggressive. He felt he did not easily make social connections and often held back when he disliked something. This could lead to frustration and grievances accumulating, and when encountering the proverbial last straw, he might react too disproportionately.
