His final paper on the clinical and therapeutic implications of the decline in androgen levels in elderly men appeared in print in the October 2005 issue of Endocrine Reviews and has nearly 800 citations in Web of Science so far . However, when these activities came to an end, and when in 2012 an article in a newsletter from the Ghent Museum of Fine Arts (MSK) detailed an important gift of five previously unknown works of art by Léon Spilliaert (1881–1946) [ mskgent.be/collectie/2012-z (accessed on 18 September 2024)] (Figure 6), a prominent Symbolist Flemish painter, I suspected that Alex had arrived at a turning point in his life. This was not wholly unexpected given that his wife Anita ‘Nini’ (née Blanckaert) in the meantime had passed away too, and their marriage had remained childless.
