Several among them produced works of art that today frequently change ownership for multiple hundreds of thousands of euros or dollars at auctions worldwide. Laethem-Saint- Martin’s somewhat secluded location—there is neither a train station nor any freeways crossing its territory—despite being relatively close to the city of Ghent, Belgium’s third largest city, has made it a favorite for many of the country’s most affluent families and notable citizens to establish residence. The Vermeulen family consisted of Alex’s father Maurice Vermeulen (1899–1990), originally hailing from Ghent, his wife Maria née Coussement (1900–1989), hailing from the West Flemish village of Oyghem [Dutch: Ooigem], and their only son Alex with his two siblings Monique and Nicole.
