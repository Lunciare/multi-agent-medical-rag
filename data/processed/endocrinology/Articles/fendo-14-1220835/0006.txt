Clinical and experimental studies have conﬁrmed that Chinese herbal medicine can improve the complex processes of these disorders, including metabolic abnormalities, neuroinﬂammation, and oxidative damage (Deng et al.). TCM has been studied clinically and experimentally in the treatment of endocrine-related disorders, with some promising ﬁndings, but there are still numerous shortcomings. most of the extracted herbal components cannot verify the purity of the the compounds; most herbal studies are based on intragastric administration, and too little is known about drug metabolism; the paucity of systematic studies on herbal medicines, and the unclear nature of the cellular and molecular mechanisms involved; animal experiments do not take into account the toxic effects of herbal medicines. All of this implies that there is still a long way to go in interpreting and deciphering the enchantment of Chinese herbal medicine.
