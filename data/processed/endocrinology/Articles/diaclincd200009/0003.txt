Before the QI initiative started, retrospective chart review showed that, at initial visits with patients (n 5 151, mean BMI 38 kg/m2, mean age 56.8 years), providers documented history of previous visits with a dietitian 15.9% of the time and made a referral to a dietitian 7.2% of the time (Supplementary Figures S1 and S2). It was also clear from providers’ unstructured interviews that the opportunity for direct counseling was limited and that a team approach or referral was needed to support patients’ lifestyle change efforts.
