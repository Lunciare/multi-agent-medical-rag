Summarize your ﬁnal outcome data (at the end of the improvement initiative) and how they compared with your baseline data. By 10 months after initiation of the QI effort, providers on average had documented patients’ history of previous visits with a dietitian 71.4% of the time, compared with 15.9% at baseline (Supplementary Figure S1) and had made referrals to a dietitian 34.2% of the time compared with 7.2% of the time at baseline (Supplementary Figure S2) during initial consult visits for patients with type 2 diabetes and obesity (n 5 178). An increase in the documentation rate of previous visits with a dietitian preceded the increase in referrals by 2 months. Fur- thermore, in a repeat survey conducted in December 2019, 20% of providers (compared with 50% at baseline) reported that, in the prior 6 months, they had assessed for previous dietitian visits only “sometimes” or “rarely.” There are multiple plausible explanations for the in- crease in documentation of past dietitian visits and referrals to a dietitian.
