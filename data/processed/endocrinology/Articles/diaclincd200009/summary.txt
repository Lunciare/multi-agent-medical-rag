Describe your practice setting and location. Dartmouth-Hitchcock Medical Center is a rural academic primary and specialty care. The outpatient endocrinology practitioners, one dietitian, and six endocrinology fellows who care for patients with general endocrine diseases, including diabetes. Describe the speciﬁc quality gap addressed through the initiative. Our ongoing quality improvement (QI) project focuses on improving support for lifestyle change (exercise and dietary counseling) provided to patients with type 2 di- abetes and obesity.
