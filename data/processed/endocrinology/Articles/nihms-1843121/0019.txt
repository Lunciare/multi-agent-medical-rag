There are likely to be alterations in the sensory nerve endings of the three CN nerves in the taste papillae/taste buds. And finally, the perception of food in the primary gustatory cortex may be altered. Investigations of animal models have also demonstrated that taste sensitivities decline with age and are accompanied by a delayed stem cell renewal that would prevent replenishment of taste receptor cells when they undergo apoptosis. Highly vacuolated cytoplasm has been noted in the taste receptor cells of taste buds. Furthermore, our recent study demonstrated a significant reduction in taste bud size and numbers of taste receptor cells per bud accompanied by altered sweet taste responsivity in older mice compared with younger ones77. In humans, we, along with others, have shown that FP density decreases with age both cross-sectionally8,9 and longitudinally.9 Since several hormones play important roles in modulating taste responsiveness via autocrine, paracrine, or endocrine mechanisms; these hormones within taste buds may change in accordance with taste alterations.
