Other ML models have been applied to metabolic proﬁles to predict future outcomes. Using random forest machine learning models, metabolomic proﬁle at 3 months can predict body composition and adiposity at 2 years of age with a predictive value of 75.8 %, sensitivity of 100 % and speciﬁcity of 50 %. These metabolites included several classes of lyso- phospholipids and are inﬂuenced by infant feeding type . Another clinical condition in which ML may add value is in the diagnostic process is the diﬀerentiation between central diabetes insipidus (CDI) and primary polydipsia, attempting to circumvent the need for the water depriva- tion and hypertonic saline tests both of which are intensive for young patients.
