The objective of the study was to validate an established AI model to automatically detect hypo- glycaemic events by using a few ECG heartbeats recorded with the wearable device. The proposed system automati- cally learnt patterns in the ECG, discriminating between heartbeats recorded during low or normal glucose levels in the same subject . AI has also been utilized to detect diabetic retinopathy (DR) in paediatric patients . DR, a leading contributor to global blindness and impaired vision, aﬀects approximately 100 million individuals with retinopathy threatening vision in around 30 million . These numbers are projected to rise in the future, thus early detection through screening in young people is recommended to facilitate timely interven- tion. Screening for DR is recommended in children with type 1 diabetes from 11 years of age and with a diabetes duration of 3–5 years. Children with type 2 diabetes patients are advised to undergo screening soon after diagnosis, and annually thereafter.
