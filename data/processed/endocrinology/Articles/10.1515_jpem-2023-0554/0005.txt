Over the decades, AI has evolved through diﬀerent phases, including expert systems, machine learning (ML) and deep learning, leading to the current era of AI technol- ogies. ML is used to develop algorithms and statistical models enabling systems to learn from data and improve performance with experience. Deep learning is a subset of ML that employs neural networks with multiple layers, resembling the human brain’s structure, for complex data processing and pattern recognition. Natural Language Pro- cessing (NLP) is the ability to use AI to understand and generate human language, facilitating communication be- tween machines and humans and computer vision is the capacity of machines to interpret and analyse visual infor- mation, such as images and videos. Robotics AI uses AI systems to control and guide robots to perform tasks autonomously or semi-autonomously. This includes tasks like automation in manufacturing, logistics and healthcare [3–5].
