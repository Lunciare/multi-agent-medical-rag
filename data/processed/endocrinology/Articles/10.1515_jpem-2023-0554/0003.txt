Following a period of quiescence, AI received a renewed focus following the creation of the internet, and from 2000 onwards, there was a resurgence of interest in neural networks, particularly deep learning, due to improved computational power, large datasets and novel training techniques. In general, AI can be divided into nar- row or weak AI, and general or strong AI . Weak AI refers to AI systems designed and trained for speciﬁc tasks or purposes. These systems are highly specialized and excel in a predeﬁned set of activities. Examples include voice assis- tants (e.g. Siri, Alexa), image recognition and language translation. Strong AI refers to AI systems with the ability to understand, learn and apply knowledge across a wide range of tasks at a human-like level.
