Analyses of adherence data using ML to identify types of patients with reduced adherence have indicated worse adherence in those who infrequently transmit data, don’t change growth hormone device comfort settings and start treatment at an older age in the following 3, 6 or 9 months. This information can be used to provide support to at-risk patients to achieve optimal adherence and, subsequently, improve clinical outcomes with growth hormone therapy .
