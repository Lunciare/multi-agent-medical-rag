At present, however, AI algorithms in studies are essentially determining bone age within models that have a value assigned by expert radiologists, therefore, introducing a risk of inaccuracy and bias. Therefore, presently more rapid and eﬃcient calculation of bone age by AI models provides the more compelling argument for using automated bone age assessment. Future work using signiﬁcantly larger and more comprehensive databases to develop a training dataset of healthy children could be used to develop AI models that independently predict bone age – essentially removing ‘human ground truth’ and eliminating the inherent biases of using data derived from radiologist assessment to provide training sets .
