This was accomplished by providing chapters and real-life cases with formative assessment in the form of open and multiple-choice questions with standard answer models. advocate by providing personalized feedback. It has to be mentioned that the pilot study outcomes were preliminary “checks” in the principles. The results of these “checks” were used to further develop the e-learning portal. Based on the insights from the pilot studies, we further developed the ESPE e-learning portal using an open source learning management system ILIAS with major emphasis on formative assessment and with options for summative assessment.
