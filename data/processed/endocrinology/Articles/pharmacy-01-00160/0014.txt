Medical students however judged the content as appropriate to difficult. The time spent depended on the length of the case or chapter studied and ranged from a mean of 17 min for the shortest case to a mean of 69 min for the longest. Furthermore, the time spent varied per subgroup. On average residents and fellows spent most time on studying the content. The time spent was mostly judged as appropriate.
