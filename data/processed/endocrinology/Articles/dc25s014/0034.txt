Subclinical hypothyroidism may be associated with an increased risk of symptomatic hypoglycemia and dyslipi- demia (129,130) and a reduced linear growth rate. Hyperthyroidism alters glu- cose metabolism and usually causes dete- rioration of glycemia. Celiac Disease Recommendations 14.31 Screen youth with type 1 diabe- tes for celiac disease by measuring IgA tissue transglutaminase (tTG) antibod- ies, with documentation of normal to- tal serum IgA levels, soon after the diagnosis of diabetes, or IgG tTG and deamidated gliadin antibodies if IgA is deﬁcient. B 14.32 Repeat screening for celiac dis- ease within 2 years of diabetes diagno- sis and then again after 5 years and consider more frequent screening in youth who have symptoms or a ﬁrst- degree relative with celiac disease.
