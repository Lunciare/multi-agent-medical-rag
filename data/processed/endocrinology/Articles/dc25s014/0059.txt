Current pharmacologic treatment options for youth-onset type 2 diabetes are lim- ited to four approved drug classes: insulin, metformin, glucagon-like peptide 1 (GLP-1) receptor agonists, and sodium–glucose cotransporter 2 inhibitors. Presentation with ketoacidosis or marked ketosis re- quires a period of insulin therapy until fasting and postprandial glycemia have been restored to normal or near-normal levels. Insulin pump therapy may be con- sidered as an option for those on long- term multiple daily injections who are able to safely manage the device.
