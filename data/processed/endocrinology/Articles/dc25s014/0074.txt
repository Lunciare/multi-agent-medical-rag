A Most youth with type 2 diabetes come from historically marginalized communi- ties, have low socioeconomic status, and often experience multiple psychosocial stressors (9,40,42,231). Consideration of the sociocultural context and efforts to personalize diabetes management are of critical importance to minimize barriers to care, enhance participation, and maxi- mize response to treatment. Screening for food insecurity, housing stability, and other barriers related to the social deter- minants of health should be part of rou- tine pediatric diabetes care (232).
