A 14.96 If LDL cholesterol remains >130 mg/dL (>3.4 mmol/L) after 6 months of dietary intervention, ini- tiate therapy with statin, with a goal of LDL <100 mg/dL (<2.6 mmol/L). Due to the potential teratogenic ef- fects, individuals of childbearing age should receive reproductive counsel- ing, and statins should be avoided in individuals of childbearing age who are not using reliable contraception.
