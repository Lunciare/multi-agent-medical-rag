B 14.52 Children and adolescents with overweight or obesity in whom the diagnosis of type 2 diabetes is being considered should have a panel of pancreatic autoantibodies tested to ex- clude the possibility of autoimmune type 1 diabetes. B In recent years, incidence and prevalence of type 2 diabetes in adolescents have in- creased dramatically, especially in histori- cally marginalized communities (185). A few studies suggest oral glucose tolerance tests or fasting plasma glucose values as more suitable diagnostic tests than A1C in the pediatric population, especially among certain ethnicities (186), while fasting glu- cose alone may overdiagnose diabetes in children (187,188).
