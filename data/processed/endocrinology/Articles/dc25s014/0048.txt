A comprehensive foot exam, including inspection, palpation of dorsalis pedis and posterior tibial pulses, and determination of proprioception, vi- bration, and monoﬁlament sensation, should be performed annually along with an assessment of symptoms of neuro- pathic pain (173). Foot inspection can be performed at each visit to educate youth regarding the importance of foot care (see Section 12, “Retinopathy, Neuropathy, and Foot Care”). TYPE 2 DIABETES For information on risk-based screening for type 2 diabetes and prediabetes in youth, please refer to Section 2, “Diagnosis and Classiﬁcation of Diabetes.” For additional support for these recommendations, see the ADA position statement “Evaluation and Management of Youth-Onset Type 2 Diabetes” (3).
