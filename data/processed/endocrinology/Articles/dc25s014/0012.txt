Following recommended eating patterns is associated with better glycemic outcomes in youth with type 1 diabetes (12). Although carbohydrate content is the primary variable for calculation of meal- time insulin doses, meals with higher fat and protein content can cause early hypoglycemia and delayed postprandial glucose excursions. Some adjustments in insulin dosing, including an increase in the calculated dose and a split dose, will improve postprandial glucose manage- ment (13–17). Physical Activity and Exercise Recommendations 14.7 Physical activity is recommended for all youth with type 1 diabetes with the goal of 60 min of moderate- to vigorous-intensity aerobic activity daily, with vigorous muscle-strengthening and bone-strengthening activities at least 3 days per week.
