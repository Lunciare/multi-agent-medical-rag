B 12.29 Refer individuals who smoke and have a history of prior lower-extremity complications, loss of protective sensa- tion, structural abnormalities, or PAD to foot care specialists for ongoing preven- tive care and lifelong surveillance. A 12.30 Provide general preventive foot self-care education to all people with diabetes, including those with loss of protective sensation, on ap- propriate ways to examine their feet (palpation or visual inspection with an unbreakable mirror) for daily sur- veillance of early foot problems. B 12.31 The use of specialized thera- peutic footwear is recommended for people with diabetes at high risk for ulceration, including those with loss of protective sensation, foot deformi- ties, ulcers, callous formation, poor peripheral circulation, or history of amputation.
