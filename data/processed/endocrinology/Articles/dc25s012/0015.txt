Adjunctive Therapy Lowering blood pressure has been shown to decrease retinopathy progression, al- though strict goals (systolic blood pressure <120 mmHg) do not impart additional beneﬁt (6). In individuals with dyslipide- mia, retinopathy progression may be slowed by the addition of fenoﬁbrate, particularly with early diabetic retinopa- thy at baseline (41–43).
