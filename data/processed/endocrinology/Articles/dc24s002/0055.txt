Screening and Testing for Prediabetes and Type 2 Diabetes in Children and Adolescents The epidemiologic studies that formed the basis for recommending A1C to diag- nose diabetes included only adult popula- guidance concluded that A1C, FPG, or 2-h PG could be used to test for prediabetes or type 2 diabetes in children and adoles- cents (113). In the last decade, the incidence and prevalence of type 2 diabetes in children and adolescents has increased dramati- cally, especially in racial and ethnic mi- nority populations (114).
