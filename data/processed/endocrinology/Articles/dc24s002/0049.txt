Type 2 diabetes is more common in African Americans, Hispanics/Latinos, Native Americans, Asian Americans, and Native Hawaiians and Pacific Islanders. Higher body weight increases diabetes risk for everyone. Asian Americans are at increased diabetes risk at lower body weight than the rest of the general public (about 15 pounds lower). If you scored 5 or higher: Lower Your Risk The good news is you can manage your risk for type 2 diabetes. Small steps make a big difference in helping you live a longer, healthier life. If you are at high risk, your first step is to visit your doctor to see if additional testing is needed. Visit diabetes.org or call 1-800-DIABETES (800-342-2383) for information, tips on getting started, and ideas for simple, small steps you can take to help lower your risk.
