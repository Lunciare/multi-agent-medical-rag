Use of A1C for Screening and Diagnosis of Diabetes Recommendations 2.2a The A1C test should be performed using a method that is certiﬁed by the National Glycohemoglobin Standardiza- tion Program (NGSP) as traceable to the Diabetes Control and Complications Trial (DCCT) reference assay. B 2.2b Point-of-care A1C testing for dia- betes screening and diagnosis should be restricted to U.S. Food and Drug Administration–approved devices at Clinical Laboratory Improvement Am- endments (CLIA)–certiﬁed laboratories that perform testing of moderate com- plexity or higher by trained personnel. B 2.3 Marked discordance between A1C and repeat blood glucose values should raise the possibility of a problem or interference with either test.
