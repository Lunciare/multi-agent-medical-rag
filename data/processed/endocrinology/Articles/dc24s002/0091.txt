The IADPSG criteria (one-step strategy) have been adopted internationally as the preferred approach. Data that compare population-wide outcomes with one-step versus two-step approaches have been inconsistent to date (213,230–232). In ad- dition, pregnancies complicated by GDM per the IADPSG criteria, but not recog- nized as such, have outcomes compara- ble to pregnancies with diagnosed GDM by the more stringent two-step criteria (233,234). There remains strong consen- sus that establishing a uniform approach to diagnosing GDM will beneﬁt people with GDM, caregivers, and policymakers. Longer-term outcome studies are cur- rently underway.
