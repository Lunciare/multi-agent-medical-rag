Future Considerations The conﬂicting recommendations from expert groups underscore the fact that there are data to support each strategy. A systematic review of economic evalua- tions of GDM screening found that the one-step method identiﬁed more cases of GDM and was more likely to be cost- effective than the two-step method (229).
