Radioiodine therapy gradually increased until 2000. How- ever, the trend of using radioiodine for hyperthyroidism seems to have decreased. In a survey of experts on thyroid disease in 1992, antithyroid drug treatment was the first choice for 81% of responders, 11% of responders chose radioiodine for first-line therapy, and 8% chose surgery as the initial treatment modality . In 2013, 97.1% of experts reported choosing antithyroid drugs, and the remaining 2.9% reported choosing radioiodine for the initial treatment modality .
