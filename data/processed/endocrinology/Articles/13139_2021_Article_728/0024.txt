PRRT has been used for thera- peutic purposes since the mid-1990s, but it has only been studied in several single-arm clinical trials. NETTER-1 was the first multicenter, stratified, open, randomized, DOTATATE . The NETTER-1 study demonstrated that Lu-177 DOTATATE showed an improved progres- sion-free survival (PFS) (20-month PFS rates, 65.2% vs. 10.8%) and higher response rates (18% vs. 3%) than high- dose octreotide treatment . Although Lu-177 DOTATATE is not yet reimbursed by the NHIS in Korea, it has been available through the Korea Orphan and Essential Drug Center since November 2019. The Korean Ministry of Food and Drug Safety approved the drug for domestic use in July 2020 and has expanded treat- ment opportunities for domestic patients with NETs to include a support program for 1 year in March 2021.
