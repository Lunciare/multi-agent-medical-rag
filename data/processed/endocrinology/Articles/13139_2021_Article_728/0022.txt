In Korea, the Korea Atomic Energy Research Institute (KAERI) has been producing I-131 MIBG since 2001; in the same year, the NHIS began reimbursing high-dose I-131 MIBG therapy. This therapy has been used to treat neuroblastomas, pheochromocyto- mas, and MTCs. In particular, more than 3000 children in Korea were treated with I-131 MIBG for neuroblastomas. The number of patients receiving I-131 MIBG therapy has increased gradually from the 1990s to the present and has maintained a similar number of cases over the past 5 years (Fig. 7).
