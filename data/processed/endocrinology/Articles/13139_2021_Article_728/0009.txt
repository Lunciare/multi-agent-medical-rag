Salivary gland damage is a common adverse effect of radioiodine therapy, and many studies were performed to understand and overcome it. Salivary gland dysfunction was clearly demonstrated after radioiodine therapy and was well evaluated using salivary gland scintigraphy 5 years after radioiodine therapy . Salivary gland massage during radioiodine therapy showed the protective effect of salivary gland function [40–44].
