SGLT Inhibition and Risk of Ketosis Individuals with type 1 diabetes (84,236) and insulin-deﬁcient type 2 diabetes are at increased risk for DKA with SGLT inhibitor therapy. SGLT inhibitor–associated DKA occurs in approximately 4% of people with type 1 diabetes; the risk can be 5–17 times higher than that in people with T1D not treated with SGLT inhibitors (237). It is important to note that SGLT2 inhibitors are not approved for use in people with type 1 diabetes. In contrast, DKA is un- common in people with type 2 diabetes treated with SGLT inhibitors, with an esti- mated incidence of 0.6–4.9 events per 1,000 person-years (238).
