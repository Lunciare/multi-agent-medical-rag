Pharmacotherapy should be started at the time type 2 diabetes is diagnosed, without delay, unless there are contrain- dications. Medication plans should have adequate efﬁcacy to achieve and main- tain individualized treatment goals with respect to glucose lowering, reduction of cardiovascular and kidney disease risks, weight management, and impacts on other health conditions and treatment burden.
