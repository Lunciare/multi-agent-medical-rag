Each approach has advantages and dis- advantages. For example, basal-prandial plans offer greater ﬂexibility for individu- als who eat on irregular schedules, have variable meal content, or otherwise beneﬁt from greater individualization and ﬂexibility in insulin administration. On the other hand, two doses of premixed insulin is a simple, convenient means of spreading insulin across the day. Moreover, human insulins, separately, self-mixed, or as pre- mixed NPH/regular (for example, 70/30) formulations, are often less costly alterna- tives to insulin analogs.
