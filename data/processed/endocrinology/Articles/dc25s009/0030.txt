A 9.23 In adults with type 2 diabetes, initiation of insulin should be consid- ered regardless of background glucose- lowering therapy or disease stage if symptoms of hyperglycemia are pre- sent or when A1C or blood glucose levels are very high (i.e., A1C >10% [>86 mmol/mol] or blood glucose $300 mg/dL [$16.7 mmol/L]). E 9.24 In adults with type 2 diabetes and no evidence of insulin deﬁciency, a GLP-1 RA, including a dual GIP and GLP-1 RA, is preferred to insulin (Fig. 9.4). A 9.25 If insulin is used, combination ther- apy with a GLP-1 RA, including a dual GIP and GLP-1 RA, is recommended for greater glycemic effectiveness as well as beneﬁcial effects on weight and hy- poglycemia risk for adults with type 2 diabetes. Insulin dosing should be re- assessed upon addition or dose escala- tion of a GLP-1 RA or dual GIP and GLP-1 RA. A 9.26 In adults with type 2 diabetes who are initiating insulin therapy, con- tinue glucose-lowering agents (unless contraindicated or not tolerated) for ongoing glycemic and metabolic bene- ﬁts (i.e., weight, cardiometabolic, or kidney beneﬁts).
