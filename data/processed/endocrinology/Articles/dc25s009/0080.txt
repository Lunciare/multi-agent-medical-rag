Preconception counseling should be part of the routine care of individuals with diabetes who have childbearing potential. Counseling should include the known ben- eﬁts and risks of glucose-lowering medica- tions as well as other medications (e.g., lipid-lowering and antihypertensive thera- pies) during pregnancy and recommenda- tions for when changes in medications should occur prior to pregnancy (see Sec- tion 15, “Management of Diabetes in Pregnancy,” for more information on pre- conception counseling and glucose-lowering treatment during pregnancy).
