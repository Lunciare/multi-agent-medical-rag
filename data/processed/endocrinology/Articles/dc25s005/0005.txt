DSMES should be person-centered— placing the person with diabetes and their family and/or support system at the center of the care model as they work in col- laboration with health care professio- nals. Person-centered care is respectful of and responsive to individual and cul- tural preferences, needs, and values. It ensures the values of the person with diabetes guide all decision-making (15).
