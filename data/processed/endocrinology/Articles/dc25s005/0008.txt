Team members acting in the DCES capacity should have special- ized clinical knowledge of diabetes and behavior change principles. Credentialing such as certiﬁed diabetes care and edu- cation specialists (CDCES) (cbdce.org/) and/or board certiﬁcation in advanced diabetes management (BC-ADM) (diabe- teseducator.org/education/certiﬁcation/ bc_adm) demonstrates an individual’s specialized training in and understanding of diabetes management and support (9), and engagement with qualiﬁed professio- nals has been shown to improve diabe- tes-related outcomes (52). There is also continued and growing evidence for the educators, peer support, and lay lead- ers in providing ongoing diabetes self- management support (53,54).
