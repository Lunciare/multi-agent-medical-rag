In some people with diabetes, hypoglycemia after exercise may occur and last for several hours due to increased insulin sensitivity. Hypoglycemia is not common in those who are not treated with insulin or insulin secretagogues, and no routine preventive measures for hypoglycemia are usually ad- vised in these cases. Intense activities, such as HIIT, may actually raise glucose levels in- stead of lowering them, especially if pre- exercise glucose is elevated (261). Because of variation in glycemic response to exercise, people with diabetes should be taught to check blood glucose levels and/or monitor CGM values during and after exercise, how to understand the effect of exercise on glu- cose, and about the potential prolonged ef- fects (depending on intensity and duration) (303).
