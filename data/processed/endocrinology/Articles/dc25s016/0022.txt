HYPOGLYCEMIA Recommendations 16.12 A hypoglycemia management surveillance protocol should be adopted by all health systems. A plan for identi- fying, treating, and preventing hypogly- cemia should be established for each individual. Episodes of hypoglycemia in the hospital should be documented in the health record and tracked to inform quality improvements. C 16.13 Treatment plans should be re- viewed and changed as necessary to prevent hypoglycemia and recurrent hypoglycemia when a blood glucose value of <70 mg/dL (<3.9 mmol/L) is documented. C People with or without diabetes may ex- perience hypoglycemia in the hospital setting. While hypoglycemia is associ- ated with increased mortality (90,91), in many cases, it is a marker of an underly- ing disease rather than the cause of fa- tality.
