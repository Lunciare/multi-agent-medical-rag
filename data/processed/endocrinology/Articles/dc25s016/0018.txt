Type 1 Diabetes For people with type 1 diabetes, dosing insulin based solely on premeal glucose levels does not account for basal insulin requirements or caloric intake and in- creases the risk of both hypoglycemia and hyperglycemia (67).Typically, basal insulin dosing is based on body weight and ex- pected sensitivity to insulin, and there is some evidence that people with renal in- sufﬁciency should be treated with lower insulin doses (68,69). An insulin schedule with basal and correction components is necessary for all hospitalized individuals with type 1 diabetes, even for those taking nothing by mouth, with the addi- tion of prandial insulin when individuals are eating.
