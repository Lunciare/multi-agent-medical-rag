For transitioning, the total daily dose of subcutaneous insulin may be calculated based on the insulin infusion rate during the prior 6–8 h when stable glycemic goals were achieved, based on prior home insu- lin dose, or following a weight-based ap- proach (70). For people being transitioned to concentrated insulin (U-200, U-300, or U-500) in the inpatient setting, it is impor- tant to ensure correct dosing by using a separate insulin pen or vial for each indi- vidual and by meticulous pharmacy and nursing supervision of the dose adminis- tered (64–66,75).
