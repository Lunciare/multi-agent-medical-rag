GLYCEMIC GOALS IN HOSPITALIZED ADULTS Recommendations 16.4a Insulin should be initiated or intensiﬁed for treatment of persistent hyperglycemia starting at a threshold of $180 mg/dL ($10.0 mmol/L) (conﬁrmed on two occasions within 24 h) for the majority of critically ill individuals (those in the intensive care unit [ICU]). A 16.4b Insulin and/or other glucose- lowering therapies should be initiated or intensiﬁed for treatment of persis- tent hyperglycemia starting at a thresh- old of $180 mg/dL ($10.0 mmol/L) (conﬁrmed on two occasions within 24 h) for the majority of noncritically ill individuals (those not in the ICU). B 16.5a Once therapy is initiated, a glycemic goal of 140–180 mg/dL (7.8–10.0 mmol/L) is recommended for most critically ill individuals (those in the ICU) with hyperglycemia.
