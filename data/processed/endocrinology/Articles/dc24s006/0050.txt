Notably, sulfonylureas in- teract with a number of commonly used antimicrobials (ﬂuoroquinolones, clarithro- mycin, sulfamethoxazole-trimethoprim, metronidazole, and ﬂuconazole) that can dramatically increase their effective dose, leading to hypoglycemia (143–145). Clini- cians should consider temporarily decreas- ing or stopping sulfonylureas when these antimicrobials are prescribed.
