BGM and CGM can be useful to guide medical nutrition therapy and physical activity, pre- vent hypoglycemia, and aid medication management. CGM metrics, including TIR (with time below range and time above range), can provide helpful insights to in- form a personalized diabetes management plan. Remote access to glucose data is growing and may help improve diabetes management (33–35). CGM systems have evolved rapidly in both accuracy and affordability. As such, many individuals with diabetes have these data available to assist with self- management and their health care profes- sionals’ assessment of glycemic status. Re- ports can be generated from CGM that will allow the health care professional and person with diabetes to view TIR, a calcu- lated glucose management indicator, and assess hypoglycemia, hyperglycemia, and glycemic variability.
