Other notable clinical and biological risk factorsforhypoglycemia areolderage, mul- timorbidity, cognitive impairment, chronic kidney disease and end-stage kidney dis- ease in particular, CVD, depression, and neuropathy (92,93). Female sex has also been found to be an independent risk fac- tor for hypoglycemia in multiple studies, al- though the mechanisms of this relationship are unclear and require further research (92).
