Setting and Modifying Glycemic Goals Glycemic goals and management should be individualized and not one size ﬁts all. To prevent both microvascular and mac- rovascular complications of diabetes, there is a major call to overcome thera- peutic inertia and treat to individualized goals (53,64). Numerous factors must be consid- ered when setting a glycemic goal. The ADA proposes general goals that are ap- propriate for many people but empha- sizes the importance of individualization based on key patient characteristics. Gly- cemic goals must be individualized in the context of shared decision-making to ad- dress individual needs and preferences and consider characteristics that inﬂu- ence risks and beneﬁts of therapy; this approach may optimize engagement and self-efﬁcacy.
