Lastly, level 3 hypoglycemia is de- ﬁned as a severe event characterized by al- tered mental and/or physical functioning that requires assistance from another per- son for recovery, irrespective of glucose level. Hypoglycemia has a broad range of negative health consequences (74). Level 3 hypoglycemia may be recognized or un- recognized and can progress to loss of consciousness, seizure, coma, or death. Level 3 hypoglycemia was associated with mortality in both the standard and the in- tensive glycemia arms of the ACCORD trial, but the relationships between hypoglyce- mia, achieved A1C, and treatment inten- sity were not straightforward (75).
