B 2.4 In conditions associated with an altered relationship between A1C and glycemia, such as some hemoglobin variants, pregnancy (second and third trimesters and the postpartum period), glucose-6-phosphate dehydrogenase deﬁciency, HIV, hemodialysis, recent blood loss or transfusion, hemolysis, or erythropoietin therapy, plasma glucose criteria should be used to diagnose diabetes. B The A1C test should be performed using a method that is certiﬁed by the National Glycohemoglobin Standardization Program (NGSP) (ngsp.org) and standardized or traceable to the Diabetes Control and Complications Trial (DCCT) reference assay.
