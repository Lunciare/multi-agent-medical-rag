Data are lacking on how the treatment of mother’s hyperglycemia in pregnancy affects her offspring’s risk for obesity, diabetes, and other metabolic disorders (201,202). Additional well-designed clini- cal studies are needed to determine the optimal intensity of monitoring and treat- ment of individuals with GDM diagnosed by the one-step strategy.
