[18F]FDG PET/CT is effective in identifying hypermeta- bolic primary cancers (i.e. adrenal carcinoma), metastatic lesions and hyperfunctioning tumors such as pheochromo- cytoma . Therefore, it is a valuable imaging method that can provide additional information about the nature of a mass, allowing differentiation between benign and malignant lesions. Malignant lesions typically present with a higher SUVmax than benign lesions, with sensitivity ranging from 93 to 100% . A recent meta-analysis aimed to evaluate the diagnos- tic value of ­[18F]FDG PET/CT in distinguishing between benign and malignant adrenal tumors, particularly in cases of adrenal incidentalomas or those discovered during stag- ing or follow-up of cancer patients .
