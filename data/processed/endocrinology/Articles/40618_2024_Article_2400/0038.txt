In conclusion, while PET/CT offers significant advan- tages in diagnosing and managing endocrine disorders, it also raises complex ethical considerations related to resource allocation, accessibility, radiation exposure, research ethics, and healthcare costs. Healthcare profes- sionals, policymakers, and ethicists must collaborate to address these ethical challenges, ensuring that the use of PET/CT in endocrinology is guided by principles that pri- oritize patient well-being, equity, and ethical healthcare delivery.
