Access to insulin pump ther- apy, including AID systems, should be al- lowed or continued in older adults as it is in younger people. Complications of the pump can be caused by issues with infusion sets (dis- lodgement and occlusion), which place in- dividuals at risk for ketosis and DKA and thus must be recognized and managed early (181). Other pump skin issues include lipohypertrophy or, less frequently, lipoa- trophy (182,183) and pump site infection (184). Discontinuation of pump therapy is relatively uncommon today; the frequency has decreased over the past few decades, and its causes have changed (184,185). Current reasons for attrition are problems with cost or wearability, loss of insurance, dislike for the pump, suboptimal glycemic outcomes, or mood disorders (e.g., anxiety or depression) (186). Insulin Pumps in Youth The safety of insulin pumps in youth has been established for over 15 years (187).
