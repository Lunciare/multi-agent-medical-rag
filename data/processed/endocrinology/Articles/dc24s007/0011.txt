The current ISO and FDA standards are com- pared in Table 7.1. In Europe, currently marketed meters must meet current ISO standards. In the U.S., currently mar- keted meters must meet the standard under which they were approved, which may not be the current standard. More- over, the monitoring of current accuracy postmarketing is left to the manufac- turer and not routinely checked by an in- dependent source. People with diabetes assume their glucose meter is accurate because it is FDA cleared, but that may not be the case. There is substantial variation in the accuracy of widely used BGM systems (15,16). The Diabetes Technology Society Blood Glucose Monitoring System Surveil- lance Program provides information on the performance of devices used for BGM (diabetestechnology.org/surveillance/). In one analysis, 6 of the top 18 best-selling glucose meters met the accuracy stan- dard (17).
