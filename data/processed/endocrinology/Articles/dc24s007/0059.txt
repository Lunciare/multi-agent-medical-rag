B 7.34 People with diabetes who are competent to safely use diabetes devi- ces such as insulin pumps and CGM systems should be supported to con- tinue using them in an inpatient set- ting or during outpatient procedures, whenever possible, and when proper supervision is available. E Individuals who are comfortable using their diabetes devices, such as insulin pumps and CGM, should be allowed to use them in an inpatient setting if they are well enough to take care of the devices and have brought the necessary supplies (275–279).
