Insulin Pumps in People With Type 2 and Other Types of Diabetes Traditional insulin pumps can be consid- ered for the treatment of people with type 2 diabetes who are on MDI as well as those who have other types of diabe- tes resulting in insulin deﬁciency, for instance, those who have had a pancre- atectomy and/or individuals with cystic ﬁbrosis (245–249). Similar to data on in- sulin pump use in people with type 1 dia- betes, reductions in A1C levels have been reported in some studies (247,250). More recently, real-world reports have shown reduction of A1C levels and reduction of total daily insulin dose in individuals with type 2 diabetes initiating insulin pump therapy (251).
