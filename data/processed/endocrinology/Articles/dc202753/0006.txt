Participants completed pre–focus group surveys and, after ses- sions were ﬁnished, were provided $65 cash as incentive for their time. Pre–fo- cus group surveys developed in REDCap were completed on tablets and con- tained questions about overall partici- pant demographics, technology use, routine endocrinology care use, social support systems, and other questions related to diabetes management. The focus groups were led by trained faculty members with expertise in qualitative research. Acting as the moderator, the faculty member was supported by grad- uate assistants who had roles that in- cluded keeping track of time spent on each prompt to ensure balance, note taking, and focus group debriefs that followed each session. To ensure satura- tion goals were achieved and for bal- ance across states, we aimed to conduct a minimum of 6 and maximum of 8 focus groups in each state (for a to- tal of 12–16) (34,35).
