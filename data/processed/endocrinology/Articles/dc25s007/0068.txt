Loop, an open-source model predic- tive control algorithm, is implemented on iPhones as an app. Prospective real- world data from 558 adults and children with type 1 diabetes on this system (255) was used to support the FDA clearance of a variant called Tidepool Loop (259). Both the Loop and OpenAPS algorithms offer direct management of algorithm ag- gressiveness through conventional pump settings. Therefore, it is advisable that health care professionals understand and offer support in tuning settings for these safe and effective technologies (254). This may include, for example, the adjustment of basal rates, insulin-to-carbohydrate ra- tios, or insulin sensitivity factors. As with any AID system, a backup insulin treatment plan is advisable.
