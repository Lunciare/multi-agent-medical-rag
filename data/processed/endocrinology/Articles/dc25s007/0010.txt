Moreover, the monitoring of current accuracy postmarketing is left to the manufacturer and not routinely checked by an independent source. People with diabetes assume their glu- cose meter is accurate because it is FDA cleared, but that may not be the case. There is substantial variation in the accu- racy of widely used BGM systems (17,18). The Diabetes Technology Society Blood Glucose Monitoring System Surveillance Program provides information on the performance of devices used for BGM (diabetestechnology.org/surveillance/). In one analysis, 6 of the top 18 best-selling glucose meters met the accuracy standard (19).
