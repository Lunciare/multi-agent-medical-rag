Insulin Pumps and Automated Insulin Delivery Systems in People With Type 2 and Other Types of Diabetes Traditional insulin pumps can be considered for the treatment of people with type 2 dia- betes who are on MDI as well as those who have other types of diabetes resulting in in- sulin deﬁciency, for instance, those who have had a pancreatectomy and/or individ- uals with cystic ﬁbrosis (241–245). Similar to data on insulin pump use in people with type 1 diabetes, reductions in A1C levels have been reported in some studies (243,246). More recently, real-world re- ports have shown reduction of A1C levels and reduction of total daily insulin dose in individuals with type 2 diabetes initiat- ing insulin pump therapy (247).
