Open-Source Automated Insulin Dosing Recommendation 7.29 Support and provide diabetes management advice to people with diabetes who choose to use an open- source closed-loop system. B Open-source automated insulin dosing (OS-AID) algorithms provide the precise code that governs their operation, so health care professionals and people with diabetes can have a more com- plete understanding of risks and bene- ﬁts (254). Any commercial entity could provide the source code for their inter- operable automated glycemic controller, but most choose not to. OS-AID algo- rithms are largely designed, maintained, and curated by people with diabetes and their loved ones.
