For people with diabetes who use insulin, insulin syringes and pens both can deliver insulin safely and effectively for the achievement of glycemic goals. Individual preferences, cost, insulin type, dosing ther- apy, and self-management capabilities should beconsidered whenchoosingamongdelivery systems. Trials with insulin pens generally show equivalence or small improvements in glycemic outcomes compared with using a vial and syringe. Many individuals with diabe- tes prefer using a pen because of its simplicity and convenience.
