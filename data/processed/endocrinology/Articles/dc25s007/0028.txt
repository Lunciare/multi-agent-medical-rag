In a subsequent post hoc anal- ysis, the isCGM group showed that the ef- fects of isCGM were present 1 week after isCGM initiation for weekly mean glucose, glucose management indicator (GMI), per- centage of TIR, percentage of TAR, and mean amplitude glucose excursion and re- mained stable from baseline to 12 weeks (74). Additionally, beneﬁts of isCGM were also reported in an RCT where the use of isCGM plus diabetes education versus dia- betes education alone showed decreased A1C levels and increased TIR as well as in- creased time in tight target range (70–140 mg/dL [3.9–7.8 mmol/L]) in the isCGM- plus-education group (8).
