Increasing levels of AMH in PCOS women inhibit the enzyme aromatase, increase the production of LH by the pituitary gland and decrease the inﬂuence of FSH on ovarian tissue, thus leading to irregular menstrual cycle and infertility. IVF procedures among patients with elevated AMH concentration may result in a higher clinical pregnancy rate and a more frequent ovarian hyperstimulation syndrome. Pregnant women with PCOS showing a high level of AMH are at risk of preterm labor in the third trimester as compared to the group of healthy women.
