As it is known that the preparation of the breast to the lactation period depends on the decreasing level of AMH, it is not surprising that breastfeeding is impaired in the group of women with PCOS (27, 161, 162) (Figure 5). 8 AMH and menopause AMH plays a role in regulating the recruitment of primordial follicles (148), i.e., the hormone may inﬂuence the time of menopause. Even though SNPs of the AMH gene (Ile49Ser; rs10407022) and AMHR2 gene (482 A→G; rs2002555) are associated with a higher concentration of estradiol in the follicular phase of healthy normo-ovulatory women (163), it is difﬁcult to ﬁnd a strong correlation between the time of menopause and genetic variations of the AMH gene. However, the SNP of the AMHR2 gene with the SNP rs20025555 (G/G) means an earlier (on average by 2.6 years) menopause than with the A/A genotype (148). Nonetheless, this is true only in the normo-ovulatory nulliparous group of women (148).
