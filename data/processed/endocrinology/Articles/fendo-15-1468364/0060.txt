Analysis of AMH levels in this group of patients would allow avoiding therapy with a GnRH agonist aimed at inhibiting estradiol production, which is associated with serious side effects (239). There is also another connection between RANKL, NFkB and AMH. AMH inhibits RANKL-dependent osteoclast differentiation by preventing the degradation of the IkB (inhibitor of nuclear factor kappa B) protein (240). Under the inﬂuence of AMH, the expression of osteoclast differentiation markers (FOS, NFATC1, ACP5) is reduced (240) (Figure 4). However, it does not affect osteoblast differentiation dependent on BMP2, which is another member of the TGFb family (240). There is no expression of AMH in osteoclasts and osteoblasts (240). Taking into account the inhibition of osteoclast differentiation, the usefulness of AMH in premenopausal and perimenopausal age as a marker of low bone mineral density is considered (241–243). Therefore, there may be a functional crosstalk between vitamin D and AMH. The serum level of 25- hydroxyvitamin D3 is inversely proportional to the concentration of AMH and positively correlated with SHBG. However, this applies mainly to women with PCOS (244) (Figure 5).
