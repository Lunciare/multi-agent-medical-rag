Dimeric AMH peptides activate the receptor AMHR2, which then together with ALKs activate the regulatory SMAD proteins 1, 5 and 8. The latter translocate to the nucleus and act together with SMAD4 and other supporting transcription factors (TFs) as regulators of the expression of AMH target genes. but most other investigated tissues also show some expression of the gene (Figure 2A). In contrast, the expression of the AMHR2 gene is far more restricted to adrenal gland, ovary, testes, spleen and pancreas (Figure 2B). The serum concentration of AMH in healthy women of the reproductive age is in the range of 1.5-4.0 ng/mL (42). Tumors originating from sex cord cells and ovarian GCs produce AMH and in those cases the serum concentration often exceeds the reference value up to 1000 times (17, 43) (Figure 3). For example, a AMH concentration of 3205 ng/mL was found in a patient diagnosed with metastatic sex cord tumor (17).
