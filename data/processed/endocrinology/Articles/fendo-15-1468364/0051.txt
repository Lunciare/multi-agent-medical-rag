The expression of AMHR2 in EC is not inﬂuenced by BMI, the patient’s age, their parity, the mass of newborns, breastfeeding time, number of miscarriages, number of menstrual years, hormonal status, use of hormonal replacement therapy or the presence of arterial hypertension. The only factor reducing AMHR2 gene expression in EC is type 2 diabetes (33). Interestingly, women with type 1 diabetes also have decreased AMH levels compared to non-diabetic women (210). Another issue is the unclear role of intracellular AMH expression in EC. Among the different types of EC, the presence of AMH in EC tissue was conﬁrmed in approximately 10% of cases, diagnosed only after the menopause and never before this moment in female life (22). This applies to EC with a good prognosis well (G1) and moderately (G2) differentiated endometroid adenocarcinoma and clear cell cancer that is characterized by poor prognosis (22).
