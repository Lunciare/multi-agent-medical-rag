The geriatric class has the highest prevalence of obesity, hyperten- sion, arthritis, and incontinence, and the cardiovascular class has the highest prev- alence of myocardial infarctions, heart failure, and stroke. Compared with the healthy class, the cardiovascular class has the highest risk of frailty and subsequent mortality. Additional research is needed to develop a reproducible classiﬁcation scheme to distinguish the natural history of disease as well as differential response to glucose management and speciﬁc glucose-lowering agents (69). Vulnerable Older Adults at the End of Life For people with diabetes receiving pallia- tive care and end-of-life care, the focus should be to avoid hypoglycemia and symptomatic hyperglycemia while reduc- ing the burdens of glycemic management. Thus, as organ failure develops, the treat- ment plan will have to be deintensiﬁed and one or more agents will need to be discontinued.
