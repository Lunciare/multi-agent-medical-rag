The Institute for Healthcare Improvement has developed an evidence-based “4Ms” framework for age-friendly health care that is being adopted by many health systems caring for older adults. The key elements of this approach to the care of older adults are Mentation, Medications, Mobility, and What Matters Most (person centered), with the understanding that any one of the components may affect another do- main (8). This approach has been con- ceptualized to address person-speciﬁc issues that may be interrelated and af- fect diabetes management in older indi- viduals in Fig. 13.1.
