Metformin Metformin is a treatment option for older adults with type 2 diabetes if prescription guidelines are followed carefully. Metfor- min may be used safely in individuals with an estimated glomerular ﬁltration rate (eGFR) $30 mL/min/1.73 m2 (111), while lower doses may be used in those with an eGFR 30–45 mL/min/1.73 m2. eGFR should be monitored every 3 to 6 months in those at risk for decline in kidney func- tion. However, it is contraindicated in those with advanced renal insufﬁciency and should be used with caution in those with hypoperfusion, hypoxemia, impaired hepatic function, or heart failure because of the increased risk of lactic acidosis. Met- formin may be temporarily discontinued before procedures including imaging stud- ies using iodinated contrast, during hospi- talizations, and when acute illness may compromise renal or liver function.
