Both studies enrolled older individuals whose blood glucose was relatively well managed (mean A1C 7.4%), and both used a crossover design comparing hybrid closed-loop insulin delivery to sensor- augmented pump therapy. A recent ran- domized controlled trial of older adults with type 2 diabetes using multiple daily injections who were unable to manage in- sulin therapy on their own revealed an in- crease of TIR of 27% over 12 weeks of AID use in addition to tailored home health care services (49). These trials provide the ﬁrst evidence that older individuals with long-standing type 1 and type 2 diabetes can success- fully use advanced insulin delivery tech- nologies to improve glycemic outcomes, as has been seen in younger populations.
