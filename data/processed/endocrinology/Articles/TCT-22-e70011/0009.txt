Seven studies used mannequins (a model of the human body for teaching anatomy and demonstrating medical skills etc.), with five focusing on external genitalia and puberal assessment (the simulation focused on external genitalia examination on the mannequin and not the whole body or other systems), one on hypoglycaemia management and the use of different types of glucagon, and one on DKA management for pediatric emergency medicine fellows and residents. Conducted by Roberts et al. , this latter study utilized an infant mannequin with structural fidelity .
