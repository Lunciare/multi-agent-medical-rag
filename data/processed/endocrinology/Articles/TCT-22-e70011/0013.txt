Another study assessed errors in dia- betes management, including insulin prescription, commu- nication, and fluid orders . Errors were primarily related to insulin type, dose, timing or failure to order. An 8-­week curriculum intervention included an online tutorial targeting baseline diabetes management knowledge, interactive discus- sions, and a case presentation, with errors assessed before and after the intervention. Using the European Society for Paediatric Endocrinology (ESPE) e-­learning website , Kranenburg et al. as- sessed communication skills in DSD through two online cases. PE fellows globally described best practices for communicat- ing with (i) parents of a newborn with CAH, and (ii) a young woman with 46, XY gonadal dysgenesis. Their responses, analysed for completeness, quality, and empathy, showed sig- nificant variability, with 15% (Case 1) and 17% (Case 2) rated as poor. Twenty-­two senior pediatric endocrinologists devel- oped and finalized assessment guidelines, adjusting them to a three-­level system with empathy as a key item due to vari- ations among experts.
