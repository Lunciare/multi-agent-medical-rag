Finally, as this was a scoping review, our goal was to explore currently avail- able educational initiatives related to PE, rather than determining the effectiveness of specific educational interventions in improv- ing the educational outcomes targeted in each paper. Based on the results of our data extraction for the intervention studies, all the authors reported that their intervention improved targeted edu- cational outcomes. However, the studies, including the topic and the type of intervention used, were diverse, making it difficult to make overall conclusions regarding effectiveness. 5 | Conclusion This review provides valuable insight into the current land- scape of PE education and identifies strengths and opportuni- ties for improvement.
