Results are reported in multiple tables. 3 | Results The scoping review identified 7914 unique sources of evidence. After excluding 2100 duplicates, 5814 sources were screened during title/abstract screening, and 137 of those were reviewed in full. Ultimately, 45 sources were included for data extraction and synthesis (Figure 1). 3.1 | Paper Characteristics Of the 45 papers, 39 were published after 2015, three papers between 2011 and 2015 and three papers between 2005 and 2010. Fifteen papers mentioned receiving funding for their study, and 33 papers had the first author as the corresponding author. The majority were from the United States of America (USA) (N = 25), followed by the United Kingdom (N = 4), Canada and Italy (each N = 2), and one study from each of Germany, Poland, Iraq, Jordan, Iran and Australia. Three studies were conducted in both the USA and Canada, and an- other three were from multiple countries around the world. In two studies, the primary author was in the Netherlands, yet the participants were from multiple countries in Asia, Australia, Europe, and North and South America .
