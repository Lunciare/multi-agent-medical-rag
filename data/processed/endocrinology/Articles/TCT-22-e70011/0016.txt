Although ef- fective for teaching basics, didactic methods alone may not equip trainees with the diverse skills needed in PE because of the passive learning role and lower knowledge retention . Educational strategies should be carefully selected to meet learning outcomes effectively. The ESPE, with ISPAD support, developed an interactive e-­learning platform offering comprehensive resources. Ng et al. found we- binars on this platform to be well-­received globally, whereas Kranenburg et al. showed that DSD-­related modules improved holistic patient management. Similarly, Bograin et al. highlighted the benefits of e-­learning's accessibility and low cost, especially for low-­ and middle-­income countries. Despite all these advantages, Ionescu et al. stressed the need for comprehensive utilization of e-­learning methods to maximize benefits. Advancement in diabetes technologies such as insulin pumps and continuous glucose monitoring necessitates improved training methods for trainees and their supervisors. As ac- tive participation and collaboration significantly increase re- tention rates , simulation-­based teaching, which engages learners actively, has been proven to be highly effective. Ken Masters's studies show that recall is highest (~ 90%) with simulated experience and lowest (~ 20%) with lectures.
