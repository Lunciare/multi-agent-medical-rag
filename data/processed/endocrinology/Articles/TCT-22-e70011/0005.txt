Five reviewers conducted title/abstract screening. Before the actual screening, a pilot with 100 articles was undertaken on Rayyan software to identify any initial areas of con- flict among the reviewers and to ensure that the inclusion and exclusion criteria were comprehensive. A 95% agreement was achieved, surpassing the 75% target. Each article was screened by two randomly assigned, blinded reviewers on Covidence, with conflicts resolved by a third expert reviewer (E.B. or M.A.). After completing title/abstract screening, results were recorded in Excel. Each of the five reviewers were assigned two random articles, ensuring each article was reviewed twice.
