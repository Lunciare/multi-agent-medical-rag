An international syllabus codesigned by ESPE for training in PE has recently been developed to stan- dardize training programs, define knowledge and skill stan- dards, and improve the quality of care provided . The ESPE, International Society for Pediatric Endocrinology and Diabetes funded by the World Diabetes Foundation, established the Paediatric Endocrinology Training Centre for Africa (PETCA) to provide and maintain high-­quality training, research, and clinical practice in PE for physicians in sub-­Saharan Africa . This scoping review examines current training and assessment strategies in PE education, identifying key focus areas and meth- odologies, and is the first to concentrate on PE education among medical trainees.
