Our review found only nine studies utilizing simulation, all demonstrating its value in enhancing clinical judgement and technical skills, especially for complex topics like DSD and pubertal assessment. “Advancement in diabetes technologies such as insu- lin pumps and continuous glucose monitoring necessi- tates improved training methods for trainees and their supervisors.” Assessment methods also play an important role in shap- ing the learning experience and outcomes of trainees. The prevalence of knowledge-­based assessments such as MCQs highlights a focus on didactic teaching. However, the com- plexity of PE care requires comprehensive assessment beyond knowledge retention.
