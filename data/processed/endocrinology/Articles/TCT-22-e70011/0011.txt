After completion, 16 of 17 residents requested additional simulated cases in the curriculum. 3.5 | Assessment Metrics Most of the studies assessed knowledge (Table S2). Physical examination was the second most frequently assessed skill , with all studies focusing on external genitalia from anatom- ical or pubertal perspectives. Of these, five used mannequins, whereas one study employed an interactive lecture, posters and summary cards . Trainee skills were assessed via question- naires administered before, immediately after and 3 months fol- lowing the intervention. 3.5.1 | Specifics of Each Metric Knowledge tests were the most frequent assessment metric used across 25 studies (Table 2). Most studies did not specify the exact test used, but nine studies used multiple choice questions (MCQs) exclusively. Other knowledge tests included open-­ended questions, mixed short answers and MCQs, and case-­based sce- narios with a nonspecified test. Error/accuracy was used in five studies. One study used a scor- ing system to assess participant performance in classifying nor- mal versus abnormal prepubescent female genital exam findings from photographs, diagnosing abnormalities and locating the area of abnormality on the image .
