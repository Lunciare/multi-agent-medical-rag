A health sciences librarian (J.-­A.P.) assisted in developing a search strategy to identify relevant literature on PE training and assessment. Database searches were done from January 2005 to September 2022 and included Medline, Embase, Emcare, Cochrane Central and Web of Science Core Collection (please see Appendix S1 for Medline search strategy), with an updated search until July 2024 using Medline and Embase. The search used subject head- ings and keywords. Results were compiled, and duplicates were removed using Covidence software (Covidence system- Australia). Full-­text sources were identified through back- ward citation searching. 2.2 | Study Selection 2.2.1 | Inclusion and Exclusion Criteria Studies were included if they (1) focused on medical stu- dents, residents or fellows; (2) addressed medical education; (3) focused on PE; (4) discussed training and/or assessment strategies (e.g., educational interventions and assessment of participants' knowledge/skills); (5) were empirical in nature (experimental, quasiexperimental, and qualitative designs); and (6) were published in English.
