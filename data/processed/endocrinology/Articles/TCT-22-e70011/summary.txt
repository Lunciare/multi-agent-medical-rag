Pediatric endocrinology (PE) is a subspecialty that focuses on diagnosing, treating and managing endocrine disorders in children and adolescents, which can affect growth, develop- ment and overall health. Early diagnosis and treatment of these disorders are crucial for physical and mental well-­being [1–3]. Common conditions managed in PE include diabetes, growth abnormalities, thyroid issues, congenital adrenal hyperplasia (CAH) and precocious puberty, with diabetes being the most prevalent in North America . Given the multidisciplinary nature of PE, training is complex, encompassing didactic, clini- cal and simulation-­based methods .
