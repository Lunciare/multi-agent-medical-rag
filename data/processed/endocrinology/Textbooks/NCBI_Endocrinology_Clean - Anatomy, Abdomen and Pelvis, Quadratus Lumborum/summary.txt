Anatomy, Abdomen and Pelvis, Quadratus Lumborum
KEYWORDS: lumborum, quadratus, quadratus lumborum, muscle, lumbar, pain, muscles, transverse, vertebrae, abdominal, iliac, somites, form, lumbar vertebrae, lateral

Introduction The quadratus lumborum is a muscle in the posterior inferior trunk lateral to the spine (see Image . Abdominopelvic Muscles). This muscle attaches to the iliac crest, the transverse processes of lumbar vertebrae 1 to 4, and the 12th rib. The quadratus lumborum's complex organization makes it difficult to precisely identify its actions through the contraction of its fibers. This muscle is an integral part of the thoracolumbar fascia. Uncertainty persists about whether a quadratus lumborum abnormality is the primary source of back pain.
