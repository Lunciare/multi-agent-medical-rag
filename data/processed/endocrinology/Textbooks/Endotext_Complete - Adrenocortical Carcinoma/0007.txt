The median overall survival (OS) of all ACC patients is about 3-4 years ( 5 , 6 ). The prognosis is, however, relatively heterogeneous. Complete surgical resection provides the only means of cure ( 5 , 6 , 15 , 16 ). In addition to radical surgery, disease stage, proliferative activity/tumor grade, and cortisol excess are independent prognostic parameters ( 5 , 6 , 15 , 16 ). The five-year survival rate is 60-80% for tumors confined to the adrenal space, 35-50% for locally advanced disease, and significantly lower in cases of metastatic disease ranging from 0% to 28% ( 5 - 7 ). ENSAT staging is considered slightly superior to the Union for International Cancer Control (UICC) staging. Additionally, the association between hypercortisolism and mortality was consistent.
