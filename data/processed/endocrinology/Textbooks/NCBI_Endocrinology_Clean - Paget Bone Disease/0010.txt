Paget Bone Disease
KEYWORDS: bone, disease, patients, paget, paget disease, disorder, usually, common, pain, fractures, skull, diagnosed, patient, treatment, following

Patients with polyostotic disease have poorer outcomes than those with monostotic disease. The morbidity is usually due to fractures, chronic pain, bone deformity, and neurological complications. Once the patient develops sarcomatous degeneration, the survival rate may be very poor. Complications Complications include the following: Secondary osteoarthritis Vertigo Deafness Dental malocclusion Tinnitus Cranial nerve compression Basilar invagination Cauda equina syndrome Fractures Hashimoto thyroiditis Osteopetrosis Dupuytren contracture Development of osteosarcoma High-output cardiac failure
