Endocrine Sex Hormones Endocrine sex hormones, due to their intricate and overlapping properties in both genders, are explained and discussed from a gender standpoint rather than by organ. Each of the sex hormones is expressed and active in both males and females; it is the levels and concentrations that help develop and define the external and internal function of the human body. Studies and patient cases have shown mutations in classical male hormones or classical female hormones can still lead to pathology when absent in either gender.
