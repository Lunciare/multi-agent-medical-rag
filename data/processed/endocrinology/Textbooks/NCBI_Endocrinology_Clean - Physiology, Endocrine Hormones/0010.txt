Thyroid-stimulating hormone (thyrotropin) (TSH) – TSH is synthesized and released from cells within the anterior pituitary gland, known as thyrotrophs. This hormone is composed of 2 subunits: 1 alpha and 1 beta. TSH is 1 of the 4 hormones which share the same alpha unit. The beta unit makes TSH unique and determines its specificity within the human body.
