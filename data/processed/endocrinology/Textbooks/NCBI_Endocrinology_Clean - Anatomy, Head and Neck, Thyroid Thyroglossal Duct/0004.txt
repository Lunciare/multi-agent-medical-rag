Due to the possibility of reoccurrences, the procedure requires excision of the mid-portion of the hyoid bone and a small amount of tongue tissue. The incision wound is then closed-up with an absorbable suture, and the dermabond is used for skin closure. In case of a malignant mass, depending on the case and team of surgeons at hand, the procedure will or will not be applied. Radioiodine and levothyroxine therapy is usually the most common treatments in these cases and is also the treatment of choice. Partial resection or complete resection of the thyroid might also be part of the procedure. Clinical Significance The clinical relevance of the thyroglossal duct adheres to its embryologic function.
