In pregnancy with type 1 diabetes, the incidence of mild and severe hypoglycemia is highest in early pregnancy, although metabolic control is usually tighter in the last part of preg­ nancy. Predictors for severe hypoglycemia are history of severe hypoglycemia and impaired awareness (166). Chronic kidney disease can be found in up to 23% of patients with diabetes. Chronic kidney disease is an independent risk factor for hypoglycemia and augments the risk already present in people with diabetes (167,168).
