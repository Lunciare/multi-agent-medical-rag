17–1 CHAPTER 17 ACUTE METABOLIC COMPLICATIONS IN DIABETES Arleta Rewers, MD, PhD Dr. Arleta Rewers is Associate Professor, Department of Pediatrics at University of Colorado Denver School of Medicine, Aurora, CO. Received in final form March 12, 2016. SUMMARY Diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA), hyperglycemic hyperosmolar state (HHS), lactic acidosis (LA), and hypoglycemia are acute and potentially life-threatening complications of diabetes. DKA and severe hypoglycemia are more common in type 1 diabetes, while HHS without ketoacidosis is associated more frequently with type 2 diabetes. In the United States, the SEARCH for Diabetes in Youth study reported that 29% of patients age <20 years with type 1 diabetes and 10% with type 2 diabetes presented in DKA at diagnosis.
