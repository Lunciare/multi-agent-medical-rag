Anatomy, Abdomen and Pelvis: Gallbladder
KEYWORDS: gallbladder, duct, cystic, common, hepatic, bile, right, bile duct, cystic duct, liver, common bile, artery, surgical, biliary, node

Introduction The gallbladder is a pear-shaped organ in the right upper quadrant of the abdomen (see Image. The Gallbladder and Bile Ducts Laid Open). It measures approximately 7 cm to 10 cm in length and 4 cm in width. Even though the organ is small, it is a common cause of abdominal pain due to gallstones, which often require surgical removal of the organ. Anatomically, the gallbladder is located anteriorly on the undersurface of liver segments IV and V. There are many variants of the anatomy of the biliary system, making exact knowledge of these anatomic possibilities crucial when performing gallbladder and biliary surgery.
