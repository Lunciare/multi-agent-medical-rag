Osteoporosis in Spinal Cord Injuries

Beyond 2 to 5 years postinjury, controversy remains in the literature concerning ongoing decremental BMD rates into perpetuity. Some reports suggest that bone loss plateaus after 3 to 5 years, while others demonstrate chronic decreases in BMD. Complications The major complication of SCI-induced osteoporosis is the fragility fracture. The majority of fragility fractures occur at the sublesion levels, especially in long bones. Many fragility fractures in patients with SCI occur spontaneously and can be missed or delayed in diagnosis.
