A recent study randomized older patients (at least 65 years of age) with subclinical hyperthyroidism to radioactive iodine or long-term methimazole therapy. The methimazole group had higher rates of euthyroidism and lower rates of hypothyroidism, suggesting that methimazole is an attractive treatment option for subclinical hypothyroidism in older patients ( 93 ).
