Ezetimibe An observational database study reported that patients taking ezetimibe had significantly reduced odds for SARS-CoV-2 hospitalization (OR=0.513, 95% CI 0.375-0.688) ( 126 ). The mechanism for this effect is not clear and randomized clinical trials are required to definitively demonstrate a beneficial effect of ezetimibe. PCSK9 Inhibitors In a randomized trial 60 patients with severe COVID-19 were treated with a single 140-mg subcutaneous injection of evolocumab or placebo ( 127 ). Patients randomized to the PCSK9 inhibitor group had lower rates of death or need for intubation vs. placebo (23.3% vs 53.3%, risk difference: -30%; 95% CI: -53.40% to -6.59%). Patients with more intense inflammation also had better survival with PCSK9 inhibition vs placebo (risk difference: -37.50%; 95% CI: -68.20% to -6.70%). Larger randomized trials are needed to confirm these results. Fibrates Fibrates have anti-inflammatory properties ( 128 ). In a cohort study fenofibrate did not reduce the severity of COVID-19 infections ( 129 ). Moreover, a randomized trial comparing 145 mg of fenofibrate (n= 351) vs. placebo (n= 350) for 10 days demonstrated that fenofibrate had no significant effect on various clinically relevant outcomes ( 130 ). In patients treated with tocilizumab the use of fibrates should be suspended ( 107 ). Omega-3-Fatty Acids Omega-3-fatty acids have anti-inflammatory properties ( 131 ). In a randomized trial of 2 grams per day of Docosahexaenoic acid (DHA) + Eicosapentaenoic acid (EPA) for 2 weeks an improvement in the clinical symptoms of COVID-19 infection and reduced markers of inflammation (C-reactive protein and erythrocyte sedimentation rate) were observed ( 132 ). In another randomized trial the administration of 400mg EPA and 200mg DHA per day decreased severity and improved survival in critically ill patients with COVID-19 infection ( 133 ). Additional studies are needed to confirm these intriguing results. Niacin and Bempedoic acid There is no information of the effect of these drugs on COVID-19 Infections.
