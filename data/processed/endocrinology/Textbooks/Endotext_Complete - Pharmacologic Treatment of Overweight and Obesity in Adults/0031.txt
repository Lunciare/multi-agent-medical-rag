Because lifestyle modifications have been limited in their success in weight loss maintenance, pharmacotherapy plays an important role in achieving clinically significant weight loss and preventing the development or exacerbation of comorbid conditions. As society and the scientific community furthers our understanding of obesity, obesity management will evolve to match the standard of care of other chronic conditions, recognizing polypharmacotherapy as a vital component of comprehensive care.
