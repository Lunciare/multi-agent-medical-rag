Moreover, it is highly sensitive for the detection of bony metastases and it might obviate the need for additional radiologic studies. In addition to a higher sensitivity, other advantages of 68 Ga-DOTA-SSA PET include patient convenience (imaging sessions take 70-90 minutes instead of 24 hours), lower radiation exposure, utility in finding unknown primary PNENs and it can lead to changes in treatment plans in about 33-41% of the patients ( 67 , 89 - 93 ). 68 Ga-DOTA-SSA PET might also be better at quantifying SSTR expression which facilitates targeted therapy such as PRRT ( 89 ). Consequently, 68 Ga-DOTA-SSA PET quickly became the imaging modality of choice ( 67 , 68 , 71 ). However, similar to other imaging studies, false positives may occur due to pancreatic uncinate process activity, inflammation, osteoblastic activity, and splenosis ( 94 ). No doubt other PET agents will follow since PNENs express a variety of receptors for which there are potential ligands.
