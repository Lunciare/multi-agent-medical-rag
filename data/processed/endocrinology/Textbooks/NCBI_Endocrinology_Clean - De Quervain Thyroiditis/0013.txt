Complications Most of the time, subacute thyroiditis resolves completely, and rarely it can cause permanent hypothyroidism. Some patients may have severe symptoms from hypothyroidism, but usually, it is transient. Rarely subacute thyroiditis has been known to cause thyroid storm, ventricular tachycardia, and fever of unknown origin.
