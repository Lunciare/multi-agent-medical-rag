Complications Musculoskeletal Complications Both GH and IGF-1 stimulate the production of periosteal bone formation. IGF-1 creates somatic growth by binding to the insulin-like growth factor-1 Receptor (IGF-1R), which is relatively ubiquitous. IGF-1R is a receptor tyrosine kinase that brings about phosphorylation and activation of several intracellular signaling pathways, one of which is the AKT pathway activation that results in somatic cell growth and proliferation. In the craniofacial region, this manifests as characteristic features such as prognathism, teeth separation, jaw thickening, frontal bossing, and nasal bone hypertrophy. The changes noted in the extremities are due to a combination of increased soft tissue, cartilage, bone overgrowth, and deformity.
