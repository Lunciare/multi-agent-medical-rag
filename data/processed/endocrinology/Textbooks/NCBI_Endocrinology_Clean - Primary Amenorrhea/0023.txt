Depending on the etiology, additional testing and monitoring are frequently indicated as well; a detailed discussion on specific management points for all of the various potential diagnoses is beyond the scope of this document. Outflow Tract Abnormalities Patients with some types of outflow obstructions (eg, imperforate hymen, cervical stenosis) can be treated with a surgical correction to relieve the obstruction. However, they may have residual issues, including endometriosis and pelvic adhesions due to the initial obstruction.
