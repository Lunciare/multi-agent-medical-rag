CONCLUSION After 60 years of its discovery, several issues of lipoprotein(a) remain unresolved, including its function and metabolism. While the causality of lipoprotein(a) in ASCVD and CAVD has now been firmly established by epidemiological, genetic association, and Mendelian randomization studies, the next challenge is to prove that lowering lipoprotein(a) levels also leads to cardiovascular benefit in patients with elevated lipoprotein(a) levels.
