Osteoporosis in Males
KEYWORDS: men, bone, osteoporosis, risk, fractures, treatment, hip, fracture, women, bmd, males, patients, years, loss, therapy

Diagnostic tools include bone mineral density testing and laboratory evaluations to identify secondary causes. Screening is recommended for men aged 70 and older or younger men with risk factors, although screening guidelines vary internationally. Management involves lifestyle modifications and pharmacological treatments. Regular monitoring is essential to evaluate treatment efficacy and progression. Despite advancements in awareness and treatment options, osteoporosis in men remains underdiagnosed and inadequately managed. This activity for healthcare professionals is designed to enhance the learner's competence in recognizing risk factors for osteoporosis in males, performing the recommended osteoporosis screening and diagnostic evaluation, and implementing an effective interprofessional management approach to improve patient outcomes.
