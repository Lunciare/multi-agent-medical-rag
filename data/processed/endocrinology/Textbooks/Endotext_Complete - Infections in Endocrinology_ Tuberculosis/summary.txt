Infections in Endocrinology: Tuberculosis
KEYWORDS: patients, pubmed, tuberculosis, adrenal, pituitary, thyroid, cases, tuberculous, free, disease, hyponatremia, primary, vitamin, hypercalcemia, involvement

ABSTRACT Mycobacterium tuberculosis, the etiological agent of tuberculosis (TB), is responsible for the largest number of deaths worldwide caused by a single organism. Over 25% of the world population is infected with M. tuberculosis, though active infections account only for a small percentage. Though some degree of endocrine dysfunction is invariable in all patients with TB, clinically significant endocrinopathy other than glucose intolerance is rare. This chapter reviews endocrine dysfunction and endocrinopathies associated with TB infection related to the adrenal, thyroid and pituitary glands. Additionally, functional derangement of sodium and calcium homeostasis is also covered.
