Semen Analysis
KEYWORDS: sperm, semen, low, normal, male, sperm count, count, motility, analysis, low sperm, infertility, laboratory, high, testosterone, spermatozoa

Introduction Semen analysis is a laboratory test performed to evaluate male fertility. Infertility is defined as the inability to conceive after 1 year of unprotected sexual intercourse. About 15% of couples of reproductive age experience infertility. The assessment of male infertility involves a comprehensive evaluation, including a detailed medical and sexual history, a thorough physical examination, and semen analyses. The male factor significantly contributes to about 30% of infertility cases and is a contributing factor in about half of cases. During ejaculation, semen is released in boluses.
