Introduction The inferior hypogastric plexus is also known as the pelvic ganglion. Classic anatomical studies have provided few details of the inferior hypogastric plexus morphology or the location and nature of the associated nerves. The fusion of the pelvic splanchnic nerves, sacral splanchnic nerves, superior hypogastric plexus, and visceral afferent fibers form the inferior hypogastric plexus. Sympathetic System, Abdominal Portion. Studies have also shown the contribution of spinal nerves S2-S5 to the inferior hypogastric plexus. Dissection of the inferior hypogastric plexus is difficult due to its location, the multiplicity of its sympathetic and parasympathetic roots, and the complex distribution of its terminal branches.
