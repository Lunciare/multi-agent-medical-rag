Multiple studies have investigated possible nerve blocks treating chronic and more acute pain in this area. Hypogastric plexus blocks relieve pelvic pain that potentially refers to the colon, lower intestines, bladder, uterus and ovaries, prostate or testicles, and other parts of the pelvis. Surgeons performing hysterectomy must remember the inferior hypogastric plexus as disruption of nerves may cause sexual, genitourinary, and colonic motility dysfunction. Radical hysterectomies may result in disruption of pelvic autonomic nerves. Other Issues The inferior hypogastric plexus sends small filaments to innervate the rectum through the pre-hypogastric fascia and the anterior portion of the Denonvilliers' fascia. We can identify 3 small plexuses from the inferior hypogastric plexus, such as the vesical plexus, vaginal-rectal plexus, and inferior rectal plexus.[12]
