In the near future, genetic testing will likely be used on a wide scale to screen children and family members at-risk of CVD with the goal of prevention. Given its current trajectory, genetic testing is becoming increasingly critical in our ability to provide accurate risk assessment as well as age appropriate and timely intervention to help guide our efforts in educating and managing youth with disorders of lipid and lipoprotein metabolism.
