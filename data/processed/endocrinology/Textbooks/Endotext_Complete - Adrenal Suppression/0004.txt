Thus, prednisolone in a dose of 5mg given at night before bedtime and 2.5mg in the morning will produce more marked HPA axis suppression compared to 2.5mg at night and 5mg in the morning. Higher evening doses block early morning ACTH surge whereas tissues sensitivity to glucocorticoids is increased in the evening and early night hours.
