Importantly, half-life and other pharmacokinetic parameters vary based on factors such as chemotherapy, CKD, age, and route of administration. Administration Available Dosage Forms and Strengths ESAs are typically sourced from transfected Chinese hamster ovary cells (CHOs) during large-scale manufacturing protocols. An isotonic solution buffers the ESA powder, which the provider can administer intravenously or subcutaneously. Darbepoetin is available in 25 μg, 40 μg, 60 μg, 100 μg, and 200 μg single-dose powder vials and premixed, single-dose syringes with the following powder and buffer dilutions: 10 μg in 0.4 mL, 25 μg in 0.42 mL, 40 μg in 0.4 mL, 60 μg in 0.3 mL, 100 μg in 0.5 mL, 300 μg in 0.6 mL, and 500 μg in 1 mL. Epoetin alfa is available in 2,000 IU/mL, 3,000 IU/mL, 4,000 IU/mL, and 10,000 IU/mL single-dose vials.
