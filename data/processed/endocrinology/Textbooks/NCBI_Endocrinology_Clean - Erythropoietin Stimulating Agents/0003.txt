Providers in the United States should review approvals and indications based on local regulatory authorities. When prescribing ESAs, providers should aim to elevate hemoglobin to the lowest concentration necessary to avoid or reduce the need for red blood cell transfusions. If no response is observed within 6 to 8 weeks, ESA therapy should be discontinued, and the patient should be reevaluated for underlying causes, such as tumor progression or iron deficiency.
