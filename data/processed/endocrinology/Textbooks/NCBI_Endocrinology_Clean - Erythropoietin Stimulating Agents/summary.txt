Erythropoietin Stimulating Agents
KEYWORDS: patients, esas, anemia, alfa, esa, epoetin, epo, darbepoetin, hemoglobin, epoetin alfa, polyethylene, iron, methoxy polyethylene, methoxy, therapy

Indications Endogenous erythropoietin (EPO) is a glycoprotein hormone that stimulates red blood cell production. The peritubular cells of the renal cortex produce most of the EPO found in a human adult, with the liver serving as the primary site of fetal production. EPO production can also occur in small quantities in the spleen, liver, bone marrow, lung, and brain. A reduced partial pressure of oxygen (pO 2 ) directly stimulates EPO production. Reduced hemoglobin levels also stimulate EPO production, albeit indirectly. Erythropoiesis stimulating agents (ESA) are recombinant forms of EPO produced synthetically via recombinant DNA technology in cell cultures.
