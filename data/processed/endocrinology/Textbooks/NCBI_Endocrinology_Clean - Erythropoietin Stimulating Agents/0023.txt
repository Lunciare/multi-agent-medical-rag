However, there was a higher incidence of adverse events such as hypertension, vascular access thrombosis, and stroke. Current guidelines advocate for tailoring target hemoglobin levels to individual needs. Optimal levels typically range between 11 and 12 g/dL, considering that higher doses of ESAs can increase the risk of thrombotic events. An interprofessional team approach and communication among clinicians (MDs, DOs, NPs, PAs), hematologists, pharmacists, and nurses are crucial to decreasing potential adverse effects and improving patient outcomes related to ESA.
