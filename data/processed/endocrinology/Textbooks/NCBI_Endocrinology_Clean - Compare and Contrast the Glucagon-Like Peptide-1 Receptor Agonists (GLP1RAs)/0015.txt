The injectable medication should be administered in the patient’s thigh, abdomen, or upper arm. Exenatide extended-release and dulaglutide can be taken as soon as the patient remembers, as long as the next scheduled dose is at least 3 days later. If outside these parameters, the patient should be instructed to skip the dose entirely and continue with the next regularly scheduled dosing day.
