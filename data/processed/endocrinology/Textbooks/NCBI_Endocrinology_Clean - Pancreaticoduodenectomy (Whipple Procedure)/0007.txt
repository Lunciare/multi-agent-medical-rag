Staging Laparoscopy Before proceeding to laparotomy for malignant disease, a staging laparoscopy is conducted to exclude metastatic disease and ensure resectability. Staging laparoscopy has shown high success rates in detecting metastatic disease and can identify occult peritoneal surface disease radiologically. Typically, 2 or 3 5-mm ports are used to thoroughly inspect all parietal and visceral peritoneal surfaces.
