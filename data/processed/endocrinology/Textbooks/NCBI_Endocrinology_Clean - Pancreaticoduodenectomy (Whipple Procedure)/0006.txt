Optimizing preoperative nutrition with enteral or parenteral supplementation Correcting hyperbilirubinemia in patients that have biliary obstruction, which usually involves endoscopic biliary stenting A thorough discussion of the procedure's risks and benefits with the patient Preparing for postoperative pain control with anesthesia colleagues: The use of adjuncts such as epidural or intrathecal analgesia, nerve blocks, and other systemic nonopioid agents is essential.
