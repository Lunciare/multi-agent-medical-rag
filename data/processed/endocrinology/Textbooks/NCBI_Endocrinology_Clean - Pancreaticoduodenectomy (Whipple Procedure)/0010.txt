The cut margin is sent for frozen section examination, and the bile duct is temporarily sutured to prevent bile leakage. Dissection of the PV continues toward the pancreatic head, including nodal tissue in the hepatoduodenal ligament distal to the common bile duct division. Retropancreatic tunnel creation: The middle colic vein is identified and traced back to its junction with the SMV, or the right gastroepiploic vein is traced backward. In some patients, a common insertion of the right gastroepiploic and middle colic veins forms the trunk of Henle. The anterior surface of the SMV is gently separated from the pancreatic neck, creating a retropancreatic tunnel. This step is avoided if the SMV or PV is involved with the tumor. The cranial portion of the tunnel is created by separating the PV from the pancreatic neck and connecting it to the initial retropancreatic dissection over the SMV.
