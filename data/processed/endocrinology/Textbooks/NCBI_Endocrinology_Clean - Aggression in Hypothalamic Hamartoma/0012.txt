A recent trial reported that 37% of patients were seizure-free after gamma knife radiation therapy, and 60% had a dramatic improvement in symptoms. Radiation damage can also occur to the surrounding vital structures, such as the optic tract. Possible complications associated with surgical resection include pituitary endocrinopathies and hypothalamic obesity syndrome.
