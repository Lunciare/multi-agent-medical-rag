Anatomy, Abdomen and Pelvis: Hepatoduodenal Ligament
KEYWORDS: ligament, hepatoduodenal ligament, hepatoduodenal, artery, hepatic, liver, portal, blood, hepatic artery, vein, lesser, common, portal vein, supply, disease

Introduction The hepatoduodenal ligament is a thick anatomical structure wrapped in the peritoneum that constitutes part of the lesser omentum. The hepatoduodenal ligament runs from the porta hepatis to the proximal 2 cm of the duodenum. The hepatic artery proper, common bile duct, and portal vein run through the ligament near its free edge to reach the liver. These 3 structures are often referred to as the portal triad. The hepatic artery proper is a branch of the common hepatic artery originating from the celiac trunk. The hepatic portal vein is the main drainage site of the spleen and the small and large intestines.
