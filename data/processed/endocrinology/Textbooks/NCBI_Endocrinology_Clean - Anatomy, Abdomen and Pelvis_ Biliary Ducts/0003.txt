Structure and Function The primary function of the biliary tract is to transport and release the bile. The gallbladder stores and concentrates bile. Bile assists with the absorption and digestion of fats and fat-soluble vitamins in addition to the removal of lipid-soluble waste products. The main components of bile are cholesterol, bile salts, and bilirubin. Bilirubin, responsible for the signature yellow-green color of bile, is also excreted in bile and is responsible for the pigmentation of feces. Embryology The biliary ducts originate embryologically from the foregut. Early in the fourth week of development, the hepatic diverticulum arises from the endodermal epithelium of the ventral foregut. The hepatic diverticulum serves as the anlage for the liver, extrahepatic ducts, gallbladder, and ventral pancreas.
