Clinical Vocabulary Biliary colic is the intermittent, dull RUQ pain due to transient blockage of bile flow Cholelithiasis means gallstones present within the gallbladder Choledocholithiasis means gallstones situate within the common bile duct Gallstone pancreatitis is pancreatic inflammation from gallstone blockage of the pancreatic duct Cholecystitis is inflammation of the gallbladder due to prolonged obstruction of the cystic duct Cholecystectomy is the surgical removal of the gallbladder Cholangitis is an infection of the bile ducts Cholangiocarcinoma is an aggressive cancer of the bile ducts
