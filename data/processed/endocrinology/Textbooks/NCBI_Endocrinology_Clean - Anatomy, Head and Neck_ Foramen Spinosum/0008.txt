tegmen tympani, internal acoustic meatus, foramen ovale, and, occasionally, the foramen spinosum. Trauma to the foramen spinosum or temporal bone, whether from road traffic accidents or direct impact, can sever the middle meningeal artery along its intracranial course, including segments near the foramen spinosum. This vascular injury may result in bleeding between the skull and dura mater, producing an epidural hematoma.
