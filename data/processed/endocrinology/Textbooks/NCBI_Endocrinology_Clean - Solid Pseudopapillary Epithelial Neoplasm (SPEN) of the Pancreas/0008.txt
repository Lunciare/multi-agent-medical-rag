Complications Most complications are associated with compression of adjacent structures, resulting in jaundice and gastric outlet obstruction. In the postoperative period, complications include pancreatic fistulas, pancreatitis, steatorrhea, infections, bleeding, biliary fistulas, and ileus. Additionally, the development of diabetes mellitus is seen in less than 1% of patients. Although rare, close follow-up is recommended to monitor for recurrence or metastasis.
