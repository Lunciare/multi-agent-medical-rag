Only four prescription drugs are currently approved for long-term use in weight reduction: Orlistat (Xenical TM ), Lorcaserin (Belviq TM ), Phentermine-Topiramate (Qsymia TM ), and Bupropion–Naltrexone (Contrave TM ). Orlistat is available for both prescription (Xenical TM ) and over-the-counter at a lower dose as Alli TM . Over-the-counter Alli TM is available only to adults aged 18 and older, and is a half-dose version of prescription Orlistat ( http://www.myalli.com ) (35). Orlistat operates at the level of the gut to inhibit pancreatic lipase, blocking the absorption of about one third of fat consumed.
