Low-fat diets appear to be associated with greater reductions in low-density lipoprotein cholesterol ( 24 , 25 , 29 ), compared to low-carbohydrate diets. The latter diets, by contrast, are associated with greater reductions in triglycerides ( 26 , 29 - 33 ), increases in high-density lipoprotein cholesterol ( 25 , 26 , 29 - 33 ), and improvements in HbA 1C in patients with type 2 diabetes ( 33 ).
