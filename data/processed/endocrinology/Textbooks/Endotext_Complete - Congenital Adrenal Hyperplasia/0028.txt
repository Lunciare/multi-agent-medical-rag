CYP17A1 encodes an enzyme that expresses both 17α-hydroxylase/17,20-lyase activities. Normal CYP17A1 activity, particularly the lyase reactions, depends on the formation of a ternary CYP17A1:CYB5:POR complex ( 146 ). Several rare genetic variants in CYP17A1 (E305G, R347H, and R358Q) are known to disrupt this complex, thus selectively impairing 17,20-lyase activity, but 17α-hydroxylase activity remains normal ( 152 - 154 ). These patients have isolated 17,20-lyase activity characterized by abnormal sexual development without hypertension or hypokalemia. The identical phenotype is produced by inactivating mutations in CYB5 . DIAGNOSIS Newborn Screening Diagnosis of 21OHD is most often accomplished by a combination of clinical manifestations and hormonal evaluation. For classic 21OHD, randomly timed blood samples show very high 17OHP by immunoassay, the basis of newborn-screening programs. The aim of screening is to identify patients who are at risk for salt wasting and permit early therapeutic intervention in infants. As of 2009, all 50 states in the United States and numerous developed countries perform newborn screening for this type of CAH within about 48 hours of birth. Normative reference values specific to each laboratory are based on weight and/or gestational age. A small number of programs perform a second screening test at about 2 weeks of life and these mainly detect non-salt-wasting forms of 21OHD. Since 17OHP immunoassay has a poor positive predictive value ( 155 , 156 )], second tier liquid chromatography followed by tandem mass spectrometry measuring various steroid panels and ratios ( 156 ) or 21-deoxycortisol ( 157 ) has been instituted with improved results. Pilot programs have shown that many treatable inherited conditions are effectively detected through genome sequencing ( 158 ). One Chinese newborn screening program has utilized targeted long-range sequencing genotyping as a second screen for CAH with a positive predictive value of 29% and a turn-around time of 10 days ( 159 ). Although not the primary target of hormonal newborn screening, other forms of CAH are sometimes detected. These may include NC-CAH, which does not typically require early treatment, as this condition is not life-threatening and is slowly progressive in terms of virilization. Additionally, deficiencies of CYP11B1, HSD3B2, and CYP17A1 may be detected if steroid profiling is employed.
