Adrenal sex hormone excess and therapeutic glucocorticoids respectively influence bone mineral accrual in positive and negative ways. Retrospective analyses of classic CAH patients have demonstrated varied results concerning bone density and fracture risk depending on age, sex, CYP21A2 variant, glucocorticoid type, cumulative dose, and onset of treatment. Fractures risk was increased 1.6-fold among more than 700 young adult 21OHD patients compared with Swedish controls ( 48 ). Genetics The gene encoding the 21-hydroxylase enzyme, CYP21A2 , is located on the short arm of chromosome 6 in the human lymphocyte antigen (HLA) complex. CYP21A2 and its homologue, the pseudogene CYP21A1P , alternate with two genes, C4B and C4A that encode the two isoforms of the fourth component (C4) of serum complement ( 49 ) ( Figure 5 ). Thus, each C4 and CYP21A gene pair lies on a 30 kilobase (kb) segment of DNA that is duplicated in most individuals. The duplicated segment also includes the 3’ end of the serine-threonine kinase 19 (STK19) gene ( 50 )and the 3’ end of the tenascin-X gene encoded on the opposite DNA strand ( 51 ). CYP21A2 and CYP21A1P share 98% sequence homology in exons and approximately 96% sequence homology in introns, but CYP21A1P carries 10 different mutations that collectively render the encoded protein inactive ( 52 , 53 ).
