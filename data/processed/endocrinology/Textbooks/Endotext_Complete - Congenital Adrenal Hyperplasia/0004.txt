ZONA FASCICULATA In the zona fasciculata, the pathway to cortisol biosynthesis proceeds with pregnenolone being first converted to 17α hydroxypregnenolone by CYP17A1 (P450c17), 17α hydroxylase/17,20-lyase, followed by conversion to 17α hydroxyprogesterone (17OHP) by HSD3B2. CYP21A2 normally converts this to 11-deoxycortisol, which is then converted to cortisol by CYP11B1 (P450c11), 11β hydroxylase, an enzyme highly homologous to CYP11B2 but lacking the ability to efficiently catalyze 18-hydroxylase and 18-oxidase steps and expressed at much higher levels than that enzyme, specifically in the zona fasciculata. A ”17-deoxy” pathway also exists in the zona fasciculata, converting pregnenolone to corticosterone, which is a glucocorticoid with approximately one third the potency of cortisol. This is not normally of great physiologic significance but can assume greater importance in patients with 17-hydroxylase deficiency (see below). ZONA RETICULARIS In the zona reticularis after the onset of adrenarche, the 17,20 carbon-carbon bond of 17α hydroxypregnenolone is cleaved by the 17,20-lyase activity of CYP17A1, yielding dehydroepiandrosterone (DHEA), which is then converted to androstenedione by HSD3B2. 11-Oxygenated Adrenal Androgens In addition to DHEA, DHEA sulfate and androstenedione, the classic “adrenal androgens,” the adrenals secrete physiologically significant quantities of 11-oxygenated (oxo) androgens, mainly 11-keto-testosterone (11-KT) ( 2 , 3 ). In addition to being expressed in the zona fasciculata, CYP11B1, the 11β-hydroxylase that converts 11-deoxycortisol to cortisol, is expressed in the zona reticularis where it converts androstenedione and testosterone to 11OH-androstenedione and 11OH-testosterone, respectively. These 11-oxo steroids can be converted into the nonaromatizable 11-keto-androstenedione and 11-KT by HSD11B2 ( Figure 1 ). 11-KT may also be 5a-reduced to 11-keto-dihydrotestosterone (11-KDHT); 11-KT and 11-KDHT are androgens that bind and activate the androgen receptor with affinities similar to those of testosterone and dihydrotestosterone, respectively ( 4 - 7 ). These androgens are secreted at similar levels in men and woman ( 5 ) and are elevated in CAH and PCOS. Thus, 11-KT appears to be the dominant circulating bioactive androgen during both normal and premature adrenarche ( 8 ) as well as in classic CAH( 9 , 10 ). These 11-oxo steroids correlate with adrenal size and may be useful biomarkers for adrenal rest tumors in CAH patients ( 11 ).
