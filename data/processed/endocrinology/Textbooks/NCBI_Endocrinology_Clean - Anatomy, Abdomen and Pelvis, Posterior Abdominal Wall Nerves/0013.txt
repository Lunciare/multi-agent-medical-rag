Anatomy, Abdomen and Pelvis, Posterior Abdominal Wall Nerves
KEYWORDS: nerve, femoral, abdominal, posterior, psoas, nerves, muscle, innervation, lumbar, wall, abdominal wall, thigh, iliac, cutaneous, ilioinguinal

characterized by a triad of radiating pain to the iliac fossa, altered sensation in its distribution, and a trigger point medial to the anterosuperior iliac spine. Ilioinguinal, iliohypogastric, and intercostal nerves are potential candidates for transfer to the femoral and gluteal nerves. This procedure has succeeded in restoring some of the motor functions of the hip and knee joints, which patients lost after the spinal cord injuries.
