coma Hypoparathyroidism Hypoparathyroidism is due to reduced activity of the gland. This condition has primary and secondary classifications. Primary: There is a gland failure which results in a decrease in PTH secretion. Patients who suffer from this disorder will often need calcium supplementation Secondary: This occurs when there is surgical removal/injury of the parathyroids. This can often be accidental during neck surgery or expected after more extensive neck surgery, such as a total laryngectomy with total thyroidectomy. Radiation therapy for various neck malignancies can also lead to the destruction of the parathyroids and subsequent hypoparathyroidism.
