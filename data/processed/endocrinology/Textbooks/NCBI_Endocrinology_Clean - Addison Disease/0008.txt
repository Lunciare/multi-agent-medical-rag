Cortisol Level A low random cortisol level is characteristically seen with Addison disease. Cortisol typically follows a diurnal pattern with the highest level in the early morning; therefore, an early morning level should be obtained. However, getting an early morning cortisol level in the emergency department (ED) is impractical. Furthermore, these results are not readily available during an ED visit. A single serum cortisol level determination is insufficient to assess adrenal function. However, a morning cortisol level >18 mcg/dL is a normal finding and may exclude an Addison disease diagnosis, while a low cortisol level of <3 mcg/dL is sufficient to diagnose adrenal insufficiency.
