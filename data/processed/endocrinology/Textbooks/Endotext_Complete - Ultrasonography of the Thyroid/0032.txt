WHAT A THYROID ULTRASOUND REPORT SHOULD INCLUDE The thyroid ultrasound report must answer the question that has been posed by the clinician and not be just a routine recitation. The ultrasonographer or the thyroidologist who interprets the images should note and record in the report the features listed in Table 3 and call specific attention to the features that reveal a higher than average risk of malignancy.
