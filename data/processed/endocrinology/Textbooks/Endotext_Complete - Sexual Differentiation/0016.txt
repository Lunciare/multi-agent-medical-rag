In the mouse, SOX9 is expressed at low levels in the bipotential gonads of both sexes under SF1 regulation ( 108 ), but persists only in testicular Sertoli cells after SRY expression has peaked ( 109 - 111 ). SRY and SF1 directly bind to several sites within a 3.2-kb testis-specific enhancer (TES) or 1.4-kb of its core element (TESCO), present approximately 14 kb upstream of the Sox9 promoter and responsible for this expression pattern ( 108 , 112 ). Together with SF1, SOX9 also binds and activates TES, thus maintaining its own expression by autoregulation after transient SRY expression has ceased in the mouse.
