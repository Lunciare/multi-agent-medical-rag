Loss of function mutations in the human RSPO1 gene and Rspo1 gene ablation in mice result in the formation of ovotestes in the XX fetus probably owing to SOX9 upregulation ( 85 , 189 , 272 ). β-catenin also activates FOXL2 winged helix/forkhead transcription factor, expressed in germ and somatic cells, more strongly in the female than the male fetal gonad from the 8 th fetal week ( 273 ) and involved in granulosa cell differentiation ( 274 , 275 ). The high levels of WNT4/β-catenin and FOXL2 counteract FGF9 and SOX9, thus leading to the stabilization of the ovarian differentiation pathway ( 260 , 261 ). FOXL2 also represses SF1 expression by antagonizing WT1 in the XX mouse fetus ( 276 ). FOXL2 and FST are needed for the survival of meiotic germ cells ( 82 , 277 , 278 ). In the XY fetus, SOX9 represses FOXL2 expression in the gonad ( 279 ). Conversely, inducible deletion of Foxl2 in adult mouse ovarian follicles leads to upregulation of Sox9 and reprogramming of adult ovaries to testes ( 82 ). In goats, XX males develop in the event of a deletion in the autosomal PIS locus ( 280 ), where FOXL2 has been identified. In humans, FOXL2 mutations result in a variety of phenotypes, from streak gonads to adult ovarian failure associated with eyelid abnormalities characterized by blepharophimosis, ptosis and epicantus inversus (BPES) ( 281 ). NR2F2, also called COUP-TFII, is highly expressed during early bipotential gonad formation, especially in somatic cells and regulates pathways involved in the first wave development of pre-granulosa cells ( 282 ). Germ cell entry into meiosis is a specific feature of initial ovarian differentiation ( Table 3 , Figs. 4 and 9 ). Once stabilized by the cooperative action of WNT4 and RSPO1, cytoplasmic β-catenin migrates to the nucleus and induces the expression of FST. The latter antagonizes Activin B, thus repressing endothelial cell migration and the coelomic vessel formation, one of the earliest testis-specific events ( 189 ). Wnt4 has a similar effect ( 278 ). MAP3K1 modulates the balance between female and male pathways. As explained above (see “FGF9 and PGD2: maintaining SOX9 expression levels”), SOX9 and FGF9 upregulate AXIN1 and GSK3β, which promote the destabilization of β-catenin, thus blocking ovarian development.
