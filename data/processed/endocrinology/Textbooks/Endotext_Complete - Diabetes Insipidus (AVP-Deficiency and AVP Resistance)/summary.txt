CLINICAL RECOGNITION Diabetes Insipidus (DI) is the excess production of dilute urine. Because of the confusion with Diabetes Mellitus, there has been a move to renaming this to AVP-Deficiency (AVP-D; Central Diabetes Insipidus) and AVP-Resistance (AVP-R; Nephrogenic Diabetes Insipidus). Diagnosis requires a targeted history, examination, and confirmation through appropriate laboratory and radiological investigations. AVP-D and AVP-R present with polyuria and polydipsia. Urine output is more than 50 ml/kg /24 hours in adults and more than 100 ml/kg/24 hours in children.
