ECONOMIC ASPECTS OF HEALTH INSURANCE COVERAGE PERCENT DISTRIBUTION OF PAYMENT FOR MEDICAL CARE EXPENSES In new analyses conducted for Diabetes in America, data from the MEPS 2008 indicate that among adults age 18–64 years with diabetes, the predominant source of payment for medical care expenses for all services was private insurance (46.8%); 17.5% of services were paid by Medicare, 15.4% were paid out-of- pocket, 11.6% were paid by Medicaid, and the remaining 8.7% were paid by other sources (Table 42.9). A similar distribution was seen for all office-based visits. For emergency room visits, 54.9% were paid by private health insurance, with 10%–12% of emergency room visits paid out-of- pocket, by Medicare, by Medicaid, or by other sources. The majority of dental visits were paid by private insurance (50.6%) or out-of-pocket (39.1%). For adults age ≥65 years with diabetes, the majority of services were paid by Medicare (62.6%), with 12.5% paid out-of-pocket and 13.5% paid by private insurance. Payment sources were distrib­ uted similarly for all office-based visits. A large majority of emergency room visits were paid by Medicare (87.1%), and most dental visits were paid out-of-pocket (77.4%).
