Cowie is Senior Advisor and Director for Diabetes Epidemiology, National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases, National Institutes of Health, Bethesda, MD. SUMMARY National data from 2009 indicate that among adults age ≥18 years with diabetes, 90.1% had some type of health insurance coverage, including 84.7% of those age 18–64 years and 99.7% of those age ≥65 years. An estimated 2.02 million adults with diabetes had no health insurance coverage, including 2.0 million adults age 18–64 years and 25,700 adults age ≥65 years. For adults without diabetes, 81.4% had health insurance coverage, including 78.3% at age 18–64 years and 99.2% at age ≥65 years.
