Correct ETT positioning places the monitoring sensors between the vocal cords, and functionality is verified per the manufacturer’s instructions. Connected to the monitor, the ETT provides real-time electromyography feedback from the vocalis muscles, allowing continuous recurrent laryngeal nerve assessment. The monitor alerts the team to nerve output or stimulation changes, significantly aiding in nerve preservation. Study results show that 83% to 85% of patients who lose signal during surgery develop postoperative vocal cord paresis or paralysis. Furthermore, recent meta-analyses suggest that intraoperative nerve monitoring is associated with a reduced incidence of recurrent laryngeal nerve injury, underscoring intraoperative monitoring's value in reducing risk.
