GOALS FOR LDL-C In 2013, the AHA/ACC eliminated specific numerical goals for LDL-C in both primary and secondary prevention. Recommendations for LDL-C lowering therapy were based exclusively on RCTs of statin therapy. These recommendations have been criticized for lacking a means to evaluate the efficacy of statin therapy. In 2018, AHA/ACC identified 2 goals for LDL-C lowering, namely, ≥ 50% LDL-C reduction in secondary prevention and ≥ 35% reduction in primary prevention. These values are based on the expected reductions achieved by high-intensity statins for secondary prevention and by moderate-intensity statins for primary prevention.
