Fetal macrosomia in utero is common with type 1 or type 2 diabetes after 26 weeks gestation (305,342,343,344) and is predicted by elevated maternal A1c (283,284,345,346,347,348), obesity (343,344,349), and excess gestational weight gain—the latter relationship noted especially in the absence of obesity (308,348). The usual method to define fetal growth restriction is by the proportion of SGA infants at birth (and small for sex and/or racial/ethnic group; SGA is <10th percen­ tile birth weight in a reference population) and to define excess fetal size or mass by the proportion of LGA infants at birth (and large for sex and/or racial/ethnic group; LGA is >90th percentile birth weight in a reference population) (13).
