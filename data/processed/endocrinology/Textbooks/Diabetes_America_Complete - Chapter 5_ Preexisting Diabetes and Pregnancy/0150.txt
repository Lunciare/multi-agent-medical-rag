Adjusted odds ratios with 95% confidence intervals were estimated for the association between prepregnancy diabetes and congenital anomalies, using multivariate logistic regression, adjusting for maternal age, parity, and the year of delivery. Etiological contri­ bution of PDM to overall congenital malformations increased between 2002–2003 and 2012–2013: the annual population attributable risk percentage rose from 0.6% (95% CI 0.4%–0.8%) in 2002–2003 to 1.2% (95% CI 0.9%–1.4%) in 2012–2013, because the prevalence of PDM increased, while the relation between PDM and congenital malforma­ tions rose only slightly.
