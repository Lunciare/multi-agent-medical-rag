Preexisting Diabetes and Pregnancy 5–21 MATERNAL COMPLICATIONS BEFORE AND DURING PREGNANCY MORTALITY AND SEVERE MATERNAL MORBIDITY Maternal Mortality In the United States, national maternal mortality assessment was enhanced by the addition of a pregnancy question (four checkboxes) to the U.S. standard death certificate in 2003 (222). WHO defines maternal death as the death of a woman during pregnancy or within 42 days after delivery when the cause is directly (obstetric complications) or indirectly related to pregnancy (preexisting disease or disease developing during pregnancy, aggravated by the physiologic effects of pregnancy).
