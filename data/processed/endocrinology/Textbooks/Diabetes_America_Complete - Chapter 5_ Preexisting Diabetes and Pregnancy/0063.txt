In a cohort of pregnant women with preexisting diabetes delivering in East Anglia, England, in 2006–2009, diabetic neuropathy was recorded in 2.7% of 408 women with type 1 diabetes and 0.7% of 274 women with type 2 diabetes (107). Similar percentages were obtained in a prospective survey of births to women with type 1 or type 2 diabetes recorded in North England in 1996–2008 (99).
