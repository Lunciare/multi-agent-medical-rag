Other indications of parathyroidectomy include parathyroid cysts and parathyroid hypercalcemic crisis (severe hypercalcemia and central nervous system dysfunction). Contraindications The following include the contraindications for parathyroidectomy: Absolute Contraindication Parathyroidectomy is contraindicated in patients with familial hypocalciuric hypercalcemia (FHH) presenting with elevated calcium and PTH levels but low 24-hour urine calcium excretion and a low ratio of 24-hour urinary calcium to creatinine clearance. Parathyroidectomy does not cure FHH-associated hypercalcemia. Relative Contraindication Contralateral recurrent laryngeal nerve (RLN) injury or vocal cord dysfunction Equipment The following equipment is needed to perform a thyroidectomy: A Bovie or bipolar cautery, harmonic scalpel, electronic tissue fusion devices, or surgical ties, according to the surgeon's preference. Frozen pathology analysis to confirm parathyroid tissue is necessary. Intraoperative nerve monitoring (IONM): RLN monitoring is an attempt to reduce the risk of nerve injury during parathyroid surgery, especially in re-operative parathyroid surgery.
