Positioning The patient is placed on the operating table supine with the neck extended and both arms tucked at the sides. A shoulder roll can be placed to improve the exposure of the neck. The operating table is slightly in the reverse Trendelenburg position. Patients with cervical spine disease should be assessed for the safety of neck extension. Technique or Treatment Procedure In bilateral exploration, a transverse low collar incision is made about one finger wide above the clavicular head in an existing skin crease. The dissection is carried down through the subcutaneous tissue and the platysma muscle. Hemostasis is obtained by electrocautery or ligation using ties. Dissection continues to elevate the upper subplatysmal flap to the thyroid notch and the lower flap to the sternal notch. Self-retaining retractors are used to retract the skin flaps. The deep fascia is opened by a midline incision along the median raphe of the strap muscles.
