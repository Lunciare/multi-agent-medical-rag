References for individual discovery cohorts are listed within the table. 14–6 DIABETES IN AMERICA, 3rd Edition identified a nonsense p.Arg684Ter variant of Inuit ancestry that is common in the Greenlandic population (frequency 17%) and is associated with 2-hour glucose and insulin levels. Stop codon homozygotes harbor a tenfold increased risk of type 2 diabetes compared to wildtype allele carriers. Definitive identification of the implicated protein allowed for functional studies: the stop codon induces lower protein levels of TBC1D4 in human skel­ etal muscle, causing reduced numbers of the glucose transporter GLUT4 and decreased insulin-stimulated glucose uptake, leading to postprandial hypergly­ cemia and impaired glucose tolerance.
