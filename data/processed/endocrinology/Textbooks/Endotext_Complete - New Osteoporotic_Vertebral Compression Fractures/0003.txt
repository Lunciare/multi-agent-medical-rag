Note that the FRAX calculator is not designed for those with osteoporosis on BMD testing but mainly for those with low bone mass. Using a specialized software (incorporated in DXA machines), Trabecular Bone Score (TBS) can be generated from lumbar spine DXA images and is a measure that reflects bone microarchitecture and predicts fracture risk independent of bone density.
