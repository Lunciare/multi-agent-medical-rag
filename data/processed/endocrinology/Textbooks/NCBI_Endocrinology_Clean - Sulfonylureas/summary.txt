Sulfonylureas
KEYWORDS: sulfonylureas, patients, hypoglycemia, glucose, dose, oral, insulin, glipizide, glimepiride, glyburide, levels, available, sulfonylurea, cardiovascular, chlorpropamide

Indications Sulfonylureas are the oldest class of oral antidiabetic medication dating back to the 1950s. All sulfonylureas contain a phenyl-sulfonyl-urea structure, which exerts the hypoglycemic effect. Patients with type 2 diabetes mellitus use sulfonylureas as monotherapy or in combination with other oral or injectable medications. Sulfonylureas are divided into first-generation and second-generation. The first-generation sulfonylureas include chlorpropamide and tolbutamide. Chlorpropamide is no longer available in the United States.
