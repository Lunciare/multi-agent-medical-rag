However, there is a 20% probability of malignant transformation. In these cases, oncological management and an interprofessional team approach are necessary. Complications Most patients with thyroid adenomas are asymptomatic. However, patients with functioning thyroid adenoma may have symptoms of hyperthyroidism, such as fatigue, sweating, tremor, palpitations, and anxiety. Some patients may complain of dyspnea, hoarseness of voice, and dysphagia due to compression of the trachea or esophagus by a growing thyroid tumor.
