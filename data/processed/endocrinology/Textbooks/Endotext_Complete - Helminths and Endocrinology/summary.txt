Helminths and Endocrinology
KEYWORDS: pubmed, helminth, type, diabetes, infections, infection, free, thyroid, pmc, free article, article, pmc free, article pubmed, helminth infections, type diabetes

ABSTRACT Helminths are parasitic worms that can infect humans. They are broadly classified as flatworms (including Cestodes and Trematodes) and roundworms (nematodes). These worms infect organs such as intestines, liver, skin, as well as other tissues. These infections are more common in underdeveloped parts of the world affecting almost one-sixth of the world population. These infections can lead to a variety of endocrine manifestations. A decreased risk of developing type 2 diabetes mellitus has been observed in affected populations.
