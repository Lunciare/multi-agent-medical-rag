POI may be inherited or acquired, with numerous potential causes of both. POI represents impaired ovarian function; however, the function may not be completely suppressed, and approximately 5% to 10% of individuals still achieve spontaneous pregnancy. Additionally, natural menopause may occur earlier than expected (eg, in the early to mid-40s), though it will not be classified as POI if the patient is older than 40.
