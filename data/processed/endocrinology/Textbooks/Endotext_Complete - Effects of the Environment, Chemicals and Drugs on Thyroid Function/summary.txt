Effects of the Environment, Chemicals and Drugs on Thyroid Function
KEYWORDS: thyroid, hormone, effect, serum, tsh, thyroid hormone, clin, endocrinol, effects, patients, clin endocrinol, metab, concentration, increase, function

ABSTRACT The sensitive and tightly regulated feedback control system, thyroid gland autoregulation, and the large intrathyroidal and extrathyroidal storage pools of thyroid hormone serve to provide a constant supply of thyroid hormone to peripheral tissues in the face of perturbations imposed by the external environment, chemicals and drugs, and a variety of diseases processes. The thyroid is subject to a great number of exogenous and endogenous perturbations . The same agent may produce alterations in various aspects of thyroid hormone economy. For this reason, it is difficult to precisely classify all external and internal influences according to their mode of action. This chapter reviews effects on the thyroid produced by changes in the external environment, chemicals and drugs. The effects of non-thyroidal illness are reviewed in Chapter 5b.
