Until clinical trial data demonstrate the superiority of utilizing advanced lipoprotein testing on clinical outcomes it is hard to recommend the routine use of such testing. However, it should be recognized that in selected patients the additional information provided can be helpful and result in changes in treatment. It is hoped that as additional drugs to treat lipids are developed and our understanding of lipid and lipoprotein metabolism expands that in the future the use of advanced lipoprotein analysis will assume a more important role in the evaluation and treatment of patients to prevent ASCVD.
