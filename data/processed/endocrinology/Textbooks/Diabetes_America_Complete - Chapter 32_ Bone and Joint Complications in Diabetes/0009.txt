In the age range considered (≥50 years), the overwhelming majority of participants had type 2 diabetes. In the NHANES 1999–2010, 0.7% of those with diagnosed diabetes matched the definition of probable type 1 diabetes. Based on NHANES data, older adults with diabetes were more likely to report a history of hip fracture, but the differences were not statistically significant (Table 32.1).
