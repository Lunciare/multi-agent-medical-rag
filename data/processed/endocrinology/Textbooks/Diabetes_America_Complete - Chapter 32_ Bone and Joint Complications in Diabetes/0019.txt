A negative T-score indicates BMD lower than this young adult average. Osteoporosis is defined as a femoral neck BMD T-score of -2.5 or lower, or history of a low trauma hip or spine fracture. A T-score higher than -2.5 but lower than -1.0 is considered “low bone density.” BMD of the femoral neck was measured using DXA in the NHANES 2005–2008 (42) and analyzed for Diabetes in America. Osteoporosis, defined as femoral neck BMD T-score less than -2.5, was present in 3.5% of adults with diabetes and 4.9% of those with normal glucose levels (Figure 32.2, Table 32.12).
