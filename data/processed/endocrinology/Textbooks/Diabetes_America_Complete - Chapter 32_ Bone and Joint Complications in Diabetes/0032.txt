Osteoarthritis is based on knee x-ray. Diagnosed diabetes is self-reported. Undiagnosed diabetes is based on A1c ≥6.5% or FPG ≥126 mg/dL without self-report of diabetes. Prediabetes is based on A1c 5.7%–6.4% or FPG 100–125 mg/dL. Normal glucose is defined as A1c <5.7% and FPG <100 mg/dL. Conversions for A1c and glucose values are provided in Diabetes in America Appendix 1 Conversions.
