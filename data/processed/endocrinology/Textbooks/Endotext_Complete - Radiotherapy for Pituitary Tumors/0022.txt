ENDOCRINE CONTROL WITH GK SRS The reported endocrine outcomes following GK SRS for acromegaly are shown in Table 4 [ 14 , 36 , 40 , 55 , 64 , 109-111 , 114 , 121 , 137-163 ]. A summary analysis of the published literature up to 2020 shows that -41% of patients achieved normalization of serum GH, after a median follow-up of 46 months. The time to reach 50% of baseline serum GH, reported in only three studies, is in the region of 1.5-2 years with a slower reduction in IGF-I levels [ 147 , 150 , 164 ], which is similar to the rate reported following conventional RT/CRT.
