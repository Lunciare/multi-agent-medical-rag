In a recently published systematic review and meta-analysis on 1381 patients with pituitary adenomas treated with GK SRS, rates of radiation-induced hypopituitarism were (11.4%) in primary GK compared to (18-32%) in post-operative GK SRS. Robotic SRS A small number of retrospective case series on outcomes following CK SRS for pituitary adenomas have been published to date ( Table 7 ) [ 190-197 ]. While the published results are comparable to the outcomes achieved with GK SRS, the same criticisms levelled at the GK SRS studies also apply to these early CK SRS series.
