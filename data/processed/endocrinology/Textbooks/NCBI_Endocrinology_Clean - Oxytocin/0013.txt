effects when given exogenous oxytocin. Management of Overdose The treatment for water intoxication involves immediately discontinuing the infusion of oxytocin and any free water. Electrolyte-free water should be replaced with isotonic saline, and using furosemide or mannitol may help promote the excretion of excess water. If neurologic symptoms are present, hypertonic saline may be administered with caution, as rapid correction can lead to complications such as central pontine myelinolysis.
