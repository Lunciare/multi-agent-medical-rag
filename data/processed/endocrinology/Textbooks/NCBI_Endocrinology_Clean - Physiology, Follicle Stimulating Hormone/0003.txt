Girls with monosomy Turner syndrome (45, XO) have an elevation of FSH up to 6 years old due to lack of negative feedback from nonfunctional ovaries, while girls with mosaicism (45, X/46, XX) have a much lower FSH elevation due to partial ovarian function. During puberty, the hypothalamus secretes GnRH in a pulsatile manner, which stimulates the anterior pituitary to increase secretion of LH and FSH. Organ Systems Involved Hypothalamic-Pituitary-Gonadal Axis The hypothalamus secretes GnRH, which stimulates the anterior pituitary to release FSH and LH. In females, FSH receptors are located in the granulosa cells of the ovaries. In males, FSH receptors are found in the Sertoli cells of the testes.
