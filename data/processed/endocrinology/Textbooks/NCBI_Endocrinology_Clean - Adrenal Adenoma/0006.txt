Evaluation The evaluation of adrenal tumors primarily focuses on 2 key goals: the first goal is to distinguish between benign and malignant masses, whereas the second goal is to determine whether the tumors are hormonally active or nonfunctioning. After detecting an adrenal mass, an adrenal-dedicated CT scan or Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) is the preferred imaging modality for evaluating adrenal adenomas. Adrenal tumor size exceeding 4.0 cm has a high sensitivity for adrenal carcinoma. In addition, adrenal lesions exhibiting less than 10 Hounsfield units (HU) on noncontrast CT imaging strongly indicate a benign adenoma. Some benign adenomas may have higher than 10 HU values. In such cases, a delayed contrast-enhanced CT scan can aid in differentiating between benign and malignant lesions.
