Although methimazole and PTU are equally effective, methimazole is preferred due to once-daily dosing and a relatively better safety profile. An exception to this recommendation is in pregnant patients, in which PTU is preferred. Methimazole is associated with an increased risk of congenital defects, and thus, PTU is preferred in managing hyperthyroidism during pregnancy. Doses: ATA (Americal Thyroid Association) guidelines provide a rough guide for the initial dose of methimazole based on free T4 levels Free T4 1-1.5 times upper limit of normal: Start methimazole 5-10 mg daily Free T4 1.5-2.0 times the upper limit of normal: Start methimazole 10-20 mg daily Free T4 2.0-3.0 times the upper limit of normal: Start methimazole 30-40 mg daily PTU is administered in 2-3 doses per day due to its shorter duration of action.
