According to the Endocrine Society guidelines, for postmenopausal women undergoing osteoporosis treatment with low bone mineral density and high fracture risk, bone mineral density should be monitored using dual-energy X-ray absorptiometry at the spine and hip every 1 to 3 years to evaluate treatment response. Monitoring bone turnover markers (serum C-terminal crosslinking telopeptide for antiresorptive therapy or procollagen type N-terminal propeptide for bone anabolic therapy) can help identify poor response or nonadherence to therapy.
