Lastly, deep gluteal syndrome is a term used to describe the compression of the sciatic or pudendal nerve by a combination of gemelli-obturator syndrome, piriformis syndrome, ischiofemoral impingement syndrome, and proximal hamstring syndrome. Symptoms include pain in the hip, buttock, and posterior thigh due to nerve entrapment. Gemelli-obturator syndrome occurs when the sciatic nerve becomes compressed between the gemellus muscles and obturator internus during passive hip rotation. Treatment for this condition can include rest, non-steroidal anti-inflammatory drugs, gabapentin, muscle relaxants, and physical therapy.
