In addition to the duality of its origin, ossification of the skull also occurs in a dualistic nature with endochondral ossification and intramembranous ossification. Endochondral ossification is the bone-forming process that utilizes a cartilage template as its initial framework before ossification is complete. The other ossification process known as intramembranous ossification occurs when dormant mesenchymal cells differentiate into osteoblasts. Therefore, the classification of foramina can be classified based on the origin of the development or on the ossification process.
