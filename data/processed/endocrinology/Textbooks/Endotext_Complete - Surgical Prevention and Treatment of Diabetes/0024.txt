The SAMPEDE study found a three-year remission rate following RYGB of 38% which fell to 22% at five-years ( 8 , 119 ). The suggestion that the metabolic effects of RYGB are attenuated with time were supported by the RCT with the longest follow up which found the remission rate fell from 75% at 2 years to 37% at 5 years and 25% at 10 years ( 7 ).
