Recurrent nephrolithiasis and urothelial bladder tumors where regular cystoscopic instrumentation is expected are considered a relative contraindication as they increase the risk of cuff erosions in AUS patients. Bladder neck contractures should be repaired and cystoscopically verified as permanently corrected at least 3 months post-correction before proceeding to AUS placement. Relative contraindications to DBACT include severe incontinence, a history of previous radiation therapy or planned radiation within 6 months of DBACT placement, and neurogenic stress urinary incontinence. Other contraindications include an active urinary tract infection, incontinence due to detrusor instability or overactivity, reduced bladder compliance, post-void residual urine volumes >100 mL, significant urge incontinence, or uncorrected bleeding disorders.
