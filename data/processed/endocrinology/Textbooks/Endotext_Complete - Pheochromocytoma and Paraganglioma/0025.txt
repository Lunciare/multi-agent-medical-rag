All patients with a biochemically positive PPGLs (except those with only elevated 3-methoxytyramine) should undergo preoperative adrenoceptor blockade with α-adrenoceptor blockade followed by β-blockers to prevent perioperative cardiovascular complications. Minimally invasive (laparoscopic) surgery is recommended for most adrenal PPGLs, and open resection for large or locally invasive PPGLs to ensure complete resection and avoid local recurrence. SDHB pheochromocytoma should be always removed by total adrenalectomy. Multidisciplinary teams at centers with appropriate expertise to ensure favorable outcomes should treat all patients with PPGL.
