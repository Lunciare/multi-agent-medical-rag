With regard to the direction of the relation­ ship, it has generally been considered that diabetes predisposes to gallstone disease, though the majority of studies have been cross-sectional. In contrast, a European prospective study found that self-reported gallstone disease preceded the diagnosis of type 2 diabetes (122). An association of insulin resistance with gallstone disease has been shown among those without overt diabetes (117,118). Insulin resistance may be the link between diabetes and gallstone disease.
