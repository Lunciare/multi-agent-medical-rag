A population-based study in Minnesota found an increase in all-cause mortality among those with NAFLD compared to the general population (SMR 1.55, 95% CI 1.11–2.11) (45). Those with diabetes also had a significant increase in all-cause mortality, independent of NAFLD; however, no test for interaction was reported.
