At the time of transplant, a higher MELD score among those without diabetes was still present. Diabetic patients received livers from donors who were slightly older and less likely to be of white race compared with donors of nondiabetic patients. Table 26.11 shows the percentages of patients with a given liver disease by diabetes status. For example, an HCV diagnosis was present among 37.2% of 33,078 listed patients with diabetes and 41.2% of 116,102 listed patients without diabetes. Because patients may have more than one liver disease, the percentages are not mutually exclusive and add to greater than 100%.
