tissue disorders, nephrotic syndrome EVALUATION History and Physical Exam Initial evaluation includes a thorough medical history, with special attention to aspects that may adversely affect bone health such as chronic immobility or oncology treatments; medication history, with a focus on past and current medications that may adversely influence bone health, such as glucocorticoids, anti-epileptic drugs, and hormonal contraception; pubertal history, dependent upon age; and family history of recurrent fractures, pre-menopausal osteoporosis, and bone disorders.
