In both sexes, CDGP is self-limited and is considered to represent a variant of normal pubertal timing. CDGP has a strong genetic component, with a positive family history of delayed puberty reported in 50% to 75% of cases ( 335 ). Distinguishing between CDGP and congenital hypogonadotropic hypogonadism (CHH) may be challenging because these conditions share clinical features, hormone levels, and radiological findings.
