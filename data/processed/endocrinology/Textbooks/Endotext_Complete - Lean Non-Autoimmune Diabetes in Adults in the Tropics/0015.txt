Generalized forms present in infancy with near-total fat loss, muscular hypertrophy, hepatomegaly, and acromegaloid features, while partial lipodystrophy (e.g., Dunnigan-type) manifests at puberty with limb and gluteal fat loss but facial and cervical fat accumulation.
