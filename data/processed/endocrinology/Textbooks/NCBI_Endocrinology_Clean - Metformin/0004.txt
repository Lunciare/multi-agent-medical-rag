Administration Metformin is an oral medication typically prescribed at daily doses ranging from 500 to 2550 mg. To reduce the risk of experiencing gastrointestinal (GI) upset, it is recommended to administer metformin with a meal. Metformin is available in 2 formulations: immediate-release form, which requires twice-daily dosing, and extended-release form, which requires once-daily dosing. Daily doses of metformin are often titrated weekly in 500 or 850 mg increments to mitigate the risk of adverse effects in patients who are administered the drug.
