Consequently, the ADA advises frequent monitoring of vitamin B12 levels, especially in patients with anemia or peripheral neuropathy. Moreover, patients on concomitant drugs, which can cause an increased risk of lactic acidosis, should undergo regular monitoring. Toxicity Metformin overdose is associated with hypoglycemia and lactic acidosis. If the clinicians suspect lactic acidosis due to toxic metformin levels, they immediately recommend discontinuing the medication and initiating hemodialysis. Metformin is easily dialyzable owing to its small molecular weight and minimal protein binding. In cases of metformin toxicity, treatment primarily involves supportive care, as no specific antidote is available yet.
