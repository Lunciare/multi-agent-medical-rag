Physicians recommend taking metformin consistently at the same time every day. Extended-release tablets of metformin are usually taken once daily, ideally with an evening meal, and it is recommended to swallow the tablet with a full glass of water. Metformin is an effective, safe, and affordable medication, which may also lower the risk of cardiovascular events and mortality (ADA, 2023). Recommended Adult Dosages Treatment of Type 2 Diabetes Immediate-release oral formulation: For the immediate-release oral form of metformin, it is recommended to begin with an initial dosage of either 500 mg, once or twice daily, or 850 mg, once daily. The daily dose of metformin is often titrated every week in increments of 500 or 850 mg to minimize any adverse GI effects. The typical maintenance dose of the medication is 850 or 1000 mg twice daily. Extended-release oral formulation: For the extended-release oral form of metformin, it is recommended to begin with an initial dosage of either 500 mg or 1000 mg once daily.
