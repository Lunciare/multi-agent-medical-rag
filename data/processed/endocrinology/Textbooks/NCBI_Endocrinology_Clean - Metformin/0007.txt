Treatment of Gestational Diabetes For the immediate-release oral form of metformin, it is recommended to begin with an initial dosage of 500 mg once or twice daily. The dosage may be gradually increased from 2000 to 2500 mg in 2 to 3 divided doses to achieve the desired glycemic levels. To achieve glycemic targets, insulin therapy may be coadministered with metformin. Treatment of Oligomenorrhea due to PCOS For the immediate-release oral form of metformin, it is recommended to begin with an initial dosage of 500 mg once or twice daily. The daily dose of metformin is often titrated every week in increments of 500 mg to minimize any adverse GI effects. Specific Patient Populations Patient with renal impairment: Dosage adjustments for metformin are based on a periodic assessment of patients' renal function.
