KEYWORDS: diabetes, disability, years, mobility, health, age, adults, defined, iadl, work, women, adl, risk, impairment, self-reporting

34–1 CHAPTER 34 DIABETES AND DISABILITY Edward W. Gregg, PhD, and Andy Menke, PhD Dr. Gregg is Chief of the Epidemiology and Statistics Branch in the Division of Diabetes Translation, Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, Atlanta, GA. Andy Menke is a Senior Research Analyst at Social & Scientific Systems, Inc., Silver Spring, MD. Received in final form January 16, 2016. SUMMARY This chapter reviews findings from national studies of prevention of and trends in diabetes-related disability, summarizes the modifiable risk factors and mechanisms for the excess disability prevalence associated with diabetes, and reviews evidence that physical disability can be prevented or modified.
