IADL impairment is defined as self-reporting some/much difficulty with: (1) household chores or (2) prepare meals. ADL disability is defined as self-reporting unable/do not: (1) use fork/knife/drink or (2) dress oneself. ADL impairment is defined as self-reporting some/much difficulty with: (1) use fork/knife/drink or (2) dress oneself. Work disability is defined as self-reporting limitations keeping you from working. Work impairment is defined as self-reporting limitations in the amount of work you can do. SOURCE: National Health and Nutrition Examination Surveys 2005–2010 34–6 DIABETES IN AMERICA, 3rd Edition In addition to the consistent association of greater age and female sex with higher prevalence of mobility disability, disability rates among those with diabetes were strongly associated with education level for mobility, IADL, and work disability (Table 34.3). College graduates had notably lower prevalence of age-standardized mobility disability (16%) than those with some college (29%) or those with no college (38%–42%). This association was also found for work disability, as 15% of college graduates and 22% of those with some college education were disabled compared to about one-third (30%–37%) of those with less than a college education. This latter finding may also be confounded by persons of lower education having occupations that either demand more physical labor or are more likely to lead to work-related disability.
