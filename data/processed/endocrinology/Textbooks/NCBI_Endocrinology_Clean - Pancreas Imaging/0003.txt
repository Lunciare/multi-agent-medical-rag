Pancreatic Adenocarcinoma, Computed Tomography). Pancreatic neuroendocrine tumors (NETs) are malignancies that form in the endocrine tissue of the pancreas. Also known as islet cell tumors, these are rare tumors occurring in approximately 1 in every 100,000 people, and only 1% of all pancreatic tumors are NETs. Image. Gastrinoma on Distal Pancreas. Cystic lesions of the pancreas are a relatively common incidental finding on body imaging, with prevalence in the general population ranging up to 14%. Classification of these cysts is crucial as they can either be true cysts, pseudocysts (usually related to pancreatitis), or related to benign or malignant neoplasms.
