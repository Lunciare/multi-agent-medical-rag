Introduction The male genitourinary tract is responsible for the excretion of urine, produced by the kidney, and semen from the sperm from the testes and the various accessory glands. The urethra serves as the final path of excretion of both urine and semen from the body. Male Genitourinary Tract. Urine is the waste product of filtered blood that leaves the kidney via the collecting system. From the renal collecting system, urine is transported through the ureters and stored in the urinary bladder for elimination through the urethra. Sperm is produced in the testes and undergoes maturation in the epididymis.
