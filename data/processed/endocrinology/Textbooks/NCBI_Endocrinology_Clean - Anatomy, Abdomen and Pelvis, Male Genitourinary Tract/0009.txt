Emission is the initial phase of ejaculation where the internal urethral sphincter closes while the prostatic urethra is filled with secretions from the seminal vesicles, vas deferens, prostatic, and Cowpers glands. The prostate receives sympathetic and parasympathetic innervation from the inferior hypogastric plexus. The prostatic plexuses innervate the corpus cavernosum of the penis and seminal vesicles. Physiologic Variants Pelvic kidney refers to inappropriate ascent during weeks 6 to 8 of development. As a result, the kidney remains in the pelvis instead of the abdomen. Because pelvic kidney rarely presents with symptoms, proper imagining must be performed before surgery in the pelvic area to rule out this anomaly. Pelvic kidney also presents with other anatomical variations in the vasculature that must merit consideration. Surgical Considerations Retroperitoneal lymph node dissection (RPLND) is an effective procedure to treat testicular cancer as it is usually the first lymph node testicular cancer spreads. However, surgical complications of RPLND make it a debatable treatment compared to chemotherapy.
