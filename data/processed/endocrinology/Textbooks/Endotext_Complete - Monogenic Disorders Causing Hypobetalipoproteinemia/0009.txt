The duodenal mucosa is white on endoscopy and intestinal biopsy reveals cytosolic lipid droplets and lipoprotein-sized particles in enterocytes ( 31 ). As one would expect the absorption of fat-soluble vitamins (A, D, K, and E) and essential fatty acids is impaired ( 31 , 32 ). Neurological and eye manifestations are milder and occur at an older age compared to abetalipoproteinemia ( 1 ). Red blood cell acanthosis is rare ( 1 ).
