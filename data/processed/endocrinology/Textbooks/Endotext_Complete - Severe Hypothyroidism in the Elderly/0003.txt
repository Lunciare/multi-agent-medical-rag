Severe Hypothyroidism in the Elderly

FOLLOW-UP When treating primary hypothyroidism, a TSH level should be checked 6 weeks after starting a dose or 4 weeks after changing a dose of levothyroxine. Doses should be adjusted to maintain a TSH level within the reference range. Maintenance of a slightly elevated TSH level may be acceptable in cases where treatment to a full replacement dose triggers ischemic symptoms or atrial arrhythmias. When treating central hypothyroidism, doses should be adjusted to maintain a free T4 level in the upper half of the reference range. The TSH level is unreliable in this setting and should not be used to guide treatment.
