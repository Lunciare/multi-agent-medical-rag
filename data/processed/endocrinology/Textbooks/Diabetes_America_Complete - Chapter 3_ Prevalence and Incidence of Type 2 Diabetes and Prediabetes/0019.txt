SOURCE: National Health and Nutrition Examination Surveys 2011–2014 PREVALENCE BY RACE/ETHNICITY As in the NHIS, age- and sex-adjusted prevalence of diagnosed diabetes based on NHANES 2011–2014 in persons age ≥20 years was highest in non-Hispanic blacks, all Hispanics, and Mexican Americans, somewhat lower in non-Hispanic Asians, and significantly lower in non-Hispanic whites (Figure 3.15, Appendix 3.4). In contrast, prevalence of undiagnosed diabetes based on A1c/ FPG was higher, though not statistically significantly, in non-Hispanic Asians (6.3%) compared to non-Hispanic blacks (4.3%), all Hispanics (4.9%), and Mexican Americans (4.7%); the lower prevalence in non-Hispanic whites (2.1%) was significantly lower than all other race/ ethnicity groups.
