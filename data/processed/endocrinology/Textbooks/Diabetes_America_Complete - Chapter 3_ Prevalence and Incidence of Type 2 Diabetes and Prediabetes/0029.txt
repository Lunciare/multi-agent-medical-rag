The age-standardized prevalence of total diabetes based on A1c/FPG increased significantly from 9.8% (95% CI 8.9%–10.6%) in 1988–1994 to 12.4% (95% CI 10.8%–14.2%) in 2011–2012 (p<0.001); however, the prevalence was similar during 2007–2008 (12.5%, 95% CI 10.9%–14.2%)
