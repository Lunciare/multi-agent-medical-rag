Undiagnosed diabetes based on A1c/FPG/2-hour PG was not significantly different by sex, though the difference in resultant total diabetes was statistically significant. The percent of total diabetes that was undiagnosed based on A1c/FPG was significantly higher in men (32.4%) than women (22.1%), while that based on A1c/FPG/2-hour PG was not significantly different by sex (Figure 3.12, Appendix 3.4).
