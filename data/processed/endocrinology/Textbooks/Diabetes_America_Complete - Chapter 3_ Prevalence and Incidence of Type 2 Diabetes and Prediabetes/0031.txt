TRENDS OVER TIME IN THE PREVALENCE OF PREDIABETES Trends over time in prediabetes prevalence have been best described based on A1c/FPG from the NHANES during 1999–2010 in adults age ≥18 years (Table 3.3) (40). Age-standardized prevalence of prediabetes based on A1c/ FPG combined was similar during 1999– 2002 and 2003–2006 at 29.2% and 29.3%, respectively, but rose significantly in 2007–2010 to 36.2%. The change over time in prevalence of prediabetes occurred because of a significant change in elevated A1c, whereas prevalence based on elevated FPG was similar over this time period. Changes over time for prediabetes based on A1c/FPG rose most significantly in those age 18–64 years versus those age ≥65 years, in women versus men, in non-Hispanic whites and non-Hispanic blacks versus Mexican Americans, in those with lower versus higher income levels, and in those with lower versus higher BMI levels.
