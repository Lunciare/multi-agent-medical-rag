Age-standardized prevalences of prediabetes based on A1c/FPG/2-hour PG were also available by Hispanic ethnicity from the HCHS/SOL during 2008–2011 (Figure 3.20) (29). About one-third of Hispanic groups had prediabetes, with prevalence being highest in Mexicans (37.8%, 95% CI 35.9%–40.0%) and lowest in Dominicans (31.8%, 95% CI 28.9%– 34.9%). Prevalences were between these groups and similar for South Americans (36.2%, 95% CI 32.9%–39.5%), Cubans (35.9%, 95% CI 33.5%–38.3%), Central Americans (35.4%, 95% CI 32.8%–38.1%), and Puerto Ricans (34.6%, 95% CI 31.9%– 37.4%). Similar patterns by race were found for men and women. Prevalences of prediabetes were higher in men than women of all Hispanic groups, particularly among Cubans and Puerto Ricans. FIGURE 3.19.
