Rehabilitation Care For patients with metastatic stage IV pancreatic cancer, discussions with the patient regarding treatment are essential. One can receive chemotherapy. However, the life prolongation will be, at best, months, yet it affects the toxicity and effects of the chemotherapy. It is important to keep nutrition at the forefront of the patient's care, as nutrition can affect wound healing.
