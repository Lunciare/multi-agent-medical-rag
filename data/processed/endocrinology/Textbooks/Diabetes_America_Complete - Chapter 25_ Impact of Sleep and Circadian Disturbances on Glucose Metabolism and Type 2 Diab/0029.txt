Similar to nondiabetic populations, the severity of untreated OSA is associated with poorer glycemic control in diabetic populations. Aronsohn et al. utilized PSG to assess OSA severity in 60 diabetic patients (133). There was a graded relationship between the severity of untreated OSA as measured by AHI and higher A1c levels after adjusting for age, sex, race, BMI, years of diabetes, numbers of diabetes medications, exercise, and total sleep time, with an effect size as large as that associated with the impact of diabetes drugs. Another cross-sectional study in 52 diabetic patients also found that increased severity of OSA was associated with increased A1c levels, after adjusting for age, sex, BMI, diabetes duration, and insulin dose (139).
