Lingual Cyst - This is a thyroglossal duct cyst occurring at the base of the tongue. These can present with difficulty in swallowing or breathing. Clinical Significance Surgical significance of PL - A clinician discovers an existing pyramidal lobe pre-operatively only 50% of the time. Because the pyramidal lobe (or DPL) is composed of normal thyroid tissue, it can harbor disease just like the 'normal' thyroid. Thus, incomplete removal of an existing PL can lead to disease recurrence and re-operation. Surgical significance of Zuckerkandl's tubercle - A posterolateral extension of the thyroid with a close anatomic relationship with the recurrent laryngeal nerve, the inferior thyroid artery, and the parathyroid glands. Large tubercles can increase the risk of suboptimal and complicated surgeries.
