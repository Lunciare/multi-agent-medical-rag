Practical Approaches to Managing Antihypertensive Medications During Screening for Primary Aldosteronism (PA) View in own window Clinical Approach Medications Typically Held Suggested Withdrawal Timing Recommended Substitute Agents (if BP control needed) How to Interpret a Negative Screen How to Interpret a Positive Screen 1. Screening Without Changing Current Medications None Not applicable Not required A negative result may be falsely reassuring if the patient has high pretest probability or is on renin-stimulating drugs (e.g., ACE-I/ARB, diuretics). Consider repeating ARR under a partial or full washout strategy if suspicion remains high. A positive ARR may be falsely elevated if the patient uses renin-suppressing agents (β-blockers, central α 2 -agonists). If feasible, repeat ARR after modifying these medications. 2.
