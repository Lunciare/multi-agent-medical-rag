One case report describes a 41-year-old woman diagnosed with adrenal insufficiency who developed Takotsubo cardiomyopathy following the initiation of hormone replacement therapy with hydrocortisone and fludrocortisone (0.3 mg/day). Notably, her cardiac function significantly improved after a substantial reduction in fludrocortisone dosage to 0.05 mg/day. Management of Overdose There is no antidote for fludrocortisone toxicity, making conservative management the mainstay of treatment. Reduction of dose, strict blood pressure control, and potassium supplementation may be given.
