During 1990–1999, the DIAMOND project detected a worldwide increase in the incidence of type 1 diabetes, with an average annual increase of 2.8% (31). The greatest relative increase was observed in the 0–4-years age group (4.0% per year) followed by the 5–9-years age group (3.0%), with the lowest in the 10–14-years age group (2.1%).
