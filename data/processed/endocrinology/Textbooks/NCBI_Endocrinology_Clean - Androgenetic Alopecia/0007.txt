Topical minoxidil is available over the counter (OTC) and is offered in various strengths, with formulations extending up to 5% solution. Higher strengths are more effective. The most common adverse effects are pruritus and local irritation, leading to flaking. The flaking issue is usually due to propylene glycol or alcohol within the drug's formulation. Minoxidil functions as a potassium channel blocker, facilitating the dilation of blood vessels. This theoretical enhancement of vascular access is postulated to enable heightened oxygen, blood, and nutrient delivery to hair follicles, stimulating the anagen growth phase. Finasteride is a 5 alpha-reductase type 2 inhibitor rather than functioning as an antiandrogen agent. Typically prescribed at a daily dose of 1 mg, it displays greater effectiveness in promoting hair regrowth at the vertex region of the scalp compared to the frontal area.
