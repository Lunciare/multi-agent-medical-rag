Androgenetic Alopecia in Male). Important Background Information Two primary isoforms of the 5 alpha-reductase enzyme exist. This enzyme converts testosterone to DHT, a compound with a higher affinity for the androgen receptor. The Type 2 isoform of the 5 alpha-reductase enzyme plays a more significant role in androgenetic alopecia. The Type 1 isoform of 5 alpha-reductase enzymes are distributed within sebaceous glands, keratinocytes, and sweat glands. On the other hand, Type 2 5 alpha-reductase enzymes predominately reside in the outer root sheath of hair follicles, epididymis, vas deferens, seminal vesicles, and prostate.
