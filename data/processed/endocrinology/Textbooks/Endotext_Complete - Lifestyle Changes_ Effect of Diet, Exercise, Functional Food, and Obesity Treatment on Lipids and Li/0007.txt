Individual SFA have diverse biological and cholesterol-raising effects. The chain length of SFA plays an important role in defining biological functions, such as susceptibility to oxidation and solubility in water, as well as effects on blood lipids and lipoproteins. The most commonly consumed SFA are palmitic acid (16:0; major source: vegetable oil, dairy, and meat), stearic acid (18:0; meat and dairy), myristic acid (14:0; dairy and tropical oil) and lauric acid (12:0; dairy and tropical oil) (U.S. Department of Agriculture, ARS.
