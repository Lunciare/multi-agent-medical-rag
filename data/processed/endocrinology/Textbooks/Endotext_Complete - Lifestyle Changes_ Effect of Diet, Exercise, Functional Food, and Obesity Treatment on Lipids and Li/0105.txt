Patients were randomized to placebo (n=514), PHEN/TPM CR 3.75/23 mg (n=214) or PHEN/TPM CR 15/92 mg (n=512), added to a standardized life-style program. Similar to the CONQUER trial, the primary end points were percent of weight loss and proportions of patients achieving 5% weight loss. The secondary end points included improvements in any of the complications of obesity, such as waist circumference, systolic and diastolic blood pressure, fasting glucose and lipid measures.
