The SCALE Obesity and Prediabetes trial investigated the efficacy and safety of liraglutide 3.0 mg, as adjunct to diet and exercise, for weight management in obese and overweight adults without type 2 diabetes mellitus ( 799 ). 3,731 individuals were randomized 1:1 to liraglutide 3.0 mg, or placebo plus diet (500 kcal/day deficit) and exercise for 56 weeks. Randomization was stratified by prediabetes status (ADA 2010) and BMI. Patients receiving liraglutide 3.0 mg lost -8.0% of their weight compared to -2.6% receiving placebo (P<0.0001). Significantly more patients taking liraglutide 3.0 mg, achieved at least 5% and 10% weight loss when compared with patients taking placebo.
