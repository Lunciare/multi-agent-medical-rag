Minimal data are avail­ able on the natural history of diabetic gastroparesis. From clinic-based literature, the natural history of diabetic gastropa­ resis may result in nutritional compromise, impaired glucose control, and a poor quality of life, independent of other factors, such as age, tobacco use, alcohol use, or type of diabetes (33). From a group of 86 patients assessed at a tertiary referral center, 20 patients, of whom 16 had type 1 diabetes, were reevaluated 12 years later, and 13 patients (12 had type 1 diabetes) were reevaluated approximately 25 years after the first study (34,35).
