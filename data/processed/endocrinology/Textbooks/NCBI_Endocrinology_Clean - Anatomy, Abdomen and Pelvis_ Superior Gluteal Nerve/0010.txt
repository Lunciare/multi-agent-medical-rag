Furthermore, abduction weakness and limping are common complications of closed antegrade insertion of femoral nails and are most likely caused by iatrogenic injury to the superior gluteal nerve and gluteus medius itself. However, by increasing the degree of hip flexion and adduction during insertion of a femoral nail, the injury risk to the superior gluteal nerve and gluteus medius is lowered. The positioning of the hip can help to achieve the appropriate degree of hip flexion and adduction, with higher degrees of these movements achievable with either the lateral position on a fracture table or “sloppy” lateral position on an ordinary table.
