TRIALS OF COMPREHENSIVE RISK FACTOR AND LIFESTYLE MANAGEMENT Few trials have been published involving multiple risk factor intervention among persons with diabetes (Table 18.10). The Steno-2 trial was conducted among 160 patients with type 2 diabetes who were randomized to intensive therapy designed to lower glucose, LDL choles­ terol, and blood pressure; after a 7.8-year follow-up, those in the intensive therapy arm had a significantly lower risk of CVD events (HR 0.47, 95% CI 0.24–0.73) (148). A further 5.5-year follow-up of this study cohort showed a subsequent 46% lower total mortality, 57% lower CVD death rate, and 59% lower risk of subse­ quent CVD events (149).
