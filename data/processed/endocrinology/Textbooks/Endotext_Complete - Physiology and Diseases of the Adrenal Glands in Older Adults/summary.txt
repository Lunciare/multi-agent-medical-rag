Physiology and Diseases of the Adrenal Glands in Older Adults
KEYWORDS: adrenal, pubmed, patients, primary, adrenalectomy, surgery, treatment, clinical, age, cortisol, elderly, endocrinol, free, hypertension, article

ABSTRACT The adrenal glands are crucial to the maintenance of physiological homeostasis, contributing to the regulation of stress responses, electrolyte balance, and metabolic processes. This chapter provides an in-depth examination of the anatomical and physiological modifications in adrenal function that occur with aging and the implications of these changes for clinical assessment. It further reviews the clinical characteristics, diagnostic evaluation, and therapeutic management of major adrenal disorders in older adults, with particular attention to the blunted clinical manifestations and the need for individualized approaches in the presence of comorbid conditions in elderly patients. The chapter also outlines advances in adrenal imaging and summarizes recent guideline updates relevant to the evaluation and treatment of adrenal diseases. Finally, it highlights the importance of personalized management strategies that consider underlying medical conditions, functional status, and overall quality of life.
