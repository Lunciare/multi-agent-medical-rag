Life-long thyroxin replacement therapy and regular follow-up are optimal for such patients. The indications for surgical removal of the ectopic thyroid include the following: malignancy, bleeding or ulceration of the gland, uncontrolled hyperthyroidism, and severe local/respiratory symptoms. Some lingual thyroids are visible in the neck, and females prefer surgical removal for cosmetic reasons. Differential Diagnosis The differential diagnosis for ectopic thyroid include the following: Thyroglossal cyst The differentiation between the lingual ectopic thyroid and thyroglossal duct cyst is by performing thyroid scintigraphy; usually, thyroglossal duct cysts remain unidentified because they do not typically contain sufficient functioning thyroid
