differentiation between the lingual ectopic thyroid and thyroglossal duct cyst is by performing thyroid scintigraphy; usually, thyroglossal duct cysts remain unidentified because they do not typically contain sufficient functioning thyroid tissue. Other neck masses are included in the differential diagnosis of ectopic thyroid Complications Fortunately, the studies have estimated the risk of developing malignancy from ectopic tissue is less than 1%. Benign neoplasms and thyroiditis are also infrequent complications. If the patient is symptomatic and kept without treatment, they are more likely to develop complications of hypothyroidism.
