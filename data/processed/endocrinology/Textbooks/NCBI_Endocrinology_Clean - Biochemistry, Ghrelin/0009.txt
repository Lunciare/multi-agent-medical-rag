Biochemistry, Ghrelin
KEYWORDS: ghrelin, hormone, effects, release, growth, studies, growth hormone, appetite, levels, ghs-, regulation, secretion, fat, ghrelin levels, food

Rodent studies have found that administration of ghrelin increased alcohol intake, while ghrelin receptor blockade had the opposite effect. Preliminary studies involving heavy users of alcohol have found that ghrelin administration increases alcohol cravings, while PF-5190457, a GHS-R1-alpha-blocker, appeared to reduce cravings.
