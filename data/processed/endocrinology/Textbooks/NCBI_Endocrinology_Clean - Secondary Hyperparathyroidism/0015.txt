A study showed that parathyroidectomy, regardless of the technique used, significantly relieved most of the initial functional signs within seven days of the procedure, but patients continued to experience mild to moderate symptoms over a six-month follow-up. Surgery and calcimimetics can offer improved survival in the initial stages of CKD resistance to standard therapy.
