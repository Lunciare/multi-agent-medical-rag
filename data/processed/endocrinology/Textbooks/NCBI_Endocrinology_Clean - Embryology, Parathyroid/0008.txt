The parathyroids appear as small, oval masses on the posterior thyroid. They are comparatively homogenous and hypoechoic when compared to the thyroid, allowing for the identification of intra-thyroid parathyroid tissue. It is useful to screen for parathyroid adenomas. Normal parathyroid glands are approximately 5 mm in size and weigh 30 to 50 mg, while parathyroid adenomas are 1 to 2 cm in size, and weigh 500 to 1000 mg. It is also useful for myocardial perfusion scans, detection of breast cancer, detection of multi-drug resistance proteins in certain cancers, and identification of parathyroid tissue. It is particularly useful in the localization of ectopic parathyroid tissue. Technetium-99 is taken up by tissues with high metabolic activity and blood flow.
