The procedure can be done in the outpatient setting under local anesthesia ( 69 ). Radiofrequency ablation and laser ablation can be also effectively used in the outpatient setting under local anesthesia for treating benign thyroid nodules, in particular autonomous nodules with a volume reduction range 50% to 85%, usually with better outcomes in smaller nodules (<10 mL) ( 69 ). Thermal ablation can also be performed in selective cases of thyroid malignancy such as low risk papillary thyroid microcarcinomas ( 79 , 80 ).
