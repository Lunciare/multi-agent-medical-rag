Biochemistry, Dihydrotestosterone
KEYWORDS: dht, testosterone, alpha-reductase, prostate, male, hair, activity, hormone, androgen, puberty, development, production, enzyme, levels, deficiency

Introduction Applying the knowledge on dihydrotestosterone-related processes spans from the prenatal development of organs to aging-related complications in males. A clinician can single-handedly tackle the issues throughout the age spectrum. This hormone finds its utility as an essential hormone in males until puberty, after which it is considered an etiology for certain diseases. The dual function of this hormone places it in the basic science and applied field of medicine. This activity aims to outline the basic biochemistry of the hormone, its physiological functions at different stages of development, and its role in certain pathological conditions. Fundamentals Androgens are endogenous steroid hormones.
