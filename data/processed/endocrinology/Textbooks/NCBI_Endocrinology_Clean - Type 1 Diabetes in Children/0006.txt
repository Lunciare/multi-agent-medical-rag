History and physical also focus on assessing issues related to glucose monitoring, insulin delivery (e.g., lipodystrophy, skin tolerance to medical adhesives on diabetes technology), and screening for symptoms of associated medical issues such as thyroid dysfunction or celiac disease. As most children with type 1 diabetes are otherwise healthy, history and physical is usually limited to assessment of pertinent diabetes care. Evaluation Particular attention is paid to home glucose monitoring to learn the patterns of glucose variability and their relation to life circumstances such as school, exercise, and physical stresses such as illness and menses. Insulin dose adjustment is performed with child and family input, as appropriate. Hemoglobin A1c is typically measured at clinic visits as a measure of average glucose over the prior two to three months.
