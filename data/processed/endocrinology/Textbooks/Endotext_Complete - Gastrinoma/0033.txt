A carefully taken clinical, personal, and family history of endocrinopathies can be particularly important in suspecting MEN1/ZES, because up to 75% have a family history of MEN1 ( Table 4 ) and 24-42% have a personal history compatible with renal colic ( 30 , 87 , 89 ). The presence of the MEN1 can affect the manifestations of ZES and aspects of its presentation, which will be discussed in a later section dealing with the diagnosis of ZES.
