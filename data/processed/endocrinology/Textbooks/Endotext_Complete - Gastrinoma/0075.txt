Other features of gastrinomas associated with a poor prognosis including advanced ENET/WHO classification, higher ENET/WHO grade, poor differentiation, other histological features, and rapid growth ( Table 10 ) ( 65 , 142 , 301 , 814 ). As mentioned earlier in the pathology section of this paper, both the recently developed TNM tumor classification systems (ENETs, UICC/AJCC/WHO) and the tumor grading systems have been shown to be the most important single factors in numerous multivariate analyses for predicting overall survival or disease-free survival in all NENs ( pNEN, GI-NENs (Carcinoids) ( Table 10 ) ( 115 , 116 , 142 , 815 - 817 ). Most (>90%) of gastrinomas are well differentiated NENs (Grade G1 or G2), and at present there is only one study just including only gastrinomas showing the importance prognostic effect of grade on survival of ZES patients ( 65 ).
