The increased widespread use of PPIs has made the diagnosis of ZES more difficult ( 51 , 55 , 56 , 59 , 78 , 185 ). PPIs are potent gastric acid suppressants and because of their long durations of action (up to one week) ( 40 , 55 , 553 - 556 ) they induce hypergastrinemia in 80-100% of normal ( 55 , 56 , 59 , 78 , 184 , 376 , 515 , 538 - 543 ).
