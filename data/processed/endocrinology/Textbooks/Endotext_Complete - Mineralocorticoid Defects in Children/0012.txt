Sodium bicarbonate or sodium citrate/citric acid 2-3 mEq/kg daily Titrate based on bicarbonate levels CONCLUSION Isolated defects in aldosterone synthesis or action are rare in children; however, it is important to identify these disorders to prevent life-threatening complications. Infants may present with salt wasting crisis while older children may present with failure to thrive, mild hyponatremia, and metabolic acidosis. The two major categories of isolated hypoaldosteronism include aldosterone synthesis defects and aldosterone resistance. There are several genes associated with isolated hypoaldosteronism, and genetic testing is an important diagnostic tool. Treatment and prognosis depend on the underlying etiology. REFERENCES 1.
