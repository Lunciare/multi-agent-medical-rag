CLINICAL MANAGEMENT OF ANTI-PSYCHOTIC-INDUCED HYPERPROLACTINEMIA Drug-induced hyperprolactinemia should be considered in the differential diagnosis of elevations of prolactin levels, sometimes greater than 200 µg/L (4260 mIU/L). Particularly when serum prolactin levels exceed 80-100 µg/L (1700-2130 mIU/L), pituitary magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) should be performed to rule out the presence of any underlying pituitary or hypothalamic masses that may contribute to hyperprolactinemia ( 193 ).
