Complications A pancreatic pseudoaneurysm can rupture and the most common location for the rupture is into the cyst, which often presents with sudden onset of abdominal pain or bleeding. Other rare complications of pancreatic pseudoaneurysms include extrahepatic biliary tract obstruction and AV fistula formation. The more common complications are: Hemorrhage Shock Multiorgan failure Death Postoperative and Rehabilitation Care Post-surgery, the patient must be monitored for hemorrhage which has a reported incidence of 20-70%. The bleeding may be due to trauma to the vessels, inadequate vessel control, or loose sutures.
