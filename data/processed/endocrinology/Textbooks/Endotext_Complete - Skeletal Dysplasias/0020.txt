CLASSIFICATION OF OSTEOPETROSIS Osteopetrosis can be further divided into ‘osteoclast rich’ and ‘osteoclast poor’ subtypes. Osteoclast rich subtypes occur from impaired osteoclastic function while osteoclast poor subtypes occur from failed differentiation of hematopoietic stem cells into osteoclast precursors.
