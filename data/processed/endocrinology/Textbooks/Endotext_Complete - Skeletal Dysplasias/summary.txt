Skeletal Dysplasias
KEYWORDS: pubmed, dysplasia, short, skeletal, bone, height, growth, osteopetrosis, disorders, free, stature, short stature, pmc, article pubmed, pmc free

ABSTRACT Skeletal dysplasias represent a complex and heterogeneous group of over 770 genetic disorders characterized by abnormalities in the development, growth, and maintenance of the skeleton. Though individually rare, these conditions collectively present a significant burden in pediatrics, particularly within endocrinology. Disproportionate growth failure, metabolic bone disease, bone fragility, and developmental delays frequently prompt investigation. Advanced molecular diagnostics, including next-generation sequencing and targeted gene panels, have revolutionized the classification and understanding of skeletal dysplasias. Contemporary frameworks integrate radiographic phenotypes with genotypic data, as reflected in the most recent (2023) Nosology and Classification of Genetic Skeletal Disorders. This dual approach not only refines diagnostic accuracy but also facilitates prognostic insight and genetic counseling.
