Following SHOX gene defects, heterozygous mutations in ACAN are the second most common cause of monogenic short stature, accounting for 1.4% of cases ( 52 , 53 ). Treatment with growth hormone and gonadotropin-releasing hormone analogs have been used in patients with ACAN mutations to halt bone age progression and improve final adult height ( 54 ). Skeletal Dysplasias That Mimic Rickets Metaphyseal dysplasias are a heterogeneous group of skeletal dysplasias characterized by metaphyseal irregularities in the long bones causing short stature and varus deformities. The major types, metaphyseal dysplasia Schmid (MIM 156500), metaphyseal dysplasia Spahr (MIM 250400), and metaphyseal anadysplasia (MIM 602111) mimic rickets due to their presentation with metaphyseal irregularities and bowing deformities, therefore, is important to distinguish these skeletal dysplasias from disorders of calcium and vitamin D metabolism ( 55 ).
