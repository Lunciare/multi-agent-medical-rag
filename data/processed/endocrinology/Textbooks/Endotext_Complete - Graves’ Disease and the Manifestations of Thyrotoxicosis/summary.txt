Graves’ Disease and the Manifestations of Thyrotoxicosis
KEYWORDS: pubmed, thyroid, disease, clin, endocrinol, patients, graves, clin endocrinol, graves disease, metab, endocrinol metab, metab pubmed, free, thyrotoxicosis, article

ABSTRACT Graves' disease (GD) is an autoimmune disorder characterized by hyperthyroidism and various systemic manifestations, including thyroid eye disease (TED) and, less commonly, dermopathy. This chapter provides an in-depth review of GD, covering its history, epidemiology, risk factors, and the molecular mechanisms underlying autoimmune hyperthyroidism. Emphasis is placed on emerging insights into the genetic, environmental, and immunological factors contributing to GD's multifactorial pathogenesis. The chapter also explores the pathogenic role of TSH receptor antibodies and their significance in diagnosis and treatment, alongside key clinical features of thyrotoxicosis.
