Diagnostic Studies Radiological imaging—most commonly a CT scan—is the preferred modality for identifying adrenal adenomas, distinguishing them from bilateral adrenal hyperplasia, and detecting rare adrenal malignancies. However, studies have found that CT imaging alone may not reliably differentiate between these conditions. The overall reported sensitivity and specificity of CT imaging for detecting aldosterone-producing adenomas are about 78% and 75%, respectively.
