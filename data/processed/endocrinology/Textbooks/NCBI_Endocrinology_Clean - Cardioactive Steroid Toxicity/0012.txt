Serum digoxin concentrations do not reliably reflect the severity of toxicity from naturally occurring CAS. If neither the quantity ingested nor the digoxin levels are available, an empiric dose of 10 vials of digoxin immune Fab is an option. In children, 5 vials are permissible. In very small children, clinicians must be cautious of volume overload. If the quantity ingested is known but not the digoxin level, clinicians may calculate the necessary number of vials using the following formula: Number of vials = Dose ingested (in mg) x 1.6 If the levels of digoxin are known, then the number of vials to give is derived by the following formula: Number of vials = (serum digoxin concentration in ng/mL) x (patient's weight in kg)/100 An empiric dose of 10 vials in adults and 5 vials in children is appropriate for severe toxicity. General Management Hyperkalemia is a marker of severe toxicity and typically corrects rapidly following Fab administration as Na + , K + -ATPase function recovers and potassium shifts intracellularly.
