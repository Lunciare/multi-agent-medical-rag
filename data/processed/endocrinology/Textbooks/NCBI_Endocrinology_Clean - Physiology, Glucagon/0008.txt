Both PGC-1 and HNF-4 are coactivators that increase the transcription of the PEPCK gene. In addition to the above functions, glucagon inhibits glycolysis by inhibiting phosphofructokinase-1. Glucagon also stimulates lipolysis and ketogenesis, and the ratio of glucagon to insulin perceived by the liver is a critical signal for the liver to convert fatty acids to ketone bodies, as in diabetic hyperglycemic coma. Related Testing Glucagon has a half-life of 3-6 minutes. Hence, the sample collection should be in chilled tubes with a proteolytic enzyme inhibitor. In adults and children, normal plasma levels are less than 120 pg/ml, whereas in cold blood, levels can reach as high as 215 pg/ml. The sample collection should be performed using chilled ethylenediaminetetraacetic acid (EDTA) or dipotassium EDTA with aprotinin tubes after overnight fasting. Storage pending measurement should be at -70°C after separation from the cells.
