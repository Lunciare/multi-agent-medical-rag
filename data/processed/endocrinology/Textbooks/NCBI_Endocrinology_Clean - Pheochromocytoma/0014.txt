Treatment / Management The definitive treatment for pheochromocytoma is surgical resection, with preoperative α- and β-adrenergic blockade being crucial. Unilateral Pheochromocytoma Most sporadic tumors are unilateral. According to the Endocrine Society Clinical Practice Guidelines, adrenalectomy via a minimally invasive approach is the preferred treatment for most unilateral adrenal pheochromocytomas. Open adrenalectomy may be considered for larger tumors or in cases where the tumor is considered invasive. Bilateral Pheochromocytoma Bilateral neoplasms are most commonly associated with hereditary cases, particularly MEN2 and VHL disease. In these cases, bilateral total adrenalectomy necessitates lifelong steroid replacement, which can lead to long-term adverse effects. Alternatively, bilateral partial or cortical-sparing adrenalectomy can be performed via any surgical approach.
