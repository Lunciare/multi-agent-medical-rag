Additionally, α-2 adrenoceptors at synaptic nerve terminals inhibit the release of norepinephrine. Histopathology Histopathological evaluation of pheochromocytomas typically reveals a zellballen pattern characterized by nests of chromaffin cells. These cells show strong positivity for chromogranin, synaptophysin, CD56, and focal positivity for S100. The presence of chromaffin cells in the extra-adrenal tissue is the only pathognomonic characteristic of the malignant form of the entity. History and Physical In pheochromocytoma, episodes of hypertension are often paroxysmal and have been associated with symptoms such as headaches, tachycardia, and sweating.
