The lack of an ability of ATT to meaningfully improve net clinical outcomes in those with DM without established ASCVD is a source of disappointment and demands future attention. Trials have focused on aspirin but it is clear that people with DM may have a poor response ( 111 ). As well as trials exploring novel regimens of aspirin, trials testing P2Y 12 inhibitor monotherapy, which may offer pharmacodynamic advantages over aspirin in this group, are warranted ( 134 ). Finally, targeting the pathological abnormalities that cause hypofibrinolysis in diabetes, such as inhibition of PAI-1 activity, may offer an alternative management strategy to further reduce vascular occlusive disease in diabetes, while keeping the risk of bleeding to a minimum.
