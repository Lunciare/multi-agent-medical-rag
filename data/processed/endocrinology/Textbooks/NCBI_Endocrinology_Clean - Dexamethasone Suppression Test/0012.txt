Results, Reporting, and Critical Findings Low-Dose Dexamethasone Suppression Test In either of the low-dose dexamethasone suppression tests (overnight or 2-day), a serum cortisol level of 1.8 mcg/dL (50 nmol/L) is the recommended cut-off value that increases the diagnostic sensitivity of the test to approximately 95%. However, at this cut-off value, the specificity of the 2-day test is superior compared to that of the overnight test (97%-100% vs 86%). A recent meta-analysis showed that abnormal and expected results for the 1 mg overnight test had positive and negative likelihood ratios of 11.6 and 0.09, respectively. On the other hand, for the 2-day 2-mg test, abnormal and normal results had positive and negative likelihood ratios of 7.3 and 0.8, respectively.
