A randomized controlled trial and its long-term follow-up found that tight glycemic control in hyperglycemic preterm infants improved early growth but increased the incidence of hypoglycemia, with no long-term neurodevelopmental benefits observed at school age. A Cochrane review concluded that evidence is insufficient to determine whether insulin treatment or reducing GIR improves outcomes in hyperglycemic very low birth weight infants, highlighting the need for further research.
