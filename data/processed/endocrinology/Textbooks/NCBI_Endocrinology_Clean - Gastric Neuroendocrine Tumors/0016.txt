Systemic therapy for unresectable or metastatic disease Systemic treatments include: Somatostatin analogs (SSAs): First-line therapy for somatostatin receptor-positive tumors to control symptoms and slow disease progression. Octreotide 100 to 250 µg subcutanously 3 times daily or octreotide LAR 20 to 30 mg intramucularly Lanreotide 120 mg subcutanously every 4 weeks Therapeutic levels of octreotide would not be expected to be reached for 10 to 14 days after LAR injection. Short-acting octreotide can be added for long-acting SSAs for rapid relief or breakthrough symptoms. Telotristat 250 mg 3 times daily by mouth can be used for refractory cases of diarrhea in carcinoid syndrome. Also, this agent can be used if symptoms are not controlled towards the end of long-acting SSAs. PRRT: 68 Ga-dotatate PET-positive tumors may be treated with 177 Lu-dotatate therapy. 177 Lu-dotatate therapy is given via peripheral intravascularly every 8 weeks for total of 4 cycles. Hold long-acting SSAs 4 weeks and short-acting SSAs 24 hours prior to 177 Lu-dotatate therapy. SSAs can be resumed 4-24 hours after each 177 Lu-dotatate cycle.
