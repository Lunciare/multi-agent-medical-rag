NHANES 1999–2004 data showed that among participants age ≥40 years, mean C-reactive protein concentration was higher among those with PAD compared to those without PAD among persons with diagnosed diabetes and without diabetes but not among persons with undiagnosed diabetes, where mean C-reactive protein concentration was lower (Table 20.9). The reason for the lower mean C-reactive protein in those with PAD among persons with undiagnosed diabetes is unknown. Mean C-reactive protein concentration was higher in persons with PAD among those diagnosed with diabetes when subjects were further stratified by age, sex, or race/ethnicity, with the exception of non-Hispanic black subjects (Table 20.9).
