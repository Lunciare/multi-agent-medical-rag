The association between Hashimoto thyroiditis and an increased risk of papillary thyroid carcinoma remains unclear, and studies offer conflicting evidence regarding how the presence of Hashimoto thyroiditis affects papillary thyroid carcinoma outcomes. Individuals with Hashimoto thyroiditis face an elevated risk for the rare malignancy of primary thyroid lymphoma, which accounts for 0.5% to 5% of all thyroid cancers.
