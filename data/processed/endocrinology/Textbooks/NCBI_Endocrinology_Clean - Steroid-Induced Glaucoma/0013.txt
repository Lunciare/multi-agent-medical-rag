Below is a list of differential diagnoses that can give rise to high levels of IOP: Primary open-angle glaucoma (POAG) Angle-closure glaucoma Angle-recession glaucoma Pigmentary glaucoma Plateau iris glaucoma Pseudoexfoliation glaucoma Uveitic glaucoma Prognosis Elevated IOP from steroid-induced glaucoma shares a similar prognosis to other types of glaucoma. If the IOP is controlled, the likelihood of glaucoma developing is very low.
