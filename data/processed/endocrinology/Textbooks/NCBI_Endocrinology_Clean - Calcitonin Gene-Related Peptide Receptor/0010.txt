Ubrogepant Dose adjustment is not recommended for patients with mild or moderate hepatic impairment. For patients with severe hepatic impairment, the recommendation is for dose adjustment. Dose adjustment is recommended for patients with severe renal impairment. Atogepant: The adverse effects reported during the clinical studies of atogepant include constipation, nausea, fatigue, weight loss, and decreased appetite. No dosage adjustment is needed for patients with mild to moderate renal impairment. It is dosed at 10 mg daily in patients with severe and end-stage renal disease. Zavegepant: The adverse effects reported during clinical studies of zavegepant include taste disorder, nausea, nasal discomfort, and vomiting. No dosage adjustment is needed for patients with creatinine clearance of 30 ml/min or more or with moderate hepatic impairment. It is not to be used in severe renal or hepatic disease. There is no adequate data on the developmental risk associated with the use of these drugs in pregnant women, as well as their presence in human milk, the effects on milk production, or their effects on the breastfed infant.
