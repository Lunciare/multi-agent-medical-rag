Differential Diagnosis The differential diagnosis of delayed puberty focuses on identifying the underlying etiology, as outlined in the preceding section, and distinguishing between constitutional, functional, hypogonadotropic, and hypergonadotropic causes. Delayed Puberty in Males and Females Chronic illnesses: Anemia, inflammatory bowel disease, cystic fibrosis, untreated congenital heart disease, celiac disease, malabsorption syndromes, chronic liver or kidney disease, tubular dysfunctions, nutritional deprivation, and chronic infections.
