Thus, hydrocortisone and cortisone acetate are the least potent and, therefore, least suppressive agents. Prednisone, prednisolone, methylprednisolone and triamcinolone are moderately suppressive, and dexamethasone suppresses ACTH the longest. Systemic Versus Compartmental Therapy Systemic GC therapy, particularly parenterally, is more likely to suppress the HPA axis.
