Given the complexity of presentation, children with developmental delay and signs of early puberty warrant comprehensive endocrine evaluation and multidisciplinary follow-up. Beyond isolated genetic forms, CPP is also a component of numerous rare syndromes. Recognition of these syndromic forms is clinically important, as they often present with additional phenotypic features that may guide diagnosis and management. These syndromes may involve structural brain abnormalities, imprinting defects, or tumor predisposition syndromes affecting the hypothalamic-pituitary axis.
