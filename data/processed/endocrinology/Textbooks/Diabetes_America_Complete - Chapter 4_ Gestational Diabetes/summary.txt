SUMMARY Since the 1979 publication on “classification and diagnosis of diabetes mellitus and other categories of glucose intolerance” by the National Diabetes Data Group, gestational diabetes has been defined as “carbohydrate intolerance of variable severity with onset or recognition during pregnancy.” The diagnosis and treatment of gestational diabetes focus on the prevention or reduction of adverse outcomes.
