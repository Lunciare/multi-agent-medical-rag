In a survey of ambulatory physician practices in the United States, a similar pattern of increasing metformin use and decreasing thiazolidinedione use was observed among prescription patterns between 1997 and 2012 (17). Metformin was prescribed in 23% of visits in 1997 and 53% of visits in 2012, whereas thiazolidine­ dione prescriptions peaked in 2005 at 28% of visits and then declined to 16% of visits by 2012.
