39–1 CHAPTER 39 MEDICATION USE AND SELF-CARE PRACTICES IN PERSONS WITH DIABETES Sharon H. Saydah is a Senior Scientist at the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention, National Center for Chronic Disease and Health Promotion, Division of Diabetes Translation, Hyattsville, MD. Received in final form September 30, 2014. SUMMARY Individuals diagnosed with diabetes often take medications to control and manage their diabetes and engage in self-care practices. Among adults with diagnosed diabetes in the United States, 88.2% use either oral medications, insulin, or a combination of both to treat and control diabetes. Diabetes treatment patterns differ by demographics, duration since diabetes diagnosis, and glycosylated hemo- globin (A1c) levels.
