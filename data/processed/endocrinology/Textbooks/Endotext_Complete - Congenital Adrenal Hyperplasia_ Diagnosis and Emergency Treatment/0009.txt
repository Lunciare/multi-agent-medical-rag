It is not necessary for increased mineralocorticoid doses during these periods of stress. It is imperative for all patients who are receiving corticosteroid replacement therapy, such as patients with CAH, to wear a Medical Alert bracelet or medallion that will enable correct and appropriate therapy in case of emergencies. It is also crucial to re-educate parents at regular intervals on the life-threatening nature of this emergency.
