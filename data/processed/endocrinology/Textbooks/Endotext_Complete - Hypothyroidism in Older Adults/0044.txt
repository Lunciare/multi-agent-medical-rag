Given this lack of stability in TFTs overtime amongst elderly patients with subclinical hypothyroidism, some experts recommend obtaining at least three assessments of thyroid axis status, the second being at least three months after the first, and the third being at least one year after the second, to confirm a diagnosis of persistent subclinical hypothyroidism before initiating treatment ( 372 ).
