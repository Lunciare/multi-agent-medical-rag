A window of opportunity exists if detected early, as the treatment of hyperglycemia may reverse SFN and decrease morbidity. Long-term follow-up of patients with LADA reveals a lower risk in the first 9 years but a higher risk for microvascular complications in later years compared to T2DM after adjustment for several factors. Patients with LADA have a similar degree of carotid artery atherosclerosis as T1DM and T2DM, though a better vascular risk profile. Three studies, the Botnia study, the Freemantle diabetes study, and the HUNT study, all concurred on the increased cardiovascular disease and mortality in LADA, similar to T2DM. Therefore, LADA is associated with both microvascular and macrovascular complications, like patients with T1DM and T2DM.
