Cardiac Chromaffin Cell Pheochromocytoma
KEYWORDS: cardiac, pheochromocytomas, tumors, tumor, patients, cardiac pheochromocytomas, surgical, primary, primary cardiac, imaging, cardiac tumors, including, blood, chest, pheochromocytoma

Introduction Cardiac pheochromocytomas (CPs) are very rare primary neuroendocrine catecholamine-secreting tumors that arise from the sympathetic paraganglia, which is made up of chromaffin cells. Paraganglia are non-neuronal cells that originate from the neural crest. The incidence of primary cardiac tumors ranges from 0.01 to 0.3% by autopsy. Thus far, CP is the rarest form of primary cardiac tumors. Nearly 98% of pheochromocytomas are found in the abdomen, with the adrenal gland being the most common site). Less than 2% of pheochromocytomas are found in the chest, with the heart being the least common site.
