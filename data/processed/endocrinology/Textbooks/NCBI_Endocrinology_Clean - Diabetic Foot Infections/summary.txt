Diabetic Foot Infections
KEYWORDS: foot, diabetic, patients, diabetic foot, infections, oxygen, infection, diabetes, osteomyelitis, ulcers, bone, disease, ulcer, amputation, healing

Introduction Approximately 29 million people have diabetes in the United States, and approximately 25% of people older than 65 have diabetes. The incidence of diabetes worldwide is projected to increase by 55% over the next 20 years, so this problem is only going to get worse. Approximately 75% of diabetic neuropathies are distal symmetric polyneuropathy (DSP). Once peripheral neuropathy develops, the annual incidence of ulcer formation increases from <1 % to >7%. The 3-year mortality for people with diabetes increases from 13% to 28% with an ulcer. The other major factor in diabetic foot infections is compromised blood flow.
