In addition, sex-specific analysis found that women had a threefold increased risk of diabetes-associated mortality, whereas men had a twofold increased risk (6). This reflected a large difference in absolute risk for women versus men. The age- and sex-adjusted all-cause mortality rate (per 1,000 person-years) for women with diabetes during the time period 1976–2001 was 12.3 compared to 5.2 for women without diabetes (6).
