Screening involving lipid measurements, family and medical history, and physical examination is needed to identify affected individuals; cascade screening can be helpful. Lifestyle modification is the first-line therapy for hyperlipidemia in pediatric patients but is usually not sufficient to achieve goal LDL levels. Available evidence suggests that treatment with lipid-lowering pharmacotherapy, such as statins, is effective and generally safe in the short and medium-term. However, further studies are needed to determine the long-term safety and efficacy in preventing ASCVD of lipid lowering medication in pediatric patients with FH.
