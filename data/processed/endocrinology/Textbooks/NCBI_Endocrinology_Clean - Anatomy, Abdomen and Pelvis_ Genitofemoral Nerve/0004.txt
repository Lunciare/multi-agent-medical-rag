Surgical Considerations Inguinal hernia repair During surgery to repair an inguinal hernia, one has to visualize the genitofemoral nerve, which is found within the deep inguinal ring. If damaged, the patient will complain of pain in the scrotal area and anterior thigh. The injury can result in genitofemoral neuralgia, causing morbidity as no good treatment currently exists. For exam purposes, one must differentiate between the genitofemoral and ilioinguinal nerves, which can be injured during surgery. Lumbar spinal interbody fusion During lumbar spine surgical procedures, the surgeon must know the anatomic relationships between the lumbar plexus and the intervertebral disc at all levels. This concept is particularly relevant when performing lateral interbody fusion by minimally invasive retroperitoneal trans-psoas approaches.
