Anemic states falsely increase HbA1C values, whereas their treatment falsely decreases it. Kidney disease can affect the values and may increase or decrease values based on the treatment the patient is getting. Erythropoietin can falsely lower HbA1c values. Hemoglobin variants like HbS and HbC also cause variations in HbA1c values. The US Preventive Services Task Force's recommendation is to screen for diabetes between 40 and 70 years who are overweight or obese and repeat testing every 3 years if results are normal.
