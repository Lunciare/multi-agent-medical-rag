Out of all these transcription factors, the mutation of Pit-I and PROP-I leads to familial cases of multiple pituitary hormone deficiencies. Blood Supply and Lymphatics The blood supply of the pituitary is unique because it is connected to the hypothalamus. This capillary system is known as the hypothalamohypophyseal portal system. The superior hypophyseal arteries form the primary capillary plexus. This plexus supplies blood to the median eminence.
