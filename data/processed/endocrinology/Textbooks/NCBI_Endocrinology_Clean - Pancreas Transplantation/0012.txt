A new patient is put 1 of the 3 lists (SPK, PTA, PAK) in the given year without being listed in the previous year. Patients listed in more than one list or at multiple centers are counted once. The waiting list for pancreas transplants is growing in many countries. The NHS Blood and Transplant showed that over 250 patients were waiting for transplantation in March 2015. More recent data from around the world suggests swelling waiting lists. Technique or Treatment A pancreas and a kidney can be transplanted serially or simultaneously. The procedure takes 3-4 hours when the pancreas alone is transplanted, while if combined with the kidney, it takes 6-8 hours. The kidney is placed on the left of the abdomen, while the pancreas is on the right. The native pancreas and kidneys are left in place.
