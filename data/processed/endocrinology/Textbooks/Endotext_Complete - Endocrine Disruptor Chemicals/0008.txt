Reproductive System Over the past few decades there has been a surge in the incidence of reproductive system related disorders among both the males and females. EDCs can be attributed to this surge. Exposure to EDCs especially phytoestrogens have resulted in early menarche and polycystic ovarian diseases (PCODs) in adolescent girls.
