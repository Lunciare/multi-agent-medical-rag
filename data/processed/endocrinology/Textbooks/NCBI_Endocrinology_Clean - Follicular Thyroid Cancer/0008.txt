Nx signifies regional adenopathy is not assessable, N0 means no palpable cervical adenopathy, N1 means ipsilateral cervical adenopathy, N2 means contralateral or bilateral cervical adenopathy, and finally, N3 means fixed cervical nodes. M means metastasis, with M0 indicative of no distant metastasis, and M1 indicates distant metastasis. Follicular thyroid carcinoma is classified into stages from I through IV. Stage I under 45 years old is any T, N, and M0, and over 45 years old is T1, N0, M0; for stage II under 45 years old is any T, N, and M1, and over 45 years old is T2, N0, M0; stage III is described for over 45 years of age with T4, N0, M0, and stage IV is defined with any T, N, M1. Prognosis The thyroid cancer death rate has remained steady despite the increase in incidence. Thyroid cancer is among the lowest mortality cancers in the United States.
