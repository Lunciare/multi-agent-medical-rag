Nonfunctioning adenomas and prolactinomas have an excellent prognosis if treated promptly with surgery or medical therapy. In contrast, functioning adenomas like Cushing disease and acromegaly are associated with several comorbidities and long-term complications. Increased mortality is associated with delayed medical or surgical treatment, especially in patients with Cushing disease.
