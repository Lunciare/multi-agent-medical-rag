IGF-1/GH: In adults, isolated GH deficiency should be further evaluated with provocative testing, such as insulin-induced hypoglycemia or glucagon stimulation tests. Conditions such as poorly controlled diabetes, malnutrition, sepsis, hypothyroidism, and hepatic or renal failure can impact the IGF-1 and GH levels. Cortisol: Early morning cortisol levels can help assess the function of the hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal axis. A morning cortisol level greater than 10 μg/dL suggests an intact hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal axis function. A cosyntropin stimulation test should be performed if morning cortisol levels are equivocal or low. TSH/free T4: Low free T4 with normal or low TSH suggests secondary hypothyroidism. Treatment / Management The management of pituitary adenomas requires collaboration between an endocrinologist and a neurosurgeon to develop an individualized, patient-centric approach.
