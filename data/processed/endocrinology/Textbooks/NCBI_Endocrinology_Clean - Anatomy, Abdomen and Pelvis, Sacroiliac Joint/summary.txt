Anatomy, Abdomen and Pelvis, Sacroiliac Joint
KEYWORDS: joint, sacroiliac, pain, sacroiliac joint, iliac, ligament, spine, surfaces, cartilage, artery, sacrum, posterior, articular, ligaments, joints

Introduction The sacroiliac (SI) joint articulates surfaces of the sacrum and the ilium and functions as the transition between the spine and the pelvis. The main roles of the SI joint are to provide stability and offset the load of the trunk to the lower limbs. The stability of the joint is provided by an extensive architecture of ligamentous structures. Structure and Function The SI joint is a diarthrodial synovial joint. It is surrounded by a fibrous capsule containing a joint space filled with synovial fluid between the articular surfaces. The articular surface is made up of two, strong, C-shaped layers.
