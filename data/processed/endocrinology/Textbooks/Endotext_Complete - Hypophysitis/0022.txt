Generally, the sella is not enlarged, pituitary enhancement is homogeneous, and the pituitary stalk often has a smooth thickening. Anti-PD-1/PD1-L1 antibody-induced hypophysitis typically lacks MRI changes and causes no mass effect symptoms ( Table 10 ). The onset of ICI-induced hypophysitis varies according to the drug used ( Table 9 ); early onset has been reported, but it can also appear several months after the initiation of the immunotherapy (144, 145). The risk of hypophysitis with Ipilimumab may be dose-dependent, with a higher prevalence reported in those receiving 10 mg/kg vs. 3 mg/kg (146-148); however, a systematic review and meta-analysis did not find a positive correlation between ICI dose used and hypophysitis risk (128). Patients receiving concomitant cytotoxic chemotherapy or with brain radiotherapy-pretreated metastases might be protected from the risk of developing hypophysitis, presumably through immune cell depletion ( 108 , 149).
