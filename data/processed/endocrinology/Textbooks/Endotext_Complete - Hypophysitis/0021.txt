Both forms of hypophysitis are more commonly associated with an initial deficit of ACTH, FSH/LH and TSH, but symptoms of adrenal insufficiency and confirmed ACTH deficiency are much more common in patients with ICI-induced hypophysitis ( 8 , 126, 127). AVP deficiency can occur in a substantial share of primary hypophysitis cases (i.e., the infundibulo-neurohypophysitis and panhypophysitis variants), while it is extremely rare in immune checkpoint-induced hypophysitis (143). Pituitary enlargement and visual impairment are much more common in primary hypophysitis, while the size of the pituitary may appear normal in ICI-induced hypophysitis (in the absence of a baseline pituitary MRI), and optic chiasm involvement is rare ( 5 , 8 ).
