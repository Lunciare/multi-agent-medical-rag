Epidemiology Hypophysitis may occur as a complication during treatment with ICI. Ipilimumab, a monoclonal antibody against the cytotoxic T lymphocyte antigen-4 (CTLA-4) is the drug that has been more strongly associated with this immune-related adverse event ( Table 8 ) (5, 115-125) . The risk developing hypophysitis is up to 18% in patients treated with anti-CTLA-4 antibodies and <5% in patients treated with anti-programmed death 1 (PD1) or programmed death 1 ligand 1 (PD1-L1) antibodies (126-130).
