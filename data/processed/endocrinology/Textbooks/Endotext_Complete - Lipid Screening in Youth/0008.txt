In fact, since nearly 35-45% of youth with abnormal lipids are of normal weight, focusing on excess weight in youth misses a substantial proportion of the population with lipid problems ( 59 ). While the origins of both abnormal lipids and obesity stem from suboptimal diets and activity levels, the two are not synonymous. This epidemiological conundrum is a key pillar supporting the NHLBI guidelines that recommend extending selective screening to universal screening for youth, depending on age group ( Table 2 ).
