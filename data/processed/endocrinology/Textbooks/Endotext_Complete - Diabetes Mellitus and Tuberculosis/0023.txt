Chronic kidney diseases can predispose to TB and its management is complicated by drug toxicity and dose adjustment of ATT is required along with careful monitoring of response and renal functions. In case of ATT induced hepatotoxicity, ATT is withdrawn and second line agents are substituted. Once liver enzymes normalize the first line drugs are restarted cautiously. Effective preventive strategies like vaccination against TB and aggressive prevention and management of DM will be the approach to be adopted till time throws new light on the means to fight these dual epidemics more effectively.
