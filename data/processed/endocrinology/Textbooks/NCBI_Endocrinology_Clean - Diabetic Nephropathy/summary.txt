Diabetic Nephropathy
KEYWORDS: diabetic, nephropathy, diabetic nephropathy, patients, renal, diabetes, kidney, albuminuria, outcomes, associated, disease, control, additionally, progression, increased

Introduction Diabetic nephropathy is the leading cause of end-stage renal disease (ESRD) in many developed countries, including the United States. As a microvascular complication, diabetic nephropathy affects individuals with both type 1 diabetes (T1D) and type 2 diabetes (T2D). The condition presents with persistent albuminuria and a progressive decline in the glomerular filtration rate (GFR). Substantial evidence indicates that early, aggressive treatment can delay or prevent the progression of the disorder. While diabetic nephropathy can develop in both T1D and T2D, the majority of diabetes cases (>90%) are T2D, which is primarily insulin-resistant.
