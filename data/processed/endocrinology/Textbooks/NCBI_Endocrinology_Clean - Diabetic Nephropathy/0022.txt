However, several randomized controlled trials have demonstrated improved cardiac outcomes with low-dose spironolactone in this population. Finerenone is a selective, nonsteroidal mineralocorticoid antagonist approved for managing CKD associated with T2D. Finerenone functions as a bulky, passive antagonist of the mineralocorticoid receptor. The medication has been shown to reduce albuminuria and improve renal and cardiovascular outcomes in patients with CKD and T2D, as demonstrated in multiple studies, including FIDELITY-DKD, FIGARO-DKD, and FINEARTS-HF. Finerenone has demonstrated effectiveness in patients with and without reduced ejection fraction. Additionally, evidence suggests it may help prevent or delay the onset of heart failure in individuals with T2D and CKD. The ARTS trial demonstrated that finerenone is at least as effective as spironolactone, with lower rates of hyperkalemia and other adverse effects. Additionally, data analysis indicates that finerenone improves albuminuria independently of changes in BP or GFR.
