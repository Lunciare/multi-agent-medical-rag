Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia Type 4
KEYWORDS: pubmed, free, pmc, free article, article pubmed, article, pmc free, endocrine, pituitary, multiple, multiple endocrine, com, mutations, neoplasia, mdtext com

ABSTRACT Multiple Endocrine Neoplasia Type 4 (MEN4) (OMIM #610755) has many similarities with MEN1, but is caused by germline mutations in CDKN1B . MEN4 is less common than MEN1. Clinical manifestations of MEN4 encompass: primary hyperparathyroidism, pituitary adenomas. and gastroenteropancreatic neuroendocrine neoplasms. In line with MEN1 other neoplasms may occur.
