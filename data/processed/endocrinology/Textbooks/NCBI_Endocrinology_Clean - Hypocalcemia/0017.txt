Complications Patients with severe hypocalcemia of less than 7 mg/dl and those with an acute drop in calcium level can develop seizures or life-threatening arrhythmias. Checking an ECG and aggressively correcting calcium levels in these situations is imperative. Patients with poorly controlled hypoparathyroidism are at increased risk of developing kidney problems, including kidney stones and chronic kidney disease, and overall decreased quality of life.
