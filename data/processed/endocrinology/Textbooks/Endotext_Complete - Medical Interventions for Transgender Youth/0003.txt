Despite the rise in the number of TGD people seeking care, there remains a lack of education and knowledge among providers as to how best serve this group ( 20 ). In this article, we define terminology, briefly review gender development, review current guidelines regarding medical treatment of pediatric TGD individuals and mental health considerations. EPIDEMIOLOGY Population-based studies in the U.S. report that 1.8% of youth and 0.6% of adults identify as transgender ( 21 , 22 ). Gender diverse describes individuals with a variety of gender identities across the gender spectrum, including those who identify as transgender ( Table 1 ). Gender dysphoria, which describes the distress associated with a conflict between gender identity and anatomy or sex, is a listed diagnosis in the Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders 5 th edition (DSM-5, Table 2 ) ( 23 ).
