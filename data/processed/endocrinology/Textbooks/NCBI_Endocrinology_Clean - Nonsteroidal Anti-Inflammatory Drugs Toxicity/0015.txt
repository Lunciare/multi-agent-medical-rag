Management of aspirin toxicity may require specific interventions, such as urinary alkalinization with intravenous sodium bicarbonate to enhance salicylate excretion, and, in severe cases, hemodialysis to rapidly reduce salicylate levels. These measures are generally not applicable to overdoses involving non-aspirin NSAIDs. While the fundamental management of NSAID toxicity centers on supportive care and symptomatic management, recognizing the differences between aspirin and other NSAIDs is crucial. Aspirin toxicity may necessitate more aggressive interventions, including urinary alkalinization and hemodialysis, due to its unique toxicokinetics. Healthcare providers should be vigilant in identifying the specific NSAID involved in an overdose to implement the most appropriate and effective treatment strategies.
