Bacterial Infections in Diabetes
KEYWORDS: diabetes, infections, pubmed, patients, infection, foot, bacterial, risk, diabetic, patients diabetes, diabetes mellitus, mellitus, type, associated, free

ABSTRACT Bacteria are microscopic single-celled organisms that exist in millions inside and outside the human body. Some bacteria are harmful and can cause a multitude of diseases in human beings. Diabetes mellitus, being a global pandemic, serves as an important cause of susceptibility to bacterial infections. Uncontrolled hyperglycemia is associated with impaired innate and adaptive immune responses that predispose to bacterial infections. In addition, chronic complications of diabetes like neuropathy (sensorimotor and autonomic) and peripheral vascular disease can lead to skin ulcerations with secondary bacterial infections. Diabetes also increases the incidence of infection related mortality.
