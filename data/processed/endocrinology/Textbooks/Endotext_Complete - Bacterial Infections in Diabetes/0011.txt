SSTIs commonly seen in diabetes include cellulitis, abscess, decubitus ulcer, folliculitis, impetigo, carbuncle and furuncle, and surgical site infections. SSTI-associated complications such as gangrene, osteomyelitis, bacteremia, sepsis, and SSTI-associated hospitalizations are higher in patients with diabetes compared to those without diabetes ( 76 ).
