Catheter Management of Patent Foramen Ovale
KEYWORDS: pfo, closure, pfo closure, catheter, patients, device, left, stroke, transcatheter, procedure, used, atrium, following, complications, foramen

Introduction Patent foramen ovale (PFO) is a small opening between the top 2 chambers of the heart (see Image. Patent Foramen Ovale). However, it is one of the most common cardiac defects. Several large, population-based studies estimated no differences in incidence (at about 9.2%) between patients with stroke and patients without a stroke. Patent foramen ovale is present in one-fifth of the population. They affect males and females equally.
