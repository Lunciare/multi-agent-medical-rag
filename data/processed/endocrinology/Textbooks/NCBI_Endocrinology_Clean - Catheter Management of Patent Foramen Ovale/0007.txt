The data is inconclusive as to whether intracardiac or transesophageal echocardiographic guidance is better for PFO closure as compared to fluoroscopic guidance alone. One study confirms the efficacy and safety of TTE guidance during the percutaneous closure of PFO, which shortens procedural time and eliminates the need for general anesthesia or endotracheal intubation. However, fluoroscopy guidance has been shown to increase the procedural time and cost associated with balloon sizing compared to TTE, which decreases both of these. After completion of the procedure, transthoracic echocardiography with a bubble study is used to confirm the device's positioning and assess for residual shunting. An electrocardiogram and chest X-ray are also useful for evaluating complications and the accuracy of the procedure. Anticoagulation therapy is given for 6 months post-operation. Dental procedures should be postponed for 6 months. If dental procedures are required, antibiotic prophylaxis should be given to prevent endocarditis within the first 6 months.
