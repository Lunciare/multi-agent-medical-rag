Reports include results showing increased (173,175,176,177), decreased, and no difference (172,174) in coronal caries prevalence or incidence in people with diabetes compared to those without diabetes. Noteworthy are a systematic review (128) and a comprehensive review (178) that both conclude the evidence for associations between type 1 diabetes and caries in children is inconclusive.
