Diabetes is defined by self-report of previously being diagnosed by a physician or by A1c ≥6.5% or FPG ≥126 mg/dL or 2-hour plasma glucose ≥200 mg/dL. Prediabetes is defined as self-report of no previous diabetes diagnosis and A1c 5.7%–6.4% or FPG 100–125 mg/dL or 2-hour plasma glucose 140–199 mg/dL. Normal glucose levels are defined as A1c <5.7%, FPG <100 mg/dL, and 2-hour plasma glucose <140 mg/dL. Data in panel A are crude estimates.
