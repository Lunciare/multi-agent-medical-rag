Nerves There are two known nerves that pass within the structures of the inguinal canal. These nerves are the ilioinguinal and the genitofemoral nerves. A third nerve, iliohypogastric nerve, supply sensation to the skin above the genitalia does not pass through the inguinal canal. It pierces the transversus abdominis then the external oblique in the inguinal area. The ilioinguinal nerve is a branch of L1. It passes through the deep inguinal orifice along with the cord structures. It provides sensation to the anterior perineum and medial and upper thigh. In males, it also provides sensation to the anterior scrotal area. In women, the nerve provides sensation to the labia majora and mons pubis. The genitofemoral nerve is derived from the L1-L2 spinal nerve roots.
