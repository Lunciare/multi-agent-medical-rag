Labiaplasty Labiaplasty is a surgical procedure aimed at altering the size and shape of the labia majora and minora. Indications for labiaplasty include congenital disabilities, aging, cancers, and cosmetic concerns. The procedure focuses on achieving a more aesthetically pleasing appearance of the labial folds. Vaginoplasty Vaginoplasty is a surgical procedure used to reconstruct the vagina or create one. Vaginoplasties are performed for various reasons, including pelvic organ prolapse, congenital disabilities, neoplasms, sex reassignment, and cosmetic purposes. The goal of this procedure is to surgically create a vagina that meets the patient's needs and preferences. Clinical Significance Understanding the anatomy of the female external genitalia holds critical importance in clinical practice. This knowledge aids in diagnosing various diseases and lesions affecting the genitals and is essential for performing procedures in the vulvar area.
