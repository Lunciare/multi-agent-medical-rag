Conversely, TT4 levels are high when the serum TBG concentration is high. The person remains euthyroid provided the feedback regulation of the thyroid gland is intact. Although changes in transthyretin (TTR) concentration rarely give rise to significant alterations in TT4 concentration, 57 the presence of a variant serum albumin with high affinity for T4 58,59 or antibodies against T4 38,39 produce apparent elevations in the measured TT4 concentration, whereas the metabolic status remain normal.
