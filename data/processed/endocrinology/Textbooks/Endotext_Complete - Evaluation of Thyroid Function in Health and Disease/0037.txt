Miscellaneous Biochemical and Physiologic Changes Related to the Action of Thyroid Hormone on Peripheral Tissues Thyroid hormone affects the function of a variety of peripheral tissues. Thus, hormone deficiency or excess may alter a number of determinations used in the diagnosis of illnesses unrelated to thyroid hormone dysfunction. Knowledge of the determinations which may be affected by thyroid hormone is important in the interpretation of laboratory data ( Table 6-7 ).
