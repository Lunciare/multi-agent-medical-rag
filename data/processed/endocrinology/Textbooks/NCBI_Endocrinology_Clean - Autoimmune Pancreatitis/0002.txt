Type 2 AIP is often associated with inflammatory bowel disease and is twice as common in patients with ulcerative colitis or proctitis as in those with Crohn disease. Unlike patients with type 1 AIP, about half of those with type 2 AIP present with abdominal pain or acute pancreatitis. Type 3: Immune checkpoint inhibitor-induced pancreatic injury is a type of progressive immune-mediated pancreatitis, an adverse effect of cancer treatment with immune checkpoint inhibitor drugs.
