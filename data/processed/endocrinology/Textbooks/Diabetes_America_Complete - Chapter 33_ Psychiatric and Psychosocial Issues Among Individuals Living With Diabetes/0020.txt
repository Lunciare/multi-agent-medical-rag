Depression is also associated with greater functional impairment (37,73,74,75) and increased likelihood of dementia (76). Meta-analysis demonstrates that depressive symptoms are associated with increased risk of mortality, primarily among adults with type 2 diabetes (Table 33.11) (77). Although these data cannot speak to causality, the consistency of find­ ings linking depressive symptoms to poor diabetes health outcomes suggests that elevations should be seen as a marker of increased health risk.
