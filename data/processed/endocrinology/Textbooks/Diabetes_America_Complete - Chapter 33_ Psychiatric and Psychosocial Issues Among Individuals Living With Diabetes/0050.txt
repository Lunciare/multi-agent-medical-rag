Research reviewed here is sufficiently suggestive to support continued study, with stronger designs, of pathways linking these problems to diabetes risk and diabetes treatment outcomes. Such studies may elucidate explanatory pathways and inform improvements in the management of diabetes and related psychiatric and psychosocial issues. These pathways are likely to be complex and bidirectional, requiring more comprehensive evaluation than has been common in the existing literature.
