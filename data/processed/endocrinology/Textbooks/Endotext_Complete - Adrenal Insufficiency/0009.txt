In recent onset central AI within 4-6 weeks or severe stress such as sepsis, a ‘normal’ level may still indicate AI. ACTH levels >300ng/L (66pmol/L) or > 2-fold the ULN confirms PAI. Cortisol levels >14.5μg/dL (400nmol/L) indicates normal HPA axis ( 27 ). Morning cortisol levels in early postoperative pituitary surgery higher than 10µg/dL (275nmol/L) are a predictor of corticotroph reserve ( 28 , 29 ).
