Additional therapeutic options for well-differentiated pancreatic neuroendocrine tumors include everolimus, an mTOR inhibitor, sunitinib, a tyrosine kinase inhibitor, and peptide receptor radionuclide therapy. Cytotoxic chemotherapy, employing agents such as streptozocin, doxorubicin, and temozolomide, is indicated for the treatment of malignant insulinoma. Liver-Directed Therapy Given that the liver is the primary site of metastatic disease, targeted therapies for hepatic lesions play a crucial role in the management of patients ineligible for surgical resection. Hepatic artery embolization leverages the principle that liver metastases predominantly derive their blood supply from the hepatic arteries rather than the portal vein. Various techniques, including bland embolization, hepatic chemoembolization, and transarterial radioembolization, are employed to deliver therapeutic agents to the lesions.
