Anatomy, Abdomen and Pelvis: Liver
KEYWORDS: liver, hepatic, portal, vein, blood, right, inferior, lobe, portal vein, branch, left, bile, drains, hepatic artery, metabolism

Introduction The liver is found inferior to the diaphragm and occupies most of the abdomen's right upper quadrant (RUQ). It is mostly intraperitoneal, from the fifth intercostal space in the midclavicular line to the right costal margin. The superior posterior aspect of the liver contains a bare area where the diaphragm and inferior vena cava are found (see Image. The rest of the liver is covered by the visceral peritoneum, which meets the diaphragm at the border of the bare area, forming the coronary ligament. The inferior liver is closely associated with the gallbladder and right kidney (see Image. Inferior Surface of the Liver).
