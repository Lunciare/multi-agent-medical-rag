In some cases, it drains directly into the retrohepatic portion of the inferior vena cava. Nerves The hepatic plexus innervates the liver. This plexus consists of sympathetic fibers from the celiac plexus and parasympathetic fibers from the anterior and posterior vagal trunks. The hepatic plexus accompanies the vessels and biliary ducts within the portal triad. Physiologic Variants Partial liver transplants can be made, primarily by harvesting the right lobe of the liver. Care must be taken to account for possible variations in the anatomy of the liver. Common variations include absent main-right portal vein (14.5%) and multiple bile duct abnormalities. Patients with portal vein abnormalities are highly likely to have multiple bile ducts (88.9%). Surgical Considerations Surgical treatment of portal hypertension secondary to liver damage or portal vein thrombosis is achieved using a trans-hepatic portosystemic shunt (TIPS) procedure. This procedure involves connecting the portal vein, which brings blood into the liver, and the inferior vena cava, which drains blood out of the liver.
