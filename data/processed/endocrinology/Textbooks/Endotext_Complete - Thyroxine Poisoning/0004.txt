Thyroxine Poisoning
KEYWORDS: ingestion, levels, children, thyroid, tablets, serum, charcoal, probably, thyroxine, symptoms, total, lt-, activated, l-thyroxine, gastric


(4g every 8h p.o.) Propylthiouracil (PTU) (May inhibit conversion of T4>T3) Activated charcoal hemoperfusion Plasmapheresis (seldom necessary) Hemodialysis (probably of limited value) Thyroid storm: demands treatment in an Intensive Care Unit. FOLLOW-UP Patients should be monitored for several days to be sure that serum T4 and T3 levels are falling GUIDELINES None applicable.
