Anatomy, Abdomen and Pelvis: Internal Iliac Arteries
KEYWORDS: artery, iliac, internal, iliac artery, internal iliac, branches, arteries, pelvic, gluteal, common, inferior, division, obturator, anterior, umbilical

This condition is typically associated with aortic or common iliac artery stenosis. Isolated occlusion of the internal iliac arteries is less common and more challenging to diagnose than aortoiliac disease. The rich collateral circulation of the branches of the internal iliac artery helps to bypass arterial obstructions resulting from surgery, trauma, or arterial disease. Isolated internal iliac artery aneurysms are relatively uncommon and are frequently associated with atherosclerosis. They occur in only 20% of individuals presenting with isolated aneurysms of either the common iliac, external iliac, or internal iliac artery.
