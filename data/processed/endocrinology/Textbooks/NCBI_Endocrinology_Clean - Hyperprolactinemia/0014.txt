These patients can be managed with medical therapy for a prolonged period. The success of pituitary surgery also depends on the size of the tumor, the serum prolactin level, and the neurosurgeon's experience and expertise. The success rate of pituitary surgery is related inversely to tumor size and prolactin levels. Although microprolactinoma surgery has a high success rate, the recurrence of hyperprolactinemia is relatively high, which is about 17% in patients initially considered cured. In the case of macroprolactinoma, approximately 50% of patients are in remission after surgery.
