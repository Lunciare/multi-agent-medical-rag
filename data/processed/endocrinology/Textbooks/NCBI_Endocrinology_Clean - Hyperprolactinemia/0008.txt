An increase in prolactin secretion is associated with sleep. However, this increase is not part of the circadian rhythm. The peak level of prolactin typically occurs between 4 AM and 7 AM; therefore, the ideal time to measure prolactin is during the mid-morning hours, preferably in a fasting state.. Food has a minimal effect on the serum concentration of prolactin. Therefore, fasting is not always a necessary condition for measuring serum prolactin levels, except when a mild elevation in the initial value is noted. If serum prolactin is elevated, the next step is to determine the cause in the following order: Exclusion of physiological causes Exclusion of pharmacological causes Neuroradiological imaging of the hypothalamic-pituitary region An extensive history and physical examination are crucial for excluding causes of hyperprolactinemia and inquiring about signs and symptoms associated with this condition. Laboratory Tests In a premenopausal female, an initial pregnancy test is essential.
