Adrenal Hypoplasia

Introduction Adrenal hypoplasia is a condition in which there is underdevelopment or hypotrophy of the adrenal cortex due to several clinical conditions. It can be divided into two categories: primary adrenal hypoplasia and secondary adrenal hypoplasia. Primary adrenal hypoplasia is caused by the adrenal hypoplasia congenita (AHC) or an intrinsic defect during adrenal gland development. Adrenal hypoplasia congenita is characterized by hypofunctional and hypertrophic adrenal glands, resulting from defects in the differentiation of the fetal adrenal glands. It occurs in many forms. Secondary adrenal hypoplasia results from pituitary or hypothalamic dysfunction.
