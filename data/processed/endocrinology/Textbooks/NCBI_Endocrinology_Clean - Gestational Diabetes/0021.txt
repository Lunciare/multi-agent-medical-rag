The risk of preeclampsia decreases from 18% to 12% with treatment. If GDM interventions, including dietary modifications or pharmacologic therapy, are implemented early enough, progression to type 2 diabetes in 10 years is reduced by 35% to 40%. Furthermore, even small reductions in BMI can result in a reduced risk for diabetes by 25%. Complications Maternal and fetal complications are associated with GDM. The fetal complications include macrosomia, neonatal hypoglycemia, polycythemia, shoulder dystocia, hyperbilirubinemia, neonatal respiratory distress syndrome, increased perinatal mortality, and hypocalcemia. Maternal complications include preeclampsia, increased risk of developing diabetes mellitus, and increased risk of Cesarean delivery.
