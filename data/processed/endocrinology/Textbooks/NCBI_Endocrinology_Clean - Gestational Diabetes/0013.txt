Despite these recommendations, compliance with postpartum testing remains suboptimal, with only between 23% and 58% of women undergoing appropriate postpartum evaluation. This disparity between postpartum testing guidelines and clinical implementation underscores the need for improved patient education and clinician engagement. Treatment / Management Nonpharmacologic Therapies GDM management begins with nonpharmacologic approaches, including increased physical activity, dietary changes, and glucose monitoring. The recommended amount of exercise for patients with GDM is 30 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic exercise, at least 5 days a week, or a minimum of 150 minutes per week. Additionally, postprandial exercise is often recommended, as it has been shown to help control glucose levels for up to 3 hours after eating.
