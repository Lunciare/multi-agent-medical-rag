In patients with findings during an oral glucose tolerance test (OGTT) of a fasting glucose of more than or equal to 5.5 mmol/L and a glucose increment from the fasting to 2-hour OGTT result of less than 4.6 mmol/L with a positive family history of diabetes, maturity-onset diabetes of the young should be suspected. Fasting glucose more than or equal to 100 mg/dL in patients with a body mass index (BMI) less than 25 kg/m 2 can also be used to help differentiate these conditions. See StatPearls' companion reference, " Maturity Onset Diabetes in the Young ," for more information on this condition.
