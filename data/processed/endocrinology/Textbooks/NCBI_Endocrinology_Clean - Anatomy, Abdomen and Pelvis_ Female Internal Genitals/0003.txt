The cervix is the lower, short, broad cylindrical part of the uterus extending from the internal to the external os and has a narrow central lumen, which opens into the uterine cavity as the internal os and into the vagina as the external os. The external os is seen as a circular opening in nulliparous females and as a horizontal slit in multiparous females. This horizontal slit divides the cervix into an anterior and posterior lip. The narrow upper third of the cervix is the isthmus, which defines the lower uterine segment and is the site for the lower segment cesarean sections. The ovaries are the primary organs of the female reproductive system. They are oval-shaped gonadal structures, approximately 3 x 1.5 x 1 cm in size, and homologous with the testis in males. They are present in pairs with the long axis oriented downward and forward. The ovaries are within the pelvis, just lateral to the uterus.
