Advances in neuroimaging, microsurgical techniques, and multimodal therapy have improved survival outcomes, although specific aggressive subtypes continue to carry a poor prognosis. Abbreviation: CSF, cerebrospinal fluid. Complications Complications of pineal region tumors arise from both the tumor itself and its treatment, including neurological deficits, endocrine dysfunction, and treatment-related morbidity, all of which can significantly affect long-term outcomes and quality of life. Complications include the following: Tumor-related complications Hydrocephalus Parinaud syndrome Raised intracranial pressure Neurological deficits CSF dissemination Treatment-related complications Surgical complications Hemorrhage Venous infarction Visual deficits Infection Cerebellar ataxia Radiotherapy effects Cognitive decline Endocrine dysfunction (hypothalamic-pituitary axis damage) Secondary malignancies Chemotherapy toxicities Myelosuppresion Nephrotoxicity Ototoxicity Infertility Shunt/ETV complications Shunt dependency Infection Obstruction Failure of CSF diversion
