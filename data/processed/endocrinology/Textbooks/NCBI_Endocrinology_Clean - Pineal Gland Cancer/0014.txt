Differential Diagnosis The differential diagnosis of pineal region tumors is broad, reflecting the variety of neoplastic and nonneoplastic lesions that may arise in this location. Careful consideration of clinical presentation, imaging characteristics, and histopathology is essential to distinguish these entities and guide appropriate management. Prognosis Summary of Pineal Region Tumors) and is closely linked to tumor histology, extent of resection, and presence of CSF dissemination. Advances in neuroimaging, microsurgical techniques, and multimodal therapy have improved survival outcomes, although specific aggressive subtypes continue to carry a poor prognosis.
