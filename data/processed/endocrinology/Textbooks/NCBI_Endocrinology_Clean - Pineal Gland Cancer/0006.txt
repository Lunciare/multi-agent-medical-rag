Tumor necrosis is common, and the mitotic rate is typically high. Immunohistochemically, pineoblastomas demonstrate positivity for synaptophysin and SMARCB1, whereas other neuronal markers, including neuron-specific enolase, neurofilament protein, and chromogranin-A, show variable expression. Germinoma Germinomas originate from primordial germ cells and share morphological features with their gonadal counterparts. The tumor cells exhibit large nuclei with prominent nucleoli, and dense lymphocytic infiltration is frequently present. Immunohistochemically, the cells are positive for placental alkaline phosphatase. CSF analysis may reveal elevated placental alkaline phosphatase and β-hCG. History and Physical The clinical presentation of pineal region tumors is mainly related to obstructive hydrocephalus and compression of the tectum or dorsal midbrain. Consequently, regardless of the pathology, the physical examination findings are similar across the different tumor types. Hydrocephalus: History: Headache, nausea, vomiting, failure to thrive, macrocephaly, blurry vision, lethargy, drowsiness, or coma.
