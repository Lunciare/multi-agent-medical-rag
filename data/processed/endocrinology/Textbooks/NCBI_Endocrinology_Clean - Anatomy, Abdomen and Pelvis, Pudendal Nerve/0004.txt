Except for the anal verge (which is the S5 dermatome), it also carries somatic sensations from the skin adjacent to the anus. Perineal Nerve As the pudendal nerve exits the pudendal canal, a second branch diverges, which is called the perineal nerve (see Image . Perineal Arteries and Nerves, Male Perineum). This branch further divides into the deep (muscular) and superficial (cutaneous) perineal nerves. These supply motor and sensory fibers to the structures in the urogenital triangle, including the external urethral sphincter, the posterior scrotum in males, and the labia minora, vaginal vestibule, lower one-fifth of the vaginal canal, and the posterior aspect of the labia majora and in females.
