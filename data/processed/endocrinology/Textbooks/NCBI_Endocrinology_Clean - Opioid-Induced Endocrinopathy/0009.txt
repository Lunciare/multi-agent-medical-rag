For those less fortunate to recover incompletely or for those who are unable to wean from opioid therapy completely, hormone supplementation remains a viable option. Complications Although the majority of sequelae associated with this disease are not immediately life-threatening, the quality of life in a patient suffering from chronic pain compounded by the insidious effects of OIE may be substantial. Of note, several case reports illustrate episodes of adrenal crisis as a possible complication in this patient population. The exact effect and role of glucocorticoid replacement in patients with OIE remain not fully understood.
