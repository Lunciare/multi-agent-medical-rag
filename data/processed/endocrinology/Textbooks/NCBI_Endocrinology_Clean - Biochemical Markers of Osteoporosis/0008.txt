In particular, these markers offer the ability to shed light on osteoporotic processes at the cellular level, adding significant value to their potential use in clinical settings. As with BTMs, miRNA expression can be assessed using serum and plasma samples, typically derived from blood collected through venipuncture. RNA can be extracted using various solvents, then reverse transcribed and amplified using commercially available kits of choice. When using the OsteomiR ® RUO assay for miRNA analysis, a starting sample size of 200 μL of plasma is recommended to ensure adequate RNA yield. After thawing and centrifuging the plasma samples for 5 minutes, synthetic RNA spike-in is mixed with the lysis buffer provided in the assay kit. RNA is then precipitated out of the solution with 100% isopropanol and glycogen. Finally, the OsteomiR ® chemistry kit is used to perform reverse transcription and PCR amplification.
