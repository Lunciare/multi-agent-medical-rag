MicroRNAs in Monitoring Pharmacotherapy Response Elevated levels of specific miRNAs, such as miR-454-3p, miR-26b-5p, and miR-584-5p, have been strongly associated with adherence to denosumab therapy. These miRNAs are also linked to improvements in BMD. As a result, miRNAs can serve as valuable biomarkers for monitoring medication adherence and assessing the effectiveness of pharmacotherapy.
