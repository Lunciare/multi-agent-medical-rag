The GeneChip™ miRNA Array profiles multiple miRNAs simultaneously, while real-time PCR quantifies specific miRNA targets. NGS provides a comprehensive analysis of the entire miRNA transcriptome, offering insights into both known and novel miRNAs involved in bone turnover processes. For a more targeted approach, the OsteomiR ® RUO assay is designed to quantify 19 specific miRNAs related to bone metabolism, making it a valuable tool for researching osteoporotic conditions and bone health. Each method enables precise miRNA expression measurement, offering the potential for more personalized and targeted diagnostic and therapeutic approaches in bone health management. Indications Bone Turnover Biomarkers Studies have shown that BTMs are valuable in identifying patients with accelerated bone turnover, predicting fragility fractures, and monitoring treatment efficacy and regimen adherence. Despite this, BTMs are widely accepted for monitoring treatment response, particularly in the first year of treatment, when changes are not yet detectable through DXA scans.
