This information should be reinforced during the annual follow-up visits by the clinicians and if possible, through a structured patient education program. All patients should be equipped with a steroid emergency card and medical-alert identification to inform health personnel of the need for increased GC doses to avert or treat AC and the need of immediate parenteral steroid treatment in the event of an emergency. Every patient should be equipped with a GC injection kit for emergency use and be educated on how to use it ( Table 12 ).
