However, in the early stages, basal ACTH secretion is normal, while its stress-induced release is impaired. Moreover, with further loss, there is atrophy of zonae fasciculata and reticularis of the adrenal cortex. Therefore, basal cortisol secretion is decreased but aldosterone secretion by the zona glomerulosa is preserved. However, hypotension in SAI may still occur and it is due to decreased vascular tone as a result of reduced vascular responsiveness to angiotensin II and norepinephrine.
