The round ligament helps maintain the anteversion position of the uterus during pregnancy. The cardinal ligaments support the uterus. Embryology The round ligament of the uterus develops from the gubernaculum. In a fetus, the gubernaculum is an undifferentiated mesenchymal tissue attached to ovarian tissue in a female fetus and testicular tissue in a male fetus. During the early stages of urogenital development in the female fetus, the gubernaculum develops as a connective tissue band. This connective tissue ligamentous band is attached to the ovary on one side and the labia majora on the other side.
