Anatomy, Abdomen and Pelvis: Uterus Round Ligament
KEYWORDS: round, uterus, ligament, ligaments, round ligament, pain, round ligaments, pelvic, ovarian, artery, pregnancy, vein, tissue, attached, uterine

Introduction The round ligament of the uterus is fibro-muscular connective tissue. It appears like a round band of rope. One side of the round ligament is attached to the superior and lateral aspect of the uterus. This attachment with the uterus is at the cornu of the uterus. From the cornu of the uterus, the round ligament crosses the pelvis through the deep inguinal ring which then traverses the inguinal canal and then enters the labia majora, where it terminates with its fibers blending into the mons pubis. There are 2 round ligaments, one on each side of the uterus.
