Diabetic Foot Care
KEYWORDS: foot, diabetes, patients, risk, diabetic, diabetic foot, ulceration, amputation, pad, vascular, including, neuropathy, nerve, plantar, deformities

Introduction Diabetes mellitus is a metabolic disorder characterized by chronic hyperglycemia. About 589 million adults aged 20 to 79 years are living with diabetes globally as of 2025, and diabetes-related deaths are estimated at approximately 3 to 4 million annually. (Source: International Diabetes Foundation , 2025) Individuals with diabetes are at increased risk of pedal ulceration due to microvascular, neuropathic, and biomechanical alterations in the foot. Neuropathy reduces pedal sensation, predisposing the foot to pressure- and trauma-related injuries. Microvascular dysfunction impairs blood flow to the lower extremities, delaying wound healing. Hemoglobin A1c (HbA1c) indicates mean blood glucose over 2 to 3 months.
