CARDIOVASCULAR RISK FACTORS Overweight and Obesity Older adults with diabetes have a much higher prevalence of overweight, defined by a body mass index ≥25 kg/m2, or obesity, defined by a body mass index ≥30 kg/m2, than those without diabetes, according to new analyses for Diabetes in America. NHANES data from 2007–2010 showed that 83.3% of adults age ≥65 years with diabetes were overweight or obese compared to 68.8% of adults age ≥65 years without diabetes (p<0.001) (Figure 16.4, Appendix 16.4).
