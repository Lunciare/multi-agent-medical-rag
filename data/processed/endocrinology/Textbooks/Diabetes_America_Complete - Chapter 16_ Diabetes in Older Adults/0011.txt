According to the National Hospital Discharge Survey and the NHIS, in 2008, diabetes-related nontraumatic lower extremity amputation rates were highest among adults age ≥75 years compared to adults age 40–64 years and 65–74 years (6.2 vs. 3.2 and 4.9, respectively, per 1,000 persons with diabetes) (20). From 1996 to 2008, the rates of amputation declined significantly among the U.S. diabetic population, especially for those age ≥75 years (20). GERIATRIC CONDITIONS In addition to the traditional cardiovas­ cular and microvascular complications, the presence of diabetes increases the risk of many geriatric conditions. Much of the previously published data on geri­ atric conditions in this section are from the Study of Osteoporotic Fractures, a multicenter, longitudinal observational study of 9,654 older community-dwelling women that began in 1986. This cohort was established to collect prospective data on osteoporosis and collected data every 2 years for 20 years. The remaining data in this section come from new anal­ yses for Diabetes in America.
