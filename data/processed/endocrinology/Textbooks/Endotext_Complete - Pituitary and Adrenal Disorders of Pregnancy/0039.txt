As with other medical problems encountered in pregnancy, it is important to provide women with evidence-based pre-conception counselling to enable informed shared decision making. Multidisciplinary team and individualized care are essential to ensure prompt diagnosis and effective management of potentially high-risk pituitary and adrenal disease in pregnancy to ensure the best outcomes for both mother and child.
