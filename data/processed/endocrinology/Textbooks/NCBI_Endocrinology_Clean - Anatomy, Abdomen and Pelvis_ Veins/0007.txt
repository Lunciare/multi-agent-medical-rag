The external iliac nodes are arranged in various medial, anterior, and lateral clusters. The medial subgroup of nodes constitutes the primary drainage channel and receives lymph from the inguinal and adductor regions. The clitoris or glans penis, the membranous urethra, the prostate, the uterine cervix, and the upper vagina are drained by these nodes as well. The internal and external iliac nodes both empty into the common iliac nodes. Nerves Many nerves innervate the abdomen and pelvic area. The visceral peritoneum is supplied by autonomic nerves, both sympathetic and parasympathetic, while the spinal nerves innervate the parietal peritoneum. The left and right vagus nerves, celiac plexus, and gastric nerves innervate the abdomen. The lumbosacral trunk, sacral plexus, and coccygeal plexus pass through the pelvic cavity, while the sacral ventral rami innervate the pelvis viscerally and muscularly. Also, parasympathetic pelvic splanchnic fibers and sympathetic sacral splanchnic fibers innervate the viscera of the pelvis, along with the superior hypogastric plexus and sacral plexus.
