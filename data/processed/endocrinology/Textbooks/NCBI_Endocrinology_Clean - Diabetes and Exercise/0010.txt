A 5- to 10-minute session of warm-up exercises, such as walking or low-intensity cycling, is generally recommended before starting physical activity, followed by 5 to 10 minutes of stretching that targets large muscle groups. After completing the main exercise, a 5- to 10-minute cool-down period similar to the warm-up helps gradually lower the heart rate. Technique or Treatment Maintaining adequate fluid intake before, during, and after exercise is essential to compensate for fluid loss due to sweating. Hydration during exercise helps prevent dehydration and supports optimal performance. Monitoring blood glucose levels before, during, and after exercise is also recommended.
