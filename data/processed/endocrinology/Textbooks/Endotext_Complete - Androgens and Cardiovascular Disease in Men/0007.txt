In this study of 210,700 men aged 40-69 years at baseline, with 9 years follow-up, 8,790 had an incident CVD event. Total T was not associated with risk of incident MI, ischemic stroke, hemorrhagic stroke, heart failure, nor major cardiovascular adverse events (MACE) defined as the composite endpoint of non-fatal MI, non-fatal ischemic stroke, and CVD death. The large size of the UK Biobank, and accumulation of outcome events over the period of follow-up, provided power to examine these associations in a robust fashion. UK Biobank used an immunoassay for measurement of serum T which may underestimate results compared to mass spectrometry ( 70 ), and UK Biobank men were generally healthier than the UK male population as a whole ( 71 ).
