Mechanism of Action The mechanism of action is identical to injected GLP-1 RAs described above. Glycemic Efficacy In a meta-analysis of five trials of oral semaglutide vs. placebo, treatment with oral semaglutide reduced HbA1c by 0.89% ( 371 ). In the Pioneer 1 study 703 patients were randomized (mean baseline HbA1c 8.0%) to placebo vs. various doses of oral semaglutide ( 372 ). After 26 weeks of treatment A1c decreased by -0.6% in the 3 mg group, -0.9% in the 7 mg group, and -1.1% in the 14 mg group compared to placebo (P < 0.001 for all results). If the decrease in A1c was adjusted for premature drug discontinuation or initiation of rescue medication the estimated decreases in A1c were -0.7% in the 3 mg group, -1.2% in the 7 mg group, and -1.4% in the 14 mg group (P < 0.001 for all). Studies have also examined the ability of oral semaglutide to lower A1c vs. other drugs. Compared to sitagliptin, oral semaglutide 7mg per day reduced A1c by -0.3% while 14mg per day reduced A1c by 0.5% (P < .001 for both) ( 373 ). In a similar trial with flexible dose adjustment of semaglutide, treatment with semaglutide (60% on 14mg per day) resulted in a 1.4% decrease in A1c while 100mg sitagliptin decreased A1c by 0.7% ( 374 ). In a randomized trial comparing switching to oral semaglutide vs. DPP-4 inhibitor continuation A1c was decreased by 0.7% in the semaglutide group compared to continuing the DPP4 inhibitor ( 375 ). In a trial comparing empagliflozin vs. oral semaglutide, treatment with semaglutide resulted in a greater decrease in A1c compared to empagliflozin (-1.3% vs. -0.9%; P < 0.0001) ( 376 ). In a comparison of liraglutide 1.8mg per day vs. oral semaglutide 14mg per day the change from baseline in A1c was -1.2% (SE 0·1) with oral semaglutide and -1.1% with subcutaneous liraglutide ( 377 ). If the decrease in A1c was adjusted for premature drug discontinuation or initiation of rescue medication then oral semaglutide treatment resulted in a slightly greater decreases in A1c than subcutaneous liraglutide (estimated treatment difference -0·2%).
