Additionally, in several of the large cardiovascular studies described above an increase in DKA was observed (CANVAS Trial- canagliflozin 0.6 vs. placebo 0.3 participants with an event per 1000 patient-years; CREDENCE Trial- canagliflozin 2.2 vs. placebo 0.2 per with an event per 1000 patient-years; DECLARE–TIMI 58-dapagliflozin 27 episodes vs placebo 12 episodes; VERTIS trial 0.3% 5mg ertugliflozin, 0.4% 15mg dose, and 0.1% placebo group) ( 144 , 154 , 156 , 160 ). In a meta-analysis of 10 studies with 71,553 participants the risk of DKA was increased (RR 2.23, 95% CI 1.36-3.63) ( 210 ). Many of the DKA events occurred in patients with T2DM treated with insulin who had reduced or stopped insulin or experienced an intercurrent illness that could precipitate DKA ( 22 , 223 ). In some instances, the patients were thought to have T2DM but actually had latent autoimmune diabetes of adults (LADA), a form of Type 1 diabetes ( 22 ). The hyperglycemia in DKA associated with SGLT2 inhibitors is typically mild because the SGLT2 inhibitors reduce blood glucose levels ( 22 ). SGLT2 inhibitors should be temporarily discontinued in clinical situations known to predispose to ketoacidosis (e.g., prolonged fasting due to acute illness or surgery) (package insert). DKA developing during hospitalizations has been described emphasizing the need for vigilance when continuing SGLT-2 inhibitors in patients admitted to the hospital ( 224 ). Patients should be educated regarding this potential complication and in high-risk patients (for example patients on insulin therapy with a history of poor glycemic control or DKA) one could provide the patient with methods to measure either blood or urine ketone levels at home to facilitate the early diagnosis of DKA. A possible mechanism for the increased risk of DKA is SGLT2 inhibitors increasing plasma glucagon levels thereby increasing ketone production ( 138 , 223 ). In combination with the low insulin levels this could potentiate the development of DKA. OSTEOPOROSIS AND FRACTURES In the CANVAS cardiovascular outcome study, the rate of all fractures was higher in the canagliflozin group than in the placebo group (15.4 vs. 11.9 participants with fracture per 1000 patient-years; HR 1.26; 95% CI 1.04 to 1.52) ( 144 ). A similar trend was observed for low-trauma fracture events (canagliflozin 11.6 vs. placebo 9.2 participants with fracture per 1000 patient-years; HR 1.23; 95% CI 0.99 to 1.52) ( 144 ).
