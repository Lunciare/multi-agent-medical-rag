A post-hoc analysis of the SURPASS-CVOT trial was presented at the 2025 American Society of Nephrology Annual Meeting. Kidney outcomes were compared in patients on tirzepatide vs. dulaglutide who had very high-risk CKD, defined as having an eGFR < 30 mL/min/1.73 m 2 ; eGFR ≥ 30 to < 45 mL/min/1.73 m 2 and micro/macroalbuminuria; or eGFR ≥ 45 to < 60 mL/min/1.73 m 2 and macroalbuminuria. After 156 weeks the composite kidney outcome of the onset of macroalbuminuria, ≥ 50% reduction in eGFR, onset of end stage kidney disease, or kidney-related death was 33% lower with tirzepatide vs dulaglutide, at 16.7% vs 23.0%, respectively (hazard ratio [HR], 0.67; P = .002). The tirzepatide had significantly lower mean reductions in eGFR, -4.4 compared with -7.5 for dulaglutide, for a significant difference of 3.1 mL/min/1.73 m 2 (P < .001). Additionally, the tirzepatide group also had significantly greater reductions in the mean percentage of urinary albumin/creatinine ratio at week 156, at -41.6 g/kg vs -27.4 g/kg for dulaglutide (-19.7 g/kg difference; P < .05). The results of these studies strongly suggest that tirzepatide has beneficial effects on kidney function. LIVER DISEASE Liver fat content was decreased to a greater degree with tirzepatide treatment compared to treatment with insulin degludec ( 402 ). Additionally, tirzepatide decreased alanine aminotransferase and aspartate aminotransferase levels ( 403 ). A recent randomized trial compared the response to tirzepatide 5, 10, or 15mg vs. placebo in patients with metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH) with moderate or severe fibrosis after 52 weeks of treatment ( 404 ). Resolution of MASH without worsening of fibrosis was seen in 10% of the patients in the placebo group, 44% of the patients in the 5-mg tirzepatide group, 56% of the patients in the 10-mg tirzepatide group, and 62% of the patients in the 15-mg tirzepatide group (P<0.001 for all three comparisons with placebo group). Improvement of at least one fibrosis stage without worsening of MASH occurred in 30% of the placebo group, 55% of the 5-mg tirzepatide group, 51% of the 10-mg tirzepatide group, and 51% of the 15-mg tirzepatide group.
