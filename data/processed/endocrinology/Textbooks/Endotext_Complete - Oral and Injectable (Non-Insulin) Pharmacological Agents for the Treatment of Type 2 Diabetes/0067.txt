Additionally, sotagliflozin improved symptoms, physical limitations, and quality of life ( 250 ). This study demonstrates benefits in patients with a reduced or preserved ejection fractions and that treatment initiated during an acute heart failure episode is beneficial. DKA was uncommon in both the sotagliflozin group (0.3%) and placebo group (0.7%) but severe hypoglycemia was increased (sotagliflozin 1.5% vs placebo 0.3%). The SCORED trial was a multicenter trial in which patients with T2DM and chronic kidney disease (eGFR- 25 to 60 ml/min/1.73 m 2 , albuminuria was not required), and risks for cardiovascular disease were randomized to sotagliflozin (200 mg once daily, with an increase to 400 mg once daily if unacceptable side effects did not occur) (n= 5292) or placebo (n= 5292) and followed for a median of 16 months ( 251 ). The primary end point was the composite of the total number of deaths from cardiovascular causes, hospitalizations for heart failure, and urgent visits for heart failure. Sotagliflozin treatment decreased the primary end point (HR 0.74; 95% CI, 0.63–0.88; P <0.001), hospitalizations or urgent visits for heart failure (HR 0.67; 95% CI, 0.55–0.82; P <0.001), and deaths from cardiovascular causes, nonfatal myocardial infarctions, and nonfatal strokes (HR 0.77; 95%CI 0.65–0.91). This benefit in cardiovascular deaths, nonfatal myocardial infarctions, and nonfatal strokes was similar in males and females, young and old patients, and in the presence or absence of heart failure, cardiovascular disease, or renal dysfunction suggesting that sotagliflozin will benefit a wide spectrum of patients ( 252 ). The reductions in non-fatal myocardial infarction (HR 0.68; p=0.0041) and non-fatal strokes (HR 0·66; p=0·012) have not been seen with SGLT2 inhibitors ( 252 ). Sotagliflozin reduced the risk of renal disease defined as first event of sustained ≥50% decline in eGFR, eGFR <15, dialysis, or kidney transplant with 1.6% events in the sotagliflozin group and 2.6% events in the placebo group (HR 0.62; 95% CI 0.48 - 0.82; P < 0.001) ( 253 ). A1c was decreased by 0.42% compared to placebo. DKA while infrequent was increased in the sotagliflozin group (0.6% vs 0.3%; P=0.02).
