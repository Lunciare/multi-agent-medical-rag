BLOOD PRESSURE GLP-1 RAs result in modest but significant reductions in systolic blood pressure (2-5 mmHg) ( 22 ). HEART RATE The effects of GLP-1 RAs on heart rate differ between drugs. Short-acting GLP-1 RAs result in a modest increase (1-3 beats per minute) while long-acting GLP-1 RAs are associated with a more pronounced and sustained increase (3-10 beats per minute) during the day and night ( 295 ).
