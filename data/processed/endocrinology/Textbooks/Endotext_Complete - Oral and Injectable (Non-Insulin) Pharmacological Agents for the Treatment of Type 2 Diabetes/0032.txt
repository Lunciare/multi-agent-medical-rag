In the RECORD trial, the rosiglitazone group had an increased rate of severe episodes of CHF resulting in hospital admission or death (OR 2.10, p = 0.001) ( 104 ). Similarly, in the PROactive trial, the pioglitazone group also had increased rates of CHF (6% vs. 4%, p = 0.007) ( 93 ). Patients treated with TZDs have a higher risk for CHF development if they have a history of cardiovascular disease ( 113 ). Interestingly, TZD-associated CHF has not been linked with increased mortality ( 93 , 119 ). Although TZDs are associated with worsening of CHF or CHF development, they are not associated with adverse effects on cardiac function or structure ( 113 ). It is thought that the CHF is mainly due to fluid retention rather than TZDs inducing primarily cardiac dysfunction ( 113 ). OSTEOPOROSIS Large, randomized trials have shown that TZDs increase fracture risk, particularly in women. In the ADOPT study, which compared rosiglitazone, metformin, and glyburide, there was no difference in the incidence of fractures in men ( 120 ). However, fractures in women at 5 years was increased in the group treated with rosiglitazone (rosiglitazone 15.1%, metformin 7.3%, and glyburide 7.7%) ( 120 ). The increase in fractures with rosiglitazone occurred in pre- and postmenopausal women and were seen predominantly in the lower and upper limbs ( 120 ). In the PROactive study there was a higher rate of bone fractures in females treated with pioglitazone vs. placebo (5.1% vs 2.5%) ( 121 ). In the RECORD trial upper and distal lower limb fracture rates were increased mainly in women in the rosiglitazone treatment group ( 104 ). Hip and femur fracture were not increased with rosiglitazone treatment ( 104 ). In the IRIS trial an increased risk of fracture was seen in both males and females (men 9.4% vs 5.2%; HR, 1.83; women 14.9% vs 11.6%; HR, 1.32) ( 122 ). In a meta-analysis of 22 randomized controlled trials with 24,544 participants with 896 fracture cases there was a significantly increased incidence of fracture in women (OR=1.94; P<0.001), but not in men (OR=1.02; P=0.83) treated with TZDs ( 123 ).
