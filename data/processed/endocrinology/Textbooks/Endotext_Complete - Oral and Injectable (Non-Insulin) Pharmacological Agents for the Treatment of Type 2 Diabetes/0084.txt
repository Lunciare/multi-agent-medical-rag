ATHEROSCLEROTIC CARDIOVASCULAR DISEASE The effect of the GLP-1 RAs on atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease has been reported ( 296 - 301 ). ELIXA In the Elixa trial 6,068 patients with T2DM and who recently had a myocardial infarction or been hospitalized for unstable angina were randomized to placebo or lixisenatide, and followed for a median of 25 months ( 296 ). The primary end point of cardiovascular death, myocardial infarction, stroke, or hospitalization for unstable angina was similar in the placebo or lixisenatide groups. LEADER Trial The LEADER trial has shown that liraglutide decreased cardiovascular events ( 297 ). In this trial 9,340 patients with T2DM at high cardiovascular risk (~ 81% with established cardiovascular disease) were randomly assigned to receive liraglutide or placebo. After a median time of 3.5 years, the primary outcome of death from cardiovascular causes, nonfatal myocardial infarction, or nonfatal stroke occurred in significantly fewer patients in the liraglutide group (13.0%) than in the placebo group (14.9%) (HR 0.87, P=0.01). Additionally, deaths from cardiovascular causes (HR 0.78, P=0.007) or any cause was lower in the liraglutide group than in the placebo group (HR 0.85; P=0.02). Interestingly patients with established cardiovascular disease or decreased renal function (eGFR < 60) appeared to derive the greatest benefit of liraglutide treatment ( 302 , 303 ). The decrease in cardiovascular events was similar in patients with and without a history of heart failure ( 304 ). Finally, a significant reduction in amputations with liraglutide vs. placebo was observed (HR 0.65; P = 0.03]) ( 305 ).
