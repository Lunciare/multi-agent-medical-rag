Further recommendations suggest adding HBOT to the standard of care treatment in patients with Wagner 3 or higher DFU that have not shown significant improvement after 30 days of treatment to reduce the risk of major amputation and incomplete reepithelialization. Recommendations also suggest against the use of HBOT in patients with Wagner 2 or lower DFU. [13] Studies have demonstrated that patients who received hyperbaric oxygen (HBO2) treatment for DFUs healed faster and remained healed at one year, more than their cohorts who were not treated with HBO2.
