The pathophysiology of kidney stone formation is diverse and includes a combination of genetic and environmental factors. Several endocrinological disorders increase the risk of stone formation. Metabolic evaluation of patients with kidney stones helps to identify the underlying etiological factors and provides an opportunity to institute preventive lifestyle and/or pharmacologic measures to reduce stone recurrence risk.
