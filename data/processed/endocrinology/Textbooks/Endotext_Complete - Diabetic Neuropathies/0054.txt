Diagnostic Tests of Cardiovascular Autonomic Neuropathy View in own window TEST METHOD/ PARAMETERS Resting heart rate Beat-to-beat heart rate Variation* >100 beats/min is abnormal. With the patient at rest and supine (no overnight coffee or hypoglycemic episodes), breathing 6 breaths/min, heart rate monitored by EKG or ANSCORE device, a difference in heart rate of >15 beats/min is normal and <10 beats/min is abnormal, R-R inspiration/R-R expiration >1.17. All indices of HRV are age-dependent**. Heart rate response to Standing* During continuous EKG monitoring, the R-R interval is measured at beats 15 and 30 after standing. Normally, a tachycardia is followed by reflex bradycardia. The 30:15 ratio is normally >1.03. Heart rate response to Valsalva maneuver* The subject forcibly exhales into the mouthpiece of a manometer to 40 mmHg for 15 s during EKG monitoring.
