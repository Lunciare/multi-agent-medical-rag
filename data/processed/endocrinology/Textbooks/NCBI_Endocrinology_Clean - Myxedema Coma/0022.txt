Differential Diagnosis Sepsis Shock Stroke Drug overdose Diabetic ketoacidosis Seizure Hypothermia Prognosis The prognosis for myxedema coma is challenging to establish due to the small number of cases reported. The mortality rate is variable, with some reports as high as 60% and others as low as 20% to 25% in the presence of advanced intensive support care. The overall mortality rate, based on a recent report summarizing publications from 2004 to 2024, was 38.8%. Shock and multiorgan failure were the most common causes of death.
