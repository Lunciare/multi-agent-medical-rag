The PitNET terminology has also most recently been supported by epigenomic data showing that PAs/PitNETs more closely resemble NETs (of lung, pancreas and enteric origin) rather than mixed adenomas of various lineages (e.g., follicular thyroid, adrenocortical, colorectal), although the clinical course of PAs is classically considered benign ( 205 ).
