Whereas clinically-silent, hormone-staining tumors only account for 7% of all pituitary neoplasms ( 26 ), functioning tumors that have evolved from such tumors comprise 25% of APT/PC ( 33 ). The commonest PC subtypes are corticotroph and lactotroph neoplasms ( 15 , 30 ). In a review of 72 published PC cases by Yoo et al , hormone IHC was positive for ACTH in 35%, PRL in 24%, GH in 14%, TSH in 6%, FSH in 7% and LH in 4%, and 15% were null cell ( 34 ). This rate of null cell PC was lower than other reports of 30% ( 15 , 30 ), possibly relating to the limited availability of prolactin IHC in historical investigations ( 35 ). Compared to pure PA series, lactotroph and corticotroph-derived neoplasms are overrepresented whilst somatotroph and null cell neoplasms are underrepresented in PC ( 26 , 33 ). The overall epidemiology of APT/PC was outlined in an ESE survey of clinicians treating APT/PC patients, where APT was defined by the responding clinician. The survey cohort comprised 165 patients (40 PC, 125 APT). APT and PC cases were similar in age at diagnosis (43 vs. 45 yr), predominant cell subtypes (corticotroph in 45% vs. 48%, lactotroph in 20% vs. 38%), and functional status (clinically functioning in 58% vs. 63%), but tumors with transformation from silent to functioning were overrepresented in PC (7% vs. 20%) ( 33 ). Regarding gender predilection, there is over-representation of males among cases of APT/PC, being 63% males in the second ESE survey. This is particularly evident among lactotroph and corticotroph APT/PC, whereas females predominate among the “benign” forms of these tumor subtypes ( 36 ). A follow-up ESE survey published in 2022 comprising 171 patients (83 new patients) with APT/PC found that at initial surgery, 67.4% were Grade 2b (with 44.8% developing metastases), compared to 8.8% Grade 2b tumors in an unselected surgical cohort ( 36 , 37 ). According to this, grade 2b tumors have a sensitivity and specificity of 68% and 90% respectively for the diagnosis of an APT, although aggressiveness is not invariable in grade 2b tumors ( 38 ).
