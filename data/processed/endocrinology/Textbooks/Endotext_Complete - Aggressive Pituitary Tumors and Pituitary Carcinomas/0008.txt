Established Somatic Genetic Contributions Established somatic genetic contributors to APT/PC are ATRX in corticotroph APT/PC, SF3B1 in lactotroph APT/PC, and TP53 in corticotroph and to lesser extent lactotroph APT/PC ( 2 ). The role of ATRX in APT/PC pathogenesis was demonstrated in a study of 30 APTs and 18 PCs which revealed loss of ATRX immunolabelling and somatic inactivating ATRX variants in 9/48 cases (19%); ATRX loss was especially prevalent in PCs compared to APTs, and in corticotroph neoplasms compared to other cell lineages ( 60 ). SF3B1 variants were first reported in the PA setting in a Chinese study in 2020, which showed a single recurrent somatic variant (R625H) in 45/227 (20%) prolactinomas and an association between the R625H variant and shorter progression-free survival ( 61 ). A multicenter European study subsequently found the SF3B1 R625H variant in only 7/282 (2.5%) of prolactinomas; although the variant was notably identified in 3/6 (50%) of lactotroph PCs, supporting an intrinsic role for SF3B1 in APT/PC pathogenesis ( 62 ). TP53 somatic variants have been demonstrated in 24% of invasive corticotrophinomas ( 63 ) and in isolated lactotroph APT/PCs ( 64 ). The link between TP53 and APT/PC pathogenesis is strongest for corticotroph tumors, with TP53 status predictive of tumor size, invasiveness, Ki67 index, Knosp grade and multimodal treatment requirement ( 63 ). Available data suggests that significantly increased p53 staining is a frequent but not invariable marker of somatic TP53 variants ( 63 , 65 ). Of note, although GNAS and USP8 account for approximately one-half of somatotrophinomas and one-third of corticotrophinomas, respectively, collective data show that these variants generally predict milder rather than more aggressive disease ( 66 ). When considering pituitary neoplasms in general, chromosomal level copy number variation (CNV) is the most frequent genetic aberration ( 43 , 67 ). CNV is particularly common in functioning neoplasms, especially prolactinomas, as well as neoplasms with high proliferative indices ( 67 - 69 ).
