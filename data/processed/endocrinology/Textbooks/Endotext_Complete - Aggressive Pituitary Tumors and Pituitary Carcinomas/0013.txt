In a case of a non-functioning PC with multiple intracranial metastases, miR-20a, miR-106b and miR17-5p were upregulated in the metastases compared to the primary neoplasm, in association with downregulation of the tumorigenesis genes, PTEN and TIMP2 , which are downstream targets of these microRNAs ( 103 ). CLINICAL PRESENTATION APT/PCs nearly always evolve from pituitary macroadenomas (maximal tumor diameter ≥1cm) ( 15 ), but, conversely, many macroadenomas and even giant prolactinomas (≥4cm) respond well to standard treatments and never exhibit aggressiveness ( 4 ). Progression of a microadenoma (<1cm) to PC is exceedingly rare ( 71 , 105 ). In the ESE 2022 survey there were just 6 microadenomas at diagnosis ( 36 ). The time from primary diagnosis with a pituitary neoplasm to presentation with APT/PC is highly variable ( 4 ). In APT, aggressiveness can be apparent from diagnosis or take months to more than a decade to develop ( 4 , 88 ). The course of APTs may be punctuated by periods of radiological and hormonal quiescence ( 106 ). Among APTs in the 2022 ESE survey, 57% of the cohort displayed initially “benign” behavior with an aggressive course becoming apparent after a median of 5.5 years (max 33 years) ( 36 ). One study showed that APTs are more likely to occur following incomplete surgical resection at an odds ratio of 6.3 ( 18 ), but another study showed no relationship between APTs and the primary surgical outcome ( 17 ). These conflicting results partly reflect the difficulty in distinguishing residual tumor from normal tissue and postoperative changes ( 18 ). In PC, the mean latency from primary diagnosis is 5-9 years, but can range from months to 43 years ( 1 , 8 , 15 , 30 , 34 , 47 , 107 - 109 ).
