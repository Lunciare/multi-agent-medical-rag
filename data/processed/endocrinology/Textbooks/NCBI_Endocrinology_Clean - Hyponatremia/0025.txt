Vasopressin Receptor Agonists (Vaptans) Selective vasopressin-2 receptor antagonists have been widely used for hypervolemic and euvolemic hyponatremia (especially congestive heart failure and SIADH). They block AVP's effects on the renal collecting duct, increasing water excretion without affecting sodium excretion, thereby increasing serum sodium levels. The SALT 1 and SALT 2 trials found tolvaptan to treat SIADH and hypervolemic hyponatremia (excluding cirrhosis) effectively. However, the tested dose of 15 mg is considered by many to be too high, as overcorrection was observed at doses as low as 3.75 mg. Patients with severe hyponatremia require intensive inpatient monitoring with vaptan use. Conivaptan is a dual vasopressin 1 and 2 antagonist, and is Food and Drug Administration-approved for inpatient treatment of euvolemic and hypovolemic hyponatremia.
