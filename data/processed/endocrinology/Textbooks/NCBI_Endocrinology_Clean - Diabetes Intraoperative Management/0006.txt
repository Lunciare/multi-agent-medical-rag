The half-life of insulin is 35 minutes, so it is titrated easily, and blood sugar monitoring should occur hourly. For those who are not critically ill and have a short procedure (less than 4 hours), which does not usually anticipate large fluid shifts, it is preferable to use rapid-acting subcutaneous (SC) insulin.
