Cretinism patients present themselves as short-statured, with coarse facial features, mental retardation, protruding tongue, etc. Thyroid cancer : Thyroid carcinomas arise either from the follicular epithelium or parafollicular C-cells. They are painless nodules and compression, displacing the adjacent structures. The carcinomas of the thyroid can manifest in the form of papillary carcinoma, follicular carcinoma, anaplastic carcinoma, and medullary carcinoma.
