Localization of endogenous thyroperoxidase in thyroid follicles is an indicator of iodination during the synthesis of thyroid hormones. Cytochemical staining of C and oxyphil cells serves as diagnostic markers for tumor, teratoma, and cyst formation in the thyroid gland. Microscopy, Light Capsules of Thyroid Two capsules entirely cover the thyroid gland. True – peripheral condensation of the glandular tissue False – the pre-tracheal layer of deep cervical fascia The gland is surrounded by a thin fibro-elastic (true) capsule. This capsule, in turn, is covered by a pre-tracheal fascia from the outside which acts as a false capsule. The true capsule gives rise to septa deep into the parenchyma dividing the gland into lobules. The septa give passage for the blood vessels, nerves, and lymphatics into the gland. Each lobule is made of an aggregation of follicles, which are the structural and functional units of the thyroid gland.
