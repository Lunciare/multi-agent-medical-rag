Many men find it a distressing and unwelcome event and increasing numbers are seeking treatment to prevent further hair loss and reverse the process. In addition, MAA may be a marker of increased risk of cardiovascular diseases. The hair follicle is a complex organ biologically. The changes in the hair follicles that lead to baldness have caught the interest of stem cell scientists, geneticists, developmental biologists, and immunologists and hair biology has become an increasingly fruitful field of scientific endeavor.
