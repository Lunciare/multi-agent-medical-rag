However, initial treatment for most types of urinary incontinence includes lifestyle modifications and pelvic floor muscle exercises. Surgery (such as a urethral sling procedure, artificial sphincter, or urethral compression implants) is reserved for selected refractory cases where less invasive treatments are unsatisfactory, and the expected benefit outweighs the risks of anesthesia and surgical complications. There are also non-surgical treatments.
