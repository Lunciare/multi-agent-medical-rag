Commercially available UWFI (at least a 100-degree field of view) includes Optos/California, Mirante, Clarus, Retcam, and Spectralis. UWFI using a scanning laser ophthalmoscope and ellipsoid mirror, captures images in small pupils and hazy media. Peripheral retinal capillary nonperfusion areas may be better delineated and planned for 'targeted retinal photocoagulation' in proliferative diabetic retinopathy or diabetic macular edema. Peripheral vascular changes may be more common in DR than was previously thought.
