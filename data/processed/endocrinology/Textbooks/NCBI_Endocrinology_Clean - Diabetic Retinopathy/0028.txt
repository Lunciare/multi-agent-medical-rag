Post-operative Care After surgery, the eye is patched. After the patch is removed, the eye is cleaned, and topical administration of medications like antibiotics with steroid eye drops and cycloplegic eye drops are started. After injecting gases like sf6/c3f8 or silicone oil, there are chances of IOP rise post-operatively, which needs to be managed with appropriate antiglaucoma medications. Patients can then be called for regular, timely follow-ups to screen their post-operative outcomes. If silicone oil tamponade is done, the patient should be explained the need for a mandatory second surgery to remove oil.
