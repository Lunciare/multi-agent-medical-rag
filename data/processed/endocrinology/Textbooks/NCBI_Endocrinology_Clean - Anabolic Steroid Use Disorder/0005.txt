Androgen intake may lead to low sperm count, small testes, high hemoglobin and hematocrit values, low serum to non-detectable serum LH, and low sex hormone-binding globulin. These findings should raise the suspicion of exogenous androgen intake in men competing in sports. Cardiovascular risk of AAS includes myocardial dysfunction, coronary atherosclerosis ; hypercoagulopathy and hepatic dysfunction , hypertension, life-threatening arrhythmia, and sudden death. Concentric Left ventricular hypertrophy is commonly seen in long term steroid users even after discontinuation of AAS. Prolonged use of AAS will also lead to elevation of LDL and reduction of HDL, conferring an increased risk of the cardiac event. AAS abuse may also lead to psychiatric and behavioral disturbances. According to an observational study by Christoffersen et al, there is increased aggression, violent behaviors, and a 9 fold increased risk of crime and imprisonment in people with AAS abuse.
