Bariatric surgery has been shown to prevent the progression of type 2 diabetes. Indications The indications for biliopancreatic diversion are primarily bariatric, with a few exceptions. The classic criteria for bariatric surgery are: Body mass index (BMI) greater than or equal to 40 and BMI greater than or equal to 35 with at least 1 obesity-related comorbidity (such as diabetes mellitus, obstructive sleep apnea, hypertension, severely limiting musculoskeletal issues) Unsuccessful nonoperative weight loss treatments Mental health clearance No medical contraindications to surgery Super obese patients with BMI >50 should be considered for biliopancreatic diversion with duodenal switch For patients who fail operative treatment with Roux-en-Y bypass and gastric sleeve, biliopancreatic diversion with a duodenal switch should be considered. Contraindications The contraindications to biliopancreatic diversion with a duodenal switch are primarily related to bariatric procedures.
