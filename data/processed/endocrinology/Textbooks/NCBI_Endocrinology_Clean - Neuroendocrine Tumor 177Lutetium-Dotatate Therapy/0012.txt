Monitoring An interprofessional team provides an integrated approach to treatment decisions, aiming to achieve the best possible clinical outcomes. Clinicians in interventional radiology, medical oncology, and surgical oncology are critical stakeholders in individualized, patient-centered treatment decisions. Randomized clinical trials with a head-to-head comparison of other treatment options, eg, targeted therapies, are still being conducted; therefore, evidence on the optimal sequence of metastatic neuroendocrine tumor treatment is unavailable. Eligible patients are encouraged to enroll in open clinical trials. Toxicity PRRT involves complex clinical and navigational patient needs, as well as thorough interdepartmental communication, to prepare a patient for each therapy. A nurse navigator is vital in providing a cohesive solution to address the multifaceted operational requirements associated with PRRT. The navigator provides critical ambulatory support to each patient throughout the treatment journey by delivering interprofessional coordination and patient education, monitoring treatment-related adverse effects, and adhering to radiation safety protocols.
