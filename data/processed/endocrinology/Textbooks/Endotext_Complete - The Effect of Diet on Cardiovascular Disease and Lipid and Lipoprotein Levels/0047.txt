In a study carried out in a single center the Atkins, Zone, Weight Watchers, and Ornish diets were compared and the effect on TG levels was also reported ( 262 ). Table 19 shows the results of this study at 2 months, a period at which dietary compliance was still high. The magnitude of weight loss was similar but the decrease in LDL-C that occurs with weight loss was blunted with a diet that was high in fat (Atkins diet). In contrast HDL-C levels increased with a high fat diet, particularly SFA (Atkins diet) and decreased with a very low-fat diet (Ornish diet). The weight loss induced decrease in TG levels was blunted by a high CHO intake (Ornish diet). These observations confirm and extend the results described above. Table 19.
