Anatomy, Abdomen and Pelvis: Stomach
KEYWORDS: stomach, gastric, left, food, right, organ, wall, cells, layer, artery, lymph, nodes, layers, lesser, fundus

Introduction The stomach is the digestive system's most dilated portion. This organ lies between the esophagus, which is superior to it, and the small intestine, which is inferior to it. The stomach is a large, muscular, and hollow organ with a great capacity to hold food. This organ can hold about 2 to 3 liters of food, acting as a blender and food reservoir. Clinically, stomach disorders such as peptic ulcers and gastric cancer are significant due to their prevalence and potential complications, particularly pain and bleeding. Surgically, the stomach is often involved in procedures like gastrectomy for cancer, bariatric surgery for obesity, and the management of peptic ulcer disease.
