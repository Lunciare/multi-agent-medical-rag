Thyroid Lymphoma
KEYWORDS: lymphoma, thyroid, thyroid lymphoma, primary, primary thyroid, treatment, like, lymphomas, thyroiditis, cases, diagnosis, symptoms, patients, dlbcl, malt

Introduction Thyroid lymphoma is classified as either primary thyroid lymphoma or secondary thyroid lymphoma. Primary thyroid lymphoma initially impacts the thyroid gland, with subsequent spread to the lymph nodes and other organs occurring later. In contrast, secondary thyroid lymphoma involves lymph nodes and other organs first, followed by subsequent spread to the thyroid. This activity exclusively concentrates on primary thyroid lymphomas. Etiology Primary thyroid lymphoma is uncommon, comprising less than 5% of all thyroid malignancies and less than 3% of all extranodal lymphomas. Primary thyroid lymphoma often manifests in individuals with preexisting Hashimoto (chronic autoimmune) thyroiditis, showing a significant correlation with previous autoimmune conditions.
