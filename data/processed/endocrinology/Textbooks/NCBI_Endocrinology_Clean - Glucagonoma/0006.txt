Laboratory Studies Patients with suspected glucagonomas should be evaluated with laboratory testing to assess for characteristic findings of this tumor, including: Fasting plasma glucagon : Fasting plasma glucagon levels are abnormally elevated, usually >500 pg/mL. Normal fasting plasma glucagon levels are less than 150 pg/mL. It is important to note that different glucagon assays may exhibit variable cross-reactivity with varying isoforms of glucagon, not all of which (nearly 70%) are biologically active. Serial measurements should, therefore, always be performed using the same assay directed against the C-terminus. Serum amino acids and zinc : Concentrations of amino acids and zinc should be checked to evaluate nutritional status. Laboratory abnormalities associated with glucagonoma can include hypoaminoacidemia (resulting from the targeting of amino acids into metabolic pathways in the liver by excessive hyperglucagonemia) and low zinc levels. Complete blood count : A complete blood count (CBC) should be obtained to check for concurrent normocytic anemia. A comprehensive metabolic panel (CMP) should be checked to detect other metabolic abnormalities.
