Some literature notes fewer recurrences, but other studies have noted a higher incidence of recurrent nerve injury. New Developments New techniques have been tried for initially treating smaller-size papillary thyroid cancer with good results, like thermal ablation and ultrasound radiofrequency ablation for treating recurrent disease. Chemotherapy This is reserved for patients with recurrent or excessive remnant disease after the initial surgical treatment and radioactive iodine ablation. The most common agents used are the kinase inhibitors that target different pathways, like the anti-angiogenic multi-targeted kinase inhibitors (aaMKI- lenvatinib, sorafenib), BRAF kinase inhibitors (vemurafenib, dabrafenib), MEK inhibitors (trametinib, cobimetinib), NTR kinase inhibitors (larotrectinib), and RET inhibitors (selpercatinib).
