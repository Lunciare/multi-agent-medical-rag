SEXUAL DYSFUNCTION IN WOMEN WITH DIABETES Female sexual dysfunction describes a departure from normal sensation and/or function during sexual activity, and includes dyspareunia, obstructed intercourse, vaginal laxity, and decreased sexual desire, arousal, or orgasm (50,104). Few studies have focused on sexual dysfunction in women with diabetes. Among existing studies, the majority are limited by small sample sizes, not including control women without diabetes, use of unidimensional measures of sexual function, or focusing on clinic or other nongeneralizable populations (105). The RRISK 2 is one of the largest studies to compare sexual functioning in women with versus without diabetes.
