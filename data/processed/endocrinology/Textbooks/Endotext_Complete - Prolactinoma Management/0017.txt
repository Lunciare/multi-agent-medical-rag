For follow-up imaging, particularly in macroadenomas, gadolinium-containing contrast agents should be used judiciously since low-level gadolinium deposits in the dentate nucleus and globus pallidus have unknown neurological impact. Unenhanced T1-weighted and T2-weighted MRI sequences should be considered during follow-up in pediatric patients, especially if good quality enhanced images have been obtained at diagnosis ( 87 , 88 ). If gadolinium-containing contrast agents are necessary, macrocyclic or newer linear contrast agents are preferred until further studies clarify possible long-term retention risks.
