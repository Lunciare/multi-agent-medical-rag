Surgical Oncology Surgical Treatment In 46XX children with classic CAH who are raised as girls, surgical intervention is often required to reduce clitoromegaly and correct posterior fusion to allow for penovaginal intercourse. A retrospective analysis from Turkey showed comparable anatomical and cosmetic outcomes for surgeries performed before versus after 2 years. However, the early surgery cohort demonstrated an increased need for repeated interventions and decreased parental satisfaction. Sexual function is another concern in females undergoing genitoplasty.
