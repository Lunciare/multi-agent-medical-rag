Due to its rarity, many healthcare professionals may have limited familiarity with the presentation and management of adrenal crises. A retrospective study in the United Kingdom revealed that adrenal crisis contributed to 10% of the deaths in patients with primary and secondary adrenal insufficiency. Patients whose adrenal crisis is quickly identified and given prompt treatment with IV fluids and parenteral steroids have a good prognosis and recovery. Those who are critically ill with significantly altered mental status, advanced endocrinopathies (eg, severe diabetes and uncontrolled thyroid disease), or multiple comorbidities have an increased risk of mortality and residual disability.
