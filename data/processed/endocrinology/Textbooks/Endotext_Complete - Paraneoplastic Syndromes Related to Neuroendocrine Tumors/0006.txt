As the majority of NENs express high levels of somatostatin receptors (SSTRs), these neoplasms can also be detected with somatostatin receptor imaging by 111 In-labelled scintigraphy (SRS; Octreoscan or Tektrotyd) or by 68 Ga-labelled positron emission tomography (PET; 68 Ga-DOTATOC and 68 Ga-DOTATATE PET/CT) that allow whole body scanning. 68Ga-laballed somatostatin analogue PET/CT has been proved to be the most sensitive method for the diagnosis and staging of NENs ( Figure 1 ) ( 32 ).
