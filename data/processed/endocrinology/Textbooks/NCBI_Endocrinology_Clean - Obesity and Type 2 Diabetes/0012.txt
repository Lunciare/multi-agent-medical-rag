Clinical Application and Treatment Algorithms Updated 2024–2025 treatment algorithms emphasize complication-centric approaches rather than relying solely on BMI thresholds. The ADA Standards of Care recommend weight management as a primary treatment goal alongside glycemic control, and recommend GLP-1 and dual GIP/GLP-1 agonists as preferred agents due to their combined benefits. The AACE algorithm incorporates the adiposity-based chronic disease (ABCD) framework, recognizing obesity as a chronic condition requiring long-term management. Early weight loss response within the first 4 weeks consistently predicts long-term success across multiple studies, providing crucial decision points for optimizing or escalating treatment. Patient selection criteria focus on individualized risk-benefit assessments. Predictors of medication success include younger age, shorter diabetes duration, higher pretreatment weight, and early response patterns.
