Clinical Significance Many healthcare quality metrics assess the effectiveness of diabetes prevention and treatment. Obesity is a significant risk factor for T2D, influencing both its development and severity. Early screening of patients with obesity, combined with intensive therapy, can lead to long-term improvement or remission. Strict diabetes control reduces complications such as ketoacidosis, diabetic ulcers, amputations, soft tissue infections, and osteomyelitis. Additionally, aggressive management of HbA1c lowers the risk of coronary artery disease and chronic kidney disease.
