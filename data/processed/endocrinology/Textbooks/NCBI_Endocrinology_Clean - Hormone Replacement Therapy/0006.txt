Progesterone prevents the lining from proliferating abnormally. If a woman has had a hysterectomy, progesterone is unnecessary. Progesterone can help with sleep disturbances and mood instability and is frequently given before bedtime. Some regimens of HRT are also approved for the prevention, not treatment, of osteoporosis. The USPSTF recommends against the use of HRT solely for osteoporosis prevention. FDA-approved treatments for osteoporosis prevention include: Norethindrone acetate and ethinyl estradiol tablets Conjugated estrogen tablets Estradiol tablets Estradiol patches Estradiol and norgestimate tablets Estradiol and norethindrone acetate tablets Estradiol and levonorgestrel patches Conjugated estrogens and medroxyprogesterone acetate tablets Conjugated estrogens and bazedoxifene tablets Testosterone therapy indications The addition of testosterone supplementation to menopausal HRT can be beneficial for women with female sexual interest and arousal disorder after other causes have been excluded and a trial of conventional HRT has been given.
