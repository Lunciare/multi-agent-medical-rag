However, these criteria are expected not to agree completely, as the former are focused on the presence of tender points while the latter on the presence of symptoms. The 1990 criteria can diagnose fibromyalgia in patients who do not have sufficiently high symptom score according to the 2010 criteria, while the 2010 criteria can diagnose fibromyalgia in patients who do not have sufficient tender points according to the 1990 criteria.
