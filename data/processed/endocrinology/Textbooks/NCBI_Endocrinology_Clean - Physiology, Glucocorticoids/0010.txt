Clinical Significance Glucocorticoids, both synthetic and natural, are used in various disorders. Endocrinologists use the treatment of adrenal insufficiency and congenital adrenal hyperplasia. Pharmacologic doses of the glucocorticoids are used to treat various allergic, immunological, and inflammatory disorders.
