SUMMARY Visual impairment (visual acuity poorer than 20/40) in those with type 2 diabetes was estimated to affect 937,000 Americans age ≥40 years in data collected in the 1990s. While data from the National Health and Nutrition Examination Surveys collected in 1999–2004 and 2005–2008 suggest that this prevalence is declining, the prevalence of visual impairment is about two to three times as high in persons with diabetes as in those without the disease.
