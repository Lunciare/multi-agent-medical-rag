Clinicians must monitor patients with giant cell arteritis because increased cumulative glucocorticoid exposure is associated with an increased risk of glucocorticoid-related adverse effects. Doctors can also track serum glucose, blood pressure, electrolytes, weight, bone mineral density, hemoglobin, occult blood loss, growth in pediatric patients, and infections. The HPA axis suppression should also be assessed using the morning cortisol test, adrenocorticotropic hormone stimulation test, and the urinary free cortisol test. When monitoring, it is essential to remember that the elimination half-life of prednisone is 3 to 4 hours in adults and 1 to 2 hours in children.
