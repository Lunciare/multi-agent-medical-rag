Phenotypic Variance in the HLA-DRB1–HLA-DQA1–HLA-DQB1 Locus Assuming the liability threshold model and a global type 1 diabetes prevalence of 0.4%, all haplotypes in HLA-DRB1– HLA-DQA1–HLA-DQB1 together explain 29.6% of total phenotypic variance. HLA-DQβ1 position 57 alone explains 15.2% of the variance; the addition of HLA-DRβ1 position 13 and HLA-DRβ1 position 71 increases the explained proportion to 26.9%. Therefore, these three amino acid positions together capture over 90% of the signal within HLA-DRB1–HLA-DQA1–HLA-DQB1. In contrast, variation in HLA-A, HLA-B, and HLA-DPB1 together explain approximately 4% of total variance. Genome-wide independently associated SNPs outside the HLA together explain about 9% of variance; rs678 (in INS) and rs2476601 (in PTPN22) explain 3.3% and 0.78%, respectively.
