A recent survey of commonly used commercial tests for measurement of free T 4 indicates that equilibrium or symmetric dialysis are the only tests that will consistently provide accurate values in subjects with FDH ( 110 ), in particular one using dialysis in combination with tandem mass spectrometry ( 111 ). FDH is suspected when serum total T 4 concentration is increased without proportional elevation in total T 3 level and non-suppressed serum TSH. Half of affected subjects have also rT 3 values above the normal range ( 112 ) ( Table 3 ). Since the same combination of test results are found in subjects with the Thr 109 TTR variant, the diagnosis of FDH should be confirmed by the demonstration that an increased proportion of the total serum T 4 migrates with HSA on non-denaturing electrophoresis or precipitates with anti-HSA serum.
