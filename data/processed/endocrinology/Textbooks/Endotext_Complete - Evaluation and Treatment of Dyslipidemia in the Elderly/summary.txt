Evaluation and Treatment of Dyslipidemia in the Elderly
KEYWORDS: statin, patients, risk, age, cardiovascular, ldl-c, ascvd, therapy, pubmed, years, events, levels, disease, trial, statin therapy

ABSTRACT The definition of elderly is arbitrary. In this chapter we will define elderly as greater than 75 years of age because both the US and European lipids guidelines use this age to differentiate therapy recommendations. Atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease (ASCVD) is a major cause of morbidity and mortality in the elderly. Age is a key risk factor for ASCVD and with identical risk factors the 10-year risk of an ASCVD event markedly increases with age. In fact, an older individual with excellent risk factors can still have a high risk for having an ASCVD event. ASCVD begins early in life and progresses until it leads to clinical events later in life.
