A similar pattern was observed when using 2 mg/L as the cutpoint (Appendix 9.30) or mean C-reactive protein (Appendix 9.31). Fibrinogen was measured in 3,235 partic­ ipants age ≥40 years in the NHANES 1999–2002. Mean fibrinogen was generally higher in people with diagnosed diabetes than among people with undiag­ nosed diabetes, prediabetes, and normal glucose regulation, especially in those age ≥65 years (Figure 9.48, Appendix 9.32). A similar pattern was observed with fibrin­ ogen ≥4 g/L (Appendix 9.33). PARITY Women with diagnosed diabetes (2.3) had a higher age-standardized mean number of live births than women who had not been diagnosed with diabetes (2.0) (Figure 9.49, Appendix 9.34).
