By definition, mean 2-hour plasma glucose was lower among people with prediabetes (125 mg/dL) than in people with diabetes and lowest among participants with normal glucose regulation (92 mg/dL [5.11 mmol/L]). This pattern by diabetes was consistent when stratified by race/ethnicity (non-Hispanic white, non-Hispanic black, and Mexican American), and concentrations were generally lower in non-Hispanic black than Mexican American participants (Figure 9.14, Appendix 9.9). Figure 9.15 (and Appendix 9.10) shows percentiles of the 2-hour plasma glucose distribution among people without diagnosed diabetes stratified by age group.
