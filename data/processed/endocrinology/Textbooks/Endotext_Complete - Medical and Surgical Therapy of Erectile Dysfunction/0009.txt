In cases of severe deformity and/or failure of medical therapy, surgical intervention may be required. Surgery for PD can take the form of 1) plication of the convex side of the penis with sutures to induce straightening, 2) incision with grafting of the concave or narrowed portion of the corpora, or 3) placement of a penile prosthetic device in cases where there is concomitant severe ED ( 45 , 48 ).
