A cross-sectional, clinic-based study of 24,151 patients from 33 countries world­ wide with type 2 diabetes, mean diabetes duration of 8 years, and without previ­ ously known albuminuria found overall prevalences of moderate and severe albuminuria of 39% and 10%, respectively (222). Compared with whites, who had the lowest prevalence of albuminuria, Asian and Hispanic patients had nearly twice the odds of albuminuria (adjusted odds ratios [OR] 1.8, 95% CI 1.59–1.97, and OR 1.7, 95% CI 1.47–1.94, respectively).
