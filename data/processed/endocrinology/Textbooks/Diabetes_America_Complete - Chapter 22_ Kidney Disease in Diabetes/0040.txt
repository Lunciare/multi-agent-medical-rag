Criteria for albuminuria: ACR: ≥30 and <300 mg/g in at least two of three consecutive random samples (296); 3.5–25 mg/mmol (moderate) and ≥25–150 mg/mmol (severe) on spot urine (297,298); ≥2 mg/mmol (260); ≥2.5 mg/mmol and <25 mg/mmol in men or ≥3.5 mg/mmol and <25 mg/mmol in women (moderate) and ≥25–150 mg/mmol (severe) (300,306); ≥50 mg/L and <300 mg/L relative to mean urine creatinine concentration (11 mmol/L in men and 8 mmol/L in women) (306); UAE: 30–300 mg/24 hours (299,304) and ≥0.03 g/L in spot urine (305); AER 2–200 µg/min (moderate) and >200 µg/min (severe) in a timed overnight sample (303). Criteria for proteinuria: ≥1+ at least twice without pyuria (301); total urinary protein measured in morning spot urine, ≥0.1 g/L and ≥0.2 g/L (302). Criteria for reduced eGFR: creatinine clearance <60 mL/min/1.73 m2 (298). ACR, urinary albumin-to-creatinine ratio; CHD, coronary heart disease; CVD, cardiovascular disease; eGFR, estimated glomerular filtration rate; MI, myocardial infarction; NR, not reported; UAE, urinary albumin excretion.
