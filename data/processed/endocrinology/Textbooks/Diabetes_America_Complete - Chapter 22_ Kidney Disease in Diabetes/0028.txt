In contrast with type 1 diabetes, no secular decline in the incidence of proteinuria has been reported in type 2 diabetes. The 10-year cumulative incidence of persistent proteinuria in the predominantly Caucasian population age ≥40 years of Rochester, Minnesota, remained 12% in those diagnosed with type 2 diabetes in 1970–1979 (n=483) and those diag­ nosed in 1980–1989 (n=680) (246). The 20-year cumulative incidence of proteinuria reported in this study, however, was 41%, higher than the 25% cumulative incidence reported in an earlier Rochester study of individuals diagnosed with diabetes in 1945–1969 (236).
