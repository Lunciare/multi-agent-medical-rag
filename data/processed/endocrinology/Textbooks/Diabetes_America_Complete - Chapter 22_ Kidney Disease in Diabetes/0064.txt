AUTONOMIC NEUROPATHY In the United States, between 25% and 28% of adults age ≥40 years with diabetes have peripheral neuropathy (492). Sympathetic neuropathy with ensuing alteration of glomerular vascular resistance is assumed to hasten deterioration of kidney function in persons with autonomic neuropathy (493).
