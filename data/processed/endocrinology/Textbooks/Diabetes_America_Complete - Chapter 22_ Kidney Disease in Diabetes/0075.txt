Furthermore, because A1c may under­ estimate the level of glycemia in uremic patients, due to the shortened lifespan of red blood cells and treatment with erythropoietin, establishing an optimal target A1c in uremic patients remains challenging (556,557). Persons with GFR <60 mL/min/1.73 m2 or on dialysis often have higher glycemic levels than expected for a given A1c, albeit with a wide vari­ ation in the glucose/A1c relationship (558,559,560,561,562,563). The Dialysis Outcomes and Practice Patterns Study (DOPPS) (564) found a U-shaped associa­ tion between A1c and all-cause mortality in hemodialysis patients with type 1 or type 2 diabetes.
