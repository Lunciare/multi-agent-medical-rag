The higher overall mortality in those with longer duration of diabetes is due primarily to the greater proportion of person-years of follow-up falling in the categories of worse kidney function (Figure 22.22C). The presence of kidney disease was associated with excess mortality from diabetic kidney disease, CVD, infections, and malignancy in the Pima Indians with diabetes and from infections in those without diabetes.
