CLINICAL RECOGNITION OF SEVERE HYPERGLYCEMIA Diabetes Mellitus is a common condition that affects over 800 million adults globally. Type 2 diabetes mellitus represents almost 95% of these diagnoses. Type 2 diabetes typically has two pathophysiologic defects: an insulin secretory defect and insulin resistance. Symptoms of uncontrolled hyperglycemia include polyuria, polydipsia, blurry vision, possibly dehydration, and weight loss. Patients may complain of thirst, sweet cravings, generalized fatigue, abdominal discomfort, and muscle cramps.
