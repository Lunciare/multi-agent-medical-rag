Patients with congenital pancreatic malformations can present as neonates with vague abdominal symptomatology, jaundice, pancreatitis, intestinal obstruction, or failure to thrive. Conversely, congenital anomalies may present in adults throughout the lifespan. Advanced imaging with MRCP and ERCP can elucidate underlying pathology in most cases. Initial management can range from a conservative approach with medical therapy to minimally invasive intervention. For patients who do not improve, a number of surgical operations on the pancreas and biliary tree have proven successful.
