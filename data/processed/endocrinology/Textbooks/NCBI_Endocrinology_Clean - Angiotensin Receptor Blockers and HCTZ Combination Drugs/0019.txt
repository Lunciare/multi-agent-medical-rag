The adverse effects associated with ARBs include dizziness, hyperkalemia, sinusitis, angioedema, cough, and enteropathy. The incidence and severity of hypotension increase with the concurrent administration of diuretics such as HCTZ. Providers should avoid prescribing candesartan to patients with hyperuricemia. Cough: ARBs increase bradykinin levels, which stimulate the production of prostaglandins that cause bronchial irritation.
