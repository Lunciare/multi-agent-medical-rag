Specific Patient Populations Renal impairment: No dosage changes are required for patients with mild and moderate renal impairment. However, combination ARB-HCTZ medications are contraindicated for patients with end-stage renal disease, as hydrochlorothiazide can induce azotemia in patients with severe renal impairment. According to KDIGO guidelines, hypertension is a significant risk factor for the development and progression of chronic kidney disease (CKD). A diagnosis of CKD is made for patients with an estimated glomerular filtration rate <60 mL/min/1.73 m 2 and a urinary albumin-to-creatinine ratio ≥300 mg/g for 3 months or longer.
