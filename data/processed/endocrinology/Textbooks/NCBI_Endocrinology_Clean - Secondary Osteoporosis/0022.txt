Treatment / Management The effective treatment of secondary osteoporosis should, first and foremost, address any underlying reversible causes. The response of secondary osteoporosis to conventional therapy is always suboptimal unless the underlying condition is addressed. Adequate calcium and vitamin D supplementation should be maintained in all patients. Weight-bearing exercise helps stimulate bone formation. There is an association between reduced physical activity and bone fracture in older men and the positive influence of exercise in women who have osteoporosis. In addition, smoking cessation and avoidance of alcohol intake should be encouraged.
