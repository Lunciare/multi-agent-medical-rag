Increase to intensive statin therapy if not at goal TREATMENT OF LIPID ABNORMALITIES IN PATIENT WITH DIABETES Life Style Changes and Weight Loss Initial treatment of lipid disorders should focus on lifestyle changes. There is little debate that exercise is beneficial and that all patients with diabetes should, if possible, exercise for at least 150 minutes per week (for example 30 minutes 5 times per week). Exercise will decrease serum TG levels and increase HDL-C levels (an increase in HDL-C requires vigorous exercise) ( 124 ). Exercise increases fitness and improves insulin resistance even with limited weight loss; reductions in obesity are even more beneficial.
