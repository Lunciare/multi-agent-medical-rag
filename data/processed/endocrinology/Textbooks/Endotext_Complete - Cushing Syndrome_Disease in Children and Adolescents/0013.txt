After recovery of the axis, regular screening for possible recurrence should be offered. Long term recurrence has been reported in 8-20% of pediatric patients with CD after initial postoperative remission ( 8 ). Screening for recurrence should be done preferably as in adults with two midnight or late-night salivary cortisol levels or with overnight dexamethasone suppression test annually ( 76 ).
