Froehner et al (2013) recently examined available comorbidityassessments to determine which may best assist in the treatment choice for elderly men with prostate cancer. A total of 1,106 men aged 65 years or older who underwent radical prostatectomy for clinically localized prostate cancer was examined with overall survival as the study endpoint. They concluded that the American Society of Anesthesiologists (ASA) physical status classification tool, supplemented by a list of more clearly defined concomitant diseases, could be useful in clinical practice and outcome studies (219). Another approach is to refer to Life Expectancy Tables (such as the Table 10 below modified from the Australian Bureau of Statistics website 2017). Such tables do not take into account an individual’s comorbidities.
