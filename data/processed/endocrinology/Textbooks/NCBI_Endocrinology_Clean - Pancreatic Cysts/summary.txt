Pancreatic Cysts
KEYWORDS: pancreatic, cysts, duct, ipmn, main, pancreatic cysts, duct ipmn, cystic, cyst, mucinous, serous, history, solid, main pancreatic, pancreatic duct

Introduction The pancreas is an important organ in the digestive system that produces enzymes for digestion and releases important hormones into our circulation that helps with metabolism. Pancreatic cysts are often detected in patients undergoing imaging with either CT or MRI for unrelated reasons due to a combination of an aging population and the overall increased use of imaging. Pancreatic cysts are identified in 2% to 20% of patients undergoing CT or MRI; however, these rates can be lower in those with no history of pancreatitis. , , On the other hand, these rates can increase to nearly one-third of patients in high-risk populations with a family history of pancreatic cancer. , These cysts can be either neoplastic or nonneoplastic. Neoplastic cysts include intraductal papillary mucinous neoplasms (IPMN), mucinous cystic neoplasm, solid pseudopapillary neoplasm, and cystic pancreatic neuroendocrine tumors.
