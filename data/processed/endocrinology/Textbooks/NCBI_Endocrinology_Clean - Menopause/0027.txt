Therefore, with treatment, the prognosis of menopausal symptoms is very good. Complications Long-term complications related to menopause are associated with decreased estrogen levels. Cardiovascular disease and osteoporosis are the most concerning of complications. Cardiovascular Disease During menopause, the decline in estrogen causes an increased risk for cardiovascular disease in women. Several changes underlie this increased risk, including negative changes in the lipid profile, impaired arterial endothelial function, and activation of the renin-angiotensin system.
