Monitoring Serum electrolytes levels, blood pressure Serum glucose levels, HbA1c Periodic eye examination (long term dexamethasone increases the risk of cataracts and glaucoma) Bone mineral density scans Creatine kinase levels (long term use of glucocorticoids such as dexamethasone predisposes to myopathy; weakness is primarily proximal) Toxicity According to the manufacturer’s labeling information, the oral LD50 of dexamethasone in female mice is 6.5 g/kg. The intravenous formulation of dexamethasone has an LD50 of 794 mg/kg in female mice. In the case of overdosage, no specific antidote is available.
