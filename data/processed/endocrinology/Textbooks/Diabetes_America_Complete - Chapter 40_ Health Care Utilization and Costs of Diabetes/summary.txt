40–1 CHAPTER 40 HEALTH CARE UTILIZATION AND COSTS OF DIABETES Laura N. McEwen, PhD, MPH, and William H. McEwen is Epidemiologist in the Division of Metabolism, Endocrinology and Diabetes and Dr. Herman is Professor in the Departments of Internal Medicine and Epidemiology at the University of Michigan, Ann Arbor, MI. Received in final form June 17, 2015. SUMMARY People with diabetes visit physician offices and emergency rooms more frequently than people without diabetes and are more likely to be admitted to the hospital and nursing homes and to receive home health care.
