SOURCE: National Home and Hospice Care Survey 2007 Health Care Utilization and Costs of Diabetes 40–59 ECONOMIC IMPACT OF DIABETES The costs of medical care for people with diabetes (direct medical costs) and the costs of lost productivity and premature mortality attributable to diabetes (indirect costs) are enormous. In this section, data from the American Diabetes Association (ADA) are used to describe the direct and indirect costs of diabetes in the United States. Data from the MEPS 2012 are also used to supplement the health care expenditure data for antihyperglycemic, antihypertensive, and cholesterol-lowering medications.
