SOURCE: National Nursing Home Survey 2004 Health Care Utilization and Costs of Diabetes 40–49 Time Since Admission The mean and median number of days since admission for people with diabetes were 754 days and 424 days compared to 853 days and 486 days for people without diabetes, respectively. The shorter mean and median length of stays for people with diabetes are likely due to the higher competing risk of death for people with diabetes. About half of resi­ dents were institutionalized for at least the entire year before the interview (Table 40.35). Forty-eight percent of people age 55–64 years with diabetes were residents ≥1 year but were less likely to be institu­ tionalized ≥1 year than residents without diabetes. The proportion increased for each age group, so that 62.5% of people age ≥85 years with diabetes were resi­ dents ≥1 year.
