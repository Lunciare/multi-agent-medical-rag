Private insurance was the most common primary source of payment for people age 18–64 years, and Medicare was the most common primary source of payment for people age ≥65 years. Among people age 18–64 years, the percentage of visits for which some payment was received from the patient (self-pay) decreased over time between 1990 and 2010, and the percentage that was paid for by private insurance increased over time.
