Indications for surgery are similar to classical PHPT and equally successful in improving bone mineral density and reducing renal problems. Serial observation is the rule for patients not willing to undergo or requiring immediate parathyroid surgery or with an unacceptably high surgical risk, with appropriate action taken as needed.
