If a patient just refuses statins (usually based on a belief that statins are toxic) we will employ other cholesterol lowering drugs. CONCLUSIONS With currently available drugs to lower LDL-C levels we are now able to markedly reduce LDL-C levels and achieve our LDL-C goals in the vast majority of patients and thereby reduce the risk of cardiovascular disease. Patients with Homozygous Familial Hypercholesterolemia and some patients with Heterozygous Familial Hypercholesterolemia still present major clinical challenges and it can be very difficult in these patients to achieve LDL-C goals.
