Prognosis and appropriate adjuvant therapy are determined by staging. Hematuria : Genitourinary atrophy can lead to asymptomatic microscopic hematuria. Because the risk of urinary malignancy is lower in women without risk factors (eg, smokers, no gross hematuria) than in men, ACOG only recommends evaluation in asymptomatic low-risk women if urine microscopy shows >25 red blood cells per high-power field. Clinical findings and diagnostic studies consistent with acute cystitis should be treated with appropriate antibiotics.
