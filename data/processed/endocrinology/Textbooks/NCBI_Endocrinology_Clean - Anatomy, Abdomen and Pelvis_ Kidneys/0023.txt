Glomerular diseases include inflammatory glomerulonephritis, often presenting with nephritic syndrome, presenting with hematuria, hypertension, and reduced glomerular filtration rate, as well as proteinuria below 3.5 g/day. Examples of conditions presenting with inflammatory glomerulonephritis include immunoglobulin A nephropathy, postinfectious glomerulonephritis, and lupus nephritis. Noninflammatory glomerulopathies present with nephrotic syndrome, characterized by proteinuria exceeding 3.5 g/day, hypoalbuminemia, and edema. Examples include minimal change disease, focal segmental glomerulosclerosis, and membranous nephropathy. Tubular diseases are commonly associated with acute kidney injury and electrolyte imbalances. Examples include acute tubular necrosis, hereditary tubulopathies, and renal tubular acidosis. Interstitial diseases encompass disorders such as acute or chronic interstitial nephritis, granulomatous conditions (tuberculosis, sarcoidosis), and amyloidosis, all of which primarily involve the renal interstitial tissue. Vascular diseases affect the renal vasculature and include vasculitis, systemic lupus erythematosus, systemic sclerosis, and atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease. Other Issues Nephrology is the medical specialty dedicated to the study, diagnosis, and nonsurgical management of renal disorders. Such conditions comprise chronic kidney disease, renal failure, diabetic nephropathy, lupus nephritis, hypertensive nephrosclerosis, acute kidney injury, glomerulonephritis, dialysis, medical management of kidney transplantation, and electrolyte disturbances.
