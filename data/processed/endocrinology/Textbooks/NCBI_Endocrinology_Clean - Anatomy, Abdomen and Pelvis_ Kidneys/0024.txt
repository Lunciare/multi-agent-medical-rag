Urology, as defined by the American Urological Association, is the medical and surgical discipline that provides care for adults and children with conditions of the genitourinary tract and adrenal glands. The specialty addresses diseases, dysfunctions, infections, and neoplasms of the genitourinary system and male reproductive organs (eg, benign prostatic hyperplasia, prostatitis, bladder and prostate cancer, erectile dysfunction, incontinence, hematuria), as well as renal and adrenal conditions requiring surgical intervention, including kidney stones, tumors, transplant surgery, and congenital renal anomalies.
