Epidemiology Graves’ orbitopathy has been said to occur in as many as 25 to 50% of patients diagnosed with Graves' disease. One study reported that the incidence of clinically apparent Graves orbitopathy was 16 per 100000 in females and 2.9 per 100000 in males.
