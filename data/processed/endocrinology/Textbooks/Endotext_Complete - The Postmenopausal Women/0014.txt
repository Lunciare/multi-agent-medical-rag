It has been speculated that higher serum calcium levels are achieved with supplements but not when calcium is absorbed through consumption of calcium-rich foods, and that transient high circulating calcium can cause tissue calcification and dysfunction. Cardiovascular risk has not been demonstrated in association with vitamin D intake ( 61 , 62 ). Practically speaking, patients should be encouraged to eat calcium and vitamin-D rich foods through their diet. Those who have documented vitamin D deficiency should be given supplements ( 56 ). Lifestyle is also important for preventing bone loss. By engaging in regular weight-bearing exercise, women lose less bone than they would if they remained sedentary ( 59 ). TREATING OSTEOPOROSIS Treatment for osteopenia and osteoporosis includes weight-bearing exercise, dietary modification, ensuring adequate calcium and vitamin D intake, and the introduction of other medications.
