The ability to modulate estrogen action via the development of SERMs provides the hope that a ‘perfect’ SERM can be produced, which will relieve vasomotor symptoms, protect the bone and the heart, maintain a favorable lipoprotein profile, and be anti-estrogenic to the endometrium and the breast. Until then, non-hormonal alternatives are available for women who cannot or do not wish to take HT, with some promising new treatments on the horizon.
