It is important to note that all of these criteria should be used for screening for further genetic testing and should not be used solely for diagnosis of dysbetalipoproteinemia. All of these screening criteria have very low positive predictive value, meaning that only a few of those who meet the criteria actually have dysbetalipoproteinemia when tested for APOE genotype ( 23 ).
