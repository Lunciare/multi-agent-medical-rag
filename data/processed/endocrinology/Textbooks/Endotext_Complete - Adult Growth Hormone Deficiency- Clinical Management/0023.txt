The mechanism for the beneficial effect of GH on well-being and QoL remains speculative ( 164 ). GH has been shown to cross the blood brain barrier ( 166 , 167 ) and to exert physiological effects in the central nervous system as evidenced by the generation of neurotransmitters ( 166 ), an effect reduced by progressive aging ( 168 , 169 ).
