Anatomy, Abdomen and Pelvis: Lymphatic Drainage
KEYWORDS: lymph, lymphatic, nodes, lymph nodes, cells, vessels, drain, fluid, drainage, tissue, abdominal, groups, inguinal, tract, flow

Introduction The lymphatic system is a system that drains the leaked tissue fluid back into the circulation. Its components are lymphatic vessels, lymphatic organs, lymph nodes, and widely scattered lymphoid tissue within the connective tissue. Lymph is the tissue fluid flowing through these lymphatic channels. Lymphatic vessels contain valves that prevent the backflow of transported lymph. The lymphatic vessels are so thin that the mere presence of valves gives the lymphatic channels the beaded appearance. Lymph flow from the peritoneum navigates through the thoracic duct to the intrathoracic lymph nodes.
