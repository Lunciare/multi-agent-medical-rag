MODY2 is stable, rarely requires insulin treatment except during pregnancy to protect the fetus from hyperglycemia, and has an excellent prognosis for avoidance of vascular complications. MODY1 and 3 frequently respond to sulfonylurea drugs (SU) initially but may progress to insulin depen­ dence and risk for development of vascular complications dependent on metabolic control. MODY constitutes about 1.5%–2.5% of new-onset childhood diabetes and should be suspected in those with: a family history of multiple affected members in two to three generations, with onset before age 35 years; absence of markers of autoimmunity; “mild diabetes” requiring <0.5 U/kg insulin from the outset or unusually prolonged “honeymoon” phase.
