Surgical Surgical interventions with laparotomy include distal pancreatectomy and/or splenectomy, central pancreatectomy, intracystic ligation of the blood vessel, aneurysm ligation, and bypass graft. Surgery has a success rate of 70% to 80%, with a risk of 10% to 50% mortality and a risk of recurrent bleeding to be 0% to 5%.
