Novel Therapy Several novel therapies include the endoscopic-guided US with or without doing cystogastrostomy using fibrin glue and histoacryl adhesives. These approaches provide a promising tool for management. The recurrence rate of bleeding remained low after this effective treatment. This approach gives a promising outcome, particularly in patients who do not show angiographic evidence and are allergic to contrast contraindication. Those who are poor candidates for surgery.
