Aortoenteric fistula Prognosis Overall, HP has a mortality rate of 9.6%. That rate increased in case of malignancy complicated with HP. If only supportive therapy administered, the mortality rate reached 90%. Complications Common Complications for HP Major hemorrhage of upper or lower GI bleeding Chronic anemia Visceral rupture and retroperitoneal hematoma. Damage to the arterial wall while manipulation causes internal bleeding. Allergic reactions to the local anesthetic, contrast dye, or sedatives. Acute kidney injury.
