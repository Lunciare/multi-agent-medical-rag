Because of the increasing frequency of T2DM among adolescents with obesity, and the worsening of diabetes-related morbidities that may result from delayed diagnosis, the clinician should be alert to the possible of T2DM in all adolescents with generalized and central obesity, and especially those with strong family histories of early-onset (< 40 years of age, one or more parent affected) T2DM ( 201 ).
