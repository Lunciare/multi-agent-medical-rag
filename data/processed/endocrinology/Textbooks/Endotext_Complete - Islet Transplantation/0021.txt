In patients with sustained optimal function, glycemic profiles approach those of non-diabetic individuals, with median glucose values around 103 mg/dL ( 95 – 112 ), low variability (SD ~14 mg/dL), and negligible exposure to hyper- or hypoglycemia; HbA1c averages ~5.6% (5.0–5.8) ( 71 ).
