Portal vein thrombosis can present with varied symptoms, including abdominal pain, ischemic hepatitis, liver failure, and/or small intestine infarction. Patients generally receive treatment with anticoagulant medication. This treatment can incur further complications by the accompanying coagulopathy often seen in these patients. The surgeon may consider a transjugular intrahepatic portosystemic shunt (TIPS) procedure in patients who do not improve or continue to worsen despite anticoagulation.
