Intratympanic Steroid Injection
KEYWORDS: ear, membrane, treatment, window, intratympanic, hearing, steroid, tympanic, inner, inner ear, round, systemic, tympanic membrane, round window, middle

International Consensus (ICON) on the treatment of Meniere's Disease recommended ITS as second-line therapy when medical treatment has failed. There is good evidence suggesting that ITSI can be used as an effective treatment for refractory Meniere disease, particularly for vertigo control, reducing the number of attacks. A double-blind, randomized controlled trial in 2016 showed that corticosteroid injections could provide reliable, complete vertigo control at 2 years in approximately 50% of cases after just a single course. However, the effectiveness of ITSI is most pronounced when conservative medical management has failed, and ITS treatment is provided as-needed therapy. Refractory Tinnitus In a study by Yener et al., high concentrations of steroid in the inner ear from an ITS injection could reduce tinnitus by increasing blood flow to the inner ear. The majority of papers suggest that ITSI is most effective in acute tinnitus rather than chronic. Autoimmune Inner Ear Diseases In autoimmune inner ear diseases (AIED), a corticosteroid is a first-line treatment. Contraindications Following are the contraindications: Uncooperative patient: absolute patient cooperation is required to ensure safe and successful administration.
