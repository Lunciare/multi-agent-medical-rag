Beyond raising the risk for contracting an infection, prognosis is frequently worse for many of these conditions which increases the frequency of rare and life-threatening infectious processes seen in those with DM. This is the consequence of disturbances in the immune system which have been well described involving both innate and adaptive immunity. However, glucose lowering therapies appear to be able to counteract some of the increased risk of infection and worsened prognosis by improving function of immune cells.
