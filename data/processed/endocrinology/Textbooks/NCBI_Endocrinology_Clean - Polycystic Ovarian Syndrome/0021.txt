Compared with placebo, insulin sensitivity in women with PCOS was improved without significantly affecting BMI. Data is limited, and its use has been mostly applied as fertility treatment of PCOS or when metformin is not tolerated, given that it has fewer gastrointestinal adverse effects. Differential Diagnosis The differential diagnoses for PCOS include the following: Use of androgenic steroids Hypothyroidism Late-onset congenital adrenal hyperplasia Idiopathic/familial hirsutism Ovarian malignancies Prognosis The prevalence of hirsutism and acne decreases with age. Ovarian volume and follicle number also decline with age, with the age-related decrease in follicle number seemingly greater than ovarian volume. While ovulation rates improve in women with PCOS as they age, successful pregnancies and live births decrease with age. A 41% to 64.8% pregnancy success rate has been identified in females treated with letrozole , a cumulative live-birth rate of 31% (average 3 attempts) with intrauterine insemination , and a 29% live-birth rate for those treated with in-vitro fertilization (IVF).
