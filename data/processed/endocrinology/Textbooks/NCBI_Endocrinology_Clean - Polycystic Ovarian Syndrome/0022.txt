Women with PCOS had a higher rate of admissions for menorrhagia (14.1% versus 3.6%), treatment of infertility (40.9% versus 4.6%), and miscarriage (11.1% versus 6.1%), and they were more likely to require in vitro fertilization (17.2% versus 2.0%). Despite the higher prevalence of risk factors noted above, a recent 32-year prospective study of PCOS women, aged 72 to 91 at the time of assessment, showed no evidence of an increase in all-cause mortality or cardiovascular death.
