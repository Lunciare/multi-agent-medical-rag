OGTT is preferred over HbA1c due to its decreased sensitivity in PCOS patients. Rescreening should be conducted every 3 to 4 years due to the higher frequency of risk factors in this population compared to the general population. Additionally, obese and overweight patients should be screened for symptoms of OSA and referred for sleep studies when this test is positive. Metabolic Dysfunction-Associated Steatotic Liver Disease Women with PCOS have 3 times the increased risk of MASLD, formerly known as nonalcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD); MASLD has been associated with androgen excess and low sex hormone-binding globulin. Routine measurement of LFT is not recommended unless the patient is overweight or obese, given the low sensitivity and specificity in the diagnosis of MASLD. In these patients, a change in management with newer antidiabetic medications like GLP-1 agonists can decrease the risk of the development of NAFLD.
