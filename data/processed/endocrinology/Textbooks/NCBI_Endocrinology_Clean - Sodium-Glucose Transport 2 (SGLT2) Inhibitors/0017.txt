As of 2025, all of these formulations remain under patent protection and lack generic equivalents in the U.S. Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus According to the 2022 American Diabetes Association (ADA) guidelines, the recommended initial therapy for T2DM is metformin in combination with comprehensive lifestyle modification. Based on additional risk factors, including ASCVD, heart failure, and CKD, the use of SGLT2 inhibitors may be appropriate. The maximum recommended doses for these agents are discussed below. Canagliflozin is initiated at 100 mg once daily and may be increased to 300 mg daily. This medication is not recommended for glycemic control in patients with an eGFR less than 30 mL/min/1.73 m². Dapagliflozin is started at 5 mg once daily, with the option to increase to 10 mg once daily to achieve glycemic targets.
