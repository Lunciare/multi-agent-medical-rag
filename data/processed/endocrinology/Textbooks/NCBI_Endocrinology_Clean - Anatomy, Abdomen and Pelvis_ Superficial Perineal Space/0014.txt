The same anatomic pathways account for the progression of necrotizing fascitis - Fournier gangrene within the perineal region. The treatment of a case of Fournier’s gangrene is a surgical emergency and requires urgent surgical excision of the dead and infected tissue. Clinical Significance The tunica albuginea, a fibro-elastic connective tissue surrounding each corpus cavernosum and the corpus spongiosum, facilitates penile rigidity during erection. Peyronies disease can lead to the development of plaques in this layer and disrupt the natural ability of tunica albuginea to perform its function. The Bartholin glands in females are within the superficial perineal space. These glands secrete a fluid that lubricates the vagina.
