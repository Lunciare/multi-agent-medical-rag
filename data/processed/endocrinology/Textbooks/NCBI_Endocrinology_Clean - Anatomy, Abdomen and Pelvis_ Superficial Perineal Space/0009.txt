Anatomy, Abdomen and Pelvis: Superficial Perineal Space
KEYWORDS: perineal, superficial, fascia, artery, pudendal, penis, superficial perineal, membrane, internal, perineal membrane, perineum, internal pudendal, deep, clitoris, males

the internal pudendal artery: the dorsal artery of the penis/clitoris, runs between the perineal membrane and pubic symphysis and supplies the corpus spongiosum and the glans of the penis in males and the glans and prepuce of the clitoris in females. Venous drainage to the superficial perineal space is derived from the internal pudendal vein, formed from the venae comitantes of the internal pudendal vein, which unite to drain as a single vessel into the internal iliac vein. The lymphatics from the skin over the external genitalia drain into the superficial inguinal lymph nodes, and the glans, corpus spongiosum, and corpora cavernosa drain to the deep inguinal lymph nodes and external iliac lymph nodes. Nerves The pudendal nerves supply the major cutaneous and motor innervation to the structures in the perineum. It is formed from the anterior division of the anterior rami of the second, third, and fourth sacral spinal nerve of the sacral plexus. The pudendal nerve leaves the pelvic cavity via the greater sciatic foramen and passes around the sacrospinous ligament to enter the anal triangle via the lesser sciatic foramen.
