An increasing proportion of youth with apparent type 2 diabetes has been reported since the late 1990s, especially in minority populations (76,77). The epidemiology of type 2 diabetes in youth is yet unclear, due to its relative rarity, the lack of standard clinical and epidemiologic definitions, and the small number of appropriate, population-based studies. Selected data on prevalence, inci­ dence, and trends in the incidence of type 2 diabetes in youth age <20 years from North American studies are summarized, with a focus on race/ethnicity-specific estimates. A comprehensive overview of type 2 diabetes is presented in Chapter 3 Prevalence and Incidence of Type 2 Diabetes and Prediabetes. Prevalence Population-based studies, where all indi­ viduals within a geographic area undergo diabetes screening, are ideal to determine prevalence, as they capture even undiag­ nosed cases. However, among the limited number of available population-based studies of type 2 diabetes in youth, few test oral glucose tolerance, and many lack key data essential to differentiate type 2 diabetes from type 1 diabetes.
