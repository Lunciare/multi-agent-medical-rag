Lifestyle Characteristics Among People With Diabetes and Prediabetes 10–17 The prevalence of smoking has been examined in multicenter trials and community-based studies (Table 10.13). SEARCH found differences in smoking status between adolescents with type 1 and type 2 diabetes, with 9.9% of those with type 1 diabetes using tobacco products and 8.1% smoking cigarettes compared to 15.7% of those with type 2 diabetes using tobacco products and 13.1% smoking cigarettes (32). Look AHEAD found that 4.4% of people with confirmed diabetes were current smokers, 45.4% were former smokers, and 50.2% had never smoked (12). TRIAD found that 15% of their sample reported that they were current smokers, and smoking was more common in younger adults, non- Hispanic African Americans, and those with less education (33).
