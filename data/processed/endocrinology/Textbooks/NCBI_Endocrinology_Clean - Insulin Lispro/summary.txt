Insulin Lispro
KEYWORDS: insulin, lispro, insulin lispro, glucose, patients, hypoglycemia, dose, risk, levels, diabetes, blood glucose, monitoring, blood, units, injection

Indications FDA- Approved Indications Insulin lispro is an FDA-approved insulin analog for treating patients with diabetes types 1 and 2 to control hyperglycemia. One unit of insulin lispro is as potent as 1 unit of human insulin in lowering glucose. Off-Label Uses Insulin lispro's off-label uses include treating patients with mild-to-moderate diabetic ketoacidosis, gestational diabetes, and mild-to-moderate hyperosmolar hyperglycemic state. Insulin lispro is also available in premixed insulin products such as protamine/lispro (50%/50%) and protamine/lispro (75%/25%). Insulin lispro-aabc, an ultra-rapid-acting insulin (URLi), received FDA approval in 2020. Mechanism of Action Insulin lispro is a rapid-acting, human insulin analog that works parenterally to lower blood glucose by regulating the metabolism of carbohydrates, proteins, and fats.
