Etiology There are numeous causes of hypothalamic dysfunction: Brain surgery Traumatic brain injury Brain tumors Radiation Chemotherapy Nutritional deficiencies (anorexia nervosa) Brain aneurysms Genetic disorders (Prader-Willi syndrome, Kallmann syndrome) Infections Inflammatory disease (multiple sclerosis, neurosarcoidosis) Paraneoplastic syndromes Rapid-onset obesity with hypothalamic dysfunction, hypoventilation, and autonomic dysregulation syndrome Epidemiology The epidemiology of hypothalamic dysfunction depends on the patient's clinical presentation and etiology. Hypothalamic dysfunction accounts for almost 20 to 35% of the cases of secondary amenorrhea in the United States.
