For tumors, surgery or radiation may be required. Hypothalamic gliomas are usually observed. A biopsy can be performed for those not involving the optic chiasm and tracts. For hypothalamic hamartomas, if symptomatic with uncontrolled seizures, surgery is recommended. Thermoablation and radiosurgery can also be used. For hormonal deficiencies, therapeutic hormone replacement is used. Nutritional guidance and certain medicines may be used to regulate the patient's appetite. Delayed puberty in Frohlich syndrome is treated in males with human chorionic gonadotropin and later with testosterone; in females with estrogen and later estrogen-progestin.
