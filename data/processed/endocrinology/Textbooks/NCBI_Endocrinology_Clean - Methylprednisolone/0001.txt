Methylprednisolone
KEYWORDS: methylprednisolone, acute, patients, doses, therapy, glucocorticoids, administration, effects, treatment, dose, syndrome, disease, administered, adverse, oral

Indications Methylprednisolone is a systemic synthetic corticosteroid that exerts a wide range of physiologic effects similar to naturally occurring glucocorticoids. Methylprednisolone is typically prescribed due to its anti-inflammatory and immunosuppressive activity in the human body. The FDA-approved indications and off-label uses for methylprednisolone therapy are listed below.
