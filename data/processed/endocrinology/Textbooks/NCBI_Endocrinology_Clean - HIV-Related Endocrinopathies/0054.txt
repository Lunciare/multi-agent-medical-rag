All patients with GH deficiency are at increased risk of reduced bone mineral density with resulting fractures, reduced lean muscle mass, and increased fat mass. Patients with GH deficiency routinely score lower on quality-of-life questionnaires than their healthy counterparts. Metabolic Dysfunction HIV- and AIDS-Wasting Syndromes Disorders of Fat Distribution, Glucose, and Lipid Metabolism Lipodystrophy is associated with dyslipidemia, insulin resistance, and chronic systemic inflammation, which can increase overall cardiovascular disease risk. Body fat distribution abnormalities can lead to stigmatization, affecting self-esteem and quality of life. The complications of diabetes mellitus are manifold and include cerebrovascular disease, cardiovascular disease, chronic kidney disease, peripheral vascular disease, and diabetic retinopathy. Dyslipidemia increases the risk of atherosclerosis and associated vascular complications. Severe hypertriglyceridemia can cause pancreatitis. Complications related to advanced HIV and immunocompromise are no longer common in developed countries due to the widespread use of ART. However, HIV status and treatment with ART increase the risk for metabolic complications and cardiovascular disease.
