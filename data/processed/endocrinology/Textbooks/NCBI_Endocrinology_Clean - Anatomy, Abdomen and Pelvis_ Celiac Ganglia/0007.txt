Celiac axis compression syndrome (also known as median arcuate ligament syndrome) presents with a triad of postprandial abdominal pain, weight loss, and an occasional abdominal bruit. Most of the symptoms produced are due to decreased blood flow to the intestine from the celiac artery. However, neurolysis of celiac ganglia is often part of the treatment to eliminate the neuronal irritation of the celiac ganglia.
