Total parathyroidectomy with forearm transplantation is associated with a lower chance of persistent hyperparathyroidism; however, there is a higher risk of hypoparathyroidism of 22% to 36% compared to 10% with subtotal parathyroidectomy. Bone mineral density improves after parathyroidectomy, but bone antiresorptive medications are recommended to treat osteoporosis in resistant cases. Non-Functioning Gastro-Entero-Pancreatic Tumors Non-functioning pancreatic NETs are the most common gastro-entero-pancreatic tumors. These lesions cause no clinical syndrome and may secrete a small amount of pancreatic polypeptide and gastrin. These represent the most frequent cause of death in MEN1 patients. There can be a delay in diagnosing non-functioning pancreatic NET due to the lack of specific symptoms or clinical findings. Radiological screening for diagnosis of pancreatic NET in MEN1 is recommended at the age of 10 years. Treatment of a non-functioning pancreatic NET is challenging and controversial because of the lack of consensus on surgical indications. Triponez et al. reported that tumor size in non-functioning pancreatic NET correlates with the risk of metastasis and death , but other studies did not confirm similar findings.
