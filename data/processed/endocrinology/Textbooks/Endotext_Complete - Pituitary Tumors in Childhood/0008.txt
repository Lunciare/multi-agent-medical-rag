ACTH-secreting adenomas have an earlier onset and predominate in the pre-pubertal period, where interestingly male cases are more frequent, while GH-secreting adenomas are very rare before puberty, except in XLAG ( 7 ). Similar to adults, presenting symptoms are generally related to the endocrine dysfunction, such as growth delay and primary amenorrhea, rather than to mass effects ( 41 , 42 , 44 - 48 ).
