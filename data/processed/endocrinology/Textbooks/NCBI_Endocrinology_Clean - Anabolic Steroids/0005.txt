While the benefits of anabolic steroid use to increase athletic performance are undeniable, they also pose a serious health risk, which is why sports organizations universally ban them. Anabolic steroid abuse can inappropriately activate signaling proteins like mTOR, disrupt normal protein synthesis, modify cell cycle metabolism, increase oxidative stress, and cause cellular apoptosis.
