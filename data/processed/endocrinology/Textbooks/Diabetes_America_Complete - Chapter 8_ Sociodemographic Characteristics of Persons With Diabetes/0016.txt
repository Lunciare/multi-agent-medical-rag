A larger percentage of adults with diabetes age 20–44 years were divorced or separated than adults without diabetes (12.8% vs. 7.6%) (Appendix 8.12). A larger percentage of men with diabetes diagnosed age ≥20 years were married than women with diabetes diagnosed age ≥20 years or men without diabetes (68.9%, 48.9%, 57.6%, respectively), and a larger percentage of women with diabetes diagnosed age ≥20 years were widowed (20.5%) or divorced/ separated (18.1%) compared to men with diabetes diagnosed age ≥20 years (5.8% and 12.0%) or women without diabetes (8.4% and 12.8%). The marital status differences between adults with or without diabetes were diminished with increasing age groups. Regardless of diabetes status, men age ≥65 years were more often married (>70%) than women (<45%) (Appendix 8.12).
