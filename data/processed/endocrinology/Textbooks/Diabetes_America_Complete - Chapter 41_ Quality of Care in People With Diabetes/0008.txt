Cholesterol control has improved dramatically: 33.2% of persons with diabetes had total choles­ terol <200 mg/dL (<5.18 mmol/L) and 10.7% had LDL cholesterol <100 mg/dL (<2.59 mmol/L) in 1988– 1994 compared to 67.0% and 55.0% in 2005–2010, respectively (Figure 41.2). Correspondingly, medication treatment for lowering blood pressure and cholesterol has increased over the past two decades, with more than half of all persons with diagnosed diabetes reporting current medication use for hypertension or high cholesterol in 2005–2010 (Table 41.1). Blood pressure control (<130/80 mmHg) in diabetic individuals treated for high blood pressure has improved dramatically from 17.1% in 1988–1994 to 43.5% in 2005–2010.
