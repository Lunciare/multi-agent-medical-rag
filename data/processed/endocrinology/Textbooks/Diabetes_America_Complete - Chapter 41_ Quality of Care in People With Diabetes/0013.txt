Indeed, 28.6% of persons with diagnosed diabetes report that they had not had a health professional check for sores or irritations on their feet in the past year (Figure 41.13). Further, 18.8% of adults with diabetes report that they have never conducted a “self-check” for sores or irritations on their feet (Figure 41.14).
