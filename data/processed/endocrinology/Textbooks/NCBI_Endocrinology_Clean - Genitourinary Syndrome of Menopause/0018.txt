Complications GSM can lead to several complications if left untreated or undertreated. These complications include chronic vaginal dryness, recurrent urinary tract infections, and painful sexual intercourse, which can significantly impact an individual's quality of life. Additionally, GSM may cause urinary incontinence, increased susceptibility to vaginal infections, and overall discomfort in daily activities. The physical symptoms may also lead to emotional and psychological distress, including reduced self-esteem and intimacy issues. Early recognition and appropriate treatment are crucial to prevent these complications and improve overall well-being.
