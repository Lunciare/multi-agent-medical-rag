Mutations in SCARB1 associated with decreased SR-B1 have been observed in individuals with high HDL-C levels ( 47 - 49 ). Heterozygotes have intermediate elevations of HDL-C between wild-type and homozygous individuals. Studies have suggested that some but not all mutations in SCARB1 result in an increased risk of ASCVD despite increased HDL-C levels ( 40 , 49 ). A decrease in adrenal function has been reported in some individuals with SCARB1 mutations likely due to a reduced ability of SR-B1 to facilitate cholesterol uptake into the adrenal glands ( 48 , 50 ). Abnormalities in platelet function have also been observed in some patients ( 50 ).
