Second, the literature notes that mixed DKA-HHS presentations are increasingly common, especially in older adults and those with type 2 diabetes, and these cases may not fit neatly into the classic criteria ( 2 ). Patients with ketosis prone type 2 diabetes present with ketosis and milder acidosis than the one expects in DKA and in some cases with near normal pH and bicarbonate.
