Insulin requirements were reduced by 50% in those in the gymnema group. Additionally, blood glucose and HbA1c levels were reduced ( 50 ). An open-label and non-randomized trial studied gymnema in 47 patients with T2DM. In addition to conventional oral hypoglycemic agents, 400 mg of a water-soluble extract of gymnema was administered for 18 to 20 months to 22 of the participants. At the conclusion of the study, the gymnema group had significantly lower fasting glucose (29% reduction, p<0.001).
