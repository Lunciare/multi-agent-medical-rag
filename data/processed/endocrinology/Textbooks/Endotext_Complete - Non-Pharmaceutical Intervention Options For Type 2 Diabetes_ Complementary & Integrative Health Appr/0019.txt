A systematic review and meta-analysis of ten studies (n=509) was published in 2021 to determine the effect of chromium supplementation on blood glucose and lipid levels in patients with T2DM. The weighted mean difference in HbA1c indicated a significant reduction in HbA1c (-0.54%; 95% CI: -0.98 to -0.09; p = 0.02). No difference in fasting plasma glucose was found (-29.65 mg/dL; 95% CI: -68.62 to 9.31; p=0.14). There was no difference found in triglycerides, total cholesterol, low-density lipoproteins, and high-density lipoproteins. Results are summarized in Table 10 ( 82 ).
