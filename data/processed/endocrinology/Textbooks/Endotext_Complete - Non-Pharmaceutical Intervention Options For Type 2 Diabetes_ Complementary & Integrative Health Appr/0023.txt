Patients were not receiving insulin or other glucose-lowering agents aside from glicazide, a sulfonylurea, of which all participants were taking 30 mg daily. Patients were randomized to 120 mg daily of cinnamon, 360 mg daily of cinnamon, or placebo for 12 weeks. Both the 120 mg and 360 mg cinnamon groups experienced significantly lower HbA1c and fasting plasma glucose measurements. There was no significant change in either parameter for the placebo group.
