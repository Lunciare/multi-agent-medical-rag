Evaluation of Infertility, Ovulation Induction and Assisted Reproduction
KEYWORDS: infertility, sperm, women, patients, pregnancy, oocyte, ivf, ovulation, age, treatment, diagnosis, factor, oocytes, reproductive, cervical

ABSTRACT Infertility is a prevalent condition among men and women and can be emotionally devastating. The cause of a couple’s infertility may be female, male, a combination, or unexplained. This chapter serves to outline the evidence-based evaluation of infertility and treatments including ovulation induction and assisted reproductive technologies. Reproduction is a mandate of nature. The continuity of species demands promulgation. However, as humans have attempted to control nature, reproduction has evolved into an option.
