Pituitary Gland Imaging
KEYWORDS: pituitary, lesions, imaging, mri, gland, patients, contrast, pituitary gland, mass, evaluating, pituitary lesions, angiography, sella, sellar, traditional

Introduction The pituitary is an important endocrine gland located at the base of the brain. It is involved in nearly all processes of homeostasis, as well as growth and development. This gland commonly develops pathology, which may result in a mass effect on adjacent intracranial structures or be hormonally active. Pituitary adenomas are present in 14% of autopsy studies and 23% of radiology studies. Given the high prevalence, either due to symptoms from an underlying disease process or through incidental discovery, it is crucial to understand the methods for imaging pituitary lesions. This article will discuss the various imaging modalities for the pituitary gland, their indications, and their limitations.
