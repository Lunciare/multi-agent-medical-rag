Environmental Endocrinology: An Expanding Horizon
KEYWORDS: endocrine, pubmed, environment, health, impact, free, disorders, exposure, endocrinology, diabetes, environmental, pmc free, pmc, article, free article

ABSTRACT Environment is an important determinant of endocrine health and certain endocrine disorders could also have a significant impact on the surroundings. Environmental endocrinology is an emerging field of medicine which encompasses the bidirectional impact of endocrine disorders and the physical, chemical, biological, and social environment of an individual. As we aim to improve endocrine health, it is also important to address the external environmental factors that may affect a given endocrine condition. As more data is emerging on this subject, it will help to formulate clinical practice guidelines and policies to optimize endocrine disorders in light of a given external environment.
