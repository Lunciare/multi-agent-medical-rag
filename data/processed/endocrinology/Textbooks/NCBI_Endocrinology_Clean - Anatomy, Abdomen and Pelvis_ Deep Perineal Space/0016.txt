The term "perineal membrane" has since replaced "urogenital diaphragm" in the Terminologia Anatomica and is now recognized as the inferior fascial boundary of this region. Despite clear evidence to the contrary, references to the urogenital diaphragm remain common in anatomical literature and illustrations, necessitating awareness of its inaccuracy.
