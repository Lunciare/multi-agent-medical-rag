Limb bud mesodermal layers organize into what is known as the zone of polarizing activity, which produces the sonic hedgehog gene product. This protein activates the genes for the bone morphogenetic protein and Hoxd-9 through Hoxd-13. These genes help modulate proper limb growth and elongation. Initially, the lower limb buds grow along the coronal plane. By weeks 6 to 8, the lower limb buds start growth along the sagittal plane and rotate internally until the knee faces anteriorly. The growth and differentiation of various limb structures proceed as described below. Vasculature The external iliac artery and a branch of the umbilical artery known as the axis artery (sciatic artery) develop in parallel. The femoral artery arises from the external iliac artery as the lower limb bud lengthens. The profunda femoris artery later arises from the femoral artery. The axis artery gives rise to the anterior tibial artery, which branches into the dorsalis pedis and posterior tibial arteries.
