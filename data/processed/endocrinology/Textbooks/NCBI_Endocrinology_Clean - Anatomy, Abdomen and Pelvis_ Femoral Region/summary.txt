Anatomy, Abdomen and Pelvis: Femoral Region
KEYWORDS: femoral, thigh, nerve, artery, muscles, muscle, hip, anterior, posterior, adductor, lateral, limb, femoris, knee, vein

Introduction The femoral or thigh region of the lower limb is bounded proximally by the hip and distally by the knee (see Image . Right Femur Anatomy, Posterior Surface). Connective tissue septae divide this region into 3 muscle compartments: anterior, posterior, and medial. Femoral muscles contribute to the hip and knee's range of motion. The lower limb's major neurovascular structures pass through the femoral compartments before entering or exiting the pelvis. Most thigh injuries arise from trauma or overuse.
