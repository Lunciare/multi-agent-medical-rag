In neonates, hyperinsulinism may occur as a transient issue due to intrauterine factors, in association with recognized clinical syndromes, or may reflect genetic mutations in the pathway of insulin secretion (Figure 5). In older children, acquired forms of hyperinsulinism, including insulinoma, autoimmune causes, and medications are more common.
