Prediabetes
KEYWORDS: prediabetes, diabetes, glucose, blood, levels, blood glucose, test, mellitus, glucose levels, diabetes mellitus, patients, treatment, type, weight, insulin

Introduction Prediabetes is a precursor before the diagnosis of diabetes mellitus. Adults with prediabetes often may show no signs or symptoms of diabetes but will have blood sugar levels higher than normal. The normal blood glucose level is between 70 mg/dL to 99 mg/dL. In patients with prediabetes, you can expect to see blood glucose levels elevated between 110 mg/dL to - 125 mg/dL. However, these levels do not meet the required criteria for a diagnosis of diabetes mellitus. For this reason, many people are not aware that they are living with prediabetes.
