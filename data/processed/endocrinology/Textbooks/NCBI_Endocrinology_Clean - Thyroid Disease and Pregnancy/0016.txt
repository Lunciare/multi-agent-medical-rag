However, it should be noted that there are substantial population differences in the 'normal’ TSH upper reference limit, and significant changes from a prior baseline should also be noted. Thyroid Antibodies in Euthyroid Pregnant Women Studies have shown the overall prevalence of TPOAb in women of childbearing age is 6% to 20%. These women are at higher risk of developing hypothyroidism during pregnancy, and therefore, thyroid function should be monitored in these women. Additionally, study results have shown that TPOAb positivity has been associated with spontaneous pregnancy loss as well as an increased risk of preterm delivery. At present, there is not sufficient evidence to warrant supplementation of levothyroxine in euthyroid pregnant women with TPOAb positivity.
