The renal vasculature arises from the spinal vertebral levels of L1 to L2 or L3. The venous system of the kidneys begins as the stellate veins in the renal cortex (see Image. Azygos Venous System). These veins coalesce into arcuate veins located near the base of the renal pyramids within the kidney. The arcuate veins drain into the interlobar veins as they near the renal calyces. The interlobar veins unify into segmental veins that finally unite to form the beginning of the renal vein in the hilum of each kidney. The renal vein is typically located anterior and inferior to the renal artery in the hilum of the kidney. Unlike the renal arterial system, the renal venous system has what anatomists refer to as a “free anastomosis” system in place.
