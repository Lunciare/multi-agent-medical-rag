Because these classification systems dictate the treatment strategies without considering the status of active inflammation, they are now obsolete. A new system, the was proposed in 1989, considering the classic signs of inflammation, pain, swelling, redness, and impaired function (see Table 4 ). The European Group on Graves Orbitopathy (EUGOGO) later amended the CAS system. According to the amended CAS, active TED scores greater than 3 out of 7 points at the first visit or greater than 4 out of 10 points at subsequent visits.
