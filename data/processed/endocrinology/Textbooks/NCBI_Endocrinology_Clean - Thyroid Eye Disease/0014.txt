Clinical assessment protocols given by the VISA and EUGOGO teams are not interchangeable. Hence, only a single system should be used for a specific patient. All the scoring systems focus on a qualitative assessment, assigning equal weight to all symptoms, regardless of their diagnostic significance. The equality of symptoms could potentially undermine the accurate monitoring of disease progression. For example, the cut-off points (3/7 or 4/10 in CAS scoring) may not accurately reflect the extent of the disease. Ocular Imaging Intraocular vasculature can be imaged using optical coherence tomography angiography (OCTA). OCTA analysis of the macula and the optic nerve head, which shows perfusion changes within the eye, corresponds to disease activity. Choroidal imaging is also performed through enhanced depth imaging, high-definition optical coherence tomography, and swept-source optical coherence tomography of the subfoveal choroid. Subfoveal choroidal thickness changes are well-documented with progression of TED, with changes in the choroidal vascularity index corresponding to the severity.
