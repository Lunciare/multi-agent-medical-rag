Moderate-to-Severe TED Active cases Early immunosuppression is essential, and corticosteroids are the first-line treatment. The EUGOGO management protocol consists of low-dose pulse therapy with intravenous methylprednisolone (IVMP), 500 mg weekly for the initial 6 weeks. Over the next 6 weeks, the dose is tapered to 250 mg weekly. Severe active cases require prolonged oral corticosteroids for 6 months. Withdrawal of steroids may worsen the disease, but this can be prevented by adjuvant orbital radiotherapy. Immunomodulators are considered the second-line treatment. Indications for their use include steroid intolerance, steroid dependence, disease refractory to steroids, or contraindications to steroid use. Furthermore, methotrexate 7.5 to 10 mg weekly has shown a significant improvement in CAS and ocular motility within 12 months of treatment. Azathioprine can be combined with low-dose orbital radiation or IVMP for moderate-to-severe TED . Mycophenolate mofetil therapy demonstrated a superior CAS response with minimal disease reactivation compared to other treatment options. Immunobiologic agents Rituximab, a humanized chimeric monoclonal antibody targeting CD20 on B cells, has shown a 100% response rate
