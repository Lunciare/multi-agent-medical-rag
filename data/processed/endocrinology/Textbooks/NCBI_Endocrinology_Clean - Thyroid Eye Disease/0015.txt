Computed Tomography Scan Characteristic fusiform enlargement of EOM is an enlarged muscle belly with relative sparing of tendons. Long-standing cases show significant EOM changes referred to as the "Coca-Cola bottle sign." Orbital imaging in unilateral TED cases confirms asymmetric bilateral EOM changes. The Barrett index is useful for quantitatively measuring EOM thickness and apical crowding. The vertical index is the sum of vertical muscle diameters divided by the height of the orbit. The horizontal index is calculated similarly. The greater of the 2 ratios is the Barrett index. A Barrett muscle index greater than 67% predicts optic neuropathy. Imaging of dysthryoid optic neuropathy may show soft tissue signs like apical crowding, enlarged superior ophthalmic vein, enlarged EOM (especially the medial rectus), Barrett muscle index greater than 67%, orbital fat prolapse across the superior orbital fissure, perineural fat effacement, and enlarged lacrimal gland.
