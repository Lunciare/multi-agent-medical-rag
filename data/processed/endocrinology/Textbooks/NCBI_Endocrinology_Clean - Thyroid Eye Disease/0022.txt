Orbital CT scan shows bilateral diffuse soft-tissue enlargement with erosion of the bony orbit. Histopathology and immunohistochemistry features on the soft tissue biopsy are diagnostic. Orbital floor fracture: Altered globe position and limitation of EOM movements A history of trauma is followed by enophthalmos and restriction of upgaze. An orbital CT scan shows the bony defect with entrapped soft tissues. Amyloidosis: Bilateral proptosis with associated neuropathy Soft-tissue biopsy reveals eosinophilic material on histopathology, with birefringence on polarized light microscopy. Prognosis Approximately 80% of thyroid eye disease cases require conservative management with topical lubricant eye drops. Approximately 5% of TED cases require systemic corticosteroids or immunomodulatory therapy. About 15% of patients undergo surgical intervention. Long-term follow-up and close monitoring for disease complications are essential.
