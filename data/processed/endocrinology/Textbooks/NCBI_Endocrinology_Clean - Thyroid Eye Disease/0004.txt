Sex : There is a higher preponderance in women due to the increased risk of autoimmune diseases in this population. Men tend to have more severe ocular involvement and worse outcomes. Genetics : Polymorphisms in genes encoding cytotoxic T-lymphocyte–associated antigen 4 (CTLA-4), human leukocyte antigen DRB1 (HLA-DRB1), and tumor necrosis factor-α are associated with TED. Systemic disease : Autoimmune disorders like pernicious anemia, systemic lupus erythematosus, Addison disease, vitiligo, celiac disease, and rheumatoid arthritis are associated with a higher risk of TED. Environmental factors : Smoking is strongly associated with an increased severity of TED. Dysthyroid status : At the time of diagnosis, 90% of patients with TED have hyperthyroidism, 6% are euthyroid, 3% have Hashimoto thyroiditis, and 1% have hypothyroidism. Radioactive iodine therapy. Treatment with radioactive iodine can cause an exacerbation in 24% of TED cases. Stress : Psychological stress can aggravate TED by rebound immune hyperactivity following prolonged corticosteroid-induced immunosuppression.
