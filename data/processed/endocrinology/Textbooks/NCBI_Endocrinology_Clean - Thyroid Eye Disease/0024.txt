Surgical orbital decompression corrects the proptosis by 2 to 2.5 mm per wall. Orbital fat is the fifth wall of the orbit that can be used for surgical decompression. Strabismus surgery is planned as a second-stage intervention, after the inflammation from the orbital surgery has subsided, and the tissue remodeling has stabilised. The restricted EOM is recessed to achieve orthophoria in primary gaze. The final correction for the eyelid retraction should be performed at least 3 to 6 months after the strabismus surgery. Blepharomyotomy, levator recession, and spacer grafts are common strategies for eyelid retraction correction.
