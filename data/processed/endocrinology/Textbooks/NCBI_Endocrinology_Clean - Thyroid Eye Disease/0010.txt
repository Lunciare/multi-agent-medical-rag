Evaluation Thyroid eye disease cases are clinically evaluated based on the disease activity and severity. The activity represents the grade of active inflammation and defines the progression of the disease. The severity reflects the spectrum of functional and cosmetic deficits. Numerous classification systems have emerged over the last century to assess and grade the clinical manifestations of TED. The most well-known system is the NO SPECS classification, which grades the clinical severity based on the mnemonic: No signs or symptoms, only (mild) signs, soft tissue involvement (eyelids and conjunctiva), proptosis, extraocular muscle involvement, corneal involvement, and sight loss (optic nerve injury). The American Thyroid Association introduced this classification in 1969. It was updated in 1977 to the modified NO SPECS classification in which each letter was given a numeral 0 through VI.
