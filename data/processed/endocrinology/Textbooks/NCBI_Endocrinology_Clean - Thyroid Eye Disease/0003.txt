With prolonged inflammation, the Thy-1 (+) cell cluster causes fibrosis of the extraocular muscles, leading to muscle-predominant orbitopathy. In contrast, TED cases with Thy-1 (+) cell clusters exhibit enlargement of the orbital adipose tissue, characterized by fat-predominant orbitopathy. Individuals younger than 40 have a higher Thy-1 (-) response and show orbital fat expansion. Individuals older than 70 are more likely to exhibit a Thy-1 (+) response, accompanied by fusiform muscle enlargement. Epidemiology The prevalence of thyroid eye disease is approximately 50% among patients with Graves disease. The annual incidence is 16 per 100,000 women and 2.9 per 100,000 men in the United States. Risk Factors Ethnicity : The prevalence is highest in Black individuals, followed by White and Asian persons. Age : TED shows a bimodal incidence peak. The incidence is increased in women aged 40 to 44 and 60 to 64, as well as in men aged 45 to 49 and 65 to 69.
