Magnetic Resonance Imaging Scan MRI is an excellent modality for assessing disease activity. MRI parameters, such as the T2 signal-intensity ratio, the T1 signal-intensity ratio following gadolinium administration, and the apparent diffusion coefficient, correlate with the CAS severity scale. In T1-weighted images, the EOMs appear isointense to facial muscles in inactive TED, but enhance with gadolinium contrast. In T2-weighted images, EOMs appear hyperintense during active TED and hypointense during inactive TED. Orbital Color Doppler Imaging This technique assesses the hemodynamic changes in the orbit following an increase in orbital volume. The primary vessels imaged are the ophthalmic artery, central retinal artery, and superior ophthalmic vein. The peak systolic velocity, end-diastolic velocity, and resistive index are important indicators of ocular perfusion. TED demonstrates orbits with an initial increase in arterial blood flow during the inflammatory active phase, followed by a significant reduction in orbital blood flow during the inactive phase.
