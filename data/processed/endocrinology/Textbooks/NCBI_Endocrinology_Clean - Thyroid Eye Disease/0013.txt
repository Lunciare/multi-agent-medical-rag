Ulcer Perforation Optic Nerve Assessment Visual acuity (logMAR or Snellen) Afferent pupil defect (present/absent) Color vision Visual field analysis Optic disc assessment (normal/atrophy/edema) A novel classification, the VISA system, was proposed in 2006 with four parameters: vision, inflammation/congestion, strabismus/motility restriction, and appearance/exposure. The International Thyroid Eye Disease Society defined separate parameters for the assessment at the first and subsequent visits in 2018. An inflammation (I) score greater than 4 of 10 points indicates inactive disease and should be managed conservatively. A score of greater than 5 out of 10 points with evidence of progression of inflammation should be managed aggressively. The clinical assessment and scoring are similar to CAS-EUGOGO recommendations except for the following changes: Chemosis :+1 point if the conjunctiva lies behind the gray line of the eyelid, +2 points if the conjunctiva extends anterior to the gray line Eyelid edema :+1 point if no overhanging of tissues, +2 points if upper eyelid skin folds or lower eyelid festoons are present Strabismus/motility restriction : Ductions measured to the nearest 5° in all 4 gazes, using the corneal reflex method.
