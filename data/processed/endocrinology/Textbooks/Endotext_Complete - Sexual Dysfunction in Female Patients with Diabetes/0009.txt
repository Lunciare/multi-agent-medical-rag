Treatment Considerations: Management requires a comprehensive approach including: Optimal glycemic control Regular screening for complications Psychological support Sexual health counseling Treatment of specific symptoms (e.g., lubricants for vaginal dryness) Partner involvement in treatment planning Early recognition and intervention are crucial for preventing progression and maintaining sexual health in women with Type 1 DM.
