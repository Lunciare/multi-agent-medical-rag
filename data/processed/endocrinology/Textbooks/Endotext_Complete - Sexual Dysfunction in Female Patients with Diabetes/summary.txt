Sexual Dysfunction in Female Patients with Diabetes
KEYWORDS: sexual, women, dysfunction, female, diabetes, vaginal, sexual dysfunction, female sexual, pubmed, disorder, fsd, treatment, health, arousal, function

ABSTRACT Female sexual dysfunction (FSD) is a significant complication of diabetes mellitus, affecting 20-80% of women with type 2 diabetes. The condition stems from multiple factors including vascular damage, neuropathy, hormonal imbalances, and psychological aspects. Sustained hyperglycemia leads to blood vessel damage and reduces nitric oxide bioavailability, affecting vaginal blood flow and lubrication. Management requires a comprehensive approach focusing on glycemic control, lifestyle modifications, and specific interventions including lubricants, medications, and psychological support. Treatment outcomes vary based on factors such as age, diabetes duration, and complication severity. Early intervention and regular screening are essential for improved outcomes.
