However, there is little phenotype/genotype correlation, even between affected siblings, suggesting that other factors may be involved in disease progression ( 86 ). A recent review of the literature, showed that AI was more prevalent and diagnosed at a younger age in patients harboring truncating mutations. On the other hand, neurological dysfunction was more prevalent, with an older age at onset, in patients carrying non-truncating mutations ( 70 ).
