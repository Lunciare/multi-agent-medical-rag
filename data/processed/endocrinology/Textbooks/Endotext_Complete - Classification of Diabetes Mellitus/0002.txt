Prader-Willi syndrome 11. Others Post-transplant TYPE 1 DIABETES MELLITUS Type 1 diabetes results from autoimmune destruction of the pancreatic beta-cells [3, 4 ]. Markers of immune destruction of the beta-cell are present at the time of diagnosis in 90% of individuals and include antibodies to the islet cell (ICAs), to glutamic acid decarboxylase (GAD65), tyrosine phosphatases IA-2 and IA-2b, ZnT8, and insulin auto-antibodies (IAAs). Individuals may convert to negative if only one marker is positive, but individual risk of developing type 1 DM increases with the number of positive markers.
