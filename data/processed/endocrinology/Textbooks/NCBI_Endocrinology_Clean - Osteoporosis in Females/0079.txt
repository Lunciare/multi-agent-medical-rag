osteonecrosis of the jaw is not entirely understood. Still, it is believed to occur because of abnormal bone remodeling and an insult such as an infection or other dental interventions. The incidence of osteonecrosis of the jaw, as well as the incidence of atypical femur fractures, is lower in denosumab-treated patients. Moreover, limited evidence also supports teriparatide therapy for treating osteonecrosis of the jaw.
