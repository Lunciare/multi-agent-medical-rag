Following discontinuation of teriparatide, the anabolic effects of bone are lost, and it is therefore recommended for the medication to be followed by a bisphosphonate or denosumab; the Endocrine Society furthermore recommends considering raloxifene or menopausal hormone therapy. Abaloparatide Abaloparatide is a synthetic analog created from a modified parathyroid hormone-related peptide using the first 34 amino acids and functions similarly to teriparatide, approved by the FDA in 2017. Abaloparatide is a daily injection of 80 μg via a pen device; however, unlike teriparatide, it does not require refrigeration after use and can be stored at room temperature for up to 30 days. The adverse event profile is similar for abaloparatide; however, hypercalcemia is less common (and less severe) compared to teriparatide. While the black-box warning for osteosarcoma was dropped for both teriparatide and abaloparatide in November 2020, abaloparatide is still limited to 2 a total duration.
