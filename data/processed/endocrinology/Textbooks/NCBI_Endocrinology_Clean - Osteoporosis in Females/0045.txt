Treatment dosages include 10 mg oral daily or 70 mg oral weekly; special formulations exist, including combination with vitamin D, 2800 IU (or 5600 IU) of vitamin D as weekly administration, and an effervescent tablet. Alendronate was shown to reduce all types of fractures and increase BMD in the Fracture Intervention Trials (FIT), Fracture Intervention Trial Long-Term Extension (FLEX), and Fosamax International Trial (FOSIT). Alendronate is also used in the prevention and management of steroid-induced osteoporosis. Ibandronate is a third-generation bisphosphonate approved in 2003, which is administered orally. Prevention dosages include 2.5 mg oral daily or 150 mg oral monthly; treatment dosages are the same as prevention. Moreover, ibandronate treatment dosage is available as a 3 mg intravenous infusion administered every 3 months. Ibandronate differs from the 3 other approved bisphosphonates, as it has only been shown to reduce vertebral fractures (but not the hip or nonvertebral fractures). Ibandronate was studied in the Oral Ibandronate Osteoporosis Vertebral fracture Trial in North America and Europe (BONE), Monthly Oral Ibandronate in Ladies (MOBILE), and Dosing IntraVenous Administration (DIVA) trials.
