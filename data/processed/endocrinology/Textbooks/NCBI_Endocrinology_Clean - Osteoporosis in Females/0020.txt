Patients are administered tetracycline, which binds to the newly formed bone and will appear as a linear fluorescence. A second tetracycline dose is administered around 2 weeks later, appearing as a second linear fluorescence on the biopsy. The distance between the 2 labels is measured, and the amount of bone formed during that interval is calculated. A bone biopsy may also demonstrate a response (or lack of) to treatment. Histologic findings of osteoporosis include thinning of the trabecular bone, which is usually generalized, and perforations of the trabeculae, along with decreased wall width. History and Physical Clinical History Age : Apart from sex, age is one of the strongest risk factors for osteoporosis and subsequent morbidity and mortality. An estimated 85% of female nursing home residents older than 80 have a diagnosis of osteoporosis. Age is associated with decreased bone mass, with increased cortical porosity by 176% to 259% from ages 20 to 90. Additionally, age increases the risk of fractures independent of bone mass.
