Additionally, lymph node metastases are a feature in almost 40% of patients. The progression of anaplastic thyroid cancer is rapid, and most patients die from local airway obstruction or complications of pulmonary metastases within 1 year. Metastases occur in up to 75% of patients. They most frequently involve the lungs (80%), the brain (5% to13%), and bones (6% to 15%).
