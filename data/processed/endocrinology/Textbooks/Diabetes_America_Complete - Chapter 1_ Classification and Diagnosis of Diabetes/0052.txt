While higher sensitivity is thereby achieved, it is at the expense of less specificity and more false positives. Referral to a physician’s office for confirmation and treatment should always follow. Even a false positive test for diabetes that is not subsequently confirmed by formal testing in a physi­ cian’s office may have some benefit, as it should alert the individual that, barring a labeling or laboratory error, he/she might be at high risk for future development of diabetes.
