The difference in these results likely reflect differences in the popula­ tions studied, the glycemic categories employed, the duration of follow-up, and the A1c assays used. The lower prevalence of high-risk A1c than either IFG or IGT in the United States (Table 1.7) means that its use for screening will yield far fewer individuals who will develop diabetes using OGTT as the gold standard for diagnosis. In a Veterans Administration study of >12,000 persons, the odds ratio for developing diabetes over 8 years of follow-up reached 15 at the IEC A1c level of 6.0%–6.4% (Figure 1.10) (195).
