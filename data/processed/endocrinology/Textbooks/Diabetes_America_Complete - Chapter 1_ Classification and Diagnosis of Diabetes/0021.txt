In the United States, A1c levels are highest in non-Hispanic blacks, next highest in Mexican Americans, and lowest in non-Hispanic whites, when plotted by age in normal glucose tolerant individuals (Figure 1.7A) and in individuals at high risk for diabetes (Figure 1.7B) (65,66). Non-Hispanic blacks with normal glucose tolerance, prediabetes, or diabetes on OGTT have higher A1c levels than non-Hispanic whites in the Screening for Impaired Glucose Tolerance study and in the NHANES III, apparently independent of a one-time measured glucose level (67).
