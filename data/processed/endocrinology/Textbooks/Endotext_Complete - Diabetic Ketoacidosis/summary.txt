INTRODUCTION Diabetic ketoacidosis (DKA) is an acute life-threatening emergency resulting from severe metabolic decompensation. DKA more commonly affects individuals with type 1 diabetes (T1DM). Approximately 2/3 of adults admitted with DKA have T1DM. DKA is relatively common and in the US; 240,000 patients had a primary diagnosis of DKA in 2020. Early diagnosis and management of DKA is essential to improve outcomes.
