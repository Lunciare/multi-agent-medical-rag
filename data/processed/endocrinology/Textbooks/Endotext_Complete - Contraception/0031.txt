They can be used alone but are often used in conjunction with a vaginal barrier method (diaphragm, sponge, or cap). Nonoxynol-9 (N-9), the most commonly used spermicide, is an agent that destroys the sperm cell membrane, thereby immobilizing sperm. But, recent studies have shown that N-9 does not protect against STIs and HIV.[clxxiv] Spermicidal formulations include gels, creams, suppositories, film and male condoms.
