Pelvimetry and Obstetrics: Past and Present The classical external pelvimetry, whose foremost representative was the French physician Baudelocque in the 18th century, was abandoned with the advent of modern technology. It had been an attempt to explain the reasons for dystocia without offering any means to solve the problem.
