Type 1 Diabetes
KEYWORDS: insulin, glucose, diabetes, hypoglycemia, blood, blood glucose, patients, disease, risk, individuals, patient, include, management, hours, treatment

Introduction Type 1 diabetes (T1D) is a condition characterized by the immune-mediated destruction of insulin-producing pancreatic β-cells, leading to absolute insulin deficiency. The metabolic, genetic, and immunogenetic characteristics of T1D are heterogeneous, with age-related differences necessitating a personalized approach for each individual. Underlying genetic risk is present in many individuals with the disease. Hence, the American Diabetes Association (ADA) recommends that first- and second-degree relatives of individuals with T1D be screened and offered T1D autoantibody testing. Individuals with multiple T1D-related autoantibodies eventually develop clinical disease. The loss of insulin secretion can occur gradually or rapidly.
