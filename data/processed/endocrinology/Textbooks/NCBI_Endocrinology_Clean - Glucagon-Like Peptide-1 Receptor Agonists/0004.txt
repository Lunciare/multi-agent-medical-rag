Pharmacokinetics Absorption : GLP-1 Receptor Agonists (RAs) like Exenatide, Liraglutide, and Semaglutide are administered subcutaneously, ensuring rapid absorption and achieving peak concentrations within hours. The novel oral formulation of Semaglutide is tailored for gastrointestinal (GI) absorption, with consideration for bioavailability. Factors such as first-pass metabolism and potential interactions with food and other medications bear relevance in determining effective dosing regimens for oral Semaglutide. Distribution : Post-absorption, GLP-1 RAs (eg, Exenatide, Liraglutide, and Semaglutide) exhibit a low volume of distribution, predominantly remaining in the bloodstream. These agents selectively target GLP-1 receptors in various tissues involved in glucose regulation, with specific affinity for pancreatic cells and other metabolic control sites.
