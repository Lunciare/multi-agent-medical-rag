Detecting of B. microti in clinical patients using fluorescent RPA method The nested PCR detection was performed on two patient samples, and both samples tested positive (Fig. 4A). The fluorescence RPA method established in this study was used to detect blood samples from two patients infected with B. microti. Positive controls were genomic DNA from B. microti, and negative controls were blood samples from health individuals and the blank control was water. Both patient samples showed clear peaks with fluorescence intensities exceeding 1000 RFU (Fig. 4B).
