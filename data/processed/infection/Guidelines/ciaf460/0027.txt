Other considerations Other considerations specific to plazomicin include the need to involve a clinical pharmacist in designing and monitoring the once daily dosing regimen, to avoid nephro- and ototoxicity. Patients with baseline renal impairment and hearing loss may not be good candidates to receive this agent.
