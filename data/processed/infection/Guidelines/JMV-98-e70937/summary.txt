A comprehensive assessment of their epidemiolo- gical features and future trajectories is essential for informing targeted public health policies and achieving international control targets. Using data from the Global Burden of Disease (GBD) Study 2021, we evaluated the incidence of five common STIs (trichomoniasis, chlamydial infection, gonococcal infection, genital herpes, and syphilis).
