REVIEW ARTICLE Emerging Non‑Traditional Approaches to Combat Antibiotic Resistance Aditya Narayan Konwar1,2 · Shabiha Nudrat Hazarika1,3 · Pranami Bharadwaj1,2 · Debajit Thakur1 Received: 8 February 2022 / Accepted: 8 September 2022 / Published online: 25 September 2022 © The Author(s), under exclusive licence to Springer Science+Business Media, LLC, part of Springer Nature 2022 Abstract An increasing number of bacterial pathogens are acquiring resistance to the commonly used antibiotics. This has spurred a global threat leading to a resistance era and has penetrated the consciousness of the common people and the clinicians alike. The delay in discovering new antibiotics has exacerbated the resistance problem, forcing researchers to focus on unconven- tional antimicrobial therapeutics that differ from conventional antibiotics. Alternative therapies have emerged in recent years, including antimicrobial peptides, phage therapy, efflux pump inhibitors, antibodies, and immunomodulatory agents, which such as CRISPR-Cas system and peptide nucleic acids aided in the development of sequence-specific antimicrobials.
