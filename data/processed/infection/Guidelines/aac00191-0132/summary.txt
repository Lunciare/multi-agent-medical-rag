HAMILTON-MILLER of Hampstead, London, N.W.3, UnitedKingdom Received4May 1983/Accepted 14November 1983 Forty-four patients (43 female, 1 male), all with a history of recurrent urinary tract infections, were treated with 250mgofamoxicillin plus 125 mgofclavulanic acid (onetabletofAugmentin)every8 hfor7 days. The microbiological cure rates were 84% 1 week afterthe end oftreatment and 67% 1 month later. Side effects, which were reported by 20% ofthe patients, were mild and in no case caused interruption of treatment.
