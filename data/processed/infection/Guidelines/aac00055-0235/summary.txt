Cefepime treatment (dosage, 2.0 g intravenously twice daily for 4 to 28 days) was successful in 36 (90%) of 40 infections of the skin and skin structure or wounds and in 16 (84%) of 19 nosocomial urinary tractinfections. Ceftazidime treatment, 2.0 g every 8 h, was successful in 34 (96%) of36 infections of the skin and skin structure and in 15 (88%) of 17 urinary tract infections.
