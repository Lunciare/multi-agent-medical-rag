Author manuscript Curr Opin Pediatr. Author manuscript; available in PMC 2025 April 01. Published in final edited form as: Curr Opin Pediatr. 2024 April 01; 36(2): 156–163. Tick-borne Infections in Children in North America Emma Taylor-Salmona,b, Eugene D Shapiroa,b Connecticut Abstract Purpose of the Review: Because both incidence and awareness of tick-borne infections is increasing, review of major infections and recent advances related to their diagnosis and management is important. Recent Findings: A new algorithm, termed modified two-tier testing, for testing for antibodies to Borrelia burgdorferi, the cause of Lyme disease, has been approved and may replace traditional two-tier testing.
