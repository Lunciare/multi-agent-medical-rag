Isothermal Titration Calorimetry (ITC) Experiments ITC experiments were performed in duplicate using a NanoITC system (TA Instruments, Lindon, UT). MtFBA was dialyzed overnight at 4 °C against buffer D [100 mM NaCl, 20 mM Tricine (pH 8.0), and 2% DMSO]. HCA and ZnCl were dissolved using the buffer in which MtFBA was dialyzed to concentrations of 5 and 10 mM, respectively. To assess the interaction between HCA and free zinc(II) ion, a solution of 5 mM HCA was titrated into 0.4 mM ZnCl . To assess the interaction between HCA and MtFBA, equal portions of 5 mM HCA and 10 mM ZnCl were mixed and the HCA/ZnCl mixture was titrated into 0.25 mM 2 2 MtFBA. ITC runs were performed at 25 °C and comprised of one injection of 1 μL followed by 24 injections of 2 μL for a total of 25 injections. Each injection was spaced 200 s apart. The average heat released for the last five injections was used to correct for the heat of dilution. Data sets were analyzed with NanoAnalyze and fit to an independent model. Biochemistry.
