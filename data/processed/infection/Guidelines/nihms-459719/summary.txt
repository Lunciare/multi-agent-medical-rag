HIV testing and care systems focused on sexually transmitted HIV in China Joseph D Tucker1,2, Frank Y Wong3, Eric J Nehl3, and Fujie Zhang4,5 Boston, Massachusetts, USA Chapel Hill, North Carolina, USA 4National Centre for AIDS/STD Control and Prevention, Chinese Centre for Disease Control and Prevention, Beijing, China Abstract Background—Over three-quarters of new HIV infections in China during 2009 were estimated to be from sexual transmission. Over half of those living with HIV do not know their serostatus and identifying and treating individuals with sexually transmitted HIV infection has been challenging.
