Several studies have shown that asthma is associated with a dysregulation of the immune response and impaired production of antiviral interferons . Participants with asthma are also more likely to have severe respiratory symptoms during an infection, which may increase the likelihood of getting antibiotics prescribed. In the present study, 33% of participants with asthma had used antibiotics because of respiratory infection within the last year.
