SYSTEMATIC REVIEW Open Access Efficacy of phage therapy in Diabetic Foot Ulcers (DFUs): a systematic review Amir Hossein Esfandiari1†, Zahra Mobarezi1†, Samaneh Abolbashari2 and Zahra Meshkat2* Abstract Background Diabetic foot ulcers (DFUs) are a severe complication of diabetes, often leading to infections caused by multidrug-resistant (MDR) bacteria, which complicate treatment and increase amputation risks. Bacteriophage (phage) therapy, targeting specific bacterial pathogens, has re-emerged as a promising alternative to antibiotics. This systematic review evaluates the efficacy of phage therapy for DFUs. Methods A comprehensive search was conducted in PubMed, Scopus, Web of Science, and Embase up to October 29, 2024, using keywords such as "phage therapy," "diabetic foot ulcers," and "bacteriophages." Included were original English-language studies investigating phage therapy for DFUs, while reviews and non-English articles were excluded. Data extraction and quality assessment were performed independently by two reviewers.
