Benrimoj3 · Francisco Mardones1 · Sarah Dineen‑Griffin1 Received: 9 January 2024 / Accepted: 10 June 2024 / Published online: 15 July 2024 © The Author(s) 2024 Abstract Background Pharmacist-led management of urinary tract infections has been introduced as a service in the United King- dom, Canada, United States of America, New Zealand, and Australia. The management of acute uncomplicated urinary tract infections by community pharmacists has gained increasing attention as a potential avenue to alleviate the burden on primary healthcare services. Aim The objectives of the review were to: (1) identify protocols for community pharmacist management of acute uncom- plicated urinary tract infections in women aged 16–65 years; (2) outline their key components; and (3) appraise the quality of protocols. Method A grey literature search was undertaken for protocols intended for use by community pharmacists for the manage- protocol and written in English.
