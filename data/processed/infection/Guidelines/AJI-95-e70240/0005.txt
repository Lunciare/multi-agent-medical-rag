Advanced multiplex immunofluorescence is a robust aspect within the emerging field of spatial proteomics, recognized as Nature Methods’ “Method of the Year 2024” . This field permits simultaneous detection of dozens to hundreds of protein markers within intact mucosal tissue sections, thereby capturing spatial relationships within the local immune landscape.
