RESEARCH Open Access Acute lower respiratory tract infections and associated factors among under-five children visiting Wolaita Sodo University Ethiopia Birhanu Wondimeneh Demissie1*, Esayas Aydiko Amele1, Yibeltal Asmamaw Yitayew2 and Zemen Mengesha Yalew3 Abstract Background: Lower respiratory infections are a leading cause of morbidity and mortality worldwide, particularly in children younger than 5 years. Even if the burden of lower respiratory infections in children under 5 years old had decreased dramatically in the last 10 years, it is still the main cause of morbidity and mortality in children under-5 years old in developing countries, so the aim of this study was to assess the magnitude of lower respiratory tract infections and associated factors among under-five children visiting Wolaita Sodo University Teaching and Referral Hospital. Method: A cross-sectional study was conducted from 1st to 30th April 2019, among under-five child/mother or care- taker pairs visiting Wolaita Sodo University Teaching and Referral Hospital.
