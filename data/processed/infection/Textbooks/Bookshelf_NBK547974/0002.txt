Likelihood score: E (unlikely cause of clinically apparent liver injury, but experience with its use is limited). 2 LiverTox Mechanism of Injury The mechanism by which apremilast might cause serum aminotransferase elevations or liver injury is not known. It is extensively metabolized by the liver predominantly by the cytochrome P450 system and is susceptible to drug-drug interactions with agents that induce or inhibit CYP 3A4 activity.
