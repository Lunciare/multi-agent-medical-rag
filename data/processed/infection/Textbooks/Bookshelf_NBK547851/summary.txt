OVERVIEW Introduction Amifampridine is an orally available potassium channel blocker that increases acetylcholine in synaptic clefts of peripheral nerve endings and is used to treat the Lambert-Eaton myasthenic syndrome. Amifampridine is associated with a low rate of transient serum enzyme elevations during therapy but has not been linked with instances of clinically apparent acute liver injury.
