(Prospective study of 20 patients with rheumatoid arthritis, 16 with lupus and 3 normal volunteers: ALT elevations occurred in 20%, 44% and 33% after 2 weeks; levels being earlier and higher in lupus patients; no difference in albumin levels; hepatomegaly occurred in some and BUN and creatinine elevations in others).
