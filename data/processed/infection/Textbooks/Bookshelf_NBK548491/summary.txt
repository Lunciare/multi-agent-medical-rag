OVERVIEW The antiandrogens are medications that act by binding to intracellular androgen receptors, preventing the effec ts of endogenous androgens on target tissues such as testes, hair follicles, hypothalamus, pituitary, ovaries and prostate gland. The antiandrogens are used for a variety of hyperandrogenic states such as acne, hirsutism, and paraphilias, but their major use is in therapy of prostate cancer.
