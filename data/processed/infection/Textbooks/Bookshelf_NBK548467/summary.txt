OVERVIEW Introduction Anagrelide is an antithrombotic and platelet reducing agent that is used to treat the thrombocythemia associated serum enzyme elevations during therapy or to instances of clinically apparent acute liver injury. Background Anagrelide (an ag' re lide) is platelet reducing agent that is used to treat thrombocytosis due to myeloproliferative diseases.
