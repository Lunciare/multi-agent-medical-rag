OVERVIEW Anticholinergics are agents that decrease or block the actions of acetylcholine on its parasympathetic nervous system receptors on smooth muscle cells, glands and the central nervous system. Cholinergic receptors are usually categorized as nicotinic or muscarinic. Anticholinergics often demonstrate differential antagonism for different conditions.
