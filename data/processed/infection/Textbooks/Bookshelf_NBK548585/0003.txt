The patient initially developed a cholestatic hepatitis while on long term nifepidine therapy. Liver tests because steadily worse and nifepidine was ultimately withdrawn, whereupon liver tests fell to normal within 4 weeks. There was a recurrence of liver injury within 2 to 6 weeks of restarting another calcium channel blocker with a similar pattern of liver test abnormalities and a similar rapid improvement upon stopping. Both nifedipine and amlodipine are dihydropyridines, but there structures are dissimilar.
