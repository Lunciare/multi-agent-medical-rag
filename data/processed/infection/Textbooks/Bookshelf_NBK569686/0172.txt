Iron blocks the transcription of inducible NO synthase (iNOS or NOSII), the enzyme being responsible for cytokine-inducible high output formation of NO by hepatocytes or macrophages. In addition, by inhibiting the binding affi nity of the transcription factors NF-IL6 and the hypoxia inducible factor-1 to the iNOS promoter iron, iron impairs iNOS inducibility by cytokines (Nairz et al. 2010).
