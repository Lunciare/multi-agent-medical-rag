DCP3 ’s broad aim is to delineate essential intervention packages and their related delivery platforms to assist decision makers in allocating often tightly constrained budgets so that health system objectives are maximally achieved. DCP3’s nine volumes are being published throughout 2015–18 in an environment in which serious discussion continues about quantifying the sustainable development goal (SDG) for health.
