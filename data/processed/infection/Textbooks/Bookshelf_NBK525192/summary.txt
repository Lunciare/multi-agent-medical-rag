Policy analysis helps decision makers achieve the greatest value from limited available resources. In 1993, the World Bank published Disease Control Priorities in Developing Countries (DCP1), an attempt to systematically assess the cost- effectiveness (value for money) of interventions that would address the major sources of disease burden in low- and middle-income countries.
