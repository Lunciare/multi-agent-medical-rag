OVERVIEW Introduction Aprepitant and its prodrug fosaprepitant are antiemetic agents that are used to prevent cancer chemotherapy related nausea and vomiting. Both aprepitant and fosaprepitant are associated with a low rate of serum enzyme elevations after treatment which are similar to rates with comparator therapies, and neither agent has been clearly linked to cases of clinically apparent liver injury with jaundice.
