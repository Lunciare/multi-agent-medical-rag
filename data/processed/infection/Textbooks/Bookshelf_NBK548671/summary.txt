OVERVIEW Introduction Atomoxetine is a selective norepinephrine reuptake inhibitor used primarily for therapy of attention deficit hyperactivity disorder. Atomoxetine has been linked to a low rate of serum aminotransferase elevations and to rare cases of acute, clinically apparent liver injury.
