OVERVIEW Introduction Aripiprazole is an atypical antipsychotic used in the treatment of schizophrenia and bipolar illness. Aripiprazole therapy has not been associated consistently with serum aminotransferase elevations and has yet to be linked to cases of clinically apparent acute liver injury.
