Recovery was prompt once atenolol was stopped. Liver histology also supported the diagnosis. The patient was also taking a variety of over-the-counter vitamins, nutritional supplements and herbal products, but had taken the same formulations for several years and restarted them without incident after recovery from the acute liver injury. This patient had not taken other beta-blockers and had no history of allergy or previous drug induced liver injury.
