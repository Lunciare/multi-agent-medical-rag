(Among 7576 women with early breast cancer randomized to anastrozole or exemestane for 5 years, event free survival was the same while mild bilirubin and ALT elevations were more common with exemestane [1.6% and 1.4%] than anastrozole [0.6% and 0.6%]; no mention of instances of clinically apparent liver injury).
