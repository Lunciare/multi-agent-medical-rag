OVERVIEW Introduction Alprazolam is an orally available benzodiazepine used predominantly for therapy of anxiety. As with most benzodiazepines, alprazolam t has not been associated with serum aminotransferase or alkaline phosphatase elevations during therapy, and clinically apparent liver injury from alprazolam has been reported but is very rare.
