Bookshelf URL: Antiulcer Agents Updated: April 15, 2019. OVERVIEW Antiulcer agents and medications for acid peptic disease are commonly used drugs that rarely cause liver injury. Most agents act by inhibition of gastric acid production, neutralization of acid or protection of the gastrointestinal mucosa from acid injury.
