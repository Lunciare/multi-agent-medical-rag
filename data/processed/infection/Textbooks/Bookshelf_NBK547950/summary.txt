OVERVIEW Introduction Apalutamide is a third generation, oral nonsteroidal antiandrogen used to treat nonmetastatic castration- resistant prostate cancer. Apalutamide is associated with a low rate of serum enzyme elevation during therapy but has not been linked to cases of clinically apparent liver injury with jaundice.
