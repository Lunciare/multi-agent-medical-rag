OVERVIEW Helminths consist of a diverse group of parasitic worms including nematodes (round worms), trematodes ( flat worms) and cestodes (tapeworms). These infect a significant proportion of people living in resource poor regions. The pathophysiology of disease varies depending on the species and the worm burden.
