Likelihood score: A (well established cause of clinically apparent liver injury). 2 LiverTox Mechanism of Injury antibodies suggest an idiosyncratic immunological reaction to a hepatic metabolite may be responsible. Amodiaquine is extensively metabolized by the liver to metabolites that are excreted in the urine. Outcome and Management There does not seem to be cross reactivity to hepatic injury among the various antimalarial agents and switching to other drug can be done.
