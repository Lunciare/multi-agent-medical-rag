Hyperthyroidism is defined as excessive thyroid function and is typically manifested by thyrotoxicosis as marked by clinical symptoms and excess circulating thyroid hormone. The major causes of hyperthyroidism are Graves disease, toxic multinodular goiter and toxic adenoma.
