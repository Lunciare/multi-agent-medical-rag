OVERVIEW Antifungal drugs are a diverse group of medications used to treat fungal infections ranging from ringworm and athletes foot to esophageal candidiasis, coccidiomycosis, and cryptococcal meningitis. The antifungals are classified into several groups based on their structure and mechanisms of action.
