OVERVIEW Introduction Atezolizumab is a humanized monoclonal antibody to programmed death-ligand 1 (PD-L1), which acts as a checkpoint inhibitor and is used in the immunotherapy of several forms of advanced or metastatic cancer. Atezolizumab like other checkpoint inhibitors has major side effects and particularly immune related conditions, including acute hepatocellular and cholestatic liver injury that can be serious and even life threatening.
