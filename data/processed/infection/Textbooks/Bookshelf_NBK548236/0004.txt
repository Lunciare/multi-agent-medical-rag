Case 2. Cholestatic hepatitis due to atorvastatin.(2) A 72 year old man with hyperlipidemia was treated with atorvastatin (20 mg daily) which he took intermittently for 4 to 5 months. Having stopped therapy for 3 months, he restarted it at a dose of 40 mg daily, and one week later developed dark urine and jaundice.
