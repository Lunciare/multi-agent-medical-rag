Channel Blockers Finally, various arterial and venous vasodilators are useful for managing hypertension in special situations. Guanfacine, minoxidil and nitroprusside have not been implicated in causing liver injury. In contrast, hydralazine is a well known cause of clinically apparent liver injury, often presenting with autoimmune features.
