OVERVIEW Ampicillin is an oral, third generation aminopenicillin and is used widely to treat mild-to-severe infections due to susceptible organisms. Ampicillin has been linked with idiosyncratic liver injury, but very rarely and largely in isolated case reports. Background Ampicillin (am" pi sil' in) is an oral, third generation penicillin that is one of the most commonly used antibiotics worldwide.
