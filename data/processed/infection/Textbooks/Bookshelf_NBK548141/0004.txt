There was no jaundice and laboratory tests returned to baseline rapidly with the patient’s clinical improvement. The elevations in prothrombin time may have been due to the antibiotic therapy and the nutritional status of the patient, rather than hepatic failure. This case demonstrates the difficulty of attributing hepatic enzyme elevations and liver damage to a medication in a critically ill patient with multiple medical problems who is receiving several potent medications, several of which can also cause hepatic injury (clavulanic acid, ceftazidime).
