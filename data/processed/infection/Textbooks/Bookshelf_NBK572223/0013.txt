Diabetes Mellitus and Metabolic Syndrome A study in Italy found vitamin D deficiency increased the risk of type-2 diabetes mellitus among patients with HIV . Similarly, other studies have confirmed low vitamin D associ- ated with poor diabetes indicators, including insulin resistance (Eckard et al., 2013a; Moreno-Pérez et al., 2013) and insulin among individuals living with HIV. Further, studies showed links between vitamin D and random blood glucose , triglycerides (Portilla et al., 2014; Schwartz et al., 2014), beta cell function (Moreno-Pérez et al., 2013), and lipoatrophy among people living with HIV . However, several studies found no association between vitamin D and metabolic syndrome or other lipid measure- ments (van den Bout-van den Beukel et al., 2008b; Eckard et al., 2013a).
