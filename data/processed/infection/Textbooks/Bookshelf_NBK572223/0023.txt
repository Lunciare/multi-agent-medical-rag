HIV-1 protease inhibitors (ritonavir, indinavir, nelfinavir) were observed to reduce hepatic production of 25(OH)D and macrophage synthesis of 1,25(OH)D in dose-dependent, reversible relationships . Separately, 1,25(OH)D was protective against mitochondrial 2 3 DNA depletion of human skeletal muscle myoblasts and myotubes following nucleoside reverse transcriptase inhibitor (didanosine and stavudine) pretreatment . In human airway epithelium-derived Calu-3 cell monolayers, 1α,25(OH)D pretreatment increased differ- 2 3 entiation (based on greater cilia and mucus secretion) and P-glycoprotein expression compared to untreated controls . Additionally, cellular transport of ART (ritonavir and sasquinavir) was higher among untreated cells compared to those treated at three hours post-treat- ment . Moreover, several studies have found links between ART and CYP24A1, which is a key 25-hydroxylase enzyme involved with converting the active vitamin D metabolite 1,25(OH)D to an inactive metabolite form (24,25(OH)D, or calcitroic acid). Another study con- 2 3 2 3 firmed that ritonavir pretreatment reduced CYP24A1 expression and was associated with increased intracellular 1,25(OH)D .
