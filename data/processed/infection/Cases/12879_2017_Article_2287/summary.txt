The same observation has been made inHepatitis C (HCV)-infected patients previously exposedto HBV and treated withinterferon-freeDAA treatments. Because of common transmissionroutes,persons may have been exposed to HCV, HBV and HIV,but few cases have been reported in this scenario to date. Frequency of HBV reactivation in HIV/HCV co-infected patients previously exposed to HBV and treated with DAA remains unclear. Herein, we report an episode of HBV reactivation inan HIV/HCV co-infected patient prescribed with sofosbuvir/ledipasvir for HCV.
