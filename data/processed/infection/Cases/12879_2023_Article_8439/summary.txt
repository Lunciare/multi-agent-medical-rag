BMC Infectious Diseases (2023) 23:451 CASE REPORT Open Access Unilateral Osler nodes, Janeway lesions and splinter haemorrhages associated with surgical arterio-venous fistula infection: a case report Pramith Ruwanpathirana1*, Harindri Athukorala1, Praveen Weeratunga2 and Panduka Karunanayake1,2 Abstract Background Osler’s nodes, Janeway lesions and splinter haemorrhages are cutaneous manifestations of infective endocarditis. They occur due to vascular occlusion by septic emboli and a resulting localized vasculitis. They are usu- ally bilateral. We report a case of unilateral Osler’s nodes, Janeway lesions and splinter haemorrhages due to an ipsilat- eral surgical arterio-venous fistula infection. Case presentation A fifty-two-year-old Sri Lankan female with end stage kidney disease presented with fever for five days with blurred vision, pain and redness of the right eye. She had a left brachio-cephalic arterio-venous fistula (AVF) created one month back.
