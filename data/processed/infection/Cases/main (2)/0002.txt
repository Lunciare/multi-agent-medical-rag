Several studies reported respiratory pathogens during the cur- rent COVID-19 pandemic, including bacteria, viruses, and fungi, as co-infections among SARS-COV-2 patients . Early observation from Yang and his colleagues in China (Wuhan) found some SARS- CoV-2 patients with viral co-infections were in serious condition. Their treatments were more complicated and more prolonged in general . Influenza viruses such as influenza A, B, and Parain- fluenza seem to be commonly observed as viral co-infection agents among COVID-19 patients that show the clinical significance and increase the severity of the respiratory infection Acute Respiratory Distress Syndrome (ARDS) .
