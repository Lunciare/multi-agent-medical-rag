SERVICE DELIVERY 6.4 Linkage from HIV testing to enrolment in care Following an HIV diagnosis, a package of support interventions should be offered to ensure 6.4.1 timely linkage to care for all people living with HIV (strong recommendation, moderate-quality Interventions to evidence). ensure timely linkage The following interventions have demonstrated benefit in improving linkage to care following an HIV diagnosis: • streamlined interventions to reduce time between diagnosis and engagement in care including (i) enhanced linkage with case management; (ii) support for HIV disclosure; (iii) patient tracing; (iv) training staff to provide multiple services, and (v) streamlined services (moderate-quality evidence); • peer support1 and navigation approaches for linkage (moderate-quality evidence); and • quality improvement approaches using data to improve linkage (low-quality evidence).
