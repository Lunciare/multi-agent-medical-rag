They are structured along the continuum of HIV testing, prevention, treatment and care. This edition updates the 2013 WHO consolidated guidelines on the use of antiretroviral drugs (1), based on an extensive review of new evidence conducted in 2015. Although countries are at different stages of ART coverage and implementation of the 2013 edition of the guidelines, in 2016 there is a consistent trend towards further expanding access to ART, initiating treatment earlier and expanding the use of ARV drugs for HIV prevention. These guidelines present several new recommendations, including the recommendations to provide lifelong ART to all children, adolescents and adults living with HIV, including all pregnant and breastfeeding women living with HIV, regardless of CD4 cell count.
