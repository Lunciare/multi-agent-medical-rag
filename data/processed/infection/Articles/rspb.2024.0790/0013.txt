(d) Performance evaluation—1968–2002 We evaluated the ability of the framework to identify antigenic cluster transitions by comparing predicted outcomes with known antigenic transitions. To assess the predictive performance relative to a baseline model (electronic supplementary material, appendix S1 and text S5), we generated a null measure of predictive performance by comparing the prediction for each isolate with a randomly assigned antigenic transition outcome. NIAViD allows comparison with a baseline model and quantified the potential of other covariates in informing the antigenic transition of the influenza A virus.
