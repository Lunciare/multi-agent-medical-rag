Currently,S1geneticanalysisisthegoldstandardforclassifyingIBV strainsandassessingvaccineefficacy. Therefore,theS1genephylogeneticanalysisis crucialforidentifyingcirculatingIBVgenotypesandlineagesworldwide. Todate,nineIBVgenotypes(GItoGIX)comprising39lineageshavebeenidentified worldwide [2,14–19]. Among these, lineages GI-1, GI-9, GI-11, GI-13, GI-16, and GI-23 havebeenreportedinSouthAmericancountries,includingColombia,Trinidadand Tobago,Uruguay,Chile,Peru,Argentina,andBrazil. However,therearenoavailabledataonthecirculationofdifferentIBVlineagesinBolivia, hinderingthedevelopmentofeffectivecontrolandvaccinationstrategies. Most IBV outbreaks in South America involve the GI-1, GI-11, and GI-16 lineages. TheGI-1lineageisprimarilyassociatedwithrespiratoryproblemsandischaracterized as a vaccine-type strain . The GI-11 and GI-16 lineages have been linked to respiratorydisease,infertility,decreasedeggproduction,andpooreggquality. Some strains within these lineages have exhibited recombination events . Recently, a new lineage,GI-23,wasreportedonlyinBrazilduringanoutbreakin2022anddeserves greaterattentionduetoitsrapidspreadtodifferentcontinentsoftheworld. The GI-23lineageisofparticularconcernduetoitshighpathogenicityandassociationwith severeupperrespiratoryandkidneydiseasesinpoultry. DespitevaccinationagainstIBVinBolivia’shigh-densitybroilerareas,smalloutbreaks continuetooccurprimarilyduetointensivefarmingpractices. Recently,therehasbeen anincreaseincasesofkidneyandrespiratorydiseasesassociatedwithIBVinfectionin Bolivianbroilerfarms;however,thecausativeIBVstrainremainsunknown. Toaddress this knowledge gap, this study aims to identify and characterize IBV strains affecting poultry production farms in Bolivia.
