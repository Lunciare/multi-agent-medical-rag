The Clinical Genome Resource Dilated Cardiomyopathy Gene Curation Expert Panel has classified the strength of evidence supporting the relationship of ABCC9 with monogenic nonsyndromic dilated cardiomyopathy as limited. 2. Forty-two genes have been associated with Brugada syndrome; however, SCN5A is the only gene classified as definitively associated with Brugada syndrome by the ClinGen Brugada Syndrome Gene Curation Expert Panel. Pathogenic variants in SCN5A account for 30% of Brugada syndrome. Pathogenic variants in other Brugada syndrome-related genes are identified in less than 1% of affected individuals.
