Evaluations can include: • Molecular genetic testing if the causative pathogenic variant in the family is known; echocardiogram, electrocardiogram, and skeletal survey, if the pathogenic variant in the family is not known. Additional studies such as brain MRI with magnetic resonance angiogram and magnetic resonance venography may be indicated. See Genetic Counseling for issues related to testing of at-risk relatives for genetic counseling purposes. Pregnancy Management Women affected by Cantú syndrome should be referred to a maternal-fetal medicine specialist for evaluation and management.
