Clinical Characteristics Clinical Description 3q29 recurrent deletion is characterized by neurodevelopmental and/or psychiatric manifestations. Other common findings are failure to thrive, feeding problems, gastrointestinal disorders, ocular issues, dental anomalies, and congenital heart defects. To date more than 200 affected individuals have been identified.
