Gil-Rodríguez et al Clinical Characteristics Clinical Description While classic Cornelia de Lange syndrome (CdLS) was formally characterized more than 70 years ago and well delineated clinically [Ptacek et al 1963, Jackson et al 1993], the identification of the molecular genetic basis of CdLS has led to the recognition of affected individuals who have milder or atypical features. Therefore, this condition encompasses a spectrum of findings from mild to severe (see Phenotype Correlations by Gene).
