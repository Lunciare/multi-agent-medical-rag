Clinical Characteristics Clinical Description TECPR2-related hereditary sensory and autonomic neuropathy with intellectual disability (TECPR2-HSAN with ID) is characterized by developmental delay and subsequent intellectual disability, behavioral abnormalities, neurologic manifestations (muscular hypotonia, sensory neuropathy with lower-limb hypo- or areflexia and ataxic gait), and autonomic dysfunction (including central hypoventilation and apnea, gastrointestinal dysmotility, dysphagia, and gastroesophageal reflux disease with recurrent aspiration). To date, more than 30 individuals with biallelic pathogenic variants in TECPR2 have been identified [Oz-Levi et al 2012, Zhu et al 2015, Heimer et al 2016, Anazi et al 2017, Patwari et al 2020, Neuser et al 2021, Palma et al 2021, Ramsey et al 2022].
