Summary Clinical characteristics TAF8-related neurodevelopmental disorder (TAF8-NDD) is characterized by brain malformations, initially in utero with fetal growth restriction and possibly brain anomalies seen on prenatal imaging. TAF8- NDD is a progressive neurodegenerative condition, with progressive global cerebral and cerebellar atrophy and grossly enlarged ventricles.
