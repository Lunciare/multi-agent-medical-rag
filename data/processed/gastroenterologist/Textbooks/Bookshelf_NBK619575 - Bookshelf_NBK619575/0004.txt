Gene-targeted deletion/duplication analysis detects intragenic deletions or duplications. Methods used may include a range of techniques such as quantitative PCR, long-range PCR, multiplex ligation-dependent probe amplification (MLPA), and a gene-targeted microarray designed to detect single-exon deletions or duplications. Exome and genome sequencing may be able to detect deletions/ duplications using breakpoint detection or read depth; however, sensitivity can be lower than gene-targeted deletion/duplication analysis. 6. To date, no large intragenic deletions/duplications have been reported in individuals with TAF8-related neurodevelopmental disorder.
