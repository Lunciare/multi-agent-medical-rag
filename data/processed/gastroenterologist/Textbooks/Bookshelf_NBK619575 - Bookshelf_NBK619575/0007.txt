Genotype-Phenotype Correlations No clinically relevant genotype-phenotype correlations have been identified. Prevalence TAF8-NDD is extremely rare. To date, 11 individuals from six families have been reported [El-Saafin et al 2018, Wong et al 2022, Nadav et al 2025]. Population screening of the pathogenic c.45+5G>A splicing variant, initially identified in consanguineous couples from a large extended Arab family from a small village, revealed an estimated carrier rate of 1/33, suggesting a founder variant (see Table 7). Other reported affected individuals are from various ancestries (German, Hispanic, Moroccan, Iranian). TAF8-Related Neurodevelopmental Disorder 7 Genetically Related (Allelic) Disorders No phenotypes other than those discussed in this GeneReview are known to be associated with germline pathogenic variants in TAF8. Differential Diagnosis The principal neurologic manifestations of TAF8-related neurodevelopmental disorder are nonspecific, making the differential diagnosis very broad. Chromosomal and monogenic disorders (including primary mitochondrial disorders) associated with severe intellectual disability, regression, and neurodegeneration should be considered.
