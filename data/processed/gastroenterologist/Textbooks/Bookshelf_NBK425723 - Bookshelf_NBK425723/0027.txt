It remains unclear if this specific pathogenic variant is associated with an increased risk of developing neoplasia (see Genotype- Phenotype Correlations). Cancer and benign tumors. Although germline SMAD4 loss-of-function (inactivating) pathogenic variants predispose to hamartomatous polyps in the gastrointestinal tract (see Juvenile Polyposis Syndrome), the gain-of- function pathogenic variants associated with Myhre syndrome show no such associations (see Clinical Description, Neoplasia). Note that somatic inactivation of SMAD4, a gastrointestinal malignancy-specific tumor suppressor gene, is found in one third of colorectal cancer specimens and half of pancreatic tumors [Chen et al 2014].
