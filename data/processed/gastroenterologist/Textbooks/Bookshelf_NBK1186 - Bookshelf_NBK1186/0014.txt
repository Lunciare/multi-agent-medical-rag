Treatment of Manifestations Consensus clinical management guidelines have been published [Pierpont et al 2014] (full text). There is no cure for CFC syndrome. Supportive care to improve quality of life, maximize function, and reduce complications is recommended. This ideally involves multidisciplinary care by specialists in relevant fields (see Table 5).
