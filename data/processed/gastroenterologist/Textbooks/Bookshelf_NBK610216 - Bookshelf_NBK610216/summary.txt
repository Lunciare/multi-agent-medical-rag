Summary Clinical characteristics MEF2C-related disorder is characterized by moderate-to-profound developmental delay with subsequent intellectual disability, hypotonia, dysmorphic features, seizures, neurobehavioral manifestations (autistic features, sleep issues, stereotypic movements particularly of the hands), vision issues, and cardiac manifestations.
