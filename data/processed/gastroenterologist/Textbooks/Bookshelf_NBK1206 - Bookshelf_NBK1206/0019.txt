Therapies Under Investigation Antisense oligonucleotide therapy. Taniguchi-Ikeda et al reported that introduction of targeted antisense oligonucleotides in cell cultures from individuals with FCMD and in mouse models rescued normal ribitol-5-phosphate transferase FKTN (FKTN; also called fukutin) mRNA expression and protein production, demonstrating the promise of splicing modulation therapy as a precision therapy for FCMD.
