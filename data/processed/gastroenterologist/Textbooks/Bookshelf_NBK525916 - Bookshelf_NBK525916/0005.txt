The type of seizure is not specific and can include generalized tonic-clonic (64%), myoclonic (50%), tonic (50%), focal impaired awareness (partial complex) (21%), and infantile epileptic spasms (15%) refractory to anti-seizure medication [Ruzzo et al 2013, Alfadhel et al 2015, Seidahmed et al 2016, Gupta et al 2017, Sun et al 2017, Yamamoto et al 2017]. EEG abnormalities are nonspecific and include multiple independent spike foci most commonly (65%), burst suppression, hypsarrhythmia, and discontinuous EEG pattern [Ruzzo et al 2013, Alfadhel et al 2015, Ben-Salem et al 2015, Palmer et al 2015, Gataullina et al 2016, Gupta et al 2017, Yamamoto et al 2017]. Jitteriness and hyperekplexia are present in 78% and 55% of reported individuals, respectively [Gupta et al 2017, Jahanpanah et al 2024]. Abnormal muscle tone. Axial hypotonia is followed by spastic quadriplegia [Seidahmed et al 2016] leading to severe contractures of all limbs and neurogenic scoliosis. Brain imaging findings. The most common findings include generalized brain atrophy (100%), delayed myelination (75%), gyral simplification pattern (62%), decreased size of the pons (62%), thin corpus callosum (55%), enlarged ventricular system (50%), and cerebellar vermis hypoplasia (41%). Other reported abnormalities include left transverse sinus thrombosis, cerebral dysgenesis, Blake pouch cyst and/or arachnoid cyst, bilateral caudate atrophy, and increased lactate peak on MR spectroscopy [Ruzzo et al 2013, Ben-Salem et al 2015, Palmer et al 2015, Gataullina et al 2016, Sun et al 2017, Jahanpanah et al 2024]. Gastrointestinal manifestations. Feeding difficulties are a major problem for most affected individuals. Contributing factors include hypotonia, gastroesophageal reflux disease, frequent vomiting, swallowing dysfunction, and gastroesophageal incoordination. Many affected individuals also have constipation. Recurrent aspiration has been reported in eight individuals. Many require nasogastric tube or gastrostomy tube feeding [Ruzzo et al 2013, Sun et al 2017, Yamamoto et al 2017]. Ophthalmologic. Most individuals are unable to fix and follow with their eyes. Cortical blindness is reported in 100% of affected individuals. An additional study reported 40% with visual impairment without further clarification [Jahanpanah et al 2024]. Apnea. Frequent apneas necessitating mechanical ventilation have been reported in several individuals [Ruzzo et al 2013, Gupta et al 2017, Sun et al 2017].
