Nonspecific dysmorphic facial features were reported in all affected individuals in one report [Alharby et al 2020]; dysmorphic features can include brachycephaly, pear-like head shape, sloping forehead, widely spaced eyes, big fleshy ears, prominent nasal tip, and micrognathia. Genetically Related (Allelic) Disorders No phenotypes other than those discussed in this GeneReview are known to be associated with germline pathogenic variants in ASNS. Differential Diagnosis The differential diagnosis of asparagine synthetase deficiency (ASD) is wide. The cardinal features of spastic quadriplegia, microcephaly, and low cerebrospinal fluid asparagine concentration may aid clinicians in differentiating ASD from disorders with overlapping phenotypes (see Table 3).
