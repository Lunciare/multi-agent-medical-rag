with asparagine synthetase deficiency (ASD). The dependence of the brain on the de novo synthesis of asparagine, and subsequent cerebral deficiency of this amino acid, helps explain the neurologic sequelae associated with this disorder. Insufficient physiologic arginine concentration causes cerebral atrophy due to decreased proliferation and increased apoptosis in neural cells and aspartate/glutamate accumulation, causing seizures and hyperekplexia due to increased neuronal excitability [Ruzzo et al 2013, Alfadhel et al 2015].
