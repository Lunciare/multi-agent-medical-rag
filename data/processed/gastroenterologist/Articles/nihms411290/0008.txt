Histone variants Histones also carry information relevant to DNA template-based tasks in their primary amino acid sequence. Structural differences between variant histones can affect their DNA binding and wrapping, post-translational modifications and ability to interact with other histones and regulatory complexes.21,31 The incorporation of histone variants into nucleosomes is carried about by histone chaperones in coordination with chromatin- remodeling complexes, which are examined below. NIH-PA Author Manuscript NIH-PA Author Manuscript NIH-PA Author Manuscript Histone variants can be grouped into canonical and noncanonical variants according to whether their expression is coupled to, or independent of, DNA replication: whereas canonical histones are concerned with DNA packaging and gene regulation, and thus are produced when new strands of DNA are synthesized, the noncanonical variants play roles in a wide variety of cellular processes including transcription and must be constitutively expressed.21 There are several genes for canonical histones, which either encode the same protein (e.g., H4) or differ only slightly. However, some of these differences may not be negligible. For example, canonical forms of H3, H3.1 and H3.2, only differ in a single amino acid residue, but are enriched in different silencing marks.30 As a result, H3.1 may selectively mark silent or non-protein-coding loci and relegate them to constitutive heterochromatin, whereas H3.2 marks facultative heterochromatin.30 Nevertheless, there are greater structural differences between canonical histones and their noncanonical counterparts (Supplementary Note 6). Noncanonical H3 variants are the centromere-specific CenH3 (mammals: CENPA), which anchor kinetochor-microtuble assemblies of the mitotic spindle,21 and H3.3, which supports active transcription.31 Universally occurring histone H2A variants include H2A.Z, which supports transcription initiation in promoters of poised or actively transcribed genes but also accumulates in heterochromatin,31–33 and H2A.X, whose role is to support DNA repair at sites of DNA double strand breaks (S139ph-H2A.X or γH2A.X).21 The animal-specific macroH2As inhibit transcription and contribute to the silencing of the inactive X chromosome of female mammals, whereas the mammal-specific H2A.Bbd (Barr body- deficient) variant is associated with active, accessible chromatin.21 The human sperm- specific TH2B
