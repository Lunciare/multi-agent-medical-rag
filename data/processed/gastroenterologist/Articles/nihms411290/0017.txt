For example, rat pups that experience more maternal care in the form of licking and grooming and arched-back nursing display reduced fearfulness, blunted responses to stress as adults and display higher maternal behavior toward their pups.67 The increased stress responses arising from low maternal nurturing were found to be due to reduced glucocorticoid hormone negative feedback resulting from increased DNA methylation and reduced H3K9ac and transcription factor binding in the glucocorticoid receptor promoter in the hippocampus.72 Further examples of environmentally-induced epigenetic changes are provided in Supplementary Note 13. The potential role for an interaction between the environment and epigenetics in evolution is discussed in Supplementary Note 14. Infantile hypertrophic pyloric stenosis with its association with male sex and first born child and resolution over time is of particular interest as a potential epigenetically-driven disease. ICC and smooth muscle cells external to the myenteric region develop from common precursors expressing the stem cell factor receptor Kit in response to specific inductive cues from their tissue microenvironment and undergo a series of gene expression changes.75 Differentiation of enteric neurons from neural crest precursors also involves major changes in gene expression patterns.7 Unraveling the epigenetic basis of these transitions may identify their role in delayed or disordered differentiation.
