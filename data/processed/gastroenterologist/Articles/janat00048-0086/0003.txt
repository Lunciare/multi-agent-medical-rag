RESULTS Small intestine The duodenum ofthe newborn opossum lacks intestinal glands and shows a patent lumen with only scattered elongated villi. Distally, the small intestine is smaller in diameter and the lumen is filled with short, immature villi. Most of the intestinal epithelial cells (enterocytes) of the entire small intestine are adapted for absorption and show extensive apical endocytic complexes, numerous cytoplasmic inclusions and large supranuclear vacuoles. Goblet and Paneth cells appear later in the nursing at weaning and the principal intestinal epithelial cells no longer show an active apical endocytic complex or supranuclear vacuoles.
