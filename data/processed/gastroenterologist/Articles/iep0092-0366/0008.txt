Arterial TNFa concentrations Baseline TNFa levels were <200pg⁄ml in the controls and remained low throughout the 6-h study period. As expected, TNFa levels increased significantly in the E. coli treatment group and peaked at 2h post-treatment before returning to baselinevalues by6h(Figure2). Microscopic analyses of feline lung and ileal epithelia during sepsis Lung tissue samples harvested 6h post-treatment and pro- cessed for light microscopic analysis demonstrated an increased presence of septal neutrophilia following E. coli treatment (Figure 3, panels a and c).
