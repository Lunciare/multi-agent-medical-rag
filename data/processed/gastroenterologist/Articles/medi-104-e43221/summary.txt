Medicine ® Bibliometric Review Microbiota–gut–brain axis and anxiety or depression A perspective from bibliometric and visual analysis Abstract Background: Anxiety and depression are mental disorders that are prevalent worldwide. Previous studies have shown that it is related to the gut microbiota. The microbiota–gut–brain axis (MGB axis) is a bidirectional signaling pathway that links the gut and central nervous systems. It plays a pivotal role in the development of psychiatric and gastrointestinal disorders, with interactions occurring via the neural, metabolic, immune, and endocrine pathways. This study aimed to reveal the research trends in the MGB axis and anxiety or depression. Methods: Documents were retrieved from the Web of Science Core Collection with search terms for the microbiota–brain–gut axis and anxiety or depression.
