Efficacy of novel cell-based treatments must be established in clinical practice. Clinical trials are needed to evaluate unresolved issues of liver cell therapies. These hurdles are likely to be overcome and will be associated with wider application of cell-based therapy in the treatment of liver diseases. Meanwhile, parallel strategies of increasing organ donation are also required in conjunction with advances in cell therapies to combat liver disease, such strategies include living donor and split liver transplantation, and the tissue engineered transplantable liver.
