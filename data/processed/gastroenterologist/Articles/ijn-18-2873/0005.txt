As delivery vectors, exosomes can be isolated and prepared in various ways (Table 1). Exosomes can be isolated by differential ultracentrifugation and density gradient centrifugation, immunoaffinity chromatography, size exclusion chromatography, polymer precipitation, and microfluidic technologies. Differential ultracentrifugation and density gradient centrifugation are considered to be the “gold standard” for the isolation of exosomes.72 Different separation methods have their advantages and disadvantages, and the best separation method can be selected according to the actual needs and conditions.
