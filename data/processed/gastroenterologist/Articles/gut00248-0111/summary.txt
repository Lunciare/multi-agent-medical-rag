28, 1283-1290 Organ culture ofrabbit ileum model for the as a investigation of the mechanism ofintestinal damage by enteropathogenic Escherichia coli R M BATT, C A IIART, L McLEAN, AND J R SAUNDERS Froml theDepartnents of Veterinary Pathology, Medical Microbiology andMicrobiology, UniversityoJ Liverpool, Liverpool SUMMARY Organ culture ofrabbit ileum has been established as a model for the investigation of themechanismofintestinaldamagebyenteropathogenicEscherichiacoli(EPEC). Loopsofrabbit ileum were filled in vivo with saline, non-enteropathogenic P-fimbriate Ecoli (PFEC), or EPEC. After45 minutes the loops were washed, then mucosal biopsies were taken andcultured forup to 48 hours. The earliest changes discernable by electron microscopy were observed at 18 hours postinfection, at which time EPEC were closely adherent tothe surface ofenterocytes atthe base of microvilli, some ofwhich were elongated. By 24 hours postinfection there were large areas of brush border effacement with pedestal formation around the EPEC. Nosuch damage wasseen in biopsiesfromthecontrolloops (saline, PFEC), andintracellularultrastructure extremelywell was preserved in all preparations for up to 48 hours.
