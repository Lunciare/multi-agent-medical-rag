DISCUSSION The finding that the rectal gland differentiates rather late in ontogeny confirms earlier reports of Hoskins (1917) and Crofts (1925). Our results, when considered with those ofCrofts, indicate a triphasic pattern ofgrowth ofthe rectal gland. The significance of this pattern remains obscure. It is possible, however, that the accelerated growth in late gestation heralds the onset ofglandular function follow- ingbirth.
