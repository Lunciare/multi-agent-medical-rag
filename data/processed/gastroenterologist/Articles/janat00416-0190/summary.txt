PHILLIPS INTRODUCTION The rectal (caecal or digitiform) gland of elasmobranchs has been noted and de- scribed by many of the early comparative anatomists (for review ofthe literature, see Crofts, 1925). Interest inthis glandwasrevived by the demonstration ofits salt- secreting capacity (Burger & Hess, 1960; Burger, 1962). The development of the gland was first describedforAcanthiasvulgaris. Comparatively latein ontogeny, an anlage is formed on the left dorsal wall of the posterior intestine and the organ is fully differentiated before parturition (Blanchard, 1878). Blanchard claimed that the glandular portion ofthe organ was of mesodermal origin, and briefly described its histology.
