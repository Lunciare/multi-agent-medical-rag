We have used an organ culture model to determine the usefulness ofthis technique in establishing the diagnosis ofCMPSE on the basis ofa single biopsy with in vitro milk challenge. Fourteen infants withdiarrhoeaclinically suspected to have CMPSEwere studied prospectively. Onthe basis ofmilk challengestudies seveninfantshad CMPSE. Theyhadclinicalreactiontocow'smilkwithassociated histological changes and depression of alkaline phosphatase levels in the jejunal mucosa.
