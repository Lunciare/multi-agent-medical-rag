Full bladder protocols have the potential to reduce OAR dose exposure via displacement of the small bowel and bladder away from the target volume, with the caveat of increased volume variability [18–20]. Simi- larly, smaller rectal volumes can reduce dose exposure, but are more difficult to reproduce.
