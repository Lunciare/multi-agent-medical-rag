Abstract Background: To evaluate inter-fractional variations in bladder and rectum during prostate stereotactic body radia- tion therapy (SBRT) and determine dosimetric and clinical consequences. Patients were instructed to maintain a full bladder and empty rectum prior to simula- tion and each treatment.
