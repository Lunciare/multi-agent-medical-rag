Caspase-11Non-CanonicalInflammasome-ActivatedSignalingPathways As described earlier, the direct interaction between LPS and caspase-11 induces the oligomerization of the caspase-11 non-canonical inflammasome. The two main inflammatory signaling pathways are activatedbythecaspase-11non-canonicalinflammasome.
