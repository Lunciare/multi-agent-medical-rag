BMC Infectious Diseases (2024) 24:801 BMC Infectious Diseases CASE REPORT Open Access Pneumomediastinum and pneumoretroperitoneum after COVID-19: concealed intestinal perforation Jiaying Shen1, Xiaoyong Shen1, Feimin Zhao1 and Jianping Yao1* Abstract Background With the prevalence of coronavirus disease 2019 (COVID-19), many severe cases have been discovered worldwide. Here, a case of concurrent pneumomediastinum, pneumoretroperitoneum, and intestinal perforation was reported. This case was the first report on COVID-19-induced related complications. Case presentation A 74-year-old female patient was hospitalized for COVID-19. Air leakage was unexpectedly found during imaging reexamination. Considering the unobvious subjective feeling of the patient, a conservative treatment was given at the early stage, and finally, sigmoid colon perforation was surgically confirmed.
