From the standpoint of patient safety and quality improvement, it is very important to document the scope identification number so that any potential adverse event is traceable, and the patients involved can be contacted as needed. CONCLUSION In summary, we report a case of iatrogenic colon mucosa damage from high-pressure water jet due to colonoscope malfunction. Endoscopists should be vigilant to recognize this potential endoscopic complication.
