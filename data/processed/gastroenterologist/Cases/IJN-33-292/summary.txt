Case Report Wilson’s Disease Presents as Recurrent Hypokalemic Muscle Paralysis Abstract Mythri Shankar, A 21-year-old euthyroid gentleman born to nonconsanguineous parents was diagnosed with bipolar Monica Nayaka1, affective disorder. He presented 4 years later with hypokalemic quadriparesis. On evaluation, he Kishan Aralapuram2, was found to have features of both proximal and distal renal tubular acidosis. Ophthalmologic Sreedhara C. examination by slit lamp confirmed the presence of the Kayser–Fleischer ring. The diagnosis of Gurusiddaiah3 Wilson’s disease was established with serum ceruloplasmin levels and 24-h urinary copper levels.
