BACKGROUND Xanthelasmaisanuncommonbenignlesion,which can be found in oesophagus, stomach and duode- num with stomach being the most common site in upper gastrointestinal (GI) tract.1 Colonic xanthe- lasmas are rarely encountered. In stomach, antrum and pyloric region are the commonly affected areas. They can be single or multiple and women are more commonly affected.2 It can be suspected by characteristic gross appearance of yellowish- white plaque or nodule and is confirmed by histo- logical examination.3 Its appearance mimics gastric malignancies like signet ring cell carcinoma4 and carcinoid, a neuroendocrine tumour.5 Histological examination of biopsied specimen can differentiate these conditions. Its clinical significance and patho- genesisis notclear. Someauthors have related it to aging process of the stomach so the prevalence is expected to increase with increasing age6 but these arealsoreportedinagegroupasyoungas3years.7 Sometimes it can be associated with dyslipidae- mias.8Itisfoundtobecoexistentwithvariouspre- malignant conditions like GI anastomosis, chronic gastritis, atrophicgastritis, Helicobacterpylori asso- ciated gastritis and gastric dysplasia so, making diagnosis of this uncommon and virtually harmless conditionisimportant.
