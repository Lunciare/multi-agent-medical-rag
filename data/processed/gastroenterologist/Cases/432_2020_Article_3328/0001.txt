Journal of Cancer Research and Clinical Oncology (2020) 146:2589–2594 REVIEW – CLINICAL ONCOLOGY Combined microscope and endoscopy total resection of primary pineal malignant melanoma: case report and literature review Jibo Zhang1,2 · Zhongwei Xiong1 · Jincao Chen1 Received: 21 April 2020 / Accepted: 18 July 2020 / Published online: 22 July 2020 © Springer-Verlag GmbH Germany, part of Springer Nature 2020 Abstract Background Primary pineal malignant melanoma (PPMM) is a rare entity of primary central nervous system melanomas, with only 26 cases reported in the literature to date. Case presentation We report the case of a 65-year-old male with a PPMM who has prolonged survival of more than 104 weeks after combined microsurgical and endoscopic total resection. This is the first report: combined microscope and endoscopy total resection; PPMM in China; PPMM with total resection alone. Conclusion Combined microscope and endoscopy total resection is beneficial to prolong the survival of patients. But the best approach to treatment needs verification from more clinical cases in future.
