CASEREPORT A 58-year-old male was referred to our esophageal clinic in 2007 for dysphagia and chest pain. He reported a history of dysphagia for solid foods and occasionally for liquids for many years, but denied heartburn, regurgitation, nausea, vomiting, anorexia, or weight loss. He was previously placed on lansopra- zole for his dysphagia without significant improvement. His medications were lansoprazole, bupropion, sim- vastatin, and niacin.
