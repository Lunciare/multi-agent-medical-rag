PGM1-CDG is caused by a deficiency of PGM1 enzyme, which affects multiple biochemical pathways including glycolysis, glycosylation, glycogen synthesis and glycogenolysis (Figure 1). Due to its involvement in glycogen metabolism, PGM1 has initially been classified as a glycogen storagedisorder(GSD-XIII). Glycosylationabnormalitiesinpatientswithvariantsin PGM1werereportedlater,andthedisorderwasclassifiedasaCDG. Uptodate,more than70individualsdiagnosedwithPGM1-CDGhavebeenreported[12,15–18].
