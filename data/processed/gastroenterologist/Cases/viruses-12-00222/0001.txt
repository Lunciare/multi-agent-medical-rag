Introduction Despite intense study, relatively little is known about yellow fever (YF) in humans beyond descriptive records . YF is classically characterized by three periods: infection, remission, andintoxication,withariseinserumtransaminaselevels. Aspartateaminotransferase(AST)levels areusuallyhigherthanalanineaminotransferase(ALT),reflectingdiseaseseverity[1–3]. Afterthe acutephase,thedurationofjaundiceisunknown. Weaknessandfatiguemaylastseveralweeks and slightly abnormal liver function may persist for 60 days or more . Here, we present an investigation of one case of symptomatic late-relapsing hepatitis after acute yellow fever virus (YFV)infection. 2.
