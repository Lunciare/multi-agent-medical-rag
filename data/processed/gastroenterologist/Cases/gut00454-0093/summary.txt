Gut, 1979, 20, 811-814 Case report Achalasia and diffuse oesophageal spasm in siblings M. DEMEULES Vermont, USA SUMMARY We report the occurrence of achalasia and diffuse oesophageal spasm in brother and sister. The familial occurrence ofthese unusual disorders is consistent with the possibility that they share a common aetiology and indicates the potential importance ofgenetic predisposition. Achalasia is uncommon, and florid diffuse oesophageal spasm rare. The relationship between thesetwodisordersiscontroversial.Theiroccurrence insiblingshasnot,toourknowledge, beendescribed before.
