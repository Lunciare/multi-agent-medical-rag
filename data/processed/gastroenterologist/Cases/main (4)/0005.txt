Moriyama et al. / Journal of Cardiology Cases 20 (2019) 183–186 185 Conclusion The present case demonstrated coronary vasospasm induced by a provocation test using ACh, during the period of chemotherapy including 5-FU, and syncope due to rapid AF that was resistant to antiarrhythmic agents, but sensitive to antianginal agents.
