The decrease in LS values was proportional to the reduction in biochemical markers of intrahepatic inflammation, AST and GGT.318 Therefore, the increase in LS measurements after alcohol use is due to the alcohol-induced intrahepatic inflammation rather than alcohol itself. In a study involving 50 patients with ALD admitted for al- cohol abstinence, the AUC for diagnosing cirrhosis in- creased from 0.92 to 0.95 after excluding patients with AST levels above 100 IU/L. Moreover, the specificity increased from 80% to 90%, with only a slight change in sensitivity from 96% to 95%.60 Therefore, VCTE should be repeated after at least one week of alcohol abstinence. Alternatively, cutoff values may be adjusted according to serum AST lev- els in patients with ALD and elevated biochemical markers due to intrahepatic inflammation.
