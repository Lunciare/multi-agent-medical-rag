Many of the research priorities relate to the need for new drugs, treatment regimens, shorter regimens, and better tolerated regimens. Evaluation of new drugs will require standardized case definitions, outcome measures, and comparator regimens as well as the ability to conduct multicenter trials . A recent publication produced consensus definitions of microbiologic and functional endpoints . In addition, a recent report of patient research priorities highlighted the importance of including quality of life outcomes in addition to microbiologic assessments in clinical trials .
