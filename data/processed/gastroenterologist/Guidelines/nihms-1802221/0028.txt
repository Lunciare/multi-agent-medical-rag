function and muscle mass. 3. Development of therapeutics and multimodal strategies targeting frailty and sarcopenia. Frailty and sarcopenia are measurable and clinically significant. The objective measurement of these conditions enables a field of research focused on reversing or slowing their consequences. This will require collaboration across many disciplines, including hepatology, surgery, nutrition, and physiotherapy; but it will also require collaboration across industries, including academia and pharmaceutical and biotechnology firms. One essential step for the development of therapeutics is certification by the Food and Drug Administration of clinically meaningful endpoints of frailty and sarcopenia.
