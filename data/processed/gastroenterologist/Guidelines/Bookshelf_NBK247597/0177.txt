Additional information which could not be meta-analysed: Adverse events In the COURTNEY199244 study it was unclear whether the figures reported were the total number of adverse events, rather than the number of patients experiencing one or more adverse events. There were 6 in the olsalazine group and 5 in the mesalazine group; 9 probably/ definitely drug related adverse events (diarrhoea in 2 olsalazine patients (1 withdrew), 2 patients in each group had abdominal pain (both in the mesalazine group withdrew and were found to have duodenal ulcers and 1 from the olsalazine group withdrew), nausea and rash in 1 olsalazine patient and 2 mesalazine patients. At the end of the 12 months two patients had colon cancer, symptomless and small; one in each group. They had had ulcerative colitis for 14.5 and 19 years.
