Clinical improvement Topical ASAs are clinically more effective at increasing clinical improvement rates compared to placebo at 2, 4 [very low quality evidence, 5 studies, N=405; very low quality evidence, 5 studies, N=391] and 8 weeks [low quality evidence, 1 study, N=287] but not at 6 weeks [low quality evidence, 1 study, N=80].
