There are an extensive number of different indices used in the published literature and many of these indices are not validated. Including all studies that have author defined definitions and limiting the review to studies that use one index carries a high risk of bias however, by choosing one index the GDG felt that too many studies would be excluded and there would be a lack of evidence to consider. The bias associated with using the author’s definitions was taken into account when analysing the data. There were no setting restrictions. A trial duration limit of 12 weeks was applied. It was thought that any drug taking longer than 12 weeks to have an effect would not be suitable for the induction of remission and more likely to be maintenance of remission treatment.
