Ulcerative colitis Inducing remission in people with ulcerative colitis escalated in the following sequence in the event of treatment failure; high dose oral ASA + beclometasone, prednisolone, inpatient drug treatment and surgery. Table 68 shows the breakdown of results. The probabilistic results allowed a ranking of the net monetary benefit to be developed and also showed the probability of an intervention being cost-effective out of 1000 simulations. Strategy 10 was cost-effective in 54% of the simulations. This shows that although strategy 10 is likely to be cost-effective, there is uncertainty in the results. This was highlighted by the confidence intervals around the ranking of the net monetary benefit which ranged from 1 to 4.
