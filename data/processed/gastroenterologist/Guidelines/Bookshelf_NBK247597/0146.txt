Important outcome IV steroids may be more clinically effective at decreasing colectomy rates at 0≤2 weeks compared to IV ciclosporin [Very low quality evidence 1 study, N=29]. 5.45.1.3 Ciclosporin dose comparison Clinical improvement There is no clinically important difference between 4mg/kg versus 2mg/kg ciclosporin in clinical remission rates at 0≤2 weeks [moderate quality evidence, 1 study, N=73]. 5.45.1.4 Corticosteroid preparation Clinical remission
