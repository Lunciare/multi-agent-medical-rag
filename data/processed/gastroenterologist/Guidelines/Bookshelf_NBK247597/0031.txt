Informed Ulcerative colitis Guideline summary 4.3 Full list of recommendations Adults, children and young people This guideline covers people of all ages with a diagnosis of ulcerative colitis. All recommendations relate to adults, children and young people unless specified otherwise. These terms are defined as follows: • adults: 18 years or older • children: 11 years or younger • young people: 12 to 17 years.
