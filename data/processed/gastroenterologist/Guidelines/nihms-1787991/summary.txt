Terdiman, MD6 American Gastroenterological Association Institute Clinical Guidelines Committee 1Division of Gastroenterology and Center for Inflammatory Bowel Diseases, Beth Israel Healthcare System, Cleveland, Ohio Introduction CD is a chronic inflammatory bowel disease with substantial morbidity when not adequately controlled.2 Historically, approximately 20% of patients with CD were hospitalized every year, and the risk of surgery within one year of diagnosis was 24%, 36% by five years and 47% by 10 years.3 In recent years, outcomes have improved, likely because of earlier diagnosis, increasing use of biologics, escalation or alteration of therapy based on disease severity, and endoscopic management of colorectal cancer.
