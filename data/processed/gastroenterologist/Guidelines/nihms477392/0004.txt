The frequency of CD in common clinical scenarios varies from modestly elevated, such as irritable bowel syndrome, to substantially elevated, such as unexplained iron deficiency anemia (Table 2).9, 10, 11 The complexity of deciding who to test is exemplified by patients with dyspepsia.
