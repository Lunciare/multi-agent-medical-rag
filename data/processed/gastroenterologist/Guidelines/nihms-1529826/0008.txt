In NAFLD/NASH, it is difficult to draw a clear picture, because patient populations studied vary according to the presence or absence of overweight/obesity, chronic inflammation, or the metabolic syndrome. In severely obese men with NAFLD and metabolic syndrome, REE was higher by 17 % than in those without metabolic syndrome (75).
