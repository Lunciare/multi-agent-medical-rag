CAVEATS: APPROPRIATE USE AND/OR POTENTIAL MISUSE OF GENETIC TESTS There are some important limitations to CYP2C19 genetic tests. Targeted genotyping tests focus on interrogating previously described star (*) alleles and therefore are not designed to detect novel variants. Furthermore, rare allelic CYP2C19 variants may not be included in the genotype test used, and patients with these rare variants may be assigned a NM phenotype (CYP2C19*1/*1) by default. As such, an assigned *1 allele could potentially harbor an undetected CYP2C19 genetic variant that results in altered metabolism and drug exposure.
