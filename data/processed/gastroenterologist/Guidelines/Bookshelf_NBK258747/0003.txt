Offer early laparoscopic cholecystectomy (to be carried out within 1 week of diagnosis) to people with acute cholecystitis. Reconsider laparoscopic cholecystectomy for people who have had percutaneous cholecystostomy once they are well enough for surgery. Clear the bile duct:  surgically at the time of laparoscopic cholecystectomy or  with endoscopic retrograde cholangiopancreatography (ERCP) before or at the time of laparoscopic cholecystectomy. If the bile duct cannot be cleared with ERCP, use biliary stenting to achieve biliary drainage only as a temporary measure until definitive endoscopic or surgical clearance.
