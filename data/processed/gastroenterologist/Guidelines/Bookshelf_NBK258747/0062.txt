bile duct stones 4.6.3 Health economic evidence A literature search was conducted jointly for questions 4 and 5 by applying standard health 1,396 references were retrieved, of which 1 was retained for question 4b and 1 was retained for question 5. Gurusamy et al. (2012) (see Table 11) used a decision tree to compare intraoperative and preoperative ERCP for patients with gallbladder stones and CBDS. The study modelled the success or failure (and subsequent repeat ERCP) of CBDS extraction, complications of ERCP, consequent cholecystectomy (laparoscopic or open) and mortality from symptoms and open operations. Whilst this analysis was directly relevant to the UK NHS, the model did not compare all the interventions included in this guideline question (as postoperative ERCP and intraoperative bile duct exploration were not included).
