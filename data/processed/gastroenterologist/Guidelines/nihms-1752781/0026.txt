What is the role of ERCP in the management for Mirizzi syndrome and hepatolithiasis? Recommendations: For patients with Mirizzi syndrome, peroral cholangioscopic therapy may be an alternative to surgical management depending on local expertise; however, gallbladder resection is needed regardless of strategy. For hepatolithiasis we suggest a multidisciplinary approach including endoscopy, interventional radiology, and surgery.
