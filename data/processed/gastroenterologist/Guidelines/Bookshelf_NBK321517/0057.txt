Internal Clinical Guidelines Team 2015 Coeliac disease Evidence for testing for coeliac disease High quality evidence from 3 studies (Hopper et al., 2008; Volta et al., 2010; Swallow et al., 2012) of 2900 adults reported that IgA EMA has moderate levels of sensitivity [85% (95% CI: 78 to 90)] and high specificity [ 98% (95% CI: 98 to 99)] in the diagnostic process for adults suspected of coeliac disease. Moderate to low quality evidence from a single study of 268 children and adults reported that IgA EMA has high levels of sensitivity [98% (95% CI: 96 to 100)] and moderate specificity [85% (95% CI: 78 to 91)] in the diagnostic process for adults and children suspected of coeliac disease. 5.1.5.3 Evidence for the sensitivity and specificity of IgA DGP High quality evidence from a single study of 212 children reported that IgA DGP has moderate sensitivity [82% (95% CI: 72 to 89)] and specificity [80% (95% CI: 71 to 88)] in the diagnostic process for children suspected of coeliac disease. High quality evidence from a single study of 144 adults reported that IgA DGP has moderate sensitivity [83% (95% CI: 73 to 93)] and specificity [80% (95% CI: 71 to 88)] in the diagnostic process for adults suspected of coeliac disease. Moderate quality evidence from a single study of 268 children and adults suggests that IgA DGP has moderate sensitivity [78% (95% CI: 71 to 85)] and high specificity [97% (95% CI: 93 to 99)] in the diagnostic process for children and adults suspected of coeliac disease. 5.1.5.4 Evidence for the sensitivity and specificity of IgG DGP High quality evidence from a single study of 212 children reported that IgG DGP has moderate sensitivity [89% (95% CI: 80 - 95)] and specificity [81% (95% CI: 71 to 88)] in the diagnostic process for children suspected of coeliac disease. High quality evidence from a single study of 144 adults reported that IgG DGP has moderate sensitivity [83% (95% CI: 73 to 94)] and high specificity [97% (95% CI: 95 to 100)] in the diagnostic process for adults suspected of coeliac disease.
