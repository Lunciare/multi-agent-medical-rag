Revisions by the external assessment centre 5.11 The EAC incorporated the changes to the updated NICE guideline on chest pain in the company economic model. In this context, the EAC assumed that HeartFlow FFR would be used following an initial CCTA, and that non-invasive CT functional imaging tests would subsequently be used only if the CCTA result were uncertain or non-diagnostic. The EAC reviewed the parameters and costs used in the company's model.
