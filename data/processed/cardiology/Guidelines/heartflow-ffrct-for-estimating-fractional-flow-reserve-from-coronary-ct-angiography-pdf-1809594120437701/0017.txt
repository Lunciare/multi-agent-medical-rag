CT Economic model 5.6 The company presented a decision-tree model based on integrating HeartFlow FFR into the existing diagnostic pathway at the time of its CT submission. A theoretical population of 1,000 patients with suspected coronary artery disease was allocated to either the current treatment pathway (based on the previous NICE guideline on chest pain) or the company's revised pathway, which included HeartFlow FFR . The cost consequences of the treatment CT pathways were compared based on the mix of diagnostic technologies used in each. The model had a 1-year time horizon, included the impact of different testing strategies, and relevant clinical outcomes. 5.7 The proportion of patients eligible for CCTA as a first-line test and their probability of having coronary artery disease were taken from Rajani et al.
