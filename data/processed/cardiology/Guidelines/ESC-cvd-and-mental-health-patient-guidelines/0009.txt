Not all mental health conditions or heart conditions are permanent, but some are. Optimal care might involve medicines, but other techniques, such as mindfulness training or cognitive behavioural therapies, work too. Both CVD and mental health conditions can have an impact on carers or family members and it is important that they receive support if needed.
