Committee comments 3.5 There are several available technologies for controlling temperature after cardiac arrest. 3.6 Using large volumes of intravenous saline to induce therapeutic hypothermia before admission to hospital can cause side effects such as pulmonary oedema. 3.7 The committee was informed that additional RCTs comparing temperature control for fever prevention with no intervention are unlikely to be done.
