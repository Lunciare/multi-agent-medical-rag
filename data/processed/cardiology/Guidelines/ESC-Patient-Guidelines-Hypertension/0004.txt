Medication may also be necessary in people with elevated BP who have other diseases, like diabetes, a history of heart disease or stroke. Your healthcare team will discuss the type of medication that is best for you. In most cases, a combination of two or more drugs is recommended. Regular follow-up visits are essential to adjust your treatment plan as needed.
