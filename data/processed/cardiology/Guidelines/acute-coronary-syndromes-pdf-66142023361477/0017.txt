[2013] 1.8.9 Offer cardiac rehabilitation programmes in a choice of venues (including at the person's home, in hospital and in the community) and at a choice of times of day, for example, sessions outside of working hours. [2013] 1.8.10 Provide a range of different types of exercise, as part of the cardiac rehabilitation programme, to meet the needs of people of all ages, or those with significant comorbidity. [2013] 1.8.12 Enrol people who have had an MI in a system of structured care, ensuring that there are clear lines of responsibility for arranging the early initiation of cardiac rehabilitation. [2013] 1.8.13 Begin cardiac rehabilitation as soon as possible after admission before discharge from hospital, and invite the person to a cardiac rehabilitation session. This should start within 10 days of their discharge from hospital. [2013] 1.8.14 Contact people who do not start or do not continue to attend the cardiac rehabilitation programme with a further reminder, such as: • a motivational letter • a prearranged visit from a member of the cardiac rehabilitation team • a telephone call • a combination of the above.
