The Assessment Group undertook one-way sensitivity analyses, which indicated that uncertainty from event incidence and fatality rates had the largest effect on the estimated ICER (ranging between £2,500 and £6,500 per QALY gained). Probabilistic analysis at 40 years of follow up resulted in a lower estimated ICER of £4,154 per QALY gained (incremental cost £212 and incremental QALYs 0.051).
