Age is a protected characteristic under the Equality Act (2010). 3.18 Acute heart failure in pregnancy is relatively uncommon. Women, trans men and non-binary people who are pregnant are at greater risk of heart failure if they have: • established chronic conditions such as diabetes or hypertension, or • congenital or acquired heart disease.
