A 70-year-old man experienced sudden-onset palpitations and nausea while at rest and immediately called emergency services. On EMS arrival, he was alert (GCS 15) and denied chest pain. He reported a history of mechanical heart valve placement and multiple prior cardiac surgeries. An ECG was obtained prehospital and transmitted to the emergency department, which was approximately five minutes away. On ED arrival, vital signs showed respiratory rate 28/min, oxygen saturation 90%, and blood pressure 112/73 mmHg. The patient appeared diaphoretic, indicating hemodynamic compromise.
