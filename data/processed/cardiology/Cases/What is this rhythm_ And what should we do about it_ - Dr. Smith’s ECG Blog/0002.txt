AV block revealing atrial activity (P waves continue), no conversion Junctional tachycardia (automatic): typically does not terminate with adenosine → if no termination, consider JT/automatic mechanism and search for underlying cause (myocarditis, infection, meds/drugs, electrolytes, etc.) Practical ED takeaway in this case: treat as SVT and try adenosine; use the response to refine diagnosis and next steps.
