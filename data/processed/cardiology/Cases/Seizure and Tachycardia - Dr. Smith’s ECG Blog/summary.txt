Case Summary
KEYWORDS: sodium, channel, qrs, sodium channel, blockade, channel blockade, bicarbonate, ecg, toxicity, large, sodium bicarbonate, avr, brugada, cocaine, morphology

A young man presented with altered mental status (AMS) and seizure-like activity. EMS was called by police after the patient was observed ingesting powder and pills, followed by erratic behavior. He was actively seizing on ED arrival; seizures resolved after 5 mg midazolam (Versed) administered prehospital. During secondary survey, a large amount of white powder was noted both in a bag and free-form around the genital area, raising concern for acute drug toxicity. Initial ECG Findings (ECG #1) Sinus rhythm, borderline tachycardia Markedly wide QRS: ~178 ms Large R wave in aVR Terminal right axis deviation Brugada-type morphology in V1–V2 This ECG pattern is pathognomonic for sodium channel blockade. Although hyperkalemia can cause wide, bizarre QRS complexes, the combination of findings (large R in aVR + Brugada phenocopy + extreme QRS widening) strongly favors toxic sodium channel blockade, requiring immediate antidotal therapy.
