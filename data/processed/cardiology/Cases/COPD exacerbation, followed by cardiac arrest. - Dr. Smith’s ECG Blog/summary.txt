COPD exacerbation, followed by cardiac arrest. - Dr. Smith’s ECG Blog
KEYWORDS: right, ecg, embolism, acute, pulmonary, pulmonary embolism, severe, findings, copd, strain, clinical, arrest, cardiac, cardiac arrest, massive

An elderly woman with a history of COPD called emergency services for severe shortness of breath. On EMS arrival, she was cyanotic with increased work of breathing. During transport, she developed a bradycardic cardiac arrest. Return of spontaneous circulation (ROSC) was achieved. A bedside echocardiogram showed a markedly enlarged right ventricle and a very small left ventricle. Based on the clinical presentation and echo findings, a provisional diagnosis of pulmonary embolism was made.
