Consider proportionality: even if the T waves are not tall in absolute size, they may be large relative to a tiny QRS, and still form symmetric triangles. Safety Message Failure to identify and treat significant hyperkalemia is potentially lethal. Hyperkalemia may be misread as normal or as ischemia/OMI, so diffuse symmetric triangular T waves (even small) should trigger urgent consideration of HyperK.
