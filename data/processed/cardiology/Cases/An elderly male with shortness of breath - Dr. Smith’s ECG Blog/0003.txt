Am Heart J. 2000;139(3):430–6. Doevendans PA et al., Am J Cardiol. 1995;75(17):1206–10. ECG Comparison Principles The key to comparing ECGs is placing them side by side. Step 1: Ensure accurate lead-to-lead comparison by checking frontal plane axis and R-wave progression. Step 2: Compare ST–T wave morphology lead by lead. In this patient with new symptoms, early repolarization is a diagnosis of exclusion. Dynamic ST–T changes between ECGs confirm an acute event in progress.
