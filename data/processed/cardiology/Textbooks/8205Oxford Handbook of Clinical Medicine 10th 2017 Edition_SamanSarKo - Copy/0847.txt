Rupert Waterhouse, 1873–1958 (British physician); Carl Friderichsen, 1886–1979 (Danish paediatrician) Weber’s syndrome (Superior alternating hemiplegia.) Ipsilateral oculomotor nerve palsy with contralateral hemiplegia, due to infarction of one-half of the mid- brain, after occlusion of the paramedian branches of the basilar or posterior cerebral arteries. GPA is a mul- tisystem disorder of unknown cause characteri zed by necrotizing granulomatous infl ammation and vasculitis of small and medium vessels.
