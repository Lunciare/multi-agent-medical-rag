Score: 0–1: home treatment if possible; ≥2: hospital therapy; ≥3: indicates severe pneumonia and should consider ICU referral. Other features increasing the risk of death are: coexisting disease; bilateral/multi- lobar involvement; PaO2 <8kPa or SaO2 <92%. Treatment: See antibiotic guidance (table 4.2 on p167).
