Coeliac disease Dermatitis herpetiformis: Itchy blisters, in groups on knees, el- bows, and scalp. The itch (which can drive patients to suicide) responds to dapsone 25–200mg/24h PO within 48h—and this may be used as a diagnostic test. The mainte- nance dose may be as little as 50mg/wk. A gluten-free diet should be adhered to, but in 30% dapsone will need to be continued. SE (dose-related): haemolysis (CI: anaemia, G6PD-defi ciency), hepatitis, agranulocytosis (monitor FBC and LFTS). There is an  risk of small bowel lymphoma with coeliac disease and dermatitis herpetiformis—so surveillance is needed.
