Act FAST Several public health measures have aimed to increase awareness of stroke and the seriousness of the condition: the relabelling of stroke as a ‘brain attack,’ and via the graphic mass media FAST campaign = Facial asymmetry, Arm/leg weak- ness, Speech diffi culty, Time to call 999.
