Assessing hypovolaemia from blood loss Treat suspected shock rather than wait for BP to fall. The most likely cause of shock in a surgical patient is hypovolaemia. Check urine output, GCS, and capillary refi ll (CR) as measures of renal, brain, and skin perfusion.
