The acute chest syndrome: Entails pulmonary infi ltrates involving complete lung segments, causing pain, fever, tachypnoea, wheeze, and cough. It is serious. Inci- dence: ~0.1 episodes/patient/yr. 13% in the landmark Vichinsky study needed ven- tilation, 11% had CNS symptoms, and 9% of those over 20 years old died.
