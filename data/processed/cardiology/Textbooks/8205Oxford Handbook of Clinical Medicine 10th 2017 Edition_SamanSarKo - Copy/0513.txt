Diarrhoea can be defi ned as: • acute diarrhoea: ≥3 episodes partially formed or watery stool/day for <14d • dysentery: infectious gastroenteritis with bloody diarrhoea • persistent diarrhoea: acutely starting diarrhoea lasting >14d • traveller’s diarrhoea: starting during, or shortly after, foreign travel • food poisoning: disease (infection or toxin) caused by consumption of food/water.  Food poisoning is notifi able in the UK (www.gov.uk/health-protection-team).
