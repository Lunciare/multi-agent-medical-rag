Blood tests: FBC, ESR, urate, U&E, CRP. Blood culture for septic arthritis. Consider rheumatoid factor, anti-CCP, ANA, other auto antibodies (p553), and HLA B27 (p551) —as guided by presentation. Consider causes of reactive arthritis (p551), eg viral serology, urine chlamydia PCR, hepatitis and HIV serology if risk factors are present. Radiology: Look for erosions, calcifi cation, widening or loss of joint space, chang- es in underlying bone of aff ected joints (eg periarticular osteopenia, sclerotic ar- eas, osteophytes). Ultrasound and MRI are more sensitive in identifying eff usions, synovitis, enthesitis and infection than plain radiographs—discuss further investigations with a radi- ologist.
