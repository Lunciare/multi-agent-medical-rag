Zoledronic acid is signifi cantly more eff ective in reducing serum Ca2+ than previously used bisphosphonates.8 Usually, a single dose of 4mg IV (diluted to 100mL, over 15min) will normalize plasma Ca2+ within a week. NB: the use of furosemide is contentious, as supporting RCT evidence is scant.9,10 It helps to promote renal excretion of Ca2+, but can exacerbate hypercalcaemia by worsening dehydration. Thus it should only be used once fully rehydrated, and with concomitant IV fl uids (eg 0.9% saline 1L/4–6h). Avoid thiazides.
