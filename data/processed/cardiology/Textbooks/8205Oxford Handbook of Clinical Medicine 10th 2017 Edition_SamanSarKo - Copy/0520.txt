Caused by blood-fl ukes (trematode worms) of the genus Schistosoma (table 9.27). 258 million people in 78 countries required treatment in 2014. The life cycle is shown in fi g 9.44. Disease develops after contact with contaminated freshwater (swim- ming, washing). Symptoms are due to an immune complex response to the migrating parasite (Katayama syndrome), or deposition of parasite eggs in body tissues. Presentation: ~50% asymptomatic or non-specifi c symptoms.
