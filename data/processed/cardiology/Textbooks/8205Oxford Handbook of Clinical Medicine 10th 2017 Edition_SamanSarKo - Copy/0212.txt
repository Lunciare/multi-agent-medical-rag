Investigations: CXR (transient segmental collapse or consolida- tion, bronchiectasis); Aspergillus in sputum; positive Aspergillus skin test and/ or Aspergillus-specifi c IgE RAST (radioallergosorbent test); positive serum pre- cipitins; eosinophilia; raised serum IgE. It is usually asymptomatic but may cause cough, haemoptysis (may be torrential), lethargy ± weight loss. Investigations: CXR (round opacity within a cavity, usually apical); sputum culture; strongly posi- tive serum precipitins; Aspergillus skin test (30% +ve).
