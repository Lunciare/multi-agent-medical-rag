Paraneoplastic syndromes Paraneoplastic syndromes13 (table 11.3) consist of symptoms attributable to a malig- nancy mediated by hormones, cytokines, or the cross-reaction of tumour antibodies. They do not correlate with stage/prognosis and may pre-date other cancer symptoms.
