GFR can also be measured by injection of a radioisotope followed by sequential blood sampling (51Cr-EDTA) or by an isotope scan (eg DTPA 99Tc, p190). These meth- ods allow a more accurate estimate of GFR than creatinine clearance, since smaller proportions of these substances are reabsorbed in the tubules. They also have the advantage of being able to provide split renal function. Inulin clearance: The gold standard for calculating GFR, because 100% of fi ltered inulin (not insulin) is retained in the luminal fl uid and therefore refl ects exactly the rate of fi ltration of water and solutes in the glomerulus.
