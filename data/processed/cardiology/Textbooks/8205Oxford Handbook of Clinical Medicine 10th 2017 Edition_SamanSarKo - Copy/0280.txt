Dysmorphia, personal identity, and acromegaly We might have devoted this box to a grotesque homunculus depicting the signs of acromegaly: all disconnected lips, hands, feet, brows, and noses. But our integra- tive ethics disallow this, and ask us instead to see if acromegaly can reveal some- thing universal about our patients and ourselves. What is it like to feel in the grip of some ‘alien puberty’ or ‘empty pregnancy’?
