Common problems include sitting in the soft tissue of the chest wall, or sitting above the eff usion, below the pneumothorax, or in the oblique fi ssure. • Is it blocked? If not successful, obtain senior advice. • Has the eff usion/pneumothorax resolved? Pneumothoraces can rapidly resolve with a correctly positioned drain. 4 Unable to aspirate from NG tube: • Is NG tube not far enough in/coiled in oesophagus? Tip is radio-opaque and should be visible below the diaphragm, if it is coiled it may lie in the pharynx or anywhere along the mediastinum. • Is NG tube passing down the trachea and into the bronchus?
