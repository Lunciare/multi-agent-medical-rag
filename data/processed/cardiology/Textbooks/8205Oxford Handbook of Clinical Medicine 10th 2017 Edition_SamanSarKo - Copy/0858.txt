Diaphragm The right side is often slightly higher (due to the liver). Causes of raised hemidiaphragm: Trouble above the diaphragm—lung volume loss or infl ammation. Trouble with the diaphragm—stroke; phrenic nerve palsy (causes, p504; any medias- tinal mass?). Trouble below the diaphragm—hepatomegaly; subphrenic abscess. NB: subpulmonic eff usion (eff usions having a similar contour to the diaphragm without a characteristic meniscus) and diaphragm rupture give apparent elevation.
