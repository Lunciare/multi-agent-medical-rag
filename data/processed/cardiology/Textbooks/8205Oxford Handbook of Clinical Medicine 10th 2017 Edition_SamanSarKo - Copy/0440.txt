Complications of myeloma • Hypercalcaemia (p676). This occurs with active disease, eg at presenta- tion or r elapse. Rehydrate vigorously with IV saline 0.9% 4–6L/d (careful fl uid balance). IV bisphosphonates, eg zolendronate or pamidronate, are useful for treating hypercalcaemia acutely. • Spinal cord compression (p466). Occurs in 5% of those with myeloma. Urgent MRI if suspected. Treatment is with dexamethasone 8–16mg/24h PO and local radiotherapy. • Hyperviscosity (p372) causes reduced cognition, disturbed vision, and bleed- ing. It is treated with plasmapheresis to remove light chains. • Acute renal injury is treated with rehydration. Urgent dialysis may be needed.
