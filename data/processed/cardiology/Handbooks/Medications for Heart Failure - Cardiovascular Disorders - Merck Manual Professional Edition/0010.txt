Medications for Heart Failure
KEYWORDS: patients, dose, heart, ace, day, failure, heart failure, inhibitors, used, renal, orally, diuretics, inhibitor, ace inhibitors, function


ACE inhibitor or ARB therapy, hydralazine is started at 25 mg orally 4 times a day and increased every 3 to 5 days to a target total dose of 300 mg/day; however, many patients cannot tolerate > 200 mg/day because of hypotension. Isosorbide dinitrate is started at 20 mg orally 3 times a day (with a 12-hour nitrate-free interval) and increased to a target of 40 to 50 mg 3 times a day. When added to ACE inhibitor or ARB therapy, hydralazine plus nitrate therapy is started at 37.5 mg and isosorbide dinitrate at 20 mg orally 3 times a day, with the maximum dose 75 mg and 40 mg 3 times a day. These doses are also available as a fixed-dose combination. Nitrates and phosphodiesterase-5 inhibitors have been used to relieve symptoms in patients with HFpEF, but neither is recommended for routine use for these patients (2).
