Lamin A/C Cardiomyopathies (Cardiolaminopathies) - Cardiovascular Disorders
KEYWORDS: patients, cardiac, cardiomyopathy, ventricular, lamin, genetic, heart, testing, family, icd, risk, failure, genetic testing, dilated, heart failure


Some of the mutations also affect skeletal muscle. Diagnosis is based on clinical and electrocardiographic factors, cardiac imaging, and genetic testing. First-degree family members have a significant risk of disease and require screening. Treatment requires usually requires an implantable cardioverter-defibrillator and a beta- blocker.
