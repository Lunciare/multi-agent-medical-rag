Testing (see table Some Causes of Nontraumatic Limb Pain) is usually necessary for other suspected causes of pain. Treatment of Limb Pain Primary treatment is directed at the cause. Analgesics can help relieve pain. Key Points Acute limb ischemia should be considered in patients with sudden, severe pain.
