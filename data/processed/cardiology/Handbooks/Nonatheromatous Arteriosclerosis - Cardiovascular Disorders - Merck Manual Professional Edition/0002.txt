Key Points Nonatheromatous arteriosclerosis causes thickening and loss of elasticity in the arterial wall in the absence of obvious atheromatous plaque. There are 2 variants of nonatheromatous arteriosclerosis: arteriolosclerosis and Mönckeberg medial calcific sclerosis. Diabetes, hypertension, and age are risk factors for the development of nonatheromatous arteriosclerosis.
