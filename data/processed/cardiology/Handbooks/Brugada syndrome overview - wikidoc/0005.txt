Drugs to Preferably Avoid Drugs which are not contraindicated in Brugada syndrome, but which should be avoided, are amiodarone, cibenzoline, disopyramide, lidocaine, propanolol, and verapamil. These agents are all antiarrhythmics. Topical lidocaine used for anesthesia is thought to be safe when used in persons with Brugada syndrome.
