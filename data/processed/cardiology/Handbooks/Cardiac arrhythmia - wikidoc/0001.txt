Cardiac arrhythmia
KEYWORDS: tachycardia, atrial, block, sinus, syndrome, impulse, origin, arrhythmia, rhythm, ventricular, junctional, bradycardia, pacemaker, bigeminy, cardiac


Overview Cardiac arrhythmia is any of a large and heterogeneous group of conditions in which there is abnormal electrical activity in the heart. The heart beat may be too fast or too slow, and may be regular or irregular. A heart beat that is too fast is called tachycardia and a heartbeat that is too slow is called bradycardia. Although many arrhythmias are not life-threatening, some can cause cardiac arrest.
