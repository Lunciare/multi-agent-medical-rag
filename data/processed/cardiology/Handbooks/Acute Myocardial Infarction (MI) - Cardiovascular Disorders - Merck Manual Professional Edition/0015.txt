Acute Myocardial Infarction (MI)
KEYWORDS: infarction, myocardial, patients, coronary, myocardial infarction, acute, ventricular, risk, elevation, left, cardiac, stemi, hours, ecg, st-segment


Initiate or continue antiplatelets, beta-blockers, ACE-inhibitors (or angiotensin II receptor blockers), and statins following initial treatment. After hospital discharge, arrange functional evaluation, manage risk factors (eg, hypertension, diabetes, smoking), and promote healthy diet and exercise.
