However, in post-MI syndrome, cardiac biomarkers do not increase significantly, and ECG changes are nonspecific. 13/14 NSAIDs are usually effective, but the syndrome can recur several times. Colchicine is effective for treatment and to prevent recurrences. In severe cases, a short, intensive course of another NSAID or a corticosteroid may be necessary. High doses of an NSAID or a corticosteroid are not used for more than a few days because they may interfere with early ventricular healing after an acute myocardial infarction.
